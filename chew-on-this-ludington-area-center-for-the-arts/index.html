Chew on This

Food-themed juried exhibition at the Ludington Area Center for the Arts on the Lake Michigan shore, inviting artists to explore the creative, cultural and visual power of food — from realistic depictions to conceptual pieces about consumption and community. 'If it involves food — or is inspired by it — we want to see it.' All media welcome including photography, collage, drawing, painting, sculpture, ceramics, fiber and installation. On view at the LACA Gallery October 2–31, 2026. Open to artists 18+ nationally and internationally; $30 for up to two works; 30% commission for members, 45% for non-members.
