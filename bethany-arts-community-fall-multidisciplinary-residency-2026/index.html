Bethany Arts Community — Fall Multidisciplinary Residency 2026

Two-week artist residency in Ossining, New York (Sep 22 – Oct 6, 2026) for 12 artists across all disciplines including photography, visual arts, film, writing, and performance. Residents receive a $450 stipend, private studio, room and board, and 3 meals daily. Each artist facilitates one community program during the residency. Requires one letter of recommendation and one reference. Funded by the National Endowment for the Arts and New York State Council on the Arts.
