Solo Exhibition Series

Rolling call for solo exhibition proposals at Atlanta Photography Group, the member-based photography gallery in Ansley Mall, Atlanta. Through the Solo Exhibition Series, artists of all experience levels work with APG's staff to organise and present a curated show in the Maloof Gallery: solo exhibitions alternate with group exhibitions there, each running five weeks, and four to five solo shows are presented each year. Exhibiting solo artists receive an $800 stipend, generously funded by the artist Beth Lilly. All APG members may submit proposals; solo and group proposals of lens-based work are welcome, with group proposals for up to two artists. At least half of the work submitted for review must be from the last eight years (2017 or later), exhibitions must be installed in under three days, and proposals should be made in consideration of the Maloof Gallery space — the gallery publishes its wall dimensions and encourages applicants to visit before applying. Installation work is welcome but must be approved and installed by the artist. A proposal is two documents: a text PDF describing the proposed exhibition, with a clear thesis statement, a description of the work and a timeline, noting any portion to be created on site, plus a CV of one page maximum per person; and an image PDF of up to 20 images, one per page, each captioned with artist, title, medium, dimensions and year, under 50MB in total, with links for online streaming in place of stills for video or audio work. The gallery notes that many factors influence which proposals are selected, that the programme is intended to provide career development and experience for visual artists, and that a successful proposal considers its unique multi-functional space and engages the audience visually and conceptually. The $40 proposal fee is paid through the gallery's shop, and because only members may submit, the real floor is that fee plus APG membership, priced on the gallery's own membership page at $25 a year for students and $55 for an individual, rising to $90 for a couple, $100 professional, $180 darkroom and $250 to $1,000 for the Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers. No juror is named for the series. There is no fixed deadline — once a proposal is submitted it goes on file and is considered each quarter when exhibitions are being planned, and resubmission is only needed if the exhibition idea changes. APG is funded by the City of Atlanta Mayor's Office of Cultural Affairs, Fulton County Board of Commissioners and the Georgia Council for the Arts.
