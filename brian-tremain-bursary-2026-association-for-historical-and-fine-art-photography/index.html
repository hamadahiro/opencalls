Brian Tremain Bursary 2026

The Association for Historical and Fine Art Photography (AHFAP) is accepting applications for the Brian Tremain Bursary, which funds attendance at its annual conference for photography students, recent graduates and early career professionals. Brian Tremain was one of AHFAP's founding members and his family has sponsored the bursary in his memory for several years; each award covers a conference ticket, costs towards travel and accommodation, and a year of free AHFAP membership. The association states that this will be the last year the bursary is awarded. AHFAP Conference 2026 runs 9-10 November at Stoller Hall in Manchester, and the association pitches the bursary as a chance to explore a career in cultural heritage photography, hear about imaging and digitisation projects, meet leading figures in the field and see demonstrations of current equipment; day two of the conference offers studio visits and workshops unique to the event. Applications go by email to Denise King at [address] and should be no more than one side of A4 in font 12, giving your name, contact details including address and phone number or the address of your business, institution or college, a short paragraph about yourself, a short paragraph about your institution or business and your position in it (or, if you are a student, the area of photography you are studying), and a short description of how receiving the bursary will help you in your work or studies. The judging panel is the association's Honorary President and its Chair; all entries are confidential, the judges' decision is final and no communication is shared about the decision-making process. The closing date is 30 September 2026.
