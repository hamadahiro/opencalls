No Place Like...

Prizes $500 / $300 / $200 Exhibition

Art Center Sarasota's Atrium Gallery juried show for spring 2027 asks artists to explore ideas of home, belonging and place in an ever-shifting world: domestic spaces, migration, memory, landscape, community or imagined environments, read personally, culturally or conceptually, and reflecting on how place shapes identity, experience and connection. Submissions are welcome in all media, with photography named explicitly alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, mixed media, digital arts, sculpture and video; work made with generative AI tools is not eligible. Editioned photographic prints are allowed if labelled as prints and numbered, and work must be original, finished within the last three years and not previously shown at the Art Center. The juror is Greg Rumph, Assistant Principal at Booker High School in Sarasota, where he runs the Visual and Performing Arts programme; he holds a BFA in Illustration from Ringling College and two master's degrees from the University of South Florida, and two of his own paintings are in the permanent collection of the National Civil Rights Museum in Memphis. Jurying is blind — the juror sees everything except the artist's name and the price — and the awards are chosen in person once the show is hung: $500 for first place, $300 for second, $200 for third, plus two merit awards and four honourable mentions. Up to three works may be entered; 2D work may not exceed 72 by 65 inches after framing, 3D work 60 by 36 by 36 inches. Note that the Art Center's own website prints the deadline as 'Sunday, February 7, 2026' while its submission portal gives 'Sunday, February 7, 2027' — 7 February 2027 is the Sunday, the portal's own clock closes the form then, and the show runs in 2027, so the year on the website is a typo. All work must be offered for sale, with no NFS or price-on-request, and the artist receives 60% of the selling price as a member or 50% as a non-member. Artists are notified by 17 February 2027, work is received in person on 2 and 3 March or must arrive by post between 1 and 3 March, and the exhibition runs 11 March to 17 April 2027 with an opening reception on 11 March.
