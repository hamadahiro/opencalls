Roadside Attractions: Art and History on Route 66

Prizes $250 Best of Show $175 1st $125 2nd $100 3rd 3 × $50 Honorable Mention

Gallery Seven and the Gaylord Building Historic Site are mounting this juried show together for Route 66's centennial, pairing an art component curated by the gallery with a history component built by the Gaylord Building, and it runs long — 12 November 2026 to 24 March 2027 in the Third Floor Founders' Gallery of the Gaylord Building in Lockport, Illinois, a National Trust site on the old highway itself. The brief is the legacy of Route 66 in Illinois and beyond, read both as a real transportation corridor and as a symbol of travel, community, roadside culture and American imagination. Photography is in: the prospectus states that all media is eligible except film/video, oversized works and special installations, and Gallery Seven has run Open Lens, its own community photography exhibition, every year since 2010. Open to any artist 18 or over with no geographic bar — the gallery accepts shipped work as well as drop-off, and will advise on shipping on request. One $50 fee covers up to five entries, with an extra image allowed free for each 3D piece. Awards total $800 in cash: Best of Show $250, 1st $175, 2nd $125, 3rd $100 and three Honorable Mentions of $50, presented at a 6–7pm ceremony on 10 March 2027; the gallery's own web page also names a People's Choice Award voted by visitors. Work must be original, never previously submitted to or shown at Gallery Seven, framed with hanging wire and bumpers, and no taller than 48 inches or wider than 36 including the frame. Everything must be for sale at the artist's own price with a 30% commission to the gallery, paid out within 45 days of closing; sold work stays on the wall to the end. Entries go by email as .jpg attachments at 72 DPI and 3000 pixels on the long side, with the entry form, and the fee is paid by PayPal on the gallery's site, by cheque or in person — note the two closing dates: email entries are accepted to 24 October 2026, but if you post a CD with the form instead it must be postmarked by 17 October. Selections are notified 28–29 October and delivered 5–7 November. The prospectus says entries are juried by independent jurors to be announced, while the gallery's own web page says the show will be juried and judged by the artist partners of Gallery Seven; no name is published either way. Anything left at the gallery after 24 March 2027 becomes its property.
