VCCA Residency at Mt. San Angelo — Fall 2027 Period

Prize Residency

VCCA's Mt. San Angelo campus in Amherst, Virginia takes applications three times a year, and the Fall cycle — for residencies scheduled September to December 2027 — opened on 1 October 2026 and closes 15 January 2027. Photography is one of the disciplines the organisation names outright, alongside poetry, fiction, nonfiction, playwriting and screenwriting, performance, film and video, drawing, painting, sculpture, installation art, music composition and cross-disciplinary art; emerging and established artists may apply, professional training and achievement are not required, and students enrolled in undergraduate, graduate or doctoral programmes are eligible, though projects of pure academic scholarship are not. Mt. San Angelo hosts around 400 artists a year in residencies of no minimum length and up to six weeks, with flexible scheduling. A residency includes a private bedroom with private en-suite bath, a private individual studio, three prepared meals a day and a community of more than 20 other artists in residence; photographers can also request use of VCCA's black-and-white darkroom during the application process. Read the money carefully: one $30 application covers both outcomes. VCCA puts the true cost of a residency at $240 per artist per day, asks accepted artists who do not receive a fully funded fellowship to contribute a minimum of $105 per day, and says it distributes a generous amount of financial aid every year — "if you are accepted and cannot pay at least $105 per day, VCCA will work with you in good faith to be sure you can come" — with reductions granted where true need is demonstrated. The same single application is considered for every fully funded fellowship the applicant is eligible for. Eight are listed for this Fall 2027 period, and the ones open to photographers are the Alonzo Davis Fellowship (American visual artists, two weeks plus a $500 honorarium, preference to those of African or Latin American descent), the Greater Opportunity Fellowships (any discipline, first-time VCCA residents, preference to artists who self-identify as people of color) and the Marian Treger Fellowship for Enduring Creativity (female-identifying visual artists emerging in mid-life and beyond whose paths have been detoured by chronic health conditions or disabilities, two weeks plus a $400 stipend). The application runs on SlideRoom and asks for the form, current work samples and a project description; letters of recommendation are not required. Application fee waivers are granted on request to Artist Services at least five days before the deadline. Artists may be in residence at Mt. San Angelo only once every two years.
