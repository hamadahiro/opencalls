5X7 Photobook Vol. 3

Prize Publication

Open call for the third volume of 5×7 Photobook, the print photobook series from Artsin Square, an independent, artist-run, ad-free international platform that has published magazines, artist books and photobooks since 2019. Vol. 3 is guest-curated by Below Grand Gallery, described by the organiser as a New York-based gallery and curatorial collective on the Lower East Side, artist-led and community-focused. There is no set theme: any genre of photography, any style and any ratio or size, and previously published work is accepted. Selected artists receive a two-page spread in the printed photobook, an online blog feature, a dedicated social media post, a complimentary digital edition and the chance to review and edit their spread before printing; the book is sold online with worldwide shipping and distributed to selected bookstores and galleries. The organiser states that artists keep full ownership and copyright of their images, with permission limited to printing and distributing them in the book and extending to no other use. Submit up to 10 photographs (ideally 300 dpi JPEG under 10 MB) with an optional bio and statement of up to 400 words. The entry form prices 3 images at $25, 5 at $30 and 10 at $40, and offers a separate optional $20 online-interview add-on (non-refundable, featured only if selected). Open to visual artists aged 18 and over from any country, and to organisations submitting on artists' behalf. Notification of selection 20 November 2026.
