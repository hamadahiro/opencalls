BLM Canyon Country District Artist in Residence 2026

Two- to four-week residency hosted by the BLM Canyon Country District in the Moab Field Office, Utah. Open to visual artists working in any medium suitable for digital reproduction, including photography, painting, printmaking, sculpture, and video. Selected artist creates work that represents the Canyon Country District; the BLM may suggest specific areas or themes within field office boundaries. Includes a $125/week stipend funded by the Canyonlands Natural History Association, access to a BLM campsite for the duration of the residency, and use of the Moab Field Office for drinking water and showers. Residency takes place between September 11 and October 31, 2026 (artist chooses dates and length). Selection panel includes BLM and Canyonlands Natural History Association staff who evaluate portfolio, resume, community engagement proposal, and application responses. Notifications by July 15, 2026.
