ACTION! 2026

Prizes Exhibition Publication

ACTION! 2026 is one of Glasgow Gallery of Photography's themed open calls — the gallery runs a roughly monthly schedule of themed group exhibitions across the year. This one asks for action: "Photography has always been used to capture action, a protest, a celebration or sporting event. Moments like the bride catching the bouquet or the winner crossing the finishing line." The gallery names sport photography, documentary and street photography as genres it expects to see, and says the call is open to more besides so long as the work meets the theme.

Unlike most of the gallery's calls, ACTION! is a digital exhibition rather than a print hang. Selected work is displayed on screen in the Glasgow gallery from November 4 to 28, 2026 and on the gallery's YouTube channel. That format is reflected in the file spec and the price: images need only 1,200 pixels on the shortest side against the 3,000 the gallery's printed shows require, and the participation fee is £22 rather than the £50 charged for the January print exhibitions.

Selected work also appears in an online gallery on the gallery's website and is included in the exhibition catalogue. Participants receive a digital certificate of participation before the show opens, and the gallery posts photos and video of how the work is displayed to its social media. The benefit list on this page does not include the Photographer of the Month consideration that the gallery's two January print calls carry, though the gallery's general information for participants lists it among what the participation fee gets you.

The submission model is free to apply, pay-only-if-selected. Each photographer submits up to 8 JPEGs at 300ppi and minimum 1,200 pixels on the shortest side, named Firstname-Surname, sent in a single batch via Smash, WeTransfer or Dropbox to the gallery's submissions address rather than as email attachments. The deadline is September 18, 2026, with acceptance notification by September 23 and payment due October 7; only selected photographers are notified.
