Blank Wall Gallery — Street Photography

Prize Exhibition prize

Blank Wall Gallery is an Athens-based photography gallery that runs themed open calls on a recurring schedule. Street Photography is the gallery's call for candid, spontaneous work capturing city life — work made in public space without staging or interference.

Selected work is shown at Blank Wall Gallery in Athens, April 24 – May 6, 2026. The exhibition format is physical only.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is by email with up to 5 images. Application is free.

The 2026 deadline was March 26.
