SE Center for Photography — The Figure as Art

Prize Exhibition

Virtual juried exhibition from the Southeast Center for Photography exploring the human figure as art — the body as form, gesture and subject. Juror Howard Schatz, the acclaimed New York fine-art photographer, selects 25–35 images for an approximately month-long virtual gallery opening 30 September 2026, with social-media promotion across SEC4P's channels and an archived online slideshow and video walkthrough. 100% of any sales go to the artist; there is no commission. Open to all photographers worldwide of any skill level; work previously exhibited at SE Center within the prior two years is ineligible.
