Kota Sepi Kota Sunyi

Tiga Mata, the Malaysian photography zine published by Zontiga, invites photographers across Southeast Asia to photograph quietness, solitude, silence and emptiness in the urban environment. The call extends the thinking behind Ahmad Zakii Anwar's Kota Sepi and Kota Sunyi — two bodies of work about Johor Bahru, the city of his birth, shown in the exhibition Sementara at GDP Campus — outward to Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Jakarta, Manila, Singapore, Hanoi, Phnom Penh and anywhere else in the region. In Malay kota means city, while sepi leans towards solitude, loneliness or a place deserted and sunyi towards silence and stillness; the organisers deliberately refuse to fix either word to a single English translation. They are explicit that silence need not mean the absence of people — solitude can exist among thousands — and that they want work made from spending time with a place rather than illustrating a word. Up to five photographs with a project statement, an account of process and intent, and disclosure of image sources where applicable; JPEG, sRGB, 3000 pixels on the longest side. Selected photographs are curated by Zontiga into a physical exhibition at Level 1, Testing Ground, GDP Campus, running 19 December 2026 to 1 February 2027 during the final period of Sementara, with printing and production costs covered by the organisers and no further fee for selected photographers. Open to amateurs and professionals who are nationals of Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste or Vietnam; applicants under 16 need a guardian's written consent. Copyright stays with the photographer, who grants a non-exclusive licence for exhibition and promotion of the project. Results are announced 19 November 2026.
