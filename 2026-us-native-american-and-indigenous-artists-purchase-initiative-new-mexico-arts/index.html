2026 US Native American and Indigenous Artists Purchase Initiative

Prize $5,000–$50,000 purchase

New Mexico Arts' Art in Public Places programme buys artwork outright for publicly accessible buildings across the state - community centres, courthouses, libraries, healthcare facilities, chapter houses, tribal administrative offices, rest areas and senior centres among them. Of the programme's purchase initiatives this is the one open nationally, rather than to New Mexico residents only or to the six-state Southwest: the goal is to diversify the state collection by buying work from artists at every career stage, and although anyone resident in the United States may apply, special consideration goes to artists who identify as members of a Native American tribe or community. Galleries may submit on behalf of a represented artist regardless of that artist's residency, so long as the artist so identifies. All visual arts media in all genres are considered, and the programme's own artist FAQ names photography explicitly alongside drawing, painting, sculpture, textile, glass, collage and the rest; work must be durable, permanent, low-maintenance and ADA-compliant once installed, and suitable for interior or exterior display. Artists submit an artist statement and up to three images of completed work currently available for purchase, priced between $5,000 and $50,000. That price is all-inclusive - it must cover materials, labour, museum-quality framing under Plexiglas, security hangers, delivery, installation, insurance, taxes, commissions, the project plaque and written and photographic documentation - so budget accordingly. There is no entry fee. Since 1986 AIPP has placed more than 3,000 works across all 33 New Mexico counties, over $21 million paid directly to artists. Finalists are notified in November 2026; purchases are then made on a rolling basis through November 2028, and inclusion in the finalist pool does not guarantee a sale.
