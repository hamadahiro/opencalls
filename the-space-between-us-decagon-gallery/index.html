The Space Between Us

Prize Exhibition prize

Free online photography exhibition exploring the emotional residue of human connection — the invisible architecture that feelings leave behind through atmosphere, absence, intimacy, silence, longing, routine, emotional distance, and memory. Curated and juried by the Decagon Gallery team. Selected work appears in a curated online exhibition with catalog, a dedicated exhibition page, an Instagram feature, and individual curatorial reflections responding to each submission. Exhibition August 15 – October 15, 2026. Open to photographers worldwide; all styles and processes welcome.
