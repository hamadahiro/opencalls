FUTURES Talents 2027

Bienal Fotografia do Porto nominates five emerging photographic artists living in Portugal to the European FUTURES platform for 2027. Each receives a €500 artist fee, up to three mentorship sessions, Magnum-led workshops and a place at the FUTURES Annual Event in Vienna, with a €600 contribution towards travel, accommodation and meals. Free to enter; shortlisted applicants must be available for a January interview.
