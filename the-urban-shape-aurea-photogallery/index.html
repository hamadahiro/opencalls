The Urban Shape

Prizes Exhibition Catalog

Aurea Photogallery runs a cycle of themed online exhibitions, and The Urban Shape is the autumn 2026 one. The framing is better than the usual architecture brief: the gallery starts from the premise that a city's shape is not its monuments but its micro-details - textures, surfaces, and the way people actually move through built space. That gives a street photographer, an architectural photographer and someone working with reflections and weather all a legitimate way in.

The call names four directions rather than one subject. Geometric lines and architecture covers structural symmetry, sharp angles, repeating patterns and dramatic shadows. Human scale and interaction covers solitary silhouettes, figures in perspective and the rhythm of people navigating the city. Textures and urban details covers weathered surfaces, worn asphalt, raw materials, typography and street signs. Reflections and atmosphere covers glass facades, puddles, atmospheric lighting and the mood of public space.

The fee structure matters and it is worth reading carefully. Submission is free: up to three images, JPG, 2MB maximum, longest side 2500px. Nobody pays to be looked at. A €30 curation and exhibition fee applies only if the gallery's team selects your work and you choose to confirm participation. A live early-bird reduces that to €25 for anyone confirming before October 20, 2026 - so a selected photographer is looking at €25 to €30, and nothing at all if they are not selected or decline. The gallery reviews on a rolling basis and warns that spots may fill before the deadline, so entering early is worth something beyond the discount.

What comes back is online, and the gallery is unusually plain about that being the point. The exhibition runs 11 November to 12 December 2026 as a curated digital show, each image with its own description and a link to the photographer's portfolio. Alongside it: a free PDF catalogue and a print-on-demand edition, a personal artist page on a custom URL, a listing in Aurea's directory of contemporary photographers, a permanent place in the archive once the show ends, Google-indexed pages, an Instagram share and a custom promotional image. The stated goal is discoverability - "real online visibility for artists — beyond Instagram" - rather than sales.

Terms are light. Photographers retain all rights to their images. Aurea does not handle sales and takes no commission, but you may publish a price and contact details so that enquiries reach you directly. Selection is by "our team"; no juror is named, and no results date is published - the submission form promises a response within 72 hours. The deadline is printed as 04/11/2026, which in the gallery's day-first convention is 4 November 2026, a Wednesday.
