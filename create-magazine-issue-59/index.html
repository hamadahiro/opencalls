Create! Magazine Issue #59

Prize Publication

Open call for Issue No. 59 of Create! Magazine, a print and digital publication for contemporary art reaching a global curator, collector, and gallerist audience. Open theme — all subjects and media welcome, with photography alongside painting, sculpture, installation, and other forms. Juried by Indira Cesarine, multidisciplinary artist and founder of The Untitled Space gallery in Tribeca, New York. Selected artists receive a four-page professional spread in the print and digital editions, website and social media features, and a complimentary digital issue. Submit up to 10 works (cohesive bodies encouraged) with a 300-word bio and artist statement. Open to all artists worldwide, no formal education or gallery representation required.
