SUB(SCAPE) — Manifest Gallery Season 22

Themed group exhibition exploring what lies beneath the visible — structural, psychological, geological, social, or conceptual systems that sustain or destabilize the world above. Open to all media including photography. Submit up to 3 entries for $45, $5 per additional. Every exhibiting artist receives a $50 honorarium. One work selected for a $1,000 Grand Jury Award. Artists receive 70% on sales. Exhibition at Manifest Gallery in Cincinnati, July 10 – August 7, 2026. 22nd season of this nonprofit creative research gallery.
