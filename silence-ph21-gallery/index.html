Silence

Curated international group photography exhibition on silence — a state the gallery sets against sound, noise and loudness, and reads in two directions at once: loneliness, alienation and a disturbing unknown landscape on one side, known tranquillity and peace on the other. The call points to the photographic contrast between deep darkness and dazzling light, to what falls silent after natural or social catastrophe — empty cities, villages, abandoned public transport, empty workplaces as reminders of the transience and fragility of humankind — and to the quiet activities of contemplation and meditation that negate the nature of action itself. Silence is noted as present in still lifes, cityscapes, portraits and many other photographic genres, and the theme is broadly construed without any photographic genre restrictions. Curated by Zsolt Bátori (PhD in Philosophy, Rutgers; founder, director and curator of PH21 Gallery, a Photolucida Critical Mass juror since 2023 and invited to Photolucida's board in 2026) and Borbála Jász (art historian and philosopher of art, PH21 vice-director, a Critical Mass juror since 2025 and also invited to the board in 2026). One curator's choice and up to three honourable mentions are announced; the curator's choice receives three free entries to any upcoming PH21 themed group call, each covering up to 15 images. Entrants may submit 3, 5, 7, 10 or 15 images, individual works or a series of at most five photographs, by email after paying the fee. THE STANDARD FEE IS PRICED BY IMAGE COUNT AND RISES AFTER THE EARLY BIRD DEADLINE: until 2 November it is €25 for the first 3 images, €30 for 5, €35 for 7, €40 for 10 and €45 for 15; after 2 November every rung goes up €5, to €30 / €35 / €40 / €45 / €50, so the stored €25 floor is only available for three days of this call and only for three images. A reduced rate of €15 (early bird) or €20 covers up to five images for students, unwaged applicants and those from low-income economies — the gallery asks for no proof of eligibility and says it trusts the applicant's judgement. Early bird deadline November 2, final deadline November 9; all entrants are notified 10–15 days after the final deadline. The exhibition runs January 19 – February 2, 2027 at PH21's venue in Barcelona's Gothic Quarter. Selected photographers pay a print and installation fee, from €45 for prints up to 40cm on the longest side, or may ship their own exhibit-ready prints; work need not be for sale, and the gallery commission on any sale is 25%.
