Darkness

Prizes Publication Online exhibition

Collect Art, the Tbilisi-based independent art magazine, invites artists to explore darkness “in its many forms — from the psychological and emotional to the physical, symbolic, and poetic,” for an online exhibition and special magazine edition. The organiser's framing: “Darkness is more than the absence of light. It is a space of mystery, introspection, uncertainty, transformation, and imagination. It can conceal, protect, reveal, or invite us to see beyond what is immediately visible,” and it asks “what can be found in the dark — and what becomes visible only when we stop looking for light?” Work may interpret the theme “through personal experience, atmosphere, symbolism, contrast, or abstraction”. Photography is named in the eligible-media list alongside painting, drawing, collage, installation, illustration, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, assemblage, fibre art, paper art, printmaking, 3D digital art, glass art and metalwork; the call is open to artists worldwide aged 18 and over. Selection buys a month-long online exhibition plus “a 2-6 page spread in both print and digital magazines” with a biography, artist statement, interview, website link and 2–10 artworks, a complimentary digital issue promoted on the magazine's channels, and a link to order the printed version; the curatorial team also picks “a few exceptional artists” for its book edition, “101 Contemporary Artists and More…”. THE COST, published in full: there is NO application fee, and a participation fee of €55 is payable only if your work is accepted. Submissions go by email and want up to 12 images with each work's title, medium, size and year, a short biography, an artist statement of at least 100 words, and website and social links. No notification date is published. Five sibling calls are open alongside this one at three prices — Winter Issue (22 November, €35), Reflection (23 November, €25), Wilderness (27 November, €55), Solitude and State of the Art (both 28 November, €55 and €25).
