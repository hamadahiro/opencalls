Community Gallery Wall Takeover

Prize Exhibition

Fotografiska Tallinn invites local and international photographic artists, image-makers, amateur photographers and photography enthusiasts to take over the walls of its Community Gallery on the museum's 1st floor — a space where art, everyday life and lively encounters intersect. The museum is looking for photo series and projects that speak to our time: work that raises timely or socially relevant questions, offers new perspectives and sparks dialogue, and that can enter into conversation with Fotografiska's wider exhibition programme. This first application round closes on 19 August 2026, World Photography Day, and selects work for the autumn 2026 season, when the museum will also present its major Vivian Maier exhibition. After that, submissions remain open year-round, with the next available exhibition slot opening in early 2027. Selected artists present their work in one of Fotografiska Tallinn's most visible and accessible exhibition spaces, supported by the museum's communications, marketing and public programme activities. Both new projects and previously exhibited works are welcome. Production of the works remains the responsibility of the artist, though the museum can recommend trusted production partners. Applications are sent as a single PDF containing 10–15 photographs, a short project description and a short artist bio.

DeadlineAugust 19, 2026
Entry feeFree (production costs if selected)
LocationTallinn, Estonia
Requirements10–15 images
