COSMOS

Photopolis Festival, the international photography and film festival held each year in Agrinio in western Greece since 2019, is assembling a group photographic exhibition called COSMOS for April 2027 as part of its pre-festival programme. The theme is deliberately wide: the festival invites photographers to look at the world around them and beyond it, naming the universe, nature, the human being, matter, relationships, harmony and chaos, and the visible and invisible connections that make up our existence as possible starting points for different photographic readings. Two photographers assess the proposals - Eleni Mouzakiti and Lila Zotou. Submitting is free. Photographers send one to three high-resolution photographs, of which at most one may be selected, and those whose work is chosen then pay 50 euros as a contribution to the costs of mounting the show, which the festival says covers the printing of the photographs, the curation and the organisational expenses. Files must be printable quality - the long edge at least 4000 pixels at 300 dpi - named with the photographer's name in Latin characters followed by 1, 2 or 3, and sent through WeTransfer or another cloud service rather than attached directly, together with a Word file giving name, city, telephone, email and Instagram handle if there is one. The festival sends a confirmation within 48 hours and asks anyone who does not receive it to get in touch. Photographs must not be created with AI technology. Entrants grant the organisers the right to publish and exhibit the work and to post it on social media to promote the festival, always with the photographer's name credited and with no fee due for that use; copyright stays with the photographer, and the festival states that the prints will be destroyed after the exhibition closes. Selected photographers are notified by email. The call closes on 31 December 2026.
