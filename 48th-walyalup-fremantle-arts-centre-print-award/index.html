48th Walyalup Fremantle Arts Centre Print Award

Prizes A$20,000 acquisitive A$5,000 First Nations Print Award A$5,000 Young Emerging Print Prize Exhibition

The Walyalup Fremantle Arts Centre Print Award returns for its 48th edition, inviting artists living and working in Australia to enter one work made by any printing process. The brief is print “in all its forms”, the governing clause in the terms is “enter one artwork only, using any printing process”, and the arts centre names “cross-disciplinary artists” among those it wants — and the arts centre’s own call-for-entries release enumerates processes it says entries “may include, but are not limited to”: “etching, lino and woodcut, screen and digital printing, textile-based processes, artist books, 3D and other innovative printmaking practices”. Photography is named on none of the five published surfaces and excluded on none of them either, so a photographic or alternative-process print — a pigment or digital print, photogravure, cyanotype, screenprint — enters under “digital printing” and under the governing clause; the centre of gravity is printmaking, and the award describes itself that way. One artwork per artist, made no earlier than 1 January 2025, with works in multiple parts counted as a single work. Entries close at 5pm AWST on 11 January 2027 — the terms and the FAQ both state that time, the call page gives only the date. A judging panel of three industry professionals, not yet named, selects the exhibition; entrants are notified by 3 March 2027, selected work is delivered by 22 March, winners are announced at the opening on 29 April 2027 and the show runs at the arts centre from 1 May to 1 August 2027. The award is acquisitive — the A$20,000 main prize is supported by the City of Fremantle — and every exhibited work must be available for sale, with a 35% commission plus GST on anything sold, so set the sale price on the entry form carefully. Artists pay the freight to Fremantle; WFAC pays the return freight and insures selected work in its keeping until 9 August 2027. Entry is A$50 including GST and non-refundable, and the form takes up to three JPEG images of the single work (longest side 800px minimum, 5 MB maximum) plus a 100-word biography, a 100-word artist statement and 150 words on the work and its installation. The age bar is 16 or older, but the reference date differs by surface: the terms and the FAQ both say “as at 1 January 2025” while the entry form’s date-of-birth note says “must be 16 years or older as at 1 June 2025” — ask the arts centre if you were born between those two dates. One further exclusion sits only in the FAQ: casual City of Fremantle staff may enter, but permanent and permanent part-time employees of the City may not.
