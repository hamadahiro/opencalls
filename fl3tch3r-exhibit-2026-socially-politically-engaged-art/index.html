FL3TCH3R Exhibit 2026 — Socially & Politically Engaged Art

Thirteenth annual international juried exhibition of socially and politically engaged art, founded in memory of designer Fletcher H. Dyer and newly at home at Fischman Gallery in downtown Johnson City, Tennessee. All 2D, 3D, video, sound, and performance documentation is eligible, with photography as its own category; open worldwide to artists 18+. Juried by Jessica “JB” Burke of UNC Charlotte, with Best of Show, two Awards of Excellence, and a slate of named awards presented at the September 4 reception. On view August 7 – September 27, 2026; late entries accepted June 24–30 at a $45 fee.
