Seeing Red

Prizes $1,500 / $1,000 / $500 Exhibition

A juried winter exhibition built on the colour red at Epperson Gallery in Crockett, California, juried by Donna Seager, who ran Donna Seager Gallery in San Rafael from 2005, co-founded Seager Gray Gallery in Mill Valley in 2011, and now runs Donna Seager Fine Arts & Artists' Books. The call reads red as humanity's oldest and most primordial pigment and asks for work that subverts, celebrates and explores its history — prehistoric ochre and Renaissance vermilion, the scarlet of Roman emperors and Catholic cardinals, the banners of political uprising, and the pure red of Eastern bridal wear and protective talismans. Photography is named outright in the accepted mediums, alongside sculpture, painting, mixed media, collage, drawing and fiber arts, and photographs must be signed and numbered on the front. Read the medium rules together, though: the same prospectus bars digitally rendered, digitally altered or digitally printed work, along with giclées, offset lithographs and reproductions of any kind, so this is a call for hand-made prints rather than inkjet output. Awards are $1,500, $1,000 and $500. Entry is open to artists 18 and over residing in the USA; work must be one of a kind, for sale, not previously shown at Epperson, no wider than 60 inches or heavier than 60 pounds, and ready to hang with D-rings and wire — sawtooth hangers are refused and three-dimensional work must be hand-delivered. The gallery retains a 50% commission on all sales and asks artists to price their submissions to reflect that split. AI-generated, assisted or enhanced work is not accepted. Applicants hear back October 12, shipped work must arrive by November 6, and the show runs November 14 to December 27, 2026 with a reception on opening day. Epperson held its 30th anniversary party in April 2026; its own exhibitions page now lists the show for November 14 to December 27, 2026, while the call terms and entry run through CaFÉ.
