Snow Much to See 2026

Prizes Exhibition at Images & Neige, Cluses (7 winners, production covered) 22 category prizes

Snow Much to See is an international photography contest devoted to snow, ice and cold, now in its second edition. It is run by the photographer Giandra De Castro and is part of the programme of Images & Neige, a festival founded in Cluses, in the Haute-Savoie Alps, by the photographer Frédéric Boiteux, which describes itself as the only photography festival in Europe entirely devoted to that theme. The festival's 17th edition runs from 22 to 24 January 2027. Every photograph must have an identifiable link with snow, ice or cold. Entries compete in 11 categories, each open to single images and to series of 3 to 20 photographs: snowy or icy landscapes; scenes of winter life; urban environments shaped by cold or snow; winter sports and leisure; wildlife and plants in cold environments; the human, social or environmental consequences of cold; poetry and reverie; minimalism, graphics and abstraction; macro and close-up; smartphone; and drone. The last three are defined by technique and accept any subject. An entrant may submit up to 60 single images and several series, and the same photograph can be entered both ways, in which case it is judged and charged twice. Seven winners are exhibited at the festival, with production paid for by the organisers. Four series winners each have three photographs chosen by the jury, printed in a 40 × 60 format with pigment inks on Hahnemühle paper and framed in wooden box frames. Three single-image winners, a first, second and third prize, are printed in 30 × 45. The prints stay with the organisers. Each category also awards one single-image prize and one series prize. No prize carries cash. Exhibition winners and category winners appear in the exhibition catalogue, each photograph with the jury's written analysis, and each receives the catalogue as a PDF. The best-ranked 10% of entries receive an honourable mention and a badge, even without a prize. Free photographs exist in only two categories, Sport and Urban. In each, the first single image and the first two photographs of a series kept in that category are free, so two single images can be entered at no cost. A series needs at least three photographs, so a series always costs something. Paid photographs from both formats are added together and charged once: €5 for up to 3, €10 for up to 10, €15 for up to 20, and €2 for each one beyond 20. From €5, the entrant receives a PDF of the catalogue with every photograph commented. From €25, which is 25 paid photographs — the organiser advertises this tier as "31 photos", counting the 6 free ones — the entry also gets a one-page written review, sent on 11 January 2027. Fees are not refunded unless the contest is cancelled. Files are JPEGs of at least 2,000 pixels on the long side, with no watermark, signature or frame, and judging is anonymous. Usual editing is allowed, including panoramas, exposure blending and focus stacking from the entrant's own shots. Photomontages and images generated or altered by artificial intelligence are not accepted, and the original file may be requested. The jury is Giandra De Castro (president), Frédéric Boiteux, Benoit Roux, vice-president of the Chamonix Photo Festival, and three people from the City of Cluses, which hosts the exhibition: Françoise Guillen, Priyam Nursimhulu and Manon Canale. Entrants keep their copyright. For selected photographs only, the organisers and the City of Cluses receive a free, non-exclusive five-year permission covering the results, the exhibition, the catalogue and promotion. The contest is open to amateur, semi-professional and professional photographers of any nationality aged 18 or over. Entries close on 30 November 2026 at 23:59 Paris time, and results are announced on 21 December 2026.
