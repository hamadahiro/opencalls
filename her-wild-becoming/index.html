Her Wild Becoming

Virtual exhibition and print catalog celebrating the evolving voices of contemporary women artists worldwide, juried by a five-person panel: Danielle Krysa (The Jealous Curator), Ekaterina Popova (Create! Magazine), Alicia Puig (PxP Contemporary), Marina Granger (The Artist Advisory) and Liza Zhurkovskaya (Kefi Art Gallery). The theme reads becoming as an untamed process — identity, intuition, resilience, transformation, and the many ways women define themselves through creative expression — honouring the courage to trust one's inner voice and keep evolving. Open to women artists of all backgrounds, disciplines and experience levels, 18 and over. All 2D and 3D media are accepted, photography named explicitly among them; film and video are not. Selected work appears in the online exhibition on visionaryartcollective.com and in the print catalog, and a small number of artists receive extra prizes from the jurors (a Substack subscription, Create! Collective membership, a Create! Magazine blog feature and two Instagram spotlights). Tiered fees: $25 for 1–2 images, $30 for 3–4, $35 for 5–6. Artists are notified within four to six weeks of the deadline and the exhibition is presented in October 2026.
