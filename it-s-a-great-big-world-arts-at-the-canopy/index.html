It's a GREAT Big World

Arts at The Canopy, a gallery and arts campus in downtown Ocala, asks artists what “the world” means to them — a city skyline, a dirt road, a kitchen table, something abstract. Photography is among the accepted media for this national online show, juried by Ronda Richley, with $200, $100 and $75 awards. AI-generated work is not eligible.
