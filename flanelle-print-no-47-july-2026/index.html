Flanelle Print No. 47 — July 2026

Prize Publication prize

Flanelle Magazine is a Montreal-based fashion photography, arts, and culture publication, founded in 2012. The magazine publishes quarterly in January, April, July, and October, with each issue produced in both print and digital formats and distributed internationally.

Print No. 47 publishes July 2026. Submissions are accepted from artists, photographers, and designers worldwide. Each photo set must include a minimum of 10 photographs following the issue theme and mood board. Editorials must be unpublished and exclusive to Flanelle.

Standard print submissions are free. Optional paid upgrades are available for cover contests, advertorial placement, or priority publishing. The magazine uses a two-phase review process, with selected artists notified individually after editorial review.

Selected work appears in the quarterly print issue and digital edition with international distribution, plus possible social media exposure across the magazine's channels. Selected artists receive digital versions of their published spreads on request. Submissions go through Kavyar (kavyar.com/flanellemag); the Print No. 47 deadline is June 1, 2026.
