SE Center for Photography — The Abandoned Landscape

Prize Exhibition

Virtual juried exhibition from the Southeast Center for Photography on the abandoned landscape — rural or urban, desert or jungle, ancient to recent — and the visible effects of time's passage, decay and deterioration across the places people leave behind. Colour and black-and-white work accepted in analog, digital or antique processes. 35–40 selected images are featured in an approximately month-long virtual gallery opening 15 September 2026, with social-media promotion and an archived video walkthrough. 100% of any sales go to the artist; no commission. Open to all photographers worldwide regardless of location, age or skill level.
