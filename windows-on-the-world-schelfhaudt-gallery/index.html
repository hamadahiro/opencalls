Windows on the World

The Schelfhaudt Gallery at the University of Bridgeport wants photographs of people outside the United States in their everyday environment, not travel highlights but the struggles, joys and celebrations of ordinary life. Up to 20 images may be entered for the January to March 2027 show; framed work is preferred, artists pay shipping both ways, and the gallery takes 50% of any sale.
