Reflections

A themed November group show at The 203 Art Space, a contemporary gallery and creative hub on the fifth floor of the Stamford Town Center mall in Connecticut, which mounts a new exhibition every month and runs workshops and community events alongside them. For REFLECTIONS the gallery asks what it means "to reflect, remember, observe, experience, and understand", and invites work that reads reflection in any of its senses — a memory, an emotion, a moment of realisation, a cultural experience, a personal journey, or an artist's reading of the world. The themes it lists are life, nature, identity, memory, society, relationships, transformation and personal experience, approached "through personal experience, observation, imagination, or interpretation". All mediums are welcome, and photography is one of the five media the entry form names, alongside mixed media, painting, sculpture and digital art. This is a physical, hand-delivered show: selections are announced the day after the deadline, work is dropped off at the gallery on 31 October and 1, 3 and 4 November, and the exhibition runs 6 November to 5 December 2026.
