Siena International Photo Awards 2027

Prizes €1,500 gear Pangea trophy Exhibition

The Siena Awards' flagship contest, open to professional and amateur photographers worldwide, returns for 2027 with entries open since 11 October 2026 and a deadline of 7 January 2027. Ten categories are published: Journeys & Adventures; Fascinating Faces and Characters; The Beauty of Nature; Animals in Their Environment; Sports in Action; Story-Telling; Documentary & Photojournalism; Street Photography; Underwater Life; and Short Documentary Film. There is no limit on how many entries a photographer may make, published and previously awarded work is accepted, and there is no restriction on when a photograph was taken. The first image is free to enter. Beyond that the published ladder is €30 for a bundle of three images including the free one, €10 for each additional image, and €35 each for a Story-Telling entry or a video; entries can be spread freely across categories and fees are non-refundable. As of 23 October 2026 the rules page still prints last cycle's early-bird window ("by December 1st, 2025" for a €20 three-image bundle, uploads by 9 January 2026), so treat any early-bird discount as unconfirmed for this edition and check before paying. Prizes are weighted towards recognition rather than cash. The Photographer of the Year receives €1,500 worth of photographic equipment, the Pangea Prize crystal trophy and a fixed €400 towards two nights' accommodation in Siena for the ceremony weekend; Best Author, category winners and second and third places in each category receive the trophy and the same €400 accommodation contribution. Highly Commended entries receive a Crystal of Honor and Commended entries a parchment. The trophies and parchments are handed over only to winners who attend the ceremony in Siena, held each October at the Teatro dei Rinnovati, and winning images go into the "Imagine all the People Sharing all the World" group exhibition that runs for over a month during the Siena Awards Festival. Photographers keep copyright; the organisers take a free licence to use entered images for promotion of the award and festival — catalogues, posters, websites and official merchandise — always with credit, and no entry is sold or used commercially by sponsors without the photographer's approval. Post-processing is limited to what could be achieved in a darkroom: cropping and brightness, contrast and colour adjustments are allowed, but key compositional elements may not be added or removed, and finalists must supply RAW files. AI-generated photographs are not accepted — the rules require images captured and edited by humans without AI algorithms. Entries are JPG, sRGB or Adobe98, 8-bit, no more than 1920 px on the longest side, with no watermarks or borders; finalists must be able to supply files of at least 2400 x 3600 px at 300 dpi. The jury page for this edition redirects to previous editions, so the 2027 panel is not yet published.
