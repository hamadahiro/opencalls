Outdoor Photography Magazine — Woodland Macro

Submit up to 5 images on the theme of woodland macro — close-up work made among trees, from fungi and bark to leaf detail and forest-floor life — for the 'One Thing This Month' reader competition (OP 334) in Outdoor Photography Magazine (UK). The winner receives a Lowepro Fastpack BP 450 AW IV backpack (RRP £139). The challenge is pegged to the main technique feature in that issue of the magazine, and entrants are asked to type the competition theme into the corresponding field on the submission form. Entries need captions of up to 75 words covering where, why and how each image was taken, plus full technical details in the magazine's stated order (camera and lens, focal length, ISO, shutter speed, aperture, tripod or handheld). Free to enter, uploaded in bulk through the magazine's Google Form, which requires a Google account because uploads run through Google Drive. The magazine publishes work taken outside in the landscape, wildlife, nature, adventure and travel genres — not fashion, wedding, field sports or interiors — and asks that images be made ethically and with sensitivity to environmental and conservation issues. Image manipulation is allowed but the magazine favours a gentle, sophisticated approach to editing. Entry is restricted to readers aged 18 or over, with one entry per person per competition, and the publisher's competition terms state that winners are notified by email within 30 days of the closing date and have 14 days to claim the prize.
