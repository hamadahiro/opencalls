Foundwork Artist Prize 2026

An annual juried award recognising outstanding emerging and mid-career artists working in any media. The honoree receives an unrestricted $10,000 grant, a studio visit with each of the five jurors, and an interview for Foundwork's Dialogues programme; three further artists are named to a Short List and each receives a $1,000 micro-grant. The 2026 jury is Stefan Benchoam (artist and co-founder of Proyectos Ultravioleta and NuMu, Guatemala City), François Ghebaly (founder of the Los Angeles and New York gallery), Manuela Moscoso (director of the Center for Art, Research and Alliances and curator of Ecuador's 2026 Venice pavilion), Gabi Ngcobo (director of Kunstinstituut Melly, Rotterdam, and curator of the 10th Berlin Biennale) and Esther Schipper (founder of the Berlin gallery). Photography is well represented: the 2025 honoree Antonia Kuo works with photochemical painting on silver gelatin paper, and photographer Clifford Prince King was on the 2023 Short List. There is no separate entry fee, but applicants must hold an active Foundwork artist membership — $6 a month, with a 30-day free trial for new members — and keep a published profile carrying at least six artworks and an artist statement throughout the selection period, which runs from 5pm PT on 28 September 2026 to 5pm PT on 31 December 2026. Staff shortlist fifty finalists for the jury, who pick one honoree and three Short List artists. Open worldwide to artists 18 and over, except residents of Argentina, Arizona, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Cuba, Iran, Kosovo, Latvia, Macedonia, North Korea, Quebec, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Serbia, Sudan, Syria and the UAE. Past honorees cannot win again, and past Short List artists must wait three years.
