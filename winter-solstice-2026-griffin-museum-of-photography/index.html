Winter Solstice 2026 — Annual Members Exhibition

Prizes Exhibition Print sales

This is the Griffin's end-of-year salon rather than a juried call: no juror is named and the museum's stated ambition is to fill the Main Gallery again, as it did last year with over 300 prints. What comes back is a wall at a real museum of photography and a shot at a collector, not a prize.

The constraints are unusually specific and they are the thing to read twice: ONE print, UNFRAMED, no larger than 13 inches on the longest side, ready to hang with clips or pins, and priced at $250 or less. A framed or oversized print is not a near miss here, it is outside the brief.

The timeline is staged and registration is the gate: you register in October, ship from 1 November, and the print has to be at 67 Shore Road by 1 December. Membership is the real cost of entry here and it is not nominal: $70 a year for an Individual, $50 if you live outside New England, $35 if you are a student or educator. There is no submission fee on top of it.
