Anderson Center Artist Residency 2027

Prize 2–4 week residency

The Anderson Center at Tower View, founded in 1995 on a historic estate in rural Red Wing, Minnesota, offers residencies of two to four weeks' duration from May through October each year. It hosts around five residents at a time and 35–40 a year, and since its founding more than 900 residents have come from 46 states and over 40 countries. Work made there has gone on to receive Guggenheim Fellowships and NEA funding.

For photographers the practical detail worth knowing is the facilities. Visual artists are provided a 15' x 26' studio, and the shared workspaces on site include a darkroom, a printmaking studio with a Charles Brand-like etching press, and an open-air forge — a rarer combination than most residencies of this scale offer.

There is no charge for the residency and all food is provided. Each resident gets a room in the historic Tower View residence with a desk, a large closet and either a private or shared bathroom, with linens and weekly housekeeping. A residency chef cooks dinner for the cohort at 6pm on weeknights using the commercial kitchen on site, and all groceries are supplied. Outside the Early Career Artist, Deaf Artists and Public Artist programs no additional funding is available, and residents arrange their own transport to the center, to Minneapolis–St. Paul Airport or to the train station in Red Wing.

The Anderson Center uses the word "artist" broadly, meaning those working in visual, literary and performing arts including those whose work doesn't fit neatly into categories and genres, and states that the program supports artists from around the world across a wide range of cultures, races, sexual orientations and genders. The cohort model is central — the organisation's stated belief is that exchange across disciplines is generative.

Applications for the 2027 season are open now, with a $30 application fee. There is only one deadline: 12 p.m. noon Central Standard Time on 12 January 2027. Jury review takes place in late January and February, and the selected artists, wait-list and runners-up are notified by 2 March 2027.
