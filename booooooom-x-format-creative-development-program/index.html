Booooooom x Format Creative Development Program

Prizes Mentorship Format subscription

A free mentorship and portfolio development program from art platform Booooooom and portfolio-builder Format, aimed at visual artists, illustrators and photographers who are ready to grow rather than those with the most polished work. The grand prize recipient receives a lifetime Format subscription, personal mentorship with Booooooom editor-in-chief Jeff Hamada, and featured coverage on both platforms; two runners-up each receive a one-year Format plan and a portfolio review with Format's Stanzie Tooth. To apply, sign up for a free 60-day Format trial (no credit card required), build a portfolio of original work, and submit two short written statements — one describing the work, one on where you are in your practice and what support you're looking for. Open worldwide to artists 18+. Applications close September 2, 2026, 11:59pm PT.
