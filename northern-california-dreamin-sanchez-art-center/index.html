Northern California Dreamin'

Bay Area and Santa Cruz artists 18 and over submit up to three works on the hopes and promises that have drawn people to the region, from past decades to a vision of the future. Sanchez Art Center's East Gallery runs the show 19 February to 21 March 2027 and can take 2D or 3D work as well as some short video. A short artist statement for each work is part of the jurying.

DeadlineJanuary 12, 2027
PrizeExhibition
Entry fee$15 per work
Eligibility18+, Bay Area only
LocationPacifica, CA
RequirementsUp to 3 works
Submit viaWebsite
ResultsBy January 20, 2027

Jury: Aimee Shapiro, Executive Director, San Mateo County Office of Arts and Culture

Organized by Sanchez Art Center
