Monochrome Photography Awards 2026

Prizes $2,000 (Pro) $1,000 (Amateur) Annual book publication

Running since 2014, the Monochrome Photography Awards is an international black-and-white competition split into Professional and Amateur sections across thirteen categories: Abstract, Architecture, Conceptual, Fashion/Beauty, Fine Art, Landscapes, Nature, Nude, Photojournalism, Photomanipulation, Portrait, Street and Wildlife. Only black-and-white photographs are accepted, though toned images such as sepia and selenium qualify, and work may be captured on film or digitally. There is no cap on how many individual images an artist may submit, no restriction on when a picture was taken, and images that have won or are currently entered elsewhere remain eligible. The overall winner of the professional section takes $2,000 and the title Monochrome Photographer of the Year 2026; the amateur winner takes $1,000 and the title Monochrome Discovery of the Year 2026. First, second and third place in every category, along with further entries the judges commend, are published in the Monochrome Photography Awards Annual Book, and honourable mentions have their names printed in it. Category winners receive the title Category Photographer of the Year or Category Discovery of the Year, a place in the online Winners' Gallery, and a winners' logo and certificate. Judging is by a panel of seventeen photographers from Japan, Norway, Slovakia, Iran, Germany, India, Spain and elsewhere. Cash prizes are paid in USD by PayPal, copyright stays with the photographer at all times, and the organisers take only a non-exclusive licence to promote the awards. Entrants must be over 18. Files are submitted at 1200 pixels on the longest side, sRGB or Adobe98, without watermarks or borders; winners supply a 3000-pixel-wide file for the book. Results are announced on 3 January 2027.
