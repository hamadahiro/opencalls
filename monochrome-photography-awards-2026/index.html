Monochrome Photography Awards 2026

Prize $2,000

Annual international competition dedicated entirely to black and white photography, running since 2014. Entries are split into Professional and Amateur sections — the organisers define professionals as photographers who earn at least half their income from photography — across thirteen categories: Abstract, Architecture, Conceptual, Fashion/Beauty, Fine Art, Landscapes, Nature, Nude, Photojournalism, Photomanipulation, Portrait, Street and Wildlife. Toned images such as sepia and selenium are accepted, work may be captured on film or digitally, and there is no limit on how many images an artist may submit or on when a photograph was taken; previously published or awarded images are eligible. The overall Professional winner takes $2,000 and the title Monochrome Photographer of the Year 2026; the overall Amateur winner takes $1,000 and the title Monochrome Discovery of the Year 2026. First, second and third place winners in every category, plus further entries the judges commend, are published in the printed Monochrome Photography Awards Annual Book, with Honorable Mention winners named in the book as well; all winners are showcased in the online Winners' Gallery and receive a certificate. Judging is by a panel of seventeen photographers from around the world, on artistic merit, originality, subject and style. Copyright remains with the photographer at all times. Results are announced by 3 January 2027.
