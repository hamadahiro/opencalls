The Female Show 2026

Group exhibition of national and international female artists at Munich Art Gallery in the Kunstareal, running October 15 – November 14, 2026, including Lange Nacht der Münchner Museen. Curated by Josephine Kaiser & Team. Seeks contemporary art by women beyond gender stereotypes and thematic clichés. Smaller formats preferred (up to 50×50 cm). Submit a PDF (max 5 pages, 5 MB) with short CV, artist statement, ~5 example images, Instagram and website to art@thefemaleshow.de. No participation or exhibition fees — only international shipping at artist's expense. Applications accepted in English or German.
