Louvre Unbound Magazine — Volumes 8 & 9 Open Call

Prize Publication

International contemporary art magazine seeking visual artists worldwide for Volumes 8 and 9 — painting, photography, sculpture, digital art, mixed media and more. Submit up to 4 artworks via Google Form for free; participation fees apply only on acceptance, with discounted tiers for these volumes: $39 (full page), $69 (double page), $89 (solo portfolio spotlight) and $239 (exclusive interview, $275 full price). Selected artists are featured in the print-on-demand edition (physical copies shipped internationally) plus digital download, and promoted across the magazine's social media channels, with distribution to 83+ countries.
