Eurazeo Photo Prize 2026

Prize €20,000

Eurazeo, a leading European private asset manager, relaunches its long-running photography patronage as the Eurazeo Photo Prize — now a project-development grant anchored in the company's Endowment Fund. Open to professional photographers of all nationalities, based in France or abroad, it invites applicants to submit a project for development. Each year one laureate receives a €20,000 grant to deliver an ambitious long-term body of work, plus dedicated artistic and strategic guidance and connections with galleries, festivals, media and leading industry professionals. Building on the Eurazeo Photo Prize that ran annually from 2010 to 2025, the new grant supports creation earlier in the process. Marking the bicentenary of photography, applications are open 2 June – 13 September 2026; free to enter.
