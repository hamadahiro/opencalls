Winter Member Show 2027

Maryland Federation of Art's winter members' exhibition at Circle Gallery in Annapolis, running 6-30 January 2027. Any original 2D or 3D work will be considered and every entry is blind juried, this year by Summer Brown, Master Instructor of Fine Art at Howard University. Cash awards total $700 - $500 designated by the juror, a $100 Conley Award restricted to 3D work chosen by Susi and Rob Conley, and a $100 Friends of the Arts award - so a photographer competes for $600 of the pool rather than all of it. Entry is open to MFA members residing in any state or territory of the US, Canada or Mexico; non-members join first (basic membership is $70 a year) and then enter at the member rate. Up to six original works made in the past three years may be entered, nothing larger than 72 inches in any direction, and work previously exhibited at Circle Gallery is not eligible; video is accepted as a link plus a still frame. Artists are notified 3 December 2026 at 5 PM, accepted and shipped work arrives 27-30 December, and there is a virtual awards ceremony on opening day and an in-person reception at Circle Gallery on the closing day, 30 January. Members retain 75% of sales. MFA's General Guidelines, which the terms of entry incorporate, regulate photographic prints directly and bar work created via image-generative AI. Founded in 1963, MFA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit supported by the Maryland State Arts Council.
