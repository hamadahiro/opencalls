InFocus Photo Exhibit & Awards 2027: Anything Goes

The 13th annual InFocus Photo Exhibit & Awards, an Edmonton-based showcase of contemporary photography curated with the stated intention of highlighting voices and perspectives that might otherwise go unseen, and treating photography as both an artistic pursuit and a tool for societal commentary. The 2027 edition is deliberately open-theme — "Anything Goes" — so any subject matter, any style and any genre of photography is eligible, and the call is open to photographers of all skill levels anywhere in the world. Submissions are reviewed by curator and co-founder Alexis Marie Chute, who runs the exhibit under Alexis Marie Productions Inc. Selected work is shown across three Alberta venues — Wild Skies Art Gallery, Red Brick Common Public Art Gallery and Stony Plain Town Hall — through February, March and April 2027, with an opening party in February. Four awards run alongside the exhibition: the organiser says selected photographers are “in the running for the People’s Choice Award and considered for the Emerging Photographer, Landscape, and Portrait Awards”, but what each of those carries in 2027 is not published — the amounts below are the 2026 edition’s, listed on the org’s own homepage under the heading “InFocus Photo Exhibit 2026 AWARDS” beside that year’s named winners. In 2026 the Emerging Photographer Award was a $200 McBain Camera gift card with a plaque and swag; Landscape was a metal print up to 20″×30″ with a plaque; Portrait was a fine art premium canvas up to 24″×36″, a plaque and the winning photo printed in Preview Art Magazine; People’s Choice was a $200 VISTEK gift card with a plaque and swag. None of the four is a cash payment — the two $200 figures are retailer gift cards. Photographers accepted into the exhibition book are paid an honorarium. Accepted photographers are notified within two weeks of the deadline. Fees are in Canadian dollars plus GST and run as a ladder: $25 for the first image, $10 for the second and $5 for each image after that, up to ten images per submission, with unlimited separate submissions allowed. Files are JPG only, maximum 3 MB, named lastname_imagetitle. Two costs to plan for beyond the entry fee: accepted photographers must have their work professionally framed, either themselves or through the exhibit's Pixportal partner, and sales are split 60% to the photographer, 40% to InFocus.
