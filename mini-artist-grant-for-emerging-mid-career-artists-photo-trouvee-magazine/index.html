Mini Artist Grant for Emerging & Mid-Career Artists

Prizes $350 Publication

The Mini Artist Grant is a small recurring grant from Photo Trouvée Magazine, the platform focused on found and family photographs as artistic source material. The grant is restricted by practice area: artists working with found photography and alternative processes.

The award is $350 plus publication on the Photo Trouvée platform. The size is intentional — a mini grant that can fund a specific small expense (a printing run, a roll of film, a small material cost) rather than a major project.

Eligibility is open to emerging and mid-career artists. Submission is through JotForm with up to 5 images. Fee is $10. The 2026 deadline was March 31.
