RED — Juried Exhibition

Red tape, red flag, seeing red, painting the town red — the Lawrence Arts Center wants to know what it means to represent the colour red, and leaves the reading entirely to the artist. The theme can be taken as intense emotion or as warning, or turned toward flowers, birds and hearts; the call explicitly encourages both established and emerging artists. Submissions are open to any medium within the size limit, which is 24 inches in any direction including frame, and selected work is not required to be for sale. The juror is Travis Millard, the artist, musician and founder of Fudge Factory Comics, with twenty years of self-published zines and gallery shows behind him and illustration work for Playboy, The Hollywood Reporter, Mad, Wired and record covers for Dinosaur Jr and Kurt Vile. Prizes are $1,000 for first, $500 for second and $250 for third. The Arts Center is a long-running nonprofit in downtown Lawrence, Kansas, with three exhibition galleries, a 300-seat theatre and seven visual arts studios. Entries go through the SlideRoom portal, which opened 1 August and closes 25 September; a maximum of three original works may be entered for $25, one JPEG per piece under 5MB. AI artwork is not permissible. Notifications go out between 5 and 9 October, work is delivered 10–21 October, the opening reception and awards are on 30 October, and the show closes 12 December 2026.
