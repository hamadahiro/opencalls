2027 AIM Fellowship

Prize Fellowship

The 47th cycle of the AIM Fellowship, The Bronx Museum's career accelerator for emerging artists based in New York City, founded in 1980 as Artist in the Marketplace. Fourteen artists are selected each year through open competition for a nine-month practicum of expert-led seminars at the museum: the art market, the artist statement, community building, communication strategies, the curator/artist relationship, financial literacy, funding opportunities and art law in the first half; studio visits, portfolio reviews, peer-led sessions, guest artist visits, career planning, exhibition preparation and time management, working with institutions big and small, audience development, community art practice, artist archives and documentation in the second. Fellows meet on Tuesday evenings, 6 to 9pm, at the museum from February to June 2027, break for July and August, and resume from September to December; the cohort then designs and co-facilitates a mandatory public AIM Convening in winter or spring 2028, and a group exhibition with a fully illustrated catalogue follows roughly every two years. There is no application fee and no cost to participate. AIM has helped more than 1,300 New York artists establish careers, among them Njideka Akunyili-Crosby, Glenn Ligon, Abigail DeVille, Byron Kim, Lucia Hierro, Jacolby Satterwhite and the photographer LaToya Ruby Frazier — the call names no accepted-media list at all, and that alumni roster is where the question of photography is answered. Eligibility is specific: at least 21; applying as an individual, not a partnership or collective; an emerging artist with two to ten years actively creating and presenting new original work in the visual arts who has yet to be substantially celebrated; a New York City resident at application and throughout the term; not enrolled in a degree-granting programme or another professional-development programme during the fellowship; not a Bronx Museum employee or a board member's relative; and not represented by a commercial gallery anywhere in or outside the US. Because stipends, honoraria and wage payments are involved, enrolment is limited to US citizens and permanent residents. Apply through Submittable with six works no more than three years old; semi-finalists must be available for an in-person group interview with the selection committee at the museum in autumn 2026. The museum is explicit that this is not a studio programme and that no studio space is provided.
