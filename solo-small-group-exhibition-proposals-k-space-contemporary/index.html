Solo & Small Group Exhibition Proposals

Prize Solo or group exhibition

K Space Contemporary is a 501(c)(3) non-profit in downtown Corpus Christi dedicated to presenting contemporary art in South Texas, and its exhibition committee reviews artist portfolios once a year to fill solo and small-group slots across four spaces — the Clower Gallery (29' x 22'), the Starr Gallery (50' x 30'), the Corner Spot (16' x 23') and the Project Space (11.5' x 14.5'). The annual application deadline is 1 February. All media are accepted, including site-specific and immersive installation, and applicants choose a gallery preference and an exhibition category of 2-dimensional, 3-dimensional, installation/immersive or mixed. The gallery is looking for original, innovative work not previously exhibited at K Space. There is no cost to apply, and submission is by digital portfolio only: an artist website or a link to a Google Drive or Dropbox folder set to "anyone with the link" so the committee can review it, plus a written exhibition proposal. The gallery asks for images of at least ten different works, with an artist statement, biography and CV. Applicants must be 18 or older; entry is open to Texas artists, with particular interest in those living in the Texas Coastal Bend and South Texas. One application per artist per year is allowed. Submissions are accepted year round but reviewed only once, in spring, so an application filed shortly after a review waits ten to twelve months for an answer. Selected exhibitions are scheduled six to eighteen months in advance and run four to seven weeks; artists are responsible for delivering or shipping their own work and for installation and de-installation during gallery hours. K Space handles publicity — a press release to local and state media, an exhibition page on its site, an email invitation to around 2,000 subscribers, social promotion and 1,000 to 2,000 printed postcard announcements — and retains 40 per cent commission on gallery sales. Work must be collected or shipped out within ten days of the close; anything left 60 days after the last day becomes gallery property.
