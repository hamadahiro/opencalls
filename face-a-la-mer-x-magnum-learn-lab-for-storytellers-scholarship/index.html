Face à la Mer x Magnum Learn Lab for Storytellers Scholarship

Magnum Photos and Face à la Mer are offering one fully funded place on the Magnum Learn Lab for Storytellers, Magnum's first long-term course: a ten-month international mentorship programme for twenty selected participants, built around immersive in-person residencies and sustained artistic and professional guidance, and supported by Magnum photographers Newsha Tavakolian, Antoine d'Agata and Rafał Milach alongside artist Tania Bohórquez and designer Ania Nalecka-Milach. The programme runs January to November 2027 in Italy, France and online. The scholarship goes to a photographer aged 18 or over living in North Africa - Magnum names Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Morocco, Tunisia, Mauritania and Sudan - and covers the full course fee of $17,450 plus visa, travel and accommodation; the recipient must be able to cover their own expenses during the third on-site workshop, in Paris in November 2027. Applying is free and Magnum states plainly that applications "do not include any fees". The successful candidate is chosen during the rencontres photo de Tanger, Face à la mer, in Tangier on November 25-29 2026, after a 30-minute interview with Magnum photographer Antoine d'Agata, artist Tania Bohórquez, a representative of Magnum Photos and a representative of Face à la Mer; the applicant is notified on November 30 2026. Magnum is looking for photographers interested in documentary photography and in working on projects, who can demonstrate a strong ability to tell stories through photography, and it encourages applications from those underrepresented in the industry, including women and female-identifying photographers, people from lower socio-economic backgrounds, LGBTQIA+ photographers and those from ethnic minority backgrounds; no minimum formal qualification is required. Applications need one PDF holding one or two photo stories totalling no more than 30 images, plus a statement of purpose in English. Face à la Mer, the Tangier Photography Encounters, is co-founded and directed by artistic director Mina Mostefa and photojournalist Wilfrid Estève and describes itself as a professional event for contemporary photography in the Mediterranean, building a cultural bridge between Africa and Europe.
