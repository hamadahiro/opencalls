55th Annual Juried Art Exhibition

Valley Art Center's annual juried exhibition, now in its 55th edition, shows in the Bowen Gallery in Chagrin Falls from November 6 to December 16, 2026. Artists living within a 250-mile radius of Chagrin Falls may enter up to three works in any medium, and Photography/Prints is one of the eight entry categories alongside textiles, glass and enamel, wood, metal and sculpture, jewelry, painting and drawing, ceramics, and mixed media. The centre usually receives around 400 works and juries down to about 45; this year only one piece per accepted artist will hang, to widen the range of artists and media on the walls. Over $3,500 in cash goes to Best of Show, first, second and third place and several community-sponsored awards. The call contradicts itself on sales: the prospectus says work does not have to be for sale, while the entry instructions require a sale price because all work entered must be for sale for this exhibit — ask the centre before entering. Valley Art Center takes a 40% commission on anything that sells.
