55th Annual Juried Art Exhibition

Prizes $3,500 In cash awards

Valley Art Center's annual juried exhibition, a fixture of the Northeast Ohio art calendar for 55 years, shows in the Bowen Gallery in Chagrin Falls from November 6 to December 16, 2026. Artists living within a 250-mile radius of Chagrin Falls may enter up to three works in any medium, and Photography/Prints is one of the eight entry categories alongside textiles, glass and enamel, wood, metal and sculpture, jewelry, painting and drawing, ceramics, and mixed media. The centre usually receives around 400 works and juries down to about 55; this year only one piece per accepted artist will hang, to widen the range of artists and media on the walls. Over $3,500 in cash goes to Best of Show, first, second and third place and several community-sponsored awards. Work does not have to be for sale, and Valley Art Center takes a 40% commission on anything that sells. Entries open on August 26, 2026.
