Beyond the Horizon

Prize Exhibition

GCAA ArtWorks, the storefront gallery and studio space of the Gulf Coast Artists' Alliance at 5546 1st Ave. N. in St. Petersburg, opens its new year with a themed juried show. "Beyond the Horizon" is put as "a metaphor for what lies past your immediate vision, knowledge, and comfort zone" — limitless potential, hope, ambition and growth — and the gallery asks artists to "interpret the theme as you like and depict your vision for the New Year". The application states that a loose interpretation of the theme will be accepted, and that all media are eligible, sculpture included. No surface names photography specifically; what does is the gallery's own online shop, which sells eleven photographs under a Photography/Digital Art heading at $145 to $240. Up to three works per artist are considered for a single non-refundable $20 jury fee. Applying takes two separate actions and the gallery says so twice: you click Submit Application on the form, then Pay Jury Fee Here, a Square checkout whose own item page states that payment does not guarantee inclusion in an exhibition. Wall work is capped at 30x40 inches, sculpture at roughly 48 inches tall by 18 wide by 18 deep. Entry images are uploaded through the form on the call page as JPEG or PNG files of about 1200x1000 pixels, named with your surname and the title of the work — the gallery says low-resolution files will be rejected — alongside the title, medium, size and retail price of each piece and an artist's statement of up to 300 words; a CV upload is optional. Work sent from out of town is accepted provided a return shipping label comes with it. The exhibition runs 7 January - 6 February 2027 with a reception on Saturday 9 January during the Second Saturday Art Walk in downtown St. Pete. Selected artists are emailed the rest of the show guidelines by 18 December 2026. Sales are handled by the gallery, which takes 35 per cent commission from GCAA members and 40 per cent from non-members. No cash award is attached to this call.
