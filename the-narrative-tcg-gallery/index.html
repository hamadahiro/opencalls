The Narrative

Prize Exhibition prize

The Narrative is an international juried call from TCG Gallery (also operating as Château Gallery), open to all forms of two-dimensional image-making — traditional photography alongside non-conventional media and alternative photographic processes. The brief uses narrative as a connective theme rather than a strict subject restriction.

Selected work is shown in an online exhibition running May 1 – June 1, 2026. AI-generated work is not accepted.

Eligibility is open globally to artists 18+. Submission is through the gallery's website with up to 3 images. Fee is $15.

The 2026 deadline was April 20; results announced April 27, 2026.
