Source Graduate Photography Online 2026 — BA

Annual graduate showcase from Source, Belfast-based quarterly photography magazine, running since 2007. Open to photographers graduating from a BA in 2026 in Ireland or the UK. Submit up to 8 images with an artist statement and website link. Selected work featured on the Graduate Photography Online platform and in a printed supplement; participants receive a 1-year digital subscription to Source (worth £26.99). MA/MFA submissions have a separate later deadline (August 7, 2026).
