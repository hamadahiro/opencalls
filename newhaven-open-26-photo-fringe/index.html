Newhaven Open 26

Prizes Exhibition Mentorship

A new open exhibition from Photo Fringe, the volunteer-led Brighton charity behind the biennial open-platform photography festival, staged on the top floor of BN9 Marine Workshops in Newhaven — a working-waterfront studio space run by Towner with Newhaven Enterprise Zone and Lewes District Council, with almost 200 square metres of hanging space. Ten solo or group exhibitions will be selected, running 2 to 31 October 2026 alongside the rest of the festival. Work should respond to the 2026 festival theme Feedback Loop, which asks artists to treat photography less as a static record than as part of a continuous exchange: conversations between communities and landscapes, stories circulating across generations, ecological cycles, industrial systems, acts of collaboration, the shifting relationship between artists and audiences. It should also connect meaningfully to Marine Workshops, Newhaven or its communities — the call is open to photographers and lens-based artists in any medium, including installation, moving image, sound and expanded photographic practice, and site-responsive proposals for new or existing work are both welcome. Collectives may apply. Apply free by email with up to 300 words on the proposal, up to 12 JPEGs or a screener link, and a website or Instagram link; BSL, English audio and video applications are accepted. One exhibiting artist receives a jury award of mentorship with Photo Fringe producer Ricardo Reverón Blanco plus an in-depth interview published on the festival site. Selected artists install and de-install their own work with team support, invigilate for at least a day, and pay the subsidised registration fee of £125 solo or £150 group plus a £100 contribution towards exhibition costs by 2 September; Photo Fringe takes 35% commission on sales to cover staffing. Entries close 11.59pm on Sunday 23 August 2026, with notifications on 28 August.
