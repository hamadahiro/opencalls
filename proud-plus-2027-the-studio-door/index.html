PROUD+ 2027

The Studio Door in San Diego holds its tenth PROUD+ exhibition of work by LGBTQ+ artists from 25 June to 6 August 2027, juried by Xiangqi Chen of OUT Museum. Three artists receive a $100 Juror's Award, and all selected work appears in the catalogue. All work must be for sale at 50% commission, and international artists can join the online exhibition only.
