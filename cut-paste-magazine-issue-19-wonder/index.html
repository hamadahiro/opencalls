Cut+Paste Magazine Issue 19 — Wonder

Prizes Publication Payment

Cut+Paste is an indie print magazine that pays its contributors, and Issue 19, the Spring 2027 edition, is themed Wonder — the small moments, details and discoveries that inspire it, and the childlike curiosity that can make familiar things feel new again. The editors want the delights of spring, especially the ones that would typically go overlooked, and for photographers specifically they mention rays of sunlight streaming through new leaves, shadow puppets outlined by golden-hour sun, bubbles from washing dishes, mushroom fairy rings and crystal-clear puddle reflections. The accepted photographic categories are portraits (faceless preferred), nature and landscape, abstract and experimental, still life and conceptual, urban and cityscape, and macro close-up detail. Submitting is free. Selected photography earns a $15 base payment plus $10 for each additional full-page image, $7 for one of two on a page, $6 for one of three and $5 for one of four; contributors can opt to have $9 taken out of their payment, plus international shipping, in return for a copy of the issue at the magazine's cost. Work that has already been posted online or submitted elsewhere is still accepted, and creators keep the right to post and sell it. Files must be high-quality PNG, PDF or JPEG. The magazine does not accept nudity, foul language, AI art, or work that closely copies someone else's.
