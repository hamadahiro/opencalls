Helpmann Fellowships 2026

Prize A$7,500 × 3

Three fellowships of up to A$7,500 for emerging South Australian creatives, funded by the James & Diana Ramsay Foundation. The fellowships are self-determined: applicants propose their own bold, ambitious project, which may be the creation of new work, an exhibition, a residency or internship, touring, specialised training, a longer professional development programme, or a combination. Photography is one of nineteen disciplines on the application form, alongside painting, sculpture, moving image, printmaking, new media and curatorial practice. Applicants must be final-year students or graduates within five years of a supported degree at Adelaide University, Flinders University, the University of Adelaide or the University of South Australia, and must currently reside in South Australia; master's and PhD candidates qualify only through their undergraduate or honours study. Applications are assessed on artistic merit, alignment with career development, feasibility, commitment to learning and level of ambition, and require an artist CV, academic transcript, budget on the Academy's template, timeline, project confirmations, two letters of support and four to six images or one to two audio-visual demos. Funded activity must take place between 1 January and 31 December 2027. There is no application fee. Outcomes are notified within four weeks of the closing date and fellowships are presented at a private event in October.
