SELF PORTRAITS 2026

Matt Ford Studio is a photographer-run gallery and studio in Hackney Downs Studios, east London, whose programme leans toward sexuality, gender, identity and fashion. SELF PORTRAITS 2026 is its winter open call, exhibition and photo book, put together by Matt Ford after he began making self-portraits as a way of working through his own fears about AI, politics and the future of an artistic career, and became interested in self-portraiture itself — as vanity, as creativity, as self-expression, or simply as a record of where you are in your life. The call invites photographers worldwide to reflect on what it means to be human in the second quarter of the 21st century. Anyone 18 or over from any country may enter, at any level of skill, and both digital and analogue work is welcome — disposable cameras, phones and high-end DSLRs alike — along with collage and experimental work that still involves some element of self-portrait. Digitally enhanced images are accepted; AI-generated images are not. Judging for finalists and winners is done by the editors of ZERO.NINE Magazine. The winner receives £500 cash, the cover of the SELF PORTRAITS photo book, an 'On The Radar' feature in ZERO.NINE and a feature about their work; all semi-finalists appear in the photo book at no extra cost, and finalists are shown in the exhibition at the studio. Entry is £13 for the first photograph and £6 for each one after; if you reach the exhibition a further £20 per photograph shown covers printing (arranged with theprintspace) and exhibition admin, or you may send your own print at your own postage cost. Semi-finalists are announced 9 November, the book launch, private view and winners announcement is 10 December, and the exhibition is open to the public 11–14 December 2026. Work is for sale during the show, with 75% to the artist.
