Heysen Prize for Landscape 2026

Prize AU$15,000 acquisitive

A biennial contemporary art prize established in 1997 to commemorate Sir Hans Heysen, run by the Hahndorf Academy in the Adelaide Hills. The 2026 edition asks artists to express their connection with — or concern for — protecting the Australian environment, taking Heysen's own record as an early environmental activist as its starting point. 'Landscape' is defined broadly to include every aspect of the natural, rural and urban landscape, and the prize is open to 2D, 3D and moving-image work; photographers have been regular finalists, and past selections include Che Chorley and Mark Kimber. The main prize of $15,000 is acquisitive; a non-acquisitive People's Choice Prize of $1,000 is also awarded. Finalists are notified on 31 August 2026 and deliver work to Hahndorf between 7 and 23 September; the finalist exhibition runs 10 October to 6 December 2026, opening with a launch on 10 October at which the winners are announced.
