ViewPoint Gallery International Photography Competition

Prize Exhibition prize

ViewPoint Gallery is a photographer-run cooperative gallery in Halifax, Nova Scotia — Eastern Canada. The annual International Photography Competition selects work for the gallery's monthly featured wall.

The structure is unusual: twelve winners are selected, and each receives a one-month featured display from June 2026 through May 2027. Each winner gets the wall for the full month rather than a single group exhibition. Production is gallery-supplied: work is professionally printed, matted, and framed.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is by Google Form with 3 images. Fee is $25. The 2026 deadline was April 15; results announced May 8, 2026.
