Trenton Arts Fellowship 2027-28

Mercer County Community College and Princeton University's Lewis Center for the Arts name one Trenton Arts Fellow for a 10-month fellowship, September to June, with studio space on the college's Trenton campus, mentorship and a $10,000 stipend and research fund. It is open to visual artists who live and work in the city of Trenton. Applicants must not be enrolled in a programme of study during the fellowship.
