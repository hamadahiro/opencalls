Beyond Borders — International Photography Exhibition

iTravelPoetry is an independent cultural organisation based in Chalandri, in the northern suburbs of Athens, working across literature, cinema and contemporary art. It runs its own cultural venue and summer cinema there, publishes, and organises competitions and festivals: the twelfth edition of its U Write Panhellenic poetry competition is open at the time of writing with €1,350 in prizes and four publishing contracts, and an iTravelPoetry International Film Festival is announced for summer 2027. Its first photography festival ended in October 2026 with the group exhibition "Within the Frame", shown at the organisation's own venue from 26 to 28 October.

Beyond Borders is its first international photography open call. The brief is deliberately wide: borders can be geographical, cultural, social, emotional or entirely personal — they can separate people, define identities or vanish in a single encounter — and applicants are invited either to explore the visible and invisible boundaries that shape their lives or to challenge the idea of a border altogether. Every photographic genre and technique is accepted, with documentary, conceptual, fine art, street, portraiture, landscape, abstract and experimental work all named.

The call is open to photographers aged 18 and over of any nationality or country of residence, emerging and established alike. Each applicant may submit up to three photographs, each as a separate JPG with its own title, sent by email with the applicant's name, contact details, the photograph's title and its year of creation. Submissions opened 17 October 2026 and close 30 November 2026.

The cost structure is the part to weigh. Applying costs nothing and no payment is asked at the application stage, but photographers whose work is selected pay a €150 participation fee if they choose to confirm their place. That single fee covers all three exhibitions rather than one per city, and the organisers describe it as covering organisational expenses for presenting the show in Athens, Paris and Rome. Each selected photograph is shown in all three cities.

What is not yet published is as relevant as what is. The organisers state plainly that exhibition dates and venue details will be announced only after the submission period has closed, so no venue in any of the three cities is named at the point of applying. No jury or selector is named, no prize or artist fee is offered, and no sales or print-production terms are published. The outcome on offer is the exhibition itself.
