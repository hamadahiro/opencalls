RED Photography Exhibit

Prize Exhibition

Dusk Gallery is a Santa Fe, New Mexico photography gallery — a sister gallery to Decode Gallery in Tucson, both founded by fine art photographer Holly Hart. RED is the gallery's call for photography where red commands the frame: documentary, fine art, abstract, and experimental approaches are equally welcome.

What's unusual is the production model. The gallery covers all professional printing on Luster paper, custom matting, and 24×24 inch black museum-quality framing — artists submit only the high-resolution JPEG. Files: 500KB–5MB, longest side 4,500 pixels. Twenty-six images are selected.

Selection is by Holly Hart. The exhibition runs August 7–28, 2026 at Dusk Gallery in Santa Fe. Prints are archived in the gallery's flat files for six months after the exhibition closes for ongoing promotion. The gallery operates on a 50/50 commission split for any sales; photographers retain all rights.

Eligibility is open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers. Submission is through the gallery's website with 5 or 10 images ($40 or $45). The 2026 deadline is June 25; notifications are sent within two weeks.
