#NewVisions2027

Texas Photographic Society's annual online student competition is open-themed and open to college students worldwide, in any photographic process from silver and alternative to collage and book arts. Juror Robin Dru Germany picks 50 images; cash awards run $300, $200 and $100, and every selected artist gets a free year of TPS student membership. Entry is $20 for five images.
