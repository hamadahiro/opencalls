Ethereal

Seeking images that explore the intangible — works that evoke lightness, transience, and the space between what is seen and what is felt. Both traditional and experimental photographic processes welcome. Juror: John Horowitz, photographer and curator whose work resides in Purdue University's permanent collection. Exhibition June 5 – July 1, 2026 at A Photographer's Place in Raleigh, NC. Opening reception June 5, 7–9 PM.
