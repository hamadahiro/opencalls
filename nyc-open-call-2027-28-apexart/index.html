NYC Open Call 2027–28

Prizes $10,000 exhibition budget NYC group exhibition

The anonymity is the point. Proposals carry no name, no CV, no links and no images, every juror sees a randomised set of at least fifty, and apexart says its staff does not influence the result. For a photographer who has an exhibition idea but no exhibition history, there are very few calls where that matters so little.

Know what the $10,000 is before you plan: $8,000 of it is the production budget you have to work within — curator travel, shipping, artist and public-programme honorariums, equipment, installation — and up to $2,000 is your own honorarium for coordinating the show and writing the brochure essay. apexart pays for insurance, advertising, publicity, documentation, brochure design and printing and the opening separately, and runs most of the logistics itself.

The format rules are strict and are the usual reason a proposal is thrown out. It must be a group show of three or more artists working independently (four if you are one of them), in English, under 500 words, with no proposed dates, no open call for artists inside it, no single unified multi-artist project, and no sales activity. One proposal per person.

This October round is for the Church Street space in New York only. If your idea is tied to somewhere else, the international open call — exhibitions anywhere in the world except New York — opens 1 February to 1 March 2027, and asks you to nominate a place you have actually visited.

No medium is specified anywhere in the call, so a photography group show is as eligible as any other; what is judged is the idea and how well 500 words carry it.

The submission form carries the only AI clause anywhere on this call: a field asking “Did you use AI in the writing of this proposal?”, whose placeholder answers the worry it raises — “This will not affect your eligibility.” Nothing on the open-call page, the process page or the FAQs mentions AI at all. Disclosure is invited rather than policed, and no use of it is barred.
