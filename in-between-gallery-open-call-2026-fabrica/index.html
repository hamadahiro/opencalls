In Between Gallery Open Call 2026

Fabrica is a contemporary art gallery in a Grade II-listed former church in Brighton's Lanes, founded as an artist-led space in 1996. Its In Between Gallery turns the gallery's picture window on Duke Street - one of the city's busiest pedestrianised streets, busy into the night - into a temporary photography gallery, and this ninth-year open call, run with Spectrum Photographic, Photo Fringe, Jane & Jeremy and POST, seeks one image by one UK-based photographer or artist group who considers themselves early to mid-career. The selected image is exhibited from November 2026 to April 2027 (with occasional short removals around venue hire), and the artist receives a £350 fee covering the exhibition, a talk at Fabrica's Photography Networking Event on Thursday 4 February 2027 (travel paid separately) and an Instagram takeover from 28 January to 4 February; the artist and image are also promoted by Fabrica and its partners. Practicalities that the panel weighs: the window is roughly 2.7 by 2.25 metres, so square formats sit well, and its light filter does not favour predominantly dark or black-and-white images. There is no theme and no entry fee, and the call is open to UK residents only. Applications go through a Google Form with up to three low-res images, a title and context document, a biography or CV, and a link to a website or Instagram; the deadline is 12 noon on Monday 26 October 2026 and the selected artist is told on Friday 30 October.
