Vital Impacts Environmental Photography Fellowships and Mentorships 2027

Annual fellowship programme from Vital Impacts, the 501(c)(3) founded by photographer Ami Vitale, supporting visual storytellers working on long-term documentary projects about environmental and conservation issues. For 2027 the foundation awards one $20,000 fellowship to an established environmental photographer, named for Dr. Jane Goodall, six $5,000 fellowships to emerging photographers, and ten places on an intensive mentorship programme; one fellow is also invited to exhibit at the 2027 Festival of Ethical Photography in Lodi, Italy. Recipients have twelve months to develop their project with publishing and showcase support from Vital Impacts. The foundation strongly favours photographers with a meaningful, sustained relationship to the community, place or issue they propose to document, and is drawn to work that moves past documenting environmental problems to the people and initiatives working toward solutions. You do not have to live permanently in a place to qualify - recurring travel, long-term collaboration or professional and academic engagement also count. Prior publication experience is not required, and applicants may write in their native language, with translation arranged. Submit 20 images from one or two bodies of work plus a synopsis, project statement and biography. Applications open August 31, 2026 on Picter; the $25 fee is waived for applicants from developing countries and on request for economic hardship.
