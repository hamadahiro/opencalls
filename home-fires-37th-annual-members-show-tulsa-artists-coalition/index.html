Home Fires: 37th Annual Members' Show

Prizes Cash awards Exhibition

The Tulsa Artists' Coalition is a volunteer-run non-profit gallery in the Tulsa Arts District that mounts around eleven exhibitions a year at no cost to the exhibiting artists; Home Fires is its 37th annual members' show, pitched as a celebration of Tulsa's art community and the bonds that make TAC a place to come home to. It is open to all media with no restriction on subject beyond hate speech, and it is a members' show in the buyable sense: new and renewing memberships are accepted up to and including the submission deadline, and membership runs from $35 for an individual ($15 for full-time students, $50 per household), so a photographer anywhere can enter by joining, though accepted work must be delivered to the gallery on 26-27 December 2026 and unsold work collected on 23-24 January 2027. Members may submit up to two original pieces made in the last two years, one form per piece, as JPEGs of at least 1,200 pixels on the long side; because of the gallery's size only one piece per artist is selected, and work may be no larger than 24 inches on its longest side and must arrive ready to hang. Selection is from the digital submissions by a Selections Committee of three jurors - artists, educators and other art professionals, changed every year - with accepted and declined artists emailed by 5 December 2026. The show runs 1-23 January 2027 with the opening at the First Friday Art Crawl on 1 January, where a Grand Prize and three juror's choice cash awards are announced; the amounts are not published. Submissions opened 8 September and close at midnight Central time on 7 November 2026 through the coalition's Submittable portal, where the entry form carries no fee. TAC retains a 35 percent commission on art sales, 30 percent if the artist is a TAC member.
