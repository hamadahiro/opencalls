Winter Artist Residency

Prize One-month Oxford studio residency

A one-month self-funded studio residency at OVADA's warehouse on Osney Lane in central Oxford, running Friday 20 November to Friday 18 December 2026. It is open to artists from Oxfordshire at all career stages, and OVADA says it is particularly interested in practitioners who would benefit from dedicated studio space and a sustained period of time to develop their practice. No medium is named or excluded, so photographers working in Oxfordshire can apply. The residency gives a place in a shared studio with four other artists on the first floor, with 24-hour access, Wi-Fi and power, communal facilities, the company of the artists working in the studios, and a connection to the programme of exhibitions and events running across the warehouse; an open studio during the residency can be arranged with OVADA. The studio is reached by stairs only, and OVADA asks artists with accessibility requirements to make contact in advance so arrangements can be discussed. Heavy machinery, welding tools and loud equipment are not permitted, and practices involving solvents, aerosols or materials needing specialist ventilation cannot be carried out indoors. The space must be fully vacated by 5 p.m. on Friday 18 December 2026 — OVADA cannot store materials, artworks or waste after the residency ends. The residency is self-funded. There is no fee to apply, and the £175 residency fee is payable only if an artist is selected; it covers the shared studio place. For scale, OVADA's own hire page prices its shared upstairs Flexi studio at £250 per month including utilities, so the residency is charged below the organisation's own rate for shared space. An application is a link to a website and/or artist Instagram account, a PDF portfolio and a statement of up to 300 words about what the artist would like to gain from the residency, sent through the form at the foot of OVADA's own residency page. Applications close Sunday 25 October 2026 at midnight.
