The New Masters 4: Women Artists of Northeast Ohio

Prizes Cash awards Exhibition

The Wasmer Gallery on the Ursuline College campus hosts the fourth edition of its juried exhibition for women artists living and working in Northeast Ohio. The gallery enumerates photography explicitly among the accepted media — traditional, digital and computer-enhanced — alongside painting, works on paper, mixed media, sculpture, glass, fiber and jewelry, and giclée reproductions are barred except for signed and numbered limited editions or photography. Open only to women artists 18 and over, emerging and professional, currently residing in the eighteen counties of Northeast Ohio: Ashland, Ashtabula, Columbiana, Cuyahoga, Erie, Geauga, Huron, Lake, Lorain, Mahoning, Medina, Portage, Richland, Stark, Summit, Trumbull, Tuscarawas and Wayne. The guaranteed minimum prize pool is $500, rising with the number of entries, and is split across Best in Show, an Award of Excellence for first prize and an Award of Distinction for second; final amounts are announced on 12 September with acceptance notifications, and cheques are issued by Ursuline College. Up to three works per artist at $10 each, capped at $30. Work must be original, made in the last five years, not made under instruction in a class or workshop, and not previously shown at the Wasmer Gallery. 2D work including mat and frame must be no larger than 30 by 40 inches.
