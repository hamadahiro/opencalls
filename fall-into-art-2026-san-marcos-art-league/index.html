Fall into Art 2026

The San Marcos Art League's autumn open show at the San Marcos Art Center, asking for work that carries the colours and traditions of the season — from pleasant autumnal memory to the new adventures that arrive as the year turns. Open to all artists across every medium, with photographs, fine art prints and digital art named explicitly alongside sculpture, textiles and pottery. Each artist may enter one original work completed in the last 24 months and not previously shown at the Art Center Gallery. Two-dimensional work must be framed or gallery-wrapped with no visible staples, wired for the gallery's hanging system, and no larger than 36 by 68 inches including the frame; three-dimensional work is considered on size and stability. All work must be hand-delivered — the Art Center does not accept shipments — with intake on 30 August, a public reception on 11 September and pickup 1–2 October. Work must be for sale at a price fixed at entry, and the Art League retains a 20% commission. Entry is $10 for Art League members with the code sent to them by email, and $20 otherwise; submissions close 28 August at 5pm or when the show fills.
