Analog Awards 2026

Prizes €1,000 Photobook publication Magazine feature Online exhibition

Analog Hub is a community built around alternative photographic processes — it writes about photographers who shoot film, print by hand, or work with cyanotype, wet plate and older processes — and the Analog Awards is its yearly contest. The brief is wide inside a narrow technical rule: all genres and approaches are welcome, from documentary projects and portraits to conceptual and experimental work, but the photograph has to be made on film or with an analog process, and digital files are accepted only where the final image was made analog. The listed categories run from film and Polaroid through salt print, calotype, albumen, wet collodion, ambrotype, tintype, daguerreotype, cyanotype, platinum/palladium, gum bichromate, carbon transfer, Van Dyke brown, kallitype, lumen print, anthotype, photogram, Woodburytype, bromoil and oil print to hand-coloured photographs, photographic collage, photo and embroidery, and letterpress. Entries are judged anonymously by a six-person jury on creativity, artistic merit, subject and style. The overall winners in both the series and the single-image categories receive a €1,000 cash prize and the title of Analog Photographer of the Year; two winners receive a free photobook publication with the publishing house Snap Collective, and finalists and honourable mentions are offered the same book route with professional design support. Winners and finalists are given an interview and portfolio in the next issue of Analog Hub Magazine, a place in the Analog Hub Annual Book, and an exhibition in Analog Hub's digital gallery. You may submit as many entries as you wish, and work previously entered in other competitions is allowed. Early-bird prices run to 22 November; late-bird prices apply from 23 November to the 20 December deadline. Winners are announced in February 2027.
