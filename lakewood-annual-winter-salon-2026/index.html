Lakewood Annual Winter Salon 2026

The Lakewood Cultural Center's annual salon, a large-scale group exhibition and art market of small, accessibly priced work, running November 7, 2026 to January 2, 2027 with a reception on November 7 from 6 to 8 pm. Photographs are named among the eligible media alongside original painting, drawing, prints and mixed media; reproductions are not accepted. Work must be two-dimensional and, per the CaFE prospectus, no larger than three feet in any direction including the frame (the Cultural Center's own event page instead describes the show as pieces 16 by 20 inches or smaller), framed and ready to hang with securely attached wire or a cleat system - nothing suspended from the ceiling or standing on the floor. Everything must be for sale and priced at $500 or less, prices cannot be adjusted after entry, and the City of Lakewood takes a 15% commission; artists sign a loan agreement so work is insured while in the Cultural Center's care, and sold work is collected after December 18. Entry is $30 for up to three works. There is no shipping in either direction - work must be hand-delivered to the Lakewood Cultural Center - so in practice the show is limited to artists who can travel to the Denver area. Laine Godsey, the City of Lakewood's Arts Programming Curator, juries the selection. The organiser's own call description sets the schedule as entries accepted on CaFE until Thursday, October 22, 2026 or until the call is full, notification of acceptance by the end of Friday, October 23, and delivery of accepted work during the week of October 26; note that one requirement bullet on the CaFE record still gives November 6, the day before the exhibition opens.
