2027 Meridian Scholar Program

Prize Residency

The University of Tampa's Department of Art + Design invites applications for the 2027 Meridian Scholar Program, a ten-day residency (formerly STUDIO-f) hosted on the university's downtown Tampa campus and open to artists, designers, creative practitioners and researchers. The 2027 cycle is built around the theme of Worldbuilding and seeks creative practitioners who intend to print, cast or digitally fabricate multiple editions during the residency period; photography is one of the accepted categories alongside craft, drawing, film/video/new media, mixed media, painting and sculpture. The selected resident works in the department's facilities in the Ferman Center for the Arts and the 30,000-square-foot R. K. Bailey Art Studios, which house drawing, printmaking, painting and ceramics studios, a photography laboratory and darkroom, and a Fab Lab equipped with laser cutters, 3D printers, CNC routers and vinyl cutters, and is given a working space alongside the department's advanced student studios. Residents are expected to involve UTampa studio art, graphic design, art therapy and museum studies students in the preparation, production and post-production of the project, to give a public lecture and Q&A, to engage with at least one class through a critique, workshop or demonstration, and to present work made during the residency in a public exhibition. The residency runs 15 to 26 March 2027. Past visiting artists include Sam Gilliam, Joyce J. Scott, Miriam Schapiro, Audrey Flack, Larry Poons and, in 2026, the Cuban artist Mabel Poblet Pujol.
