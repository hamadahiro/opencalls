31st Extremely Minnesota

Robbin Gallery's long-running juried fine art exhibition for artists living and working in Minnesota, hung in the historic Robbinsdale Library gallery just north of Minneapolis. The media list is broad and explicit: “2D and 3D visual art forms including but not limited to painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, textile, photography, digital, mixed media” — video, performance and anything requiring an electrical hook-up are excluded, and generative AI “is not allowed in part or in whole.” Prints are welcome where that is normal for the medium, “as in photography, lithography, block-printing and digital.” Open to Minnesota residents aged 18 and over; entries must be original work completed within the last three years, not made under an instructor's supervision, and not previously exhibited at Robbin Gallery. $30 covers up to two pieces, with 2D work capped at 40 inches in any direction including frame. Cash awards totalling $1,000 are presented at the opening reception on Saturday, November 7, and the show runs November 6–29, 2026. The EntryThingy call opens Monday, August 31 at 9:00 am Central and closes Friday, October 9 at 11:59 pm Central; accepted work must be hand-delivered, as the gallery cannot accept shipped artwork.
