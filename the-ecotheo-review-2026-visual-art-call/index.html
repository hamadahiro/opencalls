The EcoTheo Review — 2026 Visual Art Call

The EcoTheo Review is published by EcoTheo Collective, a 501(c)3 nonprofit literary and arts organization whose work celebrates wonder and inspires commitments to ecology, spirituality, and art. Its quarterly print issues gather poetry, fiction, essays, interviews, and visual art, and this open call welcomes photography and all other forms of original visual art that operate at the intersection of ecology, spirituality, and culture across religious and spiritual traditions.

Submissions are reviewed on a rolling basis and the organiser states the deadline is ongoing; the Submittable form carries the end date 2026-12-31 in its own markup. There is no entry fee on the Submittable route - the fee column on EcoTheo’s Submittable index is empty for this form, where other organisations render a price in the same element. The CaFÉ listing that published a $10 entry fee now reads closed. A submission is 1-10 work samples alongside a short artist statement and bio. The Review features roughly 15-20 visual artists a year across four print issues plus its website and social channels, sometimes paired with essays, statements, or interviews. Work created with the assistance of AI tools is not eligible.
