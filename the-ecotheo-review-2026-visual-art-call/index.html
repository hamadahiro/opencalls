The EcoTheo Review — 2026 Visual Art Call

Prize Print publication

The EcoTheo Review is published by EcoTheo Collective, a 501(c)3 nonprofit literary and arts organization whose work celebrates wonder and inspires commitments to ecology, spirituality, and art. Its quarterly print issues gather poetry, fiction, essays, interviews, and visual art, and this open call welcomes photography and all other forms of original visual art that operate at the intersection of ecology, spirituality, and culture across religious and spiritual traditions.

Submissions are reviewed on a rolling basis through the October 20, 2026 deadline; the $10 fee covers 1–10 work samples alongside a short artist statement and bio. The Review features roughly 15–20 visual artists a year across four print issues plus its website and social channels, sometimes paired with essays, statements, or interviews. Work created with the assistance of AI tools is not eligible.
