The Unseen — What Lies Beneath the Surface

A juried exhibition at Artspace, the artist-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit gallery in Richmond's Stratford Hills, inviting artists to explore hidden forces, dreams, memories, spirituality, mystery, imagination and the unseen systems that shape our experience of the world. Open to artists 18 and over in all genres, 2D and 3D, with work made in 2022 or later; photography sits squarely inside this gallery's programme — its own exhibition history carries photography solo shows in the main gallery, and video entries are accepted if the artist supplies the display equipment. Juror Robert Stuart — an MFA painter who moved from representation to abstraction, was selected for the New Orleans Museum of Art Triennial, holds an Academy Award in Art from the American Academy of Arts and Letters and now co-directs Gallery 22 in Staunton — awards $300 and a solo exhibition at Artspace to first place, $200 to second, $100 to third, plus up to three honorable mentions. Entry is 1–3 images with an artist statement for a $35 fee, and Artspace asks anyone for whom that is a hardship to email its exhibition committee for an exemption before submitting. The show runs 22 January – 20 March 2027, opening with an awards reception and a 6:30pm juror talk on 22 January. Accepted work is hand-delivered or shipped to Richmond at the artist's cost with a prepaid return label (plexiglass, not glass), must hang on D-rings and wire, must fit through a 33 × 82¾ inch door, and stays on view for the full run; sales are encouraged but not required, with the gallery retaining 45% commission (25% for Artspace members). The deadline is stated in Mountain Time — 11:59 pm on Friday 4 December 2026 — which is 1:59 am on 5 December for anyone on the US East Coast.
