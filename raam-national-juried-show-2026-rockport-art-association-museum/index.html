RAA&M National Juried Show 2026

The Rockport Art Association & Museum's annual national juried exhibition hangs in the Aldro T. Hibbard and Maddocks galleries on Cape Ann from 14 November to 31 December 2026, with a gala and awards reception on 13 November. Photography is one of the named media categories, alongside painting, drawing, mixed media, collage, original printmaking and sculpture. Over $26,000 in cash and merchandise awards is on offer, including a $5,000 Grand Prize. Garin Baker is juror of selection and of awards. Artists may submit up to four works but only one will be selected if accepted; the maximum size is 36 inches on the longest side plus a reasonable frame. Membership is not required to enter. The museum takes a 40% commission on sales from the exhibition. Work made in an instructional setting, including workshops and classes, is not eligible, and accepted work must stay hanging until the show closes unless sold. The AI policy is unusually specific for a photography entrant: no artificial intelligence may be used in the conception or development of the work, images must originate with the artist through a light capture process, and no synthetic images, stock imagery, replacement skies or generative fill are permitted. Editing is allowed as long as every component belongs to the artist. Results appear in the OnlineJuriedShows artist profile on 25 September 2026.
