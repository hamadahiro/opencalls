5th Annual Black Artists Exhibit

Prize Exhibition

Riverside Arts Center's fifth annual Black Artists Exhibit runs in honour of Martin Luther King Jr. Day, hosted with Ronnie and Gloria Peterson, the African American Cultural and Historical Museum of Washtenaw County, the Palm Leaf Club and Sankofa. All art mediums are welcome, so photography sits alongside painting, sculpture and fibre, and entry costs nothing — the call page says all RAC calls for art are free to apply and the centre's Artist Opportunities page says all of its calls for art are always free to enter. The geography is written as a preference, not a bar: open to artists locally, regionally and nationally, with priority possibly given to artists based in south-eastern Michigan, because the centre also wants work from outside the region that broadens the experience for its local community. Age is no bar either, though an artist under 18 needs written permission from a parent or guardian, who also co-signs any exhibition or sales agreement. Submissions run through Artwork Archive; the centre caps a submission at three artworks and the form asks for one to five images at 300 dpi and up to 5 MB each, an artist statement of up to 100 words and a bio of up to 50. Work must arrive display-ready — framed, wired or otherwise prepared — and no larger than 36 by 48 inches unless pre-approved, and anything previously exhibited at Riverside may not be resubmitted. The AI rules sit in two blocks that do not say the same thing. The eligibility criteria say work “created wholly or partially with AI tools must be disclosed at submission” and “will be evaluated under the same criteria as other submissions”, while Rules and Terms clause 2 says the centre “will not accept artwork that uses generative AI to create or assist in the creation of images, designs, text, or other creative content, whether in whole or in part”, and that disclosure is required even where the AI use is not generative, in which case it “does not automatically disqualify the work from consideration”. The narrower reading is the one to apply: generative AI is out, including as an assist, and any other AI tool used at any stage of design, editing or production has to be described in the application. The show hangs 15 January to 27 February 2027 and decisions go out on 21 December 2026. Riverside insures work while it is on the premises, to a per-piece limit of $5,000, but delivery, pickup, packaging and all shipping are the artist's cost, and work not collected within 30 days of the close may incur storage fees or be disposed of.
