Coming Together

A soft theme with a hard edge of specificity. Fotografiska is not asking for "people" or "happiness" — it is asking where togetherness actually lives: "From gatherings around a family table to moments of belonging within a chosen community, we want to see the different ways people come together around the world." The sentence about chosen community is the one to read twice; it opens the call to queer family, diaspora, congregation, team, squat, ward, band — anywhere belonging had to be built rather than inherited. The organiser adds that it welcomes unexpected interpretations alongside familiar scenes, which is a quiet warning that the familiar scenes will be crowded.

Five artists are selected and each gets €300, a feature on the Emerging platform, an Instagram feature across both @fotografiska and @fotografiska.emerging, and a mention in the Emerging newsletter. The money is modest; the audience is not, and on a free entry the ratio is unusually good. Selection is by a jury of the Fotografiska Emerging team plus a Fotografiska curator, judged on artistic and creative merit and on how the picture answers the theme.

Entry is free and the format is light: one image per submission, up to five images in total, JPG or PNG, minimum width 1,600–2,000 pixels, maximum 5 MB. Each needs an English caption (or an English translation) of up to 1,000 characters, and the organiser asks you to open it with a sentence introducing yourself — so the caption is doing double duty as a short artist note. AI-generated images are explicitly not accepted.

The window opened on 2 November 2026 at 10:00 CET and closes 3 December at 10:00 CET; the selected five are featured individually between 21 and 27 December. Entrants must be at least 18, and Fotografiska group employees and their relatives cannot enter.
