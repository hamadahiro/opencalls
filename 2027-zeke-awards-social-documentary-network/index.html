2027 ZEKE Awards

Prizes $2,500 cash ZEKE Magazine Guardian US

Social Documentary Network's annual awards for documentary photography and for visual stories about systemic change. Two first-place photographers each receive $2,500, a feature in ZEKE Magazine and in The Guardian US; honourable mentions are published too. Entries are projects of six to thirty photographs, open to amateurs and professionals worldwide.

DeadlineJanuary 15, 2027
ResultsFebruary 21, 2027
Entry fee$30 through December 31, then $35
LocationOnline
Requirements6-30 images
