Seen + Heard

Prize $800 in awards

Formerly Women of Appalachia, Seen + Heard is a regional exhibition bringing together women and people with diverse experiences of femininity to tell their stories through visual art and traditional craft. The Dairy Barn asks for work that challenges stereotypes, confronts marginalisation and celebrates the complexity of women's lives, feminine identities and gendered experience in Appalachia; jurors are told to weigh identity, belonging, resilience, visibility and social perception alongside originality, design, technique and craftsmanship, and to give priority to work that amplifies underrepresented perspectives. Accepted media are listed as collage, digital and new media, drawing, fibre arts, film and video, installation, mixed media, painting, performance, photography, pottery, printmaking, sculpture and traditional craft. One of the three jurors, Robyn Moore, is a photomedia artist and Associate Professor of Photography at Morehead State University who works in alternative and experimental photographic processes. $800 in prize money is awarded across an Outstanding Contribution award of $200, a $150 Voice and Visibility Award and three $150 Juror's Awards for Storytelling, Impact and Innovation. Each artist may submit up to three works completed on or after January 1 2024, and the entry fee is a self-selected sliding scale from $15 to $65 with no explanation required. The show runs January 23 to March 21 2027 in the Sauber Gallery; the Dairy Barn takes 35% on sales and artists pay shipping both ways.
