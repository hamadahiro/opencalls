19th Annual Open Juried Photographic Exhibit

Cary Photographic Artists, founded in 2007 and described on its own site as the Triangle's only group whose mission is to develop and enhance the practice of photographic art, has run this open juried show every autumn since then. It is a print show: digital files go through CaFE but are not what is judged, and every entry must be printed, mounted, framed and hand-delivered to the Cary Senior Center on Monday 19 October before it is judged - the club's homepage tells photographers who cannot get work to Cary, NC not to enter. About 200 images are typically submitted and 50 are hung. There are two categories, MONOCHROME and COLOR, each with $200, $125 and $75 places and three honourable mentions, plus the Bobbi Hopp Best in Show Award of $400, named for the club's late founder. Any photographic process may be used as long as the photographic process is dominant; canvas wraps and prints on wood, metal, foam core or other surfaces are accepted; framed size cannot exceed 33 inches on the longest edge; work accepted into a prior CPA juried or digital exhibition is not eligible; and the artist's signature must not be visible on the front during judging. No AI or text-to-generative-fill images are accepted, and entrants retain the rights to their images. The jurors are Sarah Shea Roberto, a North Carolina photographer specialising in the silver gelatin darkroom who teaches experimental darkroom techniques at the NC State University Crafts Center, and Stacy Bloom Rexrode, Curator of Exhibitions and Collections with the City of Raleigh. Entry opens on CaFE on 15 September; artists whose images are accepted are telephoned on the evening of 20 October and work that is not selected is collected the next day; the show hangs at the Cary Senior Center from 20 October to 19 December; and a reception with comments by the jurors and presentation of awards is at 6pm on Friday 23 October.
