64th Annual Juried Competition

Prizes $3,200 in awards Exhibition

The Masur Museum of Art in Monroe, Louisiana has run its Annual Juried Competition since 1963, when Ida Kohlmeyer - a student of Mark Rothko - took first place and the museum bought her painting Immanence. It is the museum's longest-running tradition, and a significant share of its 500-work permanent collection, which also holds Picasso, Renoir and Rodin, has been acquired directly through it. The 64th edition is open to artists over 18 living in the United States and working in any medium, with no set theme; the museum receives 1,000-1,500 entries a year and hangs the selection for four months in its historic Main House. Awards total $3,200, with $1,000 for Best in Show, handed out at the March 11 opening reception, plus a separate $100 Best Packed award for the artist who crates their work most professionally - packing peanuts are grounds for disqualification. Accepted work may be offered for sale and the museum takes no commission on those sales. Two-dimensional work may not exceed 85 by 60 inches, three-dimensional work 84 inches tall within a 40 by 40 inch footprint and 200 pounds, and video is capped at five minutes. Entry is $10 per artwork with a minimum of two and a maximum of five, so $20 to $50, and the prospectus asks for recent work (it gives two different completion cutoffs, December 31 2023 in one place and December 31 2024 in another, so confirm with the museum). Submissions open on CaFE on October 1, 2026. This year's juror is not yet named. Artists are notified January 14, 2027, work is delivered February 22 - March 4, and the exhibition runs March 11 - May 15, 2027. One caution on timing: the prospectus gives the deadline as 11:59pm on December 16, but the CaFE portal's own closing field is set a day earlier, so treat December 15 as the safe date to submit by.
