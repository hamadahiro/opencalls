PhotoForum Open Call 2026 — Photobooks and Online Exhibitions

Prizes Publication Modest payment for online exhibitions

PhotoForum Inc. — which has been publishing books and magazines since Photo-Forum magazine launched in 1974 and is funded primarily by its members — opens one call covering two strands of its 2027 programme, with a single email submission for both. The first is its print publishing programme: PhotoForum produces two publications a year and is looking for engaging recent photographic projects to be developed as its upcoming photobooks. The terms are stated plainly and are worth reading in full before applying. Each publication is issued as a limited edition with the majority sent to subscribed members; all publishing costs are covered by PhotoForum; copyright of the images remains with the artist; and the artists will not receive any payment but will receive five copies of the book. For a photographer with a resolved body of work and no publisher, a fully funded limited-edition photobook that costs nothing to enter is a substantial offer — but it is a publication, not a fee. The second strand is a series of online exhibitions planned for 2027, showcasing a wide selection of photographic work alongside newly commissioned writing, and these are open to individual photographers as well as partnerships, collaborations and collectives. Here there is money: selected photographers receive a modest fee for online publication, and publication will likely fall between March and November 2027. PhotoForum frames the commissioned writing as essential — it sees the involvement of writers as the way to create discourse around photographic artworks and widen the audience for both photography and the writing about it, which is also why its online exhibitions sit alongside a long-running essays and reviews programme rather than standing alone. Submissions for either strand are a PDF accompanied by a short artist statement of about 200 words and a bio. Decisions are made by PhotoForum's Publications and Online Subcommittees against three published criteria: do we love the work; is the proposed project cohesive and relatively resolved in its scope; and is the project consistent with the organisation's vision of continuing a strong legacy of significant photographic publications in Aotearoa New Zealand. The one hard gate is geography — submissions are welcomed from photographers based in Aotearoa New Zealand. The deadline is 5 pm on 23 November 2026.
