Bibin Photography Magazine #7 — Landscape & Identity

Quarterly contemporary photography magazine from Afghanistan, now at issue 7. The theme is Landscape & Identity, read by guest writer Nasrin Amiri, a photography educator and researcher, through a critical-cultural approach: identity as a fluid perception of the self and its relationship with the surrounding world, shaped by individual and collective memory, social relations, lived experience and the geography of everyday life. The call's own framing rejects landscape photography as the neutral recording of natural reality, treating landscape instead as a human-made construct formed where the photographer's gaze meets memory, belonging or alienation, history and technical choice - so work that reinterprets or argues with a place is the point. Submit 3 to 10 photographs, JPEG or PNG, each file under 3 MB at the highest quality you have for print, named YourName_TitleOfThePhoto.jpg, with the location and date each photograph was taken. No logos, watermarks or frames; no portraits of recognizable individuals, no images containing other artists' work or designs, no identifiable brand elements, no recognizable properties needing an owner's consent, and no merged or juxtaposed frames presented as one image. Rights granted are non-exclusive and limited to Bibin's own magazine, website, social channels and related marketing, with the photographer credited and retaining copyright. Read the compensation clause before you apply: it states plainly that submission and publication are unpaid, and that selected photographers receive no payment, no free print copy and no discount code for the print copy. Each issue's guest writer is free to read the published photographs positively, negatively or neutrally.
