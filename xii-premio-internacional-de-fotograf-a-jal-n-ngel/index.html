XII Premio Internacional de Fotografía Jalón Ángel

Archivo Fotográfico Jalón Ángel@archivo_jalon_angel

12th edition of this international photography prize organized by Universidad San Jorge in Zaragoza, Spain. Three categories: Portrait (spontaneous or posed), Travel (landscapes, cultures, traditions), and Migration & Borders (human movement, territorial and cultural limits). One photograph per category. No AI, no heavy retouching, RAW files may be requested. Winners announced June 25. Named after Jalón Ángel (1898–1976), one of Spain's most celebrated portrait photographers.
