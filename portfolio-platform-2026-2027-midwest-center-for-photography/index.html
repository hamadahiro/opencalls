Portfolio Platform 2026/2027

Prize Online exhibition

The Midwest Center for Photography's online series-portfolio program: selected photographers receive a six-month feature on the MWCP website from October 2026 through March 2027, presenting a thematic body of 10–20 images with an artist statement and biography as a virtual gallery experience. Work is evaluated on the strength of the series rather than individual images. Open internationally to all photographers; visitors can view and purchase photographs through the feature page (50% commission).
