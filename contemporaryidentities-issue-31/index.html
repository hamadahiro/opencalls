ContemporaryIdentities Issue #31

Prize Publication

Free open call for the 31st issue of ContemporaryIdentities, "an independent quarterly international online magazine covering contemporary art from around the world" whose stated focus is "to showcase and promote experimental and progressive contemporary art through short art reviews from international scholars, art writers and art critics". Selected artworks are featured in the next issue alongside the magazine's invited criticism and artist interviews. THE MEDIA DOOR IS WIDE OPEN AND SAID SO: "Visual artists in any disciplines are welcome to submit artworks in any medium for our 31st Issue" — photography is not singled out but it is not excluded by any medium restriction either, and the call carries no theme. WHO RUNS IT: editors-in-chief and founders Sara Berti and Dr. Elham Shafaei, with a curatorial board of Dr. Izmer Ahmad and Dr. Zoltán Somhegyi; the magazine states it has been chosen for the Arts and Society database, in partnership with the Conseil International de la Philosophie et des Sciences Humaines (CIPSH), UNESCO-MOST, The Global Chinese Art, Culture and Society (GCACS) and Mémoire de l'Avenir. WHAT TO SEND: three to five high-resolution images with each work's title, year, medium and size, plus an artist statement and a short bio, by email. ONE WARNING THE MAGAZINE GIVES ITSELF: "We are not able to respond to each individual. We will only contact the successful applicant" — so silence after the deadline is the expected outcome for everyone not selected, not a lost email. Applicants must be 18 or over. The cutoff is stated in the magazine's own timezone, UTC+04:00, which runs ahead of European and American clocks — a European entrant effectively loses the last part of 15 October.
