Phoebe Journal Issue 56.1 — Visual Art

Prizes $30 per piece Publication (print)

Phoebe is a journal of literature and art published at George Mason University since 1971, printing one winter print issue and one spring contest issue each year. This is the visual art call for the winter 2027 print issue, 56.1, open from August 15 to October 15, 2026 for both the cover image and the interior print and online gallery. Phoebe pays: $30 per accepted piece of visual art plus a copy of the issue, and work chosen for the print issue is also featured on the magazine's website. The editors ask for one to five high-resolution images as .png, .tif or high-quality .jpeg at 300 dpi or more and at least 1 MB, with PDFs acceptable for multiple works; the brief is simply quality art from artists at any stage of their career, with an eye for work that illustrates a narrative or conveys an emotion. Phoebe does not enumerate accepted media, but photography is plainly in the mix — the journal's own Visual Art archive for the current issue includes Leah Oates' 'Transitory Space', described as colour photography shot with an analogue camera on 35mm film, alongside Robert Frede Kenter's digital photo collage. Previously published work is not considered; simultaneous submissions are encouraged provided you withdraw promptly. AI-generated writing and artwork are not accepted, though digital manipulation and digital art are welcome. Phoebe retains first North American serial rights and all other rights remain the artist's, reverting immediately on publication. The submission fee is $3 in all genres including visual art, and fee waivers can be requested by email for artists for whom that is a hardship; the journal explicitly welcomes BIPOC, LGBTQ+ and international artists. Current and former students of George Mason's creative writing programme and other GMU affiliates are not eligible. Responses are aimed at 30 days after the submission period closes.
