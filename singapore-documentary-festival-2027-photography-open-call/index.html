Singapore Documentary Festival 2027 — Photography Open Call

The Singapore Documentary Festival, run by Objectifs, is looking for the best documentary photography from Southeast Asia for its 2027 edition. Six projects are selected for a group exhibition at the festival in April 2027; one receives the Truth Seeker Foundation Prize, a cash grant of S$5,000 for a project addressing critical social and environmental issues in the region, and the five runners-up each receive S$1,000. All six get budget return flights and accommodation to attend the opening and programmes in Singapore. Applicants must be from Southeast Asia (practitioners living outside the region may apply), have at least five years of professional photography experience including internships, editorial assignments and freelance work, be able to work in English and own full copyright in the submitted work; one project per applicant. Projects must be documentary and rooted in contemporary social issues in Southeast Asia; completed bodies of work must have concluded within the last five years, and ongoing projects are welcome. Every image must be made with a camera - AI-generated work is not accepted, and AI post-processing tools must not add or remove anything the camera captured. Submit a bio, portrait and CV, a synopsis of up to 250 words, 15 to 30 project images at 2000px on the longest side, and up to three recent projects. Deadline 30 September 2026, 2359hr Singapore time; shortlisted applicants may be contacted in October, selections announced in November, image selection and sequencing with the exhibition team from late November to January, production February-March 2027. The jury is Kelsang Dolma, Maye-E Wong, Amrita Chandradas and Chelsea Chua, alongside a representative of the Truth Seeker Foundation.
