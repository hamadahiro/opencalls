Art in Action 2027

Prizes Museum exhibition Publication

CICA Museum in Gimpo, South Korea invites artists, performers, designers and professionals in creative fields to propose a project for Art in Action 2027, the museum's cross-disciplinary presentation programme. Three things come with selection. Participants show work in the group exhibition Art in Action: Concurrent Show 2027 at CICA Museum from December 23 2026 to January 10 2027, with up to two works each; the museum's own curators and assistants print, frame and install photographs and 2D digital art at 10 x 15 or 10 x 10 inches (larger prints are possible on request, and additional fees may apply). Participants also present their project in a 10-minute pre-recorded, edited video at Art in Action: Live 2027, a one-day YouTube live-streaming event on January 10 2027, with sessions of four to six presentations followed by roughly 20 minutes of live Q&A; that presentation video is due December 23 2026. And presenters are featured in the book New Media Art 2028, expected as a full-colour paperback in December 2027. Submission is by email and costs nothing: a link to a three-minute video in which you explain the proposed project, plus a short bio. If the proposal is accepted, the artist is asked to contribute $30 USD toward event registration and $78 USD (or 110,000 KRW) per work toward exhibition processing and maintenance to confirm participation — the same per-work contribution the museum's other open calls use. Artists sending physical work pay their own shipping both ways; two-dimensional work should be no larger than 39 inches on the longest side and wall-mounted work no heavier than 10 kg. Selected presenters are notified on acceptance and register November 1-15 2026. The museum notes the schedule is subject to change.
