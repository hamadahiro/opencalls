Seeds 2026

Prize Exhibition

Westobou is a non-profit arts organisation in Augusta, Georgia whose gallery on Broad Street programmes national, regional and local artists with a focus on emerging and mid-career contemporary and experimental practice. Seeds is its twelfth annual juried exhibition of small works priced at an affordable $300 or less, timed for the holidays, and the 2026 juror is Vanna Black — an Atlanta illustrator, painter and muralist with a BFA in graphic arts from Georgia State University, represented by Cat Eye Creative, who has made work for ATL DTN, LivingWalls, the City of Atlanta, the Sustainable Forestry Industry, Hermes and Canada Goose of Atlanta. Westobou runs the juror chain as a relay: each year's juror picks the next. Applying is free, through a Google Form, and asks for a brief proposal describing what you plan to exhibit, five to ten images representative of your style, an exhibition history and bio, and a link to a website or social media. No medium is excluded — the prospectus describes the show as featuring a variety of local and regional artists working with various mediums and a variety of styles, and the parameters are about scale and format rather than material: every individual piece must fit inside an 18-inch square (or an 18-inch cube if three-dimensional), work must be framed unless there is good reason otherwise, and it must have been completed in the last 18 months, though it does not have to exist yet when you apply. Ten to twelve artists are selected, each given roughly nine feet of gallery wall to hang gallery- or salon-style with a two to three foot gap from the next artist; Westobou reserves the right to curate the layout. Selected artists must supply at least four pieces, all for sale, with a 40 per cent commission on sales. Notification lands by 28 August 2026, completed work is due in the gallery by 26 October, and the show runs 6 November to 19 December 2026 with a juror talk at 5 p.m. and an opening reception 6 to 8 p.m. on 6 November.
