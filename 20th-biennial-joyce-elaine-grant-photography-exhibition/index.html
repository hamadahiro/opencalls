20th Biennial Joyce Elaine Grant Photography Exhibition

Prize Solo exhibition

The twentieth edition of the biennial Joyce Elaine Grant Photography Exhibition, a national juried show at Texas Woman's University's East | West Galleries in Denton, running February 15 – March 12, 2027. Established in 2001 by TWU and the Photographic Artists' Coalition, a student-run photography organisation, JEG was founded by alumna Christine Shank and a small group of graduate students and named in memory of the mother of Professor Susan kae Grant, Cornaro Professor of Visual Art Emerita. Every edition is juried by a distinguished female curator; this one is judged by Maria L. Kelly, Assistant Curator of Photography at the High Museum of Art in Atlanta, who has helped organise nearly thirty photography installations there — among them Photography's New Vision: Experiments in Seeing (2025), Tyler Mitchell: Idyllic Space (2024), Underexposed: Women Photographers from the Collection (2021) and Helen Levitt: In the Street (2015) — and has curated at Friche la Belle de Mai in Marseille, Emory University, Swan Coach House Gallery and Columbus State University. Beyond selecting the group show, the juror awards one artist a solo exhibition in the TWU East | West Galleries, scheduled during the following JEG Exhibition. Open-themed and open to photographers, lens-based or lensless, video and photo-related artists based in the United States; all photographic and related processes are encouraged, including digital, video, non-silver, photo and mixed media, and photographic book arts. Work must not exceed 60 inches in any dimension. Judging is online from JPEGs of at least 1200 pixels on the longest side and no more than 5 MB; one to five images cost $35 (Basic Application) and up to ten cost $60 (Expanded Application), with fees non-refundable and treated as donations to the JEG Endowment, which funds photography scholarships for TWU graduate students. Accepted artists are notified by December 9, 2026, pay their own shipping both ways, and must deliver ready-to-hang work (D-rings and wire, Plexiglas not glass) to the gallery by February 5, 2027. The organiser's own entry page gives the deadline as November 9 while its CaFÉ listing states entries close at midnight Central on November 8 — the earlier of the two is stored here. That entry page also still prints a link to the retired 2024 CaFÉ listing, which now loads an empty page; the live listing is the one linked here.
