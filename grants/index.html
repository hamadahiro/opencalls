Grants for Photographers & Visual Artists

Funding opportunities for photographers and visual artists. Project grants, production funds, and artist support programs — apply now.

Joan Wakelin Bursary 2026

£2,000 bursary for overseas documentary photo essays. RPS x The Guardian. Free entry, open worldwide. Named after Joan Wakelin.

21c Artadia Award 2026 — Northwest Arkansas

Unrestricted $15,000 for one visual artist in Bentonville/Washington Counties, AR — Artadia × 21c Museum Hotels. Free.

Fashion Regenerated 2026 · Textile Exchange

Textile Exchange and Atmos commission photographers documenting regeneration in fashion — $10,000 winning commission, free entry.

HCP Annual Fellowship Awards 2026 · Houston Center for Photography

HCP's annual fellowship: two photographers worldwide each win $3,000 plus a solo show. 2026 juror Natasha Egan (MoCP Chicago).

MUUS Collection Research Fellowship 2026

$20,000 grant for a curator or academic to develop an exhibition or publication using MUUS Collection's archives. Travel covered.

Kometa Fellows Program 2026

€10,000 fellowships for photographers, journalists and writers documenting war, censorship, and political tension. Free to apply.

Betty Bowen Award 2026 · Seattle Art Museum

Seattle Art Museum's annual award for WA/OR/ID artists: $20,000 plus a solo show at SAM, with five further cash awards. $10 entry.

Eamonn McCabe Bursary 2026 · Royal Photographic Society

£3,000 RPS bursary for photographers under 30 on 'sporting endeavour'. Grassroots, mental health, social issues welcomed. Free entry.

FUJIFILM GFX Challenge Grant Program 2026

$100K grant program: 5×$10,000 and 10×$5,000 for personal photo/video projects, plus GFX gear loan and mentoring.

£10,000 + Giles Duley mentorship for photojournalists 24 or under (or full-time photo students). Patron: Sir Don McCullin.

Liu Shiming Artist Grants 2026

Foundation grants of $5,000 to five early-career visual artists (2–10 years) for projects bridging tradition and contemporary practice.

Eurazeo Photo Prize 2026

Eurazeo's relaunched photography grant: a €20,000 award plus mentorship and industry connections to develop an ambitious long-term project.

Artadia Awards 2026 — Boston

Unrestricted $15,000 grants for contemporary visual artists in Greater Boston. Two-round jury with studio visits.

Up to $5,000 in emergency relief for US artists (photography included) facing unexpected medical costs. Free; closes September 15.

Barcelona's Can Basté forum: free grants — 3 × €2,000 exhibition production plus a €3,000 photobook. For Spain-based emerging artists.

Eidolon Grant 2026

€25,000 grant pool for research and projects on vernacular and everyday photography. Two categories: contemporary and historical.

Artadia Awards 2026 — Atlanta

Unrestricted $15,000 grants for contemporary visual artists in Greater Atlanta. Two-round jury with studio visits.

Unrestricted $15,000 grants for contemporary visual artists in Harris County, TX. Two-round jury with studio visits.
