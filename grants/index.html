Funding opportunities for photographers and visual artists. Project grants, production funds, and artist support programs — apply now.

Theme: Proximities. For photographers under 35. €25,000 prize plus solo exhibition at Triennale Milano.

Book prize for innovation in photography scholarship. Project grant for emerging scholars and curators.

Three $10,000 grants from Women Photograph + Leica for women, nonbinary, trans, intersex documentary photographers. Free, international.
