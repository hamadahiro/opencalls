Expressions West 2027

The 2027 edition of Coos Art Museum's annual regional juried exhibition, open to artists living in thirteen western states — Alaska, Arizona, California, Colorado, Hawaii, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, Oregon, Utah, Washington and Wyoming. Coos Art Museum is the only art museum on the Oregon Coast, housed in Coos Bay's 1936 Art Deco former post office, and Expressions West is its survey of what is being made across the American West — no theme, work judged on its own terms. Photography is an explicitly eligible medium alongside painting, drawing, hand-pulled prints, mixed media, fiber and textiles, small sculpture, glass, turned and carved wood, jewellery and furniture; prints must be framed and ready for hanging and no work may exceed 48 inches in any direction including the frame. Read the exclusions before you pay: digital artwork, AI artwork, video, film, audio and giclée prints are all ineligible, so an inkjet-printed photograph does not qualify and the call wants a photographic print made another way. The juror for 2027 is Daemian Hawthorn, an artist working across drawing, painting and welded metal sculpture. Awards are $1,000 for First Place, $750 for Second and $250 for Third. Up to three works may be submitted, with five images allowed per artwork; the fee is $35 for the first submission and $10 each for the second and third. The museum states the schedule in full: submissions close 9 December 2026, acceptance or rejection is notified on 22 December 2026, accepted artwork must reach the museum by 23 January 2027, the opening reception is 6 February 2027, and work is retrieved in person on 23–24 April 2027 or returned by courier on 24 April. Artwork is insured from arrival until return. Artists are responsible for getting work back: you may arrange and pay for your own return shipping, or ask the museum to coordinate it and be charged the shipping costs plus a $30 handling fee. Work made under an instructor's supervision is not eligible, and entries must be original in concept, composition and execution. Work must have been made in the last two years (2024–2026), must not have been shown at Coos Art Museum before, and if it is part of a series the entered piece has to be distinguishable from your others. Sales are the artist's own: the museum takes no commission and processes no transactions, so you set the price and deal with the buyer directly.
