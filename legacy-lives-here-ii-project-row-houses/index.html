Legacy Lives Here II

Prize Exhibition

Project Row Houses, the community arts organisation founded in 1993 in Houston's Third Ward, invites artists aged 60 and over to submit work for the second edition of Legacy Lives Here, a multimedia exhibition celebrating the artistic voices of elders, wisdom-keepers and culture-shapers. The organisers are looking for work that reflects personal histories, cultural memory and the ongoing act of passing down knowledge through art, and the show is open to a wide range of mediums. Submissions close 11:59pm on Friday 28 August 2026 and selected artists are notified by the first week of September. The exhibition is on view in PRH's Community Gallery from 17 September to 17 October 2026. Applications are made through SlideRoom and there is no entry fee.
