To the Sea

Prize Exhibition

The Griffin Museum wants photographs of water in all its moods for a satellite-gallery exhibition at the Jenks Center, 109 Skillings Road, Winchester, Massachusetts - one of the museum's three satellite galleries, open Monday to Friday 9am to 4pm. It is a physical show, not an online one - a wave breaking, the horizon at sunset, fog rolling over the harbour, a quiet cove, a storm coming in. The museum frames the theme around a pull that is older than any of us: summers at the beach, winter walks in the sand, storm waves against the shore, and the stories about what lies beneath the tideline that have filled imaginations for generations with monsters, demons and sirens just past what we can see. To the Sea is an invitation to capture that connection, whether from the shoreline, a pier, or simply watching the tide come in - every photograph a small piece of a much older story about why, no matter how far we wander, we always find our way back to the water's edge. The exhibition runs 13 October 2026 to 1 January 2027, the longest of the museum's current open calls. Entry is free, global and open to anyone. Submissions go through a Google Form; files must be sRGB JPGs sized between 1200 and 2000 pixels on the longest side and under 1.2 MB, or they are disqualified. Work is reviewed by the museum's in-house curatorial team with final selection by Executive Director Crista Dix. Recent Griffin open calls have drawn between 200 and 400 entries; selected artists get a social media package ahead of the opening. Copyright stays with the artist throughout.
