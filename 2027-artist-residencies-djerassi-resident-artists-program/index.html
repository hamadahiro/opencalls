2027 Artist Residencies

The Djerassi Resident Artists Program's 2027 season, on a 583-acre ranch in the Santa Cruz Mountains above Woodside, California, where the programme has given more than 2,700 writers, visual and media artists, composers, choreographers, filmmakers and scientists a month of time and space across 43 years — "all at no cost to the artists". Applicants pick one category per cycle, and Visual Arts is defined as "Painting, drawing, sculpture, photography, mixed media, and installation"; the programme's own 2027 details PDF credits its illustrations to "Film photography by Jessie Barr, 2025 Djerassi Program Alumnus". Five one-month sessions run from mid-February to mid-November 2027 — 16 February to 16 March, 30 March to 27 April, 22 June to 27 July (the Leonardo@Djerassi cohort of five artists and five scientists, with Leonardo/ISAST, which includes public forums, a feature in LEONARDO from MIT Press and open studios), 17 August to 14 September, and 19 October to 16 November — and the programme anticipates accommodating around 40 to 45 new artists. Each resident gets a private live/work studio with a workspace suited to their discipline, chef-prepared weeknight dinners with kitchens stocked for the rest, laundry, SFO pickup and return and local transport during the stay, and 583 acres of trails, redwoods and site-specific sculpture; artists pay their own way to the Bay Area. It is open to national and international applicants at every career stage, eligibility has recently been expanded to include degree-seeking students, alumni must wait five seasons before reapplying, and all materials must be in English. No accommodation is available for partners, children or pets, and residents commit to the whole session — there are no partial residencies. Apply through SlideRoom with a statement of interest, descriptions of two recent projects, a CV, one to five work samples (visual artists upload up to five JPEGs), your website and Instagram, and ranked session preferences. The application fee is $35. Staff screen for completeness, then a jury of artists and arts professionals scores merit of past work, readiness to engage with a month of uninterrupted time, and potential for impact; all applicants are tentatively notified by 31 December 2026. Applications opened 1 September and close 9 October 2026.
