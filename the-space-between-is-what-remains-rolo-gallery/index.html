The Space Between Is What Remains

Prize Exhibition

A national juried exhibition at Rolo Gallery, a contemporary space that opened in May 2026 on Denver's Art District on Santa Fe. The call asks for work that holds something — a mark, a quality of light, a feeling that doesn't leave — and is deliberately loose about interpretation: abstract, figurative or anywhere between, with no requirement that work announce its connection to the theme. Photography is welcome alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, mixed media, fibre, ceramics, works on paper and book arts. Open to US-based artists 18 and over at all career stages, with no exhibition history required. The show runs September 3 – October 11, 2026, spanning two First Friday art walks and a Third Friday reception on September 19.
