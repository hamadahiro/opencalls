2026 Alan Hagman Photojournalism Grant

A $6,000 grant for professional photojournalists authorized to work in the US or its territories, for underreported stories on human rights, the environment or other pressing social topics; a panel of photojournalism peers judges storytelling, sequencing and ethics. Payment is $5,000 on award and $1,000 on delivery of 25 captioned images within 18 months. The page's status chip reads Closed; its own prose and the live Picter portal run entries to 14 December.
