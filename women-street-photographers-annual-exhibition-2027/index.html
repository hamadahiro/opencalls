Women Street Photographers Annual Exhibition 2027

Prizes 4 × $1,000 Exhibition

Two things make this call worth the fee. The jury is genuinely top of the field — Kathy Ryan ran the picture desk at the New York Times Magazine for 39 years and Sarah Meister runs Aperture — and the review is blind, so the name attached to the file does not travel with it into the judging.

The second is that the organiser pays for the print. Seventy finalists are hung at Artspace PS109 in East Harlem and the printing and framing are covered, which removes the cost that usually turns a selection into an expense. Four of the seventy also take $1,000 from Leica.

It is single images, not a series: street photography, street portraiture or abstract street work made in public since September 2020, shot on film, digital or a phone. Composites and multiple exposures are explicitly out, along with AI-generated images, so this is a call for the frame as taken.

On the money: $15 per photo, 25 photos to an entry, and a bonus rung that makes five of them free once you submit twenty or more — below that it adds up fast. The organiser also publishes a waiver, up to two free entries for anyone who cannot afford the fee (New York City residents excluded) or cannot pay by card under US embargo rules, and asks you to email for the form. That is a real door, not a formality, and it is worth using before paying for a large batch.
