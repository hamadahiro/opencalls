Monochrome 2027

No subject brief at all — the only condition is the tonality. "Any subject matter will be considered as long as the image is monochrome, whether that be black and white photography, cyanotypes, or even sepia; all will be considered." That sentence is doing real work: it is one of the few open calls at this price point that names cyanotype in the same breath as black-and-white, so alternative-process photographers are not reading themselves into a digital-print show by accident. The gallery's own framing is that monochrome strips the distractions and leaves "the light, the shadow and the subject within the image".

It is a returning show the gallery describes as one of its most popular, and one of two April 2027 exhibitions. The money is the house series': submitting is free, selection happens first, and only photographers who are selected pay the £50 participation fee. That covers printing and installation — 16"x12", 18"x12" or 12"x12" prints in frames the gallery owns — and there is a discount if you are selected for both of the month's shows. The other April show is Boundaries 2027, same deadline.

Selected work goes on the wall, into an online gallery on the gallery's site, and into the exhibition catalogue; participants also get a digital certificate, photographs and video of the work in situ for the gallery's channels, and automatic entry for Photographer of the Month. Submission is 1 to 8 JPEGs in sRGB at 300ppi and at least 3000 pixels on the shortest side, sent together via Smash, WeTransfer or Dropbox — not as email attachments, which the gallery says compresses them, and not one at a time.

Submissions close 20 November 2026, acceptances and reserve-list places are out by 25 November, and selected photographers pay by 9 December. Only the selected are contacted, and the gallery asks you to check your spam folder — so no email by 25 November is the result.
