Portals

Prizes $1,300 in cash awards Exhibition

Collage Artists of America, a Los Angeles society founded in 1988, opens its annual juried exhibition to members and non-members alike on the theme of portals. The organisers ask you to explore what defines a portal and to imagine one that resonates: an elaborate doorway leading to the known or the unknown, an opening into other universes or into the body or mind, any entrance to something real or imagined. Four kinds of work qualify — collage (flat or very low relief), mixed-media collage (moderate relief and/or different techniques and materials), assemblage (highly dimensional, multiple three-dimensional elements and/or freestanding) and digital collage, which the organisers define as a combination of multiple images and explicitly distinguish from digital manipulation of a single image, which does not qualify. For photographers that is the boundary to note: the prospectus never uses the word photograph in either direction, so the way in is the digital-collage definition itself - a photomontage is a combination of multiple images and qualifies, while a single unaltered frame does not. Nothing on any surface bars photographic material, and because the show is virtual there is no print or framing rule to fall foul of. The juror is Karin Skiba, who took her MFA at Claremont Graduate University, taught as a full-time art professor at Norco College and retired as Professor Emeritus, founded and directed the Norco College Art Gallery, was the inaugural director of the Hi-Desert Art Gallery and has curated at Joshua Tree's Juniper Arts Gallery. Awards total $1,300: $500 first place, $300 second, $200 third, a $100 Carolann Watterson Memorial Award and up to four honourable mentions of $50 each. Entry is $40 for one work and $5 for each of up to three additional works for non-members, or $35 for up to four works for members; membership costs $55 and the entry maximum is four works either way. Work shown at any prior juried CAA exhibit is ineligible, as is work made in an instructor-directed, step-by-step class or workshop, and artificially generated artwork is not accepted. The exhibition is virtual only, hosted on the CAA website and on OnlineJuriedShows, and runs 1 November to 31 December 2026 with an online reception and awards on 1 November. CAA takes no commission on sales. Entries close 1 October 2026 at 11:59 PT and the jury's decisions are announced on 12 October.
