FRESH AF

Covet Art Gallery, a storefront in Oceanside north of San Diego, opens 2027 with a juried group show for the fresh, funky and unexpected. All styles and mediums are welcome, photography included, at any career level, and the call is open internationally. Entry is $25 plus $5 per additional image, up to twenty; sales split 50/50 and the show runs 16 January to 20 February 2027.
