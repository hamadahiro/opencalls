2026 Members Show

Prize Exhibition on Blue Sky's Community Walls

Blue Sky, Oregon Center for the Photographic Arts brings its annual Members Show back in person for a second year. The top fifty selected works go up on the gallery's Community Walls in Portland, with a broader selection of submitted work shown on the Blue Sky website. There is no theme: the gallery asks only for your best work. Any Blue Sky member in current good standing may submit up to three high-resolution images, one submission per artist, with an optional artist statement of up to 100 words about your practice. Membership is not a closed gate — the call page links straight to Blue Sky's join page, and the $50 Champion tier lists eligibility to submit to the Annual Members Exhibition among its benefits, so any photographer can become eligible by joining. The exhibition opens Thursday, October 1 at 5 PM. Blue Sky is a non-profit founded in 1975 by a group of five young photographers as the Oregon Center for the Photographic Arts, now in its fiftieth-anniversary year, and the Members Show is the point in its calendar where the membership itself gets the walls.
