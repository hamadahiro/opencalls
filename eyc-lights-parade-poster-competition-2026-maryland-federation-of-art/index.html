EYC Lights Parade Poster Competition 2026

Prize $2,500

Every year on the second Saturday of December the Annapolis harbour fills with boats strung with thousands of lights for the Eastport Yacht Club Lights Parade, and Maryland Federation of Art runs the competition that chooses the image the club uses to advertise it. Original two-dimensional work in any medium is welcome, and the brief is specific: the work must depict an Annapolis landmark or the Annapolis skyline, include one or more festively decorated boats, and incorporate the EYC burgee, a flag image downloadable from the entry page. Past winners are posted at eastportyc.org/lights-parade/artists. The winning piece is selected by a committee from Eastport Yacht Club, not by an MFA juror, and becomes the official image of the 2026 parade, reproduced on posters, note cards and other promotional material; EYC awards the chosen artist $2,500 and contributes up to $125 towards framing. Read the rights clause before entering: by submitting, an artist agrees that if their piece is chosen they grant EYC an irrevocable licence to use it and copies of it on various media in connection with promoting the Lights Parade — the clause as printed still names 2024 — and in future years, and are deemed to have gifted the original work to EYC, a 501(c)(7) organisation. Every entry is shown in MFA's Online Gallery from a reveal party in November through 31 December 2026, People's Choice voting closes at midnight on 16 December and the People's Choice winner, announced at 5 PM on 17 December, receives a $70 one-year MFA membership. Entry is open to all artists residing in any state or territory of the United States and costs $10 for one or two works; up to two original works made in the past three years may be entered, work previously shown in MFA's People's Choice Voting Gallery is not eligible, and work made with image-generative AI is not accepted. Because the winner's physical piece changes hands, size matters: the work may not be smaller than 24 inches in one direction by 30 inches in the other, and no dimension may exceed 36 inches including the frame. Artists keep 80 per cent of online sales, 85 per cent for MFA members, and MFA reimburses up to $40 of shipping on those sales for artists in the 48 contiguous states. The winner announcement date is listed only as TBA.
