Arts Retreat Residency

Prize Free 2–6 week residency

A rural arts organisation in west-central Minnesota, founded as the New York Mills Arts Retreat in 1990 and expanded into a regional cultural center in 1992, hosts one artist at a time in a one-bedroom bungalow three blocks south of downtown, with studio access at the Cultural Center. Housing and studio use cost nothing and the application is free. Residencies run two to six weeks. There are two deadlines a year — April 1 for July to December stays and October 1 for January to June — and applications are accepted year round. The centre welcomes artists from all disciplines, naming performance, literary, audio and visual media, and selects on artistic merit, dedication to their craft, commitment to creative growth and interest in developing community through the arts. No stipend is offered and residents provide their own transportation and meals; the house deliberately has no television, computer or car, and staff or community members will collect artists from Perham or Wadena. Each resident creates and leads one community outreach programme — a workshop, reading, performance or other interactive event at the Cultural Center — and can develop it with Artistic Director Megan Shirley-Ross or partner with the local schools, senior center or library. Applicants are notified within 30 to 45 days after the deadline. Note that the previous round was extended from October 1 to October 15, so the window may move again, but October 1 is the published date.
