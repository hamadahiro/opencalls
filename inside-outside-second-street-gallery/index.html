INSIDE / OUTSIDE

Second Street Gallery, founded in 1973 and the oldest nonprofit contemporary art space in Central Virginia, is running a juried regional exhibition for artists living and working in the Commonwealth of Virginia. INSIDE / OUTSIDE asks for work that sits on the line between interior and exterior worlds — home and environment, personal space and public life, private memory and shared history. The gallery invites broad interpretation: Virginia's natural landscape, the built environment, domestic interiors, social and cultural histories, or the psychological boundaries between inside and out. The juror is Kristen Chiacchia, Second Street's Executive Director and Chief Curator, an art historian whose curatorial practice centres on gender bias in the contemporary art market and on artists' ties to subcultural movements; she has curated more than 67 solo shows and 34 group exhibitions at the gallery and published nine catalogues. First place takes $300 plus a solo exhibition in the gallery's Dové space from 2 June to 21 July 2028; second is $200 and third is $100, with up to three honourable mentions at the juror's discretion. Accepted media are painting, works on paper, sculpture, photography and mixed media — no video, installation, performance, sound or AI-generated work. Work must have been made in the last three years and must not have been shown in Charlottesville in the preceding year. Applications open 15 August 2026 and close 15 November; artists are notified 15 January 2027, work is delivered in May, and the show runs 4 June to 16 July 2027 with a First Friday opening. All exhibited work must be available for purchase and the gallery retains a 50% commission.
