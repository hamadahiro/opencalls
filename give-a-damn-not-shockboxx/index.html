Give a Damn

Prize Exhibition

Not Shockboxx has a house voice and it is all over this call. Give a Damn is the gallery's holiday group exhibition, pitched squarely against the gift economy it sits inside: the ask is for work that someone would actually want to receive, instead of "another scented candle, novelty mug, or panic-purchased gift set". The concept, in the gallery's own words, is simple - give art, support artists, and stop buying people things they will quietly donate in February.

What that means in practice is wide. The gallery wants original work that is thoughtful, strange, beautiful, funny, heartfelt, wonderfully unnecessary, or simply too good to have come from a big-box store, and it pre-empts the obvious misreading: giftable does not have to mean festive, tiny, beige, or acceptable to your mother-in-law. Artwork does not need to be holiday-themed at all - the gallery says it would prefer it didn't be. Submit something you already made, or use the theme as an excuse.

Photography is named outright in the eligible media, with small works, prints, editions, fiber art, sculpture, ceramics and mixed media. The one firm limit is commercial rather than formal: because this is a gift-focused show, every piece must be available for purchase and priced at $200 or less. AI-generated work is excluded twice over - once in the rules as a creation clause, once again at the end as "Absolutely no Ai produced works."

This is a room, not just a web page. The exhibition runs November 20 to 29, 2026 at the gallery in Hermosa Beach, and it is also featured during the Bad Bitch Bazaar on November 28, which the gallery expects to bring more holiday shoppers through. Accepted work must arrive ready to display, with 2D pieces wired or otherwise prepared for installation, and artists are responsible for delivering and retrieving their own work on the schedule given to participants. In-person sales are split 50/50. Listing on Artsy is optional; work sold there splits 40% to the artist, 40% to the gallery and 20% to Artsy.

Eligibility is as open as the brief: all ages, all experience levels, all backgrounds and styles, with emerging artists, students and first-time exhibitors explicitly encouraged, and artists under 18 able to participate with a parent or legal guardian involved. No juror is named and the gallery states plainly that submission does not guarantee acceptance. Entry is $20 per artwork, the deadline is October 31, 2026, and applications go through the gallery's own apply page, which requires a free artist account.
