International Photography Exhibition 168 (IPE 168)

Prizes £1,000 IPE Award £1,000 Under 30s Award Exhibition Publication

The Royal Photographic Society's International Photography Exhibition has been held almost every year since 1854 and calls itself the world's longest-running contemporary photography exhibition; the 168th edition opened for entries on 15 September 2026 and closes at 23:59 GMT on Tuesday 15 December 2026, with an extra day to 16 December to finalise entries already started. Anyone of any age or level of practice, anywhere in the world, can enter, and RPS membership is not required. There is no theme and no categories: the T&Cs say there is no restriction on genre, process or technique, that film and digital, experimental and traditional processes are all eligible, that manipulation in software or the darkroom is allowed, and that found photographs may be incorporated - but work that includes generative AI in whole or in part is not eligible. Every entrant gets one free single-image entry; further entries, each of four images from one series or four single images, cost £35 (£27 for RPS members, £20 for under-30s), and multiple paid entries are allowed, with an early-bird prize draw for entries in by 19 October. The RPS longlists, a changing guest selection panel shortlists, and shortlisted photographers are then asked to send prints for a final anonymous selection of about 100 works, which the RPS mounts and frames at its own cost for a touring exhibition at partner venues; the RPS would not normally exhibit work larger than 50 x 60 cm. Awards are the £1,000 IPE Award and the £1,000 Under 30s Award (each with a year's RPS membership), a two-week solo exhibition at an RPS partner venue, four RPS Journal features chosen by the editor, five Selectors' Spotlight statements, and digital and social-media exposure for shortlisted work. Copyright stays with the photographer; the RPS takes a four-year promotional licence. The selection panel had not been named on the RPS site at the time of listing, and no notification date is published.
