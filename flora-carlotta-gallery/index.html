Flora — Carlotta Gallery

Prize Exhibition prize

Carlotta Gallery is an online photography gallery that runs themed virtual exhibitions on a regular schedule. Flora is the gallery's plant-life-focused cycle — work engaging with the quiet intensity of plant life, from delicate details through expansive landscapes of growth.

The fee structure is staged: free to submit, with a £20–£35 fee charged only if work is accepted for the exhibition. The model lets photographers test fit without upfront cost.

Selected work is shown in a virtual exhibition with a catalogue and a participation certificate. The exhibition format is online-only — there is no physical gallery component.

Eligibility is open to photographers 18+ globally. Submission is by email with up to 3 images. The 2026 deadline was April 25.
