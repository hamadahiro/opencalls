Exhibition Program Open Call 2028–2029

Prizes Exhibition Cash honorarium

The New York Art Residency and Studios (NARS) Foundation invites proposals for solo and group exhibitions in 2028 and 2029, for its Main Gallery on the fourth floor and the smaller Project Space on the second, both set among the artists' studios of an industrial building in Sunset Park, Brooklyn. NARS presents 12 to 13 exhibitions a year and reserves both spaces for early-career or underrepresented artists and curators chosen through this annual open call, which is open to new applicants and residency alumni alike. Artists, curators, collectives and cultural producers may propose shows of four to five weeks and are encouraged to activate the space with performances, artist talks and other events; selected applicants are invited to develop their proposals further with NARS providing space, support and resources, and a small honorarium is paid to the selected artist or curator at the end of the exhibition. The applicant - artist or curator - must be US-based, though a US-based curator may include international artists in a group proposal of three to five artists, and proposed artists must have confirmed their interest before the application is submitted. Applicants enrolled in a degree-granting art programme are not eligible, and anyone who has previously exhibited at NARS must wait two full years. One proposal per applicant per open call. NARS is a 501(c)(3) supported in part by public funds from the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs; it cannot cover shipping, and artists should expect to install most of the work themselves. Applications ran 31 August to 21 September 2026, 11:59pm ET; decisions follow three to four weeks later. Materials: a resume, a 500-word proposal and up to ten work samples in image, text, audio or video.
