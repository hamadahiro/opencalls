SPIE International Day of Light Photo Contest 2026

Prizes $2,500 $1,000 Best of Science Publication

SPIE — the international society for optics and photonics, based in Bellingham, Washington — runs this contest each year in support of the International Day of Light, with the stated aim of raising public awareness of the role light and light-based technology play in daily life. It is free to enter and open to amateur and professional photographers alike, aged 18 and over, anywhere in the world.

The theme is broad within its subject: images that depict the properties of light and how it interacts with the atmosphere, nature and materials; light technology such as lasers and LEDs; pictures created with optical instruments like telescopes and microscopes; people interacting with light or light technology; and light in the betterment of the human condition. Judging weighs content, scientific interest, creativity and artistry, execution of photographic technique, and overall appeal for the International Day of Light — a mix that rewards work which is scientifically engaged and visually resolved at once.

Each person may submit a maximum of two entries, one photo per form. Every entry needs a title and an artist statement giving the subject, the location and date, the equipment used, any adjustments made and how the image ties to the theme. Files must be TIF or JPEG, 300dpi or more, at least 3,000 pixels on the longest side and between 5MB and 20MB. New for 2026, photographs must have been taken within the past two years, after 16 May 2024, and re-submissions are not considered.

Only limited enhancement for brightness, contrast and minor cosmetic flaws is allowed; black and white is fine; watermarks are not acceptable; and artificial intelligence may not be used to generate images or create composites, with judges disqualifying anything that appears to have used AI beyond simple adjustments.

Prizes are $2,500 for first place, $1,000 for second, $500 for third, and a $1,000 Judges' Choice for Best of Science. Winning photographs are also published across SPIE publications, online and in print, and displayed at SPIE conferences and exhibitions, with credit to the photographer. One prize per participant. Entries close 16 September 2026 at 11:59pm PST; winners are notified 16 October and announced 30 October 2026. Note that prize money cannot be awarded to individuals located in countries under US sanctions, though entrants from those countries are welcome to compete for recognition.
