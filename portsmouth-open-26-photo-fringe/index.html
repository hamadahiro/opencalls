Portsmouth Open 26

Prizes Exhibition Mentorship

A new curated group exhibition at Art Space, Portsmouth, staged as part of Photo Fringe 2026 by the volunteer-led Brighton photography charity behind the biennial open-platform festival. The show reads archives as feedback loops — how photographs, documents, objects, memories and stories are continually revisited, reinterpreted and reshaped — and looks for artists whose practice engages with personal, family, community, institutional, cultural, historical, digital or found archives, or that questions what constitutes an archive at all. Rephotography, appropriation, collage, installation, moving image, sound and text are all welcomed, and existing or new work may be submitted provided the relationship to the curatorial premise is clear. Open to photographers and lens-based artists working in any medium; solo artists and collectives may both apply. There are no submission fees. Apply by email with up to 300 words — or a BSL, English audio or video application of no more than three minutes — up to 12 JPEGs (72 dpi, each under 3MB) or a screener link for moving image, and a website or Instagram link. Selected artists print and provide their own work with suitable hanging options, install and de-install with support from the Photo Fringe team, and contribute to the invigilation timetable; Photo Fringe takes 35% commission on sales to help cover staffing, and travel, transport and accommodation cannot be reimbursed unless otherwise agreed. Applications are assessed by Photo Fringe producer Ricardo Reverón Blanco, who also selects one artist from the exhibiting cohort for a jury award of mentorship plus an in-depth written interview published on the Photo Fringe website. Entries close 8 September 2026, the shortlist is notified 15 September, the private view is 23 October and the exhibition runs 24–25 October 2026, during the 2–31 October festival.
