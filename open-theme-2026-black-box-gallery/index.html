Open Theme: 2026

Prizes Exhibition Catalog

Black Box runs an open-theme show roughly once a cycle, and it is the one in the rotation with no subject to answer — the gallery's own list of what fits runs from fashion to night photography, which is as close to "anything" as a themed programme gets.

The economics are the reason this gallery keeps reappearing here rather than on the pay-to-hang pile: $40 covers five images, and if the juror takes one, the gallery prints, mats and frames it at its own cost. The print then sits in the flat files for two years as promotion, and the photographer keeps every right to the file and the print.

A caution on the fee shape: $40 buys an entry of up to five photographs, not five entries. The sibling Book Project calls on the same site use a different window (5–10 images, with five as a stated minimum) — the two formats are not interchangeable.
