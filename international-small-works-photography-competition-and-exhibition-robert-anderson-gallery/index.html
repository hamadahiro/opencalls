International Small Works Photography Competition and Exhibition

International juried competition at the Robert Anderson Gallery in Denver, Colorado, celebrating the intimacy of small photographic images — work whose longest dimension, including frame, does not exceed 15 inches. Open to photographers worldwide aged 18+, with no restriction on subject matter or technique; digital, film, alternative, and mixed-media work where photography is central are all eligible. Juried by photographer Wendi Schneider and master printer Ron Landucci. Exhibition October 2 – December 22, 2026.
