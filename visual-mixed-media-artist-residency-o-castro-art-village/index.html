Visual / Mixed-Media Artist Residency

Prizes Funded residency €30/day stipend

A 30-day funded residency (October 2026) in the Ribeira Sacra, Galicia, explicitly welcoming photography alongside video, installation and land art within hybrid visual practices. Residents stay in on-site geodesic domes and receive a travel allowance, daily stipend, meals and mentorship, with accessibility support available on request. Open to legal residents of European countries other than Spain (proof of residency required).
