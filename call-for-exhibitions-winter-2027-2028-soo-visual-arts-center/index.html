Call for Exhibitions — Winter 2027–2028

Prize Exhibition

Soo Visual Arts Center is a Minneapolis nonprofit art space founded in 2001 that has exhibited more than 3,900 under-represented artists, with a stated focus on emerging artists and on work that challenges and expands conventional notions of aesthetics. Its open call fills the winter 2027–2028 gallery season and is open to individual artists, collaborative teams and curatorial proposals at any career stage, in any form of visual art. No exhibition proposal is required — shows are awarded on the merits of an artist’s previous work — and while group exhibitions are considered, the panel’s focus is on solo exhibitions, which makes this one of the more substantial free opportunities on the calendar for a photographer with a finished body of work. The curatorial panel is Sophia Abrams, a filmmaker and curator who has worked with the Brooklyn Museum and the Studio Museum in Harlem; Dahn Gim, a UCLA MFA and 2024–2026 McKnight Visual Artist Fellow; Alison Hiltner, SooVAC’s associate director; and Xavier Tavera, a photographer working between documentary and the imagined on the Latin American diaspora, whose work is in the collections of the Minneapolis Institute of Art and the Minnesota Museum of American Art. Applications ask for exactly ten work samples — images, or video clips of up to two minutes each, or a combination — with a full image list, a one-page artist statement or proposal and a résumé; the gallery is explicit that incomplete or incorrectly formatted submissions will not be considered. Entry is free. Artists selected for an exhibition cover shipping to and from the gallery, and a modest stipend is provided.
