Martin House Creative Residency 2027

The Martin House Creative Residency Program provides artists and researchers a thought-provoking environment to produce new works inspired by one of the great examples of 20th century American architecture. The 1.5-acre Frank Lloyd Wright-designed campus in Buffalo, New York serves as the creative setting. The program accepts creative makers from diverse backgrounds across multiple disciplines including architecture, design, visual arts, literature, music, dance, theater, and film. Two tracks are available: the Artist Program for developing creative works relating to Martin House, and the Researcher Program for writers, scholars, and critics conducting publishable research. Residents spend 2–4 weeks onsite, either consecutively or incrementally, between June and October 2027. Residents receive a $5,000 stipend (50% upon arrival, 50% upon completion), up to $1,000 in travel support for non-local residents, and private accommodations in the historic Gardener's Cottage. Each resident delivers a free public program to share their Martin House-inspired work. Applicants must have US work authorization (social security number required; includes permanent residents and OPT holders). The program cannot assist with visa acquisition.
