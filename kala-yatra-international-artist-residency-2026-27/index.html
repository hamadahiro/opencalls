Kala Yatra International Artist Residency 2026/27

Year-round residency in the Kathmandu valley with Kala Yatra (कला यात्रा, “Art Journey”), an artist-run not-for-profit collective that curates concept-based exhibitions, socially relevant projects and residencies alongside workshops and educational outreach. The collective says residents “will benefit from a supportive art community and valuable resources” and that it prioritises “not only your artistic endeavors but also the relationships and collaborations that may emerge”, with the chance to explore Nepal's contemporary art scene, cultural diversity and geography. The residency offers networking opportunities and presentations at Kala Yatra and partner institutions, and says it can facilitate exhibitions or help residents navigate galleries and studios. Accommodation is an individual bedroom with linens, private or shared bathroom depending on the booking, a shared kitchen, a common working space, solar hot water, wi-fi (bring your own laptop), weekly housekeeping and a digital guidebook; airport pick-up and drop-off are available at extra cost. THE ARTIST PAYS: “We run an artist-led, self-funded residency and currently have no available funding. To cover administrative and utility expenses, we charge a minimal fee based on the duration of your stay” - €200 for two weeks, €400 for a month, and a reduced €300 a month for artists who are both residents and citizens of the SAARC countries (Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka). There is no application fee and applications are accepted all year, by email: artist statement, CV or bio with a portfolio or website link, up to ten recent works in a single PDF, a copy of a valid passport and the collective's application form. Stays start at a minimum of two weeks and the collective asks artists to apply at least three months ahead because slots fill; the residency fee covers none of the airfare, visa fees, health insurance, local transport, food, materials or project costs, and airport pick-up is €25 on pre-booking. The programme is “open to, but not limited to” visual artists, painters, sculptors, ceramists, printmakers, performing artists, musicians, writers, new media artists, curators, filmmakers, theatre artists, architects, art critics and researchers, and invites anyone whose practice is not on that list to write in; a current resident is credited on the residency's own page as a “Visual Artist, Writer & Photographer”.
