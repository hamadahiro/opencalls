People 2026

Prize Exhibition prize

Grid Photo Gallery is an online gallery that runs themed photography calls on a recurring schedule. People is the gallery's call for work engaging with the human figure across all photographic approaches.

The brief is open in approach: portraits, documentary, street photography, and studio work all eligible. Diverse interpretations welcome — posed or unposed, observed or imagined. The unifying thread is the human as subject.

Selected work is shown in an online exhibition on the Grid Photo Gallery site.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the gallery's website with unlimited images (fee per image). Fee is €7 per image. The 2026 deadline was March 31; results announced May 1, 2026.
