Photodarium 2028

Prize Publication (print)

PHOTODARIUM is a tear-off calendar that reveals a new instant photo every day, each with its own little story: the front of each calendar page carries the photograph, the back a short account of how it came about together with the photographer's name, location and URL. Slanted Publishers in Karlsruhe edits the series with Raban Ruddigkeit, and Seltmann Publishers issues it; Boris Kahl is art director and project manager. Submissions must be original scans of instant photos made with a Polaroid SX-70, a 600 or a new Impossible/Polaroid camera — the editors say plainly that they do not accept fakes. Entrants pick up to five favourite calendar days and upload one photo for each, and three photos by different photographers end up competing for any one day. The back-of-page text is treated as part of the submission and will be printed, so the editors warn that a dummy description gets the whole entry refused. Files should be JPG, RGB, 350 dpi at the original size of 88 x 107 mm including the frame. Entry is free; contributors grant Slanted and Seltmann a non-exclusive right to use the photos for the calendars and their promotion, receive a free copy of the calendar if published and a 50 percent discount on further copies, and no financial compensation is paid. The call for entries opens on 1 September 2026 and closes on 25 September 2026, with production and distribution in 2027.
