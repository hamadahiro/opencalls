43rd Five State Photography Competition & Exhibition

Forty-three years is a long time for a regional photography competition to keep going, and the Five State has the numbers to show it is not ceremonial: the 42nd edition drew 648 photographs, of which juror John Finch selected 150 for the walls and distributed $1,500 in cash awards. It is the anchor of the Hays Arts Council's Winter Art Walk and opens to the public each December, running into the new year. Eligibility is geographic rather than by career stage — any photographer living in Kansas, Nebraska, Missouri, Oklahoma or Colorado — which makes it one of the few substantial shows aimed squarely at photographers in the middle of the country.

Any photographic process and any subject is accepted, but each photograph must be entered into one of three named categories — Nature, People or Open — and is judged within it, so the category choice matters. Work must have been made within the last three years and must not have appeared in a previous Five State exhibition. The 43rd edition's juror is Elise Kirk, Associate Professor of Photography and chair of the Department of Visual Art at the University of Kansas, a RISD photography MFA whose own work concerns human nature in an ecological world; she selects 100 photographs and awards $1,500 in cash across the three categories, with awards explicitly not separated by photographic process. In the 42nd edition that money ran $200 / $125 / $100 / $75 in each of the three categories, plus a dozen juror's merit awards.

The specifications are tighter than most calls and worth reading twice: JPEG only, under 30 MB, at least 1,200 pixels on the long side, and a 4:5 aspect ratio, because accepted photographs are printed by the photographer at 8 × 10 inches and the council then mats and frames them itself in 11 × 14 inch black wood frames. White or black borders are allowed. Entry is $25 covering photographs one and two, then $7.50 each for a third and fourth, payable by card or by cheque to the Hays Arts Council. Every exhibited print is priced at $100 including the frame, of which the artist receives $60, unless you mark it NFS. Entries and payments are due 23 October 2026, acceptances go out by 4 November, accepted prints must reach Hays by 20 November, and the show runs 5 December 2026 to 31 January 2027. The council’s own Five State page, which carried nothing but the 42nd edition’s results when this entry was written, now leads with this cycle: "SUBMISSIONS NOW OPEN / Deadline: October 23rd. / 43rd Annual Five State Photography Competition & Exhibition 2026 Juror, Elise Kirk", followed by a Submit Online Here link to the CaFÉ prospectus where entries are filed. The 42nd edition’s results and award list still sit below it on the same page.
