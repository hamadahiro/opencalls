Signed, Sealed, Delivered: A Postcard Exhibition & Fundraiser

Prize Exhibition

A free, open postcard show at the Albrecht-Kemper Museum of Art in Saint Joseph, Missouri: artists make original 4 × 6 inch postcard artworks, up to five each, in any medium — so photographic prints, contact prints, cyanotypes and other camera-made work all qualify. Be clear about the bargain before entering: this is a fundraiser. Every postcard is exhibited and then sold for $10 with the proceeds going to the museum, the work is not returned to the artist, and there is no prize. What a photographer gets in exchange is a museum wall from late October through 3 January and a buyer who sees their name on the back — the museum asks that the artist's name go on the reverse only, with accompanying information such as a social media handle or website, so that anyone who buys a card can find more of the work. There is no entry fee and no jurying is announced. Postcards are mailed or dropped off to the museum at 2818 Frederick Avenue, Saint Joseph, MO 64506, marked ATTN: Postcard Exhibition, by 23 October 2026.
