Lenscratch Still Life Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

Lenscratch is one of the longest-running photography blogs and online platforms, with a regular schedule of juried online exhibitions accompanying its daily editorial coverage. The Still Life Exhibition is the platform's open call for photography working within the still life tradition — from constructed tableaux and studies of everyday objects to contemporary work that uses the language of still life to explore memory, symbolism, identity, and place.

The exhibition is juried by Kimberly Witham, a New Jersey-based photographer and educator known for still life work drawing on botanicals, found objects, and the natural world. Selected images publish in an online group exhibition on Lenscratch on July 31, 2026.

Submissions are free, with one image accepted per photographer, sized to 72dpi at 1000 pixels on the long side. Caption format follows Lenscratch's standard: copyright, name, image title, Instagram handle, and website. The deadline is July 19, 2026.
