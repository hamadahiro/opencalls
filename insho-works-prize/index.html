Insho Works Prize

Prizes $300 / $200 / $100 Online publication feature

Insho is an independent online platform for short visual essays — three to twelve slides, read in under two minutes — and its curated Works section is running a free prize in two categories, Writing and Art. The Art category names photography alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics and other visual work, so a photo sequence or a small project built as a slide piece is exactly the format. There is no entry fee, and one piece may be entered in each category. Entrants build the work on the platform with a free account, each slide holding up to 600 characters and about 1,200 words across the piece, choose the Art category and submit to Works by 11:59 pm Eastern Time on 30 December 2026; submissions are reviewed before publishing and Works is curated, so not everything is accepted. Entry is open to anyone 18 or older, anywhere PayPal can send payments. The editor of Insho selects ten finalists in each category and the top three in each, judging on originality, craft and how well the piece uses the slide format; all finalists are featured on the platform, winners are announced in January 2027 and prizes are paid by PayPal within thirty days. Prizes are $300 for first place, $200 for second and $100 for third in each category. Work must be the entrant's own with rights to every element in it, and work published elsewhere before is welcome; AI-assisted editing tools are allowed but must be declared in the Credits field, and work generated entirely by AI is not eligible. Entrants keep ownership and grant Insho permission to feature the piece on the site and share it on the platform's social accounts with credit. The platform's guidelines also bar thin teasers, advertising and links to shops or ticketing pages, and ask that each piece stand on its own.
