DeHaan Artist of Distinction Award 2026 — Round 2

Funded entirely by a legacy gift from the Christel DeHaan Family Foundation and administered by the Indy Arts Council, the DeHaan Artist of Distinction Award honours up to five contemporary visual artists a year with project grants of up to $10,000 each. Photography is named in the list of eligible art forms alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, ceramics, textiles and digital or new media. The programme is built for risk: it funds aspirational, innovative projects that push an artist's practice in new directions, and applicants are asked to make the case that their work brings something new to the field. Applicants must have lived or worked in Indiana for at least three consecutive years at the date of application, must remain in the state for a year after the award activities conclude, must show five years of documented experience through solo exhibitions, national exposure, critical reviews, residencies, fellowships or juried shows, and must have generated consistent income from their work in visual art. Projects must conclude within twelve months of the signed agreement, and awardees take part in a professionally curated group exhibition at Gallery 924 at the Indy Arts Council and submit a final report documenting the project's impact. There are two rounds a year; Round 1 closed on 3 May 2026 and Round 2 closes on 18 October 2026. Two things a Round 2 applicant should weigh: the guidelines head this round "Round 2 (subject to availability of awards)", and the council has already named three 2026 DeHaan Artists of Distinction — Hazel Frost, Toby Kaufmann-Buhler and Kay Westhues — so at most two of the five annual awards are left to compete for, and the programme reserves the right to award none. Round 2 notification is December 2026, and the guidelines are internally inconsistent about the show: the conditions-of-award list puts the Gallery 924 group exhibition "tentatively for October/November 2026" while the programme schedule gives Round 2 a Fall 2027 exhibition. Applications are free and go through the council's grant platform, and alternative formats such as dictated or video responses can be arranged.
