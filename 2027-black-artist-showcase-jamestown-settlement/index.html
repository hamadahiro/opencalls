2027 Black Artist Showcase

Jamestown Settlement — the Jamestown-Yorktown Foundation's living-history museum outside Williamsburg — is working with adult Virginia artists in any medium to fill its 2027 Black Artist Showcase, on view 30 January to 28 March 2027 along the Great Hall passageways leading to the museum galleries. The theme is "Founders and Foundations", and the museum's own listing names photography among the eligible media alongside painting, drawing, printmaking and graphic arts.

Entry is free. Up to three works are submitted through the form on the museum's call page, each with title, medium, accurate framed dimensions and a reference image (JPEG or PNG, 5 MB maximum). Submissions close at 5 p.m. EST on Wednesday 4 November 2026 and selections are announced by 16 November 2026.

Selected artists sign a Memorandum of Agreement with the Foundation and return a Virginia substitute W-9 with a handwritten signature by 30 December 2026 — the Foundation pays a small honorarium at the end of the showcase, and missing that deadline means the work cannot be exhibited. Artwork is delivered by appointment to Jamestown Settlement between 11 and 24 January 2027 and must be exhibition-ready on arrival: works on paper glazed and framed, hardware installed, D-rings preferred over wire. The Foundation will not prepare, mount, frame or install hardware, and work that is not exhibit-ready is eliminated from the showcase.

Showcase artists are invited to the "After Angelo" event on 20 February 2027, where they may reserve a vendor table and sell original work, and to the "Last Looks" reception on 27 March 2027. Artwork is collected 3–10 April 2027.
