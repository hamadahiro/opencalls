2028 Solo Exhibit Series

Prize Solo exhibition

Open call for solo exhibitions at Pennswood Art Gallery in Newtown, Pennsylvania, which has shown the work of nationally and regionally prominent artists and artisans for more than forty years. Six exhibits are mounted each year, each showcasing a different style, medium, technique or approach, and photography is named among the accepted media — recent solo shows have included black-and-white photographic work. Applications are free: artists submit five to eight images representative of the style or medium of the proposed exhibit, and proposals are scored on creativity, execution and saleability. Selected artists agree to a 20% gallery commission, must make at least 80% of exhibited work available for sale, and present an in-person artist talk at the opening. Artists with a prior connection to Pennswood Village and its surroundings are awarded additional points. Jury decisions are made by December 1, 2026 for exhibits running through 2028. Residents of Pennswood Village are not eligible.
