Photo Trouvée Virtual Artist Residency — Spring 2026

Prize Residency prize

Photo Trouvée is a magazine and platform focused on found and family photographs as artistic source material. The Spring 2026 Virtual Artist Residency is the platform's structured online residency for artists working with or inspired by found/family photographs.

The residency runs ten weeks online: April 24 – June 26, 2026. The structure is intensively scheduled — weekly Zoom calls, bio/statement workshops, artist talks, group feedback sessions, and accountability groups.

Only nine artists are selected per cycle, keeping the cohort small enough for sustained group feedback. Selection is by the magazine's co-founders Juliana Naufel and Twiggy Boyer.

Eligibility is open globally. Fee is $650 — covering the structured program rather than gallery production. Partial scholarships are available for applicants who can't cover the full cost.

Submission is through JotForm. The 2026 Spring deadline was April 13.
