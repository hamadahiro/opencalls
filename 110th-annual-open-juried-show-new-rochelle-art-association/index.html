110th Annual Open Juried Show

Prizes $2,500 In cash awards

The New Rochelle Art Association has run an annual juried show since 1912, when its first exhibition celebrated the opening of the city's new library and included work by sculptor Robert Aitken, painters G. Glenn Newell, Remington Schuyler and A.G. Heaton, and illustrators Norman Rockwell, Joseph C. Leyendecker and Victor Forsythe. The 110th edition is open to all artists 18 and over, with Photography one of the judged categories alongside Oil/Acrylic, Water Media, Graphics/Pastel, Mixed Media and Sculpture/Fine Craft. A minimum of $2,500 in cash awards is distributed across the categories. Each artist may submit up to two entries for a single fee of $35; acceptance into the show automatically brings NRAA membership. All 2-D work must be suitably framed at least 0.75 inches deep — gallery-wrapped canvas is acceptable — wired and ready to hang, with no sawtooth hooks, and must not exceed 36 × 48 inches including the outside edge of the frame. Work is hand delivered rather than shipped. The exhibition runs 28 September to 1 November 2026 in the Lumen Winter Gallery at New Rochelle Public Library, open daily with library hours, with a reception on Sunday 18 October during New Rochelle's Arts Fest.
