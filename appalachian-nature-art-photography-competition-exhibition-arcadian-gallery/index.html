Appalachian Nature Art & Photography Competition & Exhibition

Regional competition celebrating the beauty of nature across the Appalachian region. Photography-specific awards include Best of Show Photography ($500), Best Landscape Photography ($250), and Best Living Thing Photography ($250), plus a People's Choice Award ($250). Two-dimensional artwork or photography depicting Appalachian nature — landscapes, plants, wildlife, and other natural subjects from any Appalachian state. Exhibition May 15 – June 20, 2026 at Arcadian Gallery in Cumberland, MD. Juried by Steven David Johnson (photography) and Terry Miller (art).

Jury: Steven David Johnson, Photography Juror · Terry Miller, Art Juror

Organized by Arcadian Gallery
