Bettie Awards 2027

Prizes $1,000 Exhibition

The Elizabeth Barker Delaplaine Award for Young Artists — the Bettie Awards — is the Delaplaine Arts Center's annual free award for high-school artists, run each March for Youth Art Month and named for a lifelong supporter of the arts in Frederick County, Maryland. Artists living in or attending school in Frederick County in grades 9 to 12, whether public, private or homeschooled, may apply at no cost with one work no more than five feet in any direction; all media are eligible, photography named alongside painting, printmaking, ceramics, drawing and sculpture. A first-round jury of Delaplaine instructors and art professionals selects at least ten Bettie Award Winners, and a second-round jury of artists from across the region picks one of them as the Bettie Award Grand Prize Winner. The grand prize is $1,000; every other winner receives $100, and visitors vote through the run of the show for a People's Choice Award worth $250. The grand prize winner is also featured in an article in Delaplaine Arts magazine, distributed across the region, and other winners' work may be published in local print and online media. Judges treat presentation as a real criterion and will not award the grand prize to work that is poorly prepared for installation. Applications close at 11:59pm EST on Monday 1 February 2027, winners are announced on 6 February, selected work is delivered 22–28 February, and the exhibition opens with an awards presentation on Saturday 6 March 2027, running to 25 April.
