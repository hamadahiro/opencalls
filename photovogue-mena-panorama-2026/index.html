PhotoVogue Regional Open Call — MENA Panorama

Prizes $8,000 in grants Publication

PhotoVogue's regional open call dedicated to the Middle East and North Africa, following its Italian, Latin American, and East and Southeast Asian Panoramas. Open free of charge to artists 18+ from MENA and its diaspora, across photography, video and multimedia in all genres — fashion, documentary, portraiture, fine art and experimental. Submit a series of up to 15 images and/or a 60-second video trailer. Three artists receive $8,000 in total grants ($4,500 Outstanding Vision, $2,000 Vision, $1,500 Rising Voice); selected artists are featured at the 2027 PhotoVogue Festival, invited to PhotoVogue Virtual Portfolio Reviews, and considered for publication across Condé Nast titles in the region including Vogue, GQ, Architectural Digest and Condé Nast Traveler. AI-generated work is not accepted.

Entry feeFree
