America the Bountiful — Photography Show 2026

Annual juried photography show at the Coastal Arts League's public gallery on Main Street in Half Moon Bay, a volunteer-run nonprofit that has served the Coastside for over forty years. This year's theme is America the Bountiful; prints on any surface are welcome, up to three entries per artist. Open to Bay Area artists 18 and over, with work hand-delivered September 28 and collected November 2. The League's own OnlineJuriedShows prospectus says "Enter by September 3, 2026", while its gallery-schedule page still reads "Enter by September 1" — enter by September 1 to be safe. Three merit awards of $150, $100 and $50 are given. All exhibited work must be for sale and must not have been shown recently at the gallery; the League retains a 30% commission. The show runs October 1 – November 2, 2026 with a reception on October 3.
