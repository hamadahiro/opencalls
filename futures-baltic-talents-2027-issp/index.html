FUTURES Baltic Talents 2027

Prize €500

ISSP, the Riga-based photography institution that has been the Baltic member of the FUTURES Photography Platform since 2022, is looking for three artists from Latvia, Lithuania and Estonia to join the platform's 2027 cohort. FUTURES was founded in 2018 with support from the EU's Creative Europe Culture programme and now pools the talent programmes of twenty leading European photography institutions, each nominating new artists every year. Selected artists receive a participation grant of €500; conversations with international professionals, online and in person; an invitation to the FUTURES Annual Event in autumn 2027 with travel and accommodation covered; access to an online learning programme run with Magnum Photos; the chance to apply to the platform's internal open call for the travelling Annual Exhibition; the chance to apply for a funded FUTURES residency and a tutoring session; invitations to events hosted by the platform's other members; and an artist page plus promotion through the FUTURES website, social media and annual publication. Access to the Magnum learning programme, the FUTURES Conversations, the annual exhibition call, the residency call, the artist page and the member invitations all continue in the years that follow. Three conditions have to hold: you are based in or come from Latvia, Lithuania or Estonia — citizenship or official residency status in one of the three countries — you can show dedication and continuity in your practice over at least three years including at least one exhibition, and you can submit a strong photographic work made in the last three years. Artists who applied in previous years are invited to apply again. The application is free: a Google Form with a PDF of one recent project, due at midnight EET on 6 December 2026. The selection committee is ISSP's own programme and managing directors together with an Estonian and a Lithuanian colleague, and it weighs who would benefit most from the platform's exposure, networking and learning. Selected candidates are notified by 20 December and must have a CV, a portrait and fifteen low- and high-resolution images ready by 1 January.
