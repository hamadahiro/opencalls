VI Barcelona Human Rights International Photo Festival

Sixth edition of the international photography festival on human rights and global justice organised in Barcelona by the Más Retina association. Photographers, professional or amateur, of any nationality and of legal age submit a documentary project of 15 images (at least seven made in 2025–2026) addressing social, climate, gender or economic justice. Ten projects selected by the jury are printed in large format and exhibited outdoors across Barcelona's districts during the festival. The grand prize is €3,000, plus €400 for the best work in each of the three remaining categories. Submission via Picter with a €5 fee per project; deadline 9 October 2026.
