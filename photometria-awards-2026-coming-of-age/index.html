Photometria Awards 2026 — Coming of Age

Prizes Nikon Z30 prize Solo exhibition prize

Open call for the 18th Photometria International Photography Festival in Ioannina, Greece. Theme: Coming of Age (18+) — exploring the layered and evolving condition of growing up, moments of rupture, and the tension between freedom and responsibility. Sole judge: Alec Soth (Magnum, Guggenheim Fellow). 1st prize: Nikon Z30 Kit with NIKKOR Z DX 12-28mm lens. 2nd and 3rd: signed photo album by Alec Soth. One top-three winner receives a solo exhibition at the next festival. Top 25 photos exhibited in Ioannina during the 18th edition (Sep–Nov 2026) and travel to other cities in Greece. AI-generated images not accepted. Entry: free for 1 photo, €5 for 3 photos.
