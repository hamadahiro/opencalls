Los Fotógrafos 2027

The fifth annual Los Fotógrafos exhibition at OBRA Collective Gallery in Charlotte, North Carolina — a photography-only group show for Latine/o/a/x photographers with ties to Latin America and Iberia. OBRA (Observe, Bridge, Respond, Art) is an interdisciplinary Latinx arts group founded in 2013 out of the Latin American Coalition and now a 501(c)(3) running classes, workshops, artist studios and a gallery on the ground floor of Charlotte's VAPA Center. The call is free to enter and open to Latine photographers based in Charlotte and the surrounding area, with original work in all styles of photography; AI-generated images are disqualified. One to ten pieces may be submitted, and the gallery will tell selected photographers how many of their pieces it can hang, with a 20-inch cap on width; the gallery takes a 20% commission, so price accordingly. Work must be delivered in person — the exhibition opens Saturday, January 9 and closes Saturday, February 6, 2027, and decisions go out by Monday, November 9, 2026. Two things to know before you apply. The organiser's Spanish text contradicts its English text in two places: it calls this the second annual rather than the fifth, and it dates artwork delivery to November 11-13 where the English says December 11-13, so confirm the drop-off window with the gallery. And OBRA's own website does not carry this call — its exhibitions page lists nothing newer than an August 2025 event — so the EntryThingy listing and the gallery's Instagram are where the current edition lives. The 2025 edition, themed Right Now, was covered by Enlace Latino NC and selected ten photographers; the organiser contact is photographer and community activist Héctor Vaca Cruz. One more: the cutoff is not end of day. The organiser's own text says, in English and again in Spanish, "Submission form will close, 12pm (noon) Sunday, October 25" / "Este formulario cierra 12pm (mediodia), Domingo, 25 de Octubre", while EntryThingy's own deadline chip on the same page reads Oct 25, 2026 11:59 PM — treat midday Sunday as the real cutoff.
