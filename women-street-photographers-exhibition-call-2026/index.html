Women Street Photographers Exhibition Call 2026

Prize Barcelona exhibition

Annual exhibition call from Women Street Photographers, open to women and non-binary photographers of any age, anywhere in the world, using any camera or phone. Single images of street photography, street portraiture or abstract street photography made in public spaces are welcome. Thirty-four photographers are selected for an exhibition at the IPFIA gallery of the Centre Cívic Pati Llimona in Barcelona, November 5 – December 11, 2026, with printing and framing costs fully covered, publication across WSP platforms reaching over 200,000 followers, and a dedicated artist page on the WSP website. Free entries are available on request for photographers facing financial hardship. Deadline July 6, 2026, 6 PM New York time.
