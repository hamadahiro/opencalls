ENDLESS SUMMER Photography Exhibit

Prize Exhibition

ENDLESS SUMMER is Decode Gallery's late-summer themed exhibition, framed around the sensory persistence of the season — golden hour repeating, humid air thick with weight, days that refuse to darken. The brief is open to interpretation across all photographic styles and approaches; the gallery clarifies that black-and-white is welcome but not a requirement.

Twenty-six images are juried by Decode founder Holly Hart, a conceptual fine art photographer based in Tucson. The selected works are shown in a three-week physical exhibition at the gallery, running September 5–26, 2026, with high-resolution JPEGs due two days after acceptance notification.

What distinguishes Decode's open calls from typical fee-based group shows: the gallery covers professional printing on Epson Ultra Premium Luster paper and museum-quality 22×22 inch white framing with precut mats. The artist provides only the file. Sales split 50/50 between gallery and photographer, and selected prints remain in the gallery's flat files for six months after the show for continued promotion.

The entry fee is $40 per entry, which includes up to 5 images. Eligibility is open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers; payment via PayPal or Square. The 2026 deadline is July 24; acceptance notifications go out within two weeks.
