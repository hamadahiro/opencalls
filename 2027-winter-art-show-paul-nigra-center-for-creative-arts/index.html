2027 Winter Art Show

The Paul Nigra Center for Creative Arts is a community arts centre in Gloversville, in upstate New York, founded in 2015 by Lexington, an accredited provider of disability services in New York State. It mounts several open shows a year — Winter, Regional, New York State Summer, Membership and Reflection — and hosts an annual exhibition of the Adirondack Life magazine photography contest winners. For the 2027 Winter Art Show it invites artists everywhere to submit work in any medium: celebrate the new year with new art, or show older work to a new audience. Entry is through OnlineJuriedShows. Up to three works cost $25, or $15 for centre members, with additional entries at $10 each ($7 for members) to a maximum of five. Work must be original in both design and personal execution, and anything previously shown at the centre is not eligible. Hanging works must weigh no more than 25 pounds including frame and hardware, and selected pieces must arrive suitably framed and ready to install. The centre does not accept content involving nudity or sexual themes, citing the range of ages and cultures among its regular visitors. Acceptances are emailed by 6 January 2027 and selected work is received at the centre between 13 and 16 January, either in person or shipped at the artist's expense with a $10 handling fee per package. The exhibition runs from 22 January to 25 March 2027, with a public opening reception on Thursday 28 January, 4–7pm; awards are chosen and announced on 27 January. The centre retains a 20 per cent commission on sales, and unsold work left uncollected 60 days after the close is forfeited.
