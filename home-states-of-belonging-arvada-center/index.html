HOME: States of Belonging

Juried Main Gallery exhibition of contemporary photo-based work at the Arvada Center (January 14 – March 28, 2027), presented in partnership with the Colorado Photographic Arts Center and Denver's Month of Photography. The show explores home as something shaped by identity, memory, movement, family, work and community, and welcomes photography, video, collage, installation, archival and alternative image-making processes. Open to artists 18+; entries accepted July 17 – October 12, 2026 via CaFÉ, with fee scholarships available for BIPOC and LGBTQIA+ artists and those experiencing hardship. Jurors: Samantha Johnston (CPAC), Collin Parson and Olive Jarvis (Arvada Center). Artists receive 60% of sales.
