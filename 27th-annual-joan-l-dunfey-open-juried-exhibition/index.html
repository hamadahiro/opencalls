27th Annual Joan L. Dunfey Open Juried Exhibition

NHAA's long-running open-themed juried show at the Robert Lincoln Levy Gallery in Portsmouth, now in its 27th year, honouring Joan L. Dunfey (1925–1998), a Seacoast patron of the arts whose fund still supports artists. It is open to artists across New England working in all mediums, and the association's programme makes clear photography counts — it also runs the annual Lotte Jacobi Juried Show in the same gallery. There is no set subject: the jury is looking for strong work of any kind. Sarah Elliott, Lecturer of Painting and Drawing at the University of New Hampshire, is the juror; she trained at Boston University and worked for years in Germany, Hong Kong, Italy, China and France, and her own practice moves between figurative and non-figurative oil, watercolour and charcoal. Artists may submit one to three works, original, completed in the last three years and not previously shown at the gallery. 2D framed work is capped at 60 inches in the longest dimension including frame; freestanding 3D work at 42 inches in any dimension; wall-hung 3D over 35 pounds needs the artist's help to install. Every entry needs an image of at least 1200px on the long side plus title, medium, dimensions, year and retail price, and a short artist statement. All work must be for sale at $100 or more, with a 60/40 split in the artist's favour, and artists arrange their own delivery, pickup and transit insurance. Prizes go to first, second and third place plus an Artist Under 30 Award, and entry is free for anyone under 30 thanks to an anonymous donor. Non-members pay $40 to $50 depending on how many works they enter, members $20 to $30. The exhibition runs 4–29 November 2026, with acceptances out by 5pm on Friday 16 October, drop-off 1–2 November and an opening reception on 6 November, 5–8pm.
