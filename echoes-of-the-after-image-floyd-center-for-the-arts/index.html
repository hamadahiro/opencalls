Echoes of the After Image

A national juried exhibition at the Floyd Center for the Arts on memory and its distortions, hung in the Hayloft Gallery from February to April 2027. Photography sits in the named media list alongside painting, printmaking and sculpture, with three cash awards totalling $525 chosen by juror Nathan Popp. Open to US artists 18 and over; the centre keeps 40% on sales.
