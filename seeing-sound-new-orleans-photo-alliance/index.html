Seeing Sound

Prize Exhibition

The New Orleans Photo Alliance - the 501(c)(3) behind the annual PhotoNOLA festival, now in its 21st year, and a gallery on Magazine Street - joins the New Orleans Jazz and Heritage Foundation for a show about the long relationship between music and photography: from stages to streets, from full orchestras to solo practice sessions. Entrants are asked to think broadly about form, format, process and approach, and the call is pitched in the spirit of Roy DeCarava's The Sound That I Saw, inviting a wide and improvisational approach to lens-based work about the sound and images that move us, in New Orleans and beyond. The juror is Bruce “Sunpie” Barnes, a New Orleans musician, photographer, author and former National Park Service ranger of thirty years who has played his own Afro-Louisiana music - blues, zydeco, gospel, Caribbean and African influenced rhythms - in more than fifty countries. Submissions are open to all photographers, NOPA membership is not required and all ages are accepted. Fine-art nudes and implied nudes are acceptable and digitally manipulated work is welcome; AI-generated photographs are not permitted. One wrinkle in the prospectus: the written instructions cap entries at five pieces while the CaFE upload form is configured for three to ten images, and the lower written figure is what is stored here. Entry is $35, or $25 for NOPA members, with bundles at $60 (fee plus a new basic membership) and $85 (fee plus a Silver membership including an online portfolio listing). Selected work is dropped off at the NOPA Center, 2011 Magazine Street, or shipped with a return label to NOPA's mailbox service at 3436 Magazine Street #134 - the prospectus warns that packages are not guaranteed at the gallery address; the show opens in late October 2026 and stays up through December and PhotoNOLA Fest. Sales are split 60/40 in the artist's favour and photographers pay shipping. NOPA's own open-calls page still lists earlier 2026 calls, so the organiser-authored CaFE prospectus is currently the only live surface for this edition.
