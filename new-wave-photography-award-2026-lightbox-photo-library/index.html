New Wave Photography Award 2026

Biennial award for emerging photographers, judged by 16 international curators and professionals from Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, and Singapore. Submit a series of 15–25 images (created after 2021) with artist statement in Chinese and English (500 words each). Grand Prize receives TWD 100,000 (~$3,000) plus exhibition. Applicants must not have held solo exhibitions at museums, commercial galleries, or art fairs, and no more than 5 group shows. Students and persons with disabilities pay reduced fee of TWD 600.
