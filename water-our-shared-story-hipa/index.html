Water: Our Shared Story

Prize $10,000 / $8,000 / $5,000

Water conferences generate a lot of paper and not much looking. This one is trying to do something about that. HIPA - the Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum International Photography Award - has partnered with the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the 2026 United Nations Water Conference, which the UAE and Senegal co-host in Abu Dhabi from 8 to 10 December, and is asking photographers worldwide for a story of five to ten pictures about how water shapes our world and the people who depend on it.

The five suggested axes are water and people, water and communities, water and planet, water and innovation, water and challenges - broad enough to take a documentary project on a drying river, a portrait series about a fishing town, or work on desalination and irrigation technology. Entry is free, open to anyone 18 or over, and the window is short: submissions opened on 16 September 2026 and close on 15 October at 23:59 UAE time, through the members area of the HIPA site. You submit a titled, described portfolio rather than single frames.

The technical bar is high and the authenticity rules are strict. Files must be JPEG of at least 5 MB, no less than 2,000 pixels on the longest edge at 300 dpi, and you upload the original RAW or unedited file alongside. Phone and drone photographs are allowed; tags, signatures, frames, borders and logos are not. Basic technical editing is fine, but advanced editing used to create illusions, deceptions or manipulations - compositing and creative retouching are named - is prohibited. Prizes are $10,000, $8,000 and $5,000, paid within two months of the announcement. Worth reading before you enter: finalists and winners grant HIPA a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free and sub-licensable licence over the submitted work for competition and HIPA promotional use, and you must be able to produce model, property and copyright releases within seven days if asked.
