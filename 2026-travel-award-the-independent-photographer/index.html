2026 Travel Award

Prize $2,700 pool

The Independent Photographer (TIP) is a London-based online photography platform that runs themed monthly competitions and publishes editorial work, interviews, and book reviews. The 2026 Travel Award is the platform's travel-themed cycle, open to work that captures the essence of a place — its people, traditions, and the rhythm of daily life, from bustling markets to serene vistas.

Total prize pool is $2,700: first place receives $1,000, second $600, third $400, and seven runners-up receive $100 each. Winners are exhibited in London and featured across The Independent Photographer's Instagram (970k+ followers). All entrants receive a free online portfolio on the platform.

Eligibility is open globally. Submissions are tiered: €15 for one image, €30 for three, €45 for six, with an early-bird 50% discount available before the early deadline. JPEG or PNG, minimum 1200 pixels on one side, maximum 10MB per file. AI-generated work is not accepted — photography-based submissions only, with film, digital, smartphone, tintype, and camera-less photography all welcome. The final deadline is August 31, 2026.
