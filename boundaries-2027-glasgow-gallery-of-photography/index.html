Boundaries 2027

Prizes Exhibition Publication

A theme the gallery says is new to it, and one that rewards thinking rather than subject-hunting: "Boundaries are all around us, physical limitations or social agreements, they can keep us safe or restrict us. You can stay within these boundaries or you can break free of them." The brief explicitly opens both doors — "either literal or in a more metaphorical sense" — and adds that you can be as experimental as you wish, which on a gallery that mostly runs single-word themes is an invitation worth taking literally. A fence and a border post will read; so will a frame-within-a-frame or a picture about what someone is not allowed to do.

This is one of two April 2027 shows and the money is structured the way the house series always is: submitting is free, selection happens first, and only photographers who are selected pay the £50 participation fee. That fee covers printing and installation — prints go up at 16"x12", 18"x12" or 12"x12" in frames the gallery owns — and a discount is available if you are selected for both of the month's exhibitions. The other April show is Monochrome 2027, same deadline, so a double submission is a real option.

What comes back is a printed wall, an online gallery page, and inclusion in the exhibition catalogue, plus a digital certificate, photographs and video of your work in situ for the gallery's channels, and automatic entry for Photographer of the Month. Submission is 1 to 8 JPEGs in sRGB at 300ppi and at least 3000 pixels on the shortest side, sent in one go by Smash, WeTransfer or Dropbox — the gallery explicitly asks you not to attach images to an email, because that compresses them, and not to send them one at a time.

Dates worth putting in a calendar: submissions close 20 November 2026, acceptances and reserve-list places go out by 25 November, and selected photographers must pay by 9 December. The gallery notifies only the photographers it selects and asks everyone to check their spam folder, so silence after 25 November is the answer.
