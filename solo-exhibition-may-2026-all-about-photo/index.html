Solo Exhibition May 2026

Prize Exhibition prize

All About Photo runs a recurring Solo Exhibition program — a monthly featured photographer slot rather than a single annual prize. The May 2026 cycle selects one photographer for a full-month online solo exhibition running May 1–31, 2026.

What's offered beyond the main exhibition: permanent gallery presence on the All About Photo platform, an exclusive interview, social media promotion, newsletter feature, international press coverage through the publication's network, and commission-free sales — All About Photo doesn't take a cut from any sales generated through the show.

Selection is by Sandrine Hermand-Grisel, photographer and founder of All About Photo. Single-juror selection.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the All About Photo website with a cohesive project or portfolio of 6–14 images. Fee is $45. The May 2026 cycle deadline was April 21; selection announced May 1, 2026 with the exhibition opening.
