FOTOGALERIE WIEN — 2027 Annual Program

Prize Exhibition

Vienna's artist-run association for the promotion of art photography and new media, founded 1981 and based at WUK, invites artists working in photography and new media to apply for its 2027 annual exhibition program. Send a portfolio as a single PDF (max 10 MB) documenting previous work or several current works or series — ideally including exhibition or installation views and technical details — together with a CV and/or biography. The curatorial collective reviews all submissions when composing the year's program of exhibitions.

DeadlineJuly 31, 2026
Entry feeFree
LocationVienna, Austria
Requirements1 PDF portfolio
Submit viaEmail

Organized by FOTOGALERIE WIEN
