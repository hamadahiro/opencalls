VISUAL Carlow 2027 Exhibition Open Call

Open call for exhibition proposals at VISUAL Centre for Contemporary Art in Carlow, Ireland, for a new commission in September 2027. Open to artists of all career stages working in any media, locally, in Ireland, or internationally — duos and groups welcome. Winning applicant receives a generous artist fee, significant production budget, and curatorial, production, technical, and marketing support. Shortlisted artists receive expansion fees to develop extended proposals. Four gallery spaces available (Main, Link, Studio, Digital). New commission only — existing work ineligible. Shortlist announced end of June 2026; expanded proposals due end of August 2026; winner announced September 2026.
