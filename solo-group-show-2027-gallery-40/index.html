Solo/Group Show 2027

Prize Exhibition

Gallery 40, the contemporary gallery at 40 Cannon Street in Poughkeepsie, is selecting artists for its 2027 solo and small-group exhibition program. Chosen artists — solo or in curated groups of up to four — receive a six-week show from February 6 to March 14, 2027 with an opening reception, and may be considered for artist talks, panel discussions or recorded interviews. All 2-dimensional work is considered, explicitly including photography; work must be for sale (40% commission), no larger than 48 x 48 inches framed, ready to hang, and not previously exhibited at the gallery. Open to US artists 18 and over; submissions are evaluated by the gallery's curators.
