Digital Explorations 2027

Prizes $500 Exhibition

Maryland Federation of Art's annual exhibition dedicated to digitally-produced art, shown in MFA's Virtual Gallery from 15 February to 14 April 2027. The call is written around what software has done to how artists capture and make work — weaving scenes together in Photoshop, painting through a screen, videos, animations and projections, sculpting with a 3D printer — and names digitally produced photography first among the media it wants, alongside digital painting, illustration, graphic design, digital video and projection; any original 2D or 3D image-based work created or produced through digital software is considered. MFA exhibitions are blind juried, with the artist's name and identifying information hidden from the juror. This edition is selected by Mollye Bendell, an interdisciplinary artist in electronic media and Assistant Professor of Digital Media and Immersive Media Design at the University of Maryland, College Park, who has shown at the Baltimore Museum of Art, the New York Electronic Arts Festival, CURRENTS New Media Festival and the Kennedy Center, and holds grants and residencies from Wave Farm WGXC, Harvestworks and the Tides Institute. Cash awards presented by the juror total $500, announced 1 March 2027 at 5pm. Up to six original works made in the past three years may be entered, uploaded through artcall.org at no more than 10 MB each; video entries go in as a link in the Linked Web Resource field plus a still frame. Work previously exhibited in MFA's Virtual Gallery is not eligible for re-entry, and MFA states plainly that it does not accept work created via image-generative AI. Entry is $45 for one or two works ($30 for MFA members) with up to four more at $6.75 each ($4.50 members); the discount is only for current members, and a non-member who pays the member rate must settle the difference before the deadline or the work is never shown to the juror. Work must be for sale or listed Price on Request, with MFA retaining 20% commission (15% for members) and only artists in the 48 contiguous states able to set a price. TWO ELIGIBILITY SENTENCES SIT IN THE SAME DOCUMENTS: MFA's invitation line — on the call page, in the 2027 prospectus PDF and in the ArtCall FAQ's own answer to "Who is this exhibition open to?" — reads artists residing in any state or territory of the US, Canada or Mexico, while a boilerplate sentence two lines later says entry is open to all artists worldwide bar sanctioned countries. MFA’s own General Guidelines settle it, and they are the document the Terms of Entry bind an entrant to: “Unless otherwise noted, open juried exhibitions are open only to residents of the United States, Puerto Rico, Canada, and Mexico. Online shows are open to artists from around the world, with the exception of sanctioned countries.” This is an online show — the work hangs in MFA’s Virtual Gallery, February 15 to April 14, 2027 — so the international clause governs, and the ArtCall Artist Conditions header agrees: “Entry is open to all artists internationally.” The ordinal is also unsettled: the call page calls this the 7th annual edition, while the 2027 prospectus PDF and the ArtCall listing both still say 5th. Founded in 1963, MFA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit supported by the Maryland State Arts Council and runs Circle Gallery and Avenue Gallery in Annapolis. The 11:59pm deadline is not given a time zone on any surface; MFA is on US Eastern time.
