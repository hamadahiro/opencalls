20/20 Vision: A 20th Anniversary Exhibition

Prize $150 / $100 / $50

Twentieth-anniversary show from Artists of Palm Beach County, presented at Studio 1608 in West Palm Beach, an art destination that has become a gathering place for artists, collectors and enthusiasts across the county. Abstract, representational, contemporary, traditional and experimental work are all welcome. Every piece must be in a 20 x 20 inch format: work on paper should be adhered to a 20 x 20 canvas, photographs may be printed on canvas or metal, and three-dimensional work must fit within a 20 x 20 inch footprint, though height may vary. Artists may submit up to three works, all of which must be for sale at the price given at submission — that price cannot be changed later — with members receiving 75% of the gross. Explicitly sexual or political work is not accepted, and neither is anything created with artificial intelligence. Membership is not required to enter but lowers the fee. Irina Rothenberg, an art collector and advisor at Findlay Galleries, judges, awarding $150 for first place, $100 for second and $50 for third. Artists are notified on or before September 9; work is delivered September 14, the opening reception is September 19 and the closing reception October 17, 2026.
