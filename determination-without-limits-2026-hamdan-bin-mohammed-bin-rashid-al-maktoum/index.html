Determination Without Limits 2026

Prize AED 45,000

Second edition of the global photography competition run by AccessAbilities Expo in partnership with the Hamdan Bin Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum International Photography Award (HIPA), documenting the achievements and everyday lives of People of Determination. Five categories: Hope in Inclusion (resilience and human connection), Artistic Inclusion (the creative talents of People of Determination in the visual, performing and literary arts), Assistive Technology & Innovation (prosthetics, apps and devices that enable independence), Sports Inclusion (athletes with disabilities and integrated sport) and Accessible Environments (inclusive urban design, buildings and transport). Each category awards AED 5,000, AED 3,000 and AED 1,000 for first, second and third place — AED 45,000 in total. The competition explicitly sets out to empower photographers who are themselves People of Determination, giving them visibility on an international platform. Open to professional and amateur photographers worldwide with no entry fee; one photograph per category, up to five entries in total. Images must be JPEG or RAW, high resolution, at least 5 MB, with no watermarks, signatures, borders or timestamps. Submissions run from 20 July to 11:59 PM UAE time on 20 August 2026, online only through the HIPA website. Winners are announced at AccessAbilities Expo in Dubai.
