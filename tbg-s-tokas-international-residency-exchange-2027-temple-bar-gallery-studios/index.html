TBG+S/TOKAS International Residency Exchange 2027

Temple Bar Gallery + Studios in Dublin runs this annual exchange with Tokyo Arts and Space (TOKAS), now in its second year, sending one artist to Tokyo for three months, 1 September to 31 November 2027. The award covers private on-site single-room accommodation and shared studio access at the TOKAS Residency building in Sumida, plus a stipend of 8,000 euro to cover living and working expenses and travel to and from Tokyo. Applications are only accepted from professional practicing visual artists who either live in Ireland or are Irish artists living anywhere in the world, and who are free to travel for the full residency period; lens-based practices are in scope - the 2026 recipient was the Irish artist and filmmaker Patrick Hough. The residency is weighted towards research, reflection and networking rather than production: TOKAS has no production facilities on site, though it connects artists to local workshops, with any associated costs paid by the artist. The shared open-plan studio is partitioned, with designated workspace available 9:30am to 11:00pm. Residents take part in public engagement and professional presentations, culminating in a three-day Open Studio event. Applications are assessed on artistic merit and quality of work relevant to contemporary visual art, motivation, recent work and activity, quality of the proposal and activity plan, and potential for artistic growth. TBG+S shortlists and TOKAS makes the final selection. Artists who have already held this exchange cannot reapply, but unsuccessful past applicants can. The application form on the TBG+S site saves progress for a maximum of 30 days, which is shorter than the open call itself, so starting too early can lose your draft. The cutoff is 5pm, not end of day: the call page reads "Deadline for Applications: Friday 27 November 2026, 5pm".
