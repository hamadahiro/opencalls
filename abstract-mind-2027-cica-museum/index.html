Abstract Mind 2027 — 12th International Exhibition on Abstract Art

The twelfth edition of CICA Museum's international abstract-art exhibition, at the contemporary art museum in Gimpo, South Korea, open to artists worldwide. The museum is looking for work that explores abstraction, Abstractivism, and the expression of the mind and of abstract concepts — and photography is named first among the welcomed media, with "Photography & 2D Digital Art" one of four submission categories alongside painting/drawing/print, sculpture and installation, and video and interactive art. Submission is free and by email: up to five JPEGs (100–500 KB recommended) and/or up to three direct links to video works, plus a bio, with the subject line "Submission: Abstract Mind 2027 – Category – Artist's Name". Selected photographic and 2D digital work is printed by the museum at 10 × 15 or 10 × 10 inches, framed and installed by its curators and assistants; larger prints can be requested and may carry additional fees. Artists sending physical work ship it both ways at their own cost, with 2D pieces capped at 39 inches (1 m) on the longest side, installation at 39 × 39 × 79 inches and wall-mounted work at 10 kg. Accepted artists contribute $78 USD (110,000 KRW) per work toward exhibition processing and maintenance to confirm participation — the same structure as the museum's other group calls. The show runs May 19 – June 13, 2027, and the museum notes the dates are subject to change.
