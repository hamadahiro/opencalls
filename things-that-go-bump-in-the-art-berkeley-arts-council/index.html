Things That Go Bump in the Art

Prize Exhibition

Halloween-themed member exhibit in the back gallery at Berkeley Art Works, the Berkeley Arts Council's space in downtown Martinsburg, West Virginia. The council is calling for artists of the spooky, gothic, medieval, dark, strange and quirky, and says glow-in-the-dark work is especially encouraged. The call does not enumerate media - it asks for artwork rather than naming photography, painting or anything else - but the council's programme is all-media, its annual Eastern West Virginia juried exhibition accepting original two- or three-dimensional work in any medium or technique. Work must be ready to hang and no larger than 24 by 24 inches, with smaller pieces preferred because the back gallery is small; three-dimensional work may be considered if it can be hung or sits on a shelf, and larger pieces may be turned away for lack of space. There is no entry fee and no submission platform: this is a members-only show and work is hand-delivered to the gallery at 116 North Queen Street between 24 and 27 September 2026. Membership can be bought at the point of entry and runs $35 individual, $25 senior and $15 youth or student, with entering exhibitions listed among member benefits. The exhibit runs 1 to 31 October 2026 and culminates in a Halloween reception and costume party on 31 October from 8 to 10 PM, where guests act as judges and vote for a Best Loved award. There is no jury and no cash prize. Proceeds and donations support the council's work connecting artists, audiences and local businesses across Berkeley County.
