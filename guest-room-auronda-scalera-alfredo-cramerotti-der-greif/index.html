Guest Room: Auronda Scalera & Alfredo Cramerotti

Open call curated by Auronda Scalera (Artistic Director, curator, and lecturer; curator of "Ding Yi: Cosmotechnique" at Fondazione Querini Stampalia for Venice Art Biennale 2026) and Dr. Alfredo Cramerotti (Director, Media Majlis Museum at Northwestern University Qatar; President of IKT) exploring the theme "Shifting Thresholds." Invites photographic and image-based works engaging with displacement as a lived experience and aesthetic condition — migration, body transformation, exile, environmental change, and more subtle forms like digital mediation and algorithmic vision. Open to everyone. Selected work featured on Der Greif's platform with a face-to-face feedback session from the curators. Works must not have been previously selected by past Der Greif Guest Curators.
