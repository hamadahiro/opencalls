how it works

step 01

submit

Submit your work through Online Juried Shows. All photographers welcome, regardless of experience.

step 02

juried

Each submission is reviewed and evaluated on artistic merit and originality by our juror.

step 03

exhibited

Accepted works are displayed in the gallery's lighted art niches and open walls for the duration of the show.

step 04

sold

Works may be reserved for purchase by gallery patrons at any time during or after the show.
