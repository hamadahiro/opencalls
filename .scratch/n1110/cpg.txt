 Open Call
 V I S I O N S
 Submit your work for our next online photography exhibition.
 The exhibition will take place on this website from December 07 to December 22 .
 Each selected artist will also be featured on our Instagram and receive a certificate of participation. Their work will also receive guaranteed consideration for free participation in our next offline exhibition in Cambridge that will take place in Autumn 2027.
 Submissions for this open call can include both singles or series 
 Submission is free 
 If your work is selected, a participation fee of £15 will apply.
 Submission deadline: November 30 
 THEME
 We continue to explore how rich the world of photography is and how many visual forms it can take. That's why we're leaving the theme open to your interpretation as artists. We welcome all forms of photography: single images, projects of any length, and all kinds of techniques and approaches.
 At the same time, we know that every exhibition is also shaped by a curatorial vision. With VISIONS, we'd like to shift our own perspective a little and show that curatorial vision can take many different forms too.
 HOW TO SUBMIT
 Send an email to info@cambridgephotographygallery.com 
 Please include the following information in your email:
 Your Name 
 Your Country 
 Title of your work
 A Short description of your work 
 A link to your Website or Instagram (optional)
 An Artist statement or Bio (optional)
 Your images as attachments or as a link to cloud storage (Google Drive, Swisstransfer, etc.)
 Make sure that we can download the files without requesting access.
 Image Guidelines 
 We don't want to limit you with strict technical requirements. 
 For the selection stage , your submission files can be of any size, format or orientation.
 If your work is selected , we'll can ask you to provide high-resolution files for best online experience.
 If your images don’t fit as attachments, please include a download link (Google Drive, Swisstransfer, etc.)
 EXHIBITION BENEFITS 
 Selected works will be presented in a curated online exhibition hosted on this website.
 Each participating artist will receive a dedicated post on our Instagram page, which currently has over 1800 engaged followers and continues to grow.
 Our previous online exhibitions attracted more than 2,000 visitors. “VISIONS” will be promoted through our existing channels to reach a similarly engaged audience of photography enthusiasts.
 Every selected artist will receive a digital certificate of participation confirming their inclusion in the exhibition, suitable for their portfolio or CV.
 All participants will receive guaranteed consideration for free participation in our next offline exhibition in Cambridge that will take place in autumn 2027.
 FAQ
 Do I retain full rights to my photographs? 
 Yes, absolutely. You keep all rights to your work. By submitting, you only grant us permission to publish your images on our website and social media in the context of this exhibition and related promotion.
 Can I submit previously published or exhibited work? 
 Yes, previously exhibited or published works are welcome.
 Can I submit diptychs, collages, virtual photography? 
 Yes. All formats and approaches are welcome.
 Can I submit AI-generated images? 
 No, AI-generated images are not accepted. 
 You may use AI tools as part of the workflow or editing process of your photographs, but fully AI-generated images are not accepted.
 How will I know if my work has been selected? 
 We’ll contact you by email.
Depending on the number of submissions, the review process may take some time. In many cases, the final decision may be made after the submission deadline has passed.
 We’ll also inform you if your work is not selected, so you’re not left waiting.
 Can I edit my submission after sending it? 
 Yes — until the deadline.
Just contact us at info@cambridgephotographygallery.com with the changes you’d like to make.
 What do you mean by “short description"? 
 A short description or a project description is a brief text outlining your work — its concept, context, or what inspired it. We believe that a description can help viewers better understand your work and serve as a key to the artist’s world.
 At the same time, we understand that not every photograph requires an explanation. In some cases (especially with a single image) the work may speak for itself. Therefore, including a description is optional, and the decision is entirely up to you.
 How many photographs can my project include? 
 There is no fixed limit on the number of images - you are free to include as many as you feel are necessary for your project. At the same time, we kindly ask you to keep some reasonable boundaries in mind, as very large projects (for example, 60 images) may be difficult to accommodate within the exhibition if most other projects are significantly smaller.
 Can I choose a cover image for my project? 
 No cover image is needed - all photographs will be displayed as part of a unified online exhibition layout.
 However, if you feel that a specific presentation format is important for your project, we are open to considering alternative approaches. In such cases, we would be happy to discuss your idea with you via email.
 Why is the exhibition only two weeks long, even though it’s online? 
 We believe that a limited time window helps the exhibition feel special. During these two weeks, we will actively promote the exhibition, post dedicated Instagram posts, and share stories together with the artists. After it ends, the exhibition will remain accessible on our website in the “Past Exhibitions” section.
 Why is there a participation fee? 
 The participation fee helps us cover the administrative costs of running the exhibition, maintaining the website, email, and other related expenses.
 We’re a small independent initiative - our goal is not profit, but sustainability.
 Are you a vanity gallery? 
 No. Vanity galleries exist to profit from artists.
 We exist to support artists, to give them space and visibility, and to grow together as a creative community.
 We’re not running non-stop open calls just to maximise profits by selecting hundreds of participants. Quite the opposite, we aim to make each exhibition we organise thoughtful and distinct, treating every open call as a specific curatorial statement, not just another routine cycle.
 How can I pay the participation fee? 
 We accept payments via Stripe or PayPal. If your work is selected, we will send you a payment link with further details.
 How exactly does the selection the next offline exhibition work? 
 For each of our annual offline exhibitions, we invite a number of photographers who have previously taken part in our online exhibitions. These invited artists don't pay any fees. We cover all the costs, including printing.
