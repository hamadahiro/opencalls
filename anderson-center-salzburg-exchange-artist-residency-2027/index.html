Anderson Center Salzburg Exchange Artist Residency 2027

Prizes Residency €600 stipend

Since 2008 the Anderson Center at Tower View has run an annual artist exchange with the City of Salzburg, and each July one Minnesota visual artist takes the Stadt:Atelier live-in studio at the Künstlerhaus in Salzburg, Austria — the building that also houses the Salzburger Kunstverein, five minutes' walk from the historic centre. The 2027 residency runs 1–30 July. The studio is a furnished 70 m² combined living and working space over two floors with kitchenette, lounge, bathroom and wi-fi, with linens and cooking and cleaning supplies provided; a €600 stipend for food is included. In return, a Salzburg artist comes to work at the Anderson Center's own artist community in Red Wing. There is no fee to apply. Eligibility is tightly drawn: the programme is open to emerging and mid-career visual artists who are current legal residents of Minnesota and have been for at least a year, and the enumerated media explicitly include photography alongside ceramics, painting, drawing, filmmaking, sculpture, printmaking, fabric, animation and jewellery — writers and performance artists are not eligible. The participating artist pays their own travel to Salzburg and back, and is asked to gift one work made during the residency to the City of Salzburg. Applications close at noon Central time on 13 November 2026 and require a CV or biographical statement of up to three pages, a one- to two-page letter of intent, and at least ten high-resolution images of recent work. Jury review runs through late November and December, with the selected artist, wait-list and runners-up notified by 5 January 2027 at the latest.
