Secrets

Juried winter show at the Falkirk Cultural Center, a city-run gallery in a Queen Anne mansion in San Rafael, on the theme of secrets. The centre's framing: "Secrets is an invitation to explore the many layers of what is hidden and what is revealed. Secrets can be personal or collective, real or imagined, inherited or invented. What might be concealed behind a face, beneath a landscape, inside an object, or between the lines of a story. What happens when something hidden comes into the light? Consider what you choose to reveal, what you choose to obscure and what you leave for the viewer to uncover." Open to artists residing in the nine counties of the San Francisco Bay Area — Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano and Sonoma — who may submit up to three separate original works not previously shown at Falkirk; all media are eligible except jewellery, AI-created images and videography. One caution before you pay: the prospectus contradicts itself on how old the work may be, saying “completed in the past two years” in its opportunity summary and “past five years” in its general information, and two years is the window Falkirk's previous call used — ask the gallery before entering anything older than that. One digital image per work. The selection juror and curator is Susan Press, who studied printmaking and ceramics at the San Francisco Art Institute and has curated and juried shows in San Rafael for Artworks Downtown, the Marin Society of Artists and Falkirk itself; the awards juror is Bree Woodward, a Bay Area figurative sculptor, creative director and arts educator. Awards of $100, $75 and $50 are presented at the opening reception on Friday 8 January 2027, 5–7.30pm, during San Rafael's Art Walk, with the ceremony around 6.30pm; the exhibition runs 8 January to 19 February 2027. Entry is $40 for the first work and $7.50 each for a second and third; diptychs and triptychs count as one piece. Notifications go out by Friday 11 December 2026, with a separate email for each work. Accepted work must be hand delivered to the gallery at 1408 Mission Avenue on Monday 4 January 2027 between 10am and 2pm — shipping is not accepted — must be ready to hang with wire on D-rings or mirror hangers, and the City of San Rafael retains a 30% commission on sales.
