Suboart Magazine — May & June 2026 Issues

Prize Publication

Suboart Magazine is an independent print magazine based in Lisbon focused on emerging visual artists. The publication runs in-depth interviews, Q&As, and features rather than thematic group features — selected artists are profiled at length.

Eligibility is open to emerging visual artists 18+ across multiple disciplines: photography, painting, sculpture, textile art, collage, and illustration. AI-generated work is not accepted.

The fee structure is staged: free to submit, with a €30 fee charged only if accepted. Selected artists receive a free PDF copy of the issue.

Submission is by email with up to 6 images. The May & June 2026 issues deadline was April 6; selections announced late April 2026.
