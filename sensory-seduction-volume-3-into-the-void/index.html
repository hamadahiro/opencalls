Sensory Seduction Volume 3 — Into the Void

Sensory Seduction is a Sydney-based independent print magazine and creative platform covering art, literature, music and culture, now open for its third volume, 'Into the Void: Exploration and Discovery'. The issue embraces all aspects of the unknown and invites work interpreting exploration and discovery in the contributor's own way — going on a journey, doing something for the first time, expansion, travel, investigating a mystery, emigration and immigration, unexpected detours, a chance encounter, embracing a new culture, taking a risk, a transitional period, getting lost, leaving your comfort zone, uncovering a secret. The magazine publishes original, unpublished work across disciplines including photography, digital art, collage, film stills and editorial work alongside essays, interviews, poetry and reviews; existing work is welcome as long as it has not been published before and fits the theme. Photographers and visual artists may submit up to six images with an artist statement and/or captions for each image, and hybrid submissions combining writing and images are accepted. Submission is via the magazine's online form and carries a A$5 non-refundable fee, which the magazine says covers its operating costs; every submission receives a response.
