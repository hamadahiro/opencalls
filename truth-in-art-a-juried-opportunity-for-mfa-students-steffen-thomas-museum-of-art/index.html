Truth in Art: A Juried Opportunity for MFA Students

The Steffen Thomas Museum of Art is a single-artist museum — one of only fourteen in the United States by its own count — built around the work of German-born American painter and sculptor Steffen Thomas. Through its College Connections programme the museum runs this free juried exhibition for MFA students, and the point is as much the apprenticeship as the wall space: selected students work alongside museum mentors on curating, loan forms and condition reports, art installation, exhibition promotion and public speaking, so the show doubles as a first practical education in how a museum actually mounts an exhibition. The top three Best in Show selections receive a monetary award. Entry is free. Submissions close 29 August 2026 and the exhibition runs 26 September to 21 November 2026 in the museum's West Gallery in Buckhead, Georgia, in Morgan County about ten minutes from historic Madison.
