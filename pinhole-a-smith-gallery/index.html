pinhole

A Smith Gallery in Johnson City, Texas announces "pinhole", a juried online photography exhibition juried by the gallery's own founder and co-director Amanda Smith, a photographer of more than thirty-five years and a Certified Public Accountant who has run over 200 juried and curated exhibitions at the gallery. Both digital and analog pinhole images are eligible. Selected work is shown in the gallery's online exhibition from 1 January to 28 February 2027, with a reception on 30 January 2027 at 2pm CT and a GalleryTalk video posted in ASG Conversations. The juror selects up to 47 images in total; all selected images are featured on the gallery's Facebook and Instagram across the exhibition, listed for sale in the online store, published in an exhibition catalogue and permanently archived, and 27 of them are chosen for "The 27", a hardbound fine art book. Awards: the Jurors Award and the Directors Award each bring a month-long online solo exhibition, a copy of "The 27" and a feature story on the gallery blog, and the Visitors Award, decided on Instagram likes, is $100. Entry is $40 for the first five images and $5 per additional image with no limit on the number submitted; images up to 1800 pixels on the longest side, JPEG at 72 ppi, unwatermarked. Jurying is blind - images are renamed and numbered before the juror sees them. Photographers submitting 13 or more images get a complimentary 30-minute review by the gallery directors; under 13 images, a review costs $20. Open to professional and amateur photographers worldwide in all photographic mediums and styles, but work previously exhibited in an A Smith Gallery competition is not eligible. The gallery states it prefers not to accept images created with artificial intelligence "at the present time due to the lack of clarity concerning copyright issues and the ongoing litigation associated with them".
