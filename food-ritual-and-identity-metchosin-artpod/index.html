Food: Ritual and Identity

Prize Exhibition

The artist-run ArtPod on southern Vancouver Island closes its 2026 season with a show about what feeds us. Food is never just food, the curators write — it is material, metaphor, ritual and resource, and it feeds identities, cultures, economies and memories as much as bodies. The call asks for work that traces the visible and invisible systems that sustain or exploit us, from harvest to hunger and feast to waste, and treats food as personal, political, ecological and sensual at once. The guiding question is what it means to be nourished in a time of both abundance and imbalance. The curatorial team is Anna Mar, Pierrette Vezina and Cindy McMath, with Janice Beaudoin jurying. Photography is explicitly welcome under the ArtPod's standing submission guidelines: limited-edition photographs and prints, including giclée, inkjet and archival prints, are accepted in editions of 25 or fewer, and photographs must be taken by the artist and may not reproduce another artist's work. On AI the ArtPod says it is still reflecting, but requires submitted work to represent the artist's own creative vision and authorship. Jurying is blind — the juror sees only the image, the medium and substrate, and an artist statement capped at 1,000 characters. Artists may submit up to three original works; diptychs, triptychs and small series count as one work if shown in a single photograph. Two-dimensional work must not exceed 7 feet high by 5 feet wide. Work must not have been shown in a juried exhibition or commercial gallery on southern Vancouver Island in the past year. Submission is CA$10 per artwork with a further CA$20 hanging fee per accepted work; the ArtPod takes 30% commission and all work must be for sale. The call runs 12 August to 9 October with results on 12 October, and the exhibition runs 23 October to 20 December 2026 with an opening gala on 24 October, 2–4 pm.
