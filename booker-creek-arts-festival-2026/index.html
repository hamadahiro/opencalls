Booker Creek Arts Festival 2026

The inaugural Booker Creek Arts Festival turns St. Petersburg's historic EDGE District into a four-day walkable gallery from 12 to 15 November 2026, with juried work by hundreds of Florida artists hung inside the district's restaurants, cafés, shops, hotels and residential buildings rather than in a single venue. Named after the urban stream that runs through downtown St. Petersburg, the festival wraps the art in live mural painting, glass blowing, dance, music and children's activities, and visitors move between venues with a map, voting by text for the People's Choice Award. Up to $50,000 in prizes is on offer — a $10,000 Booker Creek Best of Show, a $7,500 Award of Excellence, ten $1,500 Merit Awards and two $2,500 People's Choice Awards, presented on Saturday 14 November. The organisers name painting, photography, wall sculpture and mixed media among the competing media, and entries may be paintings, sculptures, ceramic works, textile works, photographs, drawings, mixed-media works, collages or assemblages. Entry is open to artists who have lived in Florida for at least six consecutive months and are 18 or older on 14 August 2026; each artist makes one application only, submitting three separate works with one high-resolution image each, for a $25 non-refundable fee. Every submitted work must have been completed in or after 2023 and must be physically hangable — digital-only works, new media, video and film are not permitted, and a single piece may occupy no more than 6 feet high by 8 feet wide. Work created solely by AI software will not be treated as the artist's own and will be disqualified. Artists do not have to be present during the festival, and visitors can buy work directly from them. Submissions close at 11:59pm EDT on Friday 14 August 2026.
