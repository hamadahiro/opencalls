12th Regional Juried Photography Exhibition

Prizes $500 / $250 / $150 Exhibition

Twelfth annual juried photography exhibition at the Rehoboth Art League's historic campus in Henlopen Acres near Rehoboth Beach, Delaware, on view January 8 – February 7, 2027. Open to photographers living in Delaware, Pennsylvania, Maryland, New Jersey, Virginia, and Washington, D.C. — members and non-members alike. Submit up to 3 works created after January 2025; all work must be for sale, with a 65/35 artist/league split. Juried by Andre Wright Jr., who also awards $500, $250, and $150 prizes. Opening reception January 8, followed by a gallery talk with the juror on January 9.
