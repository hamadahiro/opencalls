12th Regional Juried Photography Exhibition

Twelfth annual juried photography exhibition at the Rehoboth Art League's historic campus in Henlopen Acres near Rehoboth Beach, Delaware, on view January 8 – February 7, 2027. Open to photographers living in Delaware, Pennsylvania, Maryland, New Jersey, Virginia, and Washington, D.C. — members and non-members alike. Submit up to 3 works created after January 2025; all work must be for sale, with a 65/35 artist/league split. Juried by Andre Wright Jr., who also awards $500, $250, and $150 prizes. Generative AI is barred but ordinary editing is not: "While human-controlled digital editing is entirely OK, the use of artificial intelligence to create the elements in the image is prohibited. Anything that can be done in camera or with photo editing software like Photoshop is not, by our definition, considered to be using AI in a generative manner." Work made in a school, a school-monitored studio or a workshop, or under instructor supervision, is ineligible. Opening reception January 8, followed by a gallery talk with the juror on January 9. The cutoff is not end of day: the Art League states "Deadline: Monday, November 16, 2026 (4pm)". All work must be for sale - no NFS or price-on-request - and the Rehoboth Art League retains a 35 percent commission on each sale.
