ICP Latin Vistas Scholarship

Prize Up to 10 × $5,000 tuition scholarship

Up to ten $5,000 scholarships from the International Center of Photography for emerging curators, writers, photographers and image-makers from Latin America, applied against the $12,500 tuition of any of ICP's three Online One-Year Certificate programmes - Curatorial Practice in Photography, Writing and the Photographic Image, or Documentary Practice: Visual Storytelling. ICP describes the award as supporting applicants “committed to exploring new perspectives through photography and visual storytelling”, giving recipients “the opportunity to develop their practice, expand their critical and creative approaches, and engage with ICP's international community of image-makers”. It is open to applicants who are from and currently reside in Latin America. Recipients are chosen on the strength of the application materials and portfolio, and finalists may be invited to a Zoom interview. Both the programme application (through SlideRoom, $60 fee) and the separate scholarship application must be submitted by 18 October 2026; the $5,000 covers part of the tuition, so a recipient still pays the $7,500 balance plus the application fee. Scholarships are only available to students enrolling in the One-Year Certificate programmes - part-time courses and Open Education workshops are not eligible - and awards cannot be combined, so an applicant may receive only one, though ICP encourages applying for every scholarship for which you are eligible. Decisions arrive by email with the admission letter; ICP is not an accredited institution, so the certificate does not qualify for federal student loans.
