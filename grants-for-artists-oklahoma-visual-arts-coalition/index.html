Grants for Artists

The Oklahoma Visual Arts Coalition's standing grant programme for individual Oklahoma artists, free to apply and reviewed on a rolling schedule with the next deadline on October 15, 2026. Four grants run under one umbrella. Creative Projects awards up to $1,500 to help an artist make visual art for public presentation. Community/Artist Partnership awards up to $1,500 for artist-led community projects. Education Assistance awards up to $500 toward training and professional development. Professional Basics awards up to $500 toward the ordinary infrastructure of a practice. Creative Projects and Community/Artist Partnership are reviewed twice a year, in March and October; Education Assistance and Professional Basics three times, in January, March and October. The grants are open to artists working in visual-based mediums as well as to curators and art writers, and OVAC publishes a scoring matrix and a sample application for each category, so you can see exactly what a panel is looking at before you write. It also gives written feedback on every request, funded or not. Photographers are among the funded: three of the March 2026 grantees work in photography — Liz Cousins, a Tulsa portrait photographer, for Queer Tulsa; Nicol Ragland for The Legends of Black Rodeo; and Barbara Paradise Kelly for matting, framing and display pedestals. Applicants must be 21 or over and must have lived in Oklahoma for at least the twelve months before the deadline; artists currently enrolled in BFA or MFA programmes are not eligible, and you may attach your name to only one application per cycle. You do not need to be an OVAC member. Funded projects must happen after the award and within a year of receiving the funds. Expect notification within four weeks of the deadline. Applications go through Submittable.
