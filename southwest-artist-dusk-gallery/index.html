Southwest Artist

Prize Exhibition

Themed exhibition at Dusk Gallery in Santa Fe celebrating the spirit of the American Southwest — desert landscapes, indigenous cultures, borderlands, urban environments, and the region's singular light across Arizona, New Mexico, Texas, Colorado, Utah, Nevada, and California. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers. Juried by Dusk founder Holly Hart, who selects 26 images. The gallery covers professional printing on Luster paper, matting, and 24×24 inch black museum-quality framing; artists supply only a high-resolution JPEG (500KB–5MB, 4500px longest side). Exhibition September 4–25, 2026 with opening reception; post-exhibition sales split 50/50. $40 for 5 images or $45 for up to 10.
