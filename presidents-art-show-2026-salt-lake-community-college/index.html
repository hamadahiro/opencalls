President's Art Show 2026

Prizes $5,000 in awards Exhibition

Salt Lake Community College's President's Art Show reaches its 32nd edition in 2026, a juried exhibition open to every Utah artist, amateur or professional, with $5,000 in prize money on the table. Photography is named outright in the accepted-media list alongside painting, sculpture, works on paper, textiles and fibre, and decorative work in glass or wearable form. Artists may enter up to two original works completed in the last two years, at $15 per submission, and up to three images may accompany each entry — for a series, one image must show how the whole is meant to hang. The SLCC Art Committee juries on artistic theme and vision, technical execution, and aesthetic value. The prize stack is unusually well spread: Best in Show $1,500, President's Award $1,000 chosen by SLCC President Gregory F. Peterson, a $1,000 Community Award chosen by an honorary art community representative, a $500 Jury's Choice from the Art Committee, and $500 each for second and third place. Work must be exhibition-ready with wire backing on two-dimensional pieces, and three-dimensional work cannot exceed six feet or 150 pounds. Nothing is shipped: the artist or a representative delivers and collects at the South City Campus in Salt Lake City. Sales carry a 25 per cent commission, waived for SLCC students. Notification lands 15 September, drop-off is 23 October, the reception is 29 October with awards at 7 p.m., and the show runs to 19 November 2026 in the South City Campus Multipurpose Room.
