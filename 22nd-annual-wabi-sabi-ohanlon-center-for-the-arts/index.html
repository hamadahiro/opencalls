22nd Annual Wabi-Sabi

O'Hanlon Center for the Arts, a non-profit in Mill Valley, California, invites work for the 22nd edition of its annual Wabi-Sabi exhibition, on view 20 October - 19 November 2026. Wabi-sabi finds beauty in the simple, rustic and unassuming - in things unfinished, irregular, weathered or worn by time - and the call asks for work that is dynamic, spontaneous, irregular and poetic rather than perfected: a rusty object, a found fragment, a rough surface, an unexpected combination of natural materials. Contemporary, exploratory and experimental entries are encouraged. Sherrie Lovler and Colin Talcroft jury. Open to all artists aged 16 and over, two works each. This is an in-gallery show and there are no digital submissions: the registration form is completed online in advance and the work itself must be hand-delivered to 616 Throckmorton Avenue on Friday 9 October, 11 am - 4 pm, or Saturday 10 October, 10 am - 1 pm. Wall-hung pieces are capped at 40 inches wide and, on the registration form, 60 inches tall. Entry is $40, or $30 for Artist-level OHCA members, and taking out a new Artist-level membership at drop-off waives the fee. Jury results are emailed by 5 pm on Thursday 15 October; there is an art talk and opening reception on Saturday 31 October and a First Tuesday Art Walk reception on 3 November. Work previously shown at O'Hanlon is not eligible, and artists agree to donate 30% of any sale made in the exhibition's first 60 days. The call names no accepted-media list and neither do the gallery policies; photography sits squarely in the centre's programme, which has included a dedicated photography show juried by SFMOMA's Shana Lopes and recurring Wabi Sabi photography workshops.
