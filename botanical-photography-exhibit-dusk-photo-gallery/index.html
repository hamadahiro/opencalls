BOTANICAL Photography Exhibit

Prize Exhibition prize

Dusk Gallery is a Santa Fe, New Mexico photography gallery founded by fine art photographer Holly Hart, who also juries the gallery's calls. BOTANICAL is the gallery's call for plant-life photography that moves beyond pure documentation.

The brief is specific: character in a leaf, architecture in a seedpod, the formal qualities of plants seen as compositional subjects rather than botanical specimens. All styles and processes welcome.

Selection is by Holly Hart.

Selected work is shown in a physical exhibition at Dusk Gallery in Santa Fe.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the gallery's website with 5 or 10 images. Fee is $40–$45. The 2026 deadline was March 26; results announced April 9, 2026.
