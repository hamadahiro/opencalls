Fotofestiwal Open Program 2027

Prizes €1,000 artist fee Exhibition

The Open Program is the submissions route into Fotofestiwal, the International Festival of Photography in Łódź, Poland — founded in 2001 as one of the first photography events in Central Europe, visited by more than 20,000 people, and run since 2016 as a collective with no director and no vertical management structure. There is no theme: the organisers say they are looking for artists and collectives with a distinctive personality and for projects that propose a bold and original way of looking at photography and the contemporary world. Work in any technique is accepted, and the call is open with no thematic, age or regional restriction. The jury selects six projects, which are then developed with Fotofestiwal's curatorial team and shown in an exhibition in Łódź in June 2027. Each of the six selected artists receives a €1,000 artistic fee, accommodation for the festival's main opening weekend, travel costs reimbursed up to €300, and a budget of up to €600 for producing the exhibition or transporting the work. At least one of the submitting artists is also nominated to FUTURES, the European platform supporting emerging photographers. The 2027 jury is Jana Johanna Haeckel, curator of the 4th photo+ Photography Biennale in Düsseldorf in 2027 and lecturer in the history of photography and exhibition-making at KASK & Conservatorium in Ghent; Marta Szymańska, a photography curator who has co-created Fotofestiwal for more than twenty years and was deputy director of the Archeology of Photography Foundation from 2014 to 2019; Francesco Rombaldi, curator, editor and book designer, founder of the Yogurt curatorial platform and of Charta; Roï Saade, artist, designer and curator, founder of Huwawa Publishing and director of Bound Narratives, working on visual culture, displacement and cultural production in the Middle East and North Africa; and Krzysztof Candrowicz, co-founder of Fotofestiwal, artistic director of the Hamburg Triennial of Photography from 2013 to 2018 and guest curator of the Ci.CLO Biennial in Porto from 2018 to 2024, who has juried the Rencontres d'Arles Discovery Award, the Deutsche Börse Photography Prize, Prix Pictet and World Press Photo. Submissions are complete photographic projects of 10 to 20 photographs, up to two projects per person, made through Picter. The fee is €15 per project, and the organisers state that a free submission is possible in justified cases — write to the open-call address on their site. The call opened on October 1, 2026, closes on November 30, 2026, and results are announced on January 25, 2027.
