Emerging Artist Award 2027

Prizes $4,000 Solo exhibition

The Pence Gallery in Davis gives one emerging California artist $4,000 to push new work in a significant direction, plus a solo exhibit and an artist talk at the gallery from 14 January to 27 February 2028. Applicants may work in any medium except video or sound, so photography is eligible, and the gallery asks for a resume with exhibition history and education, an artist statement and 10 to 15 digital images of current work. 'Emerging' is the gallery's own definition — early in an art career, with limited exhibiting experience but not an extensive solo exhibition history — and the submitted work must be high calibre, original and made within the past five years. Applicants must live in California at the time of applying and be 18 or over. The award is sponsored by Rosa Marquez and Yatish Mishra, and the winner is announced by 30 April 2027. One caution on dates: the gallery's own Call for Artists page does not carry this cycle at all — the string '2027' appears nowhere on it, its Emerging Artist Award block still shows last cycle's extended deadline of 12 April 2026, and every neighbouring call there reads 'APPLICATION IS CLOSED' — so this listing points at the gallery's own CaFÉ record, which states 5 April 2027 at 9pm PST twice and dates the resulting exhibition to 2028. The record's structured close instant is end of day Pacific while its prose says 9pm, so treat 9pm PST on 5 April 2027 as the cutoff and confirm with the gallery if you are close to the wire.
