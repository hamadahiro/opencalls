Art of Possibilities Art Show & Sale 2027

Prizes Exhibition Cash awards

The 64th annual juried show for artists with disabilities, at the Courage Kenny Rehabilitation Institute in Minneapolis from 3 June to 9 July 2027, with Photography as one of six categories. Cash awards go to each category and a Best of Show, and artists keep 100% of sales. All work must be for sale; AI-generated work is not accepted.
