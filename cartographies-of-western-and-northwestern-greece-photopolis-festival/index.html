Cartographies of Western and Northwestern Greece

A free open call from House of Lucie Agrinio, in collaboration with Photopolis Festival and the platform The Provinces, for a photography exhibition at the House of Lucie gallery in Agrinio, Greece, subtitled Human Geography, Land and Landscape. The organisers are looking for images that capture aspects of the landscape of Western and Northwestern Greece, the transformations and changes it is undergoing, and the relationship between people and their environment or aspects of their everyday life — an invitation, as they put it, to give your own visual mapping of these regions. READ THE GEOGRAPHIC GATE BEFORE YOU APPLY: the call is not open-subject, and photographs must come from the specific regional units of Aetolia-Acarnania, Achaea, Elis, Preveza, Thesprotia, Arta and Ioannina. Both amateur and professional photographers may take part, and no age or residency condition is stated — the restriction is on where the pictures were made, not on who made them. Submit 5 to 15 photographs; entry is free. The jury is Eleni Mouzakiti, a photographer, educator and curator with a PhD in Photography from the University of Derby whose work is held in the Portland Art Museum and the Thessaloniki Museum of Photography and who is a founding member of The Provinces; Lila Zotou, a photographer and photography teacher with an MFA from the Utrecht Academy of Arts, based in Oslo and working in new documentary photography and visual narrative; and Panos Kaltsas, founding member and Director of Photopolis Festival, Director of the Streetphotofestival in Athens, and the person in charge of House of Lucie in Agrinio. Selected photographers are notified by email. Files should be 1500 px on the long side and named with the photographer's name in Latin characters followed by 1, 2, 3, and must be sent through a transfer service such as WeTransfer or SwissTransfer — the organisers ask explicitly that you do NOT attach files directly to the email — to the festival's submissions address given on the call page, together with a Word document giving your full name, city, telephone, email, Instagram if you have one, a short text about the photographs you are sending and the location of each one; a short biography is also accepted. You should receive an acknowledgement within 48 hours, and if it does not arrive the organisers ask you to contact them at the festival's general info address on the same page. On rights: copyright in the photographs remains with their creators, and entrants grant the organisers the right to publish and exhibit the work and post it on social media to promote the event, with the creators named, and with no fee payable for that use. One practical point for anyone outside the area: prints are held at the exhibition space for 30 days for collection in person and are not posted back.
