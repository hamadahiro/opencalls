Idea Capital Awards 2027

Prize $1,000 / $2,500 stipends

Idea Capital is an Atlanta grassroots funder, launched in 2008, that pools small donations from the city's arts community and hands the money straight to artists for new, experimental projects - in its own words, audacious art that traditional funding sources would find difficult to champion. Two award levels, decided by the panel rather than requested by the applicant: the Catalyst Award, a $1,000 stipend for emerging artists or a small or early-stage project, and the Vanguard Award, $2,500 for more established artists or projects further along in development. A third, the $5,000 Susan Antinori Visual Artist Award, is limited to painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics or mixed media, so photographers should read themselves into the two general awards; the Margaret Kargbo Artist As Activist designation goes at the committee's discretion to a project embodying social justice. Eligible disciplines run across visual art, digital media, film, video, performance, curatorial projects and more, and the test is whether form, process or content take a real risk - work executed in traditional ways throughout is not a fit, and neither are completed projects, thesis work, or art that is secondary to education, therapy or activism. Funds cover materials, equipment, fabrication, artist and professional fees, plus research and development such as travel or residencies, but not standard framing or mounting shows outside Atlanta. Open to working artists and cultural producers aged 20 and over living in metro Atlanta, including collectives, curators and educators; students may apply for work outside their academic programme; past awardees wait three years. One application per artist, submitted free through Submittable with a general project budget rather than line items. Applications close at 11:59pm on 1 November 2026; a shortlist is announced 1 December, awards on 7 January 2027, and funds are disbursed on 21 January.
