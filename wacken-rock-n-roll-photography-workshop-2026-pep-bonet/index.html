Wacken Rock 'n Roll Photography Workshop 2026

Prize Workshop fellowship

Open call for six free places in the 5th Rock 'n Roll Photography Workshop, led by NOOR photojournalist and former Motörhead photographer Pep Bonet with LOUD>SOCIETY during Wacken Open Air 2026 (July 29 – August 1), the world's largest heavy metal festival. Under the anniversary theme 'HARDER', the group shoots, edits and post-produces festival photography on the spot for official W:O:A channels. Selected photographers receive free tuition, on-site hosting, festival ticket, crew catering, all necessary passes and permits, and a shuttle from Hamburg; travel to Hamburg is not covered. Apply with a short bio, motivation letter and portfolio; fluent English required.
