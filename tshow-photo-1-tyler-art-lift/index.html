TSHOW PHOTO.1

Prize $1,000 / $500 / $500

First photography-dedicated edition of TSHOW, the international juried exhibition from Texas nonprofit Tyler Art Lift, created to highlight the diversity of contemporary photographic practice — traditional, experimental, documentary, conceptual, film and digital. Juried by Latvian fine art and fashion photographer Kristine Madjare, whose work has appeared in The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal and Financial Times. Exhibition at Gallery Main Street in Tyler, Texas, October 15–27, 2026, with cash prizes of $1,000 Best in Show, $500 runner-up and a $500 People's Choice chosen at the opening. Open internationally to artists 18+; $45 for the first entry, $5 each additional; 30% sales commission supports the show.
