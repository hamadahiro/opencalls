How to Hold Time

Prizes $1,000 $650 $350 $200 3×$100 Exhibition

How to Hold Time is a juried group exhibition at the Arts Center in Orange, Virginia, for artists aged 65 and over. It runs from 4 June to 31 July 2027. One line on the page calls it Hold On to Time, but the heading, the web address and the email subject line all use How to Hold Time. Applicants must be 65 or older and show an ongoing, active creative practice. The call is open to local, national and international artists. All media are welcome, and photography is named in the list with painting, sculpture, installation, video and textiles. The prizes total $2,500. Best in Show receives $1,000, first prize $650, second $350 and third $200, and three Juror's Choice winners receive $100 each. No juror is named yet. The theme comes from one of Pablo Neruda's Elemental Odes, which describes time as two rivers, one running back over life already lived and one moving forward with the present, joined for a single instant. The centre asks for work that holds time rather than represents it, memory against immediacy, erosion against revelation, and says entries don't need to illustrate the poem. Entries go by email with "How to Hold Time" in the subject line. Applicants send 5–10 images or time-based samples of recent work, a 300–500 word artist statement, a CV or short biography highlighting continued practice, and an optional reflection on time in their work. The $20 fee is paid through the centre's online shop. Artists who can't deliver and collect in person pay for shipping to the centre, and return shipping help is possible with a reusable UPS box and a prepaid return label.
