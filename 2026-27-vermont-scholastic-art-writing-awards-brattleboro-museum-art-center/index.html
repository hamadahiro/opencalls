2026–27 Vermont Scholastic Art & Writing Awards

Prizes Exhibition $12,500 / $2,000 portfolio scholarships

This is a regional round of a national programme, and the two halves live on different surfaces. The Brattleboro Museum & Art Center's own post carries the Vermont facts: it is "the Vermont affiliate for the Scholastic Art & Writing Awards", students in grades 7-12 (ages 13 and up) may enter in 27 categories, "The submission portal opens on October 1. The deadline for submissions is December 15, 2026", and "Gold Key, Silver Key, and Honorable Mention regional awards will be exhibited at the Brattleboro Museum & Art Center February 19 - March 6, 2027." The Alliance for Young Artists & Writers' own pages carry the fees, the category rules and the AI policy.

VERMONT-ONLY IS A REAL BAR AND IT NOW HAS ITS OWN FACET. The Alliance's regions index lists "Vermont Art Region - Brattleboro Museum & Art Center" and "Vermont Writing Region - Brattleboro Museum & Art Center", i.e. the whole state is one region run from Brattleboro, and a teen outside Vermont is judged somewhere else entirely. The add pass stored eligibility as student alone because no vermont slug existed and the nearest one, new-hampshire-vermont, is labelled "NH & VT only" and would have been a wrong label. This editor pass minted the vermont facet instead, alongside the twenty-odd single-state facets the site already carries (minnesota, indiana, ohio, connecticut, wisconsin, michigan, california and the rest), so the state bar is now a chip a reader can see before they pay $15 rather than a sentence buried in a description.

PHOTOGRAPHY'S WORK CAP IS 16, and it is one of only two capped categories. The Alliance FAQ: there is no limit on the number of works you may enter "with the exception of Photography and Portfolio", and "Each participant is allowed to enter 16 works in the Photography category". That sentence is also the photography affirmative for this call - BMAC's own page says "27 categories" and never enumerates them.

THE MONEY IS PUBLISHED, JUST NOT BY THE MUSEUM. BMAC's own page says only that "students can win college scholarships and other prizes". The Alliance FAQ names the figures: a scholarship committee "awards sixteen $12,500 Gold Medal Portfolio scholarships (eight writing and eight art)" and "Additional $2,000 scholarships are given to students earning Silver Medal Portfolios". Read the gate before the number: portfolios are open to grade 12 only, a portfolio entry costs $40 rather than $15, and the scholarships are awarded nationally after a regional Gold Key, not by BMAC. The eight art portfolio scholarships are the pot a senior photographer competes for; a grade-7-to-11 entrant is competing for the regional award and the February exhibition.

NO NOTIFICATION DATE IS PUBLISHED. BMAC gives the exhibition run (February 19 - March 6, 2027) but no date on which entrants hear, so resultsDate is an explicit null rather than the exhibition opening read as a notification.
