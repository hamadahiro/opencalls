Les Boutographies 2027

Prizes €1,000 €2,500 acquisition Exhibition

Les Boutographies is the European photography festival held every May in Montpellier, created in 2001 and named for a contraction of Boutonnet — the district where it began — and photography. The 2027 edition runs 8 to 30 May and its open call is live from 6 July to 22 November 2026. No theme is set. Anyone resident in Europe may apply, amateur or professional, with no condition of age or nationality, including photographers who have been selected before; applications through an agent are not accepted. Selection runs in stages: the festival's artistic director pre-selects from all entries, then a jury chooses work for hanging or for projection, with the answer given in the applicant's Photographer's Space account, and work not chosen may still be picked up for the Parallèle or Hors les Murs sections. What the festival gives back is unusually concrete. Photographers selected for the exhibition get three nights' accommodation, full coverage of the cost of transporting the work, €100 in author's rights, priority access to the portfolio readings and a place in the festival's book shop with sales remitted in full less card charges; the festival also lists €5,500 for acquisitions alongside production assistance and paid publications, and one work from the official selection is exhibited in Arles during the Rencontres, subject to the partnership being confirmed. Photographers selected for the projection get one night's accommodation, €100 in author's rights and a partner prize of production and an exhibition with the Toulouse lab Photon. Eight prizes are awarded: the Prix du Jury (€1,000 from the festival plus an acquisition by the City of Montpellier worth €2,500, given to a young European photographer whose approach the festival wants to encourage), the Prix du Public (a €500 cheque from Photon toward producing an exhibition, supported by La Gazette de Montpellier), and the Coups de cœur from Réponses Photo (a paid portfolio in the magazine), Photon (a produced exhibition hung for three months in Toulouse), Les Jours (a paid portfolio in the web magazine), The Eyes (two half-days of mentoring), the Jeune Public (honorary, awarded by young Pass Culture members) and Mutuelle des Motards (an acquisition from the official selection for their collection, on the theme the festival gives in English as liberty and support). Entries are 10 to 20 jpgs at 2,000px on the longest side, full-frame with no background except for diptychs, with any captions in a separate Word or PDF list alongside the presentation text; a CV is also required. Registration and payment are on the festival's own site through a photographer's account, which also recovers applications from previous years.
