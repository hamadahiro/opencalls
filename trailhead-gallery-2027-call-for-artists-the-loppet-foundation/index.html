Trailhead Gallery 2027 Call for Artists

Prize Solo exhibition

The Loppet Foundation — a Minneapolis non-profit that runs year-round outdoor programming from Theodore Wirth Park under a public/private partnership with the Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board — is booking its 2027 season at the Trailhead Gallery. The gallery sits inside The Trailhead, the Loppet's public building and the trail hub for walkers, skiers, bikers, runners and birdwatchers, so a show there is seen by thousands of park visitors across a four-to-six-week run. Photography is named among the accepted 2D media. The Art Committee gives priority to Minnesota artists whose work speaks to the connection between art and the outdoors, meaningful community connection outdoors, local flora and fauna, inclusion and diversity in the outdoors, sustainability and habitat restoration, wellness, or play. Eligibility as written is 'emerging and established artists in the Twin Cities and surrounding areas', and the Loppet states it strongly encourages BIPOC and Queer-identified artists to apply. Shows are 8–12 pieces, each no larger than 24 x 36 inches and up to 5 lbs, framed or otherwise ready to hang, with one or two larger works possible. Priority consideration goes to submissions emailed between 15 October and 15 November 2026; work sent after 15 November is still accepted and considered on a rolling basis as gallery space allows. This is an unpaid exhibition — there is no fee to apply and no stipend, and artists keep 100% of any sales made directly to buyers. Selection is by the Loppet's Art Committee, a volunteer group of artists, community members, and Minneapolis Park Board and Loppet staff.
