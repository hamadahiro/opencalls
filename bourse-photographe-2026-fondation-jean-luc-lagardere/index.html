Bourse Photographe 2026 — Fondation Jean-Luc Lagardère

Prize €10,000 grant

Annual €10,000 grant from the Fondation Jean-Luc Lagardère for a professional photographer aged 35 or under who wants to carry out a documentary photography production — in the social, economic, political or cultural field — in France or abroad. Applicants must already have published at least two documentary projects in the press, in a book or in an exhibition catalogue. The application dossier is submitted online and the project must be presented in French. Recent laureates include Marion Péhée, Aline Deschamps and Adrienne Surprenant.

DeadlineJune 14, 2026
Entry feeFree
EligibilityUnder 35, Professional only
LocationParis, France
Submit viaWebsite

Jury: Héloïse Conesa, Jury President · Gabriel Bauret · Christophe Caudroy · Emmanuelle Halkin · Patrick Le Bescont · Delphine Lelu · Irene Lombardo · Leo d’Oriano · Christine Yvaren

Organized by Fondation Jean-Luc Lagardère
