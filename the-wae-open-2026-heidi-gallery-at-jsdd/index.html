The WAE Open 2026

Heidi Gallery's annual open, run out of a 2,000-square-foot light-filled space with adaptive walls at JSDD in Livingston, New Jersey. The gallery was established in 2021 and is explicit about showing established, emerging and underrepresented artists together. The WAE Open is not thematic — all mediums are accepted, including installations, across three categories: 2D work, sculpture and floor installation. The 2026 jurors are Sharnita Johnson and Joel Tidey, and there is a $500 Best in Show cash award presented at the opening reception. Every entry, selected or not, is included in a digital exhibition that runs alongside the physical show. Entry is open to artists living or working in New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania or Connecticut, with one to three pieces per artist; work must have been produced in the last three years and no giclée reproductions of original art are accepted. Two-dimensional work is capped at 36 by 42 inches and 30 pounds and must be properly wired — no saw-tooth hangers — with gallery-format framing preferred; sculpture must stay within two feet in any direction and floor installations within a four-foot square and 50 pounds. Accepted artists are asked to write or record an audio description of their work, under 60 seconds, which is made available to gallery and website visitors. Work is hand-delivered on 3 October, the exhibition runs 8 October to 6 December 2026 with an opening reception and award on 8 October and a closing reception with artist insights on 6 December. All work must be for sale and the gallery retains 40% commission. Artists must carry their own insurance.
