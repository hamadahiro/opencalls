Bourse Laurent Troude 2026

Prize €8,000

Bourse Laurent Troude is a French photojournalism grant named after Laurent Troude, the Libération photographer. The 2026 edition is the grant's 8th cycle.

Eligibility is restricted to photographers under 30 with a proposed project on French territory. Subject matter is open across categories: political, cultural, social, or sports themes — but the methodological frame is specific: proximity photojournalism (a French tradition of close-engagement, on-the-ground reporting).

Prize package: €8,000 from La SAIF and Libération, a one-year Divergence Images subscription, exhibition/projection in spring 2027, and publication in Libération. AI-generated work is not accepted.

Submission is by email plus WeTransfer with 5–10 images. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 20; results announced April 2026.
