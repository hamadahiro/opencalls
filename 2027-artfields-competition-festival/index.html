2027 ArtFields Competition & Festival

Prizes $50,000 Grand Prize $25,000 Second Place $12,500 People's Choice 5 x $2,000 Merit Awards

ArtFields turns the whole of Lake City, South Carolina into a gallery for seventeen days each spring, and its signature visual-art competition is open for the 2027 edition — the festival's 15th year. Work is not hung on white walls: selected pieces go up in "galleries, warehouses, restaurants, shops, and other venues throughout downtown," so that, as ArtFields puts it, art is met "from grabbing their cup of morning coffee to getting a haircut." An average of 400 works are invited from the submissions, rated 1–10 by an independent panel of arts professionals through the ArtBooth platform. Photography is unambiguously in: the competition rules say "Artists may submit one 2-D or 3-D artwork in any visual art medium," and the entry portal repeats that the competition "is open to artists of all media." The prize pool is over $100,000 — a $50,000 Grand Prize and a $25,000 Second Place chosen by the jury panel, two $12,500 People's Choice Awards (one 2-D, one 3-D) decided by public vote, five $2,000 Merit Awards, and Southeastern Partnership Awards of exhibitions and opportunities. An artist may win only one of the top four. The entry is $25 for a single work; you may upload up to five images of that one work, and submitting a portfolio of separate pieces is grounds for disqualification. Eligibility is regional and strict: you must have lived in Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Virginia or West Virginia for at least six consecutive months before submitting, be 18 or older, and the work must have been completed in or after 2024. Work shown at a previous ArtFields is not eligible, and last year's top-four winners sit out a cycle. Submissions close 11:59 p.m. EDT on Sunday, November 1, 2026; invitations and declines go out by Wednesday, December 9, 2026; venue invitations follow by January 29, 2027 and the signed Artist & Venue Hosting Agreement is due February 5. Work must reach Lake City between March 5 and 13, 2027 (installations by March 20) and stay up for the festival, 9 April – 1 May 2027; artists other than installation-makers need not travel, as trained handlers install at each venue. Costs to weigh: you pay transport both ways and insure the work yourself, return shipping requires a prepaid label packed with the piece, and venues take 20% of any sale. Work is sold at the artist's discretion, and top-four winners agree to leave the awarded piece with ArtFields / the Lake City Creative Alliance for a further 90-day public exhibition, during which ArtFields has an option — not an obligation — to acquire it for its permanent collection, with the cash award as the full and only consideration. One technical rule worth reading twice: work "deemed not ready to hang or show as delivered will be excluded," so D-rings and wiring matter. ArtFields also bars anything "created solely through Artificial Intelligence (AI) software."
