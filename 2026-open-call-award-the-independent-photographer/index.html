2026 Open Call Award

The October round of The Independent Photographer's monthly award cycle, and the one with no theme at all: "Your choice: street, travel, fine art, landscape, documentary, portrait, and more. In October, we invite photographers of all ages, backgrounds, and experiences to unleash their creativity and share their best images". Judge: Maggie Steber, the documentary photographer and Guggenheim Fellow who led a Pulitzer Prize-winning photography team at The Miami Herald, has been featured in 16 National Geographic stories, contributes to the VII Foundation, and holds the Leica Medal of Excellence, a World Press Photo Award and the Lucie Award. Total prize pool $3,000 — 1st prize $1,500, 2nd $500, 3rd $300, and $100 to each of the seven finalists. Winners and finalists have their work professionally printed and shown at the platform's annual group exhibition at PCB Gallery in London, with TIP covering all printing, installation and display costs, and are featured to an audience the organiser states as 995k+ on Instagram and 205k+ on Facebook; every entrant also receives a free online portfolio on the platform. Fees are tiered: $15 for 1 image, $30 for 3, $45 for 6, and there is no limit on the number of separate submissions. Judging is anonymous — "the selection process is done anonymously to ensure that each contestant is judged fairly and only based on their work". Photographers retain full copyright. AI-generated images are explicitly not accepted; otherwise any photography-based work qualifies, film, digital, smartphone, tintype or camera-less, with all post-production allowed provided the result stays photography-based, and diptychs accepted. JPEG or PNG, minimum 1200px on at least one side and under 10,000px in height or width, maximum 10MB per image. Open worldwide to entrants aged 18 or over; there is no age limit on the pictures themselves, though the organiser says it is "trying to promote new content".
