RBSA Photography Prize 2027

Prizes £1,000 / £500 / £250 Exhibition

The Royal Birmingham Society of Artists' biennial photography prize, open to anyone working with any photographic process, anywhere. The RBSA is one of the oldest art societies in the country and only admitted photography into the Society in October 2022; this is the third show since that decision, and for the third time it expands to fill both Gallery 1 and Gallery 2 in the Society's Birmingham gallery. Professional and emerging artists based locally, nationally and internationally are all invited, and all photographic styles and media are eligible — work may be photography or digitally manipulated photography, and both 2D and 3D works are accepted, including installation pieces. Selection is by digital image of the original artwork only, and every entry goes through the same anonymous process judged on the quality and interest of the work; the selection panel is listed as TBC on both the call page and the exhibition pack. Three cash prizes are awarded — the John Hodgett Award of £1,000, a second prize of £500 sponsored by Judd Medical, and the Anne Judd Award of £250 — plus up to six Highly Commended certificates, and a catalogue featuring all selected works is published alongside the exhibition. Artists may enter up to four pieces in total. Entry costs £20 for one work rising to £48 for four, halved for artists under 35 (who must telephone the Gallery to pay at that rate) and free for RBSA Honorary Members and Next Generation RBSA Artists. Photographers should read the entry requirements closely: all work must be original, any manipulation must have been carried out by the artist, images created using AI are not accepted, work must have been made in the past three years and must not have been shown at the RBSA Gallery before, and all prints including digital ones must be editioned with the edition size stated. Image files must be JPG, at least 2,000 pixels across the smallest dimension at 300dpi. Work is to be framed unless an unframed presentation is intrinsic to the piece, works over 1.5m in any dimension must be discussed with the Gallery after submission, and artists supply and PAT-test their own equipment for video works. Sold work carries a 35% commission plus VAT. The exhibition runs Thursday 25 February to Saturday 20 March 2027, with a preview evening on the opening night.
