2026 Annual Members' Exhibit

Prize Exhibition

Umpqua Valley Arts gives its galleries over to its own members each autumn, and the 2026 edition is non-juried, has no theme and takes any medium: every active UVA Artist Member may submit one piece and it goes on the wall. Work must have been made within the last five years and must not have been exhibited at UVA before, must be original to the entering artist, and must not have been made with AI. Two- and three-dimensional work are both accepted, up to 60 inches on the longest side and 40 pounds, with a diptych or triptych measured as a whole; pieces arrive ready to hang with picture wire, since sawtooth hangers and clips are refused, and three-dimensional work needs a sturdy base. Artists may price the work or list it NFS, but a value is required either way for insurance, and UVA passes 70 per cent of any sale to the artist. There is no entry fee. The only requirement is a current UVA membership, which runs $65 a year for an individual and $95 for a family, $45 with a teacher ID and free with a student ID, and whose published benefits include 'Opportunity to hang one work of art at the annual Members' Show'. Applications close October 16 and artwork is dropped off on October 26 between 10am and 5pm, with mailed artwork accepted by arrangement with UVA staff; every participating artist signs an artist contract by that date. The exhibition runs October 30 to December 23 at the arts centre on West Harvard Avenue in Roseburg, opening with a reception on October 30 from 4pm to 7pm.
