The Beauty in Decay

TCG Gallery invites photographers and lens-based artists worldwide to submit work for The Beauty in Decay, an online exhibition about what remains visible as things change, weather and fall apart. The gallery's framing is concrete: peeling paint, a neglected room, a familiar object worn down by use — deterioration can expose details that were never part of the original design. It welcomes photographs that find history, tension or unexpected beauty in weathered objects, surfaces, places and the natural world. Digital, film, alternative-process and mixed-media photographic work is accepted. Work is reviewed for visual strength, originality, photographic execution, relevance to the theme and contribution to a coherent exhibition. Images generated by, or substantially altered with, generative AI are not eligible. Each entry takes up to three photographs — one is enough, the other two slots are optional — as JPG or JPEG files at least 1,200 pixels on the longest side, unwatermarked and unsigned, with a descriptive filename; multiple entries are permitted and the $15 fee is charged per entry, with fee waivers available. Entrants supply name, email and location plus title, year, medium, physical print dimensions and price or NFS for each work; website, Instagram, biography and image descriptions are optional. Selected photographs appear in a curated online exhibition with artist and artwork information, remain in the gallery's long-term exhibition archive, and may be promoted by email and through the gallery's social channels; the prospectus gives notification on 27 January 2027 but publishes no exhibition run dates for this call. Work may be offered for sale or marked not for sale, and TCG Gallery retains a 30% commission on anything sold through it. Copyright remains with the artist; selected artists grant nonexclusive permission for credited exhibition, archiving and related gallery promotion. The cutoff has a time: the call is headed "DEADLINE JANUARY 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET" and repeats "Submission deadline: January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET", with artist notifications on 27 January 2027.
