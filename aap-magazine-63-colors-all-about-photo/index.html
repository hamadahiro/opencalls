AAP Magazine #63 Colors

The 63rd printed issue of AAP Magazine takes Colors as its theme. The editors open with the premise that "life is a stream of colors" - red, blue, yellow and all the possible combinations between those primaries, black and white not forgotten - and ask for images "both where colors may not just influence the pictures, but where it may also be the subject solely". The call is explicitly open to any colour interpretation and names photojournalism, street, artistic, portrait, nude, landscape, nature, wildlife, urban, architecture, fashion, fine art and documentary photography as eligible approaches. What the magazine wants is a body of work rather than one-off frames: the brief asks "preferably a cohesive body of work or portfolio", and the judging runs in two passes - photographs are scored individually from 1 to 5 in a first round, then read as a unified group as they could be published in the issue. Work already published in AAP Magazine cannot be entered again. Twenty-five photographers are selected: $500 for first place with an exclusive interview, $300 for second and $200 for third, and the ranks from 4 to 25 are finalists. Every one of the 25 is published in AAP Magazine Volume 63: Colors, receives a free copy of the printed issue, which is sold and distributed through Blurb, and keeps a permanent place in the Winners Gallery on all-about-photo.com. The single juror is Sandrine Hermand-Grisel, the photographer, curator and editor who co-founded All About Photo with her husband in 2013 and is editor-in-chief of AAP Magazine. Entry costs $35 for the first three images and $5 for each additional image, with an early rate of $30 for the first three valid through 3 December 2026; up to 40 images may be entered, so a full 40-image entry costs $220 at the regular rate. Files are JPEG, 72 dpi, grayscale or RGB, flattened, under 2 MB, longest dimension between 200 and 1,200 pixels, no watermarks. Submission works through an account on the All About Photo website: register, upload to your gallery, then pay - and the organisers warn in capitals that an entry cannot be edited and images cannot be added once payment has been processed. The FAQ puts eligibility at "all individuals age 18 and older... professional and amateur photographers alike", while the binding Rules page sets the bar at the age of majority in the entrant's own jurisdiction and adds that the competition is void where prohibited and governed by the laws of California, so check your own jurisdiction. The published schedule is: competition opens 14 November 2026, closes 15 December 2026, judging runs 19 December 2026 to 15 January 2027, and winners are notified by email around 30 January 2027, with the winning images online after that date. Photographers retain full copyright in every entry, and cash prizes are paid by wire transfer with any foreign-currency transfer fees deducted from the winning amount.
