100 Emerging Artworks of 2026

Prize $1,000 prize

100 Emerging Artworks of 2026 is an annual open call from Arts to Hearts Project, an international art platform that publishes themed art books and runs related open calls. The 2026 edition is the fourth cycle of this title.

100 artists are selected — emerging and mid-career — across all mediums, photography included. The publication is a hardcover collector's book, distributed globally via Amazon, Barnes & Noble, and independent art bookstores. Past collaborator partners include The Jealous Curator and Singulart.

Tiered prizing: the top winner receives $1,000 cash, a 4-page feature spread in the book, and a glass trophy. Second and third place each receive 4-page spreads and trophies. All 100 selected artists are included in the published book, receive a certificate, and benefit from the title's international distribution.

Eligibility is open to artists 18+ globally. Each application can include up to 20 artworks. Fee is $35 (with an early bird discount available before the April 4 cutoff). The 2026 deadline was May 4.
