Colour Calendar Event

Prize Publication (calendar) prize

AlternativePhotography.com runs an annual Colour Calendar Event inviting artists to introduce colour into traditionally monochrome alternative processes. Eligible techniques include cyanotype, chemigram, anthotype, and others where color is normally restricted by chemistry.

The brief asks artists to push the chemistry — multi-toning cyanotypes, hand-coloring, layering processes, or building color into traditionally one-tone outputs.

AI-generated work is not accepted. Eligibility is restricted to alternative process work and requires AlternativePhotography.com membership.

Selection results in inclusion in the platform's published calendar. Submission is by email, single image. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 31.
