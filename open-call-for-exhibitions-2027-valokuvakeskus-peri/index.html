Open Call for Exhibitions 2027

Valokuvakeskus Peri (Photographic Centre Peri) is one of Finland's seven regional photography centres, working in photography and lens-based contemporary art from the Kilta Gallery in Art House Turku, a former fire station at Nunnankatu 4. It is now seeking exhibition proposals for its 2027 programme from artists, collectives and curators working professionally in those fields, and says it particularly wants to highlight the work of underrepresented artists and topics that have had little or no attention in the Finnish art field. The economics are stated plainly and in the artist's favour: Peri does not charge a gallery rental fee, and it pays an exhibition fee to every exhibitor. The number of shows is deliberately limited so that each artist can be paid properly — four exhibition periods of roughly seven weeks, with one or two exhibitions selected for each, the first opening to the public on 7 May 2027. Artists are part of the working community during installation and deinstallation, and the centre provides production support. The gallery is three rooms totalling 228.5 m² (107, 66 and 55.5 m², the last able to be darkened); partition walls take works almost anywhere, the ceiling has regular bolt points, and the street-facing stone window walls cannot be hung. Peri also says it wants to reduce unpaid application work: a proposal does not need to be production-ready, and its degree of finish is not itself a selection criterion. Proposals go through an online form with a PDF of the proposal and work samples (under 50 MiB) plus a CV. The jury is exhibition curator Maria Huokkola, photographic artist Emma Sarpaniemi and senior university lecturer in photography and author Hanna Weselius; the director takes part in the process to check each proposal is feasible with Peri's resources, and jury members declare conflicts of interest and stand aside on those proposals. The director also keeps open telephone hours during the application period, 10:00–12:00 on Friday 20 November 2026.
