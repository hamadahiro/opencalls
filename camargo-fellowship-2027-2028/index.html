Camargo Fellowship 2027-2028

Prizes €3,500 stipend Residency

Ten-week research residencies at the Camargo Foundation in Cassis, on the Mediterranean coast near Marseille, founded in 1971 by the American artist and philanthropist Jerome Hill and with more than 1,500 fellows behind it. Fourteen fellowships are awarded a year, seven to artists and seven to scholars and thinkers, across two sessions: 8 September to 17 November 2027 and 23 February to 4 May 2028. Each fellow gets a furnished apartment on the campus, a €350 weekly stipend (€3,500 over the ten weeks) and basic return travel to Cassis; the campus holds twelve apartments, a reference library, an open-air theatre and one artist's studio with a darkroom, though the Foundation supplies no art materials or technical equipment. Artists choose one of six subcategories, among them Visual Artists and Film, Video and Digital Artists, and must have maintained a professional practice for at least five years with a track record of exhibitions, publications, credits, awards or grants - the Foundation is looking for a fully developed, mature voice, not an emerging one. Teams of up to three may apply jointly, with a stipend and travel grant for each member. Applications are free and submitted in English through Submittable; supporting material may be in English or French, and an international panel of independent jurors selects. Every fellow leads one resident session for the group during the stay, and attendance at the others' is required. The Foundation has said this is the last edition to run as a public open call before the selection process changes in July 2027. Both the guidelines PDF and the Submittable portal close the window on 1 October 2026, 23:59 CET; a summary line on the Foundation's own apply page says the call runs to 30 September, so do not leave it to the last day.
