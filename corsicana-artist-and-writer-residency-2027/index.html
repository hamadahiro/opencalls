Corsicana Artist and Writer Residency 2027

Prize Fully funded 2-month residency

Two-month, fully funded studio residencies at 100 West, an unaltered 1890s Odd Fellows lodge in downtown Corsicana, fifty miles south of Dallas. Six artists and three writers are accepted for 2027 - two artists and one writer per term - across Winter (January 4 to February 23), Spring (March 1 to April 27) and Fall (October 11 to December 7). Residents are given a private furnished studio with lodging: the second- and third-floor artist studios are 2,400 square feet each, with twelve- and thirteen-foot ceilings, long drywall work walls, an intaglio press and large utility sinks. The residency also provides professional documentation of the work in photo and video, a mid-term studio visit from a regional professional, introductions to the Dallas/Fort Worth cultural network, complimentary rides to and from the Dallas airports, bicycles, and access to digital projectors, construction tools and a wood shop. Residents give a twenty-minute presentation on the second Thursday of the term and hold open studios on the last Saturday; optional studio visits with MFA students in Dallas, followed by a presentation, carry a $500 honorarium. Open to artists of international, United States and Texas origin, with applications limited to one person per studio. Ceramic, fiberglass and aerosol-based work cannot be accommodated, and sound-based work is generally not hosted, so applicants working with sound are asked to enquire first. Artists submit five examples of work and two references; the application fee is $30, and accepted applicants pay a one-time, non-refundable $200 administration fee by November 1, 2026. 179 artists and writers from the United States and eighteen countries have been hosted since 2012, and the 2026 cohort opened with the Vienna-based alternative-process photographer Kaja Clara Joo. Families and pets are not accommodated, and 100 West is reached only by a steep staircase.
