Winter Residency 2026–27

CONTACT Darkroom is a not-for-profit art centre and photographic darkroom in Totnes, Devon, housed in a repurposed abattoir and specialising in black-and-white and colour hand printing. Its winter residency is resource-based: the organisation's own page describes 24-hour-a-day access to fully equipped colour and black-and-white darkrooms, a dedicated workshop room, workspace and an open gallery, for stays of one week to one month between 1 November 2026 and 31 March 2027, with an ideal Friday start. Facilities include a 20-inch RA4 colour processor, three 5x4 black-and-white enlargers, two 5x4 colour enlargers, a large sink, black-and-white film development and an outside suntrap. The residency is designed for emerging and established photographers working on new or existing projects, testing out processes, or making work that responds to the surrounding landscape — the River Dart running from the Dartmoor moorlands to the Atlantic coast. At the end of a residency artists are invited, but not expected, to share work in a small exhibition, talk or workshop; darkroom inductions, guided walks, excursions and local gallery visits can be arranged on request. Applications are open internationally to artists working across photography, film and lens-based practices, and to those making interdisciplinary work engaging image-making, print and alternative processes. There is no application fee, and no closing date is published: complete the form on the residency page with your preferred dates and an outline of your intentions, and the darkroom will arrange a video call. Applications are considered on a rolling basis for stays inside that November–March window. The residency itself is self-funded at £500 per week for one week, £450 per week for two and £400 per week for three weeks or more; inductions to colour and black-and-white printing are charged separately, and private lodging within walking distance is around £50 per night.
