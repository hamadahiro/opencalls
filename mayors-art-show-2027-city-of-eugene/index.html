Mayor's Art Show 2027

Eugene's long-running annual exhibition of local artistic excellence, staged at the Hult Center for the Performing Arts from January 11 to February 26 2027 and opening the night of the city's State of the City address. Entry is free and each artist or collective may submit one work, completed after January 1 2025. Eight $500 awards are given: Mayor's 2D Choice, Mayor's 3D Choice, Jurors' 2D Choice, Jurors' 3D Choice, People's Choice, Curator's Choice, Hult Center's Choice and Best Depiction of Eugene. A curated selection then moves to the City Hall Gallery from April to July 2027. Applicants must live in Eugene, work in Eugene, or have a strong connection to Eugene and its land, and be 18 or older; the city runs a separate Teen Mayor's Art Show through Maude Kerns Art Center. Selection criteria are conceptual strength, artistry, innovation and diversity of materials and art practices. Two-dimensional work is capped at 72 by 60 inches framed and must arrive hang-ready with a wire and D-rings; digital video and sound submissions may run up to eight minutes. Artists set their own price and receive 70% of any sale. Optional application information sessions and one-to-one technical assistance run through late September.
