101 Art Book: Pattern Edition

Prize $500

Arts to Hearts Project selects 101 artists for a hardcover art book exploring Pattern as a visual and conceptual theme — repetition, rhythm, structure and surface — distributed globally via Amazon, Barnes & Noble and the ATH shop. All visual art forms are welcome, photography explicitly included. The top-selected artist receives a USD 500 cash award alongside publication; all selected artists are published in the book. Thirteenth edition in the 101 Art Book series; open to artists 18+ worldwide at all career stages.
