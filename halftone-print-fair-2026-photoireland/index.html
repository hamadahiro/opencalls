HALFTONE Print Fair 2026

The 12th edition of HALFTONE, PhotoIreland's annual print fair at The Library Project in Dublin's Temple Bar, running 28 November 2026 to 24 January 2027. Works are displayed salon-style and sold both at the venue and online at thelibraryproject.ie; the fair has raised almost €100,000 for artists since 2015 and supported the practice of over 800 participating artists, with sales to private, corporate and public collections. Applications are open to fine art printers, illustrators, designers, photographers and all visual artists and creatives, for works produced from 2024 onwards using any print technique — screenprint, stencil, risography, lithography, etching, inkjet printing and more — without any limitation of approach or concept. Submissions may include up to 10 artworks, with two images per work: the print itself and a photograph or mock-up showing it framed or mounted as it would be displayed. All submitted works must be available for sale. The gallery takes a 40% commission, which should be built into the price you submit, and artists invoice for the remaining 60% after the fair; PhotoIreland adds 13.5% VAT on top of the submitted price. Selected artists are contacted by 2 November 2026 and deliver work between 3 and 15 November, with unsold work collected 25 to 31 January 2027. The cutoff is not end of day: the organiser states "Deadline: 6pm Sunday 18 October 2026".
