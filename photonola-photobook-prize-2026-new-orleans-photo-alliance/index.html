PhotoNOLA PhotoBOOK Prize 2026

The New Orleans Photo Alliance's third annual photobook prize, decided around the PhotoNOLA PhotoBOOK Fair at the historic Patrick F. Taylor Library inside the Ogden Museum of Southern Art — Saturday 5 December 2026, 11am–4pm, inside the 21st PhotoNOLA festival (2–6 December). Open to any artist who has published a photobook in the past five years, with self-published titles explicitly welcomed and encouraged. Two cautions, both on the page itself: it gives the eligibility window twice and the two disagree — the guidelines say December 2021 to December 2026, while the entry form's own publication-date field still says "Book must be published between December 2020 and December 2025", which is last year's wording carried over. And as of 13 November 2026 the deadline sentence reads simply "DEADLINE: November 13, 11:59pm" - the organiser has dropped the "Sunday" that made the earlier wording self-contradictory (13 November 2026 is a Friday), so the cutoff is Friday 13 November, 11:59pm, and the 15th is not a fallback. The Grand Prize is $500 plus a Silver NOPA membership; an Award of Excellence and a Louisiana Award of Excellence — the latter reserved for a Louisiana resident, who is also considered for all three prizes — each carry a Silver membership. All three winners are offered the chance to be included at the 2026 Photobook Prize table at the 2027 PhotoNOLA PhotoWALK and Photobook Fair; the page words that as an opportunity, not a guarantee. You enter the book itself through the form on PhotoNOLA's own page: a PDF of the whole volume and a description of up to 600 words are required, a photograph of the book is optional. No juror is named.
