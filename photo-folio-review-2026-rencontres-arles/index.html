Photo Folio Review 2026

Prizes Portfolio reviews Exhibition

Official portfolio review programme of Les Rencontres d'Arles, held July 6–10, 2026 during the festival's opening week at the Maison de la vie associative. One-on-one 20-minute sessions with international experts — curators, editors, publishers, and festival directors. Packages run from 5 appointments (€200) to 20 (€550). Each year the experts vote for their favorite portfolio and select a Photo Folio Review Award winner, whose work is presented at the following edition — the 2024 winner Patrick Wack exhibited at Arles 2025. Online reservations only, open until midnight June 18 (Paris time), subject to availability.
