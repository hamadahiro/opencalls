The Magic of Light - Photography Open Call

Prizes $500 $250 $100 Solo exhibition

Art/Space 114 is a Los Angeles photography venue. The Magic of Light is one of its juried open calls — open to film, digital, and alternative process photography, including hybrid approaches.

AI-generated work is not accepted.

Selection structure: cash prizes plus a solo exhibition for the top winner. First $500, second $250, third $100. The first-place winner also receives a solo exhibition slot.

Selection is by Ann M. Jastrab, Executive Director of the Center for Photographic Art (Carmel-by-the-Sea, California).

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through EntryThingy with up to 15 images. Fee is $45. The 2026 deadline was March 28; results announced April 4, 2026.
