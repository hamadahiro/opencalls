MACK — Debut Photography Book Submissions

Prize Publication prize

MACK's annual submission window for first-time photobook authors. Founded by Michael Mack (former Managing Director of Steidl) in 2010, MACK is one of the most prestigious art and photography publishers in the world, releasing approximately 40 books per year with artists including Robert Adams, Alec Soth, and Wolfgang Tillmans. This window is specifically for artists who have not previously had a book published — seeking emerging voices and experimental approaches to the book form. Submit a PDF draft sequence and completed submissions form via email during the May 1–31 window only. No WeTransfer, Dropbox, or download links accepted. Email subject line: 'Submission: Debut Photography Book'. Response within one month after the window closes.
