Water ~ Sky

Landscape photography exhibition at Praxis Gallery in Minneapolis exploring the relationship between water and sky as elemental forms in landscape practice. The call seeks work that engages surface and depth, presence and distance, observation and sensation — sea, river, ocean, puddle, sky, cloud, weather, motion, horizon. All approaches welcome from documentary to conceptual. Thirty images selected for in-person exhibition at Praxis Gallery, July 18 – August 8, 2026. Open internationally.
