Keyhole

Prize Exhibition

Memento Mori runs a themed show roughly every month and this is the fifth of theirs in this directory; the briefs are short, specific and unembarrassed about being lowbrow, which makes them a genuinely different proposition from the usual juried-call circuit.

The practical gate is physical, not curatorial: ready to hang means hardware already attached, and the gallery says plainly it will reject anything that is not at drop-off. For a photographer that means a framed or properly mounted print with wire on the back, not a bare panel.

One clause is worth reading twice because it is specific to photographers: the gallery will not accept photographic work depicting another artist's artwork — street art included — without that artist's written permission. Documentary work made in the 40West district itself could fall foul of it.

Out-of-state artists pay shipping both ways, and the gallery says so bluntly: "Please do not submit unless you are prepared for this." What comes back is the hang, First Friday foot traffic and a sale at 60% to the artist, not a prize.
