Ploch Art Gallery 2027–2028 Season

Prize Exhibition

The Sharon Lynne Wilson Center for the Arts is a multidisciplinary nonprofit arts facility in Brookfield, in the Greater Milwaukee area, and its Ploch Art Gallery gives Wisconsin visual artists a route to a solo or group show with roughly 87 linear feet of professionally lit wall space along the outer wall of the theatre balcony, open to the public and reached by stairs or lift. The annual call fills the 2027–2028 season, which runs 1 September 2027 to 31 August 2028; exhibitions hang for one to two months and vary in length around the theatre schedule. Three kinds of proposal are accepted: an individual artist, a two-person, collaborative or existing artist group, and a curator proposing a themed show by several artists. Eligible media are enumerated as painting, drawing, sculpture, printmaking, photography, fibre arts, installation and mixed media; time-based media are not accepted and the work must be original, with no reproductions or giclée prints. Work must have been completed in the previous ten years, with a preference for newer and never-before-exhibited work, and because the centre hosts school groups it must be suitable for all ages. A vast majority of the work must be for sale and the centre takes a 30% consignment fee on any sale; exhibiting artists deliver, install and take down their own work, and nothing may be shipped. Selection for the 2027–2028 season is made by jurors Nina Ghanbarzadeh, a 2024 Mary L. Nohl Fellow in the Established Artist category, and Megan Woodard Johnson, a mixed-media artist and curator based in West Bend. Applicants must be 18 or older and must live in Wisconsin, be a native of Wisconsin, or hold a degree from a Wisconsin college or university; for a curated proposal at least half the exhibiting artists must meet that bar. An application takes 8–10 JPEGs, an image list, an artist statement, a bio of 250 words or less and a CV of up to two pages, with an optional 250-word exhibition proposal, and costs a non-refundable $20; two applications per artist are allowed. The application window is 1–31 October 2026 — the portal is not accepting entries before 1 October.
