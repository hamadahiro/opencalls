Issue 3: Art and Photography Submissions

Prize Publication

Wandering Lights runs two reading periods a year and publishes in March and September. The magazine's own schedule block: "To be considered for Issue 3, please submit between October 1, 2026, and December 1, 2026. Issue 3 will be published on March 1, 2027. To be considered for Issue 4, please submit between April 1, 2027, and June 1, 2027." December 1 2026 is a Tuesday, and the Submittable form's own end stamp resolves to the close of that day. The March 1 2027 date is a PUBLICATION date, not a notification - the magazine says only that you can "expect to hear back from us within a month after the current submission period closes" - so resultsDate is an explicit null rather than the issue release read as a result.

THE FEE: FREE ON THIS FORM, AND THE PAGE SENTENCE IS NOT ABOUT THIS FORM. The submissions page says "We have moved to Submittable and charge a small fee to cover business costs" and names no figure, which is why this row first went in as a priced-but-unquantified entry. The Submittable submission manager settles it, because the magazine runs TWO Issue 3 forms side by side and the fee column is rendered per form: Issue 3: Literary Submissions (/submit/367521/...) shows $3.00, and Issue 3: Art and Photography Submissions (/submit/367968/...), the call this row is about, has an empty fee column. Entering art or photography costs nothing. The fee field keeps the page's own contradicting sentence attached rather than hiding it, so the chip reads Free with an asterisk.

PHOTOGRAPHY IS NOT A SILENCE HERE, IT IS A PUBLISHED SECTION. The form enumerates "acrylic or oil painting, watercolor, oil pastel, sketch, digital art, and photography" and asks you to name your precise medium; Issue Two's table of contents credits Kris Carlson, Jonathan Paul-Faina ("35 mm Negative Photograph"), Julianna Kapp, Allyiah Miller-Smith, Gail Purdy and Ryan Erickson twice under the heading Art and Photography; the masthead lists an Art and Photography Editor; and Issue Two's cover, "Lunar Light", is a photograph by a contributor. If you want the cover slot, the form asks for "artwork that captures light in any form - a lamppost, a glimmer, sunlight, a candle".

RIGHTS AND AI, both quoted from the magazine: first serial rights only, "After first publication, all rights revert back to the author"; and on AI, contributors must "affirm that all work is their own original creation and has not been plagiarized, generated without disclosure, or published elsewhere (unless clearly stated). This includes AI-produced work." That is a disclosure requirement, not a ban - hence Accepted (must disclose) rather than Not accepted. Note what the magazine is not: it is not a paying market, and it says so on the same page.
