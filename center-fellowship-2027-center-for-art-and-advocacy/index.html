Center Fellowship 2027

Prize $20,000 × 6 fellows

The Center for Art & Advocacy's flagship fellowship — founded in 2017 by the artists Jesse Krimes and Russell Craig, and known until recently as Right of Return — gives $20,000 to each of six formerly incarcerated artists: $10,000 in unrestricted funds and $10,000 in project development funds. It is the first fellowship of its kind in the United States. Fellows join an annual retreat where they present their project proposals to the cohort, take feedback from their peers and meet previous fellows, advocates, industry leaders and funders, with time built in for New York City's cultural calendar. For the first time, one of the six places is reserved for an artist aged 18 to 24. Photographers are in scope on the Center's own evidence: it describes its 2023 Fellow Beverly Price as a "fine art photographer whose work focuses on the intersection of preventative justice and critical compassion" and its 2024 Fellow Antwan "Banks" Williams as "a published photographer", and the call itself invites creatives working in visual art, film, music, creative writing, performance, design and multidisciplinary practices — every discipline, in the organiser's words. Who can apply: artists directly impacted by incarceration, meaning those who have themselves been held in US prisons, jails or detention centers (applicants detained in migrant detention centers must have attained US citizenship by the time applications opened), aged 18 or over, at any career stage, with or without formal training. Individual artists only, no organisations or non-profits. Projects and applicants must be US-based, including all fifty states, Washington DC and Puerto Rico. Fellows must collaborate with an advocacy organisation while developing or executing the proposed project, though that partner does not need to be secured at the time of application — the Center will help connect fellows to advocates. A panel of external reviewers selects the cohort. Applications opened September 18, 2026 and close Sunday, October 25 at 11:59 PM; the cohort is announced publicly on January 12, 2027. There is no application fee.
