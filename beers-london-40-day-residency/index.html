BEERS London 40-Day Residency

Prize Residency

BEERS London runs a rolling, year-round 40-Day Residency for artists working in any medium, based in the UK or internationally, who are able to come to London. For forty days a selected artist receives studio and work space, an honorarium towards lodging, flight and materials, curatorial and professional development support, and the freedom to shape their own working schedule; the programme is designed to balance focused studio time with studio visits, professional development and optional public engagement, and concludes with a public presentation or exhibition of the work developed during the residency. Selected artists also receive professional documentation of finished works and promotion through the gallery's mailing list and social channels. Between two and four residency sessions are held each year and there is no application deadline: once submitted, an application stays valid for twelve months and is considered for every residency scheduled in that period, after which an artist may reapply. Applications are reviewed by the BEERS London team and guest curators on quality, clarity of proposal and feasibility, up to five artworks may be uploaded, and shortlisted applicants are contacted by email. The application fee is £20, or £25 if applying concurrently to the gallery's open call group exhibition; the gallery states the fee covers administration, selection review, platform and processing costs and does not guarantee acceptance. Participation does not guarantee representation or sales, and a standard 50/50 commission applies to any work sold. The inaugural resident, announced for the spring 2026 session, was the painter Georgia Dymock, whose residency closed with a solo exhibition in July 2026.
