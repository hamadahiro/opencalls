The Photographic Nude 2027

Prize Exhibition

LightBox's juried nude exhibit returns for 2027, and the Astoria gallery frames the subject through photography's own history: directors Michael and Chelsea Granger, who are also the jurors, open the call with Edward Weston at one end and Joel-Peter Witkin at the other — “from the classic simplicity of Weston to the complex, evocative style of Witkin, capturing the human form remains a timeless yet challenging pursuit among photographers.” The brief is deliberately wide: the artistic and creative view of the body and its form, celebrating the nude in photography by embracing a variety of styles, with the timeless elegance of a classical study as welcome as alternative and provocative work. Studies of the whole or partial human form, nude or semi-nude, are eligible. It is open to photographers worldwide working in any style or medium, and the printing method is explicitly not a gate — traditional, digital and alternative processes are all eligible, with fibre-based silver gelatin asked for traditional black and white, archival pigment inks for inkjet, and C-prints accepted. Entry is by email: the completed entry form plus jpegs at 1000 pixels in the long dimension, each file named with the photographer's name and the title of the work. The fee is $40 for up to five images or $60 for up to ten, non-refundable, payable by card, cheque, PayPal or Venmo; LightBox Supporting Members pay half and Associate Members submit at no cost. Submissions close at midnight on Sunday 10 January 2027, notification comes by email by the end of the day on 13 January, and the exhibit runs 13 February – 10 March 2027 at the gallery on Marine Drive, with accepted work due there by 6 February. What an accepted artist pays after selection is disclosed and worth planning for: prints must arrive matted, framed and ready to hang with the outside long dimension no more than 30 inches, to recognised gallery and museum standards — acid-free mats neutral in colour, black wood or metal frames that are new or in excellent condition, and acrylic rather than glass on anything shipped. The artist carries shipping both ways and must include a prepaid return label, though the gallery offers a cheaper route: matted pieces sized to 11x14, 16x20 or 20x24 will be installed in gallery frames for $15. Sales run through the gallery at a 40% commission with artists paid within 30 days of the close, and unsold work is available for pickup from 17 March 2027, with return shipping completed by 3 April 2027.
