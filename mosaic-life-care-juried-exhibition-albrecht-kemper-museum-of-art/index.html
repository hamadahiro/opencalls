Mosaic Life Care Juried Exhibition

Prize Group exhibition

Mosaic Life Care and the Albrecht-Kemper Museum of Art run this judged exhibition together for artists living within 100 miles of St. Joseph, Missouri. Selected work hangs in the Art Experience Galleries at Mosaic Life Care from 2 September 2026 and stays up for roughly six months unless it sells. Work is judged with ribbons awarded across ten categories, photography and digital art among them alongside oil and acrylic, watercolour, drawing, printmaking, ceramics, 2-D mixed media, fibre and textiles, and sculpture and 3-D mixed media; awards are announced at the opening reception. One $30 entry fee covers an unlimited number of works. Entries go to the Albrecht-Kemper Museum by post or email with a signed entry form, a completed W-9 and a separate photograph of each piece — JPEG files under 1MB named artist, entry number, title and medium, or printed photos of at least 4x6 inches labelled on the back — and the fee is paid online through the museum's call-for-artists page. Entrants under 18 need a parent or guardian signature. Work must be original and the artist's sole property, free of others' copyrighted material, and pieces shown in a previous Mosaic exhibition are not eligible; subject matter depicting cemeteries, death, violence, blood, car crashes or nudity is barred, and the venue cannot accommodate video or performance work. Work is priced at entry and the Albrecht-Kemper Museum takes a 30% commission, with the artist supplying a comparable replacement if a piece sells before the six months are up. Entries close 11:59pm CT on Friday 7 August 2026, with artists notified on 14 August and accepted work delivered on 1 September.
