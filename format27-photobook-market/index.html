FORMAT27 Photobook Market

Prize Stall at the FORMAT27 Photobook Market, Derby Market Hall

FORMAT — the UK's leading festival of international photography, established in 2004 and hosted by QUAD in Derby — is "inviting publishers, rare photobook traders, magazine dealers, independent publishers, equipment dealers, and everything in between to register their interest in taking part in the FORMAT27 Photobook Market". The market runs 12–14 March 2027, over the opening weekend of the FORMAT27 Biennale, in the historic Derby Market Hall, and the festival frames it as "exploring the possibilities of the photobook while showcasing bold new voices and seminal publishers". As with the festival's previous photobook markets, the space is pitched as a chance to meet publishers face to face and pick up books directly from the artists, and a free public programme of workshops and artist talks runs alongside it. Stalls sit inside an established market hall, so the audience is both festivalgoers and ordinary market visitors, and all participants are invited to the launch events and after-parties across the opening weekend. COSTS, stated on the callout and inclusive of VAT: full table £100, half table £55, double table £180, barrow £100; every price covers hire for the whole weekend and two chairs, and students and student groups get 20% off hire. Nothing is charged to apply — successful applicants are notified within 10 working days of the deadline and then invoiced by QUAD using the contact and billing details on the form, with payment requested once a place has been offered. PRACTICALITIES, from the application form: a full table is 6 ft x 2 ft 6 in (about 183 x 76 cm), a half table 3 ft and a double 12 ft at the same depth, and the barrows are 200 x 67 cm with lockable cupboards at the back, which the festival suggests suit smaller zines and publications; the organisers allocate spaces themselves rather than letting you pick a spot, and cannot confirm allocations until every expression of interest is in. The form also carries the cancellation terms, which the callout page does not: cancelling with at least four weeks' notice returns 75% of the hire fee and at least three weeks' notice returns 50%, so a late cancellation costs the whole table. Details on shipping, unloading and event timings go to selected traders closer to the event. This is a self-publishing and small-press opportunity rather than a juried exhibition call: the application is a registration of interest through the festival's Tally form, and applications are open from 19 October to 15 November 2026.
