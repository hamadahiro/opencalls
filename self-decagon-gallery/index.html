SELF

Prizes $1,000 Exhibition

Online juried photography exhibition from Decagon Gallery calling for self portraits that go beyond likeness — images made in the act of looking at oneself honestly, uncomfortably, tenderly or strangely, rather than vanity or performance. The brief welcomes work engaging with identity, aging, illness, grief, joy, gender, memory, solitude, family, transformation and the body, staged or unstaged, constructed or straightforward, as single images or series; what matters is that the work carries real emotional stakes and that the artist was willing to be seen. Juried by Douglas Beasley, whose own practice explores the spiritual and emotional aspects of people and place and how the sacred is recognised in everyday life. Selected photographs are shown in a curated online exhibition, published in a professionally designed exhibition catalog, and accompanied by digital badges and certificates. Open to photographers worldwide; all photographic processes and styles welcome. First place receives $500, second $250, third $150 and two honorable mentions $50 each — $1,000 total in cash awards — plus a Human Presence Award granting free entry to a future exhibition. Entry is $25 for up to 4 photographs, $5 each additional.
