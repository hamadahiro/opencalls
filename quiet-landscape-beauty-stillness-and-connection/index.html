Quiet Landscape: Beauty, Stillness, and Connection

Prizes Exhibition Catalog

PhotoPlace Gallery's autumn juried call for landscape photographs that reward slowing down. Rather than dramatic spectacle, the gallery is looking for pictures that invite reflection and reveal the subtle relationships between people, place, light and time — sweeping vistas and intimate details, untouched wilderness and cultivated fields. Natural, rural, urban and cultivated landscapes are all welcome, approached through traditional, documentary, conceptual or fine-art methods, and work may turn on solitude, atmosphere, changing seasons or the quiet moments that usually go unnoticed. Juror Kat Kiernan, formerly editor-in-chief of Don't Take Pictures and now Manager of Public Programs at the Morgan Library & Museum, will select up to 35 photographs for the gallery in Middlebury, Vermont and roughly 40 more for the online gallery. All selected work is reproduced in the exhibition catalog, kept permanently on the PhotoPlace website and promoted through the gallery's publications and social channels; online-gallery selections receive the same catalog inclusion and promotion as the wall selections, and all selected artists are eligible for exhibition awards. Entries open 8 August 2026 and close 7 September, with juror selections announced 21 September, prints due 23 October and the show running 6–28 November 2026 with an opening reception on 6 November. There is no limit on the number of images submitted.
