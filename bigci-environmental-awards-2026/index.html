BigCi Environmental Awards 2026

Prizes 4-week residency A$5,000 (×2 winners)

Annual environmental awards from BigCi (Bilpin international ground for Creative Initiatives), an established artist residency in Bilpin, in the UNESCO World Heritage-listed Blue Mountains of New South Wales, Australia. Two winners each receive a four-week funded residency at BigCi plus A$5,000 in prize money. Open to artists across disciplines — visual art, installation, multimedia, performance, music, photography, film and curating. Winners announced 22 August 2026.

DeadlineAugust 2, 2026
ResultsAugust 22, 2026
Entry feeA$40
LocationBilpin, NSW, Australia
Submit viaWebsite

Jury: Lea Kannar-Lichtenberger, Environmental Artist & Researcher · Brian Wallace, Founder, Red Gate Gallery · Dan Gottlieb, Artist & Museum Designer
