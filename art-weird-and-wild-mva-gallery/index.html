ART WEIRD AND WILD

A national call from the MVA Gallery in historic Bethlehem, Pennsylvania for the strange and the unconventional: "If your art is strange or unconventional, this is the exhibition for you. Please share your weird and wild images with the Gallery's art enthusiasts and collectors." The gallery places the show in the lineage of Surrealism, Dadaism, Expressionism, Metaphysical Painting and Magic Realism, and offers a long but open list of directions — dreams, nightmares and the unconscious; enigmatic landscapes; mysterious and supernatural scenes; bizarre creatures; eerie light and shadow; distortion and fragmentation; unexpected colour combinations; visual narratives of alienation, anger and psychological distress; subverted perspective; personal references to memory, eroticism and childhood; surprising juxtaposition of objects; metamorphosis of people, animals and objects — "and anything else -- ordinary or eccentric, subtle or dramatic -- that excites you. You have limitless creative freedom for this exhibition." Photography is a named medium and the prospectus is unusually welcoming about process and presentation: "All photographic processes are welcome", with framing in black or white preferred for a traditional presentation, white or off-white mats, and unframed mounting considered. There is no cutoff date for when the work was made, and work previously shown at MVA is not eligible. A small rotating committee of artists, gallerists, art reviewers, publishers, curators and academics juries the entries and has requested anonymity for this show; decisions are final and no feedback is given. Awards are an "Art of Distinction" award — a featured solo presentation in the gallery's Exhibition Room Two at a future date — a $200 Director's Choice award at the Executive Director's discretion, honourable mentions that carry free entry to a future MVA show, and an annual grand prize of a New York City exhibition opportunity chosen in December from everyone exhibited that calendar year. The exhibition runs 4–28 April 2027; artists are notified on or about 23 February 2027 and accepted work must reach the gallery by 31 March. Work has to arrive ready to hang with stretched wire on the back, under 48 inches framed and 45 lbs, plexiglass preferred over glass; sculpture is capped at 28 inches in any dimension. Artists pay shipping and insurance both ways and must enclose a prepaid return label. The gallery keeps 30% on sales.
