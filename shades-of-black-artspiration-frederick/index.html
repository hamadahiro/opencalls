Shades of Black

Prize Exhibition

A juried exhibition for Black History Month at Artspiration Frederick, the non-profit gallery and community art space on Toll House Avenue in Frederick, Maryland, on view 5 to 28 February 2027 with an opening reception on 13 February. Eligibility is reserved for artists who self-identify as Black and/or of African descent, and the organiser is explicit about why: the show exists to give Black artists a dedicated platform, and work does not have to address Black history or identity at all. The subtitle is A Celebration of History. A Vision for the Future, and the framing is that Black art cannot be defined by a single subject, style, history or experience — artists across mediums, generations and career stages are invited on equal terms. All media are welcome and photography is named outright alongside 2D, 3D, ceramics, sculpture and mixed media. Entry is $35 for up to three original works, submitted through CaFÉ with high-resolution 300 dpi files; two-dimensional work must be framed and wired, with sawtooth hangers refused outright. All work must be for sale, with 60% to the artist and 40% to Artspiration, and prices are locked once a piece is entered. The call is open to visual artists 18 and over residing in the continental United States. The juror is not yet named. Accepted artists are notified on 21 January 2027, drop-off runs 31 January to 2 February with shipped work due by 1 February, and pick-up is 28 February or 1–2 March; unretrieved work incurs a $50-a-day storage charge after fourteen days, so read the return-shipping terms before you send anything. Admission to the gallery is free and the space is accessible.
