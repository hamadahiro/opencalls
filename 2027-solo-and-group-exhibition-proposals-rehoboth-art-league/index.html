2027 Solo and Group Exhibition Proposals

Prize Solo or group exhibition

The Rehoboth Art League's Exhibitions Committee invites proposals for solo and group exhibitions in its 2027 gallery season at the League's campus in Henlopen Acres near Rehoboth Beach, Delaware. Open to members and non-members working in any media, including photography; artists who exhibited at RAL in 2024, 2025, or 2026 are not eligible. Solo applicants submit 5 works and groups 10, created no earlier than 2024, with an artist statement and CV. A limited number of proposals are accepted each year; selected artists are notified by August 31, 2026, and receive 65% of sales.

DeadlineJuly 27, 2026
ResultsAugust 31, 2026
Entry fee$45 ($25 members)
LocationRehoboth Beach, DE
Requirements5–10 images
