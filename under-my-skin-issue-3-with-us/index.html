Under My Skin Issue #3 — With Us (ARE YOU?)

Third issue of Under My Skin, the magazine published by the California Aggregation for Gender Diversity, a nonprofit building community, resources and public education around Transgender, Genderqueer, Intersex and Nonbinary (TGIN+) topics through history and art programming. Where the first two issues, Shadows and Euphoria, explored different parts of the self, issue 3 turns to the collective feelings of the gender diverse community and asks who is truly With Us: what being With Us looks like, how it feels when the people who say they love you vote for your demise, how politics isolate — and, on the other side, how people who will stand up for you are found. Photography and all other forms of visual art are welcome alongside poems, essays and articles; music and film can be promoted even though they cannot be printed on paper. Submissions are especially encouraged from artists who are multiply marginalised, chronically ill, disabled, trans, intersex, undocumented, incarcerated or formerly incarcerated, low-income and otherwise systemically excluded, and contributors may use a pseudonym. Contributors receive a digital copy of the published issue, credit lines and artwork descriptions alongside their images, and promotion through the organisation's website and social channels. The issue publishes December 1, 2026. CAUTION: the organiser's own events page still prints "Deadline: November 8, 2026" for this issue, while the CuratorSpace listing it links to — the only route that takes a submission — prints 14/11/2026 and is still open. The later date is published here; anyone relying on it should submit as early as possible.
