San Francisco Photobook Fair 2026

Prize Table at SF Photobook Fair

The San Francisco Photobook Fair returns to the Harvey Milk Photo Center on October 17–18, 2026, following its first edition in 2025. Photobook publishers, individual artists, collectives and zine makers worldwide can apply for a table to display and sell image-forward publications — anything from zines and hand-bound books to folios, sculptural books and coffee-table titles. Accepted vendors are notified July 16; tables are $200 (full, wall access), $150 (full) or $75 (half), and limited scholarships for free or discounted tables are allocated based on need. Vendors handle their own sales and may also propose a talk or workshop for the event.
