Urban & Pop Art Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

Juried exhibition at the Bee Cave Arts Foundation gallery in the Texas Hill Country outside Austin, celebrating work “inspired by city life, popular culture, fashion, music, advertising, graphic design, comics, typography, and the imagery that shapes our everyday world”. The prospectus is open to artists working in all art mediums, photography included, and the exhibit is open to Central Texas artists. Juror and curator J Muzacz is an Austin public artist and mosaicist who co-founded The Mosaic Workshop in East Austin. One fee covers up to three entries; work must be original, wired and ready to hang, and for sale, with a 30% donation to the foundation on any sale. The show runs October 10 – November 6, 2026, with a reception on October 10, 2–4pm.
