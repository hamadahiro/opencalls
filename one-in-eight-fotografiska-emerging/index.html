One in Eight

Prizes €1,500 Exhibition

Fotografiska Emerging and The Estée Lauder Companies' Breast Cancer Campaign invite emerging image-makers to submit work offering an authentic perspective on what it means to be affected by breast cancer, directly or indirectly. Documentary or conceptual images should be rooted in real life — hardship, strength, care, support, recovery and community. Each selected artist receives €1,500 and exhibition at Fotografiska Berlin and Stockholm (October 2026), with possible activations across Sweden, Austria, Germany and Switzerland.
