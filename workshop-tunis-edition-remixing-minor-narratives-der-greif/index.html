Workshop Tunis Edition: Remixing Minor Narratives

Der Greif, the Munich non-profit for contemporary photography, is running a collective workshop with Jaou Tunis 2026 and selecting its participants by open call. The brief welcomes all photographic genres and themes as long as the submission is a cohesive work-in-progress or completed project that challenges mainstream aesthetics; the open call itself is untethered from any theme. THE GEOGRAPHIC BAR: this call is open only to photographers and lens-based artists based in Tunis and the surrounding areas, and applicants must be available to attend the in-person workshop in Tunis on 26 October 2026, 2pm to 4.30pm, at the B7L9 Art Centre. Selection is by Ilaria Sponda, Der Greif's managing editor and project manager, an independent curator, editor and writer who co-curates Fotodoks and has been a portfolio reviewer for Hamburg Portfolio Review, Sarajevo Photography Festival and Leipzig Photobook Festival. What selected artists get is a place in the free collective workshop inside the Jaou Biennale's official programme, and their work seen by Der Greif's team of experts. Submission is free and is one project of up to 20 images, with the entry form setting a floor of 10; the workshop takes a maximum of 12 participants, all chosen through this open call. Der Greif's conditions of participation are the same non-exclusive terms it uses across its calls: selected artists grant a limited, non-revocable, non-exclusive licence covering inclusion in a Der Greif print publication and display in Der Greif contexts, with credit by name or pseudonym; publication is not guaranteed and no remuneration is paid, but anyone whose work is published in print receives a free copy and every submitter is offered a discount on further copies. Unselected work is archived unless the artist emails to revoke, and remains otherwise unused. DEADLINE CONFLICT INSIDE ONE ORGANISER: the Picter submission portal's own close field reads 18 October 2026, 9.59pm UTC (11.59pm CEST) and its results field reads 21 October, while the call's /terms page still prints "The deadline for submitting is October 12, 2026 (23:59 UTC+1)" followed by "Der Greif reserves the right to change this deadline at any time". The portal close is what actually gates a submission, so 18 October is listed; anyone reading only the terms page would stop six days early. Der Greif's own open-call board no longer lists this workshop at all, though sibling calls are still on it, so the Picter submission microsite is the only live surface — re-checked 11 October 2026.
