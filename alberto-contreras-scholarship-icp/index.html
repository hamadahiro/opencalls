Alberto Contreras Scholarship

ICP's scholarship honouring "the memory and creativity of Mexican photographer Alberto Contreras", covering full tuition and school fees for the 2027–2028 academic year for one recipient enrolled in ICP's onsite One-Year Certificate Program in Documentary Practice and Visual Journalism in New York — a programme whose published on-site annual tuition is $27,000 (standard $36,000), with a $1,500 lab fee, a $142 equipment insurance fee and a $60 application fee, $28,702 in total. Eligibility is "open to Latin American applicants currently residing in Latin America". Selection is based on application strength and portfolio; applications are reviewed by the ICP Scholarship Committee and finalists may be invited to a Zoom interview. Both the programme application and the scholarship form must be submitted by March 1, 2027; applications for the Fall 2027 admission cycle opened on October 1, 2026. Scholarships are available only to students enrolled in the One-Year Certificate programmes, not to part-time programmes or individual courses, and awards cannot be combined — an applicant may receive only one.
