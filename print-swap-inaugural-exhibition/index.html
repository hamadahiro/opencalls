Print Swap Inaugural Exhibition

Prize Exhibition prize

Print Swap is a new iOS app founded by London photographer Sam Faulkner. The app's premise is direct print-for-print exchanges between photographers — bypassing the gallery and market layer. The 2026 inaugural exhibition is the platform's launch event.

Fifty images are selected from app submissions and physically exhibited at The Print Space in London, opening May 7, 2026. All printing and exhibition costs are covered by Print Swap — selected photographers don't pay for production. Selected photographers also receive permanent Founding Artists status in the app, marking them as part of the original cohort.

Submissions are made through the Print Swap iOS app rather than a web form. Eligibility is open to photographers worldwide who can submit through the app.

Application is free. The 2026 deadline was May 1; results announced May 3, 2026.
