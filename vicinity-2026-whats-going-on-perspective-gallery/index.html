Vicinity 2026: What's Going On?

9th Annual Juried Exhibition. Inspired by Marvin Gaye's 1971 album exploring social conflict, the show invites artists to respond to 'What's going on?' through inner landscapes or broader human conditions — social, cultural, environmental. Juried by Jan Tichy, professor at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago in Photography and Art & Technology/Sound Practices, with work in MoMA and Israel Museum collections. Open only to photographers based in the Chicago area able to deliver work in person. Exhibition July 2–26, 2026 at Perspective Gallery, Evanston, IL.
