Alaska AIR Artist in Residence 2027–2028

Prizes Residency $2,000 stipend, travel & materials

Bunnell Street Arts Center, a multidisciplinary non-profit founded in 1991 in a historic Trading Post building in Homer, Alaska, runs Alaska AIR: juried, fully funded residencies of generally one month, scheduled across the Alaskan fall, winter and spring of 2027 and 2028.

One to four are awarded annually, and each carries a $2,000 stipend for a month (pro-rated if shorter), round-trip travel to Homer up to $1,000 in value, a $250 materials stipend and a private bedroom with private bath in a shared apartment above the arts centre or nearby in the Old Town neighbourhood. The studio is Bunnell's 1,000 sq ft main exhibition gallery, open to the public during gallery hours, and the equipment list includes a darkroom (no enlarger), an HD video camera and tripod, an HD/3D projector, a nipping press and a small printing press.

Bunnell accepts artists working in mediums "including (but not limited to) visual arts, literary arts, performing arts, social and civic practice, ephemeral art, moving image, movement and dance, music composition and performance", and looks for a clear, unified body of work alongside a specific proposal with a strong community engagement component — collective art-making, an Artist in Schools programme, a workshop, an exhibit, screening or reading. Residents generally give an introductory artist talk plus that community component.

Open to international, US and Alaska artists; applications are accepted 1 September to 15 October and applicants are notified by 31 December. The $25 application fee can be waived on a needs basis by emailing the programme before applying.
