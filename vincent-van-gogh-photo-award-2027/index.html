Vincent van Gogh Photo Award 2027

Prizes €1,000 Nuenen exhibition

An international photography competition run by the Rotary Club Nuenen charity in the Dutch village where Vincent van Gogh lived and worked. The 2027 edition's theme is 'Spirit of Light' — inviting photographers to interpret light through van Gogh's eyes. Entrants may submit up to three images, with a separate Student Award running in parallel. Winners share cash prizes of €1,000, €500 and €250 (with a matching student tier) and are exhibited in Nuenen. The winner is announced on 3 September 2027.
