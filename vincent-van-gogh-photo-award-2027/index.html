Vincent van Gogh Photo Award 2027

An international photography competition run by the Rotary Club Nuenen charity in the Dutch village where Vincent van Gogh lived and worked. The 2027 edition's theme is 'Spirit of Light' — inviting photographers to interpret light through van Gogh's eyes. Entrants may submit up to three images, with a separate Student Award running in parallel. Winners share cash prizes of €1,000, €500 and €250, with a separate Student Award tier of €500, €250 and €100 and are exhibited in Nuenen. The winner is announced on 3 September 2027.

DeadlineMarch 30, 2027
Prizes€1,000 / €500 / €250, €500 (student), Exhibition
Entry fee€18–€42 (€10–€20 students)
LocationNuenen, Netherlands
RequirementsUp to 3 images
Submit viaWebsite
ResultsSeptember 3, 2027

Jury: Jack Mostert · Peter Verver · Evely Duis · Ad Vereijken · John Geven · Michael Graste · Peter van Hout · Joost Keet
