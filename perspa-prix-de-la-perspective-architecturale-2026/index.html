PERSPA 2026 — Prix de la Perspective Architecturale

International architectural photography prize run by PERSPA, a French association based in Bourges. Categories span Fine Art, Aerial, Black & White, Cityscape, Minimalism, Night & Low Light, Geometry, Stairs, Historic & Heritage, Human & Architecture, and more; entries are single images. Grand prize €1,000; winning and selected works are shown in a physical exhibition in France and considered for the PERSPA annual publication. Open worldwide to professional, emerging, and independent photographers who own the rights to their work; one complimentary single-image entry for photographs taken in France.
