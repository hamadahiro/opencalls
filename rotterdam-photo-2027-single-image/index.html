Rotterdam Photo 2027 — Single Image

Prize Exhibition

The single-image strand of Rotterdam Photo's open call for its 2027 edition, held 17–21 March 2027 during Rotterdam Art Week under the theme 'VIBRANCE – The Pulse of a Living World'. Rather than a screen or a print in a booth, each selected image is turned into a large-scale public installation: a PVC print measuring 2.5 by 2.5 metres, mounted on the exterior of the festival's shipping containers and displayed in public space for the whole festival period, with production covered in full by the festival. Selected photographers are featured across festival and partner channels including LensCulture, Dodho Magazine and All About Photo, are published in and included in the festival programme, can take part in the Rotterdam Photo Talks and networking events, and reach an audience of more than 10,000 visitors in central Rotterdam. The category is open worldwide to professional and amateur photographers and image-makers working across photography and visual media; entrants must be at least 16 as of the date of entry. A professional selection committee assesses images over three rounds on visual strength, conceptual clarity, emotional and spatial impact, and relevance to the theme. Photographers may enter more than one category of the open call, and each submission is reviewed separately.
