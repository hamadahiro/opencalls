Darkness

Themed exhibition at Dusk Gallery in Santa Fe for the photograph that lives in the low light, in the quiet spaces between heartbeats — images that know the weight of an unlit room, portraits of the unseen, landscapes of the hour before dawn, still lifes of the barely visible, reckonings with the void through the lens. The gallery wants work that finds its power not in spite of the dark but because of it. Juried by Dusk founder Holly Hart, who selects 26 images for the gallery walls. The gallery covers professional Luster-paper printing, matting and 24×24 inch black museum-quality framing; artists supply only a high-resolution JPEG (500KB–5MB, 4,500px on the longest side). Exhibition runs 1–29 January 2027 with an opening reception on 1 January; prints are archived in the gallery's flat files for six months afterwards, photographers retain all rights, and sales split 50/50. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers. Entry is $40 for 5 images or $45 for up to 10.
