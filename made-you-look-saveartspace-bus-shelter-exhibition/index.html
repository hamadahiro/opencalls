Made You Look — SaveArtSpace Bus Shelter Exhibition

Prize Public exhibition

SaveArtSpace puts artwork on Miami bus shelter ad space for at least a month from January 22, 2027, curated by Pangea Kali Virga. The brief asks for maximalist, witty, attention-grabbing work that reclaims the visual landscape from passive consumption. Open to artists of all ages; fees are tax-deductible donations that buy the ad space, and anyone who cannot afford to donate may submit by email instead.
