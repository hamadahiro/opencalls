Curatorial Research Fellowship 2027

Prize €20,000

Les Rencontres d'Arles offers a curatorial research fellowship of up to €20,000, open to all exhibition curators, for an unpublished project connected to the image and to photography. It runs in two phases: a period of research around an exhibition project, and then — if the festival validates that research after six months — production of the exhibition itself. The money splits the same way. €5,000 is paid to the winner outright as an author's fee; the remaining €15,000 is conditional on validation and is a production budget steered by the Rencontres, covering loans of works, transport to Arles and back, production of the works (prints, framing, wallpapers, video and so on), the curator's invitation and, where applicable, the artists' costs in Arles during opening week or the installation, and insurance. Scenography, communication, installation, de-installation and invigilation are carried by the festival on top of that budget. In exchange the curator undertakes to select the works making up the exhibition's corpus and settle their presentation and reproduction rights, to conceive and write a presentation text for the selection of photographs, and to be present during the festival's opening week and take part in the guided tour of the exhibition. The application is a single PDF of no more than 10 MB, in the order the organiser specifies: a presentation text for the proposed exhibition project of at most 5,000 characters; the corpus of works and artists envisaged, with visuals strongly recommended; a provisional research budget of €5,000; a research schedule; a provisional production budget of €15,000; and the curator's biography, with training, full contact details and a list of the projects and exhibitions they have realised. Texts may be in French or English, ideally both. Applications opened on 16 September 2026 and close on 30 November 2026; the winning projects are announced at the end of February 2027, the research phase runs to summer 2027, and the study is delivered between September and mid-October 2027, after which production begins if the project is validated. There is no application fee. Note that this is a call for exhibition curators, not a call for photographers to submit their own pictures.
