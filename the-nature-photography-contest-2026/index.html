The Nature Photography Contest 2026

International online nature photography competition across ten categories — Natural Landscapes, Wildlife, Macro, Underwater, Birds, Plant Life, Night World, Environmental Impact, Sharing the Planet and Funny Nature — with conservation built into the entry model: every participant funds a tree through One Tree Planted, and winners choose where planting happens. Photography of the Year wins €1,000, with €100 for each category winner; finalists must supply original RAW/DNG files for verification, and digital art is not accepted. Open worldwide to photographers 18+. CAUTION on the closing date: both surfaces now print November 30, 2026 — the homepage timeline carries a bold "Extended Deadline: November 30, 2026" (visible markup, not an HTML comment) and the rules page reads "The registration period will end on November 30, 2026" — but a further move stays discretionary ("We could extend the participation period if the registration phase has not ended and the costs have not been covered").
