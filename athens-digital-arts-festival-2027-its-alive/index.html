Athens Digital Arts Festival 2027 — It's Alive

The 23rd edition of Greece's longest-running digital arts festival returns to Athens in spring 2027 on the theme It's Alive, about techno-animism and the blurred line between humans and machines. Photographers enter through the Digital Image category, which takes a series of up to five works in one application. There is no entry fee.
