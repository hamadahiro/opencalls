Eco-Process Artist Residency 2026

One-month summer residency at Blue Cabin Floating Artist Residency in Metro Vancouver (August 2026) for emerging lens-based artists. Program focuses on eco-processing and alternative photographic techniques such as phytogram, lumen printing, cyanotype, and other sustainable photographic processes. Up to 3 mentorship sessions with interdisciplinary artist Morgan Sears-Williams. Blue Cabin is situated on the traditional territories of the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh Nations. Priority given to LGBTQ+ 2S emerging artists with some photo/lens-based experience. Open to emerging artists in Metro Vancouver and Indigenous territories/reserves connected to the region. Produced by grunt gallery in collaboration with Blue Cabin Cooperative.

Prize $3,500 prize
