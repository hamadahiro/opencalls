Musa International Award for Female Photographers 2026

Prizes Exhibition €200 expenses Portfolio review

Eighth edition of the Musa Award, run by the Lecco photography school Musa Fotografia and going international for the first time in 2026. It is a portfolio prize: each entrant may submit up to three projects of 8 to 25 images, in colour or black and white, mixed technique or other media, assembled as a PDF with a project statement and biography. The theme is "Reclaiming the Frame" - repossessing the power to decide what is worth framing and narrating - and the organisers are explicit that the subjects need not be women, since the point is to see the whole world through women's eyes; every genre is welcome, from architecture and reportage to abstraction and nature. Open to female photographers over 18, amateur or professional, resident in a published list of European countries that includes the EU member states plus the UK, Ukraine, Switzerland, Norway, the Balkans and the microstates. The winner's package is substantial: twenty fine-art exhibition prints produced and donated by Fotofabbrica, an exhibition at the Colorno Photo Life Festival, a screening at the Biennale di Fotografia Femminile in Mantova, a half-hour portfolio review with its artistic director Alessia Locatelli, an editorial meeting with Grazia Dell'Oro of the publisher EMUSE, a filmed interview with curator Loredana De Pace, and €200 towards expenses; reportage projects also exhibit at the Festival Off della Fotografia Etica, and Italian entrants compete for a further show at the Festival della Fotografia Italiana. Entry is €25 for one project, €45 for two and €65 for three, paid at registration with the PDF emailed at the same time. Winners are announced in December 2026.
