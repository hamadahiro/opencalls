Graz Residency for International Photographers (GRIP) 2026

One-month residency for early-career photographers and lens-based artists in Graz, Austria. Fifth edition of GRIP, a joint initiative by Camera Austria and Kulturvermittlung Steiermark. Resident works on an ongoing project or develops new work, with access to Camera Austria's 11,000+ volume study library. Residency concludes with a presentation in Camera Austria's exhibition and library space. Open to international early-career artists working with photography and/or lens-based arts. Apply by email with informal statement of purpose and PDF portfolio.

Prize €1,000 stipend prize
