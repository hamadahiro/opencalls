REFLECTIONS

Prize Group exhibition

Kunsthalle Burkamp in Haltern am See is assembling a group exhibition on the theme REFLECTIONS, planned for 2 May to 18 July 2027 with a vernissage. The theme spans reflection as an optical phenomenon and reflection as inner, mental movement: mirrorings on shimmering materials, water, glass, light and shadow, and images caught between visibility, likeness and illusion — and also RE-FLECTIONS as echo, the return of memories, emotions and historical traces that change in the retelling. The building is a former Haltern glassworks, and the call makes a point of that history: glass, both transparent and reflective, is meant to carry particular weight in the show. The call is open to artists of all ages; no particular training or exhibition record is required. Media named as welcome include painting, drawing, sculpture and photography, as well as other forms suited to the spaces, but large-format installations, film and elaborate projections cannot be realised for technical reasons. Both existing works and concepts for new production may be submitted. Each application may carry 1 to 10 works. Selection is made by the Kunsthalle's curatorial team, and selected artists are contacted around the turn of the year 2026/27. There is no application fee. Artists who are selected pay a participation fee towards the exhibition's costs, expected to be €150–€350 depending on how much external funding the Kunsthalle secures, with the exact figure given on acceptance. Artists deliver their work to Haltern am See themselves; installation and deinstallation are handled by the Kunsthalle team or jointly with the artist, and the Kunsthalle organises sales. Material needed for presentation — plinths, framing for photographs — is the artist's to provide after consultation. Applications go by email as a single PDF containing a statement on the work and its relation to the theme (max. one A4 page), images with title, year, medium and dimensions, a short self-introduction for artists new to the Kunsthalle, and any special technical requirements.
