We: Culture

Prize Museum exhibition

International group exhibition at CICA Museum in Gimpo, South Korea, gathering work on culture in its many registers - multiculturalism, identity, subcultures, social media culture, pop culture, and ideas of nation, religion and race. Photography is named first among the welcomed media and "Photographs & 2D Digital Art" is one of four submission categories alongside video and interactive art, physical works, and performance. Submission is free and by email: up to five JPEGs and/or three links to video works, plus a bio. Selected photographic and digital work is printed at 10x15 or 10x10 inches, framed and installed by the museum's team; artists sending physical work ship at their own cost, with 2D pieces capped at 39 inches on the longest side and wall-mounted work at 10 kg. Accepted artists contribute $78 USD (110,000 KRW) per piece toward exhibition processing and maintenance. The show runs April 28 - May 16, 2027, and the museum notes the dates are subject to change.
