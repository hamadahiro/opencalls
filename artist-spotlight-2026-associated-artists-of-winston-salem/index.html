Artist Spotlight 2026

Once a year the Associated Artists of Winston-Salem takes over the Main Gallery of the Milton Rhodes Center for the Arts — the Arts Council campus building in downtown Winston-Salem — for what it calls a “highly competitive juried exhibit” of recent bodies of work by a small number of its members. The ask is a portfolio rather than single images: artists may submit up to 20 pieces of which roughly 5–8 are chosen, and the prospectus wants “a common thread that reveals an artist’s commitment to a theme, concept, or aesthetics”. Selection is by a panel of three to five jurors described only as “acclaimed local artists”; no names are published. Awards are $250, $125 and $75 for first, second and third place. Photographs are explicitly eligible — “Hand-pulled prints, original digital prints, and photographs are all acceptable” — while printed reproductions of paintings or drawings are not. Entry requires AAWS membership, which is bought online and is not itself juried ($75 a year Associate, $20 for students), and anyone featured in an Artist Spotlight in the past three years is barred. Results are posted to ShowSubmit accounts by the end of October 23. Work is dropped off on November 2 and collected on January 30, 2027; the show runs November 6, 2026 – January 30, 2027, opening with a free public reception on the 6th. Sales split 70/30 in the artist’s favour.
