TIME: 11th Annual Juried Art Exhibit

Prizes $600 in cash awards Free exhibition space

Art in the Loft is a 7,000-square-foot nonprofit gallery on the third floor of the historic Center Building in downtown Alpena, Michigan, and TIME is its 11th annual juried exhibit, mounted in celebration of the organisation's 25th anniversary. The theme is open to interpretation, abstract or literal, and the call is explicit that it takes work 'in all media including but not limited to: Oil, Acrylic, Watercolor, Photography, Pastel, Drawing, Print, Mixed Media, Sculpture, Assemblage, Installation, Fabric, and Film'. The juror is Thane Whitscell, an Alpena photographer whose images have been shown in galleries, museums and on billboards in Detroit, Las Vegas, Houston and Alpena, and whose birth photography is on permanent display in the obstetrics and gynaecology unit of MyMichigan Medical Center in Alpena; he sold his photo studio in 2015 and has been making time-themed images since. Awards are chosen by the juror in person once the show is hung: $300 and free exhibition space for the following year to Best of Show, $200 and one season's free space for second, $100 for third, plus a $50 gallery gift card for the People's Choice, which is decided by paid votes at $1 each cast in the gallery or online. The exhibition runs October 1 to November 7, 2026, with the opening and awards reception on October 1 from 6 to 8pm. Entry is $35 for the first work and $10 for each additional, up to three submissions; work must be original and made in the last three years, and crafts, kit work and straight reproductions are excluded. Accepted work can be hand-delivered between September 6 and 25 or shipped to arrive by September 25 — include a return label if you need it sent back — and must arrive framed, wired and gallery-ready. The gallery takes a 35% commission on sales.
