2027 Selected Exhibitions Program

Prize Exhibition

Capture Photography Festival's Selected Exhibitions Program is the juried core of its April festival in Vancouver. Rather than mounting shows itself, Capture selects independently organised lens-based exhibitions and folds them into a month-long, city-wide programme; the 2027 festival runs 1-30 April. Applications are open from 8 September to 18 October 2026 at 11:59pm PST, for exhibitions presented in galleries and informal spaces during April 2027. You do not have to be Vancouver-based to apply and you can apply as an independent artist, but the exhibition must take place in Metro Vancouver during at least part of April and a confirmed venue is required at the point of submission - if the space is not your own, Capture asks for a confirmation letter from the venue plus an exhibition floorplan as a single PDF. The festival is explicit that it cannot provide venues, and points applicants at community galleries, retail spaces, empty storefronts, libraries and universities, or their own studio. The 2027 jury is Dr. Curtis Collins (Executive Director, Audain Art Museum), artist Michelle Sound, curator and arts administrator Gaetane Verna, and Emmy Lee Wall (Executive Director and Chief Curator, Capture); they are looking for proposals presenting primarily lens-based work of artistic excellence across a diverse range of subject matter. A submission takes exhibition and venue details, artist and curator bios, and up to seven images at 300 dpi with a PDF of credits naming each work's specific medium - scans of negatives, transparencies, photographic prints and digital renderings are all accepted, and unfinished work is not. The fee is $20, which includes a 2027/2028 Capture Photography Festival Society membership; the festival invites anyone for whom the fee is a barrier, or who is already a member, to write for a waiver. Selected exhibitions receive a full page in the Capture Catalogue distributed free across Greater Vancouver, a webpage with up to seven images on the festival site, priority coverage on Capture's social channels, a Capture logo vinyl for the venue, the chance to join festival programming such as gallery hops, and a one-year membership. There is no cash award and no production funding. The separate Events Program - talks, screenings, tours, workshops - is free to enter, is not jury-evaluated, and opens early in 2027.
