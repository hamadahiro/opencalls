Arty Rat Magazine Open Call — November 2026

Arty Rat is an independent art journal run from Vienna by curator Oleksandr Dyvnich, showcasing contemporary visual art, illustration and "bold creative voices from around the world"; the open call page addresses itself to "artists and photographers". This is the next rolling cycle: submission is free, open worldwide to applicants 18 or older, and you may submit up to six works as JPG or PNG, either through the form on the call page or by emailing the files. Selected artists receive a free digital copy of the magazine as a PDF, photographs of their printed magazine pages and a feature on the journal's Instagram; two selected artists have their work on the magazine covers, and one selected artist takes part free of charge in a group exhibition at The Names Gallery in Vienna — a show the call page dates only as "the end of August" with no year, while the organiser's separate Vienna exhibition page advertises a Names Gallery show on November 14-15, so which show that free place applies to is not settled on either surface. A participation fee applies only if the work is selected, and the organiser lists the options openly: €35 for publication in the magazine, €65 for publication plus an artist's page on the website with biography, statement and photographs of works, €100 for a curatorial review covering magazine and website, and €100 for the Vienna exhibition. The fee is non-refundable. The most recent issue, Issue 5, was published in September 2026.
