2027 CVG Show

Prize Up to $10,000 in cash and purchase prizes

Twentieth annual all-media juried competition at Collective Visions Gallery in Bremerton, Washington, with Photography/Digital as one of three judged categories alongside Two-Dimensional and Three-Dimensional art. There is no theme — artists are asked simply for their strongest work in any medium or genre. Up to $10,000 in cash and purchase prizes is awarded across the categories. The juror is Jessica Wilks, Director of Curatorial and Interpretation at the Tacoma Art Museum, where she oversees exhibitions, collections and interpretive strategy after more than twenty years of museum work; her current projects include a reinstallation of the museum's permanent collection and a focused exhibition of Cree artist Kent Monkman. Jurying is blind — entrants' names and cities are withheld from the juror — and awards are judged in person from the original works rather than from files. Open to Washington State and Oregon residents aged 18 or over as of August 18, 2026. Work must have been completed on or after January 1, 2022, and pieces made in a class or workshop or under an instructor's direction are not eligible. Entrants must submit two works; a single entry is not accepted, and a third and fourth may be added at $10 each. AI-generated imagery is not accepted, though standard digital editing — sharpening, noise reduction, colour correction — is allowed for photo and digital work. Work sold during the show carries a 40% gallery commission. Submissions open August 18, 2026 and close November 7.
