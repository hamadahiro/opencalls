THE BLANC Artist Residency 2027–2028

Prizes Residency $500 stipend

THE BLANC gives emerging and mid-career visual artists a fully subsidised 300 sq ft studio in Midtown Manhattan for two to three months, plus a $500 materials stipend. The studio is non-live-in, so artists must be in New York for the whole period. Four sessions run from May 2027 to April 2028.

DeadlineFebruary 15, 2027
ResultsMarch 31, 2027
Entry fee$25
Eligibility18+
LocationNew York, NY
RequirementsUp to 10 images + project proposal

Organized by THE BLANC
