FULL CIRCLE

Prize Group exhibition

PARC Art Gallery is the nonprofit gallery inside the Plymouth Arts & Recreation Complex in Michigan, a building shared by more than thirty independent arts, education and recreation organisations. It is directed and curated by Élevé Art Gallery and is open to the public seven days a week, 10am to 8pm. FULL CIRCLE asks for original 2D and 3D work about cycles, connection and return: the seasons, growth, repetition and the passage of time; relationships, community and the ways people and places are linked; coming back to a person, place, memory or tradition; circles, rings, spirals and spheres as visual form; and ideas of completion, balance and wholeness. The gallery calls these starting points rather than requirements, and says it is drawn to work that invites viewers to look twice - to notice a pattern, connection or relationship that is not immediately apparent. Photography is named in the gallery's own list of eligible original media, alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, mixed media and original digital illustration, and photographs are specifically exempted from the ban on prints; giclee and fine-art reproductions of an existing painting, drawing or photograph are not eligible, and no AI-generated work of any kind is accepted. Entry is $35, non-refundable, for one to three pieces. Work must be original, independently completed without instruction, previously unshown at PARC, offered for sale, dry and finished, and suitable for all ages - the gallery serves visitors from toddlers upward and reserves the discretion to decline submissions on that basis. 2D work must hang on a wire (no plastic-covered wires, clips or sawtooth hangers), be professionally framed or have finished edges, and stay within 60 inches wide and 40lbs; 3D work must be freestanding, no taller than 40 inches and no heavier than 100lbs. Open to artists 18 and over who live or work in Michigan, with artists from elsewhere considered case by case provided the work can be dropped off and collected in person; if shipping is the only option the artist pays all costs. Notifications go out 6 September, drop-off is 8 September from 10am to noon, and the exhibition runs 10 September to 1 November 2026.
