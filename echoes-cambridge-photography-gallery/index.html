ECHOES — Cambridge Photography Gallery

Open call on the idea that every photograph is an echo — of the past, of visited spaces, lived experiences and fleeting encounters, and perhaps even of things that never happened: untold stories, imagined narratives, distant futures. Both single images and series are welcome, with no single correct approach to the theme. Selected work is shown in an online exhibition July 15–30, 2026, with priority consideration for the gallery's physical Cambridge exhibition in October.
