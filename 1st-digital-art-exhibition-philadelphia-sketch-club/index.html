1st Digital Art Exhibition

Prizes $300 Sketch Club Medal

The Philadelphia Sketch Club — founded in 1860 and the oldest artists' club in the United States — opens its first ever Digital Art Show, an open juried exhibition at its Camac Street galleries. Every submitted work must include a substantial amount of digitally created content, which suits composited, layered and heavily manipulated photo-based work; the club runs a separate annual PHOTOgraphy exhibition for straight photography and does not publish where the line between the two falls. Work must be original, not previously shown at the PSC, framed and wired for hanging, no larger than 24 inches in any dimension excluding the frame and no heavier than 30 lbs; prints deemed low-quality on delivery will not be hung. Artists may submit up to ten works, with a maximum of two accepted per artist. Works made with generative AI are not eligible in any PSC exhibition. Juror Perry Santulli selects the show, and prizes of $300, $200 and $100 are awarded with Contemporary Sketch Club Medals and certificates, plus at least three honourable mentions, presented at the reception on 11 October. All work must be for sale; the club takes 25% commission. The exhibition runs 4–25 October 2026.
