Artist Residency — July–December 2027

Kimmel Harding Nelson awards up to seventy-eight juried residencies a year to visual artists, writers, composers and interdisciplinary artists from across the United States and abroad, and this deadline fills the 2027 Session 2 window, July 12 to December 17 2027. Residencies run two to eight weeks and are entirely self-directed. Each resident gets a private bedroom and bathroom, a private studio and a $175 weekly stipend; writers and visual artists usually share an apartment with one other resident. Travel is the artist's own cost except the $100 cab fare from Omaha or Lincoln on arrival and departure, which the Kimmel Foundation pays. Selection is by a blind jury of external professionals who see only anonymous work samples and two anonymous statements — a statement of submitted works and a statement of intent — so names, credits, awards, universities, employers and gallery names must be stripped from every file. The work-sample guidelines state that "all types of visual art are accepted including but not limited to photography, painting, sculpture, ceramics, printmaking, mixed media, video, performance, drawing, or textiles", with the caveat that KHN does not hold facilities specific to every process. A number of "transitional" residencies are reserved for artists within two years of the application deadline, and a number are reserved each year for Nebraska artists. The $35 application fee cannot be waived. The application portal opens November 1 2026; results are emailed by May 1 2027.
