After Dark 2026

Prizes $500 Exhibition

Maryland Federation of Art's annual nocturnal exhibition, shown in MFA's Virtual Gallery from 15 October to 14 December 2026. After Dark explores the quiet, mysterious world that emerges once the sun goes down — the flicker of a single lamp in an open window, the starlit silence of a rural field, the way night transforms how we see, feel and imagine. MFA invites limited palettes, subtle contrasts and softened forms that express the stillness and introspection of twilight, and the show is assembled from paintings, photographs, installations and mixed media works. All MFA exhibitions are blind juried, with names and identifying information hidden from the juror; this edition is selected by Kevin Murphy, Eugénie Prendergast Senior Curator of American and European Art at the Williams College Museum of Art. Awards designated by the juror total $500, announced 2 November. Entry is open to artists worldwide with the exception of sanctioned countries, with up to 6 original works made in the past three years; work previously shown in MFA's Virtual Gallery is not eligible. Only artists in the 48 contiguous United States may set a sale price, and MFA reimburses up to $40 of their shipping on online sales; everyone else lists Price on Request and MFA refers enquiries to them. Artists are notified 24 September and retain 80% of sales (85% for members). Founded in 1963, MFA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit supported by the Maryland State Arts Council and runs Circle Gallery in Annapolis.
