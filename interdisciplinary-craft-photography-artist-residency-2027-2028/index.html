Interdisciplinary Craft + Photography Artist Residency 2027–2028

A three-month studio residency in Houston for artists whose practice sits at the intersection of contemporary craft and photography, offered by Houston Center for Contemporary Craft together with Houston Center for Photography. Residents get 24/7 access to a 200-square-foot studio at HCCC, a $1,000 monthly stipend that includes a small housing and materials allowance, and — through HCP — printing credits and full access to HCP's Digital Lab. Residents give a joint artist talk and are included in HCCC's annual In Residence exhibition. Applicants must be able to demonstrate how their work engages both craft and photography; HCCC's programme details list the accepted craft media as wood, glass, metal, fiber and clay, but HCP adds that no particular amount of photographic experience is required, only an interest in showing photo-based work. Residents must work in their studios two days a week during HCCC public hours and open the studio to visitors on Saturdays and one other day. Applications for the 2027–2028 cycle are submitted through the same online application as HCCC's general residency programme, which opens on HCCC's apply page on 1 December 2026 and closes 1 February 2027, with acceptances emailed in late May. Neither organiser has published an entry fee yet — HCP's FAQ answers the fee question with "stay tuned". International applicants must provide proof of eligibility to work in the US (a green card or appropriate visa) and, separately, an IRS tax processing number - a Social Security Number, Employer Identification Number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number - with the application.
