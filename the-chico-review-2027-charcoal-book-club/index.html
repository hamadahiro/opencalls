The Chico Review 2027

Prizes Book publication $5,450 / $2,675 / $1,500 scholarships

The Chico Review is a week in Paradise Valley with most of the American photobook world in the room, and getting in is competitive: eighty photographers are chosen by a selection jury from open application. Each attendee gets ten 20-minute portfolio reviews with a mix of speakers and reviewers, plus lectures, panel discussions, peer reviews and evening programming, and — repeatedly, in the testimonials — a great deal of unstructured time in which the actual relationships get built. The 2027 speakers are Paul Graham, Barbara Bosworth, Daniel Arnold, Rena Effendi, JH Engström, Paul D'Amato, Emma Hardy and Hannah Modigh; reviewers include STANLEY/BARKER, Trespasser, Charcoal Press, MASS Books, Kominek Editions, Pier 24, SFMOMA and the High Museum.

Two things make this an opportunity rather than a workshop listing. One is the Charcoal Publishing Prize: at the end of the week one attendee's project is chosen and then published and distributed worldwide by Charcoal Book Club, announced 18 June 2027. The other is the scholarship money, awarded on the strength of the submitted work rather than on need alone — one full tuition worth $5,450, two half tuition at $2,675 and three partial at $1,500, with academic discounts and limited need-based support also available once accepted. Alumni can return in later years without reapplying, paying the application fee or being re-juried.

Be clear-eyed about the cost if you are selected without a scholarship: tuition and board is $5,450 and lodging at the resort runs $70 to $250 a night on top, so this is a significant outlay. The application itself is $25 for Charcoal Book Club members and $50 for everyone else, rising to a $65 late fee — and the organiser’s prospectus prints two different boundary dates for that increase, 23 November in its fee summary and 26 November in the fee section of the same page, so plan on the 23rd. The organiser publishes a submission deadline of 29 November 2026 and a late submission deadline of 6 December, though the platform's own clock closes on the 29th — treat the 29th as the date and do not rely on the late window. Selected attendees and scholarships are announced 26 December 2026. One inconsistency on the organiser's own pages: the homepage describes five partial tuition scholarships while the 2027 page lists three and heads the block “6 Merit Scholarships” before saying they go to “nine accepted attendees”. The six-award breakdown above is the 2027 page's own.
