Your Best Piece — Open Call

Prizes Publication Payment

Cut+Paste is an indie print magazine that pays its contributors, and between its themed quarterly calls it keeps one route permanently open. "Your Best Piece" is exactly what the name says: send a favourite work with no need to align it to a theme. The editors describe it as a way "to give artists flexibility and a chance to submit standout work outside of our regular submission periods", and they accept a wide range of visual art — illustration, painting, photography, graphic design, lettering, digital and traditional media. For photographers the accepted categories are named individually: portraits (faceless preferred), nature and landscapes, abstract and experimental, still life and conceptual, urban and cityscapes, and macro close-up detail. Submitting is free. If work is selected it is paid at the magazine's standard rates — $15 base for photography and graphic design plus, per additional image, $10 full page, $7 for one of two on a page, $6 for one of three and $5 for one of four; art and lettering start at $25. A contributor can opt to have $9 taken out of their payment, plus international shipping, in exchange for a copy of the issue at the magazine's cost. Two things about how this route actually works. It is a slow queue rather than a decision: selected contributors are contacted by email, and a piece that is not chosen immediately "may be kept on file for consideration in upcoming themes", so submitting here puts work in front of the editors for future issues rather than a named one. And there is a rate limit — the magazine asks that artists submit only once every ten days. Files must be high-quality PNG or JPEG. Previously published work is welcome; the editors state that submissions "that have been posted online or elsewhere are still accepted" and that accepted work "can still be posted and sold by the original creator", so no rights are signed away. Not accepted: nudity, foul language, AI art, or work that closely copies someone else's.
