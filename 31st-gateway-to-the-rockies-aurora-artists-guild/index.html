31st Annual Gateway to the Rockies Art Show

Long-running juried show and sale from the Aurora Artists Guild, a volunteer nonprofit of photographers and 2-D artists in Aurora, Colorado. Photography has its own dedicated category — traditional, digital or computer-enhanced — judged alongside separate 2-D and Miniatures categories, with up to three works per category and nine in total. Janet Schreiner jurors all three. Awards for photography are Best of Show, 1st, 2nd, 3rd and honourable mentions, with monetary awards and gifts set by entry numbers and sponsor contributions. Accepted work is hand-delivered to Aurora Central Library on October 31; only Miniatures may be mailed. The show runs November 6 – December 4, 2026, with the reception and awards ceremony on November 7.
