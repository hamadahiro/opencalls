40 Over 40

Prize Online exhibition

South x Southeast Gallery invites photographers over the age of 40 to enter its 40 Over 40 open call, curated by Elizabeth Avedon. The call is open to photographers of all backgrounds and experience levels, and all subject matter is welcome — street photography, portraits, nudes, landscapes, nature, wildlife, urban, architecture and documentary work. Selected photographs are shown in the gallery's online exhibition through September and October 2026, with a Zoom reception for the exhibiting photographers on a date to be announced. Entry is by email: images should be JPEG only, 72dpi and 1000 pixels on the longest side, named lastname-firstname-title (for example mccrarynancybigredhouse), zipped into a single file and attached to an email with the subject line '40 Over 40'. The entry fee is $40 for the first four images and $10 for each additional image, paid separately through the gallery's shop.
