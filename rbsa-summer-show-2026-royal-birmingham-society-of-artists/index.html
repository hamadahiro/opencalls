RBSA Summer Show 2026

Prize £600 / £300

The Royal Birmingham Society of Artists (RBSA) is a Birmingham-based artist-led society dating to 1814. The Summer Show is its largest annual open exhibition at the RBSA Gallery in central Birmingham — open submission across all media.

All techniques and media are accepted: painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, mixed media, photography, and digital work. The show is open to UK and international artists with no eligibility restriction by career stage.

Prizes are £600 (first), £300 (second), and a set of highly commended certificates. Selected works hang in the RBSA's main gallery space.

Submissions are through Typeform with up to 3 images. Fee is tiered (£13–£39, with reduced rates for RBSA Friends and members). The 2026 deadline was May 5; results announced May 21, 2026.
