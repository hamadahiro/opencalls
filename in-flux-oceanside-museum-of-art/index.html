In Flux

Prize Exhibition

The Oceanside Museum of Art runs an Artist Alliance for artists in the San Diego region — a $25-per-person add-on to any level of OMA membership that brings an online registry listing and access to member exhibitions. In Flux is one of those exhibitions, and it takes the Alliance out of the museum and into Covet Art Gallery on South Coast Highway in South Oceanside.

The theme is deliberately loose. Artists are encouraged to submit their own reading of the title, with the call gesturing toward work that explores physical, emotional or environmental change: movement, transformation, uncertainty, a world in constant motion, whether that shift happens in the materials or in the ideas.

Juror Laurie King founded Covet in December 2023 to show contemporary artists with unusual perspectives and to give artists, collectors and the community somewhere to meet; the gallery also runs an online marketplace carrying work from artists across the country. She selects for the strongest show taken as a whole, so individual merit is not the only criterion.

Up to 20 entries may be submitted and a maximum of two works per artist will be chosen. Photography is named among the accepted media, along with limited-edition printmaking and limited-edition digital prints; giclées and other reproductions of originals are not. Entry is $25 for the first submission and $10 for each additional one, and scholarships are available for artists who ask. Submissions close 10 August at 10.59pm Pacific, notifications go out by 28 August, and the show runs 3–29 October 2026 with a reception on opening night.
