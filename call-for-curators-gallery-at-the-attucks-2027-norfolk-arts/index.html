Call for Curators: The Gallery at the Attucks 2027

Norfolk Arts — the City of Norfolk's arts agency — is seeking a curator for the 2027 exhibition at The Gallery at the Attucks, inside the historic Attucks Theatre on Church Street. The call asks for a group exhibition of at least seven artists with a connection to Hampton Roads, in contemporary 2-D work that aligns with the Attucks' cultural legacy. Norfolk Arts states that Black curators are strongly encouraged to apply.

Proposals that outline a clear strategy for attracting large, diverse audiences are preferred, including partnerships, cross-disciplinary ideas and additional programming such as artist talks, performances and demonstrations.

There is no fee to apply. The curator receives a $1,500 stipend and artist stipends total $3,500. The gallery takes no commission; artworks may be offered for sale or not. The exhibition runs for six months in a gallery of approximately 1,500 square feet. The curator may not show their own artwork. Participants must upload a W-9 and become a registered City of Norfolk vendor in order to be paid.

Materials are sent by email to Norfolk Arts by 11:59pm on 8 December 2026. A full prospectus PDF is published alongside the call on the agency's site.
