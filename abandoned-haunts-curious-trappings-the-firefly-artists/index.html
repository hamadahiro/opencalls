Abandoned Haunts & Curious Trappings

Annual celebration of the hauntingly beautiful at The Firefly Artists gallery in Northport, New York, judged by Matthew Christopher, the photographer behind Abandoned America. Artists nationwide may submit work in any medium — abandoned places, forgotten objects, and curious relics are the territory; photography sits naturally at the heart of the theme. Up to 4 works, maximum 36×36 inches, all for sale (35% gallery commission), no AI-generated work. Cash prizes of $200, $100, and $50 plus honorable mention. Exhibition October 6 – November 5, 2026 in the gallery's historic Main Street building.
