Fabulous Florals & Fine Art 2027

Prize Exhibition

Pablo Center at the Confluence, the regional arts centre in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, opens its annual Fabulous Florals & Fine Art to artists for the 2027 edition: area florists create stunning arrangements interpreting the submitted works of fine art, a botanical fantasia built around each artist's piece, and the organiser's own line is that the exhibit will last only as long as the flowers. The show runs Thursday 25 - Sunday 28 February 2027 in the James W. Hansen Gallery, and the organiser posts galleries for the 2024, 2025 and 2026 editions on the same page. There is no entry fee. Each artist may submit three pieces of original artwork made in the last five years, and the organiser's Calls to Artists page describes its three year-round galleries as featuring original works of all mediums and styles, from artists throughout the Chippewa Valley, the Midwest and beyond - photography is not named as a category and is not excluded. Submissions go by email to the visual arts contact published on the call page and must carry the artist statement or bio, title, size, price, medium and a statement about the artwork; a signed contract must be completed with the received work. Awards are decided by public vote rather than by a juror and no cash figure is published: the organiser's own 2026 results name Best Artwork, Best Floral Design and Best Pairing, with honorable mentions in each. Note three things before you plan around the window: the organiser's own index line reads "Call Open: Dec. 1 - Jan 25" while the body of the same block says to submit now until January 25th 5PM, so the posted open date is later than the posted instruction; artists deliver their own work to the building and are responsible for shipping it both ways; and the house rules state all work must be for sale, with Pablo Center taking 30% commission from members and 40% from non-members on any sale.
