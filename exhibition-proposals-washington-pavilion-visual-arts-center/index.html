Exhibition Proposals

Prize Exhibition

The Visual Arts Center at the Washington Pavilion is South Dakota's largest art museum, accredited by the American Alliance of Museums, with eight galleries in downtown Sioux Falls. Its Exhibition Selection Committee keeps a standing, no-deadline route into that programme: proposals are welcome from individual artists, artist groups and guest curators working in any medium or subject matter, and are reviewed on a quarterly basis, with the centre warning that it may take some time for a submission to be scheduled for review. The gallery list is worth reading before proposing - Gallery 301, at the entrance, frequently features photography exhibitions; the 5,000 sq ft Everist Gallery shows work in all media from established regional to international artists; the 1,700 sq ft Jerstad Gallery shows leading regional, national and international artists; the 400 sq ft Shultz Gallery is reserved for emerging regional artists; and the Contemporary Gallery covers digital, installation and new media. Applications go by email and consist of a cover letter summarising your artistic interests with a clear statement of exhibition intention and the galleries you would prioritise, a CV, an artist biography, an artist statement about the proposed body of work, and 10 to 20 high-resolution images at 300 dpi with identifying information for each. No fee is stated anywhere in the guidelines.
