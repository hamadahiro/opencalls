FUJIFILM GFX Challenge Grant Program 2026

Prizes $10,000 ×5 $5,000 ×10

Global grant program supporting image-makers to realize a personal project. Fifteen recipients are selected: 5 Global Grants of $10,000 and 10 Regional Grants of $5,000. Awardees also receive a complimentary loan of a GFX System camera body (including the new GFX ETERNA 55) and two GF lenses for the duration of their project, plus mentoring and technical support from Fujifilm experts. Projects may be realized as still photography or in a movie format. Open to both amateur and professional photographers and videographers resident in eligible countries; proposals submitted in English. Free to enter, no purchase necessary.
