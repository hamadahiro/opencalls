November Gallery Members Exhibition — Cornucopia

Prize Exhibition

Crossroads Art Center runs bi-monthly members-only shows, and November's is Cornucopia, which asks for abundance in whatever form the artist finds it — harvest scenes, fruit and florals, the textures of orchards and fields, still lifes bursting with colour, cultural or mythological readings of the horn of plenty, or purely abstract expressions of overflow and richness. Photography sits in the organiser's own eligible-media list alongside painting, mixed media, collage, fibre, sculpture, ceramics, glass, digital art, woodworking and assemblage. Only original work by the entering artist is accepted; reproductions and giclée prints are refused unless the owner approves them, though work where the print process is itself the art — etching, monoprint, silkscreen, lithograph, or images the artist builds in a program such as Procreate or Photoshop — is allowed. Work already accepted into a juried show at the centre may not be entered into a juried show again. Everything must be for sale, must stay up for the run, and 2-D pieces must be wired rather than hung on sawtooth hangers. The gallery takes 30 per cent of sales and the artist keeps 70. Entry costs $15 per submission with a limit of three, and is open to current CAC gallery members, a $50-a-year status ($40 for resident artists) that is separate from the centre's Artist-in-Residence programme. Entries run October 1 to 18 through ArtCall, artists hear by October 23, work is delivered November 10 to 13, and the show opens on November 20 and runs live and online until January 3, 2027.
