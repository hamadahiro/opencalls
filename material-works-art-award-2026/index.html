Material Works Art Award 2026

Prize $2,500 purchase award

Material Works is an artist-run platform founded in 2024 that supports artists through a single annual award and builds a contemporary art collection from it. One artist receives $2,500, and the structure is worth reading carefully: the award is exchanged for an original artwork of similar monetary value, negotiated with the artist after selection, which then joins the Material Works collection and is considered for future exhibition opportunities — so this is a purchase award rather than a no-strings grant, with shipping covered by the organisation. The winner is also interviewed about their work and practice with a permanent profile on the Material Works site, featured on its social channels, and invited to sit on the jury for future opportunities; a shortlist of artists receives the same interview, profile and feature. Selection is on the merit of the submitted portfolio, and past editions are public — the 2025 award went to Jonathan Lanier alongside a shortlist and fourteen honourable mentions. Photography is named among the eligible practices, together with painting, installation, sculpture, drawing, printmaking, mixed-media, experimental and conceptual art; music, writing, culinary, film and time-based media are not accepted this cycle. Applicants must be over 18 and may enter once, uploading one to fifteen images plus an image list, with an artist statement, bio, CV, and a studio or portrait image all optional. The application review fee is $40, and the organisation says it will publish one no-cost application day on its social channels, outside of which the fee cannot be waived. Note the deadline is whichever comes first: the first 300 applications or December 6, 2026 — once the cap is reached the form closes, so applying early matters.
