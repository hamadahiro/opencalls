2027 National Juried Exhibition

Prizes $1,000 / $500 / $250 $125 honourable mention Exhibition

The Delaplaine's annual all-media national open, hung at its Frederick, Maryland arts centre from May 1 to July 11, 2027, with the juror's talk and award announcement on opening day. Photography is named outright on the organiser's own page for this exhibition: "All media are eligible, including but not limited to: painting, printmaking, photography, ceramics, drawing, installation, and sculpture", and the platform prospectus repeats the openness from the other direction — "There are no theme, size or media requirements or restrictions for this exhibition." THE 2027 JUROR is Timothy Paul Brown, Director of IA&A at Hillyer, the contemporary art centre of International Arts & Artists in Washington DC, with more than 25 years across contemporary art, museum education and exhibitions; he holds a Master's in Museum Education and a BFA from the University of the Arts, Philadelphia. JUDGING RUNS IN TWO ROUNDS and only the first is remote: the juror works virtually through the entry portal to decide what gets in, then judges the awards IN PERSON after the work is installed — so the cash decisions are made from the prints on the wall, not from JPEGs. THE MONEY: $1,000 first place, $500 second, $250 third and $125 honourable mention, itemised identically on the organiser's page and the prospectus. WHAT IT COSTS BEYOND THE FEE: this is a ship-or-drive show. Accepted work must be hand delivered or received at 40 S. Carroll Street, Frederick between Monday 19 and Sunday 25 April 2027, shipped work must include a prepaid return label, and everything must stay on the wall for the full ten weeks; pick-up is 4–5 pm on Sunday 11 July or 9 am–5 pm on Monday 12 July 2027, and work left later "may be disposed of at the Delaplaine's discretion". Work must have been made within the past two years and must not have been shown at the Delaplaine before. Two-dimensional wall work need not be framed but must arrive wired to a professional standard for the gallery's hanging system — the prospectus is emphatic that sawtooth hangers and any kind of tape will be refused. Work may be entered for sale or marked NFS — "Artists are not required to offer work for sale" — but if it sells the Delaplaine retains 30% commission from a non-member and 20% from a member, a ladder that appears only in the platform record's Legal Agreement section and nowhere on the gallery's own page. THE ORGANISER'S OWN PAGE FOR THIS EXHIBITION STILL CARRIES THE 2026 EDITION — 2026 juror Kyle Hackett, a March 2026 deadline, a May–July 2026 run — and the Opportunities index above it still lists the National Juried Exhibition under "Call for entry coming fall of 2026". Entries nonetheless opened on 17 September 2026 and the platform record is live, so every 2027 figure here is taken from that record, not from the gallery's unrefreshed page. AI IS GOVERNED BY A STANDING HOUSE POLICY, NOT BY THIS PROSPECTUS, which never mentions it: the Delaplaine's Opportunities page — the parent route this call sits under — answers the FAQ "What about AI-generated or AI-assisted artwork?" with guidelines that bind every "exhibited artwork": work "solely AI-generated, defined as autonomously created by AI" will not be considered, AI-assisted work may be "provided that the artist's input clearly dominates", any AI use "must be fully disclosed" with an explanation of the tools and processes, and the public label will read "Created using the assistance of artificial intelligence".
