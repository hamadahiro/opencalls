Open Call M-ZONE 2027 — Weinviertel

Prizes €3,000 project budget Four-week exhibition

No medium is named anywhere in this call, in either direction. The organisers ask for "künstlerische und kuratorische Konzepte" — artistic and curatorial concepts — so what is being judged is an exhibition proposal, not a body of images, and a photographer competes on the same terms as a sculptor or a performance maker.

The usual way to settle a silent call is to test the programme against its own past roster, and here there is none to test: the M-ZONE exhibition strand is new for 2027, and the venue's own page is a room-hire page offering the hall for "Ausstellung, Theaterproduktion, Workshops, Vorträge oder Feierlichkeiten" without listing a single past show. What can be measured is the organiser's remit. The person this call prints as its own contact for questions, Verena Mayrhofer, holds Kulturvernetzung's Coordination Office for Visual Art and is described by the organiser, on its Come In! Galerie page, as an artist working in "Medienkunst, Installation, Fotografie". Photography sits inside the remit of the office running the call.

It is still not an invitation. An applicant here is pitching a show for a 150 m² hall, the organisers explicitly want projects that draw the room into their conceptual and formal development, and the jury scores "räumliche und konzeptionelle Schlüssigkeit" — spatial and conceptual coherence — alongside artistic quality. A strong portfolio with no idea for the space is the wrong submission for this one.
