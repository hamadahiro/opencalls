Lure of the Local 5: Love Letters

Prizes $150 Critic's Choice $150 People's Choice Purchase Award

Fifth edition of the Mundelein Arts Commission's annual juried exhibition, which takes its name from Lucy Lippard's The Lure of the Local and this year runs on the theme Love Letters. It is free to apply and free to participate, artists of any medium and skill level are encouraged, and the show is built around “artists who live and work among us – our teachers, neighbors, friends, and fellow community members”. Awards are a $150 Critic's Choice with three honourable mentions, a $150 People's Choice with a runner-up, and a MAC 10th Anniversary Purchase Award; the commission is also taking separate email proposals for participatory installation works. The exhibition runs October 16 – November 22, 2026 at the Archer Business Center in downtown Mundelein, open Saturdays and Sundays 11am–5pm with weekend workshops and live music, and opens with a reception on October 16 that typically draws 400–500 visitors.
