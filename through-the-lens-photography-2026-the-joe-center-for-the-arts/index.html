Through the Lens Photography 2026

Prize Exhibition

Free juried DIGITAL exhibition ('Photography Without Walls') from The Joe Center for the Arts, a community non-profit art center in Port St. Joe, Florida, held online while the physical building undergoes renovations. Open to all photographers; all photo-based works are eligible with no limit on subject matter or technique (no AI-generated work). Submit up to 5 pieces. As a no-fee digital show, monetary awards are not given, but award certificates (Best of Show, First, Second, Third, Honorable Mentions) are mailed, and all accepted entries are featured in the online gallery and on social media. On view August 7 – September 11, 2026.
