Queens Arts Fund: New Work Grant 2027

A project grant of $3,000 to $7,500 for Queens-based artists to make new work — work that has not been produced or presented to an audience before. The Queens Arts Fund is administered by NYFA and supported by public funds from the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs through the Greater New York Arts Development Fund, in partnership with the City Council. Photography is a named discipline: applications are sorted to peer-review panels by discipline, and Visual Arts explicitly covers Painting, Photography, Printmaking/Drawing/Book Arts and Craft/Sculpture. To apply you must live in Queens or hold a full-time studio lease there (PO boxes are not accepted), be at least 18 by 31 December 2026, and propose a project with a free or sliding-scale public component — an exhibition, screening, reading or workshop — taking place in Queens between 1 January and 31 December 2027. Artist collectives may apply through one Queens-based lead applicant. Grant funds can cover artist fees (the fund actively encourages applicants to pay themselves), technical assistance, planning and preparation, supplies and materials, venue rental for the project, in-state travel and marketing, with individual supply items capped at $1,000. The equipment line is narrower than it first looks: cameras and lighting are eligible only as equipment “related to the creation and promotion of virtual programming”, and equipment or capital goods whose total exceeds $1,000 are ineligible outright. Studio or living rent, residency fees, acquisitions, out-of-state travel and reception catering cannot be covered. Applications are scored by peer panels on four equally weighted criteria including creativity and artistic merit, and recipients may be awarded 50–100% of what they request, so the form asks for a contingency plan. Only one application to either Queens Arts Fund programme is allowed per applicant per year; non-profit organisations apply instead to the QAF Arts Access Grant. The cutoff is not end of day: NYFA's timeline reads "Applications Close Tuesday, November 17, 5:00 PM ET" and the Submittable form closes automatically at that time. ONE EXCLUSION TO CHECK BEFORE SPENDING TIME: full-time degree students cannot be the lead applicant — NYFA's Who Cannot Apply list bars anyone "currently enrolled at the time of application or who will be enrolled in 2027" in a degree-seeking programme, regardless of field of study.
