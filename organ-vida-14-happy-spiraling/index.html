Organ Vida 14 - Happy Spiraling

Prize €300 prize

Organ Vida is a Croatian photography festival held annually in Zagreb. The 2026 edition is the festival's 14th cycle, themed Happy Spiraling.

Selected photographers receive a €300 artist fee, mentorship, and exhibition at the Museum of Contemporary Art Zagreb (MSU) in June 2026. Travel and accommodation for selected artists are covered.

Eligibility is open to photographers and visual artists globally. Submission is through Picter with up to 25 images. Fee is €35. The 2026 deadline was March 18; results announced mid-April 2026.
