Fins, Feathers, and Fur

Prizes $150 / $75 / $50 Exhibition

"Beyond the surface of animal portraiture lies a rich world of pattern, rhythm, and tactile wonder." The St. Charles Arts Council's juried autumn show asks for work that investigates the physical and visual surfaces of the animal kingdom — "literal representations, or abstract explorations" — under the subtitle *Textures of the Living World*. It hangs in the council's SCAC Gallery at 121 2nd Street in historic downtown St. Charles, Illinois, from 30 October to 20 December 2026, with awards handed out at the opening reception on Friday 30 October, 5–7pm. All media are accepted — the organiser says so twice, "All mediums are accepted, 3d encouraged" and "All forms of media accepted" — so photographs compete alongside everything else; the judge is Chris Hughes, an award-winning member of Oil Painters of America who sits on the boards of the Naperville Art League and the DuPage Art League and teaches at both. Awards are Best in Show $150, second place $75, third place $50, plus honourable mentions at the judge's discretion. Entry is $35 for non-members and $20 for SCAC members, selected from a drop-down at checkout, and one entry covers up to two pieces; only one entry per artist. Work must be original, created within the last five years, and must not have been shown in any previous SCAC exhibit. Everything must be for sale — the council is explicit that the "NFS" option in the entry form must not be selected — and sales split 60% to the artist, 40% to the council. Two practical constraints before you enter: all work must be wired to hang, with sawtooth hangers refused, and you must be able to make the in-person dates yourself, drop-off Monday 26 October 2–6pm and collection Monday 21 December 2–6pm, which effectively limits this to artists who can get to St. Charles twice. Two clauses of the artist contract are worth reading before you pay: all artists with work in the show "are asked to sign up to volunteer at The ARC" on two-hour shifts, and any work not claimed within seven days of the pick-up date, without notice, "becomes the property of the St. Charles Arts Council". Entries close 11 October 2026 and the jury decision is posted 14 October.
