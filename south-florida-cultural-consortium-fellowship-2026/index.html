South Florida Cultural Consortium Fellowship 2026

The arts councils of Broward, Martin, Miami-Dade, Monroe and Palm Beach counties jointly award grants of $7,500 or $15,000 to visual and media artists who have lived and worked in the five-county South Florida region for at least a year — among the largest regional artist awards in the US. Established in 1988, the consortium has granted over $4 million to more than 300 artists, with recipients' work presented in exhibitions. Photography and media work are eligible (samples from the past three years); degree-seeking students are not. Attendance at one of the online workshops is required to apply; all three (22 July, 4 August and 22 August) have now passed, and the Submittable portal accepts a recorded viewing instead, stating that having either attended or watched the workshop video is mandatory and supplying the recording link and passcode. Funded in part by the National Endowment for the Arts and the State of Florida.
