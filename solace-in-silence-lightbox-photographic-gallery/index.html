Solace in Silence

Prize Exhibition

LightBox's winter members exhibit asks where you find solace and when you welcome silence: a winter's night with the moon reflecting on snow, a moment awake in a quiet house while others sleep, a stroll down a gravel lane at dusk. The gallery wants visuals of a momentary pause — the places and things that make you feel grounded, and where your mind reaches when you are stressed, grieving or weary, whether inside your home, in the vastness of wilderness, or in the delicacy of a flower. Against what the call describes as a day of rapid-fire imagery and political chaos, the show is pitched as a collective stilling of the mind. The call is inclusive rather than competitive: LightBox states that every member who submits will be juried into the exhibit, with LightBox member Joni Kabana named as juror. Entry costs nothing, and members submit three to five images as jpgs of 1000 pixels in the long dimension, emailed with a completed entry form. The show runs 12 December 2026 – 30 January 2027 at the gallery on Marine Drive in Astoria, Oregon, and is carried on the gallery's social channels, show page and newsletter. The gate is membership, not the entry fee, and the gallery's two tiers are not interchangeable for this show. LightBox's membership guidelines publish Supporting membership at $100 per calendar year — a 10% discount on prints, services and workshops, gallery support, library access and a member webpage — and Associate membership at $25 per month, which adds the Wednesday image-sharing evenings, first invites to special events, a flat $25-for-10 rate on LightBox juried group shows and, the line that matters here, participation in members group exhibits. The call page and the prospectus both say only “members of LightBox Photographic Gallery” without naming a tier, and the guidelines add that the gallery likes to treat all members the same whatever the level, so a Supporting member may well be welcome; but the only published benefit list puts this exhibit on the $25-a-month tier, so budget for that and ask the gallery before joining at the cheaper rate. Photographers interested in joining are asked to contact the gallery through its site. Participants selected for the gallery have their work printed by LightBox at 50% of normal cost, at 9 x 13 up to 12 x 18 inches, matted and framed for the exhibit; bare prints can be collected at the gallery afterwards or shipped for $20. Members may instead send an original matted or framed print, with complimentary frame rental offered for the run of the show; work not printed by LightBox must reach the gallery by 9 December 2026. Gallery-printed bare prints carry a preset $200 price unless the artist asks that a work not be for sale, originals are priced as the member wishes, and the gallery keeps a 40% commission, paying artists within seven days of the show's close. Notification of accepted entries is 11 November 2026.
