The Autumn Book

Woofermagazine — an independent photography magazine that began on Instagram in May 2020 and now carries over a million tagged images — is selecting photographs for its next collective photobook. Autumn is the brief, read through content or aesthetics rather than literally: the call asks for images that carry autumn’s feeling, naming freedom, intensity, stillness, escape and transformation as ways in. Colour and black and white are both welcome. A selection of submitted work is published in the book and credited to the photographer. Terms worth reading before you pay: €16 buys one submission of up to five photographs, and you can buy further submissions to send more; all participants receive a digital version of the book (due November 2026) and a 25% discount on the printed edition, but no free print copy and no fee; copyright stays with you, while submitting grants publication in the print and digital editions and sharing on the magazine’s Instagram, including to promote future open calls. 18+, any nationality.
