Revelations

LoosenArt's open call for Issue Collective #3, a publication dedicated to what emerges beneath the surface of things. The call asks what it means to reveal something today, and what appears when we look more closely, listen more carefully and move past the veil of appearances — revelation as a moment of insight, discovery or unveiling, whether an unexpected perspective, a personal truth or an overlooked aspect of reality. Entry is free and open worldwide, and the organiser lists the accepted media plainly: photography, video, digital, visual design, visual data, installation, sculpture and painting. Each artist may submit one to three works. Beyond the publication, a selection of the works chosen for it will be considered in a further selection process leading to a group exhibition to be held in 2027 in one of three Italian cities — Milan, Rome or Cagliari — which the organiser has not yet fixed. Submission is through a Google Form — the same form LoosenArt uses for the Aesthetic Dynamics call running alongside this one, with a required “Select the call of interest” dropdown that assigns your entry to this one. It asks for name, country, email, website, Instagram, an artist statement of up to 70 words in English, and up to three files of no more than 5 MB each, renamed for the work's title. LoosenArt's site is a spare Google Sites presence run from Quartu Sant'Elena in Sardinia, and beyond the call text it publishes no further terms; no charge of any kind is mentioned at any stage.
