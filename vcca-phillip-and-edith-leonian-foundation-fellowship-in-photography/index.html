VCCA Phillip and Edith Leonian Foundation Fellowship in Photography

Two-week fully-funded residency at Mt. San Angelo in Amherst, Virginia for US citizens or permanent residents. Supported by the Phillip and Edith Leonian Foundation, established in 2009 to support photographic education and documentary photography. Includes private bedroom with en-suite bath, individual studio space plus darkroom access, and three meals daily in a cross-disciplinary community. Residency dates available May 1 – August 31, 2027. Applications open June 1, 2026. Fee waivers available on request by September 10, 2026. Apply via SlideRoom.
