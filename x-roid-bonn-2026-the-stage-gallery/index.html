X ROID Bonn 2026

Prize Exhibition prize

X ROID Bonn is the international instant art expo organized by The Stage Gallery in Bonn, Germany. The expo is dedicated to instant photography — Polaroid and Fuji Instax. The 2026 edition is the 7th cycle.

The 2026 expo runs three days (May 29–31) at The Stage Gallery, structured as a juried exhibition with artist talks, a Pola walk through the city, and creative exchanges between participants. Media partnership with Analog Forever (the analog photography magazine).

The selection is process-restricted: only original instant photographs accepted — no prints. Accepted formats are SX70, 600, Spectra, 4×5, Packfilm, Polaroid Original, Impossible Project, and Fuji Instax in all sizes.

Practical terms: the €225 fee includes professional framing. Gallery takes 25% commission on sales. Artwork is uninsured during the exhibition.

Eligibility is open globally to instant photography. Submission is by email with up to 4 Polaroids or 2 large-format works. The 2026 deadline was March 31.
