30×30 Call Out — Annual Exhibition 2027

Prizes Exhibition Sales

The 30×30 Call Out is the small-format, affordable strand of Visual Arts Scotland's Annual Exhibition, which returns to the Upper Galleries of the Royal Scottish Academy Building on The Mound, Edinburgh. The entry form gives the exhibition dates as January 8 to February 3, 2027; work is shown and sold from shelves and print racks alongside the main hang.

Each artist may submit a maximum of two works, priced at no more than £250 and made within the last two years. Each piece is presented on a precisely 30×30 cm mount — the form is explicit that "the work itself can be smaller" — with work on paper on good-quality backing card, any card mount of professional quality, and a maximum thickness of 2–3 mm for the wrapped work. No canvases on stretchers, no boxed canvas and no thick board. Pieces are wrapped in clear cellophane and labelled on the back with artist name, title, medium and sale price.

Registration is through a Google Form, which states "Closing date for submissions 27th November 2026" and lets the VAS team track work as it arrives; no substitutions and no late deliveries are accepted. Work should be posted to Visual Arts Scotland, Studio G49, Out of the Blue Drill Hall, 36 Dalmeny Street, Edinburgh EH6 8RG to arrive by Friday 18 December 2026 — the call page's date, where the form says 22 December — or hand-delivered to the RSA upper galleries via the rear entrance on Sunday 3 January 2027. Unsold work is collected from the RSA on Friday 5 or Saturday 6 February 2027. VAS's own opportunity card carries the window "27th September 2026 - 3rd January 2027" and names no closing date, so the form's 27 November is the only one the organiser states.

There is no entry fee and no selection stage: VAS lists "Guaranteed inclusion in the members 30X30 section of the Annual Exhibition" as a membership benefit, and the only reservation stated is that VAS "reserves the right to reject any work which is poorly presented or does not fit the presentation guidance". All work must be for sale, and VAS takes 40% commission on the sale price, reduced to 35% for Professional Members — so a £250 piece returns £150 to a General Member. Artists are responsible for their own artwork and public liability insurance; VAS does not insure work.

The call is open to VAS members only. General membership is £40 a year, Emerging membership £28 for students, recent graduates and artists under 23, and Professional membership £54 by election; VAS describes General membership as open to "artists and makers engaged in the visual arts and/or contemporary craft in Scotland", and offers fee support to artists living with disability or receiving universal credit. Membership can be bought at any time on the VAS website.

The 30×30 page does not enumerate accepted media. The parent Annual Exhibition states it welcomes "all areas of visual arts and contemporary crafts, including but not limited to painting, printmaking, fine-art photography, drawing", and the 30×30 rules explicitly provide for work on paper on a good quality backing card.
