Nachleben 2026

Open call for the Nachleben photography festival in Verona, Italy, September 2026. Three photographers or collectives selected for exhibition. Selected artists receive two nights accommodation during opening weekend, meals, travel reimbursement up to €300, and a production budget of up to €1,000 for prints and installation. Open to photographers of any age. No thematic restrictions — jury selects on artistic merit.
