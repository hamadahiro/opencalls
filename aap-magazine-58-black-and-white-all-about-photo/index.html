AAP Magazine #58 Black & White

Prizes $1,000 prize Publication prize

AAP Magazine is the print publication of All About Photo, the photography platform run by Sandrine Hermand-Grisel. Each issue is themed and curated from open-call submissions, with the magazine functioning as both contest and editorial outlet. Issue #58 is themed "Black & White" — open to all interpretations of monochrome photography across photojournalism, street, documentary, portrait, landscape, wildlife, architecture, fashion, and fine art.

Twenty-five winners are published in the printed AAP Magazine Vol. 58 alongside the online Winners Gallery on the All About Photo platform. Each published photographer receives one complimentary copy of the magazine and is featured across All About Photo's social channels.

Cash awards total $1,000: $500 first place, $300 second, $200 third. Twenty-two additional finalists (4th through 25th) are recognized but do not receive cash.

Submissions require a minimum of 3 images forming a cohesive body of work or unified series connected by subject, style, or concept; up to 40 images may be submitted per applicant. Entry fees follow a tiered structure: early bird (through May 15) is $30 for the first 3 images and $5 each additional; regular fee (after May 15) is $35 for the first 3 and $5 each additional. The 2026 deadline is June 2; winners announced approximately July 15. AI-generated images are not accepted; eligibility is open to professional and amateur photographers aged 18 or over.
