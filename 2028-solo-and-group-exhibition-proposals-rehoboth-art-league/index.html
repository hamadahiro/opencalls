2028 Solo and Group Exhibition Proposals

Prize Exhibition

The Rehoboth Art League, an art centre founded in 1938 on a historic homestead campus in Henlopen Acres near Rehoboth Beach on the Delaware coast, is taking proposals for its 2028 season of solo and group exhibitions across four galleries — Corkran, Tubbs, Ventures and Homestead. All mediums are accepted, and the prospectus addresses photography directly in its currency rule: work must have been produced, or for photographs shot, no earlier than 2025, must not have been shown at RAL before, and must be available for the whole run of the exhibition. Solo applicants submit 5 works; groups submit 10, together with an artist statement and a summary CV or bio. Images must be under 4MB with a minimum dimension of 1960px, and only online entries are reviewed. Selected artists get a show in the League's galleries, promotion through its website, social media, printed brochures and press, and keep 65% of anything sold during the exhibition — but note the trailing clause: after the show, 35% is still owed to the League on any work sold to a buyer who first saw it on display during the exhibition. All work must be for sale unless the Exhibitions Director approves an NFS listing, artists pay their own delivery and shipping both ways, and selected artists are expected to attend their opening; housing is not guaranteed but is usually available over opening weekend. Proposals are reviewed by the League's Exhibitions Committee, which says it can accept only a limited number each year; decisions go out on or before 31 August 2027. Artists who showed in the 2025 or 2026 solo/group exhibitions, or who are exhibiting in 2027, are not eligible. Entry is $25 for RAL members and $45 for non-members, non-refundable. NOTE ON TIMING: this is a long-lead call — entries close at 4pm on Monday 26 July 2027, more than nine months out — so it is worth bookmarking rather than rushing.
