TSHOW.2

Prize $3,000 / $1,500 / $500

Second edition of TSHOW, the international juried exhibition from Texas nonprofit Tyler Art Lift. Open to painting, drawing, sculpture, printmaking and photography up to 80×80 inches — a chance for photographers to show alongside other media with serious cash on the line: $3,000 Best in Show, $1,500 runner-up and a $500 People's Choice award chosen by visitors. Exhibition at Gallery Main Street in downtown Tyler, Texas, September 23 – October 10, 2026, with opening reception October 3. Open internationally to artists 18+; $45 for the first entry, $5 each additional; 30% sales commission supports the show.
