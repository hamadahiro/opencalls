2027 Workspace Artist Residency

Center for Book Arts awards up to four New York-based emerging and mid-career artists a six-month keyholder residency running April to September 2027, inviting them to explore the book as a time-based medium — "a sequence of spaces, each space perceived at a different moment", in Ulises Carrión's phrase — and as a tool for political engagement, since printmaking and independent publishing have long been central to social and cultural transformation. Residents receive 24/7 keyholder access to shared studio space for six months, a $1,500 honorarium, a $500 material stipend, storage space, basic supplies and tools, free classes in bookbinding, letterpress and RISO, consultations and professional development with staff and visiting curators, scheduled site visits to partner institutions working with artists' books and publishing, support from volunteers, access to Materials for the Arts, and opportunities to present work in exhibitions and public programs. Applications are reviewed blind by an independent panel of arts professionals from a broad range of backgrounds. The program promotes experimentation in book arts and artists from all disciplinary backgrounds are encouraged to apply — CBA aims for a balanced cohort of experienced book artists and artists working in other disciplines with a clear interest in exploring the field. Applications ask for an artist statement and CV, a portfolio of work samples, and a brief proposal naming the projects you would pursue and the equipment, tools and classes at CBA that most interest you. Housing is not provided. The selection panel meets in early October, shortlisted artists are interviewed virtually in mid-October, residencies begin in April 2027, and residents present their work in an artist talk at the end of the program and take part in a group exhibition in 2029.
