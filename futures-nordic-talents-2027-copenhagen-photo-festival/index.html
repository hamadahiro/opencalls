FUTURES Nordic Talents 2027

Prizes €500 FUTURES platform membership CPF 2027 screening

Copenhagen Photo Festival nominates four Nordic photographers and lens-based artists each year to the FUTURES platform, the European network of photography organisations, and this is the open call for the 2027 cohort. Membership carries an artist fee of €500 and access to online conversations with experts, tutoring sessions, the annual FUTURES platform event, events hosted by the network's other member organisations and FUTURES meet-ups, plus continuous promotion on the platform's website, social media and print material; members can also enter two open calls exclusive to FUTURES artists, one for a travelling exhibition that moves between partnering venues across the year and one for an annual exhibition. On top of that the festival gives the nominated artists a screening of their submitted work during Copenhagen Photo Festival 2027, publication in the FUTURES archive on the CPF site, promotion on CPF's channels, free entry to the 2027 festival, an invitation to the festival's networking days and a portfolio session with Maja Dyrehauge Gregersen. Three eligibility conditions all have to hold: you have been resident in Denmark, the Faroe Islands, Finland, Greenland, Iceland, Norway or Sweden for at least the past 12 months; you were born in 1988 or later; and you have either studied photography or art, or your CV shows continual professional experience over the last three years, such as taking part in an exhibition or a publication. Applicants who entered previous FUTURES open calls may apply again unless they were previously selected as platform members. The application is a single PDF of at most 10 MB — CV and bio on no more than two pages, a project description on no more than one page, and 10 to 20 photographs from your latest or current project — submitted through the festival's own form with a DKK 100 administrative fee. The organisers ask for a cohesive photography or art project with a conceptual background that relates to your wider practice, ongoing or new. The festival's Programme Committee, whose members work in curation, publishing and visual storytelling, makes the selection; applicants hear back in mid-December 2026 and the four talents are announced in February 2027, ahead of the November 2027 festival. Previous CPF nominees include Emma Sarpaniemi, Inuuteq Storch, Jenni Toivonen and María Kristín Antonsdóttir.
