September 2026 All Media Show

Prizes $100 Exhibition

Crossroads Art Center in Richmond represents more than 225 mid-Atlantic artists and runs a bimonthly juried call with cash awards of $100 for first place, $75 for second and $50 for third. The September edition has no theme — it is an open juried show judged by a guest juror, shown live in the gallery and simultaneously online. Photography sits in the organiser's own eligible-media list alongside painting, sculpture, mixed media, digital art, fiber, wood, metal, ceramics and assemblage. The gallery accepts only original work by the entering artist; reproductions and giclée prints are refused unless approved by the owner, though work where the printed process is itself the art — etching, monoprint, silkscreen, lithography — is welcome. AI-generated images, professionally photographed images by another person, another artist's images and instructor's images are all strictly prohibited. Entry is $15 per submission for the general public with a limit of three, or $12 per submission for gallery members who also get a fourth entry free. Submitted work must be taken off the market everywhere else — other galleries, online marketplaces, personal sites and social selling platforms — from the moment of submission until the artist is notified. Artists are notified by 21 August, drop-off runs 9 to 12 September, the open house is 18 September and the exhibition closes 8 November 2026, with unsold work collected from 10 November. All work must be for sale, must stay up for the full run, and all wall-hung and 2D work must be wired — no saw-tooth hangers. Commission on sales is 30%.
