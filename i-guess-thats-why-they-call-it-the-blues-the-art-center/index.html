I Guess That's Why They Call It The Blues — Fall Student & Member Showcase

Prizes Exhibition Cash awards

The Art Center at Johns Creek runs this showcase for its own community twice a year, and the autumn 2026 edition takes its title from Elton John: I Guess That's Why They Call It The Blues. The brief is deliberately wide - "The blues can be color, emotion, memory, a landscape, a melody, or metaphor" - and the call lists possible directions rather than prescribing one: the colour itself, blues as music or cultural history, melancholy and longing, solitude and reflection, water, sky, atmosphere and nature, memory and nostalgia. Langston Hughes and Kandinsky are both quoted; creative interpretation is explicitly encouraged.

Photography is one of the named eligible media, alongside mixed media, ceramics, drawings, fiber art, printmaking, painting, collage, digital art and sculpture. The curator's email is published for questions about any medium not listed. AI work is excluded outright: all artwork must be an original creation of the submitting artist.

The gate here is membership, not selection. The show is open to current members and to students who have taken a class at The Art Center in the last 12 months, with students and members judged separately; anyone whose membership has lapsed is pointed at the centre's membership offerings. Entry costs nothing, each artist may submit two works - students may submit two from each class they are currently enrolled in - and every artist is guaranteed acceptance of one work. There are monetary awards, ribbons and other recognitions on top of that, presented at the reception.

Submission is low-tech: fill in the Google Form exactly as you want your wall label to read, then email images of the works to the curator with 'SMS' and your name in the subject line, the titles in the body or as filenames. Image quality for the application does not need to be high.

Physical requirements are specific. 2D work cannot exceed 48 x 48 inches framed or 20 lbs; works on paper should be framed with acrylic or plexiglass; everything must arrive dry and ready to hang with wire suited to its size and weight, and sawtooth hangers are not recommended above 16x20 inches. Unframed canvases are fine if back-stapled with painted edges and hanging wire. 3D work caps at 50 lbs and 24 inches wide, with freestanding pieces to be discussed with the curator. Every work needs an identification label on the back with artist name, title and price or NFS, and anything mailed in must include a prepaid return label.

Deadlines: submissions close October 9, 2026, acceptances go out October 16, drop-off is October 19-20 (10am-4pm), the exhibition runs October 26 to November 20, 2026, the Awards & Reception is November 13 at 6-7pm with awards at 6:30, and work is collected November 29-30. Sales are split 75/25 in the artist's favour.
