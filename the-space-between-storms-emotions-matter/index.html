The Space Between Storms

Prize Exhibition

Emotions Matter is a New York 501(c)(3) that runs peer support groups, education and advocacy for people affected by borderline personality disorder, and its BPD Art Show has been going since 2017. The fifth edition, 'The Space Between Storms: Borderline Personality Disorder Beyond the Moments You See', asks for work about the moments that usually go unseen - the space between crises, and the experience of hope, healing, recovery, identity and ordinary life with BPD. It is open to anyone with lived experience of BPD as an individual, a family member or a professional, anywhere in the world. Submissions are one to three original 2D or 3D artworks with a separate artist statement for each piece addressing the work, its connection to the theme and the applicant's own BPD journey; statements go to the jurors only and are not published without permission. Nothing is shipped - the show is virtual and judged from uploaded images, with selected artists supplying a high-resolution file afterwards. This year the show sits inside a three-night virtual BPD Art Festival in the week of 26 October 2026, with selected work in a virtual exhibit on the Emotions Matter site and an optional live Artists Roundtable. Selected artists sign a waiver allowing display but retain ownership of their work throughout. The $40 entry fee offsets technology, printing and administrative costs; a limited number of entry-fee scholarships are available first come, first served to applicants in financial hardship, by email to info@emotionsmatterbpd.org.
