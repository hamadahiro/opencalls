Solstice Magazine Issue 71 — Editorial Submissions

Prize Publication

Solstice Magazine is a London-based fashion publication. The Issue 71 editorial submissions call is open to fashion and editorial photographers — work suitable for the magazine's editorial spreads rather than fine art portfolios.

Submission is structured around fashion-shoot conventions: minimum 5 looks, 3 images per look. The structure favors complete editorials rather than single images.

Selected work is published in Issue 71 of Solstice. Submission is by email. Application is free. Eligibility is open globally. The 2026 deadline was March 21.
