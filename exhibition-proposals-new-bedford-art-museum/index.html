Exhibition Proposals

Prize Museum exhibition

New Bedford Art Museum takes exhibition proposals on a rolling basis from artists, independent curators, scholars, partner institutions and community members, with its exhibition committee reviewing submissions quarterly. The museum plans two to five years ahead, so this is a long-horizon route into a programme that takes photography seriously without being a photography programme: its 2026 group show Vanishing Ecologies: Speculative Futures lists photography among the media it brings together, and the two juried calls the museum has run that we track both name photography among accepted work — in each case alongside every other visual medium, not on its own. There is no fee and no theme; a proposal goes to the Executive Director by email and must explain how the exhibition fulfils the museum's mission, supply a checklist of the artworks with sample images, titles, media and a proposed gallery layout, describe the theme and its influences, propose display timing, list related talks or workshops, and give a timeline. CAUTION on the money: the museum asks each proposal to include a budget covering shipping, installation and de-installation and reception expenses, plus proposed sponsorship partners and a publicity plan - the page names no stipend or production fund, so clarify who pays for what before committing. Curators are told not to include their own work in a proposal.
