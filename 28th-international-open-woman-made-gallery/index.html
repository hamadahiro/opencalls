28th International Open

Prize Group exhibition

Woman Made Gallery invites submissions for its 28th International Open, a juried exhibition showcasing work in all media by women and non-binary artists from around the world, and says it is inviting works that explore and push conceptual and material boundaries. The show runs February 27 to March 27, 2027 at the gallery's Halsted Street space in Chicago; entries are due December 18, 2026 and artists are notified January 15, 2027. The juror is listed as TBA and the entry form link had not gone up when this call was posted — the gallery says it will follow shortly. The $30 fee covers one submission per artist of one to three artworks. Woman Made offers around 30 fee waivers per juried exhibition, 15 of them reserved for ALAANA/BIPOC and/or LGBTQIA+ artists, granted on a rolling basis for financial instability or low-income status, student or recent-graduate status, or other demonstrated need; members at the Enhanced Artist level and above receive one free entry a year. The gallery welcomes art from all women, including trans women and femme/feminine-identifying genderqueer and non-binary artists. Work previously shown at Woman Made cannot be resubmitted, and pieces may not exceed six feet horizontally or 75 pounds unless the artist delivers and collects them; video, sound and digital works need presentation instructions. Artists may use AI tools for research or inspiration, but submissions consisting of AI-generated images, text or objects with minimal human intervention will not be included.
