28th International Open

Woman Made Gallery invites submissions for its 28th International Open, a juried exhibition showcasing work in all media by women and non-binary artists from around the world, and says it is inviting works that explore and push conceptual and material boundaries. The show runs February 27 to March 27, 2027 at the gallery's Halsted Street space in Chicago. DEADLINE EXTENDED: both surfaces now give 28 December 2026 - "Entry Due Date: December 28, 2026" on the gallery's calls page and "Entry deadline Dec 28, 2026 · 11:59 PM" on the EntryThingy portal, which earlier gave 17 December; December 17 and December 18 now return zero hits on both pages. The notification date is still published two ways: "Jury notification Jan 2, 2027" on the portal's calendar block against "Notification: January 15, 2027" in the portal's prose and on the gallery's page, so expect a decision in January rather than on a particular day. The juror is listed as TBA. Awards selected by the juror are $500 first prize, $250 second and $100 third, plus honourable mentions. The $30 fee covers a submission of one to three artworks, with a maximum of two entries per artist; work may be offered for sale or listed NFS, and the gallery retains a 40% commission, paying the artist within 30 days of a sale. Woman Made offers around 30 fee waivers per juried exhibition, 15 of them reserved for ALAANA/BIPOC and/or LGBTQIA+ artists, granted on a rolling basis for financial instability or low-income status, student or recent-graduate status, or other demonstrated need; members at the Enhanced Artist level and above receive one free entry a year. The gallery welcomes art from all women, including trans women and femme/feminine-identifying genderqueer and non-binary artists. Work previously shown at Woman Made cannot be resubmitted, and pieces may not exceed six feet horizontally or 75 pounds unless the artist delivers and collects them; video, sound and digital works need presentation instructions. Artists may use AI tools for research or inspiration, but submissions consisting of AI-generated images, text or objects with minimal human intervention will not be included.
