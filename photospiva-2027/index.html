PhotoSpiva 2027

Prizes $1,000 $750 $500 3×$150 3×$100 Exhibition

PhotoSpiva is the longest-running photographic competition of its kind in the United States — Spiva Center for the Arts in Joplin, Missouri has run it every year since 1977, and the 2027 edition follows the 50th. The founders' brief still governs it: “present an exhibition of excellence in photography, celebrating the scope and vigorous activity of today's contemporary photographers”, and co-founder Jim Mueller's note that “we have intentionally avoided any categorization of either photographers or their work” is why there is still no theme and no category. Any photographic process qualifies — traditional, digital or photo-based mixed media — provided the work is two-dimensional, original, and has never been exhibited at Spiva before; three-dimensional work is not eligible. Entry is open to any amateur or professional photographer in the United States or its territories, with no age floor and no career-stage bar. Selected work hangs 20 March – 29 May 2027 in Spiva's galleries in the Harry M. Cornell Arts & Entertainment Complex, with the opening reception and awards on Friday 19 March 2027, 5:30–7:30 pm. The award ladder is cash and substantial for a $40 entry: $1,000 first, $750 second, $500 third, three $150 Merit Awards and three $100 Honorable Mentions — $3,000 in all. A new juror is invited each year from among, in the organiser's words, the nation's most respected photographers and curators; the 2027 jurors are Louviere+Vanessa, the collaborative duo whose work crosses photography, film, painting and printmaking, named on the call's own CaFE record — the organiser's own page has not caught up and still carries the 2026 juror, Dan Winters. Know the fee structure before you start: the $40 buys exactly five images and you must enter five, no fewer — the prospectus explicitly allows entering the same photograph five times if you do not have five — and each image beyond the fifth is $10. All images must go in one application; files are JPEG in RGB, with roughly 1600 pixels on the long edge given as a guideline rather than a rule. Acceptance or declination is sent by email by 27 January 2027. Two costs land only if you are selected. Accepted work must be printed, mounted on a rigid backing and matted — and may not be framed; Spiva partners with a local print house that offers accepted artists discounted printing and matting, though you may prepare your own to its specifications. Shipping and insurance both ways are the artist's responsibility, and the Center's “all risk” policy covers only theft and fire. Sale is optional, and the split on anything sold is 60% to the artist. Entering grants Spiva the right to reproduce an entry for publicity tied to this or future exhibitions. Two things to check before you click through. The entry window opens on 6 November 2026 at midnight Central, so the CaFE record carries no apply button until then — at the time of listing its page showed the prospectus but not the “Apply on CaFE” route a live sibling record carries. And the organiser's own page contradicts itself: its “How to Participate” block is stale and still prints the 2026 cycle's “Begin entering on November 7, 2025” and “Deadline is Jan 5, 2026”, while its “terms and conditions” link opens the 2026 prospectus. The binding window is the one in the “PhotoSpiva 2027 Key Dates” block and in the 2027 prospectus: 6 November 2026 to 4 January 2027 at 11:59 pm Central. The competition is presented by Freeman Health System with support from the Missouri Arts Council.
