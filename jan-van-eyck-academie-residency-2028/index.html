Jan van Eyck Academie Residency 2028

Eleven months in Maastricht among some 36 artists, curators, designers and writers, with a furnished studio apartment, a €1,500 monthly stipend (rent deducted), a €2,000 project budget and the academie's photography and audiovisual lab. Thirty to 32 places are chosen by a six-person committee for the 2028 cycle. Selected participants pay a €2,750 enrolment fee, and the €65 application fee is waived for DAC-list countries.
