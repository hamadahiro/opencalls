Small Wonders

Prize Exhibition

A juried exhibition built entirely around scale: every submitted work must measure 11 x 14 inches or smaller, frame included. The Guild frames it as a challenge rather than a restriction — thoughtful composition, experimentation and creative problem-solving inside a compact format, from bold statements to quiet observations. Open to all media and all subject matter, which puts photography on equal footing with painting, printmaking and sculpture, and one of the two jurors is a photographer: Zackary Petot, a multidisciplinary artist working in printmaking, photography and mixed media, and the Guild's Executive & Artistic Director. He judges alongside Kathryn Nahorski, the Guild's former Executive Director and current Development Director, who has spent fourteen years at the organisation and staged hundreds of exhibitions in professional galleries and alternative spaces. The St. Louis Artists' Guild has been running since 1886 and shows in a contemporary gallery and education space at Jackson and Forsyth in Clayton, Missouri. Entrants must be 18 or over and resident in the United States. Work must be original, completed within the last three years, and not previously shown in the Guild's galleries. One application per artist, carrying up to three works; files are JPG or PNG, maximum 1920 pixels on the longest side at 300 dpi, up to 50MB each. Wall-mounted work needs wire and screw eyes, French cleats or another professional hanging device — no saw-tooth hangers, no clip frames — and canvases must be framed or gallery-wrapped. Works must be priced for sale or marked NFS with an insurance value; the Guild insures 65% of the stated value and takes a 35% commission on sales. Accepted work can be hand-delivered or shipped via FedEx or UPS at the artist's cost. The call closes at midnight on October 31, 2026 and the exhibition runs November 20, 2026 to January 2, 2027. The Guild charges an entry fee and prints no amount on either its own call page or the visible prospectus, where EntryThingy shows only "Paid Entry"; the platform's own listing data for this call gives a floor of $10, and the exact charge is settled at checkout after creating an account.
