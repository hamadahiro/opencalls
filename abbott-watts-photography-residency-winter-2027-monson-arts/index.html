Abbott Watts Photography Residency — Winter 2027

Prizes Residency Stipend

Monson Arts' dedicated photography residency, reserved within its general open call, gives one photographer per session access to the private studio and darkroom of Todd Watts in Blanchard, Maine — adjacent to the former home of Berenice Abbott. The darkroom takes silver gelatin prints up to 40×50 inches from negatives between 35mm and 8×10 and supports other analog and alternative processes; the digital side has PC workstations with 34-inch monitors and Adobe Creative Cloud, a 44-inch Epson printer, scanners, trimmers and a 48×96-inch dry-mount press. Residents supply their own film, paper, chemistry and ink. The winter 2027 open call runs August 1 to September 15, 2026 and covers three sessions, of which the four-week January 25 – February 18 session is the one that carries the Abbott Watts placement. Residents receive a private bedroom in shared housing in Monson, all meals, a $500 stipend for the four-week session, and join a cohort of five visual artists and five writers. A car is needed for the eight-minute drive to the Blanchard studio; cell service is very limited in both locations. Applications go through SlideRoom and are open to artists at any career stage, US and non-US citizens with working knowledge of English, who are not currently enrolled in a BFA or MFA program.
