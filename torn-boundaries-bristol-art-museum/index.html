Torn Boundaries

Prizes Museum exhibition Cash awards (amounts not yet published)

National juried call for collage from Bristol Art Museum, the nonprofit museum on Wardwell Street in Bristol, Rhode Island, for a show in its main galleries from 31 January to 1 April 2027. Torn Boundaries asks artists to explore the boundaries that define, separate, connect and sometimes divide us - physical or psychological, personal or cultural, geographic or political, historical or imagined - and the prospectus frames collage as the means: cutting, tearing, layering, juxtaposition, fragmentation and recombination that disrupt established relationships, asking what happens when an edge is crossed, a border redrawn, a familiar image removed from its context. This is a collage call, not a photography call: the prospectus names no media beyond collage and carries no photography clause either way, so photomontage and photo-collage fit its own definition of the form while a straight photograph does not. The juror is Francine Weiss, an artist, art advisor and museum professional with curatorial posts at the National Gallery of Art, Harvard Art Museums, deCordova, Fitchburg Art Museum, the Photographic Resource Center and Newport Art Museum, and a PhD in American art and the history of photography from Boston University. Cash awards are promised but the amounts are not yet published. Entry is open to US residents aged 18 or over; up to three works may be entered ($25 for one, $35 for two, $45 for three) as JPEGs under 4 MB, works no larger than 40 inches in either direction (diptychs and triptychs count as one), unframed work accepted with clips, magnets or an alternative hanging system, with a statement of up to 200 words. Accepted work must arrive by 15 January 2027 - the prospectus says by UPS in one line and by US Postal Service in another - or be hand-delivered on 14 or 15 January with a prepaid return label; the museum takes no commission and puts collectors directly in touch with the artist. Reception 7 February 2027. Entries close at midnight on Sunday 29 November 2026 through OnlineJuriedShows.
