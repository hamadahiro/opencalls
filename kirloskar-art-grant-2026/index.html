Kirloskar Art Grant 2026

Prize INR 5 lakh cash grant each (2 awards)

The second edition of the S. L. Kirloskar CSR Foundation's annual grant for lens-based practitioners, supporting artists whose work interrogates contemporary relationships between people, environments and systems in an age of globalisation, digital saturation and cultural transformation. The Foundation frames photography and moving image not only as tools of representation but as instruments for questioning dominant narratives and creating new ones, and invites proposals engaging critically with sustainability and environment, science and innovation, biodiversity and nature, culture and heritage, or community and social change. Two recipients — one photographer and one moving-image artist — are selected by a jury, each receiving a production grant of INR 5 lakh, of which up to 35 per cent may be taken as an artist fee or honorarium, plus opportunities to show the completed project at Kirloskar Group premises and other venues across India. Applications are open to emerging practitioners working in documentary, conceptual, experimental or new media formats who reside in India and hold Indian citizenship; individuals and artist duos or collectives may apply. Submissions go by email and comprise a project proposal of up to 1,000 words, a 500-word artist statement, a portfolio of five to ten captioned images or videos as a PDF under 5 MB, a CV, a budget breakdown and a project timeline. Equipment purchases are not covered, though project-related consumables such as film, cards, ink and paper are. The project period runs 21 September 2026 to 21 March 2027, and copyright remains with the artist.
