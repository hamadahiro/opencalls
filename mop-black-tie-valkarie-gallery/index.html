MOP: Black Tie

Prize Exhibition

Valkarie Gallery's entry in Denver's Month of Photography 2027: a juried show of classic black-and-white photography in Lakewood, Colorado. The gallery asks for "traditional, sleek, and elegant black-and-white photography that embraces the power of light, shadow, and form" — from high-contrast street portraits and cinematic architectural geometries to minimalist landscapes and editorial fashion — and says it is looking for "technical mastery, deliberate composition, and a refined aesthetic that honors the rich heritage of silver-halide and classic archival black-and-white printing", photographs that "command attention without the noise of color". Eligible mediums are named: traditional silver gelatin prints, darkroom processes, digital black-and-white and archival monochrome inkjet prints. The jurors are Valkarie residents and Denver photographers Robert Newman and McCory James. Entry is $20 for up to three original 2D works, open to anyone who lives or works in Colorado; no AI art is accepted, no digital enhancement is allowed, and work may not exceed 36 × 36 inches. Artists are notified by Friday 19 February 2027, accepted work is dropped off at the gallery between 20 and 28 February, and the exhibition runs 4 to 28 March 2027 with an opening reception on Saturday 6 March, 5–8.30pm. Work must be delivered protected and labelled with the artist's name, phone number, price and title; the gallery takes a 30% commission on sales, prices cannot be changed after acceptance, sold work stays on the wall until the show closes, and payment reaches the artist within a month of closing. Valkarie, open since November 2013, describes itself as an artist collective; the gallery is at 445 S Saulsbury Street and is the winner of Lakewood's Community 2026 Platinum Award for best in art dealers and arts & crafts.
