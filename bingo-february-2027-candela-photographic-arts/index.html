BINGO February 2027 — Cheek to Cheek

Prize Exhibition

The second run of Candela's “give two, get one” small-print open call, and the first with a theme. Candela Photographic Arts — the Richmond, Virginia gallery and independent photobook publisher that became a 501(c)(3) in 2026 after fifteen years as Candela Books + Gallery — asks for “interpretations of touch and fresh takes on the photographic tradition of the nude – particularly, the beloved bottom”. Cheek to Cheek “explores intimacy through touch, gesture and form”: hugs, kisses, caresses, the spaces between, or a favourite derrière, with unconventional, experimental and playful readings of every form of love explicitly welcome. The gallery adds its own disclaimer — keep the work “thoughtful, playful, and/or conceptual”, because Candela “isn't interested in nude imagery that feels exploitative or needlessly vulgar”. Fourteen small photographic works are selected and hang from 8 January to 13 February 2027, alongside Candela's January/February solo exhibitions, before a ticketed Valentine's bingo fundraiser on 13 February at which every framed print on view goes home with a winner. Selected artists send two unframed editions of their image: the first is an in-kind donation that Candela frames and mats at its own cost and gives away on Bingo Day, the second enters a randomised swap that mails another participant's print back to the artist, postage covered by the gallery. Submission is free — “there is no fee to submit to the Bingo show” — and opened on 3 November 2026. Up to three photographic prints may be entered; each must exist in at least two editions (variant editions accepted) and measure no more than 12 inches on the longest side. Submissions take 1–3 JPEGs, the artist's contact details, each work's title, year, medium, dimensions and edition information, and a short statement or bio. Accepted artists are announced on 1 December 2026 and must ship both prints to the gallery by 15 December. Candela encourages alternative processes, tiny prints, variant editions, and work from “side quests, one-off projects, proofs, or works-in-progress” as readily as finished bodies of work — and is candid that the exhibition “is philanthropic and community-focused in nature and may not be a great fit for artists looking for big sales opportunities”. The gallery names no juror. Artists after the gallery's flagship juried show are pointed at UnBound16!, whose own open call runs 5 January to 12 February 2027.
