SHOTS Magazine #174 — Poems & Prophecies

Prize Publication

Spring 2027 issue (#174) of the black-and-white photography quarterly, independently published since 1986. The theme is POEMS & PROPHECIES, and the magazine asks explicitly for a broad, unusual and creative interpretation — "there is no need to be literal." Submit up to 8 images, grayscale, 300 ppi, minimum 12 inches on the longest side, named Lastname_Firstname_Title. Colour files are converted to greyscale on receipt, so the work has to survive the translation. Submission is free for current and renewing subscribers (up to 8 images); non-subscribers pay $28 for up to 8 images, and either group can add a further 8 images for another $28. Every photographer who submits is mailed a copy of the issue whether or not their work is selected — which is also how you find out: SHOTS sends no notification letters, so the answer arrives with the magazine. Open to photographers internationally, all processes, formats and experience levels. Initial selection is made by editor Elizabeth Flinsch, who then builds a layout and goes over the pairings with publisher Douglas Beasley. The magazine states on the submissions page that all images remain the copyright of the photographer "without exception", and warns readers to be suspicious of contests and publications that ask for ownership. Accepted photographers' city/state/country and website are printed in the back of the issue unless they ask otherwise. Online submission only — SHOTS does not review websites and does not accept unsolicited emailed files.
