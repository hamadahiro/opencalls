LensCulture Black & White Photography Awards 2026

Prizes $10,000 Photo London 2027 exhibition

LensCulture's annual monochrome award, judged by a seven-person international jury and carrying $10,000 in cash prizes. THERE IS A FREE ENTRY ROUTE: "Start for free with a single image. One entry per person"; extra single images are $10 each or 5 for $35, and a series of up to 10 images is $45. Students currently enrolled, or who graduated in 2025, get 30% off the paid fees (proof of status is requested if the entry is selected), and an optional written review by a LensCulture professional adds $25 (available on series or 5+ single entries). PRIZES: Series 1st $3,500, 2nd $2,000, 3rd $1,000; Single Image 1st $2,000, 2nd $1,000, 3rd $500 — the six awards sum exactly to the advertised $10,000. In total 33 photographers are honoured: 6 winners, 7 Jurors' Picks (one per juror) and 20 Finalists, all featured on the winners' page and in LensCulture's worldwide promotion. Top winners "will be exhibited during Photo London 2027" — the award page names the fair — with an opening reception for LensCulture artists, invited media, photo editors and industry insiders; series winners receive a solo feature published on LensCulture. Entries are pre-screened by LensCulture editors, who curate hundreds of Editors' Picks into the competition gallery and onto the platform's Instagram and Facebook channels during the call. WHAT TO SUBMIT: strong series or outstanding single images made in monochrome — classic darkroom, digital or experimental — created within the last 10 years; series are judged as a unified thematic or aesthetic group of up to 10 images. ELIGIBILITY: the competition rules state entrants "must be over 18 years of age" and that it is "open to everyone that is 18 years and older, professional and amateur photographers alike". RIGHTS: "All photographers retain full copyrights for their own work"; entrants grant LensCulture a right to reproduce submitted photographs, with photographer credit, to conduct and promote LensCulture competitions, and winning photographs may later appear in travelling group exhibitions or screenings with credit. Work previously awarded a cash prize in a LensCulture competition cannot win cash again, but former finalists and unawarded submissions remain eligible. Deadline Wednesday, October 14, 2026.
