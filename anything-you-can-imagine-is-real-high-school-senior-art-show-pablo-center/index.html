Anything You Can Imagine Is Real... — High School Senior Art Show

A free show at Pablo Center at the Confluence for graduating high school seniors across the Chippewa Valley, and an unusually concrete first break: the first thirty submissions each receive a $250 cash stipend, additional cash prizes (sponsored by Cohen Law) go to the top three pieces, and selected work hangs in the Center's Foust Gallery. Pablo Center asks for whatever the senior year produced — "Whether your work is bold, thought-provoking, personal, or completely unexpected" — and the venue's exhibitions programme takes "original works of all mediums and styles", with no medium excluded, so photographic work is as eligible as anything else. Submission is by email to the visual arts coordinator and the call runs from 1 November to the 1 February deadline. The exhibition is on view 22 February to 19 April, with an artist reception and awards on 12 March, 5-7pm, in the Robert Manz Green Room.
