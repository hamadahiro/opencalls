Your Photography Could Take Flight at Treasure Coast Airport

Prize Public art exhibition at Treasure Coast Airport

A free, single-image public art call from the St. Lucie Cultural Alliance, a St. Lucie County arts organisation on Florida's Treasure Coast that is sponsored in part by the State of Florida's Division of Cultural Affairs, publishes Art St. Lucie Magazine, runs the Downtown Fort Pierce Art Walk and administers the county's Art in Public Places programme. One photograph will be reproduced as a professionally installed wrap on a Fort Pierce Utilities Authority utility box at Treasure Coast Airport in Fort Pierce - in the call's own words, ‘turning everyday infrastructure into a vibrant piece of public art’ - in a spot residents and travellers pass daily; neither surface says how long the wrap stays in place. The theme is open: aviation-themed work — airplanes, flight, aviation history, the spirit of travel — is encouraged as a natural fit for the location but is explicitly not required, and the call asks equally for landscapes, wildlife and anything that captures the beauty, energy or character of the Treasure Coast. Who can apply is stated plainly as photographers of all experience levels, with no residency or age bar on either surface, though the invitation is addressed to Treasure Coast photographers and the Cultural Alliance notes that its own members have preference in selection. There is no entry fee. Submission is one Cognito form per image, so a photographer entering more than one picture fills the form again; the form asks for the title, a description of the work, an uploaded image, and confirmation that you can supply a high-resolution file, since the printed wrap covers a box 58 inches tall and 56 inches wide with a 39-inch back. Two conditions are worth reading before you enter: the Cultural Alliance reserves the right to use digital images of accepted and exhibited artwork for its own promotion and publicity, and it states that while it will do its utmost to protect work in its possession it is not responsible for damage or loss. No artist fee, cash award or print stipend is named on the call page or the form — the opportunity on offer is the installation itself. No jurors or selection panel are named, and no notification date is published.
