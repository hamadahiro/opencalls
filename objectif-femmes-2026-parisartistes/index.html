Objectif FEMMES 2026

Prize Exhibition prize

Objectif FEMMES is an annual call from PARISARTISTES# focused on women photographers. The 2026 edition takes the bicentennial of photography (1826–2026) as its prompt — work that engages with photography's history, technique, or 200-year material lineage.

Selected artists exhibit at Salons Aguado, an exhibition space in Paris. Eligibility is restricted to women photographers, 18+, with the call open to both professional and emerging photographers — career stage is not a filter.

AI-generated work is not accepted.

Each application includes up to 10 photographs and an artist statement. Submission is through the Objectif FEMMES website. Fee is €40. The 2026 deadline was April 30; results announced November 10, 2026.
