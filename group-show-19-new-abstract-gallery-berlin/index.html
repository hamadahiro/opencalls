Group Show #19

Open call for seven solo wall presentations in Group Show #19 at New & Abstract's gallery in central Berlin, staged during Berlin Art Week. The gallery asks for new and innovative positions across all media — painting, drawing and printmaking through sculpture, installation and wall-mounted objects, and explicitly photography, digital works and mixed media — and says only: surprise us with your style. Seven winners are chosen to show physically, each given three to four metres of wall on which one or more works are hung in consultation with the gallery; a further twenty honourable mentions are featured on the gallery's Instagram feed and stories. Work must be no larger than 200 × 200 cm, available for the exhibition and offered for sale both on site and in the online gallery, with the artist keeping 60% of any sale. Exhibited works are insured for the run; work must reach Berlin at the artist's own arrangement, and the artist covers return shipping if a piece comes back. Founded in 2019 as an Instagram platform, New & Abstract has shown more than a hundred artists on 100 square metres in Berlin since June 2024. Entry is a €20 processing fee per artwork submitted, non-refundable, and the gallery states plainly that no further costs arise if you are selected for the exhibition, the online gallery or Instagram promotion. Open worldwide to artists aged 18 and over. Winners and honourable mentions are announced by 24 August; work must reach Berlin by 7 September; the show runs 10–24 September 2026 with an opening reception on 10 September.
