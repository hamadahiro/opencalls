Eerie Expressions

Prize Group exhibition

The San Marcos Art League's October show at the San Marcos Art Center, asking for work that captures the spirit of the month — spooky, scary, black-light, Goth-inspired, spirits and monsters and goblins, from the whimsical to the wonderfully dark. Entry is open to all and every medium is eligible, with photographs and digital art named explicitly alongside fine art prints, sculpture, textiles and pottery. Each artist may enter one original work completed in the last 24 months and not previously shown at the Art Center Gallery. Two-dimensional work must be framed or gallery-wrapped with no visible staples and wired for the gallery's hanging system — no saw-tooth hangers — and no larger than 36 by 68 inches including the frame; three-dimensional work is judged on size and stability and must stand unaided. Subject matter has to suit a family-oriented venue. All work is hand-delivered — the Art Center does not accept shipments — with intake on 4 October between 1pm and 4pm, a public reception on 9 October from 7 to 9pm (an hour later than usual, to find the darkness), and pickup on 29–30 October. Work must be for sale at a price fixed at entry and the Art League retains a 20% commission. Entry is $10 for Art League members using the code emailed to them and $20 otherwise; submissions close 2 October at 5pm or when the show fills.
