2027-28 Call for Exhibitions

Prize Solo or group exhibition

The Gallery at Penn College is a 3,000-square-foot contemporary art space on the third floor of the Madigan Library at Pennsylvania College of Technology in Williamsport, and it fills its season from an annual open call. This round is for the 2027-28 schedule: artists may apply for solo, two-person or group exhibitions, and every entry is juried through a blind selection process by a gallery committee of college faculty and staff, K-12 art educators and artists from the surrounding community. Review takes place across the Fall semester with notification in January. Nothing is charged to apply. Accepted artists are provided with housing, meals, a solo exhibition honorarium, shipping and a travel stipend, and gallery staff install every show; artists must have enough work for a solo exhibition unless applying as a two-person pairing, and site-specific installations the artist installs themselves need a planning document approved before scheduling. The gallery has 82 to 250 running feet of linear wall space and an 11-foot-4 ceiling. Campus visits are an important part of the programming though some exhibitions are scheduled without one; visiting artists give a 20 to 30 minute talk at a public reception held on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, and are encouraged to propose class-specific programs or community workshops. Promotional support runs to an annual gallery catalog, postcards, press releases and web and social media. The call itself enumerates no media, but photography is part of the gallery's own programme: its regional juried exhibition states "All media considered" and the director's list of accepted media for the gallery's 2026 community exhibition names photography outright, alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, fiber, ceramics, digital art, mixed media, wood, metal, crafts and installations. Applications ask for 10 to 15 digital images (JPG or JPEG, 4 MB maximum each), an image list with dimensions and medium, a CV and an artist statement; video work is submitted as a PDF of links. Note that Williamsport Regional Airport connects only to Washington-Dulles, with no other direct flights or trains.
