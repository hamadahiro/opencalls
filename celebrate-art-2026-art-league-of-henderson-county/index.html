Celebrate Art 2026

The Art League of Henderson County's annual regional juried exhibition at Art Place Gallery in Hendersonville's Historic 7th Avenue District, with $2,500 in cash awards. Photography is both a submission category and an award category: alongside a $1,000 Best in Show, $250 category awards go to Work on Canvas or Panel, Work on Paper, Photography, Two-Dimensional Mixed Media, Three-Dimensional and Digital/AI Created Artwork, each with an honorable mention; fiber is also a submission category, though it is not named in the awards list. Juror Jane Allen Nodine is a Distinguished Professor Emerita and former Gallery Director at the University of South Carolina Upstate, an NEA/SECCA Southeastern Seven Fellowship recipient and a two-time South Carolina Arts Commission Artist Fellow; the awards judge, who selects from the installed exhibition, is announced at the opening event. Open to artists 18 and over residing in North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia or Tennessee, with up to three original works for $40 — work made in instructional classes or workshops is not eligible. Entries are juried digitally from submitted images. Accepted work must be hand-delivered (shipped work is not accepted), all artwork must be offered for sale with a 30% commission to the League, and pieces stay on view through October 31 even if sold. The exhibition runs October 1–31, 2026 with an opening reception and awards on October 2.
