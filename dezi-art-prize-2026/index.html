Dezi Art Prize 2026

Two $1,000 unrestricted cash grants for early-career visual artists, run by Dezi, a career-support platform for artists launching in 2026. Photography is named in the eligible-media list alongside painting, drawing and illustration, sculpture, filmmaking, design, UI/UX design, 2D/3D animation, fashion, and fiber and material arts. Open worldwide to artists 18 and over, one entry per artist per cycle, with no degree, gallery representation or exhibition history required. An independent jury of working arts professionals reviews every submission and scores it on four equally weighted criteria — quality of the work, originality and point of view, the artist statement, and the pitch for the grant — with jurors recusing themselves from any application where they know the applicant. Only the two winners receive cash; the top 30 ranked applicants are featured in Dezine, the platform's magazine, and get a named profile on the Dezi site, the top 20 of those also receive a free one-on-one career audit, and every applicant regardless of ranking is offered a post-close career-guidance interview. Winners agree to credit the prize on any new work made with the funds and to share the finished work within a year for possible inclusion in a future Dezi exhibition. Applications opened on 15 July, the jury reviews through September and winners are announced on 10 October 2026. The application fee is $30 flat with no added processing fees; the $20 early bird closed on 1 August 2026. The organisers offer a discount for students and will consider a waiver on request for anyone else for whom the fee is a barrier. Organised by Phistream LLC and led by founder Nitya Mehrotra, a RISD and SAIC graduate whose work spans animation, public art and gallery exhibition.
