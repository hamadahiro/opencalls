Next Generation: University Art Competition 2026

Honeywell Arts & Entertainment runs Next Generation: University Art Competition, a free statewide juried exhibition for students currently enrolled at an accredited Indiana college or university and working towards an associate's, bachelor's or master's degree in any field of fine art or craft. Students on other degree paths are welcome to apply, and the jurors are asked to consider artistic quality and merit regardless of degree. Any two-dimensional or three-dimensional work is accepted provided it fits the gallery, and the presentation rules distinguish photographic and digital work from other media, so photographers may enter prints. Selected work goes on display from 10 December 2026 to 13 January 2027 in the Clark Gallery, which wraps the semicircular Porter Lobby of the Honeywell Center in Wabash and sees more than 170,000 visitors a year across 10-plus exhibits. The published award list is $175 Best of Show, then $150, $100 and $85 for first, second and third place in each of the 2D and 3D categories, plus six honourable mentions; the organiser states no overall total. Awards are announced at the public closing reception on 13 January at 5:30pm. Work must be original, completed within three years of the deadline, not previously shown at the Clark Gallery, and no taller than 8.5 feet, deeper than 2 feet or wider than 5 feet; because the gallery is a public multi-use space, nudity and other adult content are not accepted. Entry is free through CaFE, which accepts up to three images. Honeywell Arts & Entertainment is part of The Honeywell Foundation, a nonprofit public charity that also programmes the Eagles Theatre, Charley Creek Gardens and five other venues in the Wabash cultural district.
