Sublime Photo Awards 2026

Prize $1,000 in cash prizes

The Sublime Photo Awards are a small independent international competition established in 2025, built around a single idea taken straight from the dictionary: the sublime is that which inspires awe through elevated quality, whether beauty, nobility or grandeur. The organisers argue a sublime photograph is reached by great technical skill, an incredible composition or a powerful message, or the sum of several of these, and the award is open to everyone from amateur to professional aged 18 and over, worldwide. There are five categories: Black & White (toned images such as sepia or selenium are accepted here), Creative (abstract, conceptual, fine art, still life, experimental), Nature (landscape, wildlife, macro, seascape, trees, underwater, astrophotography), People (adult portrait, children, family and wedding, fashion and beauty, self-portraits, culture, staged) and Street (light and shadow, people, candid and staged moments, silhouettes). Any capture device is allowed in every category — digital, analogue, drone, mobile, tablet, computer, scanner — and panoramas are accepted. Computer-generated images, whether AI or other technologies, are permitted only in the Creative category. Images must carry no watermark, logo, copyright line or signature. One photo per category may be entered at no cost, but free entries are not eligible for the cash prizes; the paid ladder is $18 for two photos, $25 for three, $31 for four and $36 for five. One caveat worth knowing before you enter: because of state competition law, residents of Maryland, Colorado, Nebraska, North Dakota, Vermont, Montana and Arizona may use only the free option, which means entrants in those seven states cannot compete for the cash prizes at all. The jury is Loredana Denicola, a photographer and visual artist, Daria Bonera, director of DB Agency, and the visual artist Joana Dionisio. Grand prizes are $500, $300 and $200 for first, second and third, each with a place in the Sublime winners gallery, Instagram exposure and a certificate with a winner's badge; ten honourable mentions per category receive the gallery placement, social exposure and an honourable-mention badge. Entries close 1 November 2026 and winners are announced on 3 January 2027.
