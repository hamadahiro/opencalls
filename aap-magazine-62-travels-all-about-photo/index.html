AAP Magazine #62 Travels

Prizes $500/$300/$200 Publication

All About Photo is an international photography platform founded in 2013 by photographer Sandrine Hermand-Grisel and her husband, built to give photographers visibility: curated exhibitions, competitions and editorial alongside AAP Magazine, a printed publication given over entirely to photographic portfolios. Each issue is built around one theme.

Travels is the theme of the 62nd issue, and the call is written to take the pressure off gear and distance alike — your past or current adventures, the four corners of the globe or your own doorstep, a view camera on a tripod or a phone from your back pocket. All genres, capture types, colour and black and white, traditional and non-traditional photographic and digital post-production processes are accepted.

What the magazine wants is a portfolio, not a single frame: entries are judged individually in a first round and then as a unified group, as they could appear across a spread, and only a unified group gets published. Judging is blind — no names reach the juror. Work previously published in AAP Magazine cannot be entered again.

Twenty-five photographers are chosen. First place receives $500 and an exclusive interview; second $300; third $200; and the twenty-two finalists ranked 4 to 25 are published alongside them. Every one of the 25 gets their image or portfolio in the printed Volume 62, a free copy of the magazine, which is sold and distributed through Blurb, and a permanent place in the online Winners Gallery. Judging is by Sandrine Hermand-Grisel, who edits the magazine.

The competition opened October 3, 2026 and closes Tuesday November 10. Up to 40 images may be entered at $35 for the first three plus $5 for each additional image; entering on or before October 16 drops the first-three price to $30. The FAQ says it is open to anyone 18 or older, professional or amateur, but the binding Rules page sets the bar at the age of majority where you live and declares the competition void where prohibited. Judging runs November 14 to December 15, 2026, with winners notified by email around January 1 — both the call page and the Rules print that year as 2026, which their own judging dates rule out, so read it as 2027.
