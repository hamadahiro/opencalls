Multi-Exposed

Praxis Photo Arts Center is a Minneapolis nonprofit gallery for fine art photography. Multi-Exposed is one of its themed juried exhibitions — focused on photography that engages with time overlaps and memory: multiple exposures (in-camera or in post), composite imagery, layered processes, and other approaches that visualize time as compounded.

Selected work is shown in a physical exhibition at Praxis Gallery in Minneapolis, April 18 – May 16, 2026.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through Smarter Entry with up to 10 images. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 22; results announced April 7, 2026.
