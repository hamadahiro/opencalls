A Land Remembered — Elliott Museum Call for Artists

Prizes $300 $200 $100 Exhibition

The Elliott Museum in Stuart, Florida, run by the Historical Society of Martin County, is building a juried contemporary art component into A Land Remembered, an exhibition inspired by Patrick D. Smith's novel of Florida's pioneer era. Photography is named in the call's own media list alongside painting, drawing, watercolour, mixed media, printmaking and sculpture, so documentary and landscape photographers of old Florida - cattle drives and cow hunters, early homesteads, native wildlife, rivers, prairies, hammocks and pine forests, frontier family life - are squarely in scope. Each artist may enter ONE original artwork, existing or new, and must hold the legal right to exhibit and, if applicable, sell it. Generative AI work is barred: artwork created wholly or substantially through generative AI, or whose visual content was generated or substantially altered with it, may not be submitted, while traditional digital tools used on your own original work are permitted. The signed agreement states the door plainly - open to artists of all ages and from all geographic locations, with under-18s entering through a parent or guardian's co-signature. Entry is free and runs in two stages on a JotForm linked from the call page: the Call to Artists agreement first, with contact details, originality and generative-AI certifications and an electronic signature, then the artwork submission itself, which asks for title, year created, medium, dimensions, retail price or NFS, insurance value if requested, one digital image and an artist statement or bio. A selection panel appointed by the Elliott Museum reviews entries after the deadline and notifies everyone by email and phone on 5 November; jury decisions are final and accepted work is inspected again on delivery. Awards are $300 for first place, $200 for second and $100 for third. Count the logistics before you enter: this is a physical show, selected artwork must reach the museum in Stuart by 20 November 2026, 2-D work needs professional presentation and secure hanging hardware (sawtooth hangers are refused), and accepted pieces must stay up for the full run, 2 December 2026 to 30 May 2027 - roughly six months off the wall, whether or not the work sells. Sales are encouraged and run through the museum, which collects the money and forbids side deals: the contract you sign sets the artist's share at 75% of the retail price, though the call page's own summary block advertises 65% - price the work against the contract, and expect payment within 30 days of the museum receiving cleared funds from the buyer.
