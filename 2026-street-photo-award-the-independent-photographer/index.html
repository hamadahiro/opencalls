2026 Street Photo Award

Prize $2,700

The Independent Photographer is a London photography platform that runs monthly themed competitions. The 2026 Street Photo Award is the platform's street-themed cycle.

The brief calls for candid moments: from packed corners to stark geometry. Subject is open inside the genre.

Top prize is $1,000 plus exhibition at PBC Gallery in London plus a feature on The Independent Photographer platform. Total prize pool is $2,700 across category placements. AI-generated work is not accepted.

Selection is by Gulnara Samoilova, photographer.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the platform's website with 1, 3, or 6 images. Fee is tiered $15–$40 by submission scale. The 2026 deadline was March 31.
