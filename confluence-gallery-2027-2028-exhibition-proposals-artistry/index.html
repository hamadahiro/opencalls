Confluence Gallery 2027-2028 Exhibition Proposals

A free call for exhibition proposals from Artistry and the Minnesota Valley National Wildlife Refuge, whose Confluence Gallery sits inside the Refuge's Education and Visitor Center in Bloomington. The Refuge runs to more than 14,000 acres along 70 miles of the Minnesota River, and the gallery exists as a conduit between that landscape and the communities around it, so proposals are asked to focus on nature, natural elements, ecology, conservation or the beauty of the Minnesota River Valley. Minnesota-resident artists aged 18 and over working in any visual medium may propose a show for the 2027 or 2028 programme year; solo exhibitions are the likely outcome, though two artists may be paired. Each selected artist also presents a workshop, artist talk or other engagement event, developed with Artistry and Refuge staff. Each selected artist receives a $300 stipend. A proposal is ten images, work details and a statement of 1,000 characters or less, reviewed by a curatorial panel on relevance to the gallery's mission, artistic excellence, originality and technical skill.
