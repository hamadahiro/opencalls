Solo Exhibition June 2026

Prize Exhibition prize

All About Photo runs a monthly Solo Exhibition program — each month features a different photographer with a dedicated month-long online presentation. The June 2026 cycle is the next available slot.

The selected photographer receives a full month of online solo exhibition (June 1–30, 2026) featured on All About Photo's homepage, competition gallery, and showroom pages. Beyond the month, the work is permanently archived on the platform with a dedicated 20-image portfolio page, accompanied by an exclusive artist interview and social media promotion across the platform's Instagram, Facebook, and other channels.

Past winners have been featured in major international press outlets. Sales are commission-free — the artist retains 100% of the sale price.

Submission is open to photographers worldwide at any career level. Submissions are 6 to 14 cohesive images forming a project or portfolio. JPEG format, 72 dpi, no watermarks, flattened layers, file size under 2 MB, longest dimension max 1,200 pixels.

Selection is by Sandrine Hermand-Grisel, Founder & Editor of All About Photo. Entry fee is $45. The 2026 deadline is May 19; judging runs May 23–31; the selected photographer's solo exhibition opens June 1.
