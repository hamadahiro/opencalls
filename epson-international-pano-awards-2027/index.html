Epson International Pano Awards 2027

Prize US$1,000 Curator's Award

18th edition of the world's largest international competition dedicated to panoramic photography, entries now open. Three divisions run in parallel: Open Awards (open to all photographers, US$22 per image), Amateur Awards (US$20 per image, restricted to photographers earning less than US$20,000 a year from photography) and VR/360 Awards (US$20 per image). Open and Amateur each take Nature/Landscapes, Aerial and Built Environment categories; VR/360 runs as a single category. A panorama is defined as a minimum 2:1 aspect ratio on the long side, and entries may be stitched and/or cropped from multiple or single captures, at any focal length. Open and Amateur files must be exactly 3000 pixels on the long side and no more than 1500 on the short side; VR/360 files exactly 6144 × 3072 pixels. There is no age limit and no cap on entries, but images already shortlisted (top 10 in any category) or given a major or special award in a previous Pano Awards may not be re-entered. Entering five or more images at once takes 20% off the total entry fee — note that these multiple-entry discounts are withdrawn in the final entry period, which follows the June 21, 2027 early-bird close and runs to the July 12 deadline; the per-image fee itself does not rise. Early-bird entrants who also score in the top 50 of Open or Amateur, or the top 20 in VR/360, are in the running for the US$1,000 Curator's Award, chosen by curator David Evans. At least three images in Open or Amateur are required to be eligible for Photographer of the Year. Computer-generated images and generative AI content of any kind are prohibited and RAW files may be requested for suspect entries. Judging runs August–September 2027 with results due in October. The 2027 prize pool has not yet been announced; the 2026 pool was worth over US$50,000 including US$15,000 cash.
