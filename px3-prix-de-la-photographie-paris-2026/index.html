PX3 Prix de la Photographie Paris 2026

Prize €5,000

PX3 (Prix de la Photographie Paris) is an international photography competition based in Paris, awarding work across eight categories: Analog, Architecture, Book, Fine Art, Nature, Portraiture, Press, and Advertising. The structure separates traditional editorial categories (Press, Portraiture) from medium-specific tracks (Analog, Book), giving applicants flexibility in how they position a series.

The main prize is €5,000, with category and subcategory recognitions stacking on top. Winners are exhibited at PX3's annual Paris showcase and featured in the prize's annual book and online archive.

The 2026 jury includes James Wellford (National Geographic), Steve McCurry (photographer), and Samuel Nja Kwa (Forbes Afrique), with the full jury list published on px3.fr. Entry fees range from €20 to €50 depending on category and submission tier. Eligibility is open to photographers 18 and over worldwide. The deadline is June 17, 2026; AI policy is not stated on the public submission page, so applicants making AI-assisted work should email organizers before entering.
