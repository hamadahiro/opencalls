Lucky Star Issue 6: Womanhood & Femininity

Prize Publication

Lucky Star is an independent photography magazine made in San Francisco by Madison Blanchard and sold through Green Apple Books and Beeks Books in the Bay Area and Waste Store in London. Its sixth issue is themed on womanhood and femininity: the call asks for projects about what it means to be a woman in society, women's empowerment, girlhood, female relationships, what femininity means and represents, how body and object contribute to those ideas, and social norms and the breaking of them, with the interpretation left entirely to the artist. Any medium of photo-based artwork is welcome, but it must be submitted as a series or body of work of at least five images, and ongoing projects are accepted. Only women, femme and female-identifying artists may submit to this issue; artists from anywhere and of any age are welcome. The deadline is Tuesday 13 October 2026 at 11:59pm Pacific. Entry is through a Google Form linked from the magazine's Submit page; the form requires a Google sign-in to open, so the fee, if any, could not be read - neither the magazine's site nor its own announcement mentions one, and the previous issue was free to enter.
