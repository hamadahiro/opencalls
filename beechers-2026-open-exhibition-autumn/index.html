Beecher's 2026 Open Exhibition — Autumn

Beecher's is a gallery in the market town of Ross-on-Wye, on the edge of the Wye Valley, running a year-round programme of solo and group shows. Its Autumn Open has no set theme and is open to any artist living in the UK, emerging or established, working in painting, drawing, illustration, printmaking, sculpture, mixed media, photography or digital art — all media are eligible except reproductions, film and video. There is a dedicated photography prize, chosen by juror John Bulmer, the photographer who has documented Britain with a photojournalistic eye since the early 1950s and whose exhibition The North opens at Beecher's in January 2027. First prize is £250 cash plus a year's representation by the gallery, inclusion in its 2027 group exhibitions, a professional portfolio review and career planning session, and promotion across Beecher's website, newsletter and social channels; further prizes in each category include group exhibitions, business mentoring and marketing, and there is a Visitors' Choice Award decided by votes at the gallery. Judging is anonymous from the submitted images. Around 60 works are selected and hung salon style. Work may not exceed 100cm in any direction or 25kg, must have been completed within 36 months, and must arrive framed and ready to hang. Artists may enter up to six works, and all work must be for sale, with the gallery retaining 50% commission and donating a tenth of that to a partner charity. Selections are announced 6 September, the private view is 17 September, the exhibition runs 18 September to 17 October, and unsold work is collected between 17 and 19 October.
