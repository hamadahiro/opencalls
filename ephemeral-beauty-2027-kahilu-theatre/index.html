Ephemeral Beauty — 2027 Juried Art Exhibition

The annual juried exhibition of Kahilu Exhibits, the visual arts programme of Kahilu Theatre in Waimea on Hawaiʻi Island, built this cycle around flowers and the paradox that the most fleeting thing in nature is also the one most permanently woven into human civilisation. The guest juror is Andrzej Kramarz, Gallery Director of the East Hawaiʻi Cultural Center in Hilo, and the brief ranges deliberately wide — flowers as signals of fertile land and water, as the lotus of Buddhist thought, as the blood-born blooms of Greek myth, as the ʻōhiʻa and lehua of Hawaiian moʻolelo — inviting artists to work anywhere in that territory. No medium list is published for this show; the brief's own note on what is sought says all approaches are welcome and asks artists to respond to the flower “across any medium”, and Kahilu Exhibits' separate call for exhibition proposals names photography among the media it programmes. Open internationally to artists 18 and over. The fee is $25 for the first sample and $5 for each additional media sample; accepted work hangs in Kahilu's Simperman and Suli T. Go Galleries with an opening reception on Thursday, January 14, 2027, and artists pay shipping both ways (Kahilu asks for two-day shipping or Mokulele Airlines' Wikiwiki service to Waimea-Kohala Airport, and specifically not ground services). Sales split 60% to the artist, 40% to Kahilu, and Kahilu retains sales rights for 45 days after the exhibition closes; work does not have to be offered for sale. Both the organiser’s own page and the CaFE record read “2027 Juried Art Exhibition, Ephemeral Beauty” and “This exhibition will be on view January 14, 2027 through February 28, 2027”. The CaFE record reads "Deadline for artwork submissions: Monday, December 7, 2026 at 11:59 PM HST" with "Artists notifications will be made via email by Monday, December 21, 2026".
