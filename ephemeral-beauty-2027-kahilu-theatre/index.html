Ephemeral Beauty — 2027 Juried Art Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

The annual juried exhibition of Kahilu Exhibits, the visual arts programme of Kahilu Theatre in Waimea on Hawaiʻi Island, built this cycle around flowers and the paradox that the most fleeting thing in nature is also the one most permanently woven into human civilisation. The guest juror is Andrzej Kramarz, Gallery Director of the East Hawaiʻi Cultural Center in Hilo, and the brief ranges deliberately wide — flowers as signals of fertile land and water, as the lotus of Buddhist thought, as the blood-born blooms of Greek myth, as the ʻōhiʻa and lehua of Hawaiian moʻolelo — inviting artists to work anywhere in that territory. No medium list is published for this show; the brief's own note on what is sought says all approaches are welcome and asks artists to respond to the flower “across any medium”, and Kahilu Exhibits' separate call for exhibition proposals names photography among the media it programmes. Open internationally to artists 18 and over. The fee is $25 for the first sample and $5 for each additional media sample; accepted work hangs in Kahilu's Simperman and Suli T. Go Galleries with an opening reception on Thursday, January 14, 2027, and artists pay shipping both ways (Kahilu asks for two-day shipping or Mokulele Airlines' Wikiwiki service to Waimea-Kohala Airport, and specifically not ground services). Sales split 60% to the artist, 40% to Kahilu, and Kahilu retains sales rights for 45 days after the exhibition closes; work does not have to be offered for sale. One caution worth knowing before you apply: the listing's own prose is part-recycled from the previous cycle — it calls the show the "2026 Juried Art Exhibition", gives an impossible run of "January 14, 2026 through February 28, 2027", and dates the submission deadline "Friday, December 12, 2025" with notifications "Monday, December 22, 2025", while the platform's machine deadline field, last edited 14 September 2026, closes entries on 7 December 2026. The 2027 dates in the shipping and reception block are all weekday-correct, the 2025 ones are weekday-correct for 2025, so the stale block is the December one. Apply by 7 December to be safe, and confirm with the gallery if you need the later date.
