Elemental: Earth, Air, Fire, Water

Prize Online exhibition

An international juried online exhibition built on the four elements — earth, air, fire and water — not merely as scenery but as pressure, weather, heat, erosion, breath, flood, smoke, stone, ash and atmosphere. Work may engage the elements directly or symbolically, exploring beauty, danger, memory, destruction, renewal and transformation. All two-dimensional images with an analog or digital photographic base are welcome, with no restrictions on size or year of production. The show runs online October 1 – November 1, 2026.
