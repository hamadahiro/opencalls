2026 Emerging Vision: CPAC Biennial Student Show

Biennial juried student exhibition at the Colorado Photographic Arts Center (CPAC) in Denver, open internationally to current undergraduate and graduate students enrolled at a post-secondary institution who are not graduating before the end of the 2025–2026 academic year. All lens-based media eligible. Juried by Laura Sackett, creative director and co-founder of LensCulture. Best in Show receives a feature article on LensCulture.com, promotion in its newsletter (580,000+ subscribers), $150 custom printing from Digital Silver Imaging, and CPAC membership; Honorable Mention receives printing and membership. Exhibition November 20, 2026 – January 2, 2027.
