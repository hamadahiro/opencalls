AAP Magazine #56 Shadows

Prize $1,000 prize

All About Photo Magazine (AAP Magazine) runs themed photography contests on a rolling schedule. Each issue is built around an open call. AAP Magazine #56 is the shadow-themed cycle — work engaging with shadow as subject, structure, or compositional element.

Selection is by Sandrine Hermand-Grisel, founder and editor of All About Photo. Single-juror selection.

The winner receives $1,000 cash and publication in AAP Magazine #56. Selected photographers are featured on the All About Photo platform.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the All About Photo website with up to 40 images. Fee is tiered $30–$35. The 2026 deadline was March 31; results announced May 30, 2026.
