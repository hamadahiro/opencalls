As Much Truth As One Can Bear

Prizes $1,500 $1,000 $500 Exhibition

The Arts Center in Orange, on East Main Street in Orange, Virginia, is running a juried exhibition built on James Baldwin's essay and proposition about truth. The show runs from 5 February to 27 March 2027. Eligibility is the first thing to check. The call is open to emerging and established artists of African American heritage, students included, who are 18 or older. All media are accepted, and the centre's list names photography alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, installation, digital and text-based work, video and textiles. There are three cash prizes, $1,500 for first place, $1,000 for second and $500 for third. The page heads them "Up to $3000 in awards and more" without saying what the more is. No juror is named. The page does list the selection criteria: conceptual strength, originality, relevance to the theme, technical quality and emotional impact. The brief asks artists not to illustrate Baldwin or his biography. It asks for work about identity, race, memory, justice, love and survival, and about personal or collective truths, visibility, silence or resistance. Entries go by email, with "As Much Truth As One Can Bear" in the subject line. Applicants send 5–10 images or time-based samples of recent work, an artist statement of 300–500 words and a CV or short biography, and pay the $20 fee through the centre's online shop. The exhibition is physical. Artists who can't deliver and collect in person pay for shipping to the centre, and return shipping help is possible if the work arrives in a reusable UPS box with a prepaid return label.
