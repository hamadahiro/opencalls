Magnum Opus 2027

Prizes Cash prize for every awarded work Possible solo exhibition in 2028 for Best of Show

Magnum Opus is the Sacramento Fine Arts Center's premier annual juried show — the centre defines a magnum opus as an important work of art and asks artists to enter the most outstanding thing they have made recently. It is open to all media, and photography is accommodated by an explicit exception rather than by omission: the rules bar copies, giclées and enhanced giclées and "works by any commercial printing process (except for photography)", so photographic prints are eligible where other printed reproductions are not. Class and workshop art, crafts and copyrighted works are excluded, as is anything already shown in a juried show at Sac Arts. Work must be original and finished within the last three years, must be exactly as presented in the online entry, and must be listed for sale — the centre retains a 50% commission on sales for non-members and 30% for members, so prices should be set accordingly. Two-dimensional pieces should not exceed 40 lb or measure more than 4 × 8 feet; anything larger than 29 × 35 inches must be glazed with plastic rather than glass. Hanging work must have clean edges and be framed unless it is three-dimensional, canvases under three-quarter gallery wrap must be framed, and every hanging piece must be wired with D-rings only, wire ends taped, with no protruding nails, staples or sawtooth hangers. The artist's name, address, phone, email, title, size, media and price go on the back of 2-D work and on the back or base of 3-D work. Entry is through Artwork Archive at $25 per work for a maximum of three pieces, with a 20% discount for members, whose codes are sent out by email and phone. Staff will also enter work on an artist's behalf in person at no additional cost by appointment, though appointments are not accepted on the day of the deadline. Awards carry a cash prize for every awarded work, and Best of Show may receive a solo exhibit at the centre in 2028. The 2027 judge is Jenny Williams, a native Californian painter who studied studio art at Brunel University in London and holds a BA in Design Studies from San Francisco State University; she works in an American Impressionist, largely plein-air idiom, teaches fine art to children and youth with special needs, and belongs to the Women's Caucus for Art and the American Impressionist Society. Entries close on 19 January 2027 at 11:59pm, acceptances go out on 29 January, work is received on 13 February by appointment, and the exhibition runs 16 February to 13 March 2027 at the centre in Carmichael, with a closing reception on 13 March from 5 to 7pm and pick-up straight afterwards. Shipped work must arrive no later than the receiving date, and the artist initiates and pays for all shipping. The show chair is Paul Dessau.
