The Company We Keep: Creatures Beside Us

The Company We Keep: Creatures Beside Us is a group exhibition at Bushwick Gallery in Brooklyn on the presence of animals in our lives — as confidants, muses, and mirrors.

The theme is explored through humor, tenderness, intimacy, and allegory, and the call is open across mediums including photography. Artists may submit up to ten artworks.

The show runs November 5–12, 2026. Submission is through the gallery's website, with a $25 fee per image.
