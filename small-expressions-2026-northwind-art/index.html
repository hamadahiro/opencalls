Small Expressions 2026

Northwind Art's juried show for the giving season asks what an artist can do inside sixteen inches. Everything shown — frame and mount included — has to fit within that in any direction, and the organisers call it one diverse exhibit, welcoming entries in all mediums and naming photography first in the list. All work must be for sale, and the show is run differently from most: rather than hanging everything for the full run, one to three works per artist are on the wall at a time with more held in backstock, and a piece is replaced as soon as it sells. Two Northwind Art curators, the local artists Kim Kopp and Anne Hirondelle, judge on quality, professionalism and sales potential, and one $250 Jurors' Choice Award is given. Up to seven images may be entered for a single $35 fee — per artist, not per work — and that fee is waived on the honour system, using the code DISCOUNT, for artists from historically underrepresented BIPOC and LGBTQIA+ communities. No AI-derived imagery. Accepted work must be delivered in person to the Jeanette Best Gallery at 701 Water Street in Port Townsend; the gallery will not accept shipped work or ship unsold work back, so this is realistically a regional call. The exhibition runs 19 November 2026 to 4 January 2027.
