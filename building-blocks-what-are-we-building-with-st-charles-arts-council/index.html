Building Blocks: What Are We Building With?

Prizes $150 / $75 / $50 Exhibition

A blind-juried open call from the St. Charles Arts Council for an exhibition at its gallery in historic downtown St. Charles, Illinois. The premise is elemental: every creation begins with a single component, whether a stroke of paint, a block of wood or a spark of imagination, and the council asks how the individual components an artist chooses dictate the meaning of the final structure — for the artwork, for a life, or for a community. All art mediums are accepted and three-dimensional and assemblage work is actively encouraged, which leaves photography fully eligible. Artists may enter one or two pieces; the fee is $35 for non-members and $20 for council members, chosen from a tiered drop-down at entry. Work must be original, created within the last seven years, and never previously shown in a St. Charles Arts Council exhibit, and all entries must be for sale. Awards are presented at the opening reception, timed so that every participating artist can arrive: $150 for Best in Show, $75 for second place, $50 for third and further honourable mentions at the judge's discretion. Entries close 11 August 2026; work is dropped off on 31 August, the exhibition runs from early September to 25 October 2026 with an opening reception on the evening of 10 September, and pick-up is 26 October. The council notes it has no storage space, so artists unable to attend drop-off or collection must send someone in their place.
