44th Rock Island Art Guild Fine Arts Exhibition

Prizes $1,500 $750 $500

Long-running juried exhibition presented with the Figge Art Museum in Davenport, Iowa, on view in the museum's fourth-floor gallery September 5, 2026 – January 10, 2027. Open to artists within a 200-mile radius of the Figge working in all media — photography, painting, sculpture, installation, sound, video, digital art and works on paper. Juried by Katherine Alcauskas, chief curator at the Chazen Museum of Art. $1,500/$750/$500 general awards plus watercolor and specialty awards.
