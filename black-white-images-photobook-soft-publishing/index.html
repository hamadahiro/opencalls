Black & White Images — Photobook Open Call

Prize Publication

Open call from Treviso-based independent publisher Soft Publishing for black-and-white images of any subject — cohesive bodies of work or individual images. Up to 10 images per artist, minimum 2000px on one side, submitted with full captions (title, year, medium, dimensions) and a short description. Open to artists from anywhere and of all ages. Selected work is published in a limited-edition photobook. Free to enter via Google Form; selected artists are contacted by email.
