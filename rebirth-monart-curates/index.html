REBIRTH — Monart Curates

Prize Exhibition prize

Monart Curates is an independent curatorial platform running pop-up exhibitions in London. REBIRTH is a curated group exhibition held in Bermondsey, central London, running May 14–19, 2026.

The exhibition is multidisciplinary: painting, photography, installation, textile, and video are all eligible. The brief is held under the REBIRTH theme but the curatorial register is open across approaches.

Selection is by Monica Mardare, independent curator. Single-curator selection.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through CuratorSpace with up to 2 works. Fee is £99 — a higher entry fee that reflects the production cost of the physical pop-up exhibition rather than a competition prize fund.

The 2026 deadline was April 15.
