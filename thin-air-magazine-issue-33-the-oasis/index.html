Thin Air Magazine Issue 33 — The Oasis

Prize Publication (print)

Thin Air is a print literary and arts magazine produced by graduate students in the MFA programme at Northern Arizona University in Flagstaff, and it is reading visual art for its 33rd print issue, themed 'The Oasis'. The editors take the dictionary definition — a fertile green area in an arid region; something that provides refuge, relief or pleasant contrast — and push at it: in a turbulent and at times desolate world, what is your oasis, where do you seek shelter, and what spaces sustain you? They are equally interested in the conditions that produce an oasis, since one can only exist when surrounded by a severe lack of something crucial to survival — so what is your desert, and what drives you to look for relief? The call also asks whether an oasis can be a form of resistance, thinking of the sanctuaries that form in the midst of violence, fear and escalating political tension. For this issue the editors are specifically looking for art, photography and comics that represent the notion of the oasis, with an interest in pieces that work as full-page spreads as well as graphic narratives and poetry comics; outsider art is always welcome, as is anything that surprises or subverts expectations. Colour submissions are open, with particular interest in high-contrast pieces that capture the shades and hues of the oasis. Photographers may send up to five pieces at a minimum of 300dpi with a short description of each work and a two to five sentence bio. Submitting visual art is free — the written-genre categories carry a $3 reading fee that visual art does not — and only unpublished work is considered, though simultaneous submissions are encouraged. Reading begins in August each year and runs until the deadline. One restriction to note: Thin Air does not accept work from anyone affiliated with Northern Arizona University within the last seven years.
