Main Gallery Exhibition Submissions

Prize Gallery exhibition in Portland

Blue Sky, the Oregon Center for the Photographic Arts, accepts proposals for its main gallery year-round. The Portland non-profit has shown photography since 1975 and is currently scheduling new exhibition selections for late 2027 and beyond, so a proposal sent now is read against that calendar rather than a single themed show. The Exhibition Committee reviews every submission equally and takes work from emerging, mid-career and established artists; Blue Sky membership is not a prerequisite for consideration. The gallery states plainly that it receives hundreds of submissions each year, cannot respond to status enquiries and does not give feedback on work it turns down; it asks for up to six months to reply. An automatic email confirms that a submission arrived, and the gallery asks anyone who does not get one to submit again. All work must be photo- or video-based. A submission is 15 to 30 images from a single body of work, formatted as one horizontal 8.5 x 11 inch PDF (one image per page, black background, no borders, no watermarks) or a zip of individual JPGs, with the whole upload kept under 10 MB; video goes in as a zip or as a PDF carrying a viewing link. A concise artist statement and a CV go with it. Separate bodies of work are submitted individually, and the gallery asks explicitly that nothing be sent by post or email — only through the form on its submissions page. There is no submission fee.
