InCadaqués Photo Festival Open Call 2026

Prizes €1,000 Exhibition

Open call for the 10th edition of InCadaqués, the photography festival held in Salvador Dalí's village of Cadaqués on the Costa Brava. All photographers are welcome with a free-theme project of 10 to 15 photos; 20 projects are selected for exhibition during the festival. The grand prize winner receives €1,000 plus production costs, accommodation and transportation for the opening weekend; 14 finalists each receive €150 plus production of three exhibited images. Two further €1,000 awards — the Women Photography Award and the New Realities Award — and three €300 special mentions are also given. Results by the end of August 2026.
