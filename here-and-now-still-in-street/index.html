Here & Now

Prizes Exhibition Zine

Open call from London street photography collective Still In Street for Here & Now, a one-night exhibition at the Photobook Cafe, Leonard Circus, London, on August 20, 2026, 6–10pm, accompanied by a zine. Looking for candid, unstaged street photographs shot in London between 2020 and 2026 — no composites or AI-generated images. Submit up to 3 photographs by email (under 1MB each) with your name, Instagram handle, and a brief description; one image per photographer will be shown. Free to enter; a £20 printing fee applies to selected works, and selected photographers keep their print and receive a copy of the exhibition zine.
