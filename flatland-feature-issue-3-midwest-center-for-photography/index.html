Flatland Feature — Issue 3

Prize Online exhibition

Midwest Center for Photography (MWCP) is a Wichita, Kansas gallery that runs themed exhibitions alongside two online feature programs. Flatland Feature is the series-portfolio slot — a place for an extended body of work rather than the single-image group shows that fill most of the gallery's calendar.

Selected photographers receive a six-month feature on the MWCP website, January through June 2027. Each feature presents 10–20 images in a slide-show format with an accompanying artist statement and biography, closer to a solo presentation than a group exhibition entry.

Visitors can view and purchase photographs through the feature page. MWCP takes 50% commission on sales, and no shipping is required — the work exists online only, so there are no framing or print costs unless a sale is made.

Eligibility is open to all photographers internationally, with no restriction on subject matter, genre or photographic medium. Selection is based on the thematic series submitted rather than on individual images, and a brief artist statement and bio are required. The fee is $100 for 10 images, $150 for 15 and $200 for 20. The Issue 3 deadline is October 1, 2026.
