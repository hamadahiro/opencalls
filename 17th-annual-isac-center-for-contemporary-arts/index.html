17th Annual Intercollegiate Student Art Competition

Prizes $300 Best in Show $100 First Place Photography Exhibition

The Center for Contemporary Arts in downtown Abilene, Texas runs its Intercollegiate Student Art Competition for a seventeenth year, and photography is one of its four judged categories alongside non-digital 2D, digitally created 2D and 3D work. Entry is free and open to students enrolled at Abilene-area and regional colleges and universities; each student may submit up to two pieces in any medium that meets the category's size and weight limits, and work must be original and not previously exhibited at the CCA. Entries are juried by professional contemporary artists, and selected work hangs in Gallery 3 at the CCA from 4 December 2026 to 23 January 2027, with a closing reception and awards on 23 January, 5-7pm. Cash prizes are $300 for Best in Show and $100 for First Place in each of the four categories, including First Place Photography; those five winners also receive a one-year CCA Student Artist Membership, with the usual 20-hour volunteer requirement and application fee waived for a year. Photographs and digital prints must be matted and at least 8 by 10 inches, framed unless canvas-wrapped or mounted on board with proper hanging equipment, no wider than 48 inches, no taller than 60 inches and under 30 pounds. Submission is through the organiser's online entry form, one form per piece, and work must be delivered in person to 220 Cypress Street in Abilene on 28 November, 11am-5pm, or by noon on Monday 30 November; late entries are not accepted even when the form arrived on time. Unselected work is collected after notification, exhibited work from 25 to 30 January 2027, and the prospectus warns that anything left more than 30 days past those dates becomes CCA property. Awards are Best in Show and Best in Category, with optional second, third and honourable mentions.
