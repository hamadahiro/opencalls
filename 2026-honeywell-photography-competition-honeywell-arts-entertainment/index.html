2026 Honeywell Photography Competition

Prizes $200 Best of Show Exhibition

Juried photography competition and exhibition at the Clark Gallery in the Honeywell Center, Wabash, Indiana. Open to U.S. residents of all ages — amateurs, professionals, and students — in three categories: Color, Black & White (including sepia), and Altered Images. Artwork must be original and completed within five years of the deadline, and ready to hang. Jurors are not announced in advance to protect the integrity of the show. Awards include Best of Show plus first, second, and third place in each category and ten honorable mentions. Exhibition August 18 – September 23, 2026.
