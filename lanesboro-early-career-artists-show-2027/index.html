Lanesboro Early Career Artists Show 2027

Prize Group exhibition

Group exhibition highlighting the work of three or more early career artists from Minnesota, Iowa, or Wisconsin at the Lanesboro Arts Gallery, February 6 – March 20, 2027. Lanesboro Arts defines an early career artist as one with great potential who has not yet established significant gallery representation. All visual arts media welcome, including photography. Applications due December 7, 2026 at 12 pm noon; accepted artists contacted by December 23.
