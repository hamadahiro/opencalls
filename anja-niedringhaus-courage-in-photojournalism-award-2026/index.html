Anja Niedringhaus Courage in Photojournalism Award 2026

Award honoring courageous, high-impact photojournalism by women, nonbinary, and gender-diverse creatives. Submit 12 photographs (6 from 2024–2026, 6 from broader career) plus a 500-word statement. Open to women, nonbinary, and gender non-conforming photojournalists working full-time in journalism. Self-nomination or third-party nomination accepted. Named after Pulitzer Prize-winning photographer Anja Niedringhaus (1965–2014).
