Portfolio Review II

Fotografiska Emerging is the international photography platform run by Fotografiska, the museum group with locations in Stockholm, Tallinn, Berlin, New York and Shanghai. The Portfolio Review is a recurring open call pitched as a developmental opportunity rather than a prize competition: selected photographers get direct one-on-one feedback from Fotografiska exhibitions staff.

This second edition is reviewed by Marie-Luise Mayer and Sofia Liljergren. Mayer is Exhibitions Manager at Fotografiska Berlin and has overseen the Emerging Berlin programme since 2023; she studied art history and cultural studies at Humboldt University of Berlin, holds a master's degree in Photography Studies and Research from Folkwang University of the Arts, and through the Alfred Krupp von Bohlen und Halbach Foundation's fellowship for photography curators worked at Museum Folkwang in Essen, Fotomuseum Winterthur, Munich City Museum and the Victoria and Albert Museum in London. In 2024 she curated Meet Me Halfway for EMOP Berlin. Liljergren is an Exhibitions Manager at Fotografiska in Stockholm, with a background in journalism and art history and studies in France, Spain and Sweden.

Fotografiska pitches the brief as having one requirement only — the work must be a visual project with a clear narrative, a series that functions as one body of work rather than a set of individual frames. The terms are a little more specific than that: entrants must be at least 18, each project goes in as a single PDF of 5 to 15 images under 10 MB with a description of up to 500 characters, and up to three projects may be submitted, one per entry. There is no nationality, residency or career-stage restriction, and the only people excluded outright are Fotografiska group staff and their relatives.

Submissions close 5 November 2026 at 6:00 PM CET, and Fotografiska says it will contact everyone, selected or not, on 18 November. Review day is 26 November 2026, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM CET, held over Microsoft Teams in English, with each session running 20 minutes and the reviewer assigned by Fotografiska rather than chosen by the artist. Time slots are fixed and cannot be rescheduled, places are limited, and applicants are asked to confirm availability in that window before submitting. Fotografiska says further editions are planned in time zones suited to a wider global community.
