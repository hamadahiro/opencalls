Photography Exhibition 2026

Prize Exhibition

Gutman Gallery's only single-medium exhibition of the year, run by The Guild of Artists & Artisans in downtown Ann Arbor. The call carries the gallery's ongoing storytelling theme into a question for photographers — “What compels one to take photographs? Is it to share stories, to seek connection?” — and asks for work that captures each photographer's own reason for using the medium, explained in the artist statement. Photographers of all subject matter and all formats may enter as long as the work can be displayed in a gallery; 2D work must be wired and ready to hang, and artists must be able to document their process and verify authorship. One entry fee covers up to three works, with two images allowed per work. Editing software is fine provided all imagery originates from the artist's own creative source, but work composed entirely by or relying on AI-generated imagery is ineligible. Jury decisions go out September 4–5, accepted work is delivered September 9–19, and the exhibition runs September 26 – October 31, 2026 with an opening reception on the 26th. The Guild takes 30% commission from members and 40% from non-members on sales.
