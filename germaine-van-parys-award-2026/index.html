Germaine Van Parys Award 2026

Named for Belgium's first female photojournalist, who made her debut at Le Soir in 1918 and founded the Van Parys agency in 1956, the Germaine Van Parys Award is a Brussels-based programme for women photographers working in a documentary approach, organised by the non-profit Out of Focus and now in its third edition after laureates in 2024 and 2025. The 2026 call runs from 15 September to 1 December 2026 and is open to women photographers of all ages and nationalities, including anyone who identifies as a woman; applications are free and may be written in French, Dutch or English. There are two categories. The Germaine Van Parys Award itself is a €8,000 production grant for a professional photographer - one whose work has previously been exhibited or published - to make an unpublished documentary project related to Brussels over ten months; the money is meant for production, travel, equipment, developing and digitising, while the organiser covers exhibition prints and framing for the show that follows. The Germaine Van Parys Mentorship selects five women photographers residing in Belgium for ten monthly in-person sessions in Brussels, held in French on the first Thursday of the month from February 2027, with a €1,000 grant each towards a project of their own choosing; applicants must be able to take part in French and commit to all ten dates. Both strands end with a Brussels exhibition in January 2028 - the award winner's finished work shown alongside a projection of the mentorship projects - and the award project is also published as a portfolio in Médor magazine. Applications go through a form on the website as a single PDF of no more than 10 MB: for the award, a CV of up to two pages, a short biography, 10 to 15 images and a project statement on the Brussels subject; for the mentorship, a CV, biography, 15 images, a project description and a motivation letter. One project per applicant. A jury of photography and visual-arts professionals will be published on the website; winners are announced on 21 December 2026 and must be available for a meeting on 7 January 2027.
