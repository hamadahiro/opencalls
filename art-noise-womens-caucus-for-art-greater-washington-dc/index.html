Art & Noise

Prize Exhibition

The Washington, DC chapter of the Women's Caucus for Art invites women and non-binary artists based in DC, Maryland or Virginia to make work about how sound and silence shape a creative life. The premise is the music blasting in a studio, the ambient hush of a museum, the rhythm and harmony artists reach for without words. Photography is named among the accepted categories alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, collage, fiber work, mixed media and free-standing sculpture; commercially produced prints, giclees, jewellery and performance are not accepted, and video or film needing an external power source is considered case by case. Work is juried by LaPrea Rich, an abstract expressionist from Prince George's County, Maryland who has shown in more than twenty juried exhibitions since 2023 and is a member of the Arlington Artists Alliance, the Maryland Federation of Art and the McLean Art Society. Selected work hangs at the Harmony Hall Arts Center in Fort Washington, Maryland from November 30, 2026 to January 9, 2027, with an artist reception on December 5. Two-dimensional work must be no larger than 72 inches in any direction including its frame and must arrive exhibition-ready with wire and D-rings; sawtooth hangers are refused. Harmony Hall takes 25 percent of any sale and WCADC takes no commission. The chapter is entirely volunteer-run and directs entry fees to its Visual Arts Scholarship and to artist relief.
