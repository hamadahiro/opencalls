Art Hop 2026

Prizes $1,000 Best in Show Category awards

Art Hop is a statewide juried exhibition run by Hill Country Arts Focus, a 501(c)(3) founded in 2007 to promote the arts in the northern Hill Country of central Texas. The 2026 edition runs the whole of October in Georgetown's historic downtown district, with selected work shown at the Georgetown Public Library and Lamppost Coffee, both just off the courthouse Square. Photography is one of six judged categories, alongside 2-D Representational, 2-D Abstract, 3-D Representational, 3-D Abstract and Digital Art; the photography category covers camera-generated photographic images. Awards run to $1,000 for Best in Show plus cash or prizes totalling almost $4,000 across the individual categories, announced at the reception ceremony. Judging happens in two rounds — online in late August, then in person before the exhibit opens, which determines placements and cash awards. Entry is $45 for up to three artworks or $55 for four or five, with a limit of five entries per artist, submitted through EntryThingy. Wall pieces including photography may be up to 48 inches on any side including the frame and must arrive ready to install with professional hardware. The call is open to any artist aged 16 or older residing in Texas.
