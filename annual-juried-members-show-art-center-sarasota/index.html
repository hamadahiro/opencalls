Annual Juried Members Show

Prizes $500 Exhibition

The Annual Juried Members Show at Art Center Sarasota — a 501(c)(3) art centre on Sarasota Bay with four galleries, in its 2025–26 centennial season — is free from a prescribed theme and welcomes original work in all media, with the call naming painting, drawing, photography, printmaking, mixed media, digital arts, sculpture and video. Every current member who submits is guaranteed inclusion, so the jurying decides the awards rather than the hang: Swiss-born Sarasota muralist Alissa Silvers reviews all required information except the artist's name and the artwork price, and selects the awards in person once the show is installed. Awards are 1st place $500, 2nd $300, 3rd $200, plus two Merit Awards and four Honorable Mentions. Work must be original, completed within the last three years and not previously shown at the centre; editioned prints are eligible where labelled and numbered, which the call spells out as covering photography, and copies or reproductions are not. 2D work is capped at 30 × 30 inches after framing. All work must be for sale, with the artist keeping 60% of the selling price. Membership runs from $25 a year for students and $75 for individuals. The exhibition runs 3 December 2026 – 9 January 2027 with an opening reception on the 3rd; accepted work is received in person on 17–18 November or must arrive by post between 16 and 18 November.
