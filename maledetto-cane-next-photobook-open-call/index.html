Maledetto Cane — Next Photobook Open Call

Prize Photobook publication

Rome-based independent photobook publisher autoproduzioni Maledetto Cane is looking for a photographic body of work with a coherent authorial visual language and the potential to be published as a monographic photobook. The selected body of work will be developed into a monograph by the press. All submissions are also considered for inclusion in Maledetto Cane #3, the third issue of the collective periodical. Submit a portfolio of no fewer than 40 JPEGs by email or WeTransfer, with a short synopsis or artist statement; a draft PDF of the proposed book may be included but is not required.

DeadlineSeptember 15, 2026
Entry feeFree
LocationRome, Italy
Requirements40+ images
Submit viaEmail

Organized by autoproduzioni Maledetto Cane
