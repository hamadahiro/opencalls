Trois Points Mag — Vol 14: Party

Prize Publication (print) prize

Themed print issue (Vol 14) of Trois Points Mag, an independent contemporary photography magazine based in Paris. The Party call invites photographers to explore the many dimensions of celebration — gatherings, nightlife, the dance floor, bodies in space, the after and the in-between, being together or apart. Submit up to 8 high-resolution images (300dpi) via email to troispoints.paris@gmail.com. Smartphone photography is not accepted. There is no submission fee; a small voluntary donation is welcome but optional. Deadline 15 September 2026.
