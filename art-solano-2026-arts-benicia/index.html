Art Solano 2026

Arts Benicia's county-wide survey of the artists of Solano County, open to any subject and to all 2D and 3D visual media plus video — photography included. Eligibility runs to artists living in Solano County or whose main studio is there, which covers Benicia, Dixon, Fairfield, Suisun City, Rio Vista, Vacaville, Vallejo and the communities of Allendale, Hartley and Green Valley. The juror is Glen Helfand, a writer, critic, curator and professor in the Graduate Fine Arts Program at California College of the Arts, whose writing on art, culture and design has appeared in Artforum, Aperture, The Guardian, Photograph, SFMOMA Open Space and W, and who has organised exhibitions for the de Young Museum, the San Jose Museum of Art, the San Francisco Art Institute, the Mills College Art Museum, Art Toronto and the San Francisco Main Library. Two-dimensional work may not exceed 36 inches wide or 72 inches high including the frame, and 3D work is capped at a 36 by 36 inch footprint; staff should be contacted about installations or unusual sizes. Work previously shown at Arts Benicia is not eligible. Wall works must be wired rather than hung from sawtooth hangers, there is a 35-pound weight limit on hanging work, and the prospectus states that giclées are not accepted, so check with the gallery before submitting inkjet prints. Arts Benicia handles all sales and artists receive 60%. Entries close 2 August 2026 at 11:59pm Pacific, jurying runs 4–15 August and artists are notified 18 August. Hand delivery is 1–2 October and shipped work must arrive by 2 October. The exhibition opens with a members' and artists' preview on 22 October and a public reception on 24 October, and runs to 20 December 2026 at the gallery on Commandant's Lane. The show is supported by a grant from the Solano County Arts Council.
