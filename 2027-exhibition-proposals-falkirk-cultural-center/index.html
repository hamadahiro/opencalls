2027 Exhibition Proposals

The City of San Rafael invites artists, groups and organisations to propose exhibitions for the 2027 calendar at two civic venues: Falkirk Cultural Center, an 1888 Queen Anne and Eastlake mansion on the National Historic Register that the City owns and operates and that has served the Marin community as a cultural center since 1974, whose three second-floor rooms are the gallery space, and the recently renovated San Rafael Downtown Library, one of California's Carnegie libraries, open again after a restoration that preserved the 1909 building. Falkirk hosts five or six exhibits a year, each running roughly six weeks, with most openings timed to the Downtown San Rafael Second Friday Art Walk. Proposals are judged on community interest, quality of work and a track record of achievement, and applicants are asked to show they can curate an exhibit large enough to fill one gallery room at Falkirk or the Library's display shelves, which are built for three-dimensional work. All media are accepted except AI-created images and videography, and no residency requirement is stated. Proposals go first to City staff, then to the Public Art Review Board in October, and selected proposals move into a contract. Exhibitors handle their own transport, installation, take-down, title cards and opening reception, and provide images good enough for the City to build a virtual version of the show for accessibility; the City provides staffing during open hours, show cards and promotion. Sales run through the City on a 70/30 split in the artist's favour. Application is $5 through CaFE.
