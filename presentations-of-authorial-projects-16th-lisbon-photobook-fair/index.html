Presentations of Authorial Projects — 16th Lisbon Photobook Fair

The third of the 16th Feira do Livro de Fotografia de Lisboa's parallel open calls — alongside its photobook dummy exhibition and its self-publishing and small publishers space — this one offers photographers a speaking slot rather than a table. Selected authors present their own photobook project to fair visitors at the Arquivo Municipal de Lisboa – Fotográfico on Rua da Palma, in sessions running across the fair: Friday 27 November between 5:30pm and 7:30pm, and Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November between 11:00am and 12:30pm and again from 2:00pm to 7:30pm. Each presentation runs a maximum of 25 minutes and a projector is available. Pre-registration is through a bilingual Portuguese/English Google Form and closes at 9:00pm on 12 October 2026; the fair's organisation confirms by 6 November whether the presentation will go ahead and at what time. There is no participation fee. The organisers note that authors whose project includes a photobook for sale should also register separately for the Espaço dos Autores e Pequenos Editores, and that dummies can be submitted in parallel for pre-selection in the dummy exhibition — the three calls share a deadline but are entered independently.
