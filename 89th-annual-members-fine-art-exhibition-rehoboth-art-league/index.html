89th Annual Members' Fine Art Exhibition

Prizes $500 Best in Show / $50 Best Photograph Exhibition

The Rehoboth Art League's longest-running exhibition reaches its 89th edition, hanging in the Corkran and Tubbs Galleries on the League's Henlopen Acres campus from 23 July to 22 August 2027, with an opening reception on Friday 23 July (5–7pm) and a gallery talk by the awards judge, Martha Spak, on Saturday 24 July (10am). There is no theme. Applications close at 4pm on Friday 16 July 2027. The show is open to current Rehoboth Art League members only — membership must be current when you apply and for the run of the exhibition, and the League holds sales payments for lapsed memberships. Membership runs from $30 a year for students up to $1,000; the $30 Student level is the one tier whose published benefits do not include "Eligibility to participate in Members' Exhibits (some are juried)", so $50 Individual is the cheapest level that can enter. On top of that the registration fee is $25, non-refundable, and each member may submit exactly one work. Photography is explicitly accepted, alongside digital art and sculpture: the prospectus asks for original work and bars reproductions and giclée prints "except for photography". Work must be less than two years old (nothing made before 2025), must not have been exhibited at the League before, and framed or unframed may not exceed 48 inches on its longest side; sculpture should be small to medium in scale and weight. Hanging work must arrive wall-ready with wire — no sawtooth hangers — and the Exhibitions Director decides placement. Drop-off runs 12–18 July 2027 and unsold work is collected 23–26 August, after which unclaimed pieces incur $5 a day in storage. Everything in the show must be for sale: nothing may be listed NFS, the artist sets the retail price, and the League keeps a 35% commission, paying the artist 65% monthly on sales. Twelve named awards are listed. The two largest are open to any medium — the Sarah Ethel Tunnell Award for Best in Show ($500) and the Mary Hall Leach Award for Outstanding Work ($250) — and photography has one award of its own, the William Horn Award for Best Photograph ($50). The rest are mostly medium-specific: the Delaware Watercolor Society Award of Excellence ($300), the Faucett Okie Emerging Delaware Artist Award ($300, oil or pastel in a traditional style, Delaware residents only, applied for by email with a statement), the Thomas McFarland Skelly Award for a painting of exceptional merit in any medium ($200), the Howard Schroeder Award for best portrait or figurative work ($100), the Mary Derrickson McCurdy Memorial Award for honourable mention ($100), the Connie Boland Award for best still life ($100), the Louise Chambers Corkran Award for best sculpture ($50), the Adelia E. Chiswell Award for outstanding watercolour ($50) and the Col. W.S. Corkran Award ($50) for the best painting of the Eastern Shore, also applied for by email. Selected works appear on the League's website and in its promotional, educational and press materials.
