smallART 2026/27

Arts Illiana's Terre Haute gallery devotes its winter slot to small-scale work, with nothing larger than 12 by 12 inches framed for two-dimensional pieces or 12 inches cubed for sculpture. Entries must be original work in any art medium, made in the past five years and not previously shown at the gallery; the prospectus names photography outright among the processes it expects artists to describe, citing 'inkjet print of digital photograph on archival paper' and 'dye sublimation print of digital photograph on aluminium' as model entries. There is no entry fee and artists may submit up to five works. A guest curator selects, and cash awards are given in specific categories. The show is open ONLY to artists in Arts Illiana's six-county Indiana region - Clay, Parke, Putnam, Sullivan, Vermillion and Vigo - plus the Illinois counties of Clark and Edgar. Accepted work is delivered 27-31 October and 3-7 November; the exhibition opens with a reception on Friday 13 November 2026 and runs to 6 February 2027. Everything hung must be for sale, with the gallery taking a 30% commission, and framed work must be glazed in acrylic or Plexiglas rather than glass.
