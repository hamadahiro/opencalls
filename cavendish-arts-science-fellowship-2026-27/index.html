Cavendish Arts Science Fellowship 2026–27

Annual fellowship in partnership with Girton College, Cambridge, supporting artists to engage with physicists and others at the Cavendish Laboratory. Runs October 2026 – October 2027 with a Cambridge residency of 6–12 months (can be split across visits). Includes £10,000 stipend, £10,000 production budget, up to £3,000 travel support, rent-free accommodation and meals at Girton College, and a studio/creative workspace plus flexible working spaces at the Cavendish Laboratory. Open internationally to artists of any medium or nationality — no prior science experience required. Must be 18+ and out of full-time education by October 2026. Joint applications accepted.
