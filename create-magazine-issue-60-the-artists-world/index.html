Create! Magazine Issue #60 — The Artist's World

Open call for Issue No. 60 of Create! Magazine, an artist-led print and digital publication for contemporary art. The theme, The Artist's World, asks what it means to build a world as an artist — a universe made through the work, an independent practice that makes its own opportunities, or simply inhabiting the planet; the brief is open to all interpretations and all subjects and media are welcome, photography among them. Juried by Marina Granger, founder of the New York advisory The Artist Advisory, former guest curator of the magazine's 2024 Women's Print Issue and curator of Lunation at Warnes Contemporary and The Feminine Divine at Kaliner Gallery. Selected artists receive a four-page spread in the print and digital editions, website and social features, and a complimentary digital issue. Submit up to 10 works (cohesive bodies and series encouraged) with a 300-word bio and artist statement; $35 covers three images. Open to artists worldwide with no academic background required, and there are no further fees if published.
