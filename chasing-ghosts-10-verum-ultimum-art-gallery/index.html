Chasing Ghosts 10: Piercing the Veil through Remembrance, Legacy & Beyond

Prizes Online exhibition Catalogue

The tenth edition of Chasing Ghosts, the series Verum Ultimum's director conceived when the Portland gallery was celebrating its third year and describes as a favourite with artists and collectors alike. This year's edition is virtual. The emphasis, as always, is the idea of revealing the unseen, and “ghost” is not meant to be read literally — the gallery suggests this round's interpretations might be loaded with the added turmoil of the current social, health, environmental and political climate. It asks whether you express your deepest fears or concerns through your work, what parts of the past leave their mark on it, whether a legacy is revealed through your work or your process, and whether memory is a vital vehicle of expression for you. Any interpretation of the call is encouraged, and all mediums and modes of expression are welcome. Entry is $25 covering two images, with additional images at $5 each up to six; applicants must be 18 or older and the call is open internationally. Selected work is shown in Verum's virtual viewing galleries, which display work at scale, and carried through an exhibition book, an exhibition page, the website at large and blog posts, with social media promotion staying on the show for four weeks. Work may be listed for sale or entered as exhibition-only; the gallery takes a 30% commission on sales, provides prepaid shipping labels when work sells, and allows no substitutions of accepted work. The exhibition opens in mid-October and runs for a month.
