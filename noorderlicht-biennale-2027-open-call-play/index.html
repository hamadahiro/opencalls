Noorderlicht Biennale 2027 Open Call — PLAY!

Noorderlicht's 2027 biennale invites photographers and lens-based artists worldwide to propose existing, adapted or new work on play — including AI, generative, interactive and game-based pieces. Around 75 artists show across Groningen, Friesland and Drenthe from September 2027; each is paid a Mondriaan Fund artist fee of €640 to €3,179 and Noorderlicht covers printing, framing, insurance and transport.

LocationGroningen, Netherlands
