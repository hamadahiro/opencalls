Aesthetic Dynamics

Prize Publication

LoosenArt's open call for a new issue of its Issue Collective editorial project, on the theme Aesthetic Dynamics. Entry is free and open worldwide, and the accepted media are stated plainly: photographic, video and digital works are welcome, with no restriction on style or genre. The theme asks for contemporary readings of aesthetic dynamism, a lineage the call traces from the nineteenth-century study of the organic world — when the microscope, X-rays and microphotography opened up dimensions that had been difficult to observe — through Art Nouveau's curved, sinuous lines and fluid forms, and Futurism's speed, movement and simultaneity, to what those ways of seeing can become now. Selected works are brought together inside the publication as a single editorial narrative connecting different contemporary interpretations of the theme. A selection of the works chosen for publication will also be considered for a further selection process leading to a group exhibition to be held in 2027 in one of three cities — Milan, Rome or Cagliari — which the organiser has not yet fixed. Submission is through a Google Form asking for name, country, email, website, Instagram, an artist statement of up to 70 words in English, and up to three image files of no more than 5 MB each, renamed for the work's title. The deadline is 17 October 2026. Be aware that LoosenArt's site is now a spare Google Sites presence run from Quartu Sant'Elena in Sardinia, and beyond the call text itself it publishes no further terms; no charge of any kind is mentioned at any stage, either on the call page or in the entry form.
