Tallgrass Artist Residency 2027

Prizes 2-week residency $650 stipend

A free-to-apply, ten-night residency in the tallgrass prairie, run since 2016 as a programme of the Kansas Arts Commission. Artists are housed at Matfield Station in Matfield Green, Kansas — a community of about fifty people, twenty miles from the Tallgrass Prairie National Preserve and home to a number of artists and community builders. The residency exists "to create connection and conversation across the Great Plains through engagement with the prairie ecosystem", and the organisers welcome "applicants from all backgrounds and career levels, media and practice", naming visual artists alongside writers, performers, composers, landscape architects and social practitioners, so a photographer applies on the same footing as anyone else. Residencies run in consecutive ten-night blocks between April and September 2027. Each artist receives lodging for the ten nights, a $650 stipend from the Kansas Arts Commission for travel and materials — reimbursed after the residency is completed, not in advance — and a connection to staff at the Tallgrass Prairie National Preserve, who may grant special access on request. Artists travelling from 700 or more miles away as the programme's one "ecoregion exchange" resident receive an extra travel stipend. What is asked in return is substantial and worth reading before applying: a public programme during the two weeks, daily Instagram posts documenting the experience, a written reflection for the residency website afterwards, and a commitment to share post-residency work at a time and venue agreed with staff. Artists arrange and pay for their own transport — a reliable vehicle is described as essential given the rural setting and the twenty-minute drive to the nearest groceries — and buy their own food, though cooking and eating utensils are provided in every room. There is no dedicated studio space: each artist’s apartment has a table and chair, and other local work spaces may be arranged with advance notice, depending on what the artist needs. The programme prioritises artists who currently live in a place that is ecologically prairie or grassland, with one residency period each year reserved for an artist from outside that ecoregion; it does not accept applications from international residents, or from artists who have held the residency before. Applications opened 1 October 2026 and selected artists are notified on 15 December 2026.
