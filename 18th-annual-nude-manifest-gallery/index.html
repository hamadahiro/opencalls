18th Annual NUDE — Manifest Gallery

Prizes $50 honorarium $1,000 Grand Jury

18th edition of Manifest's annual themed exhibition on the uncovered human form, open to all media including photography. Submit up to 3 entries for $45, $5 per additional image. Every exhibiting artist receives a $50 cash honorarium. $9,000 in Grand Jury Awards distributed across nine Season 22 exhibitions ($1,000 per show). Artists receive 70% on sales. Exhibition at Manifest Creative Research Gallery in Cincinnati, Ohio, August 14 – September 11, 2026. Open globally to all artists; original works only addressing the human form theme.
