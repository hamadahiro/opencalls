SCG Artist in Residence & Teaching Program 2027 — National

Three-week teaching residency (August 13 – September 3, 2027) at Blue Line Arts in Roseville, California, funded by Placer Community Foundation. The selected artist receives a $10,000 honorarium, $1,470 per diem, up to $800 travel and $800 car-rental reimbursement, up to $4,200 housing reimbursement, and up to $500 for supplies and shipping. In exchange the artist gives a lecture, holds open studio hours, hosts school tours, and teaches two workshops. Open to US artists 18+ outside the six Northern California counties covered by the regional track. Applications close at 5:00pm PST on 31 December 2026, not at midnight.
