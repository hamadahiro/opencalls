Abundance — SaveArtSpace Billboard Exhibition

Prize Billboard exhibition in NYC

Public art exhibition on billboard advertising space in New York City, curated by independent curator Lauren Hirshfield, opening September 4, 2026. The theme invites artists to consider cycles of care, giving, and receiving — what abundance looks like, and how it might be manifested. Open to all mediums, including photography, and to artists of all ages and backgrounds. Selected works are displayed on NYC ad space for at least one month; artists are notified after August 3, 2026. SaveArtSpace is a non-profit that has placed artists' work on commercial ad space since 2015, and fees are tax-deductible donations supporting exhibition production.
