2027 Thin Line Photo

Thin Line's storytelling festival in Denton, Texas invites photographers worldwide to submit to five themed categories. Selected photographs are printed on archival paper, mounted frameless and shown in Denton galleries during the March 2027 festival and for weeks after, and photographers keep the prints. Every accepted photographer gets an Artist Pass, plus hotel nights if travelling from outside Dallas–Fort Worth.

DeadlineJanuary 15, 2027
Prizes$500 award, Exhibition
Entry fee$8.50 per image ($4.25 each after 5 in one order)
LocationDenton, TX
RequirementsSingle images or series
