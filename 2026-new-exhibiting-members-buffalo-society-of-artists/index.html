Prize Exhibition membership

Annual jury for exhibiting membership of the Buffalo Society of Artists, founded in 1891 and one of the oldest artist societies in the United States. Exhibiting membership is the gate to the Society's juried programme - the annual Catalogue Exhibition, the Open Members show, the Summer and Thumb Box exhibitions - and its 226 current exhibiting members include working photographers, so a photographic practice is judged on the same footing as painting or printmaking. Applicants upload a resume, a brief artist statement and 9 to 13 works made in the last three years; video artists submit a single MP4 of up to ten minutes instead. Three of the uploaded works must then be brought as originals to an in-person review on Saturday, October 10 at Our Lady of Hope Parish in Buffalo, drop-off 9:30-10:15am and pick-up by 1pm. The seven-member review panel is named in advance and includes two photographers - Christina Buscarino, gallerist of the C. Stuart and Jane H. Hunt Art Gallery, and Nate Ely, education director at CEPA - alongside Hallwalls deputy director Parrish Gibbons; it scores each applicant on artist statement, innovation, technical facility, presentation and exhibition history. Open to artists 18 or over who currently live in Western New York and are not enrolled in an undergraduate visual arts degree. The $25 jurying fee is non-refundable, the application window runs September 1 to midnight on October 2, 2026, decisions are sent by mail by October 17, and accepted artists then pay $50 annual dues.
