LACDA 2026 International Juried Competition

The relaunched flagship competition of the Los Angeles Center for Digital Art, the downtown LA gallery founded in 2004 that has collaborated with curators from MoCA, LACMA and the Hammer Museum. Open internationally to digital art and photography — any style where digital processes were integral to creating the image. The winner receives a solo exhibition in LACDA's main gallery (September 3–26, 2026) with a reception, plus ten museum-quality prints up to 44x60 inches (a $2,500–3,000 production value); all entrants get consideration for future LACDA group shows. Entry is $25 for three images ('back to 2004 price'), multiple entries allowed. Juried by founder and director Rex Bruce.
