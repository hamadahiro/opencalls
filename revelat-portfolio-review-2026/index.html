Revela'T Portfolio Review 2026

Portfolio review open call for Revela'T, the contemporary analog photography festival in Vilassar de Dalt near Barcelona (October 1–18, 2026, main weekend October 16–18). Eighteen photographers are selected for nine face-to-face 20-minute sessions with the ten invited professionals — curators, editors, gallerists and festival directors, three hours of feedback in all — plus a full festival pass; one participant additionally wins production and exhibition of their project at Revela'T 2027. Open to photographers of any nationality or residence working in chemical or digital photography; selection favors projection, originality, and a compelling concept, and the stage of development of the project is not taken into account. Applicants send between 10 and 15 JPGs, minimum 1500 px on each side. A €25 application fee applies to all entrants (€15 for Revela'T Friends), and the 18 selected participants are then required to pay a €100 participation fee.
