Portrayed 5: A Juried National Exhibition of Portraits

Prizes $500 / $300 / $150 Exhibition

Portraiture calls are usually either painting competitions that tolerate photography or photography competitions that want faces. Portrayed 5 is neither: d'Art Center hangs its Main Gallery with whatever best answers the brief, and the brief is simply a human figure or a human face, made in the last eight years, in any medium and any style the artist works in.

The money is real for a show at this scale — $500, $300 and $150, selected by a guest juror rather than the gallery — and the entry structure is unusually forgiving: $35 buys three submission slots, which the organisers say outright can be three different works, one work with detail shots, or the same image three times. Each extra work after that is $5, up to ten.

Two things to price in before entering. The gallery takes 40% of any sale, which is at the high end for a nonprofit art centre; and if your work is accepted you pay to ship it to Norfolk and must include a prepaid FedEx or UPS return label, since d'Art will not use the postal service and will not accept freight. Work has to arrive by 5pm on 19 December, five days before Christmas.

The juror is unnamed at the time of writing. Gallery Director Amanda Bradley curates the selection and the guest juror picks the winners, so the pool is shaped twice. The show runs 9 January to 6 February 2027 with a streamed reception on 14 January, shared with The Deets in the adjacent Vault, and stays online afterwards on d'Art's archive site.
