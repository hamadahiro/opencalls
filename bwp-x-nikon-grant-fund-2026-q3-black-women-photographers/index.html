BWP x Nikon Grant Fund 2026 — Quarter 3

Prizes $2,500 Nikon Z6III kit

Third-quarter 2026 cycle of the Black Women Photographers x Nikon $50K Grant Fund — a year-long program supporting Black women photographers globally through four quarterly grant opportunities. The Q3 recipient receives a $2,500 USD unrestricted cash grant plus a Nikon Z6III with 24-70mm f/4 lens kit. Funding can support photography projects, production, travel, equipment, or other professional needs. BWP is a global membership community founded in 2020 by Polly Irungu, with members across 60+ countries and 35 U.S. states. Applications for Q3 close August 21, 2026; a final Q4 cycle follows later in the year.
