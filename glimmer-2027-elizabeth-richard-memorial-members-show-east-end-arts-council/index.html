GLIMMER — 2027 Elizabeth Richard Memorial Members' Show

Prize Exhibition

The first exhibition of East End Arts Council's year and its annual members' show, held in the council's downtown galleries at 48 and 11 West Main Street in Riverhead, New York, from 23 January to 27 February 2027. The 2027 theme is GLIMMER, an exploration and celebration of light in all its forms — from shimmer, reflection and radiance to glow, shadow and illumination — and the council invites artists to consider how light influences what we see, what we feel and how we experience the world around us, interpreted literally, symbolically or abstractly. Member artists of all disciplines may submit one original artwork; painting, photography, sculpture, mixed media and other disciplines are all named as welcome. The show is non-juried, so there is no selection panel, though the council reserves the right to request an alternative piece if it feels a work does not suitably meet the theme. Participation is open to East End Arts Council members with active memberships — individual membership starts at $45 a year on the council's own membership page — and the submission fee on top of that is $5, which the organisers say helps cover administrative and installation costs. Entry is through the council's own online submission form, which doubles as the loan sheet and carries the full consignment terms: work must be ready for installation on delivery, the artist is responsible for transporting it to and from the gallery at their own expense, no additional fine art insurance is provided, and on a sale the artist chooses in advance between taking 70 percent, taking 50 percent, or donating the whole amount to the council. Submissions opened 12 October 2026 and close 3 January 2027; selected work must be dropped off no later than 9 January, with artists contacted about drop-off or shipping, and the opening reception is on 23 January 2027. Unsold work must be collected within 14 days of the exhibition closing, and the loan sheet states that work left beyond 90 days is considered a donation to the council and becomes its property.
