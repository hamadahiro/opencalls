Convergence of Creativity 2026 — Unexpected: Look Again!

Prize Gift certificates up to $150

The City of San Ramon's third annual juried exhibition takes 'Unexpected — Look Again!' as its theme, asking artists to find the surprising, unconventional, humorous, mysterious or unforeseen in everyday objects, nature, relationships, memories, materials or imagination, whether read literally or conceptually. Photography is one of six named categories, alongside mixed media and printmaking, painting, drawing, ceramics, sculpture and textiles, and digital art; both two- and three-dimensional work is accepted. It is an annual event featuring work from artists around the country, open to anyone aged 16 through senior adults. Work must be original and conceived and created entirely by the entering artist, with no AI-generated work accepted, and pieces shown in last year's San Ramon juried exhibits are not eligible. Two-dimensional work may not exceed 30 by 40 inches including the frame and 40 pounds; three-dimensional work must stay within 18 inches each way and 50 pounds. Accepted work is hung on a Walker display system and must arrive exhibit-ready, cleanly framed and wired with D-ring hangers. Up to three entries per artist at $15 each, with a $5 student rate via a coupon code. Selection and awards are decided by a blind jury that does not see personal information, and the awards are gift certificates: $150 for Best in Show overall, then $100, $75 and $50 blue, red and white ribbons in each category. The exhibition runs November 14 to December 16 at the Lindsay Dirkx Brown Gallery in the San Ramon Community Center, with a public artists' reception on November 14; sales carry a 20 per cent commission to the city and work must be hand delivered and collected on set dates. Note that the CaFÉ application portal does not open until August 14, 2026, at cityofsanramon.info/unexpected; artists are notified October 16.
