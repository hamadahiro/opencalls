Snapped-2026: Art Bias Photography Open

Prizes $100 / $75 / $50 per category Exhibition

International juried photography exhibition at Art Bias Studio 114, an artists' collective gallery in San Carlos, California. Four individually juried categories: Black & White, Color, Film Captured, and Youth (18 and under). Open to photographers worldwide who own copyright to their work; note that selected prints must be hand-delivered to the gallery (no shipping or receiving available). Juried by Natalie Saprykina (art advisor, founder of White on White Gallery) and Michael Endicott (Bay Area photographer, former Edge of Frame Gallery director). Cash awards of $100, $75, and $50 for first, second, and third in each category, plus honorable mentions. Exhibition August 2 – 27, 2026.
