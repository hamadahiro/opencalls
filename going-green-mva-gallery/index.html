GOING GREEN

A national call from the MVA Gallery in historic Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, built entirely around one colour: "Green is located in the center of the color spectrum as if to proclaim its significance as the center of the color universe!" The gallery asks how you use it - "Does the color permeate your entire image or is it used in small sections? Does it appear in your artwork in objects or in nature or in portrait studies or in abstract design? Is Green purely decorative, or is it a narrative or structural device?" - and sets no other limit: "No restrictions on subject matter or style exist for this exhibition. All approaches are acceptable - traditional or experimental, representational or abstract, unified or fragmented, lucid or mysterious." Photography is a named medium and the prospectus is unusually welcoming about process and presentation: "All photographic processes are welcome", with framing in black or white preferred for a traditional presentation, white or off-white mats, and unframed mounting considered. There is no cutoff date for when the work was made. A small rotating committee of artists, gallerists, art reviewers, publishers, curators and academics juries the entries and has requested anonymity for this show; decisions are final and no feedback is given. Awards are an "Art of Distinction" award - a featured solo presentation in the gallery's Exhibition Room Two at a future date - a $200 Director's Choice award at the Executive Director's discretion, honourable mentions that carry free entry to a future MVA show, and an annual grand prize of a New York City exhibition opportunity chosen in December from everyone exhibited that calendar year. The exhibition runs 4-28 April 2027; artists are notified on or about 11 February 2027 and accepted work must reach the gallery by 31 March. Work has to arrive ready to hang with stretched wire on the back, under 48 inches framed and 45 lbs, plexiglass preferred over glass; artists pay shipping and insurance both ways and must enclose a prepaid return label. The gallery keeps 30% on sales.
