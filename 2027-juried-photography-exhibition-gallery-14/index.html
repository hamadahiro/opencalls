2027 Juried Photography Exhibition

Gallery 14, in Hopewell, New Jersey, describes itself as "a co-op gallery of fine art photographers" that shows its members' own work and sponsors shows of other artists and upcoming photographers, and this is the open, all-comers round of its calendar — the annual juried show that fills the gallery for the whole of January. It is photography only: the prospectus states that images "must be originated as a photograph and can't be A.I. generated", which is about as clear a medium gate as a photographer could ask for, and it is the gallery's own sentence rather than a platform's boilerplate. No theme is set, so it is a straight read on the work. Entrants may submit up to eight photographs at $12 for each image submitted, in JPG, with no signatures or other identifying marks on the files. The jury is three people and is worth reading before you decide: Molly Roberts, a documentary photographer and visuals editor whose 35-plus-year career includes The Washington Post Magazine, USA Weekend, Smithsonian Magazine and National Geographic Magazine, and who has taken the NPPA award for Best Use of Photography in a Magazine and been a National Magazine Award finalist; Parvathi Kumar, a fine art and street photographer with recognition from Lens Culture, Life Framer, Exibart and Pure Street Photography, a finalist for the Lucie Foundation, Women Street Photographers and the Istanbul and Brussels street photo festivals, published in the British Journal of Photography, and author of the 2021 photo book Everyday Blackness: Celebrating Exceptional Women; and Nils Huehnergarth, a fine art photographer of candid, street-inspired documentary work whose pictures are in the permanent collection of the New Jersey state capitol in Trenton and who joined the gallery as a member in 2026. Cash awards go to Best in Show, Award of Excellence and Award of Merit, with three further photographs given Honorable Mention; the gallery does not publish the amounts. The exhibition is open to the public Sunday, January 10 to Sunday, January 31, 2027, weekends only, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., with a private awards reception for participating artists and one guest on Saturday, January 9. Two practical things to weigh before entering. First, the prospectus is emphatic that "accepted artists must deliver their ready-to-hang prints to Gallery 14 in person" — drop-off is Friday, January 1, 4-6 p.m. or Saturday, January 2, 10 a.m. to noon, pick-up January 31 or February 1, and while accepted entrants are told they will be sent information about framing requirements and alternative drop-off arrangements, the default assumption is that you can get to Hopewell twice. Second, the gallery takes a 35% commission on sales and all work must be self-insured and stay up for the full run. Selections are notified by November 30, 2026. Submitting also grants the gallery permission to use accepted images, with an artist credit line, on its website and in printed material promoting the show. The curator is Rebecca DePorte.
