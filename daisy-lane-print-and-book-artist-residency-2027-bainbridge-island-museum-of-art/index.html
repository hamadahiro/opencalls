Daisy Lane: Print & Book Artist Residency 2027

Prizes $350/week stipend Residency (2–4 weeks)

Bainbridge Island Museum of Art has spent years building the Cynthia Sears Artists' Books Collection, and Daisy Lane is the workshop end of that commitment: a newly renovated, light-filled studio a few minutes from the museum, equipped in part with presses and equipment acquired from Paper Hammer and Marquand Books, the letterpress, printmaking and book design studio in Tieton, Washington. The residency is year-round and holds two artists at a time — one from the surrounding community and one from outside the region — each for two to four weeks, with studio access from 8am to 10pm daily. The curator of the books collection, Erin Zona, frames it as support for the stage when new ideas are still taking shape rather than for finished projects.

Read the scope carefully before applying. This is a print and book residency: the named processes are book arts, letterpress, screenprinting, Risograph and related forms, and applicants are asked to be comfortable with at least one of the studio's offerings, though familiarity need not be advanced and orientation and instruction are provided. Photography is not enumerated as a medium — the relevance here is for photographers whose work ends up as an artist's book or a photobook, or who want to print photographic work through Riso or letterpress. Traditional and new technologies are both encouraged, and artists at all career stages are invited.

The residency now pays: the CaFÉ record's Stipends and Housing block gives every resident a $350-a-week unrestricted stipend and a $500 materials budget, with a one-bedroom apartment for non-local artists, ferry tickets and a Bistro expense account on top. Applying costs nothing: the application is free, with an optional $25 support payment offered on the platform, and asks for contact details, a project proposal, an artist biography and five to ten work samples. The museum's own page states that applications for 2027 resident artists open on 1 October 2026; the portal record puts the close at 1 December 2026, so at the time of writing this is a call to note now and submit to in October. No selection panel is named.
