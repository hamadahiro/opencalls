Small Town: Small Works 2026

Prize Exhibition

The annual small-format open call at the Reeves House Visual Arts Center, the visual arts wing of Woodstock Arts — a four-acre arts complex north of Atlanta with a performing arts centre, an outdoor music venue, a sculpture garden and a gallery whose shows rotate every eight weeks. Everything in the show is 15 inches or smaller in any direction including the frame, and the call is deliberately hyper-local: entrants must be 18 or over and live within a 15-mile radius of Woodstock, Georgia. Fifteen dollars covers up to five submissions. Photographs and other works on paper must be framed and protected under glass or plexiglass, and hanging hardware is strictly specified — wire, D-rings or a cleat only, no sawtooth hangers, plastic frames or clip frames. Only original work is eligible and pieces previously shown at the Reeves House are not. All work is for sale, split 60% to the artist and 40% to the Reeves House; because the show is hung as a rotating stock, sold pieces go home with the buyer and another work comes out of storage to replace them, so the organisers ask that submissions be similar in size. Notifications go out by 13 October, signed contracts are due 6 November and work is dropped off 2–7 November. The exhibition is on view 13 November 2026 to 2 January 2027, opening with a reception on 13 November from 6 to 8pm.
