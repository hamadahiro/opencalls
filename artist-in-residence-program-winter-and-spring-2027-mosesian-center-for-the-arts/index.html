Artist-in-Residence Program: Winter and Spring 2027

Prize Residency

The Mosesian Center for the Arts in Watertown runs its second Artist-in-Residence Program with a straightforward proposition: free studio space and professional development support, no fee to participate beyond a refundable key deposit. Photography is named directly in the list of eligible visual-art disciplines, alongside painting, sculpture, printmaking and mixed media, and the programme also takes performing, literary, interdisciplinary and socially engaged artists. Residents get a private or shared 11 by 22 foot studio with 24-hour access for three months, a welcome reception, and $45 an hour (minimum 1.5 hours) for any workshops or classes they teach during the residency. Two to three General Artist Residencies are offered free of charge. One Ignite Artist Fellowship is selected from this cycle, carrying the same free studio space and professional development support for an emerging artist from the global majority or a historically marginalised community, plus a $1,000 stipend towards materials, transport or project costs. The programme is aimed at emerging and mid-career local and regional artists looking for time and space to experiment. Housing, per diem and materials are not otherwise included, and residents are expected to share their work through at least one public-facing event — an open studio, artist talk, workshop or performance. Winter residency runs January to March 2027, spring runs April to June, and selected artists are announced 15 October 2026. Applications ask for eight images or files, a CV, an artist statement and a short bio.
