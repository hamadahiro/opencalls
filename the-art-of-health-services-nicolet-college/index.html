The Art of Health Services

A free open call from The Gallery at Nicolet College in Rhinelander, Wisconsin, part of the college's ongoing "The Art of" exhibition series. The Art of Health Services asks for artwork inspired by healing, resilience, caregiving and the impact healthcare workers have on individuals, families and communities, and is mounted in recognition of the college's Nursing, Medical Assistant, Phlebotomy, Radiography, Substance Use Disorder Counseling and Human Services programmes. The call names no medium restriction and asks for work "through a variety of artistic media" that explores the experiences, connections and dedication that define careers in health services. There is no entry fee and no commission on sales — if a piece sells, the college passes the enquiry to the artist and the two settle it directly. Artists may submit up to three pieces. Entries are reviewed by Nicolet's Team Art committee together with health and human services faculty, with acceptances going out the week of 19 October. Hand deliveries are taken on 20 November and shipped work must arrive by 25 November with return paperwork and pre-paid postage included; the opening reception is 9 December and the show closes 23 January 2027.
