LatinXperiencia 2026: La Resiliencia

Cabrillo Gallery's annual LatinXperiencia series returns for National Hispanic Heritage Month with a call to Latino/a/x artists living anywhere in the US, working in any art media or discipline. The theme is resilience — the capacity that lets us bend without breaking, whether through personal upheaval such as illness, the end of a relationship or the death of someone we love, or through the larger pressures of authoritarianism, threats to immigrant communities and communities of colour, economic crisis, war and environmental disaster. Work does not have to address Latino/a/x themes or culture, though it can; the gallery welcomes any interpretation, imagery or content that captures the idea of resilience. Juried by Alyssarhaye Graciano, Visual Arts Curator at MACLA (Movimiento de Arte y Cultura Latino Americana) in San Jose, an author, textile artist and advocate for emerging and established artists of colour. Up to three works per artist; entries must be original and not previously shown at Cabrillo Gallery, and no AI-generated images will be considered. Three Juror's Awards of $300, $250 and $200 are presented. This is a physical exhibition — accepted artists deliver or ship work at their own expense and must include a prepaid return label. Selected artists are notified by 17 August. The show runs 28 September to 27 October 2026 at the gallery in Library 1002 on the Aptos campus, with a reception on 26 September and a curator's talk during it. The submission deadline was extended to 4 August.
