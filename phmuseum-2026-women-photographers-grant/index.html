PhMuseum 2026 Women Photographers Grant

Now in its 10th edition, the PhMuseum Women Photographers Grant supports the work and careers of female and non-binary photographers of all ages and from all countries, working across documentary, fine art and conceptual photography. The open call awards a total of $11,000 in cash prizes plus €5,000 in MPB gear vouchers: $5,500 (1st), $2,200 (2nd), $1,100 (3rd), a $2,200 New Generation prize for entrants aged 30 or under, and two MPB Special Mentions of €2,500 in vouchers each. Selected work is shown in a solo exhibition at PhMuseum Lab, screened at the PhotoVogue Festival, and featured via a Vogue Italia interview; nine free one-to-one portfolio reviews are awarded, each a 30-minute session with a mentor of the recipient’s choice from the PhMuseum Education Program. The grant promotes stories told from a female and non-binary perspective and advances gender equality across the photography industry. Submissions now close on 22 October 2026 — the organiser extended the original 15 October deadline — and the fee published on the call page is $45. Both figures move within a single cycle: $35 early-bird to 24 September, a cut to $25 in early October, back to $40 on 12 October, $25 on 15 October, and $45 under the extension. Check the deadline and the figure on the call page before paying. The grant's FAQ still prints an older ladder (€25 until 19 September, €30 until 10 October) but ends by deferring to the call page — "unless differently displayed on the open call landing page" — and the landing page carries the current dates and prices.
