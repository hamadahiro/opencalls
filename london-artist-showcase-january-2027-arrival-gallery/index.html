London Artist Showcase — January 2027

Prize Exhibition

Arrival Gallery runs three-day group shows in a rotating list of cities, and this call is for its London show at 56 Dawes Road, Fulham, from 15 to 17 January 2027. It has an open theme and is open to all visual media, including fine-art, documentary, portrait, street and experimental photography; AI-assisted work is on its list of accepted mediums. Submitting is free, but the show is paid for by the artists who are selected. The London page sells three routes: a Digital Showcase from £65, where the work is shown on screens without shipping anything; a Physical Showcase from £165, with the work hung in the space; and a limited Featured place with extra promotion, whose price is not published. When read on 7 November the page listed 40 digital, 40 physical and 5 featured places as available in the current batch. The exact fee, format and timeline are sent after selection, before the artist confirms a place. Physical work has to be delivered or shipped by the artist. Applying starts with a mailing-list signup. The application link asks for a name and email address, with a marketing-email consent box and a choice of medium, and sends a Typeform link to that address. Selected artists get a place in the show, preparation guidance and promotion through Arrival's channels, and may be considered for its other cities.
