Prix Révélation SAIF x La Kabine 2026

Prize €2,000

Prix Révélation SAIF x La Kabine is an emerging photography award organized jointly by SAIF (the French Society of Visual Arts Authors) and La Kabine, the Arles photography center. The 2026 cycle is one of the SAIF prizes for visual arts authorship.

The award supports emerging contemporary photography. Top prize is €2,000 plus exhibition during Festival OFF Arles — the parallel program running alongside Rencontres d'Arles, where curators, editors, and gallerists travel each summer.

Selection structure has two tiers. The winner receives the cash prize and Festival OFF exhibition. Beyond that, 30 finalists have their work projected at La Nuit de l'Émergence (the festival's emerging-photography evening event), with €100 each.

Eligibility is restricted to professional photographers, all nationalities, 18+. Submission is through JotForm with 10–15 images. Fee is €5. The 2026 deadline was March 29; results announced June 2026.
