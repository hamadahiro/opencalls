22nd International Alternative Processes Competition

Soho Photo Gallery's 22nd International Alternative Processes Competition, the annual juried show devoted to historic and handmade photographic processes at New York's longest-running co-operative photography gallery. Accepted processes include, but are not limited to, albumen, cyanotype, Van Dyke brown, platinum/palladium, gum bichromate, bromoil, salt print, tintype, ziatype, daguerreotype, image transfers, liquid emulsion, anthotypes, photogravure and chemigrams, as well as handmade prints made from digital negatives - the gallery's rule is that the image must originate in an alternative process and must not be AI-generated. There is no limitation on subject matter, and no image may measure more than 40 inches on its longest side. The juror is Amy Arbus, who photographed downtown New York for The Village Voice from 1980 to 1990 (the work that became On the Street 1980-1990), has published The Inconvenience of Being Born and The Fourth Wall, has taught at the International Center of Photography and Maine Media Workshops, and whose prints are held by the Metropolitan Museum of Art, MoMA and the New York Public Library. Selected work is exhibited at the gallery's West 23rd Street space in Chelsea from 2 to 20 December 2026, with an opening reception on 3 December. Prizes are $300, $200 and $100 plus honourable mentions - the gallery's own page says two, the CaFÉ prospectus three - and the gallery takes 30% commission on sales during the show. Open worldwide to photographers 18 and over, excluding Soho Photo Gallery members. The deadline moved from 23 September to 2 October 2026; winners are notified on 29 October and accepted work is due at the gallery by 18 November.
