Catskill Art Space Call for Submissions 2026

Prizes $1,000 honorarium Solo presentation

Catskill Art Space, a nonprofit contemporary art center in Livingston Manor, New York (a renovated 1929 theater that has presented work via Pace Gallery), invites single-artist exhibition proposals for its programme. Selected artists receive a $1,000 honorarium toward art transit and preparation and a solo presentation in a ground-floor gallery, chosen by the Artists Council. All media are welcome, including photography; artists who exhibited in the 2017–26 season are ineligible, and regional (Catskills/Upstate NY) artists are prioritized.

DeadlineJuly 1, 2026
ResultsAugust 2026
Entry feeFree
LocationLivingston Manor, NY
Submit viaWebsite

Jury: CAS Artists Council
