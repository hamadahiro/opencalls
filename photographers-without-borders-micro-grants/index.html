Photographers Without Borders Micro-Grants

A $250–$500 USD micro-grant awarded to a different photographer every month by Photographers Without Borders, the nonprofit registered in both the US and Canada that has built its programme around ethical, community-rooted visual storytelling. Submissions take the form of a photo essay posted to PWB's members' community platform, and the brief is explicit about relationship: it is aimed at photographers with strong connections to the communities in their stories, whether they come from those communities themselves or have built those relationships over time. Subjects range across land and water protection, Indigenous science and wisdom, climate change and regeneration, and everything intersecting in between, including human rights, Indigenous sovereignty and land back. Mixed-media photographers are welcome. Recipients receive $250–$500 USD. There is no submission fee, but applicants must be PWB members: basic membership is $9.99 a month and includes access to the community platform, the Storytelling for Change learning series and digital copies of the PWB magazine, and the organisation offers a needs-based membership to anyone who cannot afford it. Grants are awarded monthly on a rolling basis, so there is no fixed closing date.
