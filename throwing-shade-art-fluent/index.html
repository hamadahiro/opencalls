THROWING SHADE

Art Fluent's online juried exhibit on light and shadow — the organisers write that the two 'speak to the parts of us that words miss', light revealing and shadow concealing, and that in the space between them mystery and emotion come alive. They want your version of that in any medium. Open worldwide to amateur and professional artists in all fine art media, with photography named explicitly in the list alongside oil, acrylic, watermedia, pastel, drawing, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, fiber, wood, ceramics, film/video and digital art; AI will not be accepted, work previously selected for an Art Fluent exhibit is not eligible, and neither is work copied or made under an instructor's guidance. Curated and juried by Creative Director Amy Matteson Neill with an anonymous committee of jurors — there is no personal feedback. Best of Show takes $750 and two Director's Choice awards take $250 each; every accepted piece is shown in the online gallery at art-fluent.com, sales enquiries go straight to the artist and the artist keeps 100% of any sale. JPEGs are due by midnight MST on Friday, January 8, 2027, with notification and the online gallery opening by February 5. Entry is $25 for the first piece and $10 for each additional, up to twenty in total; diptychs and triptychs upload as a single image, and a portion of the entry-fee proceeds is donated to arts organisations.
