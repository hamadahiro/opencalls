Solo and Small Group Exhibits

Prize Solo or group exhibition

The Delaplaine seeks proposals for solo and small group exhibitions on an ongoing basis, for five galleries on two floors of its building in historic downtown Frederick, Maryland; openings are held on most first Saturdays. Proposals go to an Exhibition Selection Panel of artists, art professionals, art consultants and art educators, which asks whether the work is enriching, cohesive and relevant, and whether the installation suits the spaces available — the Delaplaine states plainly that artists are not judged on their backgrounds or writing skills. All media are encouraged and the call is open to anyone: past exhibitors have been local and international, professional and non-professional, self-taught and highly trained. Applicants submit 6–20 work samples with a bio/CV, artist statement and exhibit proposal; no commercially made prints, giclées or reproductions of an original work are considered. Proposals are reviewed every other month (received Jan 15–Mar 14, notified by Apr 8; Mar 15–May 14 by Jun 8; May 15–Jul 14 by Aug 8; Jul 15–Sep 14 by Oct 8; Sep 15–Nov 14 by Dec 8; Nov 15–Jan 14 by Feb 8), and shows are scheduled about two years in advance, which gives artists room to develop the body of work they intend to exhibit. Fees are $7 for Delaplaine members and $37 for non-members — the figure the Delaplaine's own FAQ and the CaFÉ prospectus text both give, though CaFÉ's fee panel prints $35 for non-members, so budget for the higher one.
