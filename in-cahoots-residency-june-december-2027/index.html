In Cahoots Residency — June–December 2027

One- to three-week residencies at In Cahoots in Petaluma, California — pastoral Sonoma County, close to San Francisco and the Pacific coast — with a focus on artists' books, letterpress, printmaking, writing and collaboration. The studios specifically support printmakers, letterpress printers, book artists and writers and those applications are given priority, but the residency states it does occasionally host artists working in other mediums such as painting, photography and mixed media — a natural fit for photobook makers and photographers working in print. Three to five residents attend at a time, each with a private guest house or summer sleeping cottage, 24-hour access to the shared studios, weekly grocery runs and group field trips; residents cook for themselves. This application round, open August 27 to October 1, 2026, covers residencies taking place June through December 2027. The residency is self-paying — $995 for one week up to $2,500 for three in an individual cottage, with student rates, cheaper May–October sleeping cottages and a 25% discount for collaborators sharing housing — but ten full or partial Fellowships are awarded for this period (twenty per year), designed for artists for whom the experience would otherwise be out of reach financially, with particular welcome for emerging artists with under ten years in the field, recent students, people of colour, career returners and adjunct teachers; fellowship applicants not selected are automatically considered for a paid place. Applicants must be 18 or older with prior experience in art or writing, and visual artists submit 8–10 images, a CV, application questions and three references through CaFE with a $25 fee. Applications are reviewed by a jury team and decisions go out about eight weeks after the deadline.
