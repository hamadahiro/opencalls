Assignment Literary Magazine — 2026 Winter Issue: Visual Art

Free visual-art submissions for the Winter 2026 issue of Assignment, the literary magazine run by Southern New Hampshire University's Master of Fine Arts in Fiction or Nonfiction program. Art is considered for the front and back covers of the issue as well as for internal use; artists may submit up to five pieces per submission, formatted one piece per page, as high-resolution images. Review is blind — the guidelines ask that no name or identifying information appear on the submission. Simultaneous submissions are accepted provided the editors are told if work is placed elsewhere, and everything submitted must be original and previously unpublished. The magazine publishes using First North American Serial Rights, after which all rights revert to the author, and states that none of its content is available for use by AI. Submitted work must be the artist's own with no AI used in its creation — a line that appears in the guidelines and again on every one of the four entry-form categories. The submission window for the issue closes November 15, 2026, across all four categories (visual art, poetry, fiction and non-fiction), and the entry route is Submittable with no fee on any category. Two things to weigh: the call asks for "visual art" and nowhere enumerates the media it accepts, so photography is not named either way — what settles it is the magazine's own record. Its online-only section ran a photographic essay, "Through Mami’s Eyes", a visual diary of the artist’s mother through the Covid-19 pandemic by Gabrielle Ghzala LaGuerre, described there as "a photo-based artist whose work explores people in their natural environments", and its Featured Artwork posts carried Guilherme Bergamini, a "Reporter photographic and visual artist" who "has developed projects with photography" — both in 2023, which is where that section stops. Neither the guidelines nor the entry form mentions payment; the stated return is publication.
