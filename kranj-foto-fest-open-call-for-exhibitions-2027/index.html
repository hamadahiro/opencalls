Kranj Foto Fest Open Call for Exhibitions 2027

Prizes Solo exhibition €1,000 €400 travel

The exhibition open call for the seventh edition of Kranj Foto Fest, a Slovenian festival of contemporary photography founded in 2021 and run by the non-profit Zavod Photo Dialogue. Up to thirteen projects are selected. Twelve of them enter group exhibitions with the festival covering the full production cost of the work — printing and installation — plus three nights' accommodation during opening week, 26–29 August 2027. From those, one grand prize winner receives a solo exhibition with production costs covered, a €1,000 cash prize and up to €400 in travel support to attend the festival. There is no thematic or technical restriction, but the photographs must form a coherent story, series or project. Applicants may be photographers, visual artists or visual storytellers working individually, in duos or as collectives, from anywhere in the world, and the terms set a floor of 18 years. Each project is 10 to 20 JPG images with a minimum of 1,500 pixels on the longest edge, submitted at a resolution suitable for printing, with a project description of up to 2,500 characters and a bio of up to 1,500; text may be in Slovene or English, and English is recommended. Up to two projects may be entered, at €12 for one and €16 for both. Artists who have exhibited at the festival before may reapply only with a different project; festival staff, sponsors, jury members and their relatives may not apply. The jury is Maryam Wahid of Tasweer Photo Biennale, Amin Yousefi of Photoworks and Photography+, Edda Fahrenhorst of horizonte zingst and Yeast Photo Festival, Peter Rauch of the University of Ljubljana's Academy of Fine Arts and Design, and festival founding director Fernanda Prado Verčič. Judging takes place in January 2027 and results are emailed in February. The 2027 festival runs 26 August to 26 September in Kranj. The cutoff has a time, and it covers the account as well as the upload: "The deadline for creating an account, completing registration and submitting entries is 10 January 2027 at 23:59 (Central European Time)."
