Deep Shadows: The 2026 Lenscratch Halloween Exhibition

Lenscratch's Halloween show — the site has been running one on and off since 2011 — a free online juried exhibition for images that step out of the everyday and into the uncanny. The site's framing is that Halloween and photography go together in their shared ability to suspend reality and put the strange centre stage, and it asks for one picture that interprets the magic and suspense of the season. Juror Douglas Breault will select fifty images for the exhibition. Entry is free and strictly one image per photographer, submitted through a Google Form with a caption formatted exactly as Lenscratch specifies — copyright symbol, your name, title, location, website, Instagram handle. Submissions close October 18, 2026.
