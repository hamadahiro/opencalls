3rd Annual Printmaking Micro-Residency

Prize $400 stipend

A month-long printmaking micro-residency at The Cedars Union, a non-profit artist incubator in Dallas, free to apply and open to artists of any medium — the call says so explicitly, and adds that no prior printmaking experience is required. One artist is selected to work with Taylor Gibson, the organisation's manager of programming, to brainstorm, produce and curate a closed edition of twenty hand-pulled prints in the process of their choosing: relief, intaglio, screenprint or collagraph. The imagery and every technical decision — number of layers, colours, composition, paper, image size, embellishments — are the artist's, all materials are provided, and ownership of the image stays with the artist, with The Cedars Union retaining the right to use it for marketing and outreach. The edition becomes a thank-you gift for the organisation's major donors. The artist receives a $400 stipend, five prints from the finished edition and a print placed in The Cedars Union's permanent collection. Apply with a 200-word proposal for what you intend to produce and how your work would translate into printmaking, optionally with sketches, plus three to five examples of relevant work. Two conditions to weigh before applying: you must be a current Cedars Union member (Community or Cohort) both when you apply and during the residency, and Community Membership is $80 a month on a six-month commitment or $100 month to month, so the residency is realistically for artists already in or moving to Dallas; and you agree to be present for the entire project. Community Membership applications are accepted year round. Previous recipients were Victoria Brill and Bao Nghi Ngo.
