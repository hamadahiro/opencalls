Krampusnacht IX

Memento Mori's ninth annual Krampusnacht, a group exhibition celebrating the folklore, mystery and mischievous spirit of Krampus — from traditional Alpine legend to contemporary reinterpretation, and the eerie, playful, macabre and beautifully bizarre sides of the figure. The gallery is a lowbrow/outsider contemporary space and tattoo studio in Lakewood's 40West Arts District, on the First Friday art-walk circuit. Open nationally to 2D and 3D work; photographic work is welcome but must be printed and framed or mounted and ready to hang, and the gallery will not accept photographs depicting another artist's work, street art included, without permission. Up to two pieces at $25 each. All work must be for sale with 40% of the sale going to the gallery, and out-of-state artists cover shipping both ways. Drop-off 28 November, show 4–23 December 2026, First Friday reception 4 December.
