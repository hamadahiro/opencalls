Queensland Regional Art Awards 2026

Prizes A$25,000 Solo exhibition Residency

Flying Arts Alliance's annual awards for artists living in regional and remote Queensland, outside the Brisbane City Council boundary. Flying Arts bills it as Australia's largest regional art awards prize pool and puts the total at A$326,800, but that figure counts the stated value of in-kind marketing, residencies and exhibitions alongside roughly A$62,500 in actual cash. There is no theme in 2026. The overall winner receives A$25,000 in non-acquisitive cash, a two-week solo show at Jan Manton Gallery in March 2027, a residency in Bundaberg and a print editorial profile. The Mervyn Moriarty Landscape Award adds A$10,000 plus a residency in Cairns and a solo show at FireWorks Gallery; the First Nations Artist Award is A$10,000; the Remote Artist, Emerging Artist and Young Artist awards are A$5,000 each, and the two People's Choice awards are A$1,500 and A$1,000. Entries close at 3pm on 18 September and finalists are announced on 15 October, with the winners named in person at the Gala Awards Night in Brisbane on 26 November. Artists must be 15 or over; work must be original, completed in 2026, and must not have previously won a major state, national or international prize. Size limits are 120 × 120cm and under 10kg for framed 2D work, and digital work must run under five minutes. Finalists must make the work available through 2027 for the statewide touring exhibition and deliver it to the Brisbane office at their own cost. AI may be used only as a tool: Flying Arts states that AI must not be "the substantive final result, presented as the work, regardless of medium", entrants must declare this and describe every AI tool used, and works in breach are not eligible for awards. A 20% commission applies to sales.
