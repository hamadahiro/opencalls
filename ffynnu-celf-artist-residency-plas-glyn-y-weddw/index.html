FFYNNU Celf Artist Residency

Open to artists living and working in Wales only. Four paid residencies at Plas Glyn-y-Weddw, an arts centre and accredited museum in Llanbedrog on the Llyn Peninsula in north Wales, run in partnership with the community climate movement GwyrddNi and funded by Arts Council of Wales, GwyrddNi, Adra and the John Andrews Trust. Two of the four places are reserved for artists based in north Wales (Ynys Mon, Gwynedd, Conwy, Denbighshire, Flintshire and Wrexham). It is aimed at artists whose practice already connects with environmental, ecological or climate subjects, and it is open across visual art forms. Each residency runs 14 flexible days between October 2026 and early March 2027 and pays a £3,500 artist fee (£250 a day) plus £500 for materials and kit, £35 a day subsistence, up to £500 of travel, free on-site accommodation and a workspace, along with two days of mentoring with an industry specialist and two further paid networking days with the other residents. The emphasis is on research and experimentation rather than finished work - there is no expectation to produce a resolved piece - and each residency ends in a sharing of the artist's own choosing and scale. Applicants must be over 18, able to demonstrate two years of committed practice, and not currently studying in higher education. Answers to the application questions may be given in writing, audio or video; an Access Fund, staff-assisted application sessions and a Disability Arts Cymru officer on the selection panel are all built in, though the current on-site accommodation is not wheelchair accessible. Free to apply, and the deadline is 11pm on Friday 4 September 2026, with shortlisting and interviews in mid-to-late September.
