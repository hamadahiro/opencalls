Exhibition Proposals

Prize Gallery exhibition

Standing open call for exhibition proposals at Aurora PhotoCenter, the artist-run nonprofit photography centre in Indianapolis's Factory Arts District. Aurora's programme mixes emerging work by local artists with shows by internationally exhibiting, award-winning photographers, and proposals are welcome from individual artists, collaborations and curators. Submissions are by email and free of charge: an exhibition description covering the artists and the major themes of the show in no more than 500 words, a biography of the artist or artists covering previous exhibitions and relevant experience in no more than 500 words, a vision-and-execution statement giving the approximate number and size of works, formatting, framing, installation requirements and any special programming to accompany the exhibition, and no more than 10 JPEGs of work to be included. Proposals are evaluated on a rolling basis as they arrive rather than against a fixed deadline. Aurora asks that hard-copy materials not be sent unless requested, and cannot provide feedback on individual proposals because of the volume received. Applicants needing accommodations to submit can contact director Mary Goodwin directly. Aurora runs three gallery spaces alongside a darkroom, digital lab and residency programme.
