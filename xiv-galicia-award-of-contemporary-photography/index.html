XIV «Galicia» Award of Contemporary Photography

Prizes Touring exhibition Book

The fourteenth edition of the Premio «Galicia» de Fotografía Contemporánea, founded in 2013 within the International Outono Fotográfico Festival and run by the Asociación OF. Outono Fotográfico with the Xunta de Galicia, the Deputación de Lugo and the University of Vigo's Ourense campus. It seeks complete photographic projects on a free theme, judged above all on how they sit within the discourse of contemporary photography, and is open to any author working with photography or the photographic regardless of nationality. Two awards are made from the same pool of entries. The Jury Prize produces an exhibition of the winning project with a curator appointed by the association, opening in Lugo and touring Galicia and Portugal for two years before the exhibition becomes the artist's property, plus a book in the "Galicia Contemporary Photography Prize" series from Difusora de Letras, Artes e Ideas, distributed in bookshops with a percentage of the print run to the author. The Organisation Prize produces a comparable exhibition at the Alterarte Gallery on the Ourense campus with an accompanying booklet. Projects must have been made between October 2016 and October 2026 and must not have been fully funded by another competition or published in full. Submit up to 15 images (minimum 3,000 px on the long side) with a synopsis of up to 2,500 characters and a 1,500-character biography. Entry costs €22 per project.
