2027 CPAC Open International Call for Photography — MOP Denver

Selected photographs will be projected onto the Daniels & Fisher Tower on 16th Street Mall in downtown Denver, as part of Night Lights Denver, every evening through March 2027 — the full run of the biennial Month of Photography Denver festival. Horizontal images can instead be programmed onto the Denver Theatre District's new LED screen in Skyline Park. The theme is open-ended and photographers of every skill level are invited: film, scanner, screen-capture, digital camera and any form of mobile photography are all eligible, with no video. Work for the clocktower should be vertical. Submit up to three images; CPAC Executive Director and Curator Samantha Johnston makes the final selection.

DeadlineJanuary 8, 2027
PrizeMonth-long projection exhibition
Entry fee$10
LocationDenver, CO
RequirementsUp to 3 images
Submit viaWebsite
ResultsJanuary 26, 2027

Jury: Samantha Johnston, Executive Director & Curator, CPAC

Organized by Colorado Photographic Arts Center
