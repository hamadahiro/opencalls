14th Annual Abstract Sanctuary

Verum Ultimum's long-running abstraction show returns for a 14th edition, open internationally to painters, printmakers, photographers, digital artists and sculptors working non-objectively. The exhibition is virtual: an online gallery page with scale views, blog posts and four weeks of promotion, a 30% commission on sales and no cash award. Two images are required, up to five allowed.
