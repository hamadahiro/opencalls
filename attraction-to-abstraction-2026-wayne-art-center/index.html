Attraction to Abstraction 2026

Wayne Art Center's juried exhibition of abstract work, hung in the Vidinghoff and Radnor Township Galleries outside Philadelphia alongside CraftForms, the centre's 31st International Exhibition of Contemporary Fine Craft. Photography is one of the five media named as eligible — "Painting, Works on Paper, Photography, Mixed Media and Fiber Works" — so this is one of the few regional abstraction shows where lens-based work is explicitly invited. Up to three abstract works may be entered on one application at $25 per piece; each must have been completed since January 2024, must not have been shown at Wayne Art Center before, must be at least 24 inches in each direction including any frame, and must be for sale, with the centre retaining a 40% commission. All accepted work is hand-delivered to and collected from Wayne, Pennsylvania on set dates — nothing is shipped in either direction, with no exceptions. The juror of entry is Vlad Milan Duchev, born in Pokrov, Ukraine in 1965, who trained first in painting at an art school in Bila Tserkva and then as a classical double bassist at the Music College of Zhytomyr, the Music Academy of Kharkiv and the National Conservatory of Ukraine in Kyiv, painting alongside his musical career throughout. Registration runs 22 September to 21 October 2026, notification 31 October, hand delivery 23 November, and the exhibition runs 6 December 2026 to 23 January 2027, opening the evening of 5 December at the CraftForms Preview Party. Note that the two surfaces disagree on scope: the CaFÉ prospectus opens the call to "WAC students, WAC members and regional artists 18 years of age and older" without defining the region, while wayneart.org's own page for the show describes it as work "by our adult students and WAC community members".
