Juried Artist Membership 2026

Prizes Juried artist membership Group exhibition

Once a year the Rockport Art Association & Museum - founded in 1921 on Cape Ann, with galleries at 12 Main Street - juries new Juried Artist Members, and Photography is one of its four standing categories alongside Painting, Graphics and Sculpture. Admission is by merit: "Juried Artist Members ... are admitted through an annual jury process in the fall that evaluates the merit of their work based on professionalism of technique and originality." Applicants submit five original works in one category, created solely by the applicant - no work made in classes or under supervision - framed or presented to a professional standard, and the actual physical works must be delivered to the museum for the jury to see (drop-off Tuesday 27 October, pick-up Thursday 29 October). Photographs may not be printed smaller than 11 by 14 inches and frames may not exceed 20 by 24 inches at the opening. The jury votes silently and a photography applicant needs at least four of five votes in favour; works that split 60/40 are reviewed a second time, when a juror may speak for or against. No feedback is given. The RAA&M states there is no longer a New England residency requirement to apply. Accepted artists have about two works chosen for the New Juried Artist Members Show, 7-29 November 2026, with a reception on 7 November, and membership then brings "exhibition opportunities in solo, member group, non-juried & juried shows; opportunities to instruct and attend workshops; and participation in year-round classes, sketch groups, lectures and artist demonstrations". The application fee is $50 whatever the category - you may apply in more than one category on a single fee, but must submit five works per category - and it is deducted from the $150 annual dues if you are accepted. The association insures nothing: "The RAA&M does not offer insurance on works submitted by applicants."
