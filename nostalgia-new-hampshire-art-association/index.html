Nostalgia

Member show at the New Hampshire Art Association's Portsmouth gallery built around memory and the act of looking back — personal histories, shared experiences, places once known, cherished objects, cultural touchstones, and moments that continue to resonate as they pass on. NHAA welcomes artwork in any medium and names photography outright in its list alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, installation, fibre arts and digital work, provided it engages with the theme. This is not a juried show: all member entries are accepted, though the association notes it may have to limit accepted works by size if response outstrips available wall space. Entry is $12 for the first piece and $10 for a second, and current NHAA artist membership is required; membership is itself juried, with rolling digital applications reviewed by a panel of five jurors, results within 45 days, a $25 application fee and annual dues of $50–$125 on acceptance. The exhibition runs 2–27 September 2026 at 136 State Street with an opening reception on 4 September, 5–8pm; work is dropped off 30–31 August and collected 27–28 September. Sales split 60/40 in the artist's favour.
