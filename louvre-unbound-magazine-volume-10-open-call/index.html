Louvre Unbound Magazine — Volume 10 Open Call

Prize Publication

International contemporary art magazine seeking visual artists worldwide for Volume 10 — photography, painting, sculpture, digital art, mixed media, video, installation, performance and fashion. Submissions are free: up to four artworks through a Google Form, in English, French or Portuguese, with CV, portfolio and press features as genuinely optional extras — but an artist statement, a short biography, country, hometown and an Instagram handle are all required fields, all four artwork slots are marked required on the form despite the “up to four” wording, and a PayPal email (or a Stripe link instead) must be supplied at submission for the fee charged only if selected. Participation fees apply only after acceptance, and Volumes 9 and 10 carry discounted tiers: $39 for a full page (portrait plus one or two works, $49 full price), $69 for a double page of two to four works ($89 full price), $89 for a six-page solo portfolio spotlight of six to eight works ($139 full price) and $239 for an eight-page interview with five or six works ($275 full price). Louvre Unbound describes itself as a non-profit platform taking no profit from artist fees or print sales, and promotes featured artists weekly for two months across its social channels. Artists previously published in the magazine may submit again with new, unpublished work. The front cover is chosen by the editorial team and the back cover by public vote from the previous volume’s artworks.
