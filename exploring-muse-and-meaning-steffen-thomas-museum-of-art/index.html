Exploring Muse & Meaning: A Juried Opportunity for Undergraduate Studio Arts Majors

Prizes $500 / $300 / $100 Exhibition

The Steffen Thomas Museum of Art is a single-artist museum in rural Morgan County, Georgia, built around the work of German-born American painter and sculptor Steffen Thomas, and this is the undergraduate strand of its College Connections programme - the sibling of the MFA-level Truth in Art show it ran earlier in 2026. The point is as much the apprenticeship as the wall space: selected students exhibit at a fine art museum while working alongside museum mentors on curating, loan forms and condition reports, art installation, exhibition promotion and public speaking. Entry is free, and the museum's own call banner limits it to undergraduate studio arts majors in Georgia. Applicants email a bio of up to 250 words, an artist statement of up to 150 words and high-resolution images of three to five works made within the past year, 2D or 3D, each labelled with title, date, dimensions and medium; no piece may exceed 72 x 60 inches or 50 pounds, and artists are responsible for transporting work to and from the museum. The top three Best in Show selections receive $500, $300 and $100. Submissions close 1 November 2026 and the exhibition opens 9 January 2027 at the museum on Bethany Road in Buckhead, about ten minutes from historic Madison.
