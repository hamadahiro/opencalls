No Limits 2026 — Golden Duck Gallery

Prize €1,000 prize fund

Golden Duck Gallery's annual birthday show, an open-topic international open call in central Budapest, a few hundred metres from the Great Market Hall. Physical and digital painting, photography, mixed media, sculpture and installation are all welcome; the constraints are price caps (€1,000 for work up to 60×60 cm, €4,000 up to 100×100 cm, none for 3D) and a ready-to-hang requirement. Submitting up to five works is free, and selected artists then decide whether to exhibit and pay an exhibition fee of €70 for one work, €100 for two or €120 for three, covering all exhibition services for the four-week run; the jury also picks three Curator's Pick works that exhibit free, and artists aged 14–25 are juried separately and exhibit free of charge. A €1,000 prize fund is split across a €200 Excellence Prize and €150 each for the Concept and Talent Awards. Work can also be shown digitally on screens in New York, Vienna and Budapest. The exhibition runs October 6 – November 14, 2026, opening October 9 at 6pm.
