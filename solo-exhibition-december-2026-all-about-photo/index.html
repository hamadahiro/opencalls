Solo Exhibition December 2026

Prize Exhibition

All About Photo runs a Solo Exhibition every month, and December 2026 is the next open slot. The selected photographer gets the whole month — 1 to 31 December 2026 — across the site's home page, competition gallery and photography exhibition directory, plus an exclusive interview, a dedicated portfolio page holding 20 images in the Photographers Directory, and a permanent place in the showroom archive after the month ends. The organiser also commits to specific press: a special-edition newsletter announcing the exhibition, promotion across its Instagram, Facebook and Twitter accounts, and guaranteed publication on Bored Panda, Visura and Photophiles Magazine, with past winners having been picked up by The Times UK, National Geographic España, The Eye of Photography and Digital Camera World. Sales during the exhibition are commission-free — the photographer keeps the full price. The juror for this cycle is Olga Karlovac, a self-taught photographer based in Zagreb whose trilogy “before winter”, “the disarray” and “escape” sold out its first edition and whose work has run in Black+White Photography and the Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung. The theme is open and every genre is invited — photojournalism, street, fine art, landscape, portraiture — from professionals and enthusiasts alike, anywhere in the world. What matters is cohesion: the entry is 6 to 14 images for a flat $45, and the organiser is explicit that only cohesive projects or portfolios will be published as a Solo Exhibition, so a set of unrelated strong frames is the wrong submission here. Two practical notes. This is an online-only exhibition — there is no physical venue and no cash prize; what is awarded is a month of placement, an interview and the press package above, so judge the $45 against visibility rather than against an award. And entry runs through an account on All About Photo's own site: the “Enter Competition” button redirects to the site's sign-in and back to this page, so there is no separate submission portal to bookmark. The deadline is Tuesday 17 November 2026, two weeks before the exhibition opens.
