Here & Now

Prize Museum exhibition

Bristol Art Museum, a nonprofit museum on Wardwell Street in Bristol, Rhode Island, closes its year-long recognition of America's 250th anniversary by turning from national history to the place it calls home. Here & Now is a regional juried exhibition of work inspired by Rhode Island, the greater Narragansett Bay area or the Southern New England region: the prospectus lists photography first among the media it brings together, alongside painting, printmaking, fiber, mixed media and other two- and three-dimensional work, and the subjects it suggests run from coastal landscapes, Bristol Harbor and working waterfronts to architecture, neighbourhoods, natural environments, community life and personal interpretations of place, whether representational or conceptual. The juror is Dan Wallace, a RISD-trained landscape architect with Sudbury Design Group since 1985. Selected works hang in the Livingston Gallery from December 6, 2026 to January 10, 2027, with a reception on December 6 from 2 to 4pm; the museum's other galleries show harbour photographs by featured Bristol artist Brendon Adams and new work from its 2026 Harbor Residency at the same time. Entry is open to artists in Rhode Island and the surrounding region who can hand-deliver accepted work on November 23 or 24 (no shipped work is accepted); work must have been completed in 2026, be no more than 40 inches in any direction (3D work under 50 lb), and up to three pieces may be entered - $15 for one, $25 for two, $35 for three - as JPEGs under 4 MB. The show is part of the museum's annual fundraising, and exhibiting artists agree to donate 50% of any sale to the museum. Entries close Sunday November 8, 2026 through OnlineJuriedShows.
