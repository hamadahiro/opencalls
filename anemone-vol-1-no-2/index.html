Anemone Vol. 1, No. 2

Prizes Publication Payment

Anemone is a print magazine published out of Bozeman, Montana by pulphead, a community writing and coffee space, and is built around what it calls the analog perspective: a framing rooted in continuity, tangibility, lived experience, slowness, emphasis on process and “grace for imperfection”, set against one of replicability, speed, binary outcomes and optimization. Its first issue came out in July 2026 and submissions are now open for Vol. 1, No. 2. Photography and visual art sit alongside short fiction, creative nonfiction, reportage and poetry as full submission categories, and the theme is stated broadly as the world as told by Montanans; all styles and all subjects are welcome, with the subject asked to evoke the themes, pitfalls, joys and moments of the day as best one reasonably can. Photographs must be unpublished, though exceptions are made for work self-published to an online portfolio or personal socials, and files are uploaded through a form on the magazine's own site as JPEG, JPG or PNG at 150 to 300 dpi and under 20 MB, accompanied by a single written document captioning each image with date, location, title, medium and inspiration. There is no submission fee. The magazine is emphatic about generative tools: “use AI to originate work and you will be choked out with a Full Nelson, then disqualified.” Contributors are paid — the guidelines state that no work will be published without net-ninety payment arranged from the date of publishing, at negotiated per-word or per-placement rates — and unselected work is held in a slushpile for possible use in a later edition.
