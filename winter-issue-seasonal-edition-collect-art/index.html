Winter Issue — Seasonal Edition

Collect Art, the Tbilisi-based independent art magazine, is reading for its Winter seasonal issue, and this one is open-ended by design: “there is no theme for this edition.” Photography is named in the eligible-media list alongside painting, drawing, collage, installation, illustration, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, assemblage, fibre art, paper art, printmaking, 3D digital art, glass art and metalwork, and the call is open to artists worldwide aged 18 and over — so it is the organiser's one current route for work that does not answer to a theme. What selection buys is the magazine: “each selected artist will receive a 2-6 page spread in both print and digital magazines, featuring a biography, statement, interview, website, and 2-10 selected artworks”, plus a complimentary digital issue promoted on the magazine's channels and a link to order the printed version; the curatorial team also picks “a few exceptional artists” for its book edition, “101 Contemporary Artists and More…”. No online exhibition is promised in this block. THE COST, published in full: there is NO application fee, and a participation fee of €35 is payable only if your work is accepted — a price that sits between the organiser's €25 and €55 calls. Submissions go by email and want up to 12 images with each work's title, medium, size and year, a short biography, an artist statement of at least 100 words, and website and social links. No notification date is published.
