BBA 360 — Online Exhibition 2026

Prizes Cash prize Exhibition

Open call from Berlin's BBA Gallery for its 360° digital exhibition format. Open to artists and photographers 18+ worldwide working in any medium except video and sculpture, with no theme restrictions. Around 45 artworks are featured in the immersive 360° online gallery; 15 shortlisted artists are additionally presented in an online group show with Artsy profiles and promotion through BBA channels. The first-prize winner receives a cash award, an Artsy profile, and a physical exhibition at BBA Gallery in Berlin in 2026/27. €29 fee covers up to 5 works; no shipping required for the online round.
