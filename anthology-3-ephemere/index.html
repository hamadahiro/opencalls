ANTHOLOGY 3

Prizes Exhibition Publication

ANTHOLOGY is Ephemere's cyclical curated group exhibition and publication series, and its third volume forms part of the Ephemere Photo Fest 2026 in Tokyo. Exactly nine artists are selected each round — "nine distinct petals of a single flower" — a number chosen so individual narratives complement rather than compete with one another across the gallery walls and the pages of an accompanying photobook. Applicants submit a portfolio of 15 to 20 images; the curatorial team, led by Ephemere founder Anne Murayama and assisted by Alfred Ong, then selects the nine final images that will represent each artist in both the show and the book. Ephemere handles all printing, layout design, framing and installation. ANTHOLOGY 3 runs 17–27 October 2026 on the 3rd floor of Three Space Tokyo. Applying is free, but selected artists pay a strictly non-refundable all-inclusive participation fee — ¥27,500 for Japan-based artists, $275 for visiting artists — covering the 11-day gallery placement, curatorial administration, professional printing and installation, and one complimentary copy of the photobook. Attendance is mandatory: proxy representation is not allowed and selected artists must be physically present in Tokyo on their designated Artist Talk day to give a 10–15 minute presentation alongside their work. Travel and accommodation are the artist's own cost. Two group video meetings in September cover logistics, and artists who join the take-down on 28 October may take their prints home free of charge.
