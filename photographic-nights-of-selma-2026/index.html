Photographic Nights of Selma 2026

Prize Exhibition

The seventh edition of a festival founded by French photographer Stéphane Kossmann — who also founded Les Nuits Photographiques de Pierrevert — in partnership with Foot Soldier Park and Arts Revive in Selma, Alabama. Around thirty selected artist projects are shown each evening on a giant outdoor screen, with daytime given over to exhibitions across the Selma area, encounters between photographers and the public, portfolio readings and conferences. There is no set theme: the artistic committee asks only for a unique perspective and a clearly asserted point of view in a series of fifteen to thirty photographs, and is looking for a broad panorama of recent work of all kinds — reflections on society, landscape, fashion, portraiture, still life, explorations of intimacy, or proposals diverging from the current zeitgeist. Selected series are presented as photo-sound montages of no more than four minutes; the festival's video and sound editors can build the montage, or photographers may submit their own edit, and an original soundtrack may be supplied. Each selected photographer commits to attending the evening of their screening and the following day; photographers stay in local homes for the duration, limited to fifteen places, with lunches covered by the festival and travel at the photographer's own expense. Prizes are awarded at the end by a jury of figures from photography and the arts and by the public, and the Jury Prize carries an invitation with travel expenses covered to the Nuits Photographiques de Pierrevert in the south of France in the last week of July 2027. Applicants must declare any use of image-generation tools. The festival runs 6–7 November 2026 and access to exhibitions, screenings and conferences is free.
