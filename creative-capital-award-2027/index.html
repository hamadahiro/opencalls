Creative Capital Award 2027

Prize $15,000–$50,000

Creative Capital is one of the major US grant organizations supporting individual artists. The 2027 award cycle is the program's 27th year. Since founding, the organization has committed over $60 million to 750+ projects across disciplines.

The award structure has two tracks. The main Creative Capital Award provides $15,000–$50,000 in project funding plus advisory services and career development support — funded over the course of the project. The newer State of the Art Prize awards $10,000 unrestricted grants to individual artists.

The application is three rounds: Round I (project proposal), Round II (detailed materials for shortlisted applicants), Round III (panel review). The brief is open across disciplines: photography and visual arts are eligible alongside other artistic disciplines.

Eligibility is restricted: US citizens, permanent residents, Tribal ID holders, or O-1 visa holders aged 25+ with at least five years of professional artistic practice. Applicants must not be enrolled in a degree-granting program.

Application is free, made through Creative Capital's website. The Round I 2027-cycle deadline was April 2, 2026 at 3 PM ET; final results announced early 2027.
