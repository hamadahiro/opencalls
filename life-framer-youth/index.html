Life Framer — Youth

Life Framer runs as a twelve-part monthly photography award with a different judge each round. Youth is the November 2026 theme, and the brief - opening on F. Scott Fitzgerald's "Youth is a dream, a form of chemical madness" - asks for a scene or a detail showing the habits, experiences or behaviour of the young, from birth through toddler, child, teen and pre-adult. Dreams, fears, freedom, experimentation and play are all named; the organisers say the theme is as broad as the entrant wants to make it.

The judge is Matthieu Paley, a French documentary photographer who works immersively and over the long term across Central and South Asia - Mongolia, Afghanistan, China, India, Sri Lanka - living alongside the communities he photographs. His work has been published in National Geographic and recognised by World Press Photo and Pictures of the Year International, and youth sits at the centre of his practice as a formative stage rather than a demographic.

Entry is priced by the number of images: $20 for one, $30 for three, $40 for six, or a $100 twelve-month membership that allows up to ten images in every monthly theme, all in USD. The monthly first prize is $2,000 out of a $24,000 annual fund shared between the twelve monthly first-prize photographers, and the first- and second-prize images each month are exhibited in galleries around the world. Entry is open worldwide to amateurs and professionals aged 18 and over, photographers keep full copyright, analog and digital devices including phones are accepted along with editing, mixed media, collage and compositing, and AI-generated imagery is not accepted.
