Cedarhurst High School Art Competition 2027

Prizes $2,000 scholarship Exhibition

Two juried competitions for high school students, run together by Cedarhurst Center for the Arts, an AAM-accredited museum and sculpture park in Mount Vernon, Illinois. Selected work hangs in the New Semantics Gallery of the Mitchell Museum, January 24 to March 21, 2027. The Cedarhurst High School Art Competition is open to students in grades 9 to 12; the Cedarhurst Administrative Counselors Art Scholarship is a separate competition for graduating seniors, for a one-year college scholarship of $2,000 contingent on the student's formal acceptance to a university. Seniors may enter both. Eligibility is also geographic — 28 named counties: Alexander, Clay, Clinton, Edwards, Franklin, Gallatin, Hamilton, Hardin, Jackson, Jefferson, Johnson, Lawrence, Marion, Massac, Monroe, Perry, Pope, Pulaski, Randolph, Richland, Saline, St Clair, Union, Wabash, Washington, Wayne, White and Williamson. The call page words this as students in those counties, the registration form as high schools in them. The accepted-media list names “painting (oil, acrylic, watercolor), drawing, sculpture, printmaking, photograph (digital or film), pastels, textiles, ceramics, glass, metal, and wood”, with no installations; work must be original, and the organisers reserve the right to disqualify AI-generated images or work that violates copyright. Entry is normally school-mediated: a teacher registers the school, is emailed a shared Google folder and sheet for uploading JPEGs, may enter up to 25 artworks with a limit of 3 per student, and is invoiced $3 per artwork once the window closes. Seniors submit one portfolio of six works for $10. Home-school students, and anyone who wants to enter without a registered teacher, are told on the call page to contact the competition's organiser directly. Awards total more than $3,000 and are chosen after the work is delivered: the $2,000 scholarship, $200 Best of Show, $100 / $75 / $50 for first, second and third in both Best of Two-Dimensional and Best of Three-Dimensional Art, and twenty honorable mentions of $25 each. Accepted work is emailed to registered teachers by the end of December 18, 2026 and must reach the Mitchell Museum by Friday January 15, 2027; it cannot be removed before the exhibit closes, and is collected March 23 to April 2, 2027. The organiser's two surfaces disagree on the deadline and the earlier is stored: the call page requires uploads by 5pm December 2, 2026, the registration form says artwork must be registered by December 3, 2026.
