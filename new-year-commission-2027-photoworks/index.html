New Year Commission 2027

Prizes £500 Publication

Photoworks, a registered charity that describes itself as the only organisation with a national remit for photography in England, is commissioning one photographer or lens-based artist to make a single new image marking the start of 2027. The organisation has no physical venue — the Brighton address on this listing is its postal address, and the commission runs entirely online. The brief is deliberately loose and playful: a non-conventional image that indicates the beginning of a new year, with room to experiment rather than illustrate. The commission pays a £500 fee and comes with an Instagram takeover of the Photoworks account in January 2027. The finished image travels further than most single-image commissions: Photoworks shares it across its social platforms, sends it to a mailing list that it describes as including curators, editors, artists and industry professionals, and prints it as a postcard that ships with every Photoworks shop order through 2027. Entry is free and no images are uploaded — the form carries no file field at all — but the application is more than a link: it asks for your name and email, a portfolio link (website or Instagram), and a written answer to “How would you approach creating an image for this commission?”. Everything after that, from location and age range to ethnicity, gender and caring responsibilities, is optional equal-opportunities monitoring, and applying adds you to the Photoworks mailing list. The call is explicitly international: the organisation says it looks forward to hearing from applicants wherever they are in the world, the form's location question offers “Outside the UK, please state country”, and the organiser's standing page for this commission says it goes to an artist at any stage in their career. Three things to know before applying. No selection panel is named and no notification date is published, so there is no stated timetable between the 5 December deadline and the January takeover. The deadline is printed as “Deadline 5 December.” with no time of day, and this organiser does publish one when it has one — its previous Photography+ open call closed at 9:00 am GMT — so treat 5 December as the last safe day rather than a full one. And note the route: the page's web address still carries last year's slug while the page itself is headed New Year Commission 2027, and the only 2026 inside the call block is the credit line on Ning Cheng's previous commission.
