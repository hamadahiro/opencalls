First Encounter

Kora Art Collective is a curator-led platform founded in Austin, Texas to show work by emerging and mid-career artists of African descent, working through exhibitions, private sales, cultural programmes and advisory services. First Encounter is a two-part exhibition presented during the Austin Studio Tour, one of the largest art events in Texas, which runs over the weekends of 14-15 and 21-22 November 2026. The collective is calling for work across painting, drawing, prints, photography, sculpture, mixed media and textile and fibre, and will consider selected craft-based work alongside the visual art submissions. Entry is free and open internationally. Artists submit three to ten images of available work, one entry per artist, giving title, year completed, medium, dimensions and price where known, along with an artist statement, CV or bio and a link to a website, portfolio or Instagram. Two-dimensional work cannot exceed 36 by 36 inches; three-dimensional work cannot exceed 24 inches in each direction or 15 pounds. Framing is not required but work must arrive presentable. The exhibition runs 14 November to 6 December 2026.
