CRUSH

Prize Exhibition

Peel's annual end-of-year group show, celebrating "another year of Peel Gallery", and as in previous years the brief is "peel" themed artwork — "that can be whatever that means to you". The show is titled CRUSH (the banner image on the call page is the only place the title is written; the page's heading is set as artwork rather than text). Peel describes itself as "an art gallery, community event space, digital photography lab, and retail space promoting contemporary art", dedicated to "supporting the work of emerging, mid-career, and under-recognized artists"; its owner and director, Lindsay Metivier, is a photographer, and the building runs a digital photo lab alongside the gallery. Read the terms before you plan around this one: there is NO JURY and no selection — "IF YOU SUBMIT, YOU ARE IN! Don't wait for an confirmation or acceptance email … as long as your work adheres to the guidelines" — and no prize, award or cash is offered. What is offered is a real wall in a real gallery for most of a month: the exhibition runs 9 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, with an opening reception on Friday 11 December, 6–9pm. One artwork per artist, 2-D or 3-D in any medium that is neither toxic nor perishable, nothing larger than 30 by 40 inches, and works on paper and prints must be framed. The practical gate is geography rather than eligibility: work is hand-delivered to 708 W Rosemary Street in Carrboro on 4, 5 or 6 December during open hours, ready to hang, so this suits photographers in or near the North Carolina Triangle. Artists submitting to the show are offered 20% off Peel's own photo lab services if they need prints made, by filing a lab order after the entry form. Entry itself is free and the call sets no age, residency or membership bar.
