Artist Grant — November 2026 Cycle

An unrestricted $1,200 USD grant awarded to one visual artist three times a year, running since 2017. There is no proposal to write: applicants email a link to their website or three to five images of recent work along with a CV, resume or short bio. Open to visual artists aged 18 and over anywhere in the world working in painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics, printmaking, photography, mixed media, installation and new or alternative visual art forms; film, video, music, theatre and dance are excluded, and past winners may not reapply. Grantees are featured on the Artist Grant website and social channels. Results are sent within six to eight weeks of the deadline.
