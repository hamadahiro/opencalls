EXPOSURE 2026 — 30th Annual Members' Juried Exhibition

Prizes $400 in awards prize Exhibition prize

The Photographic Resource Center (PRC) is a Boston-area photography organization that has run its annual members' juried exhibition for thirty years (the 2026 cycle marks the 30th edition). The show is hosted at University Hall, Lesley University in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

The 2026 cycle is juried by Audrey Lee Sands, the Richard L. Menschel Associate Curator of Photography at the Harvard Art Museums. Twelve photographers are selected from the membership pool, with each chosen photographer exhibiting three works.

Cash awards: a Juror's Choice Award of $200 and two PRC Choice Awards of $100 each. Beyond cash, the exhibition runs July 31 through September 6, 2026 at the Lesley University venue, with selected work catalogued by PRC.

Eligibility is restricted to PRC members through September. The application fee is $35. Submissions go through a Google Form. The 2026 deadline is June 15.
