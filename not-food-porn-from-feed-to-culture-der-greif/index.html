(Not) Food Porn — From Feed to Culture

Open call from Der Greif, the Munich non-profit for contemporary photography, timed to the International Day of Awareness of Food Loss and Waste on 29 September. The brief asks lens-based artists to work critically at the intersection of food imagery, consumption and culture: highly stylised "food porn" has reshaped how we perceive, desire and celebrate what we eat, and opposing feeds lean into raw, unedited aesthetics, while behind both sits massive global food waste and a rapidly shifting relationship to sustenance. Submissions are one project of up to 10 images, cohesive and not previously featured by Der Greif. Selected work is presented in an online exhibition in Der Greif's Pulse section, and selected artists are invited into a small-group online roundtable critique facilitated by Der Greif staff as part of its Face-to-Face programme, with visibility across Der Greif's international community and a possible invitation to submit a portfolio to Der Greif Studio for sales and commissions. Selection is judged on artistic quality, conceptual clarity, openness to dialogue and process, and engagement with themes of identity and community. Der Greif describes this as a free submission with an optional €5 or €10 donation, but the Picter entry page carries a flat €10 fee; artists who cannot contribute financially can email the organiser for a 50% or 100% discount code. Open to photographers and videographers internationally, particularly emerging and mid-career artists, with no age or regional restriction.
