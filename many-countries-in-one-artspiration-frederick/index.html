Many Countries in One

A celebration of immigrant artists who enrich the cultural fabric of the United States, hosted by Artspiration Frederick, a nonprofit art organisation and community artist space in Frederick, Maryland. Artists born outside the US and now living in the continental US are invited to submit original work in any visual medium — photography, painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, fibre art, ceramics, mixed media and digital art among them — reflecting their own voice and experience. Cuban-American artist, curator and critic F. Lennox Campello serves as juror and curator. Up to three works may be entered; 2D work must be framed and wired. The exhibition runs January 1–31, 2027 in the Artspiration Frederick Gallery.
