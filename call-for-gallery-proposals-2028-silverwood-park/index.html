Call for Gallery Proposals (2028)

Silverwood Park, a Three Rivers Park District facility on 120 acres north of Minneapolis, is programming the 2028 season of its 1,300-square-foot gallery and Annex Gallery. Proposals may be existing work, new projects or site-specific installations; the park shows nature-inspired art, photography included. Free to enter, open to Midwest artists 18 and over; selected artists get a small stipend and cover transport and installation themselves.

DeadlineJanuary 31, 2027
PrizesExhibition, Stipend
Entry feeFree
EligibilityMidwest US only, 18+
LocationSt. Anthony, MN
Requirements10 images + proposal
