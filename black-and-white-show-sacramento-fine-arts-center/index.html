The Black & White Show

Prizes Cash awards Solo exhibition

All-media juried show at the Sacramento Fine Arts Center in Carmichael, California — submissions must be black, white, and greyscale only, including framing. Up to three pieces per artist ($25 each or three for $60; members $20/$50, youth $15/$40); work must be original and finished in the last three years. Digital art and photography explicitly accepted. Judged by painter and former professional photographer Karen Burns. Exhibition July 14 – August 8, 2026.
