48 Stunden Neukölln 2027 — Soft Rituals

Berlin's Neukölln district becomes a 48-hour open gallery every June, and anyone may apply with a project on the 2027 theme, Soft Rituals. No venue or Berlin address is needed to apply: a jury decides in February, and selected artists then find and link a Neukölln space. Funding for 2027 is not yet approved, so the edition may be scaled down.
