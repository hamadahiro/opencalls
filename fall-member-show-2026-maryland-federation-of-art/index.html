Fall Member Show 2026

Prizes $700 Exhibition

Maryland Federation of Art's annual members' exhibition at Circle Gallery in Annapolis, running 30 September to 24 October 2026. Any original 2D or 3D work will be considered, and all entries are blind juried — artists' names and identifying information are hidden from the juror. This year's selections are made by Janine Yorimoto Boldt, Curator of American Art at the Palmer Museum of Art at Penn State, who previously held curatorial positions at the Chazen Museum of Art and the American Philosophical Society and earned her PhD in American Studies from William & Mary. Awards designated by the juror total $700, presented at a virtual awards ceremony on 30 September, with an in-person reception at Circle Gallery on the show's closing day. Entry is open to MFA members residing in any state or territory of the US, Canada or Mexico. Up to 6 original works made in the past three years may be entered, nothing larger than 72 inches in any direction, and work previously exhibited at Circle Gallery is not eligible. Artists are notified 3 September and retain 70% of sales (75% for members). Founded in 1963, MFA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit supported by the Maryland State Arts Council.
