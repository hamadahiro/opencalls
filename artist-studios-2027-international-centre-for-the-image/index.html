Artist Studios 2027

PhotoIreland offers four year-long studio spaces at the International Centre for the Image, its cultural space in Dublin supported by the Arts Council of Ireland, for the period January to December 2027. The studios sit in the centre's Atelier, a spacious sound-panelled room with a workshop area and a well-equipped kitchenette, laid out as a shared open studio for four artists; each workspace has a sit-stand adjustable desk, an ergonomic chair and a generous set of shelves. Studio artists also get regular, easy access to the centre's art library, art bookshop and staff, and can use the studio seven days a week during opening hours. The studio comes at no cost to the artist, and PhotoIreland provides a bursary of up to €1,000 reimbursable against materials; studio artists are required to take part in studio visits and talk programmes and are paid for that time. In exchange the centre asks for a real commitment: use of the facilities at least three days a week, for the whole January–December 2027 period — studios are not offered for shorter terms, and failing the three-day commitment means losing the space. Open to photographers and visual artists based in Ireland, at any level of practice from recent graduate to established; any visual arts discipline is welcome, though the setup suits desk-based practices best. The centre is wheelchair accessible and artists with all access needs are welcome, with specific requirements noted in the submission; because the studios and workshop are shared open-plan, applicants are asked to keep their future studio-mates' varied practices in mind. PhotoIreland is a signatory to the Safe to Create Code of Behaviour. Apply in two steps: complete the online Google Form, then send one PDF of no more than 10 MB containing a CV and artist statement, a concise statement of motivation setting out how you would use and benefit from the studio, an outline of previous projects (linked or embedded) and any specific requirements you foresee. Multiple files are not processed and submissions that deviate from the process are not considered. The call opened 5 October 2026, submissions are reviewed 15–30 November, artists are contacted between 1 and 13 December 2026, and studio residencies commence 4 January 2027. The cutoff is 6pm Irish time on Sunday 15 November, not the end of that day, and the organisers state plainly that they do not extend deadlines. The call is planned to be annual and past studio artists may reapply.
