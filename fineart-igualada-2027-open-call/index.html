FineArt Igualada 2027 — Open Call

Open call to select the exhibition projects for the 15th edition of FineArt Igualada, the Catalan photography festival that turns the city near Barcelona into a large space dedicated to photography each spring. Photographers, collectives, and visual artists worldwide can propose a photographic project with a singular gaze — work that moves, provokes reflection, and opens new conversations through the image. Proposals are submitted by email; the festival runs February 19 – March 14, 2027 across venues in Igualada.
