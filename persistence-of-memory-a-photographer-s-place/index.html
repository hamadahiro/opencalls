Persistence of Memory

A monthly juried show in Gallery X at A Photographer's Place in Raleigh, built on the question "If the past is never entirely gone, what remains?" The gallery invites photographic artists to submit work for an exhibition "examining the complex and often elusive relationship between memory, time, and perception", noting that while "a photograph can preserve a fleeting moment, but memory is far less predictable. It distorts, reconstructs, conceals, and reveals." The call seeks "images that consider what remains after the moment has passed" — the imprint of a person, the familiarity of a place, the weight of history, the passage of time, or the fragments of an experience that refuse to fade. Interpretations that are personal, historical, psychological, conceptual, poetic or experimental are all encouraged, as is work exploring remembrance and forgetting, nostalgia and displacement, identity and transformation, or "the blurred boundaries between what is real and what is remembered". Selection weighs relevance to the theme, technical quality and originality, and artistic vision and storytelling. Selected work hangs in Gallery X at the front of the space from 4 December 2026 to 2 January 2027, with an opening reception on 4 December, and is then presented on the gallery's website to be viewed year-round. Up to 5 JPEGs, colour or black and white, minimum 3000 pixels on the longest side; work must be delivered to the gallery by 2 December. Accepted artists may optionally have their work printed by the gallery on its Canon imagePROGRAF PRO-1100.
