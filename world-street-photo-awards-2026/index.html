World Street Photo Awards 2026

Prizes €2,500 Berlin exhibition WSPA photobook

Berlin-run international street photography award, judged blind by a five-person jury of working street photographers and carrying €2,500 in cash across 20 categories. It runs again after a 2025 edition whose winners page and digital photobook are both published on the site; no surface anywhere on the organiser's domain numbers the editions, so take no ordinal from this listing. THE MONEY, ITEMISED: the FAQ states "A total of €2,500 in prize money will be awarded across all categories" — Photographer of the Year takes €500 and each category winner €100; with 20 categories on the categories page that is 20 × €100 + €500 = €2,500 exactly. The FAQ's "All Winners Receive" list runs an official digital Winner Certificate, a Digital Winner Badge and exposure through the organiser's own channels; the homepage adds a trophy, downloadable medals, a year in the online Winners' Gallery and a place in the official WSPA photobook, and extends the medals, certificates and gallery year to honourable mentions. The photobook, like the exhibition, is a homepage-only promise — the FAQ's winners list does not mention it and the Terms & Conditions carry no prize section at all — but the 2025 digital photobook is published on the site, so the product does ship. THE BERLIN EXHIBITION IS ADVERTISED ON ONE PAGE, UNNAMED, AND SILENT ON FOUR OTHERS: the homepage promises "Exhibition at a Renowned Berlin Gallery" and "an exhibition in Berlin" without naming the venue, while the word does not appear at all in the FAQ's "All Winners Receive" list, on the About page's benefits list, on the categories page, or anywhere in the Terms & Conditions except as a promotional use of entrants' images (§5.2). Treat it as a stated intention, not a contracted one. FEES DISAGREE ACROSS THE ORGANISER'S OWN PAGES: all four surfaces agree the first photo costs €18 (T&C §4.1: "The fee for the first photo is €18"), and discounts for extra photos apply automatically — the homepage ladder reads €15 each for photos 2–4, €13 each for 5–10 and €10 each for 11+ and the public submission page repeats those four tiers word for word, while the FAQ alone gives €15 for 2–4 and €13 for 5+ with no €10 tier. Entering one photo in a second category carries a surcharge the homepage never mentions: €10 per category in the T&C §4.2, €12 per category in the FAQ. The T&C settles which figure governs: "The actual fee you will pay is always the fee displayed on our website" (§4.5). There is no entry cap — "you can submit as many images as you like, across multiple categories" — and every photograph and category is scored independently, so cost scales with volume. CATEGORIES (20): Reflection, Weather, Geometry, Black and White, Color, Silhouette, Light & Shadow, Movement & Motion, Transport, The Decisive Moment, Minimalism, Night & Low Light, Cinematic Streets, Abstract & Surrealism, Juxtaposition, Layers, Details, Animals, Mobile Phone Photography and Open Theme. WHAT COUNTS AS STREET: "photos that were taken in public space and showcase real authentic moments of life on the streets. Photos should be candid / unposed / not staged." ELIGIBILITY: no gate at all — "open to photographers of all skill levels and nationalities, both professional and amateur"; entrants under 18 need written parental or guardian consent. Previously published or previously awarded photographs are eligible provided the entrant retains full copyright, and work currently entered in other competitions is explicitly allowed. JUDGING is anonymous: photographers' names are hidden during evaluation, judges cannot see each other's scores, each image is scored 1–100, and "Judges are prohibited from entering the competition themselves." RIGHTS: "Participants retain full copyright ownership of their submitted photographs"; entering grants a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use the photographs for promotional purposes — website, social media, press releases and exhibitions — always with photographer credit, and anyone wanting to limit that use must write to the organiser before submitting. Files: JPEG, sRGB, 7 MB maximum, no watermarks. Organised by Unseen Imaging GmbH of Weinbergsweg 5, Berlin (Amtsgericht Charlottenburg HRB 267930 B), under German law. Final deadline Saturday, 10 October 2026 at 23:59 CEST; winners revealed 8 November 2026.
