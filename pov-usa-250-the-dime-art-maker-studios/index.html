POV USA 250

Prizes $1,750 in cash prizes Exhibition

POV USA 250 is a juried group exhibition at The Dime Art & Maker Studios, an art space at 351 High Street in Burlington, New Jersey. Its theme is America at 250: "a visual showcase that is uniquely American, in all its contradictions", celebrating the dream while questioning the reality. All visual media are accepted, including photography, painting, drawing, sculpture, collage, ceramics, metal and glass. Work must be original and made by the entrant. Fine art prints are not permitted, and AI-generated images are not accepted. Wall work must be wired for hanging, with a greatest dimension of 48 inches, and 3D work can weigh up to 75 lb. Artists can enter up to three works, for $15 for one, $25 for two or $40 for three, through the form on the studio's Exhibitions page. Accepted works are judged and cash prizes are awarded; the organisers put the total at $1,750. The Dime takes a 25% commission on sales. Acceptance is announced on 28 October, and accepted work has to be dropped off at the studio on 30 or 31 October, 10am to 3pm. The opening reception is on 13 November, 7 to 9pm, the show closes on 7 January 2027, and work is collected on 8 or 9 January. The deadline is 25 October at 11:59pm according to the organisers. The prospectus image on their site still gives 24 October.
