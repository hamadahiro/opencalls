Fashion Regenerated 2026

Prizes $10,000 commission 2×$2,000

Photography and filmmaking competition from Textile Exchange, the global nonprofit driving sustainable fiber and material production, in partnership with Atmos. Visual storytellers are invited to document regeneration across the fashion, textile, and apparel industry — from fiber cultivation to circular design. The overall winner receives a $10,000 paid commission to create new work documenting regeneration, publication through Atmos and Textile Exchange, and an exhibition at the 2026 Textile Exchange Conference; two runners-up receive $2,000 each plus promotion and conference display. Open to photographers and filmmakers 18+ of any nationality; winners must be available to travel autumn 2026 – summer 2027 for the commission. Submit up to 25 photographs, a short film, or both.
