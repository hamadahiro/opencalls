Lucid Art Residency 2027

Prize 3-week residency

Three-week residency in Inverness, California, on the edge of Point Reyes, founded in the lineage of surrealist painter Gordon Onslow Ford. Residents get a fully furnished house, the skylit Ark studio, optional water-based printmaking facilities, mentorship meetings with artist associates, and curation into an online exhibition of residency work. Open to US and Canadian artists 18+ with an MFA or equivalent and exhibition history, working in nonrepresentational styles across visual arts, eco-art, video, writing, music, or choreography; first-time applicants only. No stipend or meals, and a car is required.

DeadlineOctober 26, 2026
ResultsJanuary 2027
Entry fee$40
Eligibility18+, US only, Canada only
LocationInverness, CA
Requirements10 images

Jury: 5-member jury of artists, educators & scholars

Organized by Lucid Art Foundation
