Neuwerk Lake Residency 2027

The first edition of a new studio residency and exhibition on Lake Constance, run by the Neuwerk cooperative with the Cultural Office of the City of Konstanz. Neuwerk eG is an unusual host: a cooperative of craftspeople, trades, service providers and freelancers working under the motto "Gewerbe tragt Kultur" (business carries culture), which "creates space for artistic work, cultural exchange and new ideas". The programme was initiated in 2026 and starts in 2027, and the setting is part of the pitch - Konstanz sits in the corner where Germany, Austria and Switzerland meet, and the residency's name takes up the lake. WHAT IS ACTUALLY PROVIDED, in the organiser's own list: two to three months in the studio with accommodation and utilities included; exclusive use of the 116 square metre studio at the Neuwerk, which the site states is step-free; a following exhibition of two to three months at venues in the Konstanz region - for this first residency the vaulted cellar of the Kulturzentrum am Munster in the old town; 1.000,00 EUR exhibition remuneration; a separately negotiated budget for realising the exhibition, which can cover installation, design and publicity; promotion of the show and a vernissage; and introductions to the art and cultural scene of Konstanz and the Lake Constance region. There is no application fee. BE CLEAR ABOUT WHAT IS NOT PROVIDED, because the organiser is: "Reisekosten, Lebenshaltungskosten, Versicherungen und Honorare werden nicht ubernommen. Die Kunstlerinnen und Kunstler mussen ihren Aufenthalt selbst finanzieren konnen" - travel, living costs, insurance and fees are not covered, artists must be able to finance their own stay, and a funding overview for the stay is a required part of the application. The studio and the accommodation themselves cost nothing. Residency is compulsory: on-site presence "ist ein wesentlicher Bestandteil des Programms", with short absences of a few days possible by prior arrangement. Dates for this first round: studio 1 May to 30 June 2027, vernissage mid-to-late June 2027, exhibition June to September 2027. A public accompanying format - an artist talk, open studio, workshop or studio conversation - is part of the residency and the resident helps design it. Photography is covered by the organiser's own affirmative: "Die Neuwerk Lake Residency ist offen fur alle Sparten der zeitgenossischen Kunst und Gestaltung" - open to all disciplines of contemporary art and design. Who it is for: professionally working, up-and-coming artists with an independent practice in contemporary art or design; applications are open regionally, nationally and internationally, from the Lake Constance region, the German-speaking countries, Europe and beyond; sufficient German or English is required; there is no age limit, though the programme is aimed in particular at artists at the start or middle of a professional career. Selection is by a jury drawn from Neuwerk eG, the Kulturamt Konstanz, artists from the Konstanz region and further cooperation partners, judging artistic quality and independence, the quality and feasibility of the planned project, what the residency would mean for the artist's development, the potential for exchange and cultural mediation, and the contribution to the diversity of the programme; no individual jurors are named and "Die Juryentscheidungen werden nicht begrundet" - decisions are not explained. Applications are taken only through the online form on the residency's own site, in German or English, as PDFs: at most 10 MB per document, portfolio at most 25 MB, 32 MB in total, comprising the completed form, a motivation letter of at most two A4 pages describing and justifying the planned project, a proposal for the accompanying programme of at most one A4 page, an artistic CV, a portfolio of selected work from the last two to three years, proof of registration and an identity document (passport and any residence documents for non-EU applicants), and the funding overview. Incomplete or late applications are not considered. Deadline 30 October 2026 (the German page now prints 30.10.2026 in four places; the English page still says 31 October 2026 - the earlier date is the one stored), with selection and offers in early December 2026.
