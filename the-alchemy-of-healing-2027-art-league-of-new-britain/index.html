The Alchemy of Healing 2027

Prizes $200 / $100 / $75 Exhibition

The premise is unusually honest for a themed group show. Most calls about healing want the after picture; this one says so and then refuses it: "Healing is often presented as a moment when the pain is gone and everything is made whole again. The Alchemy of Healing exhibition challenges that idea as healing can be messy, nonlinear, unfinished, and deeply personal. With that said, sometimes healing does not mean returning to who we were before we were wounded. Sometimes it means becoming someone new." If you have a body of work about illness, grief, disability, addiction, survival or care that has never fitted a prettier brief, this is a venue that has already said it will read it.

It is open to all mediums and photography is named in the list, so photographers are not squeezing into a painting show. Each artist may submit up to three works made in the last three years, original and not previously exhibited at ALNB, and each entry needs a statement of 100 words or fewer answering the show's question. That statement is hung in the gallery with the work, which makes it part of the piece rather than paperwork.

Juror Tyler Booth is the CEO and President of InterCommunity Health Center — a health-sector juror rather than a curator, which fits the subject and is worth knowing before you choose what to send. Jurying is hybrid: selection happens online from photographs, then the prize winners are picked in person after drop-off, so the physical object matters for the awards. Those are $200, $100 and $75, on an entry fee of $25 for a first work and $15 thereafter, or $15 and $10 if you are an ALNB member.

Practicalities: images must be unsigned JPEGs under 4 MB in sRGB, ideally 1920 pixels on the long side, because jurying is anonymous. Nothing may exceed 72 inches unframed; 2D work must arrive wired or with D-rings — no sawtooth hangers, no easel-back frames. AI-generated imagery is not acceptable and AI-assisted work must be disclosed at submission, with failure to disclose risking disqualification from awards. Registration closes 11 December 2026, notifications go out 20 December, and the show runs 9 January to 14 February 2027 with an opening reception on 9 January.
