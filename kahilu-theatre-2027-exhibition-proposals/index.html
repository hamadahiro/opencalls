Kahilu Theatre 2027 Exhibition Proposals

Prize Exhibition

Kahilu Exhibits invites proposals for solo and group exhibitions in the theatre's galleries in Waimea, on Hawaiʻi Island, for three 2027 slots: June 10 – July 25, August 5 – September 12, and September 23 – October 31. Open internationally to artists 18+ in all media, including photography. Up to six proposals will be selected. Kahilu handles installation, signage, and promotional materials; artists receive 60% on sales but cover their own shipping, with no artist fees or honoraria provided.

DeadlineAugust 10, 2026
Entry fee$45
Eligibility18+
LocationWaimea, HI
Requirements10–20 images
Submit viaWebsite

Jury: Kahilu Exhibits Committee

Organized by Kahilu Theatre
