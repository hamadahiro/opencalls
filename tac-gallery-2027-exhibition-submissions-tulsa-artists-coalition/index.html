TAC Gallery 2027 Exhibition Submissions

Prize Solo or group exhibition

The Tulsa Artists' Coalition, a 501(c)(3) that runs TAC Gallery in the Tulsa Arts District, is taking proposals for its 2027 exhibition season. The gallery at 9 East Reconciliation Way mounts a new exhibition every calendar month, and artists may propose a solo show, a collaboration with other artists, or a themed group show they would curate. Submissions in all media and disciplines are eligible, subject to the physical constraints of the space - two 24-foot main walls that take work up to 11 feet high - with an emphasis on the contemporary perspective and a body of work with a consistent style or theme. Work should have been completed within the last several years (unless a retrospective) and not shown in Tulsa in the last two years. Read the geography before applying: the Coalition's mission is to support artists in Tulsa and the surrounding area, so out-of-area proposals are accepted but capped at two per eleven-month season, and artists living in Tulsa temporarily, on a fellowship for instance, count as out of area. There is no submission fee. Applicants upload ten images or video with brief descriptions including media and dimensions, a statement of the proposed exhibit's theme, and a bio or resume, through Submittable. A Selections Committee of three jurors - artists, educators and other art professionals, changed each year - reviews the proposals and the 2027 schedule is settled by 15 November 2026. TAC provides installation help, a First Friday opening reception, press and social promotion, and volunteer gallery sitting, and has historically absorbed most exhibition costs; artists cover shipping both ways, provide statements and a price list, and TAC retains a 35% commission on sales (30% for members). The gallery carries no insurance on exhibited work.
