Madrid Artist Showcase — December 2026

Arrival Gallery runs short group shows in a rotating list of cities, and this call is for its Madrid show, 2 to 5 December 2026, at Calle Valencia 17, 28012 Madrid, Spain. The theme is open and the gallery takes submissions from painters, photographers, sculptors, illustrators, textile, mixed-media and digital artists, describing itself as working with emerging, independent and mid-career practitioners. Photographic, moving-image and screen-based work is explicitly catered for: alongside hung physical work, Arrival presents digital pieces on curated display screens, in digital exhibition formats, in online artist features and in city activations, which makes the digital route realistic for a photographer who does not want to ship and frame prints. AI-assisted work is on the accepted-mediums list. Submitting work for review is free and there is no entry fee. The cost comes after selection, and Arrival publishes floors rather than a final price: the Digital Showcase route starts from £65 and the Physical Showcase route from £165, with a third 'Featured Artist' route whose price is not published at all. The full format, fee, timeline and preparation details are sent to selected artists before they confirm a place, and physical work has to be delivered or shipped by the artist. Applicants pick their preferred route — digital, physical or featured — when they submit, or ask the gallery to recommend one. Places are sold from a limited release: when this page was read on 7 November 2026 it listed 40 digital, 40 physical and 5 featured places available, so confirmation secures a slot from the current batch rather than selection alone earning a wall. No juror or selector is named and nothing is awarded — the outcome is the show itself. Applying starts with a mailing-list signup that emails out the real application form. Read it for what it is: a paid-participation showcase, free to enter, where the real decision comes once a price is quoted. CAUTION, MEASURED 9 NOVEMBER 2026 — THE TWO ARRIVAL SURFACES DISAGREE ABOUT BOTH DATES, AND ONLY ON THIS CITY. The call page says “Exhibition Dates 2nd to 5th December, 2026” and “Application Deadline 24th November 2026”; the signup page it sends you to (arrivalgallery.myflodesk.com) is headed “MADRID SHOWCASE — 25TH TO 28TH NOVEMBER 2026, CALLE VALENCIA 17” and states “Deadline to apply: 6th November 2026”, a date already past, under a line describing the venue as “one of Brooklyn’s creative hubs”. The gallery’s five other city forms each match their own call page to the day, so this one reads as an unrevised page rather than a change of plan — but the form is where you hand over your email, so confirm the date with the gallery before counting on 24 November.
