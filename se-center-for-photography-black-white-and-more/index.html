SE Center for Photography — Black, White & More

The SE Center's open call on monochrome — images produced with a single hue rather than recording the colours of the thing photographed. The Center is looking for all forms of monochrome imagery, black-and-white and toned photography alike, any subject, analog, digital or antique processes, and photographers of every skill level and location may enter. The juror is Jennifer Schlesinger, an artist, curator, gallerist and educator based in Santa Fe whose own practice runs through the historical development of photography as an artistic medium — from the camera obscura to the nineteenth-century albumen process to twentieth-century gelatin silver printing, which along with albumen are her chosen mediums; her work mostly concerns landscape and how humans interact philosophically with the natural world. Between thirty-five and forty selected images hang in the SE Center's virtual gallery for about a month, with the selection also carried across the Center's social channels, an archived online slideshow and a video walkthrough. Entry is $35 for up to five images and $6 for each additional image to a maximum of fifteen; members pay $28 and $5. Work exhibited at the SE Center within the previous two years is not eligible, and there are no refunds for images not selected. The Center takes no commission — the artist keeps 100 percent of any sale. The deadline has moved: submissions opened 16 September 2026 and now close 13 December, with selections announced 30 December and the exhibition opening 9 January 2027. Both organiser surfaces carry the new dates, read 14 November 2026: the prospectus calendar reads “Submissions Close 12/13/26, Selections Announced 12/30/26, Exhibition Opens 1/9/27” and the SE Center’s own Smarter Entry board lists this call as “Black, White, & More Closes Dec 13”. The earlier published dates were 1 December and 15 December.
