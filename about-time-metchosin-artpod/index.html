About Time

Prize Exhibition

Metchosin ArtPod is an artist-run gallery on southern Vancouver Island, and About Time is its autumn exhibition on how we experience time — personal, social, political, everyday, generational or historical. Work may dwell in the overall or the nearby, the microcosm or the immeasurable: the abstract mood of time, the urgency of process, a specific hour. All mediums are welcome and photography is spelled out in the submission guidelines — limited-edition photographs and prints, including giclée, inkjet and archival prints, are accepted in editions of 25 or fewer, and photographs must be taken by the artist and may not reproduce another artist's work. On AI the ArtPod says it is still reflecting, but requires submitted work to represent the artist's own creative vision and authorship. Jurying is blind: the juror sees only the image, the medium and substrate, and the artist statement, which is capped at 1,000 characters. Artists may submit up to three original works; diptychs, triptychs and small series count as one work if shown in a single photograph. Two-dimensional work must not exceed 7 feet high by 5 feet wide and there is no minimum size. Work must not have been exhibited in a juried exhibition or commercial gallery on southern Vancouver Island in the past year. Submission is CA$10 per artwork, with a further CA$20 hanging fee per accepted work payable before drop-off; the ArtPod takes 30% commission and all work must be for sale. Exhibiting artists get a social media promotion package and a QR code beside their statement linking to their own site. The exhibition runs 28 August to 18 October 2026.
