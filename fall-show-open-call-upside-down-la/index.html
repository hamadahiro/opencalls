Fall Show Open Call

Prize Exhibition

Upside Down is a donation-based coffee, art and community space in Westwood Village serving the neighbourhood and UCLA, open since 2019 and owned and operated by Jews for Jesus, with a gallery that has staged more than twenty exhibitions since 2019. For its fall show it is looking for local Los Angeles artists with pieces, new or old, that explore the concept of lingering. Suggested threads include staying, stopping, remaining, holding back, pausing, rest before movement, the end of the celebration, silence, calm after the climax, home, consideration of place, presence, retention, leftovers and assemblage. The organisers frame the theme around Shemini Atzeret, the eighth day of assembly, which falls outside the usual seven-day cycle and reads as an extra day to gather before separating, and they ask what it looks like to recognise the sacredness of a moment even as it passes, to stay after the party is over, to reflect before rushing on. All mediums are accepted, including visual arts, poetry and music, and up to three pieces may be submitted. There is no entry fee. Accepted work is shown in the fall exhibition, which runs 17 October to 4 December 2026 with an opening reception; selected artists can exhibit and sell their work and take part in exhibition-related events.
