November 2026 All Media Show

Crossroads Art Center in Richmond represents more than 300 artists and runs a bimonthly juried call with cash awards of $100 for first place, $75 for second and $50 for third. The November edition has no theme — it is an open juried show judged by a guest juror drawn from the Richmond arts community (a gallery director, curator, museum official, arts professor or accomplished artist), shown live in the gallery and simultaneously online. The juror for this cycle is Doug Mock, an award-winning watercolour artist and teacher from White Stone, Virginia, whose jury fee is being donated to the Rappahannock Art League capital campaign. Photography sits in the organiser's own eligible-media list alongside painting, mixed media, collage, fiber, sculpture, ceramics, glass, digital art, woodworking and assemblage. NOTE THE WINDOW: entries are only accepted from 1 to 18 October 2026, so the portal does not open until 1 October — the gallery routes submissions through a per-show artcall.org subdomain, and the November one is now published on the call page as november2026cacallmediashowcfe.artcall.org. Entry is $15 per submission for the general public with a limit of three, or $12 per submission for gallery members who also get a fourth entry free. The gallery accepts only original work by the entering artist, and requires that artists use their own photographic references: AI-generated images, professionally photographed images by another person, another artist's images and instructor's images are all strictly prohibited, with a two-year ban for violations. Artists are notified by 23 October, drop-off runs 11 to 14 November by noon, the exhibition opens on Friday 20 November from 5 to 8 pm and closes on 3 January 2027, with work collected from 5 January. Commission on sales is 30%.
