Women United Art Magazine — Issue XV

Prize Publication

The Autumn 2026 issue of an international quarterly print and digital magazine dedicated to women in the arts, distributed worldwide through Amazon. The call is open to all women artists regardless of age, experience, education, race, sexual orientation or location, and the magazine states its focus explicitly: painting, drawing, printmaking, photography, digital art, collage, fibre and textile art, sculpture and installation. Selected artists are featured in Issue XV in either the In The Spotlight or The Curator's Pick section — the editors decide the length — plus a social media feature and a complimentary digital copy; artists picked for In The Spotlight are contacted with further questions about their work and practice. Everyone who submits receives a complimentary digital copy, and artists not selected for this issue may submit to the following one free of charge. The entry fee is a self-assessed sliding scale with no proof asked for: €35 standard for artists with stable income, institutional support or grant funding, €25 reduced for early-career or freelance artists, the underemployed and those carrying unpaid care responsibilities, and €10 symbolic for students and artists facing economic or structural limitations. The magazine says the fees fund waivers for artists who need them. Prepare an artist bio and an artist statement of up to 300 words each and between two and four images at a minimum of 300 DPI, cropped and ready for print, each filename formatted NAME_TITLE_YEAR_DIMENSIONS_MEDIA. Entries close 15 October 2026, selected artists are announced 25 October 2026 and the issue publishes in late November 2026. Editor-in-Chief is Mona Lerch, who founded Women United ART MOVEMENT in 2021.
