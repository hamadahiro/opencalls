47th Biennial Museum Exhibition

Prizes $6,750 In cash awards

Since 1960 the Montgomery Museum of Fine Arts has partnered with the Montgomery Art Guild on a biennial survey of contemporary regional art in the museum's galleries; this is the 47th edition, on view in the Atrium, Blackmon and Weil Galleries from December 2026 to March 14, 2027. Photographs are named in the organisers' own media list alongside paintings, drawings, prints, sculptures, ceramics, computer graphics, mixed media, fiber art and fine crafts. There is no theme. Open to Alabama residents aged 19 and over, with no membership requirement; up to five works completed in 2024, 2025 or 2026 and not previously shown at the museum. Work made from photographs the artist did not take is barred, and there can be no AI assistance in any part of making the work. Wall pieces may not exceed 44 inches on the longest side including the frame. The juror is Gary Chapman, Professor Emeritus of Painting and Drawing at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, who selects from digital images and picks the award winners from the works on the wall. Over $6,750 has been raised for cash awards so far and neither the guild nor the museum takes a commission on sales.
