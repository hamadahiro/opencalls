Something Personal 2026

Prizes Top 100 print exhibition Prizes

The 29th edition of Something Personal, the annual competition run by the San Francisco chapter of American Photographic Artists, is built entirely around work made for no client: images commissioned by a paying client for publication or another designated use do not qualify as personal work and may be disqualified. Entry is open to all photographers and all types of photography, with no theme and no categories beyond that distinction, and judging is blind — file names are hidden from the jurors and photographers' names are disclosed only after the process is complete. The chapter selects a Top 100, shows the prints at an exhibition and industry event pitched at peers, art lovers and art buyers, and may include selected work in a photo book. APA SF describes an acclaimed judging panel and a lineup of prizes but publishes neither list, and the call sends entrants to the chapter site to see the growing list; as of 27 October 2026 that site carries nothing for this edition — the newest item on its exhibitions-and-contests index is the 2025 call for entries, and its past-judges and prize pages are dated 2016 and 2021 — so anyone deciding on the strength of the jury is paying before it is published. Each entry fee covers one image, and a photographer may submit as many images as they like in batches of ten or fewer before the deadline. Non-members pay $35 an image; the member rates are $10 (student), $15 (ProPlus or Professional), $25 (Emerging or Supporter) and $30 (Contributor or Educator), and APA membership starts at $50 a year. Files are .jpg, sRGB, 72 ppi, at most 2 MB, at least 1,800 px on the short side and no more than 2,400 px on the long side, with the photographer's full name and the image title in the filename. One cost sits past the entry fee: photographers selected for the Top 100 who want to be in the exhibition pay for their own printing and framing and must deliver prints to the chapter's designated framers by a deadline the chapter says is still to be announced. Entrants keep full copyright and grant APA SF only the rights named in the rules — exhibition and display at APA events, use on APA websites and in promotional material, and reproduction in APA-approved publications including photo books that may be sold to support the chapter. Entries close 20 November 2026, Top 100 notifications go out 1–5 December, and the print exhibition and industry event follow in January (the call's timeline prints "January 2026", which the rest of its own dates place in January 2027).
