F-Stop Magazine Issue #140 — Portfolio 2026

A portfolio issue of F-Stop Magazine, the online photography magazine publishing themed issues since 2004. Issue #140 asks for a single project or body of work rather than a loose group of pictures: 12 to 20 photographs plus a project statement, which is required for Portfolio issues. One submission per person per issue, entry is free and there is no payment for images that are included. Submissions go by email; selected work is published on the magazine's site and stays in the archive indefinitely. Publication is expected around December 1, 2026.
