THE REVEAL: The Sublime Uncertainty of Almost Knowing

Inaugural international print biennial from ShadowBoxX Gallery in downtown Waukegan, Illinois, co-sponsored by the Waukegan Printmakers Association. The theme is the moment every printmaker knows: the work is done, the decisions are made, the outcome has been imagined, and still, for one brief moment, you almost know. Entries must be original work that substantially uses an analog printmaking process, and the organisers' list of eligible processes runs from etching, engraving, drypoint, aquatint and mezzotint through linocut, woodcut, wood engraving, stone and plate lithography, monotype, monoprint, collagraph and traditional screenprinting to cyanotype and “related historic, alternative and experimental analog printmaking processes” — which is the way in for photographers working in cyanotype and other alternative processes. Mixed-media and hybrid works qualify when an analog printmaking process is a substantial and identifiable component of the finished piece. Digital technology used incidentally during development does not automatically disqualify a work, but digital printing may not be the primary means by which the finished image is produced, and giclée prints, inkjet reproductions, commercial digital reproductions, photographic reproductions of paintings or drawings and AI-generated imagery reproduced as prints are all ineligible. There is no size limit; editioned prints, variable editions, monoprints, monotypes and other unique impressions are all accepted. Selected work is exhibited for roughly three months, opening in conjunction with November ArtWauk and running from November 2026 into February 2027, with a collectors' preview on Zoom before the reception. Sales are split 50/50 with the gallery and artists set their own prices; artists cover shipping and, for international entrants, customs. THE REVEAL is conceived as a recurring biennial and returns in 2028. Entries close 24 October 2026 and acceptances are notified on 31 October.
