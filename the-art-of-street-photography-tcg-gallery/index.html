The Art of Street Photography

TCG Gallery invites photographers and lens-based artists worldwide to submit work for The Art of Street Photography, an online exhibition of photographs made in public life. The gallery's framing is that the street offers no arrangement and no second take — a gesture, a collision of shapes, a person passing through a patch of light — and that what matters is what the photographer sees in the moment. Photographs from cities, small towns, neighbourhoods, transit spaces and other places where public life unfolds are welcome, and the prospectus states explicitly that people need not appear in every frame. Digital, film, alternative-process and mixed-media photographic work is accepted. Work is reviewed for visual strength, originality, photographic execution, relevance to the theme and contribution to a coherent exhibition. Images generated by, or substantially altered with, generative AI are not eligible. Each entry takes up to three photographs — one is enough, the other two slots are optional — as JPG or JPEG files at least 1,200 pixels on the longest side, unwatermarked and unsigned, with a descriptive filename; multiple entries are permitted and the $15 fee is charged per entry, with fee waivers available through the gallery's waiver application. Entrants supply name, email and location plus title, year, medium, physical print dimensions and price or NFS for each work; website, Instagram, biography and image descriptions are optional. Selected photographs appear in a curated online exhibition running 1–31 January 2027 with artist and artwork information, remain in the gallery's long-term exhibition archive, and may be promoted by email and through the gallery's social channels. Work may be offered for sale or marked not for sale, and TCG Gallery retains a 30% commission on anything sold through it. Copyright remains with the artist; selected artists grant nonexclusive permission for credited exhibition, archiving and related gallery promotion. The cutoff has a time: the call is headed "DEADLINE DECEMBER 20, 2026, 11:59 PM ET" and repeats "Submission deadline: December 20, 2026, 11:59 PM ET", with artist notifications on 27 December 2026.
