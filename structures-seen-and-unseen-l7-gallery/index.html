Structures Seen and Unseen

L7 Gallery's international online juried show on structures seen and unseen, from the veins of a leaf to a trestle bridge. Photography is one of eight accepted media, and Greg Robinson, chief curator of the Bainbridge Island Museum of Art, picks a $300 first place plus two honourable mentions. Accepted work must stay for sale for the run of the show, with the gallery taking 40%.
