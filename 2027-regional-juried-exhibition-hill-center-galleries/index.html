2027 Regional Juried Exhibition

Prizes $1,000 First Place $750 Second Place $500 Third Place Solo exhibition invitation

Hill Center Galleries occupies the Old Naval Hospital on Capitol Hill, where gallery space is spread through the corridors and rooms of a building in constant public use. The 2027 Regional Juried Exhibition is its yearly open-submission show, and it runs long: January 18 to May 1, 2027, with an opening reception alongside the juror on Friday, January 29, 2027.

Eligibility is regional and strict — artists living in the District of Columbia, Maryland and Virginia only — but the medium is wide open: original 2D work in any medium, which is where photography enters. The practical constraints are the venue's: work hangs on the galleries' cable-and-hook Arakawa system, dimensions must include frames and be listed width by height in inches, and there are no size restrictions because some of the spaces carry 14-foot ceilings.

The juror is Florencio Lennox "Lenny" Campello, who curated the 2025 "Women Artists of the DMV" survey show and works as a critic, artist, art blogger and arts advocate. He asks for a short artist statement and a description of each submitted piece. First Place takes $1,000 and an invitation to a spot in the 2027 Solo Artist Exhibitions, installed in the galleries in September 2027; Second Place is $750, Third Place $500, and there are Honorable Mention certificates.

Entry is $45 for up to five pieces and $10 for each additional piece, paid through EntryThingy, with one entry per artist; the call notes a limited number of entry fee scholarships for those who enquire. Submitted artwork must be from 2023-2026, not earlier, and must not have been shown at Hill Center Galleries before.

Everything submitted must be available for sale. Selected artists sign the Hill Center Galleries Consignment Agreement, the galleries sell at the price entered in the application plus DC sales tax, and they retain a 30% commission; no price changes are allowed after the deadline. Delivery is in person on Sunday, January 10, 2027, 10:00am-3:00pm, and pickup on Sunday, May 2, 2027, in the same window — the call says plainly that they cannot accept shipments or ship work back.

Submissions close Thursday, November 5, 2026, with notifications on or about Friday, November 20, 2026.
