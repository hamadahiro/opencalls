17th Big Gay Art Show

Prizes $500 / $250 / $150 Exhibition

Sedona Arts Center's Big Gay Art Show was, in the centre's own words, originally envisioned in 2009 by a group of artists and community members — some affiliated with the centre, others with Sedona Pride — as an inclusive platform to uplift and amplify queer artists in the local arts scene; the centre's own CaFE listing for this edition instead dates that founding conversation to 2012, so treat the start year as the organisation's two surfaces leave it. It is now produced entirely by the arts centre at Sedona Pride's request, and the 17th edition hangs on-site at 15 Art Barn Road from June 4 to July 18, 2027, with an opening reception on June 4, 4–6pm. It is a juried exhibition and sale celebrating creative expression from the LGBTQIA+ community and its allies, and the centre is explicit that entry is not restricted to LGBTQ+ artists — the call is open to allies too, to artists of all backgrounds and experience levels, and to all mediums, with photography and digital media named among the media the centre's exhibitions carry. Entry is $40 for one submission of up to three JPEGs at a minimum 300 DPI, cropped to the artwork with no mat or frame, each file named with the piece's title and listed on the entry form with dimensions, medium and price. Artists must be 18 or older, the work must have been made in the last three years, and it cannot have hung in any previous Sedona Arts Center exhibition. Awards are First Place/Best of Show $500, Second $250 and Third $150, with honourable mentions at the jurors' discretion and a People's Choice announced at the end of the run. Notifications go out by May 7, 2027; accepted work must reach Sedona — shipped or hand-delivered — by June 2. All entries must be for sale at a price the artist sets, and the centre retains a 35% commission, paying out in the month following a sale — a lower cut than the 40% it takes on its Juried Friends Show. Shipping runs at the artist's expense in both directions: artists pay to get work to the centre and must supply a FedEx or UPS prepaid label for the return of unsold work. One eligibility caveat to settle before paying: the CaFE record for this call states only '18 years and older', while the centre's own Call to Artists page states eligibility for its juried exhibitions as 'U.S. artists, 18+' — the two surfaces disagree and the broader one is the call's own application document, so non-US photographers should confirm with the centre first. The centre asks selected artists to help promote the show through their own channels and states plainly that while it promotes the exhibition through press releases, mailings, its website and social media, it cannot guarantee any particular level of visibility. Sedona Arts Center has been a creative home in Sedona since 1958 — more than 68 years, by its own count — and is a nonprofit in Uptown Sedona whose gallery, in its own words, 'represents over 100 professional local and regional artists', with a ceramics studio operating continuously since 1958 and a 2026–27 exhibition schedule that also includes the Vision and Sound African American experience exhibit and the First Peoples Exhibit.
