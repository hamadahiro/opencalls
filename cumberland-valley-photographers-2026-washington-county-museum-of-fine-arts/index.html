Cumberland Valley Photographers 2026

Annual juried photography exhibition at the Washington County Museum of Fine Arts, this year celebrating contemporary perspectives as the nation approaches its 250th anniversary. Photographers explore history, identity, place, and civic life through four themes: Unfinished Revolutions, Power of Place, Tell Everyone's Story, and American Experiment. Respond literally or metaphorically through documentary, conceptual, figurative, or abstract approaches. All photographic processes accepted. Submissions open April 1–30, 2026. Selected work exhibited June 20 – October 11, 2026. Open to photographers from Maryland, Virginia, West Virginia, Pennsylvania, and Washington, DC.
