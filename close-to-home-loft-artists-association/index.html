Close to Home

The Loft Artists Association, founded in 1978 by three artists who took studios in a vacant lock factory and now settled into two galleries and fourteen studios at 575 Pacific Street in Stamford, Connecticut, opens its autumn juried exhibition to all artists. Home, the gallery says, can be a place, a person, a memory, a community or simply a feeling, and artists are invited to consider what feels familiar, intimate or deeply known and what happens when you look at it again. Variations in size and shape are encouraged and both two- and three-dimensional work is wanted; the entry rules state plainly that all mediums are acceptable, so lens-based work is eligible on the same terms as everything else. Anna Patalano juries, and there are cash awards for Best in Show and first, second and third runner-up. Entry is $30 for the first work and $15 for each additional, up to five, and multiple entries must be submitted together on EntryThingy to get the discount. Wall work may be up to 60 inches wide and 72 inches high before framing and sculpture up to six feet. Notification is 26 October, delivery 1 and 2 November, and the show runs 7 November to 20 December 2026 with an opening reception on 7 November. Work for sale is encouraged and the association takes 20% commission. Past juried shows here have drawn over 300 entries from more than 120 artists.
