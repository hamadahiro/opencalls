Open Call March 2027: Group Exhibition

Prize Group exhibition

Bushwick Gallery announced this group show as Electric Tenderness: Where Chaos Meets Care and has since rescheduled it: the page is now headed "Open Call March 2027: Group Exhibition" and says there is no set theme.

The call is open across mediums, photography included. The form takes up to ten files, one image per artwork, at $25 per artwork, paid by card in the form itself.

The show runs March 11-18, 2027, opening with a reception on Thursday 11 March, 6:00-8:00 PM. The gallery has withdrawn the deadline - the page reads "Submission Deadline: To be announced" - and states that existing submissions and payments carry forward.
