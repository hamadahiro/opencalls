Artist-in-Residence — July–November 2027

Surel's Place runs month-long live-work residencies in Garden City, Idaho, in the late artist Surel Mitchell's own home and studio, and it takes applications against two fixed deadlines a year. This is the second of them: 1 March 2027, for residencies beginning roughly four months later. There is no application fee and no residency fee - rent, utilities and wi-fi are covered, and residents receive a living stipend of $100 a week, a $300 travel stipend, the use of the house bicycles, free or discounted tickets to local art events, marketing through the organisation's channels, and logistics and installation help for their public events. Each resident is assigned a host who introduces them to the local arts community, and one residency each cycle includes outreach to local schools through the PARTNER Schools Program. Photography is not in the org's medium list - it names "painters, writers, musicians, architects, filmmakers, and choreographers" - but the governing clause is "any artist who needs a place to focus", and the organisation's own upcoming-artists roster currently lists Eli Craven, October 2026, Photography/Sculpture. The property cannot take loud or messy processes such as welding or blacksmithing, and the org warns that work which is predominantly conceptual may find less enthusiasm with the jury. Residents must live at the house, take no other employment during the stay, give at least one public workshop of two to three hours, present a show or lecture on the residency work, and donate one piece to Surel's Place for future fundraising; the commission on sales arising from a residency is 35% of the retail price, dropping to 15% on merchandise or on work tangential to your medium, such as books. Applicants must be 25 or older and genuine non-smokers - the whole property is non-smoking and the org is blunt about it. Partners may join - no more than two adults, and there may be a surcharge for the extra utilities - but children and pets cannot. Note that the March round is when applications from playwrights and screenwriters reopen after a pause. One thing to confirm with the org before you plan travel: the apply page's deadline table gives this round's window as July to November 2027, while the FAQ and the apply page's own note about literary applicants both say July to December. Visual artists complete the online Artist-in-Residence Application in one sitting - it cannot be saved part-way - and email the accompanying cover letter, proposal including a workshop outline, CV, and 4 to 6 images with an image list by midnight MST on the deadline; the organisation says late applications can be held for the next round.
