Slow Digestion — SaveArtSpace Billboard Exhibition

Prize Billboard exhibition in NYC

Public art exhibition on billboard advertising space in New York City, curated by Jude Griebel, opening October 23, 2026. Slow Digestion sets out to subvert the billboard as corporate ad space that normalises ultra-processed food, inviting work on harvesting, factory farming, the unseen labour behind agriculture, and food histories embedded in communities and private kitchens. The organisers want artworks that treat eating as a storied, reflective, personal and sometimes disquieting act — pieces that can be slowly digested by the public in spaces built to promote rapid consumption. Open to artists of any age, skill level or background; all art is accepted except corporate logos, personal advertising and video, and still JPEGs only, so photographic work is eligible. Artists are notified after September 28, 2026 and selected work is on view for at least one month.
