In The Abstract 2027 — In-Person Exhibition

Prizes $800 / $400 / $300 Exhibition

The gallery half of SFVACC's annual abstract open call: a juried show at TAG Gallery, 5458 Wilshire Boulevard in Los Angeles, running 24 February to 19 March 2027. The theme is abstraction broadly understood — "shapes, forms, color, line and texture as opposed to representing an accurate depiction of visual reality" — and the organiser asks for "a well-rounded range of 2D and 3D abstract art." Photography qualifies on the prospectus's own wording — up to six original works per artist, juried from JPEGs — but this edition carries format bars the virtual one does not: "No AI. No Video. No giclee prints.", a 30-inch maximum in every direction including the frame for wall work, 2D work wired and ready to hang, and "Only plexiglass on 2D work. No glass!" If your prints are pigment inkjet, put the giclée line to SFVACC before paying — the organiser does not define the term. Juror Genie Davis, writer, curator and owner of Diversions Fine Arts Gallery in Manhattan Beach, picks an $800 Best of Show plus a listing on SFVACC's Best of Show page, $400 first place, $300 second place and up to four Honorable Mentions, announced at the opening reception. The hard constraint is delivery: artists must hand-deliver and collect the work themselves at TAG Gallery, on set dates in February and March, and no shipped artwork is accepted at all — which in practice limits this edition to artists who can get to Los Angeles. Commercial terms are specific too: all accepted work must be for sale, priced at $250 or more, TAG Gallery takes 35% of any sale and pays the artist 65% about 60 days after the show closes, while SFVACC takes nothing. Work made under an instructor's direct supervision is not eligible, a submitted work cannot be substituted or withdrawn, and work already shown in an SFVACC exhibit should not be entered. Entry is $40 for the first piece and $5 for each of up to five more; entries close 24 January 2027 at 11:59pm PST with acceptances emailed by 31 January.
