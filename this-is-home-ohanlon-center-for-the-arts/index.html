This is HOME

Prize Exhibition

O'Hanlon Center for the Arts, the non-profit arts centre in Mill Valley, California, invites work for “This is HOME”, an in-person gallery show running 24 November – 17 December 2026 and juried by Craig Coss. The theme is put as a set of open questions rather than a brief: “Just what—and where—is home?” For some, the call says, home may be about family, or a place where old friends gather, or “an inner place of meditative peace”; it could be “a cozy place for one—or a vast space that includes many”. The call asks whether home is domestic or wild, a place of belonging and comfort, or something that “trigger[s] memories imbued with unsettling reminders of misfortune and dysfunction”, and whether it points backwards to origins or forwards to “a destination or state of being we have always longed for”. Open to all artists aged 16 and over, two works each; traditional and non-traditional materials are both acceptable and the call enumerates no media list. This is an in-gallery show with no digital submissions: the registration form is completed and the entry fee paid online in advance so the centre can print the labels, and the work itself must be hand-delivered to 616 Throckmorton Avenue on Friday 20 November, 11 am to 4 pm, or Saturday 21 November, 10 am to 1 pm. Late delivery is not allowed. Wall-hung pieces are capped at 40 inches wide including the frame; the gallery policies set no height limit for most exhibits, but the registration form caps every piece at 60 inches tall. All work must arrive ready for display and securely wired. A résumé and/or artist statement of up to two pages may be attached if wanted. Entry is $40, or $30 for Artist-level OHCA members; paying by card adds a 3% processing charge, and cheque, cash or Venmo may be paid at drop-off instead. The form offers a third route — joining as a new Artist-level member waives the entry fee — but that membership is $140 a year, so it is a saving only for an artist who means to keep entering. Jury results are emailed by 5 pm on Monday 23 November, and non-selected work is picked up on Tuesday 24 November or Monday 30 November, 10:30 am to noon, or on Tuesday 1 December, 10:30 am to 2:30 pm and 5:30 to 7 pm. There is a First Tuesday Art Walk reception on 1 December and an art talk and opening reception on Saturday 5 December. Work previously shown in the O'Hanlon or Loft in-person galleries is not eligible, though the same pieces may be considered for an online show. Artwork need not be for sale; where a sale does happen the buyer pays the artist directly and the artist agrees to donate 30% of the sale price to OHCA on any sale within the exhibition's first 60 days. Photography sits inside the centre's programme rather than at its edge — OHCA has run a dedicated photography show, “IN SIGHT — Photography”, juried by SFMOMA assistant curator of photography Shana Lopes, alongside recurring photography workshops — cyanotype, wabi-sabi camera walks, and black-and-white film developing in a plant-chemistry darkroom.
