The Song of Water — Sutri International Photography Festival 2026

The Doebbing Foto Festival's international open call selects the exhibition projects for the second Sutri International Photography Festival, running 21 November 2026 to 24 January 2027 in the hill town of Sutri, north of Rome. The 2026 theme is water, read through the title The Song of Water: the call asks photographers to treat water not as a backdrop but as the centre of a visual narrative - its vital force, the memory of territories, marine and river landscapes, climate change through drought, flood and coastal erosion, the human bond with water, architecture and water management, and the symbolism, spirituality, rites and myths of rebirth and purification. Open to individual photographers, professional and amateur, to photographic collectives and to photography schools, with no limit on age or nationality; every artistic approach from documentary to conceptual is accepted, and work made with AI is not. Each submission is a coherent project of 8 to 15 photographs, JPEG, at least 2500 pixels on the long side, with files named FirstName_LastName_WorkTitle_SequenceNumber; multiple distinct projects may be entered. Three winning projects are shown in full in the official festival circuit and promoted on the festival's website and social channels; other works judged worthy are placed in an off-circuit trail of 1 to 3 images per author in partner venues around the town. Printing and installation costs are covered in full by the organisers for every selected photographer, winners and off-circuit alike. The jury is the festival's artistic direction - Raffaella Canfarini, Vittorio Faggiani and Debora Valentini - with a final juror still to be named. Entries and proof of payment must arrive by 20 October 2026, 11:59pm, emailed to the festival's published address with a title, captions, a short bio, a project text and the entry form from the site; the fee is paid by bank transfer.
