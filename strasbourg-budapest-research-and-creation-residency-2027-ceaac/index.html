Strasbourg <> Budapest Research and Creation Residency 2027

CEAAC's exchange with Budapest Gallery gives one visual artist or curator a month in Budapest in June 2027, with a studio-apartment on Klauzál square, return travel paid and a €1,000 grant from the gallery. Open in all media, at any age or nationality, to early and mid-career applicants who can show a link to France's Grand Est region and are registered under the French artist-author regime. Duos may apply.

DeadlineDecember 13, 2026
Prizes€1,000 grant, Residency, Travel
Entry feeFree
EligibilityFrance only, Emerging artists, Mid-career
LocationBudapest, Hungary
RequirementsProject proposal
