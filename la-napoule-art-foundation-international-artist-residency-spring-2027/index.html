La Napoule Art Foundation — International Artist Residency Spring 2027

One-month international artist residency at a historic château on the French Riviera, overlooking the Mediterranean Sea. Open to emerging and established artists of all disciplines including photography, aged 18+, with no limitation on nationality. Residents receive accommodations in the château, a private studio workspace, daily breakfast, and most evening meals. Participants must communicate effectively in English or French. Residency dates March 2 – 30, 2027. Applications are evaluated on artistic vision, professional commitment, work quality, project significance, and collaborative willingness. Artists may apply once per calendar year; previous recipients may reapply but are given lower priority. Note: the historic château is not handicapped accessible.
