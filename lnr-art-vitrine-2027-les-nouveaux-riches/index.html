LNR Art Vitrine 2027

Prize Exhibition

Annual open call for the "Kunst-Schaukasten" (Art Vitrine), a curated public-space display case run year-round by Les Nouveaux Riches, the Austrian contemporary art and culture magazine, at Hauptstrasse 84 in Seeboden am Millstaetter See. The org describes it as "an interface between art and the public, contributing a novel dimension to the public space", showing work by national and international artists. Accepted media are enumerated and photography is named: "Text art, painting, sculpture, photography, digital art, illustration." Submit ONE work as a .jpg at 300 dpi with a minimum height of 80 cm; the vitrine takes portrait-oriented images only and the optimal size is 100 x 80 cm, though works need not be displayed at original size. Also required: name, work title, work caption, an artist statement of 250 characters and a link to your website. The org publishes a "Transparency" paragraph that answers the cost and rights questions: because of weather and the risk of damage it exhibits printed reproductions rather than originals, "printed by us"; it covers the cost of producing the prints and accompanying postcards; it takes NO commission if an original work is sold; and it promotes the work on its website and social channels. Entry is free. Only selected artists are contacted, by email, so there is no notification date for everyone else.
