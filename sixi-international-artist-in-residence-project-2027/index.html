Sixi International Artist-in-Residence Project 2027

Sixi Museum is a non-profit institution in Nanjing combining collection, exhibition, academic research and public education, and its International Artist-in-Residence Project, launched in 2024, is recruiting for three-month residencies in two 2027 sessions — spring, March to May, and autumn, September to November, with the exact dates set around the selected artists' preferences; the programme describes itself as “capable of accommodating two artists concurrently”. The project runs on a non-profit basis and covers the visa application, round-trip flights, insurance for the stay, accommodation in either a private apartment in the city centre or a private loft studio on the outskirts, personnel and technical assistance, and a material allowance; support also includes airport pickup, a welcome packet, orientation, English–Mandarin translation, project liaison and introductions to the local art scene. Residents take part in artist talks, workshops and open studios during the stay, Sixi expects a tangible and presentable outcome, and residents have the opportunity to show that work in an exhibition at the end; the artist is also expected to donate a selection of the work made during the residency to Sixi Museum. The 2027 call has two themes and an application may address either: 'Flux and Imprints: Contemporary Echoes of Migration History', on cross-regional mobility, cultural reconstruction and memory — the family histories of overseas Chinese, the legacy of colonialism in Asia, identity within global geopolitical frameworks, and the tension between archives, oral history and personal memory; and 'Folds of Text: Cross-Media Intertextuality and Intervention', which treats text as a fluid medium and invites interdisciplinary work across literature, linguistics, music and sound, and the visual arts. Artists from all disciplines are welcome and the call names no media list, but the programme's own record includes photographers — the 2025 winter resident Oscar Lebeck is described by the museum as a German artist who moves between photography in his study of the structural remains of cultural sites. Applicants must be at least 18 and able to complete the whole residency, there is no nationality restriction, and sufficient spoken and written Mandarin or English is required. The application deadline is 15 November 2026 at Beijing time (GMT+8), which is the organiser's stated clock and runs ahead of most applicants' own. Applications go by email to Sixi as a single PDF or ZIP containing a CV with profile photo, an artist portfolio and a residency proposal, with 'Residency Application' in the subject line; international applicants are told to allow two to three months for visa processing. The Sixi AiR Committee assesses the documents, may hold a video interview, and promises a final response no later than mid-February 2027.
