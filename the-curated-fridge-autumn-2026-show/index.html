The Curated Fridge — Autumn 2026 Show

Seasonal juried photography show from The Curated Fridge, the long-running Somerville project that exhibits selected prints on its now-iconic refrigerator and shares the work online. The Autumn 2026 open call has no theme and is juried by two Boston-area photographers, Stella Johnson and Iaritza Menjivar; this edition will also travel to the Ishibashi Gallery in Concord, Massachusetts, the exhibition space at Middlesex School that shows emerging and mid-career artists and brings students into direct contact with their work. Johnson is a documentary photographer and educator who holds teaching positions at Boston University and Lesley University College of Art and Design, received a Core Fulbright Scholar Grant to photograph in Mexico and Fulbright Senior Specialist grants to teach in Mexico and Colombia, and has twice been named to Photolucida's Critical Mass Top 50. Menjivar's projects explore identity, community and resilience; she received a Commonwealth Heroine Award from the Massachusetts Commission on the Status of Women in 2024 for the Museo Inmigrante project, which also earned a Rising Star Award from the Massachusetts History Alliance and an Award of Excellence from the American Association for State and Local History. Entry is free and open to all photographers worldwide, amateur and professional. Submit up to five images, and note that this call has two halves: you must email the JPEGs (1280px on the longest side, 72ppi, sRGB) and also post actual prints, no bigger than 4x6 or 5x5 inches, with your name on the back, to the Somerville address — local photographers can drop them off instead. Work accepted into a previous Curated Fridge show is not eligible, and no AI photography is accepted. Selected prints are archived; the rest are destroyed unless you enclose a self-addressed stamped envelope. The deadline is a postmark date, so allow time for the envelope to arrive.
