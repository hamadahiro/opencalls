1st Annual National Juried Exhibition

Prize Group exhibition

The Visual Arts Gallery at Governors State University, south of Chicago, launches its first National Juried Exhibition and invites artists living anywhere in the United States to submit contemporary work. The gallery — which sits alongside the Nathan Manilow Sculpture Park and has run a national juried programme since 2010 — says it is looking for work that reflects innovation, technical excellence and compelling ideas across a broad range of media, and encourages artists at every career stage. Guest juror Kimberly Oliva is a Chicago-based Midwest curator and the owner of Oliva Gallery, where she has curated more than 175 interdisciplinary exhibitions; her curatorial work has been shown at SOFA Chicago, Door County Contemporary and art and design fairs across Texas, and she founded the Mozart Park Mural Program. She will select works on artistic merit, conceptual strength and craftsmanship. Entry is $35 for up to three original works made in the past three years, submitted with an artist statement and full details for each piece. Work previously shown at the gallery is not eligible; Governors State alumni are welcome to apply. The exhibition is on view 7–18 December 2026 and 4–22 January 2027, with a closure across the university holiday break.
