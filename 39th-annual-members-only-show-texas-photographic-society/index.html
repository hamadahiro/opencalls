39th Annual Members Only Show

Prize $400 / $250 / $150

Texas Photographic Society's 39th annual members show, juried by Amy Holmes George, a fine art photographer, Fulbright Scholar and former TPS executive director whose own work moves between traditional photography, digital imaging, alternative processes and mixed media. The call is open themed and welcomes photographers of all levels internationally who are at least 18; all photography-based work is encouraged, including digital, silver and alternative processes. Selected work is exhibited at the Center for Contemporary Arts in Abilene, Texas, opening November 13 2026 and running to a closing reception on January 15 2027, with prints due at the gallery by November 6 and local drop-off available November 2 to 6. Awards are $400 for first place, $250 for second, $150 for third and up to five honorable mentions; TPS board members may enter but cannot win. Entry is $30 for five images plus $5 for each additional image, up to twelve, and you must be a current TPS member, though you can join or renew inside the CaFE application at the time of entry (memberships start at $20 student and military, $40 individual). Work previously shown in a TPS exhibition is not eligible, and TPS takes no commission on prints sold during the show. One caution on timing: TPS states the deadline as 11:59pm Mountain on Friday 2 October, but the CaFE portal's own closing field is set a day earlier, so treat 1 October as the safe date to submit by.
