Call for Artists: 2027 Exhibition Season

The Red Brick Center for the Arts, the City of Aspen's arts centre, invites artists living and working in Colorado to apply for its 2027 exhibition season. The programme runs solo exhibitions in the West Gallery and curated group exhibitions in the Main Gallery, and artists selected through this call are considered for the four 2027 slots: 11 February to 26 March, 10 June to 31 July, 12 August to 15 October, and 2 December 2027 to 10 January 2028. There is no fee to apply. The Gallery Committee reviews submissions and recommends artists whose work, in the centre's words, demonstrates artistic excellence, originality, and meaningful engagement with contemporary ideas and practices; the Red Brick's recent programme includes the 2025 Colorado Juried Exhibition and a solo show by the photographer Wendi Schneider. Photography is named outright in the accepted media, alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, ceramics, textiles, sculpture, mixed media and experimental work, and proposed work must not have been exhibited at the Red Brick before. Applicants submit an artist statement and biography, 8 to 12 images of recent work labelled with title, dimensions and medium, and a 250 to 500 word exhibition proposal describing the themes, any new work planned for 2027, and how it relates to the wider practice; a CV and website are optional. Artists are responsible for delivery and return shipping; work is offered for sale during the show with a 60/40 split in the artist's favour, and the exhibition agreement asks that photographs and other paper works be framed under glass or Plexiglas unless another presentation is agreed. Applications close 30 September 2026 and artists are notified on 6 November 2026.
