HIGH 5: HUMANS

Short-format open call from Decagon Gallery, open for only two weeks. HIGH 5 asks for a single photograph for $5, and each paid submission includes one free invitation for another photographer to enter (invited submissions do not carry a further invitation). The theme is HUMANS: photographs where human presence matters — a face, a body, a gesture, a relationship, a private moment, a chance encounter, or something harder to name. Portraits, street photography, documentary work, self-portraits, staged photography, family photographs and experimental approaches are all welcome; the person does not need to be fully visible and the photograph does not need to explain who they are. The gallery is simply looking for images that make you feel there is someone there. Open internationally to photographers 18 and over. Selected photographs are presented in the HUMANS online exhibition, on view 30 September – 31 October 2026, and one photograph is chosen as the winner, earning that photographer five free entries to future Decagon Gallery open calls. Notification is 21 September.
