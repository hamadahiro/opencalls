The Big Small Show 2026

Prize Exhibition

Drawing Rooms calls this the eleventh year of The Big Small Show, though its own exhibition archive lists editions only for 2013 to 2017 and 2023 to 2025. The premise has stayed the same: send good recent work, no theme, and let the curator — Gallery Director Anne Trauben, one of the two artists the organisation says have run it for more than twenty years — build the connections. For a photographer that is a different proposition from a photography competition — your print is placed against painting, collage and sculpture in a reading of the room, not scored against other photographs.

Scale is the practical hinge. Work hand-delivered to Jersey City can be any size; anything posted has to be 18 x 24 inches or under and flat enough for a rigid mailer or a tube, with return postage inside. Unframed prints are welcome, which keeps the cost of entering low, but they have to be sturdy enough to hang from clips — no soft, creased or fragile paper.

There is no prize. What the $35 buys is consideration for a curated show in a 501(c)(3) gallery at Mana Contemporary, a curator's statement, a review, the Artsy listing, and a look-in for Drawing Rooms' other venues through the year. Work is insured while the gallery holds it, and every piece must be for sale at a 60/40 split in the artist's favour, settled within 30 days. Anything the gallery moves to Artsy stays for sale there up to two months past the closing, and submitting agrees in advance to a collector discount of up to 10%.

Dates to hold: notification on 5 November 2026, hand delivery 20–22 November (Drawing Rooms on Newark Avenue or The Oakman on 1st Street), mail-in arriving by 23 November, and the show itself 10 December to 17 January, opening the weekend of 11–13 December.
