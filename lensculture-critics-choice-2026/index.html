LensCulture Critics' Choice 2026

Global competition where 20 leading photography critics and curators each select 3 photographers. Up to 60 winners featured in LensCulture online gallery. Top Ten receive $1,000 cash grant each plus international group exhibition at Photo London 2027. Exposure to 3 million global audience. Open to all photographers worldwide.
