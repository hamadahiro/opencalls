Sixth LaGrange Southeast Regional

Prizes Museum exhibition Purchase Awards

Juried regional at the LaGrange Art Museum in Georgia, continuing the lineage of the LaGrange National begun in 1964. Open to artists 18+ residing in Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Virginia, and West Virginia, with original work from the last two years in drawing, painting, printmaking, sculpture, photography, ceramics, or mixed media. Purchase Awards add works to permanent collections, alongside juror-selected Merit Awards; no commission on sales. Exhibition September 19, 2026 – January 9, 2027.
