SE Center for Photography — The Art of Architecture

Prizes Exhibition prize Portfolio review prize

The Southeast Center for Photography (SEC4P) is a Greenville, South Carolina photography organization (104 S Main St, Suite 841) that runs themed virtual juried exhibitions. "The Art of Architecture" is the center's open call for architectural photography in all forms — urban or rural, black-and-white or color, analog, digital, or antique processes.

The 2026 cycle is juried by Michael Pannier, a Greenville-based fine art photographer with thirty years' practice. Selection takes 35 to 40 images for an approximately one-month virtual gallery, with social media promotion across SEC4P's Facebook, Instagram, and X channels and an archived online slideshow plus video walkthrough. Selected artists also receive a portfolio review with the SEC4P director.

Entry fees: $35 for non-members for up to 5 images ($6 each additional, max 15), or $28 / $5 for SEC4P members. There is no sales commission.

Eligibility is open to all photographers regardless of location, age, or skill level. Work previously exhibited at SEC4P within the prior two years is ineligible. The 2026 deadline is July 1; results are announced July 15.
