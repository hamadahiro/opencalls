In the Moment

Prize Exhibition

Members' exhibition mounted as part of PhotoMidwest's 2026 Biennial Festival, shown at the University of Wisconsin's Pyle Center in Madison and in the organisation's online gallery. Each PhotoMidwest member may enter one image, free of charge, on any subject - the show is framed around photography's ability to make a moment permanent, whether it documents a slice of reality or a vision from the artist's imagination. There is no jury: the Pyle Center has wall space for up to 80 framed pieces according to the call page, or up to 75 according to the entry form, and if more entries arrive than there is wall they are hung in the order they were entered, first come first served. Every entry appears in the online gallery regardless, and members who would rather not frame can enter for the online gallery only. Framed work is capped at 30 inches on the longest dimension; files should be 2000-2500 pixels on the long edge, sRGB, under 2.5 MB, with no watermarks or borders. The exhibition runs November 2, 2026 - January 29, 2027. Entry requires PhotoMidwest membership.
