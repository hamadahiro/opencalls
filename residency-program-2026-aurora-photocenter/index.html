Residency Program 2026

One residency for artists working in photography or photo-based media at Herron School of Art + Design. Two-week intensive work period (August 10–21, 2026, fixed dates). $1,000 stipend plus up to $500 travel reimbursement. Includes darkroom facility, studios, and basic chemistry. Housing on CAMi campus for out-of-town residents. Resident must give a talk during or after the residency. Previous Aurora exhibitors (2025–2026) ineligible.
