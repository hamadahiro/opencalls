Southern Gothic: Artists Working Below the Mason-Dixon

Prize Exhibition

Southern Gothic is a curated virtual exhibition in NOT REAL ART's First Fridays series, online from 6 November to 31 December 2026 and featuring artists from, inspired by, or transplanted to the American South. Curated by Kirsten Bengtson-Lykoudis, a former New York gallery director and photographer now based in Richmond, Virginia, with editor-in-chief Morgan Laurens as co-organiser, it aims to move beyond stereotypes and present contemporary work by artists working below the Mason-Dixon line, exploring the region's contradictions of identity and place. Open to artists worldwide with a connection to the South, whether living, working, or making work about or within the region. All mediums and styles are welcome except work entirely generated by AI. Submit up to four works as high-resolution JPEGs with an artist bio and statement through the form on the organiser's site; there is no fee, and the call is also posted on EntryThingy. Applicants are also considered for a long-form interview on the NOT REAL ART platform and a guest appearance on its podcast. NOT REAL ART is a Los Angeles arts publisher and grant-maker powered by the 501(c)(3) Arterial; its submission terms grant a non-exclusive promotional licence while artists keep full copyright, and there is no payment for selection. Deadline 24 September 2026.
