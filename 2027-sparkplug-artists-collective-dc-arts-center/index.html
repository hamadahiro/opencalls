2027 Sparkplug Artists' Collective

The Sparkplug Artists' Collective is the DC Arts Center's one-year programme for artists seeking to establish, reignite or sustain a professional art career. Around ten artists are chosen each year through an application and interview process, from deliberately wide-ranging backgrounds and levels of experience. Members meet monthly for studio visits, moderated peer critiques and social events, with art excursions and workshops alongside — and, crucially, exhibition opportunities in the centre's own galleries in Adams Morgan, Washington DC. DCAC has two renovated spaces: the Main Gallery, about 700 square feet, which takes full-size work in all media, and the 125-square-foot Nano Gallery for small-format work. Artists keep 60 per cent of the price of anything sold, or 70 per cent for DCAC Artist Members. The centre states that it accepts applications from all artists working seriously in their field and does not discriminate on any basis; note that the programme itself runs in person in Washington DC, with monthly meetings and studio visits through the year, and that the collective has so far drawn its nearly 100 members from the greater Washington, D.C., area. Applications ask for an artist statement and biography, a résumé and work samples from the last five years, current or past. There is a $25 application fee and a $250 programme cost, both waived for DCAC Artist Members. Applications close at 11:59 PM on 29 November 2026; shortlisted candidates are interviewed after the deadline, decisions are communicated in December, and the 2027 cohort runs from 1 January to 31 December 2027.
