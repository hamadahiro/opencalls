New Terrain: A Juried Exhibition

VCP's 2027 juried show looks forward, asking for photographs that imagine environmental futures: restoration and rewilding already underway, or landscapes built from speculation and invention. Lucas Foglia juries, and selected work hangs in the main gallery in Brattleboro from March to May 2027; any photographic medium, any age, any country. Accepted artists print, frame and ship their own work, and VCP keeps 40% of any sale.
