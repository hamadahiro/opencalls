Wild & Free — SaveArtSpace Billboard Exhibition

Public art exhibition on billboard ad space throughout New York City, curated by Gigi Chen, a contemporary wildlife-focused artist and nature photographer. Theme explores the close connection between nature, mythology, and civilization — how the natural world coexists with urban environments. Open to artists of all ages and backgrounds. Selected works displayed on NYC billboards for at least one month starting June 26, 2026. SaveArtSpace is a non-profit that has run public art billboard exhibitions since 2015.
