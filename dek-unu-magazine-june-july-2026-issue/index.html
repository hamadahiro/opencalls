Dek Unu Magazine — June & July 2026 Issue

Prize Publication prize

Dek Unu Magazine is an international not-for-profit photography journal that publishes monthly. The structural premise is unusual: each issue features one accomplished artist rather than a multi-photographer group format. Issues are published as both print and online paperback.

Eligibility is open across all fine art photography genres and techniques. Selection criteria pair technical excellence with artistic vision and conceptual intent — the magazine is looking for resolved, mature bodies of work.

The 2026 June/July cycle is a combined double issue — one selected artist for two months of feature.

Submission is by email with 11 images. Application is free. The 2026 June/July deadline was April 20; selection announced around May 1, 2026.
