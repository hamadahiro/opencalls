38th NSA National Online Juried Show

Prize $1,850 in cash awards

The National Society of Artists' 38th annual juried show, run entirely online: nothing is shipped to a venue, and every juried work is displayed and offered for sale on OnlineGalleryShows.com from October 4 to December 4, 2026. NSA is a 501(c)(3) founded in 1985 and based in Texas, set up for artists working across all media rather than one, and it names photography alongside painting, drawing, computer-generated art and sculpture. Amanda Barry Jones is the juror. Awards total $1,850 in cash: $750 Best of Show, $500 first, $250 second, $100 third, two $50 Honorable Mentions (the second at the juror's discretion) and the $150 Gay & Philip G. Paratore Memorial Award. Open to artists in the United States and Canada working in 2D or 3D; work must be original, made within the last three years, never accepted into a previous NSA national show, and offered for sale for the run of the exhibition. AI work is not accepted and the show is family-friendly, so no nudes, gory violence or obscene work. Entry is $35 for the first submission and $15 for each additional with no cap on submissions, though a maximum of three works per artist can be juried in. Sales are split artist 65%, NSA 20%, OnlineGalleryShows 15%, and artists ship sold work themselves, unframed and freight-free within the continental US. Notifications go out by September 28.
