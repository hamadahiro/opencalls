Metamorphosis vol. I — Art, Performance and Celebration

dotART and Exhibit Around take Ovid as a starting point for a six-volume photography project, and the first volume is about transformation in art, performance and collective celebration. Entry is free — three single photos for non-members — with selected work printed in the book and shown at Trieste Photo Days 2027. Prizes include a Celiberti sculpture and two nights in Trieste.
