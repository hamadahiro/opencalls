Rarefied Light 2026

Annual statewide juried exhibition with juror Nevada Wier (National Geographic, Smithsonian). Submit 1–20 images of any photographic form except AI-generated work. Selected work travels to galleries in Anchorage, Fairbanks, Juneau, and Cordova from October 2026. Includes three-day workshop (August 14–16) on color, light, action, and pattern in image-making.

Jury: Nevada Wier, Photographer
