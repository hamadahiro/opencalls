Visual Arts — Embodied Making: Performance Art, Images and Objects

Five-week faculty-led thematic visual-arts residency (July 5 – August 6, 2027) asking artists to think expansively about performance — how it emerges through movement, stillness, reading, listening, endurance, care and collective action — and how performance and materiality intersect, with images and forms activated and transformed through embodied interaction. Banff's landscape is treated as a collaborator, opening site-responsive and place-based work. Artists get individual studios, shared production facilities with technical staff, one-to-one faculty mentorship and studio visits, workshops and peer critique. Lead faculty are Brendan Fernandes and Claire Fleming Staples, both there for the full five weeks, with Abril Zales (Chief Curator, Museo Tamayo) joining as guest faculty July 12-23. The program explicitly caters to artists working in photography alongside ceramics, digital media, painting, drawing, printmaking, papermaking, performance art, sculpture and installation. Open to individual artists or duos at any career stage, 18+, domestic and international; applicants need post-secondary training in visual arts or equivalent recognised experience. All participants receive the Banff Centre Arts Scholarship, which covers tuition plus a contribution toward accommodation and meals.
