Beyond Borders II: Global Voices

Prize $500 First Place

Beyond Borders II: Global Voices is the second edition of The Art Center's exhibition about world cultures, and the brief is unusually clear about what it does not want. It asks for a global viewpoint "not as a generalized or stereotypical concept, but as something specific, individual, and deeply felt", and states that the show aims to move past simplified narratives and superficial notions of multiculturalism toward the richness, tension, overlap and individuality that exist within and between communities.

Suggested themes include cultural heritage and identity, migration and diaspora, tradition and transformation, intersectionality and hybridity, cultural contrast and exchange, and personal narratives within a global context. Entries may be celebratory or critical, bold and expressive or quiet and contemplative — what the curators say they value above all is authenticity and specificity of voice.

Eligible media are enumerated and include photography, alongside mixed media, ceramics, drawing, fibre art, printmaking, painting, collage, digital art and sculpture. 2D work is capped at 48 × 48 inches framed and 20 lbs, works on paper must be framed with acrylic or plexiglas, and pieces must arrive dry and wired ready to hang. AI-generated work will not be considered.

Each artist may submit up to three works for a non-refundable $25 entry fee. First Place receives a $500 cash award, with additional cash prizes and recognitions presented as part of the exhibition. The show is juried and judged by the curatorial staff of The Art Center.

The deadline is August 14, 2026, with notification on August 21 and drop-off from August 31 to September 2. The exhibition runs September 11 – October 17, 2026, with the awards reception on October 17 from 6 to 7:30pm. Artists keep 75% of sales, with a 25% commission to The Art Center, which is a nonprofit in Johns Creek, Georgia.
