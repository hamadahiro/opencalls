Wytches II

Prize Exhibition

A group show at Dorado 806 Projects, an artist-run studio gallery on Broadway in Santa Monica, built around the witch as a figure of intuition and connection to the earth rather than the patriarchal caricature. The curators describe digging into 'the enchantment of moonlight and the rhythms of nature to look at the intuitive, connected beings who have been unjustly stigmatized throughout history'. Photography is named outright among the accepted media, alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, fiber and sculpture. Work must be original, unsold, wholly the artist's copyright, and no more than 36 inches in any direction — though a series of individually compliant pieces may be submitted together. Works on paper must be framed and everything arrives ready to hang. $35 covers up to three images. Curated by Amelie Laurice and Meghan DeRoma; the opening reception is October 7, 2026, with an in-person run of about four weeks and an online show to November 30. Sales split 50/50, with Dorado donating a portion of its share to a local non-profit, and the gallery states that entry fees fund its free community events and group exhibitions. Artists are responsible for delivery and collection.
