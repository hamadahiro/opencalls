FotoDoc Photo Contest 2026

International documentary photography contest with three categories: Single Image (1–3 photos), Photo Essay (6–8 photos), and Portfolio (10–15 photos). Free to submit; $29 publication fee only if selected. 60 finalists published in festival catalog. 25 winners exhibited at FotoDoc Festival in São Paulo in November 2026. Overall Portfolio winner receives trophy, airfare, and accommodation to attend the festival. No AI-generated images accepted.
