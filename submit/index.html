What gets listed

I add a call when:

  • photographers can apply to it themselves, not only through a nomination or an organization;
  • the organizer’s own website has a public page with the deadline, the fee and how to apply;
  • the deadline hasn’t passed;
  • selected artists get something real: an exhibition, a publication, a residency, a grant or prize money (a certificate or a social media feature alone isn’t enough);
  • every fee is stated up front, including fees charged only to selected artists.

If a call you sent isn’t on the site, one of these was missing when I checked. Once the organizer’s page has it, send the call again.

Information is provided for convenience. Details may change. Always verify them on the official call website.
