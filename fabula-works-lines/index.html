Fabula Works — Lines

New open call from the Rome curatorial platform Fabula Works, themed Lines. Two photographers are selected for the first exhibition of 2027 at Millepiani in Rome (29 January – 31 March 2027, opening reception 29 January at 6pm), with printing and exhibition promotion fully covered; the organiser reserves the right to present a solo show where one submission shows a singular vision. Guest juror is Eric Bouvet, the French photojournalist and VII Foundation member, five-time World Press Photo winner and two-time Visa d'Or News recipient. All genres are welcome; the call wants photographs that make you look twice and create their own visual logic. Submit 10–25 photographs made within the last ten years as a single PDF with a biography and artist statement, in English or French; a non-refundable €25 contribution is paid via PayPal before completing the form. Open to anyone aged 18 or over, from any country. Fully AI-generated images are not allowed, though AI-assisted retouching of your own photographs is permitted. Selected photographers are contacted around six weeks before the opening. Deadline Sunday 15 November 2026, 23:59 CET. CAUTION BEFORE YOU PAY: the call page itself has been password-protected since at least 26 October 2026 and still is on 29 October — fabula-works.com/opencall001 returns HTTP 401 to every fetch rung and, opened in Chrome, shows nothing but a Squarespace lock icon and a password box. Fabula Works’ own home page names no current open call: under “Past Open Calls” it says only that “The open call for our Fall exhibition concluded in August 2026”, with Carolyn Mendelsohn as that round’s guest juror and an opening on 6 November. Everything above was recorded while the page was still public and cannot be re-checked today; ask Fabula Works directly before sending the €25.
