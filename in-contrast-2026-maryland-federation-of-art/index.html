In Contrast 2026

Maryland Federation of Art's juried group show on contrast as a working tool — the chiaroscuro of the Baroque and the oppositions of Fauvist colour, but equally contrasts of texture, subject and concept. Selected work is shown in MFA's Virtual Gallery from 15 December 2026 to 14 February 2027. All MFA exhibitions are blind juried, with artists' names and identifying information hidden from the juror; this edition is selected by Sena Clara Creston, an interactive installation artist and Associate Professor of Media Arts at Sonoma State University, who studied Photography and Imaging at NYU and took an MFA in Electronic Arts from RPI. Cash awards presented by the juror total $500, announced 7 January 2027. Artists working in all mediums are invited and any original 2D or 3D work is considered; up to 6 original works made in the past three years may be entered, and work previously exhibited in MFA's Virtual Gallery is not eligible for re-entry. Because this is an online show, MFA's General Guidelines open it to artists worldwide with the exception of sanctioned countries — the call page also carries a US/Canada/Mexico line copied from the physical Circle Gallery shows, which the guidelines and the entry form both override. Only artists in the 48 contiguous United States may set a sale price, and MFA reimburses up to $35 of their shipping on online sales; everyone else lists Price on Request and MFA refers enquiries to them. Artists are notified 19 November and retain 80% of sales (85% for members). Founded in 1963, MFA is a non-profit whose Circle Gallery in Annapolis is Maryland's oldest operating artist gallery. MFA's own surfaces contradict each other on who may enter: the head of the call page and the ArtCall terms both say entry is open to artists worldwide bar sanctioned countries, while the Terms of Entry block on the same page and the ArtCall FAQ restrict entry to artists residing in the United States, Canada or Mexico.
