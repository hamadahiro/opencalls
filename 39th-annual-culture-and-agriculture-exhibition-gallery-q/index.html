39th Annual Culture & Agriculture Exhibition

Prizes $2,850 in cash awards Exhibition

A national juried exhibition at Gallery Q, an artist-owned gallery in downtown Stevens Point, Wisconsin, running March 3 to April 29, 2027 and "celebrating our connection to the land and the art of farming since 1985". Work must be inspired by agriculture, "whether representational or abstract, and may include rural landscapes, farm life, or broader agricultural themes and issues"; the organisers describe an exhibition that moves "from the land and livestock to the laborers, tools, and traditions" with styles "ranging from representational to abstract". Open to all artists 18 and older, with work "created within the past five years" that represents the artist's current body of work. The accepted-media sentence is a dimensionality list rather than a medium list — "All two- and three-dimensional media, digital art, and video art are welcome" — so photography is admitted as two-dimensional work but is nowhere named in the prospectus, and a photographer entering should expect to be juried beside painting and sculpture. Awards total $2,850 across eight prizes, itemised in the prospectus as Best of Show $1,000, People's Choice Award $500, 1st Place $450, 2nd Place $350, 3rd Place $250 and three Honorable Mentions at $100 each; the People's Choice "will be selected at the conclusion of the exhibition". The online jury runs February 2–5, 2027 with notifications on February 7; final award selections are made in person on March 3–4, 2027, after the juror has seen the installed show, and "all exhibition and award decisions are made solely by the juror" — Gallery Q membership "provides no preference or special consideration". The 2027 juror is Roberta Condon, an award-winning Wisconsin artist, Master Circle pastelist with the International Association of Pastel Societies and Signature Member of the Pastel Society of America, whose American Pastoral series "explores the changing Midwestern agricultural landscape and the loss of the family farm"; she won Best of Show in the 2026 edition. Up to two works may be entered for the $30 fee, and artist statements are published in the exhibition, online catalog and marketing. Costs if selected sit with the artist: physical work must arrive February 24–27, 2027, wall-hung work wired and ready to hang (no easel-back or sawtooth frames), in durable reusable packaging with a prepaid, pre-addressed return shipping label enclosed — "artwork arriving without a prepaid return shipping label will not be installed in the exhibition" — and the artist insures the work in transit, though Gallery Q insures it from arrival through pickup or return. Accepted video art is delivered as a file and is not shipped. Sales are split 60/40 in the artist's favour. One clause to diary: unclaimed physical artwork left beyond 30 days after the exhibition becomes the property of Gallery Q.
