PhotoIreland FUTURES Talents 2027

Tenth edition of the FUTURES Talent Call run by PhotoIreland as the Irish member of the FUTURES Photography Platform, the Creative Europe-funded network that since 2017 has pooled the talent programmes of leading European photography institutions — 23 members as of 2026, among them Der Greif, FOMU, Foto Arsenal Wien, FOTODOK, Fotofestiwal Lodz, Photo Elysée, Fundació Foto Colectania, ISSP, Organ Vida, Triennial of Photography Hamburg and Void. Free to enter and open to artists based in Ireland and the UK, as well as Irish and UK artists practising abroad; the call takes photography and its expanded practice at any level, and the jury is explicitly looking for contemporary lens-based artists regardless of curriculum or experience who have shown dedication and serious intent in their practice. Selected artists receive a participation fee of €500; Futures Conversations with international professionals, online and in person; covered travel, accommodation and per diem for the FUTURES Annual Event, which in 2027 takes place in October in Vienna hosted by Foto Arsenal Wien, with the talks and workshops programme included; the chance to apply to the internal open call for the annual travelling exhibition, curated in 2027 by Foto Arsenal Wien, Fotofestiwal Lodz and the Finnish Museum of Photography; the chance to apply for a mentorship session with a FUTURES member organisation; access to Magnum workshops developed for FUTURES artists; an artist page on futures-photography.com; and promotion through the platform's channels and the annual publication, which in 2027 marks the network's tenth anniversary. PhotoIreland has put 45 artists forward to date, among them Ciarán Óg Arnold, Megan Doherty, Miriam O'Connor, Róisín White, Vera Ryklova and Yvette Monahan. Apply by emailing a single PDF containing a 200-word third-person bio, an artist statement, a CV, a description of your latest project in up to 500 words, a selection of low-resolution images from that project and brief information on past projects; receipt is acknowledged within two working days. Selected artists then supply fifteen flattened TIFFs at 300 dpi, 4500 px on the long side, plus a portrait, due by 17 January 2027. The jury sits from 30 November to 20 December 2026 and all applicants are contacted before 10 January 2027. The cutoff is 6pm Irish time on Friday 27 November, not the end of that day, and the organisers state plainly that they do not extend deadlines.
