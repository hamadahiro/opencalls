Three Artist Show

Curated group exhibition at Dusk Gallery in Santa Fe celebrating the power of the series — three photographers are selected, each with a unified body of work connected through subject matter, tone, colour palette, or conceptual framework. Twenty-six images total are juried by Dusk founder Holly Hart. The gallery covers professional Luster-paper printing, matting, and 24×24 inch black museum-quality framing; artists supply only a high-resolution JPEG. Exhibition runs November 6–27, 2026 with an opening reception; post-exhibition sales split 50/50 and photographers retain full ownership and rights. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers. Entry is $40 for 5 images or $45 for up to 10. The submission form itself asks for 6 to 15 images, which does not match the call page's 5- and 10-image fee tiers.
