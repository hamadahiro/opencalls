PLACE/MEMORY — CONTACT Photography Festival

Open call photography exhibition at Northern Contemporary Gallery in Toronto, presented as part of the 2026 CONTACT Photography Festival — Canada's largest photography festival. Photographers are invited to submit work that represents their current practice through the medium of photography, responding to the theme PLACE/MEMORY. Physical group exhibition runs May 21 – June 2, 2026, with an opening reception on May 21, 6–9pm. One submission per artist via the official Google form. Work drop-off May 19; pick-up June 3. Part of the CONSTELLATION program curated by Su-Ying Lee and Emilie Croning.
