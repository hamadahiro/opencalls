Self-Publishing and Small Publishers Space — 16th Lisbon Photobook Fair

Alongside its photobook dummy exhibition, the Lisbon Photobook Fair runs a Self-Publishing and Small Publishers Space — the Espaço dos Autores e Pequenos Editores — giving photographers who publish their own work, and the small presses that publish them, a place to sell at the 16th Feira do Livro de Fotografia de Lisboa, held 27–29 November 2026 at the Arquivo Municipal de Lisboa – Fotográfico on Rua da Palma. The Authors' Space is managed by the fair's own organisation and asks a single one-off contribution rather than taking a cut: €10 covers up to two different titles and €20 covers three or four, in both cases with no limit on the number of copies you bring and, in the organisers' own words, no commission whatsoever on sales. Pre-registration is through a bilingual Portuguese/English Google Form submitted together with a maximum of six photographs of each photobook (72 dpi, 2 MB maximum, named for author and work) sent by WeTransfer to the fair under the subject 'Self-Publishers'. A registration only counts once the form, the images and the contribution have all been received. Pre-registration closes at 21:00 GMT on Monday 19 October 2026 — the form now carries that date marked “atualizado”, extended from 12 October, confirmations go out on 23 October, and books must reach the Arquivo Municipal by 16 November, delivered in person Monday to Saturday, 10am–6pm, or by post at the author's own cost; unsold copies are collected from the archive after the fair.
