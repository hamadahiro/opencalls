Call for Art: 2D Temporary Public Art Installation

Prize $400 stipend and use fee

RoCo invites artists to propose three images for a temporary public art installation outside its main facility on East Avenue, a twice-yearly call with deadlines on September 15 and March 15. The selected series is printed on weather-safe material at roughly 9 feet high by 6 feet wide and shown to the public 24/7 for four to six months; RoCo covers all production and installation costs and pays the artist a $400 stipend and use fee for exhibiting preexisting images. Files must be vertical and delivered at 12,000 x 8,000 pixels. Of particular interest are projects that connect viewers to the location, community and history of the neighbourhood, that challenge passersby, and that address social and political issues; RoCo welcomes visual artists of all genres and also invites collaborations between visual and literary artists. Open to local, national and international artists, including those who have already exhibited at RoCo, though submissions from Rochester artists may occasionally be given preference. The site is an urban park in downtown Rochester seen by 300 to 600 people a week. Proposals go by email as a single PDF with contact details, a CV, a project proposal of up to 500 words and the three captioned images. Artists are selected by October 15 and April 15.
