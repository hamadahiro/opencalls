Visual Arts — Sounding Like a Mountain

Five-week faculty-led thematic visual-arts residency (May 17 – June 18, 2027) built around acoustic ecology: how places sing, how memory persists in vibration, and how listening to the land can re-orient ethical and political relations. Participants explore how sound activates multiple temporalities shaped by land use, migration, ecological disruption and colonial infrastructures. Artists get individual studios, access to shared production facilities with technical staff, one-to-one faculty mentorship and studio visits, workshops and peer critique. Faculty are Xenia Benivolski, Elina Waage Mikalsen and Kathryn Yusoff. The program explicitly caters to artists working in photography alongside ceramics, digital media, painting, drawing, printmaking, papermaking, performance art, sculpture and installation. Open to individual artists or duos at any career stage, 18+, domestic and international; applicants need post-secondary training in visual arts or equivalent recognised experience. All participants receive the Banff Centre Arts Scholarship, which covers tuition plus a contribution toward accommodation and meals.
