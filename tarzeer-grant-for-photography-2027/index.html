TARZEER Grant for Photography 2027

Prizes PHP 300,000 Exhibition

First edition of a grant from Tarzeer Pictures, a Manila gallery and studio whose programme runs exhibitions, projections and a roster of Philippine image-makers. The grant supports the development of a new photographic project for exhibition, and selected recipients receive a total of PHP 300,000: a Project Fund of PHP 200,000 to produce the work during 2027, and an Exhibition Fund of PHP 100,000 to present it in a show at Tarzeer Pictures. The call is open to individuals working across all modes and forms of photography — new or ongoing projects that have not yet been exhibited, presented as still images, multimedia or mixed media. Applicants must be Filipino and 18 or over, at any stage of their career; students may apply as long as the project is not a school requirement, and Filipinos living abroad may apply and may make the work wherever they propose, with no requirement to produce it in the Philippines. One entry per applicant, and applying is free. The application portal opens 1 September 2026 and closes 15 October 2026 at 11:59pm Philippine Standard Time; everything goes in as a single PDF of 25MB or less containing a 150-word biography, a project proposal (cover, a 100–200 word project statement, a 250–500 word artist statement), up to fifteen pages of work samples, and a two-table funding plan itemising how the Project and Exhibition Funds would be spent. There are no restrictions on how the money is spent as long as it can be justified. Shortlisted applicants are invited to present their projects to the Selection Committee — Tarzeer Pictures plus a special jury — during November 2026, and recipients are announced in January 2027; the number of grantees is decided during selection. Images made with AI or machine learning are barred in any capacity. Recipients keep copyright, but grant Tarzeer exclusive rights of first exhibition within a year of the project's completion, including the sale of exhibited works on agreed terms, with the gallery's commission going back into funding future grants, plus non-exclusive non-commercial use for publicity and catalogues. The grant is framed as a partnership: recipients give regular production updates and take part in the gallery's public programmes during the exhibition.
