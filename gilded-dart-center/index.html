Gilded: A Juried National Exhibit of Artworks with Gilded Elements

d'Art Center's Main Gallery in Norfolk wants work in any medium whose gilded elements — a thin layer of gold, silver, palladium, aluminium or copper — are integral to the piece, shown 13 February to 13 March 2027. Open to artists 18 and over living in the United States, with work made in the last eight years, under 50 pounds and 60 inches. Awards are $500, $300 and $150.
