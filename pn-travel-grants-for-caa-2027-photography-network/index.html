PN Travel Grants for CAA 2027

Prize Travel grant up to $1,500

The Photography Network, a US non-profit for people working on the history, theory and practice of photography, funds members who are presenting at the College Art Association conference. CAA 2027 is in New York, and the grant covers transportation, lodging and conference registration: up to $500 from within 500 miles of the conference site, up to $1,000 from elsewhere in North America including Canada and Mexico, and up to $1,500 from outside North America. Awards are partial support, decided on a combination of merit and need with the stated goal of spreading the money among as many members as possible. This is a narrow door and worth reading before starting: an applicant has to already appear in the CAA 2027 programme as a session chair or presenter, be a current PN member, and be a graduate student, contingent or junior faculty, unaffiliated scholar, independent professional, or an employed professional without sufficient travel funding. Payment is made from a US bank by Zelle, wire transfer or check to an account in the applicant's own name, which the organisation ties to keeping its 501(c)(3) status. Membership is $25 for unaffiliated and student members, $50 affiliated and $125 sustaining, and the organisation offers free memberships on request to anyone facing financial hardship, with no proof of income required. The application is a short Google Form: the abstract of the paper or session, where the applicant is travelling from, a total CAA travel budget, and a statement of need. Winners are notified by 13 December 2026. One thing to note on the organiser's own page: the deadline is written as "Friday, December 6 at 5pm Eastern", but 6 December 2026 is a Sunday, so anyone close to the line should treat 5pm Eastern on Friday 4 December as the real cutoff and ask the co-chairs if in doubt.
