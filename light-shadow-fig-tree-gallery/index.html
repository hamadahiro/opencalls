Light/Shadow

Prize Exhibition

All-media juried exhibition at Fig Tree Gallery, the long-running artists' co-op in downtown Fresno, exploring the broad concept of light and its counterpart. 2–5 images of 2D or 3D work; photographic and digital work accepted printed on permanent media such as paper or canvas. Open to California artists 18+ who are not gallery members; work must be original, for sale, and made within the last three years. Exhibition September 3–27, 2026 with opening during Fresno's September 3 Art Hop; in-person delivery August 21–23. Juried by a five-artist panel; 30% commission on sales.
