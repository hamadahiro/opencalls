Art Squared 2026

Prize Exhibition

Metro Art Studios is a 501(c)(3) housed in a three-storey renovated corset factory in Bridgeport's South End, with 38 loft studios for professional musicians and visual artists and Crown Gallery on the ground floor. Art Squared is its annual fundraising exhibit, timed to open during the city-wide Bridgeport Art Trail, which brings close to 2,000 visitors through the building over the weekend. Every work is square - a minimum of 10 x 10 inches and a maximum of 14 x 14 inches including any frame - and is priced by the artist at $100, $200 or $300, with a 50% commission retained by Metro and 50% paid to the artist within 30 days of the show closing. All wall-hung fine art media are welcome and photography is named explicitly, alongside painting, printmaking, textiles and mixed media; work must be finished and able to hang on a single nail. There is no entry fee and each artist may submit up to three original works, one image per work. Entries must be entirely the entrant's own and made without an instructor's supervision; copies of existing artworks or work based on others' copyrighted art are not eligible. Open to artists aged 18 and over: the prospectus's eligibility section sets no geographic bar while its summary describes the show as an opportunity for artists of the greater Bridgeport region, and the binding practical constraint is that accepted work must be dropped off in person at Metro Art Studios on 21 October, 1 to 3 pm - the prospectus calls that a Saturday, but 21 October 2026 falls on a Wednesday, so confirm the day with the gallery before you plan the trip. Accepted artists are notified 13 October; the exhibition runs 7 November to 11 December 2026 and unsold work is collected on 12 December. Art is insured while at Metro, with liability for theft or damage limited to 50% of the sale price. Metro's own site currently carries last year's Art Squared entry form, so the CaFE prospectus is the live surface for the 2026 edition.
