Prix Yzza Slaoui de la Découverte

Prizes €2,000 Solo exhibition

The Rencontres de la Photographie de Marrakech has created a new prize in memory of Yzza Slaoui, aimed at revealing and supporting one Moroccan photographer whose work shows a personal vision and real artistic quality. The theme is free. The winning project earns a solo exhibition or an official projection inside the 2027 festival programme plus a €2,000 cash award decided by the international jury — and, importantly, the festival covers production, printing, framing, transport and installation separately, so none of that comes out of the €2,000. Entry is free and open exclusively to photographers of Moroccan nationality who own the rights to the images; the project must be unpublished in Morocco, or at least never have had a solo exhibition there. No age limit applies, though the prize is aimed first at emerging photographers. Applicants send a body of 15 to 30 photographs in a chosen, numbered sequence, at a resolution good for a 30 cm print, together with a two-page statement of intent, a CV, a ten-line biography and proof of Moroccan nationality. Dossiers may be written in French or English, and one project per photographer. A first selection is made by the festival's artistic committee before the international jury deliberates on 9 October 2026.
