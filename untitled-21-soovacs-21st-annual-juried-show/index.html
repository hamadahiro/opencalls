Untitled 21: SooVAC's 21st Annual Juried Show

The 21st edition of Untitled, the flagship annual juried exhibition of Soo Visual Arts Center — a Minneapolis nonprofit art space founded in 2001 that has shown more than 3,900 under-represented artists and states a focus on emerging artists and on work that challenges and expands conventional notions of aesthetics. Each year SooVAC invites guest jurors with local, national and international reputations to select work from open submissions; the guest jurors for Untitled 21 are Bo Young An and Robert Cozzolino. The gallery frames the series as a chance to work in a professional art space with influential art figures toward an exhibition with a highly regarded reputation, accepts all media from artists at any stage in their careers, and selects directly from the submitted work samples — so the work must be available for exhibition rather than proposed. The submission fee is $25 and the general submission form opens 4 November 2026. Both routes are live — the $25 general form and a fee-waived “Current SooVAC Donors Only” form — and both close Monday 7 December 2026 at 11:59 PM. The guidelines ask for exactly 5 work-sample images.
