Flatland Feature — Issue 2

Prize Online exhibition

Midwest Center for Photography (MWCP) is a US-based online photography platform that runs themed exhibitions and feature programs. Flatland Feature is the platform's online series-portfolio program — a slot for extended bodies of work rather than single-image group shows.

Selected photographers receive a six-month feature on the MWCP website, August 2026 through January 2027. Each feature consists of 10–20 images presented with an accompanying artist statement and biography — closer to a gallery solo than a group exhibition entry.

Visitors can view and purchase photographs through the feature page. MWCP takes 50% commission on sales; photographers retain rights.

Eligibility is open internationally, with no restriction on subject matter or genre. Submission is through MWCP's website. Fee is $100 for 10 images. The 2026 Issue 2 deadline was extended to July 31.
