Power Plant Grant 2026

Five $12,000 project grants for visual artists who live in Indiana, run by the Indianapolis non-profit Big Car Collaborative — the organisation behind the Contemporary Art Museum of Indianapolis — with funding from the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts' Regional Regranting Program. Photography is named outright among the eligible practices, alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, book art, ceramics, fiber, printmaking, digital and media works, film, video, performance art, sound art and social practice. The programme backs new, public-facing projects that happen in unexpected places, challenge mainstream methods of thinking, making or presenting, and build new kinds of audiences; it will not fund conventional private studio work, and equipment purchase, studio rent, travel or professional fees cannot be a proposal's sole or primary purpose. Applicants must be over 21 at the time of application and may not be full-time students; awarded artists should remain Indiana residents for the whole award period. Teams, partnerships and unincorporated individuals are eligible, non-profits and artist-run spaces are not, and previous recipients must wait two years. Eligibility recently widened from the Indianapolis area to the whole state, and Big Car's own venues cannot host a funded project. Applications close November 20, 2026 at 11:59pm EST; ten finalists give a virtual project presentation in the second week of December (the Submittable guidelines say Friday December 11; the programme timeline on powerplantgrant.org provisionally lists December 9), all applicants are told by email around December 15, and grantees are announced publicly on December 18, with the grant period running January to December 2027. Free to apply, with virtual info sessions through October.
