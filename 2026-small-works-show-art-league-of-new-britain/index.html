2026 Small Works Show

Prize Exhibition

The Art League of New Britain's annual holiday-season show of work made on an intimate scale, on view 28 November to 20 December 2026 with an opening reception on Saturday 28 November from 3 to 5 pm. This one is non-juried: every submitted artwork is accepted, there is no juror and the league lists its awards as none, so what is on offer is a month of wall space in a real gallery rather than a prize. Each artist is given a designated area that the ALNB gallery team hangs. All original media are accepted and the league runs its own annual photography juried exhibition, but digital art, including AI-generated imagery, is not eligible. The total combined unframed width of one artist's works may not exceed 14 inches; 2D work must arrive ready to hang on wire or D-rings, with no sawtooth hangers or easel-back frames, and wall reliefs are capped at 8 inches deep and 40 pounds. Every piece must be listed for sale at $35 or more and stay on display until the show closes, sold or not, though a sold work may be replaced for another entry fee. Open to artists 18 and over with original work made in the past three years that has not been shown at ALNB before. Up to five entries at $15 each, $10 for members, registered through the league's own online portal by 20 November at 11:59 pm; the league's record for the show also flags a gallery-sitting requirement.
