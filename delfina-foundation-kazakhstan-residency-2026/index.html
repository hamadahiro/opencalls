Delfina Foundation — Kazakhstan Residency 2026

Prize Residency prize

Delfina Foundation is a London-based contemporary art residency. The 2026 Kazakhstan Residency is fully funded with support from SÄR Foundation, launching a new five-year series of annual residencies for artists from Central Asia.

Eligibility is restricted to contemporary visual artists from Kazakhstan. The residency is three months; the 2026 cycle runs September 21 to December 11, 2026 (autumn season at the Delfina Foundation).

Application is free, made through Cognito Forms with the completed application form. Applicants are expected to read the Guidance Notes document before applying.

The 2026 deadline is June 7, 2026 at 23:59 BST.
