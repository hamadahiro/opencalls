Artist Submissions

Open, year-round submissions to Carter Burden Gallery, a Chelsea gallery at 548 West 28th Street devoted entirely to professional artists aged 60 and over. The gallery is a programme of the Carter Burden Network, a non-profit established in 1971 that promotes the well-being of seniors 60 and older, and it states its goal plainly: that older, lesser-known artists must not be overlooked due to age or decreased marketability in the current art scene. It focuses on NYC artists over 60 and describes its community as re-emerging professional artists, exhibiting work it calls vibrant, cutting-edge and important regardless of the artist's age. Submissions are accepted from professional older artists, aged 60 and older, currently living and working in the New York City metropolitan area full time, working in any medium - photography included - who are interested in exhibiting their work. There is no fee and no deadline: applicants email the Carter Burden Network submissions address with either a link to their website or 10-20 JPEGs representative of recent work (72dpi, no larger than 400-500kb), the date, medium, dimensions and pricing of all works, and a CV and bio, and must state the area they are based in and their age in the body of the email. The gallery reviews submissions three times a year, and it recommends visiting the gallery before submitting. It does not accept mail-in submissions or walk-in portfolio reviews. Separately from this rolling route, Carter Burden also runs nationally open juried exhibitions on its own schedule.
