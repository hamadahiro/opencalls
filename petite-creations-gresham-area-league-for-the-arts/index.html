Petite Creations

Prize Exhibition

The Gresham Area League for the Arts' winter exhibition, hung in the Gresham Council Chamber Visual Arts Gallery in Oregon over the peak holiday season and mirrored in GALA's online galleries. The brief is scale: two-dimensional work must measure no more than 9 by 9 inches including the frame and no less than 5 by 5, and three-dimensional work must fit inside a 9-inch cube. Within that constraint the league is open across media and names photography directly alongside painting, drawing, mixed media, digital art, sculpture, pottery, fibre art and other 3-D work. Artists may enter up to three pieces for a single $30 submission fee, and GALA retains 20% commission on sales. Preference goes to artists in the greater Gresham area, though the league may widen the pool depending on the number of submissions; in practice the binding constraint is that artists must live within 30 miles of Gresham, because accepted work has to be delivered to the gallery in person. Work featuring nudity or sexual content is not accepted. Entries close 28 October 2026 and the exhibition runs 23 November 2026 to 8 January 2027.
