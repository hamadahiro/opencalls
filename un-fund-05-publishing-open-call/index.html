un/fund 05 — Publishing Open Call

Fifth edition of un/fund, the open call run by un/produced — a non-profit production company and artist consultancy registered in Amsterdam and Paris — which has reinvested over €200,000 into un/signed and un/represented artists since 2022. This edition turns one photographer's body of work into a first photobook: the book is fully funded by un/produced and published in collaboration with Witty Books, the Turin independent press founded by Tommaso Parrillo in 2012, which works with artists through sequencing, rhythm, material and tactile form rather than handing back a finished template. The selected photographer receives complete financial support, so production costs are eliminated, plus professional guidance through every phase of the publishing journey, from image editing and sequencing to material design and distribution, and joins the un/fund community with promotion across the un/fund and un/produced networks. There is no set theme. Projects may be finished or still in progress and no book dummy is required; the organisers say explicitly that they welcome experimentation, critical thinking and a commitment to exploring underrepresented or overlooked narratives, and that no prior book-making or publishing experience is needed. Open internationally to photographers — including creative duos, where only one member applies on behalf of both — who have no ongoing gallery or agency representation, have up to seven years of professional practice and have not yet published a photobook, where a self-published zine does not count. The seven years are counted from your first exhibition, publication or paid commission rather than from when you first picked up a camera, and career breaks for parental leave, illness or caring responsibilities are not counted; showing in group exhibitions or selling prints yourself does not count as representation. One project per application, one application per photographer, up to 30 images with a project statement and a short biography, all materials in English. Entry is €10. Submissions opened 19 October 2026 and close 24 November 2026 at 23:59 GMT. Shortlisted applicants are contacted in December 2026 and the winner is announced shortly after. The organisers cannot give individual feedback on applications.
