Crocker Kingsley 2027

Prizes $2,000 Exhibition

A long-running national juried competition presented by Blue Line Arts in Roseville, California, open to all media including photography. Juried by Naz Cuguoğlu, curator of contemporary art at the Asian Art Museum in San Francisco. Selected works are shown across Blue Line Arts' four galleries (January 2–February 13, 2027), and a selection travels to the Crocker Art Museum in Sacramento. Cash awards total $5,500 across five places. Open to US residents 18 and over; original work completed in the last two years that has not previously been exhibited at Blue Line Arts. Entry is $45 for the first image plus $5 for each additional image (up to 10 more).
