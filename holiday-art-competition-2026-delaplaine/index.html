Holiday Art Competition 2026

Prizes $1,000 Exhibition

Juried competition and exhibition presented by the Delaplaine Arts Center with the Holiday Committee of Celebrate Frederick, on a secular theme of "The Holidays in Historic Frederick" — winter, downtown, snow and light, built around recognisable Frederick landmarks such as the Carillon, Schifferstadt, the Clustered Spires, Carroll Creek Linear Park and the streets around Market and Patrick. All two-dimensional work in any medium is accepted and photography is named outright alongside painting, drawing, hand-pulled prints, etchings, collage and digital painting; work must be original, unpublished commercially, and able to reproduce in black and white. Judging runs in two rounds — remote review of digital entries, then in-person judging after installation — and accepted work is announced 7 November. The Judges' Choice award is $1,000, with a Barbara Maghan People's Choice certificate voted at the reception and an optional Judges' Special Award. The reception is 2 December 2026, 5:30–7pm, and selected work stays on view in the Community Outreach Gallery 5–27 December. Entrants should note that the Holiday Committee retains exclusive commercial rights to the winning artwork for two years for poster production and five years for other promotional pieces. Accepted work is hand-delivered or shipped to Frederick, framed and wired ready to hang. Entry is $20 per piece.
