Open Call

South x Southeast Gallery's end-of-year open call is curated by Celso González-Falla, and it sets no theme at all: it is open to all photographers, in any genre, with the curator's eye as the only filter. González-Falla is a collector rather than a gallerist — president of the Sondra and Charles Gilman Jr. Foundation and of The Cintas Foundation, and a trustee and then chairman of the board of Aperture Foundation from 2005 to 2014. ARTnews listed him and his late wife Sondra Gilman among the top 25 photography collectors in 2004 and the top 10 in 2011; three books have been published on their collection, including 'Shared Vision' (MOCA, 2011) and Adam Weinberg's 'From the Heart: The Power of Photography' (Aperture, 1998). He has curated for this gallery before, most recently its Black + White call. Selected photographs are shown in the gallery's online exhibition through January and February 2027. Entry is by email: JPEG only, 72dpi, 1000 pixels on the longest side, each file named lastname-firstname-title (the gallery's own example is mccrarynancybigredhouse), all images zipped into one file and attached to an email with the subject line 'Open Call'. The fee is $40 for the first four images and $10 for each additional image, paid separately through the gallery's shop. Caution: the gallery's post carries two different closing dates. Its title — and the Latest News list on the gallery's own front page — read 'Deadline December 6, 2026', while a line inside the post body still reads 'Deadline December 15, 2025', a leftover from an earlier González-Falla call. The post is dated 7 November 2026 and the show runs in January and February 2027, so the December 2026 date applies. Confirm with the gallery before entering late in the window.
