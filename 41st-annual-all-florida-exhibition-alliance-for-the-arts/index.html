41st Annual All-Florida Exhibition

The Alliance for the Arts' flagship annual juried show, open to artists living anywhere in Florida and described by the organisation as "the most anticipated of the year" — work from across the 65,000 square miles of the state brought together as a portrait of contemporary Florida art. Photography is one of the named entry categories, alongside painting (oil, acrylic, collage, watercolour, mixed media), drawing, sculpture, ceramics, metalsmithing, glass, digital media, video, printmaking, fibre and textiles. An entry is one to three artworks: $25 for the first, $7 for each additional — though the CaFÉ checkout panel prices the extra works at $5, not $7, so budget for the organiser's higher figure and expect to be charged the platform's. Awards are $1,000 cash for Best in Show, a $300 Blick Art Materials gift certificate for second, $150 cash for third, and a Staff Choice award of a $75 artist membership. Only original work completed in the last two years and not previously shown at the Alliance is eligible; no giclée reproductions. Accepted 2D work must be wired and ready to hang and may not exceed six feet in any direction; 3D work is capped at six feet and 150 lbs. Artists pay their own shipping both ways. Every selected work must be offered for sale to the public at a price the artist sets — entries submitted not for sale are rejected — and the Alliance retains a 40% commission on works sold by non-members, 30% for members. The juror is not yet named. Notification by email on 9 February 2027, accepted work delivered 1 March, and the exhibition runs 5–27 March 2027 in the Alliance's Fort Myers galleries with an opening reception on 5 March. The Alliance for the Arts is a long-running non-profit arts centre on McGregor Boulevard with galleries, a theatre, an art house cinema, an outdoor ArtsPark and a weekly market; it also runs a standing, year-round call for curated-exhibition proposals in all media, photography explicitly among the themes it wants to explore. The cutoff has a time, stated twice: "Deadline for Entries: Sunday, January 24, 2027, by 11:59 pm" and, under Application Requirements, "All entries must be submitted online no later than January 24, 2027, by 11:59 pm. No materials will be accepted at the Alliance."
