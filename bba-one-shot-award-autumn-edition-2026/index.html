BBA One Shot Award - Autumn 2026

The Autumn edition of BBA Gallery's single-image photography prize: one photograph, in any photographic medium or genre, from any artist over 18, with no restriction on training or nationality. A shortlist of 20 images is professionally printed and hung at BBA Gallery in Berlin with all production costs covered by the gallery and no shipping cost to the photographer, while a longlist of 50 is shown on a large digital display in the same space. The exhibition falls in the last week of October and is a full-space joint show combining the winning entries of the Summer and Autumn editions. First prize is €300 in cash alongside the exhibition, an Artsy profile and promotion across BBA's social channels and newsletter. Entry is a single JPG, PNG or HEIC file up to 3MB - the low ceiling is for jury review only, with a print-ready file requested if you are shortlisted - and further artworks can be added at the checkout for €9 each. Photographers retain full copyright; selected work must be available exclusively for sale through the gallery for the run of the show and three months after, on a 50/50 commission split.
