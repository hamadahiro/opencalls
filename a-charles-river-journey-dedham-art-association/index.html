A Charles River Journey

Prizes Over $2,500 in cash awards Exhibition

Eighty artists, eighty miles: the Dedham Art Association and the Wellesley Society of Artists, working with the Charles River Watershed Association, are assembling an exhibition that follows the Charles River along its full course from Hopkinton to Boston. Artists are asked for visual impressions of the river — its meandering natural landscapes, the communities it connects, and its role as both a recreational and an ecological resource — with the flora, fauna, people and places reimagined as a record of the environmental and social narratives of the moment. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside painting, drawing, mixed media and collage, and printmaking; each artist submits one original two-dimensional work. Eighty works will be juried in and are eligible for over $2,500 in cash awards. The call is open to artists living in New England, emerging and established, as well as students enrolled at New England universities. Selected work is announced November 30, 2026, delivered in January, and shown at the Dorothy and Charles Mosesian Center for the Arts in Watertown, Massachusetts from January 22 to March 12, 2027, with an opening reception on January 28. One caution on the date: the ArtCall entry page's header field reads 'Entry Deadline: November 15, 2026 at end of day', while the same page's own IMPORTANT DATES bullet still says 'ARTCALL will be live for submissions from April 30, 2026 - November 1, 2026' — the two disagree by two weeks, so enter by November 1 if you can.
