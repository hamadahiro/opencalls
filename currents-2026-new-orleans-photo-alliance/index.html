CURRENTS 2026

Prize Exhibition at the Ogden Museum of Southern Art

CURRENTS is the annual PhotoNOLA showcase at the Ogden Museum of Southern Art, and the one exhibition the New Orleans Photo Alliance reserves for its own members: eight to ten portfolios chosen to give an overview of contemporary photographic practices and projects, with up to four images hung per selected photographer. Membership is the only gate and it can be bought at the point of entry, with the NOPA team verifying membership before jurying begins. Entry is $25 for members, or $60 for the submission plus a new NOPA membership bought at the point of entry. The 2026 juror is Bill Boling, a book publisher and the creator of Fall Line Press, who lives and works in Milledgeville, Georgia; he studied photography under Stephen Shore at Bard in the early 2000s and has exhibited at the New Museum in New York and the Window Gallery at the University of Auckland. Entrants submit between four and ten images, JPG, up to 5MB, sized to 1800 pixels on the short side. Photographers accepted into the immediately preceding CURRENTS are asked to sit this one out, and images shown in previous CURRENTS exhibitions cannot be resubmitted - though different images from a previously featured series are eligible. Selected work must arrive framed and ready to hang at the NOPA Center rather than the Ogden, and shipping is the artist's responsibility. The exhibition runs 4 December 2026 to 1 February 2027 during the 21st PhotoNOLA festival. PhotoNOLA's own page for this edition, photonola.org/currents-2026/, is live (posted 11 September 2026) and reprints the fee pair, the four-to-ten image range, the juror and the exhibition dates, so the org surface and the CaFE prospectus agree; the festival's /calls-for-entries/ index now returns 404.
