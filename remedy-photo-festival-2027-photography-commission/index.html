REMEDY Photo Festival 2027 — Photography Commission

REMEDY Photo Festival and Lincoln Museum & Usher Gallery invite photographers and artists working with photography to propose a new body of work exploring health, care, wellbeing and lived experience. The selected artist receives a £5,000 commission — a £3,500 artist fee plus a £1,500 production budget — to develop work for exhibition at REMEDY Photo Festival 2027 at a venue in Lincolnshire, with curatorial support from the REMEDY team and support from Lincoln Museum & Usher Gallery. The commission asks what role photography can play in changing how we see, understand and talk about health; proposals may explore illness and disability, care and caring relationships, identity and belonging, recovery and resilience, physical and mental wellbeing, and communities and connection. Outline concepts are welcome — the selected artist develops the proposal through research, engagement and collaboration during a November 2026 – May 2027 development period, and collaborative or socially engaged approaches are encouraged. Apply with up to 5 images (JPEG, 1000px longest side), a project statement of up to 300 words and a 100-word bio.
