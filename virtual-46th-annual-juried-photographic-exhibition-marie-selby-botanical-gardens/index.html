Virtual 46th Annual Juried Photographic Exhibition

Prize $5,000

Marie Selby Botanical Gardens has staged an annual juried photographic exhibition since 1980, making this the forty-sixth edition. The Gardens sit on Sarasota Bay in Florida and hold one of the world's major living collections of orchids, bromeliads and gesneriads across two campuses — Downtown Sarasota and Historic Spanish Point. The 2026 show is presented virtually, in partnership with the Observer Media Group: photographs are entered through an online portal, and accepted entries and winners are posted to both selby.org and yourobserver.com.

Five categories are open — Living Art, Make a Splash, Plant Portraits, The Birds and the Bees, and Black + White — and a juried panel selects on artistic merit, creativity and visual impact. Above the categories sits the Richard and Ellen Sandor Photography Prize, a $5,000 annual award for the best artistic representation of an orchid species, drawn from any category.

The eligibility rule is specific: images must have been taken within the past year at one of the two Selby Gardens campuses, must be original and owned by the entrant, and must not have been previously published for compensation. Volunteers cannot enter work made while serving. Files may not carry watermarks, words, graphics or clearly visible people.

Each photographer may enter up to three images. Entry is $45 for the first and $15 for each additional, or $30 and $10 for Selby Gardens members. The submission window opens at 10am on 3 August 2026 and closes at 5pm on 10 August. Awards are announced online on 27 August, and the exhibition is on view from 27 August through 30 September 2026.
