Berlendis Venice Art Prize 2027

Prizes €2,500 €1,000 5 exhibition prizes

The second edition of a Venice prize run by Kosmos Contemporary Venice APS with Marignana Arte, on the theme "Have We Crossed the Threshold? A Journey Beyond Borders, Identities and Languages" — crossing as an aesthetic, political and existential practice. Photography is explicitly named among the eligible media, alongside painting, drawing, printmaking and graphic arts, collage, sculpture and installation, textile art and video art. A jury of eight selects twenty finalists, ten in an Emergent section for artists aged 18 to 30 and ten in a Contemporary section for artists over 30, who show together at Spazio Berlendis — a 300-square-metre former boatyard carpentry workshop in Cannaregio that has hosted the Gwangju Biennale and currently houses the Grenada and Guatemala pavilions of the 2026 Biennale Arte. The finalists' exhibition runs 20 February to 21 March 2027 with a dedicated publication, and every finalist receives a free copy. Cash prizes are €1,000 gross to the Emergent winner and €2,500 gross to the Contemporary winner, with withholding tax of 25% for Italian residents and 30% for others. Five further special prizes offer exhibitions: two solo shows at Marignana Project in 2027 (one per section), a group show at Fondazione THE BANK ETS in Bassano del Grappa, a solo or group show at Galleria Alessandro Casciaro in Bolzano or Venice, and a solo or group show at Spazio Magazzino. Each artist submits one work, up to 200×200cm for two-dimensional pieces and 20kg maximum, with a 4-to-10-page PDF portfolio and one to three images. Works remain the property of the artists, but shipping both ways, customs and insurance are at the artist's expense. Finalists are announced by 10 December 2026.
