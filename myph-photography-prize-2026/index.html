MYPH Photography Prize 2026

Prizes Fujifilm camera 50,000 UAH tuition

The third annual, free prize from the MYPH school of conceptual and art photography, open to Ukrainian citizens living in Ukraine or abroad who have no more than five years of active practice with the photographic medium — which MYPH defines as conscious, regular, consistent work in art photography, from making one's own projects and series to exhibitions, competitions, residencies, portfolio reviews, publication and engagement with the artistic community. Fine art, conceptual and documentary photography are all invited, and every photographic technique and form of visual manipulation is accepted, from analogue shooting to digital collage and experiment. Applicants submit a series or project of eight to ten photographs made in 2025 or 2026; ongoing projects are welcome, and last year's winners may enter with new work. The Grand Prix takes a Fujifilm X-series camera and lens, a 50,000 UAH MYPH tuition certificate, membership of the Ukrainian Association of Professional Photographers, and showings at Art Market Budapest and Art Kyiv Fair; second and third places take MYPH tuition certificates of 25,000 and 10,000 UAH rather than prize money, and a separate Fujifilm 'Best Debut' prize goes to an author showing a series publicly for the first time. Eight finalists exhibit together at Dymchuk Gallery in Kyiv, with the show travelling in Ukraine and abroad as part of MYPH Photo Festival, and partner prizes include a 15,000 UAH cash award for one woman photographer, print production, photobooks and further tuition certificates. Applicants must be 18 at the time of applying. Finalists are announced on 13 October 2026 and the winners at the exhibition ceremony on 23 October.
