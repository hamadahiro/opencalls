The Crow Show 2027

Prizes $100 awards Exhibition Catalogue

The thirteenth annual national juried exhibition at The Studio Door, the gay-owned and operated gallery Patric Stillman founded in San Diego in 2014, celebrating the crow. The gallery's framing is that the crow has long been a symbol of mystery, transformation and intelligence across cultures, and artists are invited to explore its many faces, whether rooted in reality or soaring through fantasy: creative interpretations of black birds, ravens and other Corvus species are welcome. The call opened 15 November 2026 and the exhibition runs 19 February – 2 April 2027, with two public receptions, on 20 February and 27 March. Walkthrough videos of past editions are on the gallery's YouTube channel. Each artist may enter up to three works, at $35 for the first and $5 for each additional entry. Submission is through CaFE: one to three JPEGs, at least 1,200 pixels on the longest side and under 5 MB. The accepted media are painting, drawing, photography, printmaking, assemblage, collage, mixed media, fibre art, artist books and sculpture, with oversized jewellery that can be presented as fine art also eligible; performance art is not accepted, and video, film, installation or anything needing mains power requires gallery approval before submission. Framed two-dimensional work must use plexiglass rather than glass because of shipping and may not exceed 60 inches high by 40 inches wide including the frame; sculpture must fit through an average doorway and stay under eight feet. Glass and ceramic works are accepted only if hand-delivered. Patric Stillman is the sole juror; he served on the jury of the Oceanside Museum of Art's 2025 Biennial and writes a monthly arts column for The WORD San Diego. Three artists each receive a $100 Juror's Award for imaginative and compelling interpretations of the theme, judged on originality, execution, emotional impact and interpretation. The juror selects works for the physical gallery and further works for an online gallery, and applicants choose which they want to be considered for; online-only selections are equally eligible for Best in Show. The gallery calls this a national exhibition based in the United States, and because of shipping costs, tariffs and customs, international artists may take part in the online exhibition only, with international sales handled by the gallery. All selected work, in gallery or online, appears in the exhibition catalogue, available online and print-on-demand. Jury review runs 17–21 January with notification on 22 January 2027. All work must be for sale, with the gallery retaining 50 per cent and commission cheques mailed within 30 days of closing; artists cover shipping and supply prepaid return labels.
