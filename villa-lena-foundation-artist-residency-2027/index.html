Villa Lena Foundation — Artist Residency 2027

Residency program for artists of all disciplines in a 19th-century villa in Tuscany, Italy. Residencies run 4–5 weeks between April and October 2027. Includes studio space, accommodation, and half-board meals five days a week. Residency fee of €150/week covers room, studio, and meals. Artists must donate a completed work to the foundation's collection. Access to ceramic and wood workshops, screening room, and weekly excursions.
