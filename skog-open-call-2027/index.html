SKOG Open Call 2027

Prize Exhibition at SKOG in 2027

SKOG, a cross-disciplinary exhibition space in Oslo dedicated to progressive contemporary art, photography and collectible objects, invites proposals for its 2027 exhibition programme. Both solo and group exhibition proposals are accepted, from emerging and established artists worldwide at any career stage and in any medium or technique. Applications are free and reviewed by SKOG's curatorial team; many of its exhibitions are developed collaboratively, so selected artists may be invited to show alongside others in a curated group presentation. Applications ask for a portfolio, CV, project description, and the materials, techniques and dimensions of the proposed work, against the gallery floor plan and its 2,750 mm ceiling height, and may be written in English or in Norwegian, Swedish or Danish. SKOG is seeking grant funding towards transport costs and artist fees but says it is unlikely to cover travel or accommodation in Oslo, and will discuss costs with shortlisted artists before deciding.
