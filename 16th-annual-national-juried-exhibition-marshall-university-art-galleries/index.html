16th Annual National Juried Exhibition

Marshall University's School of Art & Design invites artists to its 16th Annual National Juried Exhibition, shown in the Charles W. and Norma C. Carroll Gallery in the Visual Arts Center in Huntington, West Virginia from 18 January to 25 February 2027, with an awards reception on 18 February. Artists may submit up to three works in any medium, so photography and lens-based work are eligible. The juror is Rachel Rearick, Executive Director of Contemporary Craft and an adjunct faculty member at Carnegie Mellon University teaching museum operations and public art; she is an artist member of the Associated Artists of Pittsburgh and the Pittsburgh Print Group. Entrants must be 18 or over and hold permanent residence in the United States, with exceptions for students and military personnel holding US resident status; work previously shown in a Marshall University gallery will not be considered. Entry is $30 for up to three works, or $15 for current Marshall students with ID, and everything is handled through the entry form on the gallery's own site. Jurying runs 3 to 14 November 2026 with email notifications on 18 November, and accepted work must reach the gallery between 7 and 11 December. All accepted pieces must arrive display-ready, and artists cover delivery and insurance in transit.
