Blank Wall Gallery — Best Portfolio 2027

Prize Solo exhibition

Blank Wall Gallery's annual international portfolio contest, run to find and promote a single photographer rather than a single strong image. The winner receives a two-week solo exhibition at the gallery's new premises in Athens, with Blank Wall producing and financing up to 40 prints (40x50cm and 50x70cm) in its own labs at no cost to the artist, and covering everything else needed to stage the show even if the photographer cannot attend. The gallery sends newsletter invitations to its full contact list, issues a personalised press release to magazines, presents the exhibition on its website alongside its other solo shows, and afterwards compiles a detailed folder of publications, installation photographs, posters and other material and sends it to the artist. The winner also receives a certificate of achievement and a free entry to the following year's competition. Two further portfolios receive certificates of honourable mention and the right to submit ten photographs to one of Blank Wall's group contests, and every entrant gets a 25% discount on prints made at the gallery lab through the end of 2027. Entry is open to amateurs and professionals over 18; each artist may submit up to three portfolios of 10 to 20 photographs each. Submission is by email, WeTransfer or Dropbox after paying the fee.
