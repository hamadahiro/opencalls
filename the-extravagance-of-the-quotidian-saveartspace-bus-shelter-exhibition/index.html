The Extravagance of the Quotidian — SaveArtSpace Bus Shelter Exhibition

Prize Bus shelter exhibition in Miami

Public art exhibition on bus shelter advertising space in Miami, FL, curated by Miami-based artist Hermes Berrío. The theme invites work that transforms the everyday — ordinary objects, domestic rituals, street corners, gestures, and fragments of daily life — into something undeniable, holding memory, identity, struggle, humor, and grace. Open to all mediums, including photography, and to artists of all ages. Selected works are displayed on Miami bus shelters for at least one month starting July 31, 2026. SaveArtSpace is a non-profit that has run public art billboard and transit exhibitions since 2015; fees are structured as tax-deductible donations supporting exhibition production.
