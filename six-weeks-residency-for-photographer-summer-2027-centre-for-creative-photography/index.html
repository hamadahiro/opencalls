Six Weeks Residency for Photographer, Summer 2027

Prizes Six-week summer residency Accommodation €300 grant Darkroom and digital facilities

The Centre for Creative Photography - Luovan valokuvauksen keskus ry, founded in Jyväskylä in October 1989 and an association of over 130 members as the Centre last counted them in 2022 - runs the photography side of Ratamo, the printmaking and photography centre housed in the city's former railway roundhouse, the Veturitallit. Ratamo's residency alternates year by year between a printmaker, hosted by the Jyväskylä Art Museum, and a photographer, hosted by the Centre; summer 2027 is the photographer's turn, and the call is for a six-week stay to be taken between 1 May and 31 August 2027 - the application asks which dates you want. The resident gets free accommodation in the centre's guest room in the city centre, a private room with shared kitchen and bathroom; free use of the computer, the digital equipment and the analogue and digital darkrooms; and a 300 euro grant toward living and material costs - so this is a facilities-and-time residency rather than a funded commission, and anyone applying from outside Finland should budget for travel and food. What makes it worth the application is the workspace: Ratamo keeps a working photographic darkroom alongside printmaking studios in one building, with Galleria Ratamo downstairs, where the Centre programmes six to eight photography exhibitions a year at three and a half weeks each, classes and workshops running through the space, and professional artists and experienced amateurs renting studio time, so a resident lands inside an active analogue and hybrid community rather than an empty studio. The Centre maintains active international contacts and lists the residency on Res Artis. The only thing asked of the resident is an optional artist talk. Applications go in a single PDF by email and must carry name, address, telephone, e-mail, nationality, website, date of birth, the residence dates you have in mind, a CV, a working plan and a minimum of six images with clear descriptions; the deadline is 31 January 2027. Neither the call page nor the Res Artis listing names a jury, a selection date or an AI policy, and neither sets an age, nationality or career-stage restriction.
