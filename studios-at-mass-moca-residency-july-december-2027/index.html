The Studios at MASS MoCA Residency — July–December 2027

The Studios at MASS MoCA runs year-round on the museum's North Adams campus, hosting up to ten artists at a time for stays of two or four weeks, and the general open call for residencies between July and December 2027 is live. The programme primarily seeks visual artists — the organisation's own list names painters, sculptors, installation artists, photographers, video and new-media artists, fiber artists and printmakers — alongside writers in all literary disciplines, and artists from around the world are encouraged to apply. There is no application fee, and an artist must be at least 18 to apply; the organisation states that age is otherwise not a consideration in selection. A residency carries a private furnished studio with 24/7 access, a private bedroom in a shared apartment across the street from the museum, one communal meal a day with the other residents, a studio visit with a MASS MoCA or guest curator, optional open studios and presentations, a year of priority access to Assets for Artists career workshops, and three months of museum membership covering free gallery entry and free admission to the nearby Clark Art Institute. Read the money carefully, because one application covers two very different outcomes. The Studios charges rent: the standard rate is $650 per week, already subsidised against a stated operating cost of over $1,000 per week. On the same general application you can ask to be considered for financial aid, where artists "typically pay between $0 – $450/week depending on their needs," and for one or more fully funded fellowships. The General Fellowship is the one with no strings: it "will offer full fellowships that do not have geographic or demographic limitations… regardless of discipline or location," and funds all residency fees for up to four weeks in residence. A Fellowship for Black or Indigenous Artists and Writers funds all residency fees for up to four weeks. A Family Fellowship gives three parent-artists a fully funded August 6–23, 2027 residency with a private three- or four-bedroom apartment, up to two studios with 24/7 access, free reserved places in Camp MASS MoCA for children in grades 2–9, and a $600 stipend. Further partner fellowships are tied to specific communities — the Speranza Foundation (Lincoln City Fellows), YoungArts alumni, Art+Everywhere (MFA graduates of the Art Institute of Boston, Lesley University Art+Design and Clark University), and MFA or BFA fellowships with Brooklyn College, the Corcoran School, Maine College of Art and Design, MassArt, MICA, RISD, SAIC, Tyler at Temple, University of Oregon, UT Austin, Virginia Commonwealth University and Yale. You apply by ticking the relevant box on the general application; there is no separate form, and every applicant must first be accepted through the regular jurying process before a fellowship can be awarded. The organisation asks you to indicate whether you also want to be considered for a regular, partially subsidised residency if no fellowship is awarded — so an unsuccessful fellowship applicant can still be offered a place that costs money. Two routes do NOT lead to a funded place: the Early Application, which gives 9–15 months of lead time but is explicitly for artists planning to fully fund their stay at the $650 weekly rate and is not considered for financial aid or fellowships; and the Alumni application, where returning artists are not eligible for fellowships and pay $400 per week for four weeks or $450 per week for two. Applications close November 1, 2026 at 11:59pm EST. Calls open four times a year, two months before each deadline, with general-application deadlines on May 1 and November 1. The organisation says most residents do not pay the full rate: "The majority of our residents attend with full or partial financial aid." Up to ten images may be uploaded, each under 2MB, or a single PDF of no more than ten pages covering no more than ten pieces; video samples run to ten minutes in total. Notification takes up to three months and comes through Submittable.
