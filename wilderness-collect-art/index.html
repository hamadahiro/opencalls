Wilderness

Collect Art, the Tbilisi-based independent art magazine, is calling for work for an online exhibition and special print edition on the theme of WILDERNESS — “not just a place on a map. It is a state of being — raw, untamed, unfiltered, and free from control.” The organiser wants “works exploring Wilderness in all its forms — from untamed landscapes, natural forces, forests, deserts, oceans and mountains to themes of human-nature relationships, ecological fragility and resilience, solitude, survival, freedom, animal instinct, the feral and primitive, inner wilderness, bodies as landscapes, mythological wilds, and the edge between civilization and chaos,” read “literal, symbolic, emotional, political or deeply personal.” Photography is named in the eligible-media list alongside painting, drawing, collage, installation, illustration, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, assemblage, fibre art, paper art, printmaking, 3D digital art, glass art and metalwork; the call is open to artists worldwide aged 18 and over. Selection buys a month-long online exhibition plus “a 2-6 page spread in both print and digital magazines” carrying a biography, artist statement, interview, website link and 2–10 artworks, a complimentary digital issue promoted on the magazine's channels, and a link to order the printed version; the curatorial team also picks “a few exceptional artists” for its book edition, “101 Contemporary Artists and More…”. THE COST, published in full: there is NO application fee, and a participation fee of €55 is payable only if your work is accepted — so applying costs nothing, and the €55 is what you pay to take the spread and the online show. Submissions go by email and want up to 12 images with each work's title, medium, size and year, a short biography, an artist statement of at least 100 words, and website and social links. No notification date is published. Five sibling calls from the same magazine are open alongside this one at three different prices — Winter Issue (22 November, €35), Reflection (23 November, €25), Darkness (27 November, €55), Solitude and State of the Art (both 28 November, €55 and €25) — and State of the Art is the one that promises its spread to “each participant” rather than to selected artists.
