Artsin Square Prize 2026

Prizes $1,000 cash Publication in Artsin Square magazine

First edition of the Artsin Square Prize, an international open call run by Artsin Square, the independent artist-run publisher behind the magazine, artist books and the 5×7 photobook series. One artist receives a $1,000 cash prize, a feature in the print edition of Artsin Square magazine, an interview published on the Artsin Square website and a permanent artist feature there, plus promotion through the newsletter and social channels; up to three shortlisted artists get a dedicated online presentation and social promotion, and the organiser states that no further fee is asked of the winner or the shortlisted artists. There is no set theme, so applicants submit work that best represents their current practice, and any visual medium is eligible — photography is named alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics, printmaking, textile, installation, collage, digital art and mixed media. Artists at all career stages may apply, collectives count as one applicant, and galleries or arts organisations may submit on an artist's behalf with permission. Submit 3 to 10 works as JPEGs no larger than 5 MB, uploaded through the application form; an artist biography of up to 250 words is optional. The entry form sells three fixed bundles — 3 images at $30, 5 at $35 and 10 at $40 — and its upload field asks applicants to choose 3, 5 or 10 images and upload the same number, so the stated 3-to-10 range is bought in those three steps. Applications are reviewed by the Artsin Square selection committee on artistic and conceptual strength, originality, quality and consistency of the work and the development of the artist's practice, with the committee's decision final. Finalists and the winner are announced in the first week of December 2026 and all applicants are told the result by email. Entry grants Artsin Square permission to use the submitted images in connection with the prize — in the print magazine, on the website, in interviews and through the newsletter and social media — if the artist is selected as winner or shortlisted.
