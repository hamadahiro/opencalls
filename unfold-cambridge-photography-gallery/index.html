UNFOLD — Cambridge Photography Gallery

Prize Exhibition

Cambridge Photography Gallery's first annual physical exhibition, bringing together artists invited from its previous online shows alongside new voices found through this open call. Rather than setting a narrow theme, the gallery asks how your photography unfolds — as a story, a memory, a place, a relationship, an identity or a way of seeing. Some projects reveal themselves slowly while others leave questions unanswered; some look outward, others inward. Singles and series are both welcome, there is no fixed limit on how many images you may submit, and there is no right or wrong interpretation. Selected works are printed and presented as part of the group exhibition in Cambridge, UK, running 9 to 11 October 2026, and each participating artist receives a dedicated post on the gallery's Instagram and a digital certificate of participation. Submission is by email and free; a £30 participation fee applies only if your work is selected. For the selection stage files can be any size, format or orientation; selected artists are then asked for high-resolution files suitable for printing, around 300 ppi and at least 3000 pixels on the longest side.
