2027 Getxophoto Open Call

Getxophoto is an international image festival held in Getxo, on the Basque coast near Bilbao, since 2007, and is organised by the non-profit Begihandi Kultur Elkartea. Its programme is built almost entirely of open-air installations in public space, which the festival describes as a defence of physical and online public space as a place of meeting, experimentation and celebration; it also welcomes projects that emerge from online spaces and approach digitality critically. The 21st edition runs 27 May to 20 June 2027 under the theme "Tide", set by curator Cale Garrido, who asks for work that treats photography not as a static object but as a practice in constant movement - images that circulate, change context, are archived and retrieved - and for projects that make visible what had remained hidden, unsettle what seemed fixed, transform memory, or sketch new forms of collective action. Proposals may use photography, video, actions, installation, digital art or any other visual practice, and may be submitted in English or Spanish. Three winning artists each receive an exhibition or installation produced at the festival's cost, a €500 artist fee, two nights' accommodation during the Opening Week, travel support of up to €150 for international artists (€100 for national ones) and media coverage. An international jury decides by mutual agreement on artistic criteria, conceptual development and fit with the theme, with gender parity and inclusion also taken into account. Each project needs 10 to 20 images and costs €22 to submit; up to three projects may be entered, each at the same fee. Applicants from countries affected by war, or living in refugee camps, who cannot pay the fee are invited to write to the organisers about alternatives. The shortlist is announced on 26 November 2026 and results on 30 November. Copyright stays with the artist throughout; entries are not accepted by email.
