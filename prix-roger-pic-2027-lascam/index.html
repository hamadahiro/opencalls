Prix Roger Pic 2027

Prizes €4,000 €1,240 exhibition rights Exhibition

LaScam — the Société civile des auteurs multimédia, the French authors' society for multimedia work — has run the Prix Roger Pic since 1993, named for the humanist photographer, director and copyright campaigner who instigated it, and awards it each year to the author of a photographic portfolio that documents the real and questions the human with singularity. This is the 35th edition, run in partnership with Fisheye magazine. The award is €4,000, and with it comes €1,240 (gross, or excluding tax) in droits de monstration — an exhibition fee paid to the winner for showing the awarded portfolio in LaScam's own gallery in Paris, where Vincent Gouriou's Les ombres claires, the 2026 winner, hangs from 5 October 2026 to 19 February 2027. LaScam states the prize is meant for photographers whose talent has not yet had the recognition it deserves, and the roll of laureates is the best argument for entering: Vincent Gouriou (2026), Véronique de Viguerie (2025), Corentin Fohlen (2024), Mário Macilau (2023), Baudouin Mouanda (2022), Alexis Vettoretti (2021), Sandra Reinflet (2020), Tomas van Houtryve and Denis Dailleux (2019), Laura El-Tantawy (2018), Michael Ackerman (2009), Christian Lutz (2011) and Martin Kollar (2005). Entry is free and there is no membership requirement — work is read from photographers who do not belong to LaScam — but the call is for professional photographers over 18, of French or any other nationality. The portfolio must be unpublished and must not have won any award (contest, festival, book, slideshow) before the date of registration; one portfolio per author per year, and a different one each year; only complete bodies of work are considered, never unfinished projects. Note the language condition, which is easy to miss and decides whether you can apply: accompanying texts must be written or translated into French. The online file is a portfolio of 20 photographs (JPG, 1–5 MB each, at least 2000 pixels on the long side, with IPTC title, description and photographer's name), a title and caption of up to 300 characters for each photograph, a 900-character summary plus a presentation and letter of intent (PDF, two A4 portrait pages maximum), and a biography of the author and any co-authors (same format). Two stages follow: the authors of LaScam's commission des images fixes draw up a pre-selection of ten portfolios, then a jury renewed every year — commission members, the president of the LaScam-Vélasquez association, and outside figures from photography, where possible including a previous laureate — picks the winner from those ten, with registrations processed December to February and the pre-selection and jury sitting March to April. Two conditions sit in the regulations rather than the call text, and registering online is what accepts them: photographs entirely generated by AI are not accepted, and every candidate grants LaScam free use of up to two of their photographs to promote the prize, worldwide for a year and tacitly renewed unless cancelled two months before the year ends. The deposit page runs 4–30 November 2026 and opens at 08:00 Paris time on the first day.
