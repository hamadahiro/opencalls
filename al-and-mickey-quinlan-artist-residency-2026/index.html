Al & Mickey Quinlan Artist Residency 2026

Eight-week residency at the Dome House — a twin-lobed concrete structure built into sand dunes on Lake Michigan adjacent to Whitefish Dunes State Park on Wisconsin's Door Peninsula. Open to one artist residing in the Midwest US (Wisconsin, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, South Dakota) working in drawing, painting, printmaking, photography or related fine-art mediums. 2026 residency runs August 2 – September 27. Semi-private loft living quarters and adjacent studio/workspace provided, fully furnished. Resident must provide a minimum of 5 hours/week of educational outreach (workshops, artist talks, demonstrations). Partnership between the Quinlan family and the Miller Art Museum.
