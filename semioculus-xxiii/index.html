Semioculus XXIII

Prize Publication

Semioculus is an independent international art zine and artist community originally founded in Tartu, Estonia, grown from a hobby project started by a group of college course mates into an international community and published quarterly in digital and limited print runs, dedicated to dark and surreal work from around the world — its own aesthetic keywords are dark, dreamlike, macabre, obscure and surreal. Submissions are open for the twenty-third issue, and the guidelines set no theme beyond that aesthetic: artists are free to send new pieces or older work, provided the publication rights still belong to them. Self-published work and images previously posted to an artist's own website or social media are fine. Any medium that can be published both digitally and in print is accepted as long as it fits the aesthetic; AI-generated work and fan art of any kind are not accepted. Visual art should be sent as JPG, PDF, TIFF or PNG at the highest resolution possible and no lower than 200 DPI, no more than six pieces at a time, with filenames formatted ArtistName_TitleOfWork; poems and flash fiction are also accepted, up to six A5 pages. Submissions are taken by email only, with the artist's name, titles, an optional short text tied to the work and a link to a website or social media in the body of the message. The zine is run as a passion project and cannot currently offer artists payment — it is free to enter, the original artist retains all rights to their work, and selected artists are contacted within one to two weeks of the deadline. Only artists aged 18 and up are accepted, as the zine occasionally features mature themes. Submissions close 16 August 2026.
