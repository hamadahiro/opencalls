BROAD Magazine — Dreams Issue

Independent visual-culture photography magazine publishing themed print issues alongside its website and social feeds. For the Dreams issue, BROAD is after visual interpretations of dreams in every sense — the subconscious, the surreal, the symbolic, the personal, the political, the spiritual — living in the body, the landscape, family histories, imagined futures, and the fragile space between reality and illusion. Poetic, documentary, conceptual, experimental, and deeply personal approaches all welcome. A one-time $30 CAD community membership (not a recurring subscription) unlocks the project page for unlimited submissions during the window; the magazine is community-supported with no government funding or sponsorships. Selected contributors are contacted within two weeks after the deadline.
