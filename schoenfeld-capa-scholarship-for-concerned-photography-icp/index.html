Schoenfeld-Capa Scholarship for Concerned Photography

Prize $12,500 full tuition scholarship

A full-tuition scholarship created in honour of ICP founder Cornell Capa's idea of Concerned Photography - a practice rooted in empathy, ethical storytelling and social engagement - and of longtime ICP board member Pat Schoenfeld. The award covers the entire $12,500 tuition for ICP's online One-Year Certificate in Documentary Practice: Visual Storytelling, and is open to all domestic and international applicants. Recipients are chosen by ICP's Scholarship Committee on the strength of the application and portfolio, with emphasis on clarity of vision and potential impact in documentary photography. Both the programme application and the scholarship form must be submitted by the 18 October 2026 deadline; the programme application itself carries a $60 fee, so a scholarship recipient's total cost is that fee alone. Scholarships and fellowships cannot be combined - an applicant may receive only one award - and applicants are automatically considered for ICP's merit-based awards through a section inside the programme application. Scholarships are only available to students enrolling in the One-Year Certificate programmes; part-time courses and Open Education workshops are not eligible. ICP is not an accredited institution, so the certificate does not qualify for federal student loans. Applications are made through SlideRoom; results are emailed with the admission decision.
