Thoughts of Summer

Prize Exhibition prize

The Southeast Center for Photography (SEC4P) is a Greenville, South Carolina photography gallery and education center that runs themed juried exhibitions on a regular schedule. Thoughts of Summer is the gallery's seasonal call — work engaging with summer as subject and feeling: family, friends, vacations, summer light.

Selection is by Lisa Woodward and Mia Dalglish, the co-curators of Pictura — the photography curatorial collaboration. Two-juror selection.

Selected work is shown in a physical group exhibition at SEC4P in Greenville. Eligibility is open globally.

Submission is through Smarter Entry with up to 25 images. Fee is $35. The 2026 deadline was April 19; results announced April 30, 2026.
