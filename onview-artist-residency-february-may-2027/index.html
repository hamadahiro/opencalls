ON::View Artist Residency — February–May 2027

Prizes Funded residency $500 stipend

A fully funded residency in a street-level storefront studio at ARTS Southeast in Savannah's Starland District, where large east-facing windows let the neighbourhood watch the work happen around the clock. The residency provides free use of the 12-by-12-foot studio, free lodging at the nearby 5th Dimension Apartment and a $500 stipend that may go to travel, materials or anything the resident chooses, for an artist or curator making new work, continuing a project or doing research. Residents are encouraged to give a public lecture, workshop or other interactive event, and must work in the studio during ARTS Southeast's public open hours at least one day a week. Open internationally and to collaborative teams; artists may work in any medium suitable for the space, and current or recent ARTS Southeast studio members are not eligible. Programme partners include the SCAD Museum of Art and Telfair Museums. The $50 application fee can be waived on request if it is an undue burden.
