Oddly Familiar — 2026 Members Exhibition

EBPCO's annual members' show, on the déjà-vu sensation of liminal space — 'places that look exactly like memories from your childhood, but you can't quite place where or when you saw them'. The brief asks for the unexpected angle rather than the grand view: the organisers frame it as 'less Ansel Adams and more Edward Weston. Less Martin Scorsese, more Quentin Tarantino. Less Steven Spielberg, more Wes Anderson.' Work must be photography-based — traditional, digital or alternative process — made in the last three to five years, not previously exhibited at Oakland Photo Workshop, and not made with generative AI. Framed dimensions cannot exceed 24 inches and accepted work arrives framed and ready to hang. Members may submit up to five images at no charge, and membership is open to anyone at $50 a year, $250 premium, or $25 for students and low-income artists; it must be active through December 4, 2026. Jurying is blind, by Kendra Luck and Kevin Flynn, and the collective typically accepts 30–50 pieces for the gallery plus another 10–20 for the online exhibition, with a catalogue for sale after opening. Submissions close September 28 at 11:59 pm PT, notification is October 11, artwork drop-off is mid-November, and the exhibition runs December 4 to January 17 at Oakland Photo Workshop, opening Friday December 4, 6–9 pm. A virtual office-hours session for submission help runs September 15.
