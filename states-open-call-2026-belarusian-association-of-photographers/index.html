States — Open Call 2026

Prize Exhibition

«States» is run by an independent curatorial team with informational support from the Belarusian Association of Photographers (BAP), which hosts the call on photounion.by; the page states plainly that BAP is not the project's organiser and is not responsible for it. The open call is framed by the organisers as "more than the emotions of one person — a frame, a canvas, a boundary and a portal at once". The call asks photographers to stop and consider how the world is refracted inside a person through what they live through, both literal and metaphorical, and to look at those inner worlds without black and white — in all their shades, colours, reflections and registers of perception. What interests the organisers is not only what a person feels but what brings them into that state, how it alters their perception of the world around them, and how that world is itself able to immerse a person in particular states. No genre, style or manner of interpreting the theme is prescribed: documentary story, portrait, autobiographical statement, observation of an environment, conceptual series or a visual investigation of a social phenomenon are all welcome, provided the work carries the author's own vision of a state and its connection to person and world. Entries go into one of two categories — Solo, a single self-standing image, or Series, four to ten photographs held together by one idea, theme or visual concept and read as a single statement. Work must have been made no earlier than 2024, files are JPEG at a minimum 4000 px on the long side and up to 5 MB, and a description of up to 300 words covering a short biography, the title, the work itself and its relation to the theme must accompany the application. Works created entirely with generative neural networks are not accepted. Entry is free and one person may submit a single application in one category. Selected authors become participants in the project's final exhibition, which opens on 26 September 2026 in Zhodishki, Belarus. Applications are sent by email with «Состояния» in the subject line; finalists are announced on 12 September 2026.
