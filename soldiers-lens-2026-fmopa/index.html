The Soldier's Lens 2026

FMoPA's second annual curated exhibition honoring military life, open to active-duty service members, veterans, and immediate family members. Free to submit up to 3 photographs capturing lived experience in and around military service; a panel versed in art and military service selects the show. Selected works are exhibited at FMoPA in Tampa November 5, 2026 – January 10, 2027, timed with Veterans Day, and featured in the museum's online gallery. All ages welcome (under-18s with parental consent).
