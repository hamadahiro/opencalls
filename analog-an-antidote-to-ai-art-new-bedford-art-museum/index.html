Analog: An Antidote to AI Art

Juried exhibition at the New Bedford Art Museum celebrating what algorithms cannot replicate: intuition, vulnerability, touch, chance, personal history, and the decisions embedded in the creative process. Rather than rejecting technology, the show asks what makes art unmistakably human, seeking work that reveals touch, struggle and experimentation. Open to all visual art media and genres including photography, with work created wholly or partially through generative AI expressly ineligible. Juried by Roddy Schrock, sound artist and former director of Eyebeam, New York's center for art and technology. Exhibition December 10, 2026 - March 21, 2027, with an opening reception December 10.
