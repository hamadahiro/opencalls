Bay Area Photography Open Exhibition 2026

Prizes Group exhibition prize Catalog prize

Second annual juried exhibition by The Image Flow (Mill Valley, CA) showcasing photographic artists in the extended San Francisco Bay Area. Open theme — all subject matter and media explorations eligible including digital, alternative processes, and analog. AI-generated images excluded. Restricted to photographers in nine Bay Area counties: Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma. Jurors: Ann Jastrab, Nelson Chan, and Nathan Lomas. Selected work shown in a group exhibition with reception at The Image Flow gallery; online showcasing; softcover catalog produced.
