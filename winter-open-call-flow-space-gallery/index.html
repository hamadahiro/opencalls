Winter Open Call

Flow Space Gallery, a Chelsea gallery at 526 W 26th Street, is taking submissions for its Winter open call, looking for emerging and mid-career artists who identify as women or queer for solo and group shows. Artists working in all mediums are encouraged to apply and photography is named explicitly in the presentation rules — works on paper and photographs must arrive framed and ready to hang. All work submitted is considered for future exhibition opportunities rather than one fixed show, so the gallery treats the call as an ongoing route into its programme. Applications go through a Google Form carrying a PDF of five to ten images with material, dimensions, year, title and price for each, plus an artist statement, bio and CV, and a screenshot of the $20 submission payment. The gallery states plainly that it is a small team and only accepted artists will be contacted, and that unsolicited applications outside this process are not accepted. Shipping work to the gallery is the artist's responsibility.
