Exhibition Proposals: Main and Nano Galleries

Prizes Gallery exhibition 60–70% of sales

The DC Arts Center accepts exhibition proposals and artwork submissions from artists and curators year-round, for solo and group exhibitions in either of its two galleries. The Main Gallery is approximately 700 square feet and takes full-size work in all mediums; the Nano Gallery is approximately 125 square feet for small-format work that can be easily hung. Artists may indicate a preference, but final placement rests with DCAC.

All DCAC exhibitions are curator-driven. An accepted application adds you to DCAC's roster of approved exhibiting artists, and the curatorial team reviews that roster when planning exhibitions. A curator may select a proposal as submitted, include pieces from your body of work in a group show, or pair you with a curator from DCAC's roster to develop the proposal; interest is followed by a studio visit. DCAC notes this stage is entirely curator-driven and may move quickly or slowly, with your work remaining under consideration either way. Applicants who are not accepted receive the committee's feedback and are encouraged to apply again.

The application asks for an artist statement and biography, an artist résumé, proposed exhibition details (title and description), an image or video list, and high-resolution images or videos that reflect the work as you intend it to be exhibited. The fee is $25, waived for Artist Members. Proposals are reviewed quarterly by DCAC's Visual Arts Committee against artistic quality, conceptual coherence, readiness for exhibition, growth potential, alignment with DCAC's mission, and marketability and appeal.

Artists retain a 60% commission on all work sold, or 70% for Artist Members. DCAC states that it is committed to supporting artists at all stages of their careers and across all disciplines, that it welcomes applications from all artists, and that it does not discriminate on any basis.
