Joshua Tree Highlands Artist Residency 2027–2028

JTHAR is an artist-centric nonprofit residency established in 2007 that each year invites six artists from around the world to work in the high desert near the west entrance of Joshua Tree National Park. Three seven-week residencies are on offer for 2027-2028: 4 May to 22 June 2027, 24 August to 12 October 2027, and 11 January to 29 February 2028 - though jthar.org's own summary labels that third window January to March 2028, a month wider than the prospectus, so confirm the winter dates if that is the session you want. Each resident is assigned their own house on five acres of panoramic desert, may work there or in the JTHAR studio - a large space divided by movable walls - and receives a $1,500 scholarship to defray the cost of taking part, spendable on materials, transport or meals. Photography is named outright in the accepted media alongside drawing, painting, sculpture, film, video, new media, installation, fiction and non-fiction writing, interdisciplinary work, social practice and architecture; musical submissions are not being accepted this cycle. Selection is by JTHAR board members and selected jurors on the merit of the portfolio and the residency proposal, with priority to proposals that anticipate transformations and discoveries inspired by place. Visual artists submit a minimum of five images and every applicant submits a project proposal. Three terms worth knowing before applying: residents are required to donate one completed work to the JTHAR program, artists must provide their own transport and need a vehicle for the stay, and the residency cannot accommodate partners, family members or pets - the seven weeks are attended in full, no exceptions. There is a mid-residency review with the board and the option of an open-studio exhibition at the end. Open to US and international artists aged 18 and over; the fee is non-refundable and JTHAR says it cannot waive it, as it funds the residency stipends. Applications open on 15 September 2026 and close 15 December 2026 at 11:59 PM Mountain Time, with selected artists announced on 28 February 2027.
