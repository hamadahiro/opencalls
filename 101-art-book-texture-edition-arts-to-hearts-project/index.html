101 Art Book: Texture Edition

Prizes $500 Publication 4-page feature

The fifteenth edition of the 101 Art Book, a global publishing project that brings 101 artists together around a single open theme in each volume. Texture Edition is for artists whose work is shaped by surface, material and the physical decisions of making — surfaces built by hand, found in the world, or created entirely through mark and process. Painting, drawing, photography, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, fibre, printmaking, collage, found objects, paper, digital media and installation are considered on the same terms; the organiser states a strong ceramic surface competes with a strong painted one, not against a quota, and that photographers who study bark, rust, stone, fabric and weathered walls are working directly with the theme. Rachel Burke, a Meanjin/Brisbane-based multidisciplinary artist and designer, is guest curator for this edition and reviews every submission alongside the Arts to Hearts editorial team. The book is a premium hardcover distributed through the Arts to Hearts Project Shop, Amazon and Barnes & Noble, with distribution expanding into independent bookstores. Every selected artist receives a dedicated two-page spread with their work and artist profile plus an Official Certificate of Recognition. From the 101, three artists receive additional recognition: the Winner takes a $500 cash award and a four-page feature, the two runners-up a two-page feature, each of the three with a glass trophy, certificate, Arts to Hearts promotional feature and Artsy artist listing. Open worldwide to individual artists aged 18 and above, emerging to established. Copyright never transfers to the organiser, there is no publication or page fee if selected, and every applicant receives personalised jury feedback and a Certificate of Participation. The entry fee is tiered by the number of works — $21 for 1, $35 for 3, $47 for 5, $62 for 10, $100 for 20 — and is non-refundable; an optional $22 add-on for a published virtual studio visit interview is offered at checkout. AI-generated artwork is not eligible, though digitally built texture is welcome where the artist made the surface. The call closes at 11:59pm local time on 15 October; results are announced within three weeks of the deadline.
