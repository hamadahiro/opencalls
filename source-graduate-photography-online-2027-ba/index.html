Source Graduate Photography Online 2027 — BA

Prizes Online exhibition Publication

Source's annual graduate showcase, running since 2007, opens its 2027 BA cycle. Open to any BA photography course in the UK or the Republic of Ireland; a year group books a place together (minimum five students) and a lead student uploads everyone's material and makes one payment. Each participating student gets their own permanent page on the Source website carrying up to eight images, a 120-word artist statement and a link to their own site — the magazine's point being that the work stays visible long after degree-show week, in front of a readership it describes as curators, picture editors and publishers. Three selectors then review everything submitted and pick favourites, which are published on the site and in the printed Graduate Photography Online supplement. The 2027 BA selectors are Jess Baxter, Assistant Curator of International Art at Tate Modern; Jean-Christophe Godet, founder and artistic director of the Guernsey Photography Festival; and Isaac Huxtable, Assistant Curator of Photography at the V&A. Submission is eight jpegs at 72 DPI, 1024px on the longest edge, sRGB — fewer than eight is allowed, moving image is not accepted, and each student picks one of six categories (Documentary/Photojournalism, Commercial/Fashion, Landscape, Portraiture, Staged/Constructed, Urban/Suburban Landscape). If the work is made for a gallery, Source recommends one of the eight be an installation shot. The £33 (UK) / €38 (Ireland) per-student fee includes a free one-year digital subscription to Source, which the magazine values at £44.99. The MA/MFA phase of the same programme has a separate, later deadline of 7 August 2027.
