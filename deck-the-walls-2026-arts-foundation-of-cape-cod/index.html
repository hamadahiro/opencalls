Deck the Walls 2026

Prizes Exhibition Up to 50% of sale price

The Arts Foundation of Cape Cod’s annual holiday art auction returns, with bidding online and in person and a buy-now option, and the foundation makes a point of opening it to artists near and far rather than keeping it regional. Photography is named among the welcome media, alongside acrylic, oil, watermedia, pastel, drawing, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, fibre, wood, metal, glass, ceramics and jewellery; work must be original and ready to hang. Unusually for a holiday show, there is no maximum size — the foundation says outright that this is not a typical small-works holiday show and pieces of any size are welcome — and its experience is that work priced between $300 and $2,000 sells well here. Submission is free and an artist may put forward up to three pieces, though in most cases only one is chosen; space is limited and the foundation’s committee retains full discretion over selection. Each artwork is submitted with up to three JPEGs, a main shot plus up to two details, at least 2000 pixels on the longest side and under 5 MB, since the images are used for the online sale. Selected artists are notified by email on November 13; work is dropped off on November 16 at the Sketch Pad on Pearl Street in Hyannis or shipped to arrive by November 23 at the artist’s expense with a prepaid return label. The public preview goes online November 20, the buy-now event runs November 27 to December 11, and an open house is held at the Sketch Pad on Saturday, December 5. The money terms are not on the call page — they sit in the Submittable guidelines, and they matter: the foundation asks artists to donate 100% of the sale proceeds, and states that “the option for the artist to receive 25% or 50% of the sale value is also available”, chosen on the submission form, so the most an artist can keep is half. Bidding opens at 60% of the value the artist states, with an optional reserve price below which the work will not sell — the foundation’s own worked example is a $1,000 artwork opening at $600, where an $800 sale on the 50% option returns $400 to the artist. Sales are confirmed by December 16 with cheques mailed within 30 days; unsold work is returned or donated to the foundation according to the artist’s stated preference, and the rest of the proceeds support its work across the region’s arts and cultural community.
