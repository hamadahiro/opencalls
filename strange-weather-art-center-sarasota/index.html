Strange Weather

Prizes $500 Exhibition

Art Center Sarasota's Atrium Gallery show for autumn 2026 celebrates the surreal, inviting artists into worlds where reality bends, logic dissolves and the unexpected becomes possible. 'Weather' here is a metaphor rather than a subject: submissions may read it as environmental, emotional or psychological, catching the moment when the familiar turns strange, and the call asks for dreamlike imagery, psychological landscapes, improbable juxtapositions, transformation and ambiguity. Submissions are welcome in all media including photography, and AI-generated art is not allowed. The juror is Danny Canton, a veteran of more than fifty years in the film industry who has worked as a director, producer and most often as a director of photography, and who studied cinematography at the School of Visual Arts before building the online film programme at Full Sail University. Jurying is done blind — the juror sees everything except the artist's name and the price — and the awards are chosen in person once the show is hung: $500 for first place, $300 for second, $200 for third, plus two merit awards and four honourable mentions. Up to three works may be entered; two-dimensional work may not exceed 72 by 65 inches framed. Note a date conflict on the organiser's own surfaces: the Submittable form's text says Sunday 6 September while the Art Center's website and the portal's own closing time both give Monday 7 September, so aim for 6 September to be safe. All work must be offered for sale — no NFS or price-on-request — and the artist receives 60% of the selling price as a member or 50% as a non-member. Artists are notified by 16 September, work is received in person on 29 and 30 September or by post by 30 September, and the show runs 8 October to 14 November 2026 with an opening reception on 8 October from 5 to 7 pm.
