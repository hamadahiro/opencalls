2026 Small Works Exhibition & Sale

Prizes Cash awards Exhibition

The Medford Arts Center, a community arts centre at 8 North Main Street in Medford, New Jersey, closes its 2026 exhibition year with Small Works, an all-media show of pieces twelve inches or under on every side, frame excluded. The organisers are unusually plain about how selection works and it is worth reading before entering: this show is "juried for Awards only" and "ALL works will be included in the online exhibit", so entry buys a place in the online show and in the printed brochure and YouTube video, while the jurying decides the prizes rather than who gets in. Artists who can hand-deliver also have the option of having their pieces displayed physically in the gallery in Medford. The juror is Gwynn Di Pilla and the show is chaired by Diane Emerson and Susan Barnes. Photography is named in the eligibility line itself - the call is "OPENED TO - Artists & Photographers, 18 years and older" - and the centre has run a dedicated photography exhibition, Looking Through the Lens, in the same year. Cash awards go to first, second and third place with three honourable mentions; the amounts are not published in the prospectus. Up to five pieces may be entered at 30 dollars for the first and 10 for each additional, or 20 and 10 for members of the centre, all non-refundable. Work must be original and conceived and signed by the artist, must not have been shown at the centre before, and must be family friendly. Every piece must be for sale: the centre takes a 30 per cent commission, collects sales tax, notifies the artist of a sale, and the artist then ships the sold work to the buyer, so entrants are told to price work to cover shipping and not to add extra shipping charges except for buyers outside their own country. Entry images should exclude frame and mat, be cropped to the edge of the image, 300 dpi and under 5 MB. The deadline is midnight on Monday 30 November 2026; the awards reception is on Friday 4 December from 6 to 8pm, awards and the exhibit go online on Saturday 5 December, and the exhibition runs to 7 January 2027. The centre states that work is handled with all possible care but that it will not be held responsible for loss or damage from any cause.
