2026 Looking Through the Lens Juried Exhibition

Prizes Cash awards Exhibition

An open photography exhibition at the Medford Arts Center in New Jersey, shown online and - for artists who can hand-deliver - in the gallery at 8 N. Main Street. The 2026 edition runs 2 October to 5 November, with an awards reception on Friday 2 October from 6 to 8pm. Bill Horin is Juror of Awards and the show is chaired by Diane Emerson and Susan Barnes. Entry is open to artists 18 and older, who may submit up to three pieces; the prospectus heads the show an Open Photography Exhibit and the Center's own listing says all media of that work are accepted, provided it is family friendly. Work must be original, conceived and signed by the artist, and must not have been shown at MAC before. This is juried for acceptance - not everything entered gets in, and only accepted work appears in the online exhibit. Accepted artists who can deliver in person on 30 September (2-4pm) or 1 October (4-6pm) also have the option of hanging in the gallery, framed and wired with no sawtooth hangers; all accepted work goes into a downloadable brochure, a YouTube video and the Center's online shop. Fees are $30 for one piece, $40 for two and $50 for three, with MAC members paying $20, $30 and $40. Awards are given by the Center's listing to first and second place plus three honorable mentions, and by the prospectus to first, second and third place plus three honorable mentions; no amount is published on either. All work must be for sale, the Center takes a 30% commission, and artists are responsible for shipping sold work to the buyer. Notification is by email on 29 September. The Medford Arts Center is a 501(c)(3) non-profit; this edition is funded in part by a grant from the Burlington County Board of Commissioners, a partner of the New Jersey State Council on the Arts.
