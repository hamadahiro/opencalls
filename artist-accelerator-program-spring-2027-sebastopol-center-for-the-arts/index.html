Artist Accelerator Program Spring 2027

SebArts’ Artist Accelerator is a free, three-month professional development cohort for Sonoma County artists, built to move a practice toward being sustainable as well as creative: artistic development, financial management, marketing and sales, social media, curatorial guidance in the art market, collector relationships, critiques and exhibition opportunities. New for this cycle, accepted artists also receive a $500 stipend. The Spring 2027 cohort runs April 1 to June 30, 2027, takes six to eight artists, and meets for a minimum of four hours a week — typically Tuesdays noon to 2pm and Thursdays 10am to noon, with additional events and some evening sessions — at the centre’s building on South High Street in Sebastopol, California. Applicants must be 18 or over, live in Sonoma County, and be available for the full term; artists at any career stage are encouraged, professional and non-professional alike, and all creative disciplines are welcome, so photographers are eligible. The application is free and asks for a short narrative on the artist’s background and focus, two to ten work samples, a résumé, and a signed letter of support or testimonial from someone familiar with the applicant’s work who can speak to its practice, relevance and community impact, including their name, title and contact details. The programme is sponsored by Creative Sonoma and the County of Sonoma; its workshop series is also offered free to the public by RSVP.
