Visage

Prize $1,000

An international call for works of portraiture at Manifest Gallery in Cincinnati — traditional and non-traditional approaches in any media, self-portraits or portraits of others, singular figures or groups, from the most traditional to the most conceptual or experimental. Selected works are exhibited November 6 – December 4, 2026, in the gallery's 23rd season. Every exhibiting artist receives a $50 cash honorarium, one work from each exhibition period wins the $1,000 Grand Jury Award, and artists receive 70% on sales. Submit up to 3 entries for $45, $5 per additional entry. Notifications by September 28.
