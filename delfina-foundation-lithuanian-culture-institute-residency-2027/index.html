Delfina Foundation — Lithuanian Culture Institute Residency 2027

Delfina Foundation and the Lithuanian Culture Institute fund a twelve-week London residency in the spring 2027 Collecting as Practice season, for artists whose work asks what gets kept, archived and remembered. The package covers flights, a single room, £40 a day and a £1,000 materials budget. Applicants must have been active in Lithuania's art scene for three years.
