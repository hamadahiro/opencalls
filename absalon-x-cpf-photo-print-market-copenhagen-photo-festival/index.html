Absalon x CPF Photo Print Market

Prize Exhibition stall

Copenhagen Photo Festival and Absalon, the Folkehus — community house — at Sønder Boulevard 73 in Vesterbro, are running their photo print and photobook market again on Sunday 22 November 2026, 10am to 3pm. The festival selects 22 to 24 participants from all submitted proposals, and the market is open to two kinds of applicant: photographers and artists exhibiting prints, and photobook sellers — publishers, vintage book dealers and others. Festival director Maja Dyrehauge Gregersen frames it as wanting to "celebrate what already exists" and, in the name of sustainability, to breathe life into the photo prints sitting in drawers, adding that the market now also opens its doors to photobook publishers showing work in book form. What you get is a table measuring 220 × 85 cm in the hall at Absalon, alongside other photographers, publishers and photo artists, and an audience that comes specifically to buy contemporary photography and photobooks. The festival encourages you to bring a tablecloth and stands to display work on and in front of the table, and to bring work in a range of price ranges. Setup runs from 8am with latest arrival at 9am, and you must deinstall before 4pm. Applying is free, via a form on the festival's own site, but it is a real application rather than a sign-up sheet: the required fields include a PDF with a selection of your prints, the number of prints or books you will bring, your price level, the sizes of your prints, your education and background, your reason for applying, your Instagram tag, and images and a logo the festival can use to promote the market; if you are selected the stall costs DKK 400, payable immediately on receiving the acceptance email, and that amount is non-refundable. One caution on the date. The festival's listing page says both "we are open for applications until 19 October 2026" and, lower down, "Deadline for applications: 20 October 2026" (its Danish line misspells the month). The application page itself states 19 October 2026 twice, in Danish and English. A third block, the form's own intro, still carries 20 February 2026 from the previous edition. Treat 19 October as the deadline. Note also that this is a market, not an exhibition or a juried prize: there is no fee paid to you, no award and no catalogue — you sell your own prints and books and keep what you sell, and you need to be able to get yourself and your work to Copenhagen for the day.
