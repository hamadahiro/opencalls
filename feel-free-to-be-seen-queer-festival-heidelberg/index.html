Feel Free To Be Seen

Queer Festival Heidelberg, a German non-profit running since 2009, invites queer photographers and visual artists to submit a solo or group project of 10 to 15 images on the theme. Professionals and amateurs are both welcome, entry is free, and selected projects are exhibited across Heidelberg during the festival month of May 2027.

DeadlineJanuary 6, 2027
PrizesExhibition, Online exhibition
Entry feeFree
EligibilityLGBTQ+
LocationHeidelberg, Germany
Requirements10–15 images
Submit viaWebsite
ResultsMarch 5, 2027

Jury: Christina Bauernfeind, performance artist, curator, art educator · Frederik Busch, media artist, photographer and university teacher · Margaux Corda, photographer, visual artist · Marius Emmerich, lgbttiq* coordinator, Office of Equal Opportunities Heidelberg · Dominic Hauser, founder of Queer Festival Heidelberg · Lydia Metral, photographer · Martin J.V Müller, founder of Queer Festival Heidelberg

Organized by Queer Festival Heidelberg
