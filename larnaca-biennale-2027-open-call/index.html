Larnaca Biennale 2027 Open Call

Fifth edition of the Larnaca Biennale in Cyprus, October 13 – November 26, 2027, under the theme Metatopies: Geographies in Motion — place as an event in constant transformation, shaped by geological, anthropological, social, political and historical shifts. Curated by Niki Papaspirou and Marios Fournaris; selection is by the Biennale's own six-member Jury Committee, whose decision the rules make final and none of whose members may compete. Open worldwide to artists, photographers, designers and art students 18+, individually or in groups; material, technique and medium are open. Thirteen awards at the opening ceremony, including the Grand Prix, three awards of excellence and six awards of distinction. €50 fee per proposal for individuals (€75 for duos, €100 for groups of 3–5); submit up to three images per work with an optional 200-word statement.
