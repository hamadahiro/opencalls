The Tom Stoddart Award in Collaboration with GOST Books

Prizes Photobook publication £5,000

Photobook award from the Ian Parry Foundation honoring photojournalist Tom Stoddart, run in collaboration with independent publisher GOST Books. Open worldwide to established photographers (18+) working in long-form documentary photography with an in-depth project nearing completion and suitable for a book. The recipient's project is published by GOST as a clothbound hardback with worldwide distribution, supported by a £5,000 grant paid in two installments toward expenses and travel to Verona to oversee printing, plus media coverage by The Guardian. Submit a biography, project description, and up to 40 representative images, with an optional PDF of book design. No AI-generated or digitally manipulated imagery.
