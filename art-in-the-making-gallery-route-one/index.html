Art In The Making

Prizes Exhibition Six juror awards

The annual juried exhibition of Gallery Route One, the 501(c)(3) non-profit in Point Reyes Station whose stated mission is to originate and present contemporary art exhibitions, educational programs and community outreach. The 2027 edition runs 9 January to 13 February, with a reception and awards on Saturday 9 January from 3 to 5pm. Mike Grady, a Bay Area painter, writer and educator, is the juror, and will select six artists for awards. Jurying is blind and done from the digital images submitted; any contact with the juror by an applicant during the process automatically disqualifies the artist. All media are accepted except video, crafts and installation, and no work may exceed 48 inches in any direction. Entry is $20 per artwork, up to a maximum of four entries, and eligibility is limited to artists currently living in Northern California. Accepted work must be clean, framed, wired and ready to hang or place on a base, and labelled on the back with the artist's details, title, medium, dimensions and price. Work is hand-delivered on 3 or 4 January or shipped at the artist's expense in a returnable two-way carton — work packed in styrofoam or not prepaid for return will not be accepted — and the gallery does not insure work in the gallery or in transit. Work is for sale unless marked NFS, with a 45% commission to be included in the price and a note on whether the frame is included. Notification is emailed in the first week of December 2026.
