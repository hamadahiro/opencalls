RBSA Friends Exhibition 2027

Prize Exhibition

Annual exhibition giving RBSA Friends — artists at any stage of practice — a wall in the RBSA Gallery in Birmingham, one of the oldest art societies in the country, which was among the first to give its subscribers their own show. This edition spreads across both Gallery 1 and Gallery 2 and runs Thursday 7 January to Saturday 6 February 2027, with a preview evening on the opening night. Artists working in any media, including photography, may submit; selection is by digital image of the original artwork and every entry goes through the same anonymous process, judged by Honorary Curator Simon Conolly RBSA, Friends Coordinator Charles Weston RBSA and Linda Nevill RBSA. Friends may enter up to three 2D wall-hung works or up to four 3D plinth or freestanding works, with four works the maximum in any mix. Entry itself is free, but the call is only open to artists holding an active RBSA Friends subscription, which can be bought on the RBSA site at £47 a year (£42 by Direct Debit, £120 for three years, £190 for five) and is not itself selected for — and selected artists then pay a display fee of £15 for 2D works under 60cm or £23 over 60cm, due by 10 December and halved for artists aged 35 and under. Photographers should read the entry requirements closely: work must have been made in the past three years and must not have been shown at the RBSA Gallery before, reproductions of original works such as giclee prints are not accepted, every print including digital ones must be editioned with the edition size stated, and images created using AI are not accepted. All work must be for sale for the full duration of the exhibition, with the Gallery taking 35% commission plus VAT on anything sold, and up to six Highly Commended certificates are awarded. International artists may enter but carry their own customs paperwork and charges, and work that has to be couriered back incurs a £20 re-packaging charge. A catalogue of all selected works goes on sale through the run and into the RBSA Archive, and the Gallery runs events during the exhibition for Friends who want to apply to become an RBSA Associate. Entry deadline Tuesday 10 November, 4.00pm.
