World Sports Photography Awards 2027

The World Sports Photography Awards, sponsored by Canon, are open for their 2027 edition, free of charge and open to photographers of all ages from any country - the organisers expect mainly professional and semi-professional sports photographers, with some amateurs. Entrants may submit up to ten single images made between December 2025 and November 2026, placed across 24 sporting categories, with no limit on how many categories one photographer enters. Each category produces a top ten - Gold, Silver, Bronze and seven Special Merits - and an overall top three is chosen from the category Gold winners. The organisers say prizes are provided by the awards' sponsors but publish no schedule of them, and this cycle is run entirely online with no in-person awards. Judging is by a panel of ninety named senior figures from sport, media, brands and photography, recused from any category where they have a conflict of interest; they include Tony Duffy, the photographer who founded Allsport, Lee Martin of Getty Images, Justin Feck of The Daily Mail, Andrés Ballesteros of the EFE agency and Jamie Schwaberow of NCAA Photos. The manipulation rules are strict and worth reading before entering: no multiple exposures, polyptychs or stitched panoramas made in software (in-camera versions are accepted), no adding, rearranging, reversing, distorting or removing anything within the frame, cropping and the removal of sensor dust or scan scratches only, and no staging or recreation of events without disclosure in the caption. All entries must be created with a camera; synthetic or AI-generated images are barred and generative fill brings automatic disqualification. Entrants keep copyright and grant a non-exclusive, irrevocable licence for uses connected with the awards, with no payment due for them. Entries opened on 9 September 2026, a shortlist follows in December and the winners are announced on 14 January 2027.
