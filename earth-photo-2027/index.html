Earth Photo 2027

Prizes £1,000 Exhibitions Residency

Earth Photo is the free international platform for photographers and filmmakers who work on the natural world, its peoples and environments: "From the effects of climate change and biodiversity loss to powerful acts of restoration, resilience and hope, Earth Photo sheds light on the stories behind the images." Established in 2018 by Parker Harris with the Royal Geographical Society and Forestry England, and organised in partnership with Photoworks, it is open to artists and image-makers worldwide working in any lens-based media. Each entrant may submit up to ten works, still or moving - short films between five seconds and five minutes - with a CV up to 500 words, a 100-word artist statement and a caption or statement for each work. Work should have been made in the last five years, though earlier images may be included as part of a long-term documentary project, archive or vernacular series. The Earth Photo team longlists the most compelling submissions for a jury of industry experts chaired by Louise Fedotov-Clements, Director of Photoworks, with the shortlist then considered for exhibition and awards; final decisions rest with the Earth Photo team and its exhibition and award partners. The awards are substantial and various: the Earth Photo Award 2027 (£1,000), the Climate of Change Award (£500), the Moving Image Award (£500), a two-week Sidney Nolan Trust residency at The Rodd with access to Nolan's photographic archives for a UK-based practitioner, two David Wolf Kaye Future Potential Awards of £1,000 plus mentoring for practitioners aged 25 or under, a £400 Photoworks digital residency, mentoring from the New Scientist editorial team with a possible feature in its Aperture section, and a QUAD feature exhibition. Shortlisted and winning work shows at the Royal Geographical Society in London from 5 July to 24 August 2027, with the prize giving and launch on 15 July, and then tours UK venues - the 2026 edition travelled to National Trust properties, Belvoir Castle, Chester Zoo, Heligan Gardens and Castle Ward among others - with publication in the RGS magazine Geographical and RGS membership for the award winners. The cutoff is not end of day: the KEY DATES block reads "Online submission deadline 3 February 2027, 5pm GMT". Entrants are notified on 11 March 2027; high-res files are due 25 March.
