Vision(ary) 2026: Raising Our Voices

Prize Exhibition

Vision(ary) is the Griffin Museum of Photography's annual outdoor public art exhibition. The exhibition transforms downtown Winchester, Massachusetts into an open-air gallery — selected work is installed in public spaces around the town from June through September 2026.

The 2026 theme is Raising Our Voices: advocacy and social justice. The brief asks photographers to submit complete projects rather than single images that respond to the theme.

Selection is by Elizabeth Krist, independent visual editor formerly with National Geographic.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through CaFÉ with 5–10 images of a complete project. Fee is $45. The 2026 deadline was April 5; results announced after May 1, 2026.
