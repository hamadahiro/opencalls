Photoville Festival 2027 — Call for Proposals

Prizes Exhibition Payment

The 16th annual Photoville returns to New York for "three weeks of free public art, exhibitions, and community programming in Brooklyn Bridge Park and across all five boroughs", and the call is as wide as the festival: "We are looking for ALL types of photographic and lens-based work. We welcome everything from documentary and photojournalism, to fine art, sports, and conceptual work. Alternative processes such as photo collage, tintypes, and cyanotypes are all welcome too!" The festival wants "global narratives, local histories, studio portraiture, street photography, collaborative archives, community-driven projects, and interdisciplinary approaches" and work on "immigration, healthcare, education, racial inequality, climate change, social justice, incarceration, women's rights, youth and intergenerational stories, police reform, housing, joy". Because the shows stand in public parks, it asks you to "avoid images that may be shocking — particularly images of nudity, sexual intercourse, drug use, or violence". There are three applications — two for exhibitions (open-air structures sited across the five boroughs, and the festival's signature shipping containers in Brooklyn Bridge Park) and one for public programming, a talk, workshop or interactive experience; you may submit more than one proposal, and Photoville steers unsponsored artists to the open-air route because container slots are few and costly. The economics are the point: "Independent artists selected for the festival do not pay exhibition costs. Photoville covers all design, production and installation expenses to ensure equitable participation." For a selected artist or artist-led collective with no institutional support or sponsorship, Photoville states it will "cover the cost of printing, exhibition structures, installation, public liability insurance, publicity, and will pay a modest artist honorarium", the amount varying with the number of artists supported and the scope of the project. Artists, non-profits and commercial entities that DO arrive with sponsorship are asked for a contribution instead, assessed per proposal and quoted at $5,000–$40,000 for open-air and $12,000–$40,000 for a container. Eligibility is deliberately unrestricted: "All photo-based artists (and collectives, organizations, and companies representing this work) are welcome to apply! There is no limit or prerequisite regarding artist age, professional qualifications, or time period the work was made in." Selected artists get a one-to-one virtual meeting with the Photoville team and then work with it to produce the exhibition. Photoville is supported in part by public funds from the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs with the City Council, with Brooklyn Bridge Park and NYC Parks as host partners.
