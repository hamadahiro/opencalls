Creative Catalyst Fellowship 2027

Prize 8 × $30,000

The AAPI Civic Engagement Fund gives up to eight artists $30,000 each to make work that engages the public on issues facing Asian American and Pacific Islander communities. The 2027 theme is Belonging, which the Fund defines as a sense of community, home, agency and the essential right for communities to flourish - a counterweight to racism and violence, and a way of recognising how AAPI communities have shaped this country. Photography is named outright among the funded forms, alongside printmaking, illustration and modular installations, with digital and media-based work, written and narrative forms, and hybrid or interdisciplinary projects also eligible; the Fund says it is drawn to work that is portable and reproducible, and cannot fund feature-length film, fully staged theatre, dance, event-based work needing rehearsal infrastructure, or projects that depend on large-scale or fixed installations. As a nonpartisan nonprofit it also cannot fund work advocating for or against a candidate. It is a yearlong programme running January to December 2027: fellows attend a virtual orientation, a required two-day in-person retreat on February 16-18, 2027 with travel and accommodation covered, virtual check-ins, and submit a narrative report, with finished artwork due August 15, 2027 and promotion running to December. Beyond the money, the Fund provides a communications consultant, digital advertising, introductions to AAPI organisations, help placing the work in galleries and venues experienced with social-justice art, and production of posters, books and other replicas. Artists keep ownership; the Fund takes the right to reproduce the work for its charitable activities. Applying is free, through a Google Form, with up to five work samples in a single PDF. Open to individual artists 18 or over based in the United States - organisations are not eligible and international applicants cannot apply, though US citizenship is not required. Two optional information webinars run on September 1 and September 4, 2026.
