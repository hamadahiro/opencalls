Feria de Fotografía y Afines 2026

The editorial fair at the third Freezer International Photography Festival in La Plata, Argentina, held under the edition's theme Lo real. Photographers, collectives, publishers, small presses and photography-adjacent projects are invited to bring fanzines, prints, photobooks and photographic objects to a two-day market at the Edificio Karakachoff's Espacio Dickinson on Friday November 13 and Sunday November 15, with free public entry. The festival frames it as “un gesto de comunidad” — a place to make projects visible and build networks beyond the screen. Stands are a 90×90 cm table with two chairs at ARS 50,000 a day, a self-supplied clothes rail at ARS 40,000, or both at ARS 65,000; stands for the UNLP university photography programme are free, and payment is by local transfer or PayPal from abroad. Applications close September 14 or when places run out, and selected projects are announced September 18.
