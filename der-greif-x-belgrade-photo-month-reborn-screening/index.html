Der Greif x Belgrade Photo Month: Reborn Screening

Der Greif and Belgrade Photo Month want lens-based work on being reborn: transformation alongside continuity in personal identities, collective histories, shifting landscapes or changing societies. Selected participants are showcased in the festival's main screening event in Belgrade on 9 May 2027. Up to 15 images per project, and the €15 donation is optional.
