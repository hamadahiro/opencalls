Parallel Voices 2027

Prize Exhibition

Parallel Voices is the portfolio track of the Photometria International Photography Festival in Ioannina, Greece, sitting alongside the festival's single-image Photometria Awards and its Photobook Open Call. The festival is run by Entefxis, a civic non-profit organisation based in Ioannina, and the festival's own page lists earlier editions of this contest in 2018-19, 2020 and 2021.

Unlike the festival's single-image award, there is no set theme: entrants submit fifteen photographs on a subject of their own choosing, and the festival asks for work that holds together as an idea open to multiple perspectives or narrates a story. The jury selects eight images from each portfolio it takes forward.

Nine portfolios are selected and shown as a group exhibition titled "Parallel Voices" at the 2027 festival, at a venue the festival chooses. Photometria pays for production — the selected pictures are printed on canvas and installed outdoors, and the show runs for about two months. What then becomes of those prints is stated two different ways by the organiser: the call page says the printed portfolios are kept in the festival archive, which holds the right to manage and re-exhibit them, while the submission prospectus says the prints are made for this exhibition only, will be destroyed when it ends, will not be shipped or returned, and that any re-exhibition needs the photographer's prior agreement. Copyright stays with the photographer on both surfaces.

The jury is internal: Panagiotis Papoutsis and Achilleas Tziakos, the festival's founders, and Panagiotis Pappas, its artistic director.

Entry costs €20 per portfolio and is non-refundable; it is paid as a product on the festival's own photobookstore, and only applications that have both completed payment and sent the portfolio are eligible. Files go by WeTransfer, SwissTransfer or FromSmash. Image files must be JPG, 1500 pixels on the longest side at 300 ppi, named first name-last name-image number. The same transfer must carry a PDF with the photographer's portrait, project title, contact details, a bio of up to 150 words, a project description of up to 150 words and, optionally, an artist statement of up to 300 words. Submissions are open from 1 November 2026 and close 15 December 2026 at 11.59 pm; results are announced by 15 February 2027. There is no cash prize or artist fee, and selected photographers who take up the discounted accommodation pay for it themselves.
