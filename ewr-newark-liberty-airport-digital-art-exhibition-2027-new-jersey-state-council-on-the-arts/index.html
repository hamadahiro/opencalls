EWR Newark Liberty Airport Digital Art Exhibition 2027

The Art of New Jersey is the annual digital exhibition inside the public art programme at Newark Liberty International Airport's new Terminal A, shown on the digital welcome banner spanning the departures area and rotated through large digital display units across the terminal for about a year. The Port Authority of New York and New Jersey's Art Advisory Council reviews submissions and selects roughly twenty artists; each selected artist receives a $1,000 stipend for the use of their work and retains copyright, and names and images are circulated through the Port Authority's, MANJ's and the State Arts Council's channels and a supporting website. Photography is explicitly in scope: the call asks for completed, two-dimensional, still work in digital form, either high-quality digital images of paintings, drawings, prints and photographs or work born digital, including digital photographs. Moving image, performance, new designs, tourism-style posters and sketches are not considered, and AI-generated work is excluded. Artists submit up to two previously completed works plus a short bio, a statement of any connection to Newark or Elizabeth, and a brief description of each piece; selected artists supply a PNG or JPG 1080 pixels high and are paid by direct bank deposit. Free to enter. Open to artists and artist teams 18 or older who are current residents of New Jersey — every team member must qualify — with priority to artists living in Newark and Elizabeth, where the airport sits; artists selected for the 2026 exhibition and submissions from artists' estates are not eligible. Notifications in spring 2027, exhibition opening in summer 2027.
