Women's Studio Workshop — Artist's Book Residency Grant

Prize $350/week stipend

An eight- to ten-week residency for artists to produce a limited-edition book work (edition of 50–100), printing and binding their own books in WSW's studios with technical support. The grant includes a weekly stipend of $350, up to $1,000 for materials, up to $250 for US travel, free on-site housing and 24/7 studio access. Completed books enter collections at over 450 institutions worldwide, including the Library of Congress. Applications open August 1, 2026; the residency takes place the following September–June. Relevant to photographers working with the photobook and artist-book form. If the artist chooses to sell the edition through WSW, WSW takes a 30 percent commission on each book sold.
