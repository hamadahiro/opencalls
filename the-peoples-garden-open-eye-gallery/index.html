The People's Garden

Rolling open call from Open Eye Gallery in Liverpool, gathering the public's own garden pictures for The People's Garden, a growing display within the gallery's exhibition Is This A Garden? (26 June – 31 August 2026). The gallery welcomes family album photographs as readily as recent pictures — a family gathering, a football game, a visiting puppy, your own garden or a public one. Selected images are printed and added to the exhibition as they arrive, so earlier submissions hang for longer. Send up to three digital images at the highest resolution possible, 30MB maximum each, by email with your name, postcode, an optional text of up to 100 words about the images, and a statement confirming the images are your own and you have the right to show them publicly. No AI-generated or digitally manipulated images. Open to anyone over 18 currently based in the UK; one submission per person.
