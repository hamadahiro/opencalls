Paralaje Magazine — No. 2: Fine Art Black & White

Prize Publication in print & digital

Paralaje is an independent contemporary photography magazine published in Spanish with English translation, in print and digital editions, directed and edited by George Riverón; its name borrows the astronomy term parallax for the idea that no image is absolute and everything changes with the point from which you look. After a first issue built from an open call, the second call is dedicated to black-and-white fine art photography: series in which light, shadow, form, the body, space, memory, intimacy or abstraction come together into a visual language of their own. The editors say they are not after a particular aesthetic or a closed definition of fine art, but want personal, coherent projects that can sustain a point of view across a series, and they will judge on the visual strength of the images, the coherence of the series and the distinctiveness of the approach. Any photographic process qualifies - digital, film, alternative processes or experimental techniques - and the call is open to photographers of any country, background, age or level of experience. A submission is a series of 4 to 8 black-and-white photographs with a title, a short statement, a short biography, city and country of residence, and a website or Instagram if you have one, sent through a Google Form linked from the call page. Selected series appear in Paralaje No. 2, dated October 2026, in both the print and digital editions, with the magazine reserving a final editorial pick of which images from each chosen series are printed. There is no submission fee, no payment or free copy is mentioned, and the deadline is 30 September 2026.
