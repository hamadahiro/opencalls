Shape 2027

Prizes Exhibition Publication

Shape 2027 is one of Glasgow Gallery of Photography's themed open calls — the gallery runs a roughly monthly schedule of themed group exhibitions across the year. The gallery's framing: "Along with capturing light and shadow, photography is a great way to capture and explore shape. Whether man made or found in nature, shapes can be found all around us and with the right composition can really make a photograph." It points to the silhouette, the architecture of a building and the geography around us as starting points, and asks to see how photographers explore the theme in their own work.

The exhibition runs January 7 through 30, 2027 in the gallery's Glasgow space, sharing the January programme with Elements 2027. The submission model is free to apply, pay-only-if-selected — a £50 participation fee covers printing and installation, and the page states a discount is available to anyone selected for both exhibitions in the same month. The frames used belong to the gallery, and prints are presented at 16"x12", 18"x12" or 12"x12" depending on the image.

Selected work also appears in an online gallery on the gallery's website and is included in the exhibition catalogue. Participants receive a digital certificate of participation before the show opens; their work is photographed in situ once installed, with those images sent to them directly and shared on the gallery's social media, and filmed for the gallery's social channels. Selected work is also automatically put forward for the gallery's Photographer of the Month.

Each photographer submits up to 8 JPEGs at 300ppi and minimum 3,000 pixels on the shortest side, named Firstname-Surname, sent in a single batch via Smash, WeTransfer or Dropbox to the gallery's submissions address rather than as email attachments. The deadline is September 18, 2026, with acceptance notification by September 23 and payment due October 7; only selected photographers are notified.
