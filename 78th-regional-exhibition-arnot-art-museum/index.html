78th Regional Exhibition

Prizes $10,000 Exhibition

For over seventy-five years the Arnot Art Museum's Regional Exhibition has gathered artists from across the Northeast and Eastern Canada into a museum setting, and the 78th edition is open now. Photography is named outright in the museum's own media list alongside painting and drawing, sculpture, graphics, clay, textiles, jewelry, wood and metal work, and mixed media. The Museum keeps a primary focus on representational art but explicitly welcomes non-objective work for the Regionals, so the door is open to conceptual and abstract lens-based practice too. Up to $10,000 in cash awards is distributed among the juror selections, and selected artists receive two complimentary tickets to the Gala Reception and Awards Ceremony on 19 December. Work is shown in the Museum's West Wing Galleries from 29 September to 19 December 2026. Entry is open to artists 18 and over living in Connecticut, Delaware, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island or Vermont, or in Ontario, Canada; work must be no more than five years old and not previously shown at the Museum. Maximum two entries per artist, nothing over 96 inches or 150 pounds, and a 40% commission applies to sales made during the exhibition.
