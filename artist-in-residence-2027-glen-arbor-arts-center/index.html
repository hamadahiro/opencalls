Artist in Residence 2027

A two-week residency on the edge of the Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore in northern Michigan, offered several times a year by the Glen Arbor Arts Center to give visiting artists a respite from daily responsibilities and concentrated time to work. Photography is named explicitly among the disciplines considered, along with creative writing, visual art, sculpture, fibre arts, ceramics and music. There is no residency fee — housing and studio space are provided free — and the only cost is a non-refundable $35 application fee. The housing has been relocated for this round to a new space on the arts centre's own campus in Glen Arbor: furnished accommodation with a private bedroom and en-suite half bath plus a shared living room, kitchen and full bathroom, with bed linens and towels provided and a local host for each resident. The residence is a second-floor walk-up, not wheelchair accessible — applicants must be able to climb stairs unassisted — is smoke-free, allows no pets, and art materials may not be used in it. The studio is a restored tool shed at historic Thoreson Farm inside the national lakeshore, 3.7 miles from the arts centre, a rustic seasonal building with a cement floor, overhead lighting, space heaters and some shared equipment, divided with the centre's ceramics program. Each residency is a one-person opportunity — groups and collaborative teams are not considered — and residents commit to the full fourteen days, give a community presentation on the second Thursday of their stay, and may be asked, but not required, to donate one work made during the residency to the centre's permanent collection. A car is effectively necessary: Glen Arbor is walkable but the studio is out at the farm and Traverse City is 26 miles away. This round covers the 2027 season, May to October, though July and early August are generally unavailable because the apartment space is in use. Visual artists submit six to eight images plus a resume; applications are reviewed straight after the deadline with notification by mid-March. Anyone selected for a Glen Arbor residency in the past three years is not eligible. The cutoff is not end of day: the organiser states "Deadline for 2027 application submission: November 5, 2026 at 2:00 pm".
