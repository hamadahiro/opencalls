Artist in Residence 2027

Prize 2-week residency

A two-week residency on the edge of the Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore in northern Michigan, offered several times a year by the Glen Arbor Arts Center to give visiting artists a respite from daily responsibilities and concentrated time to work. Photography is named explicitly among the disciplines considered, along with creative writing, visual art, sculpture, fibre arts, ceramics and music. There is no residency fee — housing and studio space are provided free — and the only cost is a non-refundable $35 application fee. Residents stay in a furnished second-floor walk-up apartment with a bedroom, living room, small bath and galley kitchen a short walk from a grocery and restaurants, and bring their own linens and towels; the apartment is not wheelchair accessible, is smoke-free, allows no pets, and art materials may not be used in it. The studio is a restored tool shed at historic Thoreson Farm inside the national lakeshore, 3.7 miles from the arts centre, a rustic seasonal building with a cement floor, overhead lighting, space heaters and some shared equipment. Each residency is a one-person opportunity — groups and collaborative teams are not considered — and residents commit to the full fourteen days, give a community presentation on the second Thursday of their stay, and may be asked, but not required, to donate one work made during the residency to the centre's permanent collection. A car is effectively necessary: Glen Arbor is walkable but the studio is out at the farm and Traverse City is 26 miles away. This round covers the 2027 season, May to October, though July and early August are generally unavailable because the apartment is in use. Visual artists submit six to eight images plus a resume; applications are reviewed straight after the deadline with notification by mid-March. Anyone selected for a Glen Arbor residency in the past three years is not eligible.
