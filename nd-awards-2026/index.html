ND Awards 2026

Prize $3,500 prize

ND Awards is an annual international photography competition associated with ND Magazine. The 2026 edition runs across multiple categories: Architecture, Editorial, Fine Art, Nature, People, and Special — covering most major commercial and editorial photography genres.

Total prize pool is $3,500 across categories. Selected work is featured on the ND Awards platform and promoted through ND Magazine's channels.

The 2026 jury is large — over 25 photographers, photojournalists, professors, and interdisciplinary artists from multiple countries. Notable jurors include Lydia Panas, Mahe Elipe, Valery Poshtarov, Oded Wagenstein, and photojournalist Rui Caria.

Eligibility is open globally to photographers 18+, professional and non-professional. Submission is through the ND Awards website with 1–5 images per entry. Fee is tiered $19–$29 by submission scale. The 2026 deadline was April 26; results announced November 22, 2026.
