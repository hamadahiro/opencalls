River Margin — Issue 1

Prize Publication in Issue 1

River Margin is a new Australian and international literary journal founded in 2026 by the Melbourne-based visual artist, musician and writer Lana Eileen, and it is assembling its first issue. The focus is ecology, botany and wilderness: the journal was created as a space for overlooked aspects of the natural world — tiny wild-growing plants, threatened rivers, things hidden in the undergrowth. The name refers to the riparian zone, the natural border between water and land, and the editorial position follows from it: River Margin is a space where the liminal, the edge-dwelling, the peripheral and the unnoticed can find a voice. Photographers can submit up to seven images that relate in some way to ecology, botany, the environment, conservation or wilderness areas — rivers and bodies of water, insects, wildlife, fungi, plants, forests, mountains. Both digital and film photography are accepted; AI-generated work is not, and for visual art more broadly the journal excludes digital art as well. Files go by a file-sharing service such as WeTransfer to the journal's submissions address. Submissions are open worldwide and free, and the editors aim to respond within two weeks. The journal is candid that as a new publication compiling its first issue it is not in a position to pay contributors, so the return is publication in Issue 1 alongside poetry, essays, short fiction and creative non-fiction on the same themes. Submissions for the first issue close on 1 November 2026.
