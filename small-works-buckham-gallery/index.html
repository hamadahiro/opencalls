Small Works

Buckham Gallery in Flint, Michigan - the exhibiting arm of Buckham Fine Arts Project, a non-profit founded in 1984 - is taking entries for Small Works, a national juried exhibition built around the idea that a small picture holds a viewer differently from a large one. The gallery puts it plainly: large artworks dominate and attract the art world's attention through instant impact, while smaller works are powerful in a quieter way, drawing viewers in for a close, intimate experience. It is a national open call for visual art in any traditional or non-traditional media, two-dimensional or three-dimensional, with all subjects, themes, media and approaches considered, so photography is eligible alongside everything else. The one hard constraint is size: no original work may be larger than 12 inches in any dimension, and mats and frames must stay inside that 12-inch limit too. Unusually for a fee-charging open call, the gallery pays the artist rather than only charging them. Every artist selected for the exhibition receives a 50 US dollar stipend towards shipping costs, which the gallery openly acknowledges may not cover the full cost of sending work and having it returned. Entry costs 25 dollars for one to three works, paid by card or PayPal through a link that arrives in the confirmation message after the form is submitted, and the fee is non-refundable; members of the Buckham Arts Collective may enter free of charge, though they do not receive the shipping stipend. Entrants must be 18 or over and resident in the USA. All work must be original in concept, and the gallery excludes both reproductions and AI-created work. Applications go through a Google form linked from the gallery's own call page by 21 November 2026, and every entrant is told the outcome by 5 December 2026; selected artists then complete a consignment agreement and must get their work to the gallery between 16 and 22 December 2026, handling packing, shipping and return shipping, or dropping off and collecting in person. The exhibition itself runs 8 to 30 January 2027 at the gallery in Flint: the submission form states that the Small Works exhibition will be held January 8 - January 30, 2027, and the gallery's published 2026-2027 season carries an unnamed National Juried Exhibition in that same 8 to 30 January slot. One sentence on the call page is stale and should be read as such - it places an Abstraction exhibition at 9 to 30 January 2027, while Abstraction is in fact the juried show hanging now, 13 November to 19 December 2026, on the gallery's own front page and in its published season; the deadline, the notification date and the delivery window are identical on every surface. Selection is by blind review from the digital files, judged on whether the work brings perspectives not already shown in the area, is made to professional standards and makes a unique statement in its medium. The gallery's jury and presentation guidelines also bind selected work: it must arrive ready to hang with D-ring or eye-hook hardware and braided picture wire rated to the weight, no sawtooth or clip hangers, works on paper matted under glass or plexiglas unless the exhibition committee approves another approach, and anything not claimed within 30 days of the exhibition's end becomes the property of Buckham Gallery. Stipend cheques are posted once the work has arrived in good condition, within five business days of the opening. Work may be offered for sale but does not have to be; artists set their own retail prices, and the gallery takes a 35 per cent commission on anything sold, leaving 65 per cent to the artist, and covers card processing fees out of its own share. Buckham's funders include the Charles Stewart Mott Foundation, the Michigan Arts and Culture Council, the National Endowment for the Arts and the Community Foundation of Greater Flint.
