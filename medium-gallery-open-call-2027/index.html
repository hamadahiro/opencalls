Medium Gallery Open Call 2027

Prize Exhibition

Medium Gallery was founded on 8 January 1991 as part of the Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Bratislava, and its programme spans every form of contemporary art the school teaches — photography, painting, intermedia, graphic design, sculpture, applied arts, architecture, design, restoration, textiles and visual communication. For 2027 it is again inviting exhibition projects from students of art fields, beginning and established artists, and curatorial projects, from Slovakia and abroad; the gallery board deliberately balances student presentations against shows by established local and international artists. The 173-square-metre space can be partitioned, a practice begun by former gallery head Jana Kapelová in 2018, so applicants choose one of three configurations: SMALL, the back room, best for compact solo shows; MEDIUM, the two front rooms, for a solo or smaller group show; or LARGE, the whole gallery. Proposals are assessed by a five-person board of three school representatives, the gallery manager and one external member. The gallery covers the space and its running costs, invigilation, technical equipment, plinths and basic installation supplies, transport of works by agreement, graphic design and a print run of posters in the gallery's visual identity, press and social media, photographic documentation of the opening and installation views, the opening itself, and partial help installing; artists cover opening refreshments, their own installation labour, any equipment the gallery cannot supply, and returning the space to its prior state. Applications are free and go by email to the gallery, with a short and detailed project description, chosen space, CVs, an artist portfolio as a single PDF, a preferred exhibition date, an installation plan and any accompanying programme. Final bachelor's and master's theses are not eligible. The deadline is Sunday 16 August 2026.
