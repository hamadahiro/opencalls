Photography of Place

Prizes $10,000 in awards Exhibition

International juried photography exhibition at the Robert A. Peck Gallery, a 6,000-square-foot teaching gallery at Central Wyoming College in Riverton. The call seeks images shot in Wyoming, North Dakota, South Dakota, or Montana that capture unexpected aspects of the region with innovation, aesthetic finesse, and thought-provoking narrative — explicitly avoiding typical visitor-guide imagery. Open to artists worldwide; all photographic formats accepted. There is no entry fee and the gallery takes no commission on sales, fully insuring exhibited work. Juried by Lonnie Slack, Nita Kehoe, and George Kahrl. $10,000 in awards. Exhibition September 11 – October 16, 2026.
