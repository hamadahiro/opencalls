31st Annual NO DEAD ARTISTS International Juried Exhibition

Prize Solo exhibition at Ferrara Showman Gallery

Ferrara Showman Gallery on Julia Street in the Arts District of New Orleans is taking submissions for the 31st annual edition of NO DEAD ARTISTS, its international juried exhibition of contemporary art. The gallery tells the show's history on its own call page: Jonathan Ferrara founded it in 1995 "to give a voice to emerging artists", the name coming from "the old adage that artists never achieve success until they are dead" — a notion the show turns on its head, and one it has often made good on by giving emerging artists their first break. It was a New Orleans call in the 1990s, grew to cover Louisiana, became a national juried exhibition in 2010, and in 2014 Matthew Weldon Showman opened it internationally. The gallery says the exhibition has led to national press coverage, gallery representation, art fair exhibition and acquisitions by museums and other prominent collections. Three arts professionals jury each edition — the panel changes every year and the 2027 jury is not yet named, though past jurors include Prospect.1 founder Dan Cameron, New Orleans Museum of Art director Susan Taylor, former Andy Warhol Museum director Eric Shiner, MacArthur Fellow John Scott, Ballroom Marfa co-founder Fairfax Dorn and collector Beth Rudin DeWoody. The grand prize is a solo exhibition at the gallery the following year for one jury-winning artist, which the entry record dates to 2028. Photographers are in on an explicit affirmative rather than on silence: the gallery's own entry record states "Living artists worldwide, at any stage of their careers may apply. All media is accepted." Entries are up to ten artworks — images or video, and detail, installation or contextual shots count toward the ten — for a $40 fee per submission, and the two surfaces disagree on how many bites you get: the gallery's own instructions allot each entrant ten images or videos in total, while the entry record sets a maximum of three submissions per artist, each charged its own $40. Read the money terms before you enter: every submitted work must be available for sale through the exhibition, selected work carries the gallery's standard 50% sales commission, and the artist pays all shipping, including return shipping of unsold work and any import/export duties and taxes. There are no size limits beyond the gallery itself — walls up to 19 feet, ceiling 11.5 feet. Selected work must reach the gallery by 7 September 2027; the show opens to the public on 11 September, has its reception on 2 October during Arts District New Orleans' First Saturday openings, and closes 23 October 2027.
