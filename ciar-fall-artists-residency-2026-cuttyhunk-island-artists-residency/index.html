CIAR Fall Artists' Residency 2026

Prize 8-day island residency prize

Eight-day residency on Cuttyhunk Island off the Massachusetts coast, September 9–16, 2026. Open to visual artists at all career stages working across drawing, painting, collage, small sculpture, photography, and video. Artists must have a portable studio practice suitable for the island setting. Programming includes communal meals prepared by resident chefs, complimentary golf carts to roam the island, and conversations with visiting artists Claudia Keep and Randall Exon. Housing at the historic Avalon Inn (linens and towels provided); base cost $1,950 (triple) to $3,600 (single) plus ferry travel. Multiple full and partial fellowships available — the new Environmental Artist Fellowship, the Open Merit Fellowship, the Paul Cuffe Fellowship for an artist of color, the Wellspring Fellowship for parent artists, and work-study options — covering up to the full residency cost.
