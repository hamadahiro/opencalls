Pearl Press — Solo Issue Open Call

Pearl Press is an independent online publication, 28 issues deep, that normally runs themed group issues. The next one is different: the whole issue goes to a single photographer, or to a collaboration between two. The call asks for 20 to 30 pieces with a one-page bio and a description of the work, and sets no theme at all — 'there is no concept to adhere to', so the brief is simply a body of work you are proud of. The selected artist is interviewed inside the issue (both photographers, where two apply together) and promoted on the publication's Instagram. Submission is free and by email, with visual work at a minimum of 300 dpi; more than 30 pieces may be sent, though only part of what arrives will be used. The publication was founded by Delilah Twersky, a fine-arts photographer and creative writer who works primarily with medium-format film and whose own work has been published by Dashwood Books, Waif Magazine, Aint-Bad and Sissy Screens; Pearl Press itself has been interviewed by Chill Subs.
