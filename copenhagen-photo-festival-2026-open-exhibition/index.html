Copenhagen Photo Festival 2026 Open Exhibition

Annual open exhibition at Copenhagen Photo Festival 2026 on the theme 'Forestil dig...?' (Imagine if...?). 50 selected works printed, framed, and exhibited at the Royal Theatre (Det Kongelige Teater) from 11–17 June 2026. Non-selected submissions shown digitally during the festival (11–21 June 2026). Submit one square (1:1) JPG at 300 dpi, printable at 30×30 cm. Prizes: 4,000 DKK / 2,000 DKK / 1,000 DKK CEWE printing vouchers. Selected photographers get free festival access and reception invitation. Open internationally to photographers 18+.
