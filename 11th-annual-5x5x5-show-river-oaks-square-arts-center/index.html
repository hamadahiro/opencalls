11th Annual 5x5x5 Show

Prizes $500 / $100 / $100 / $100 Exhibition

River Oaks Square Arts Center's small art spectacle returns for an eleventh year, gathering miniature work from artists across the United States into a signature end-of-year show that doubles as the Alexandria arts centre's holiday buying event. Nothing may be larger than five by five inches or twenty-five square inches, and three-dimensional work has to stay inside the same envelope at five inches high, wide and deep. The call is open to all media, 2D and 3D, but originals only: reproductions are barred, though etchings and woodcuts count as originals in their own right. Each artist may enter up to three works for a single $15 fee, one image per piece, and all entered work must have been completed within the past year. Framing is capped at eleven by fourteen inches and wire is the preferred hanging hardware. The guest juror is Tyler Barnes, a second-generation artist from Alexandria who took his BFA at Northwestern State University and his MFA at Texas A&M University-Corpus Christi and is now Associate Professor of Art at the University of Mississippi. Awards are $500 for Best in Show, $100 for the Beck Crowell Creative Award and $100 each for two Merit Awards, presented at the opening reception. All work must be for sale and the centre takes 30 percent. The exhibition runs 12 November 2026 to 6 February 2027 alongside an online virtual show.
