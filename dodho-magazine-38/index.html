Dodho Magazine #38

International open call for the next print edition of Dodho Magazine, conceived as an artistic and curatorial volume rather than a conventional magazine and distributed to curators, festival directors, galleries and publishers looking for new voices in contemporary photography. Two routes: the Magazine pack, for projects and portfolios published inside the magazine, at €45 for one portfolio, €75 for two or €90 for three; and the Cover pack, for single images considered for the front page, at €45 for three images, €75 for six or €90 for ten. Everything is completed in one registration — details, upload and payment in the same form — with all material sent as a single ZIP named for the author; fragmented submissions are not accepted. Files must be JPG at 72 dpi and at least 1200 pixels on the longest side, and a PDF with a project text may be included for the Magazine option. Work that has already been published or awarded is eligible as long as the photographer holds the rights, and there is no restriction on when the images were made. The displayed price is final, with VAT added automatically for applicants in Europe where it applies. Photographers retain copyright throughout, granting only a promotional licence that can be revoked on 15 days' notice unless the work has already gone to print. Collectives and duos may apply as a single entrant. Every applicant is told the outcome roughly a week after the deadline and is given access to the digital edition afterwards to show the editorial criteria behind the selection. Submissions run from 1 July to 6 September, selection 7 to 11 September, notification 12 to 13 September, and publication on 25 September. Dodho has published 37 print editions over more than 11 years, featuring 222 photographers from 32 countries.
