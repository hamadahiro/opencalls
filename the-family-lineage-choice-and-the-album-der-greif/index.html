The Family — Lineage, Choice, and the Album

A new edition of Der Greif Pulse, the Munich non-profit's online exhibition strand, opened on 22 October 2026 and looking beyond the traditional family portrait. The call addresses family in every form — "whether through bloodline, heritage, or chosen community" — and encourages work on the performance of familial roles and on the deliberate choices behind what gets preserved in the album versus what stays unrecorded. Der Greif names vernacular archive interventions, intimate documentary projects, staged visual narratives and conceptual studies of shared memory among the approaches it wants, and asks what "the family photo" means today. Submit one project of at least 10 images; the conditions of participation state that video is not part of the submission format for this round, and projects previously selected in a Der Greif opportunity should not be re-submitted. Selected artists get an online exhibition in the Pulse section and a place in an online roundtable critique session with peers facilitated by Der Greif staff, plus visibility across the organisation's international community; they may also be invited to submit a portfolio to Der Greif Studio, which opens routes to artwork sales and commissions. Selection is judged on artistic quality, conceptual clarity, openness to dialogue and process, and engagement with identity and community. Entry is free — Der Greif, a registered non-profit, suggests an optional €5 or €10 donation and will issue a 50% or 100% discount voucher on request to anyone unable to contribute. The call is international with no age or regional restrictions and is aimed particularly at emerging and mid-career artists. Results are announced in November 2026 and the online exhibition follows in December 2026. THE CUTOFF IS TIMEZONE-SPECIFIC: the call page prints "Deadline: November 16, 2026 at 11:59 PM CET", which is 10:59 PM in the UK and mid-afternoon in the Americas on the 16th, not end of day where you are.
