Art & Woman Edition 4

Prizes $500 Publication

The fourth volume of Arts to Hearts Project's Art & Woman series, a storytelling-based publishing project that has published more than 300 women artists since 2024. Guest curator for this edition is Liz Lidgett, founder of Liz Lidgett Gallery and Design in Des Moines and author of Art for Everyone. The edition is intentionally open-themed: rather than responding to a set concept, artists are asked to submit work that reflects their own journey and practice, whether that means identity, memory, belonging, resilience, transformation, family, culture, motherhood, healing, loss or joy. Submissions are welcome across all visual media, with photography named alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, printmaking, ceramics, textile art, mixed media, collage, digital art, installation, illustration and experimental practice, and there is no requirement to make new work — existing, previously exhibited or already sold pieces are eligible so long as the artist holds the rights. The top winner receives a USD $500 cash award, a four-page editorial feature in the book, a written artist interview on the Arts to Hearts site, an engraved crystal trophy and certificate, and newsletter and social promotion to the project's audience; the first runner-up also receives a four-page feature, an interview and a trophy, and the second runner-up a four-page feature and a trophy. A separate optional $22 add-on buys a published virtual studio-visit interview if selected; it is not part of the entry. The book is distributed worldwide through Amazon, Barnes & Noble and the Arts to Hearts shop. Open to women and womxn artists worldwide aged 18 or over at any career stage, including collaborative work where the submitting artist identifies as a woman. Entry is tiered by image count, from $21 for one image to $100 for twenty. Applications close at 11:59pm EST on 30 August 2026.
