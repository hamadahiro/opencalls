Lowe House 2027

Prizes $500 Exhibition

Maryland Federation of Art has been invited to hang a show in the Lowe House of Delegates Office Building at 6 Bladen Street in Annapolis, the working office building for the Maryland State Delegates, and this call fills it for a three-month run from 13 January to 12 April 2027 — a General Assembly session's worth of daily foot traffic from legislators, staff and visitors. Original two-dimensional work in any medium is eligible, with no dimension exceeding 30 inches in height or 24 inches in width including frame. Photographic prints must have been analogue or digitally processed by the artist, who must have controlled cropping, colour balance, contrast and the final printing, and any limited-edition photographic print must be signed and numbered; work made with image-generative AI is not accepted and no reproductions are. All MFA exhibitions are blind juried, with names and identifying information hidden from the juror. This edition is selected by Teddy Johnson, Academic Chair of Visual Arts and Associate Professor of Visual Arts at Anne Arundel Community College, where he was also Cade Gallery Director from 2017 to 2023; he holds a BFA from MICA and an MFA from the Lamar Dodd School of Art at the University of Georgia, has shown solo at the American Poetry Museum, the Bromo Seltzer Arts Tower and the Museum of the Shenandoah Valley, and has held residencies at La Macina di San Cresci in Chianti in each of the past four years. Awards designated by the juror total $500, presented at a virtual ceremony on Zoom on 20 January 2027. Entry is open to MFA members or to artists residing in Anne Arundel County, Maryland — everyone else needs a membership, currently $70 a year. The entry fee is $45 for one or two works, $30 for members, plus $6.75 each for up to four more ($4.50 for members), and up to six original works made in the past three years may be entered. Work previously exhibited at the Lowe House Office Building is not eligible for re-entry. Artists are notified at 5 PM on 10 December and keep 70 per cent of sales, 75 per cent for members. Founded in 1963, MFA is a non-profit whose Circle Gallery is Maryland's oldest operating artist gallery.
