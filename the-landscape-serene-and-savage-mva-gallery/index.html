The Landscape: Serene and Savage

National juried exhibition at MVA Gallery (Modern Visual Arts) in historic Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, on landscape in both its tranquil and its ferocious registers — gardens, seacoasts and sunsets as readily as thunderstorms, cliffs and floods, with human figures absent, dominant or diminutive. Seascapes, architectural elements, single flowers, trees, birds, animals, reflections and shadows all qualify, in any style from representational to abstract. Photography is one of the accepted media alongside painting, printmaking, sculpture and mixed media, and the gallery sets no cutoff date for when the work was made. Open to US residents 18 and over; $35 for three images, $5 for each additional up to nine. An Art of Distinction award gives one artist a special exhibit in the gallery's Exhibition Room Two, a $200 Director's Choice may be granted at the executive director's discretion, and an annual grand prize of a New York City exhibition opportunity goes to one artist drawn from everyone exhibited at MVA during the calendar year, and honourable mentions are granted free entry to a future MVA show. Accepted artists ship at their own expense and must enclose a prepaid UPS or FedEx return label, and sales are split 70/30 in the artist's favour. Notification on or about January 26, 2027; exhibition April 4–28, 2027. The jurors have requested anonymity.
