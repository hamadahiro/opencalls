Lightvisions

Prizes $500 $350 $150 Online exhibition

Photo Artfolio returns to the theme of its inaugural exhibit with a new round of Lightvisions, a global online exhibition about the expressive power of light. The call asks for photographs in which light is essential to the image's impact — the organiser's own examples are a face lit by a flickering screen, hard sunlight cutting across an empty street, the glow of a carefully lit still life, and an ordinary space made unfamiliar by reflections and shadow. Work that finds something unexpected in available light is welcomed alongside work built through deliberate lighting and experimentation, and the call is open to all photographic genres and approaches: documentary, portraiture, street, landscape, nature, architecture, still life, conceptual, fine art and experimental photography, in colour and in black and white alike. Entry is open to photographers worldwide, with amateur and professional photographers both encouraged to apply. Up to 20 images may be submitted, at $20 for the first image and $5 for each additional one; fees are non-refundable and do not guarantee acceptance. Files are JPEG only, a minimum of 1,200 pixels on the longest side and no more than 5 MB. All work must be original and made by the submitting photographer, and AI-generated imagery is not eligible. Cash awards of $500, $350 and $150 go to the top three images, and every accepted photograph is shown in the online exhibition, which opens on the same day the results are announced. Photographers keep full copyright; submitting grants Photo Artfolio permission to show accepted images on its website, social channels and promotional material, with credit where feasible. Sales enquiries are passed to the photographer's own website and the photographer receives the whole of any resulting sale — the platform takes no commission. Submissions are reviewed by Creative Director Julia Cumes with an anonymous committee of jurors, and results are emailed on 3 February 2027 to the address on the entrant's CaFE account. The entry window closes at 11:59pm MST on 13 January 2027.
