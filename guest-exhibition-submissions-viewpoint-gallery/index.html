Guest Exhibition Submissions

Prize Solo or group exhibition

Standing call for guest exhibitions at ViewPoint Gallery, "an artist-run co-operative" at 1475 Bedford Highway in Bedford, Nova Scotia, founded in July 2000 as a collaboration between photographers and printmakers and focused on fine art photography exclusively since 2006. It shows monthly exhibitions and has hosted over 250 of them, and it fills roughly one guest slot a year: "In addition to the solo shows there is one guest exhibition and several group exhibitions shown each year." THIS IS A SELF-PROPOSED EXHIBITION, NOT A THEMED GROUP CALL. The submission page says "ViewPoint Gallery welcomes submissions for solo shows", but the gallery's own SHOW YOUR WORK page says it "welcomes submissions for solo and group shows by guest artists" and the guidelines ask that "If the applicant is a group of photographers, samples of each artist’s work is necessary" — so a collective can apply. The applicant picks which of three parts of the gallery to use, with wall runs of 34'9", 29' or 20', walls 10' high, lit by natural light and 3000K LED. WHAT IT COSTS: nothing to apply, but a "$150 non-refundable exhibition fee" falls due on confirmation of the exhibition date — it pays for posters and vinyl lettering — and the exhibitor also carries shipping, insurance and all import duties, charges and taxes in both directions. The gallery says plainly that "As a non-profit organization the Gallery is unable to pay artist fees", and that work on its walls is not insured. WHAT THE EXHIBITOR DOES: prepare and print the images, take part in the installation, remove the work immediately after the close, cater the opening reception and give an artist talk — normally 2 pm on the second Sunday after the opening, in person or virtual. The gallery supports poster and promotional design, promotes the show on its site and social channels, hosts the opening, facilitates the talk and staffs the space during gallery hours. TIMELINE AFTER ACCEPTANCE: fee plus a 2–3 sentence statement and 3–4 images within 30 days; final selection, printing and layout 3–6 months before opening; window-poster images at 3 months; press kit at 2 months; rack-card images at 7 weeks; work arrives 2 weeks before opening. WHAT TO SEND: exhibition title and a 150–250-word artist statement; bio or CV; 10–20 images as work prints (minimum 8x10 inches) or digital files (maximum 1920 by 1080 pixels, sRGB); an image list giving title, year, medium and framed and unframed dimensions; 4–8 exhibition-quality prints of at least 8x10 inches, which need not be the works proposed; and a description of the printing, framing and matting method plus the proposed installation, number of works, space required and any unusual requirements. Packages must be mailed or dropped off in person during opening hours, and without a self-addressed envelope carrying Canadian postage or International Reply Coupons the gallery disposes of submission materials rather than returning them. A group of photographers must include samples of each artist's work. The Programming Committee accepts or declines; the gallery's board holds final authority. The deadline is March 15 for the next calendar year and "Submissions are not reviewed before this date." Among the content questions the gallery asks applicants to work through: "Have you used generative AI in the creation of any of your images? Is so, please describe extent and purpose of usage."
