Black and White: 2026

Prizes Exhibition Catalog

A juried group exhibition celebrating black and white photography — its classic, nostalgic and dynamic qualities in contemporary practice. Juror Todd Johnson selects 26 works for the physical Portland gallery show (October 1–25, 2026), with 50 more in the online gallery and all 76 reproduced in the printed catalog. Selected photographers receive free professional printing, matting and framing.
