Tomorrow, Imagined: 17th Annual Patricia Sitar Juried Exhibition

Prize $450 / $250 / $150 / $100

Sitar Arts Center's Cafritz Gallery in Washington DC has hosted an annual public juried exhibition for seventeen years, each edition with a new theme. Sitar staff decide which works enter the show and a fresh panel of guest jurors, drawn from practising visual artists and arts professionals across the DC community, judges the awards. Tomorrow, Imagined asks artists to explore what lies ahead: the hopes, questions and possibilities that shape where we are headed as a community and a world. The centre is clear that this is not about predicting what will happen but about asking what future one is imagining and what future one is helping to create, and artists are encouraged to interpret the theme broadly. All mediums are welcome across two-dimensional, three-dimensional and film, video and new media work, and photography is one of the mediums named on the centre's own entry form. AI-generated imagery is not allowed. Open to artists aged 16 and over resident in the United States, with entrants required to be 16 by the date of submission. Prizes run to $450 and $250 for first and second place among adult artists aged 22 and over, $250 and $150 for young adult artists aged 16 to 21, and a $100 People's Choice award. Entry is free for anyone aged 16 to 21 and for Sitar community members up to three pieces; everyone else pays $15 for three works and $5 for each of up to two more. Accepted work is delivered to the centre in January 2027 and the exhibition opens on February 8.
