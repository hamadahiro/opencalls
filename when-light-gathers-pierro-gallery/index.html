When Light Gathers

Prize Exhibition

The Pierro Gallery of South Orange opens a group exhibition bringing together local and regional artists with connections to Jewish culture and community. Opening in December during Hanukkah, the show is timed to a season associated with light, gathering and reflection, and it is explicitly not a religious brief: the gallery states that artists are not expected to engage with religious or cultural themes in their work, and that the exhibition exists to let many voices be seen together. Emerging and established artists are both welcome, and up to three works may be submitted with title, medium, dimensions, year and price; a short artist statement and bio are requested only if the work is selected. Founded in 1994 and run by the South Orange Department of Recreation and Cultural Affairs, Pierro is a non-commercial municipal gallery that charges no submission fee and takes no commission on sales, inviting a voluntary donation instead. The gallery accepts photography alongside painting, sculpture and mixed media, and works needing glass or Plexiglass must meet its standard framing guidelines. Artists are notified the week of 12 October 2026, and the exhibition opens 3 December 2026 at the Baird Community Center, 5 Mead Street.
