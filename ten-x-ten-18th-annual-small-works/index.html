Ten x Ten – 18th Annual Small Works

Prize Exhibition

Ten x Ten is the Midwest Center for Photography's annual square-format exhibition, now in its 18th year. The brief is unusually concrete: every accepted print is exactly 10 inches by 10 inches, and every print is sold at the same price — $200 each — for the duration of the show. The format levels the field across genres and career stages and produces a uniform grid hang that has become the show's signature.

MWCP is a small photography-focused space in Wichita, Kansas. Selected work is exhibited both physically and online; MWCP covers all printing costs for accepted entries, which is unusual for a juried show at this scale. A $30 frame rental is the only out-of-pocket expense for selected artists who want their work framed for the wall.

Submissions are open internationally to photographers working in any process — film, digital, iPhone, or alternative. The 2026 exhibition runs November 14 through December 31, with the submission deadline August 1.
