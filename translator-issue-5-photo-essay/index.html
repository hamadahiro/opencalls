Translator Issue #5 — Photo Essay

Prizes £300–500 Publication

Translator is a London-founded print magazine that translates and platforms long-form journalism from beyond the Anglosphere — started in 2025, four paper issues published so far, with a core team in London working with contributing editors, translators, writers and publishers worldwide. It commissions one photo essay for each paper edition, and is now pitching for Issue #5. The brief is photographs that explore a social phenomenon or tell a human story, taken by local photographers outside the Anglosphere, ideally with a close personal familiarity with the phenomenon or story the pictures describe; the magazine asks for a unique angle not found in English-language media, locally specific but globally relevant, or something altogether singular to your own media landscape. Translator pays £300–500 and pairs the photographer with an editor to write the text that runs with the images, and the essay is published in print and online. Pitches go to Frankie Jenner, Translator's Managing Editor & Culture Editor, as a short account of the story you want to tell and the angle English-language media has missed, with a preview selection if the pictures already exist and links to previous work. The submissions page the link above reaches also carries a pitch form rather than an address — name, email, a short bio, where you are based and the pitch, with Photo Essay among the format options. Past photo essays have covered Berber and Khartoum in Sudan, the Gen Z protests in Nepal, the gardens of Babur in Afghanistan and Algeria's unfolding eco-crises — and for Issue #5 the editors say they are looking for work from regions they have not yet covered, which is a real constraint worth reading before pitching. There is no submission fee. READING THE PREVIOUS ESSAYS IS NOT FREE, WHATEVER THE CALL SAYS: the Instagram call tells pitchers they can read them online for free by signing up to "our free membership – Language Curious", but the membership ladder on translatormag.co prices Language Curious at £6 a month or £60 a year, and the tier it does mark FREE is Open-Minded, which buys the weekly Overheard newsletter and "free access to a selection of articles on the web" rather than the full archive Language Curious unlocks. Back issues can also be bought in print from the magazine's stockists or through its own shop, which ships worldwide. NOTE ON WHERE THE DEADLINE COMES FROM: the photo-essay commission, the fee band and the brief are all on Translator's own submissions page, which carries no date; the 26 October 2026 close for Issue #5 is published only by the magazine's own Instagram account, @translatormag. The site's own nav lists Issues #1 to #4, which is consistent with #5 being the edition in hand.
