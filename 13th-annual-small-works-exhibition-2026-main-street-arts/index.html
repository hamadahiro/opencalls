13th Annual Small Works Exhibition 2026

Prize $2,000 in cash awards ($1,000 Best in Show)

Main Street Arts is a non-profit gallery in the village of Clifton Springs, New York, occupying a 3,600-square-foot building over two floors with exhibitions, workshops, youth programs and an art resource library. Its Small Works show is the thirteenth annual national juried exhibition of work measuring 12 inches or less per side; framing may extend the whole piece to 16 inches total, but the artwork itself has to stay within 12. The call is open to artists working in all media excluding video/sound and installation art, and to all U.S. residents 18 years or older. $2,000 in cash awards is split between a $1,000 Best in Show, two Main Street Arts Choice Awards of $250 each and ten honourable mentions of $50. Entry is $30 to apply with one, two or three pieces. The juror for this edition has not been named yet — the 2025 show was juried by gallery curator Heather Olson. Selection is made from the images submitted through Submittable, so nothing should be sent until an acceptance email arrives; JPEGs should be at least 1000 pixels in any direction and named after the artwork. Accepted work is due by October 10 and may be delivered in person or shipped by UPS with a pre-paid return label valid for 90 days. The exhibition fills the main-floor gallery and runs online alongside it from November 7 to December 23 2026, opening with an artist reception on the afternoon of November 7.
