Sum Sum Summ-A-Time

Prize Online exhibition

Drawing Rooms, a nonprofit art space in Jersey City, gathers work about summertime for an online-only show on Artsy — scenes and places, summer food, leisure, flora and fauna, and the colours and sensations of the season. Photography sits alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, collage, fibre and sculpture, with no size limitation and no AI-generated or reproduced work accepted. Three images are covered by the entry fee and further images may be added for $5 each. The exhibition runs August 6–30, 2026 on Artsy.net, with exhibited work offered for sale at a 60/40 split in the artist's favour.
