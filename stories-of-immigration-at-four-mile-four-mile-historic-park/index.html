Stories of Immigration at Four Mile

Prizes Solo exhibition $50

Denver's 1859 stagecoach-stop museum marks Month of Photography with a juried show on immigration stories, judging analog and digital entries separately. Top scorers in each category win a one-month solo exhibition in the park's education center plus $50, and selected work is delivered ready to hang. Human-made photographs only.
