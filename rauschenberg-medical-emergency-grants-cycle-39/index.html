Rauschenberg Medical Emergency Grants - Cycle 39

Prize Up to $5,000

One-time grants of up to $5,000 for recent unexpected medical, dental and mental-health emergencies, funded by the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation and administered by NYFA. The eligible Visual Arts category names Photography outright - 'work in traditional and experimental photography or any work in which photography or photographic techniques are pivotal, if not exclusive' - alongside craft/sculpture, painting and printmaking/drawing/book arts, and the programme also covers digital/electronic arts, video/film and choreography. Applicants must be 21 or older, reside in the United States, the District of Columbia, a Tribal Nation or a U.S. Territory, and meet the income cap: adjusted gross income no greater than $80,000 for an individual or $160,000 for joint filers, averaged over the last two federal tax returns. You also need recent and sustained artistic activity - at least one public-facing opportunity a year since 2022 - and you cannot be enrolled in any degree-seeking programme or have received a Rauschenberg Medical or Dancer Emergency Grant within the last five years. Cycle 39 covers emergencies occurring on or after 1 April 2026. Applications opened 20 October and close at 5:00 PM ET on 17 November 2026, with notification on 23 December 2026. Treatment is only covered if it took place in the U.S., D.C., a Tribal Nation or a U.S. Territory. Artists whose direct medical costs are covered by Medicare or Medicaid may instead request up to $5,000 in documented living expenses. Portfolios and work samples are not requested - applications are reviewed for demonstrated need - but your CV must show five years of practice. Roughly $138,000 is distributed each cycle and demand outstrips it, so applying is not a guarantee of an award. Awarding is a two-step process: NYFA staff screen for eligibility, an outside panel then scores on demonstrated need and severity, and after that review the top-scoring application is funded automatically while the rest of the top tier go into a lottery for the remaining awards. Cycle 40 opens 15 December 2026 and closes 12 January 2027.
