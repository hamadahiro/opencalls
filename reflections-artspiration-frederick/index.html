Reflections

Prize Exhibition

Artspiration Frederick's first cash-and-carry exhibition, on view in the Artspiration Gallery in Frederick, Maryland from 4–20 December 2026. Artists are invited to explore the theme of reflection in nature, in memory, in the self, or in the world around us. Original work in any visual medium is welcome and photography is named outright alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, fibre art, ceramics, mixed media and digital art, with the show seeking a diverse range of artistic voices, perspectives, techniques and subject matter. Open to visual artists 18 and older residing in the continental US; up to three pieces may be submitted and all three may be selected, and work must not have been previously exhibited at Artspiration Frederick. All work must be for sale and the gallery retains 40 percent of a sale, with two-dimensional work framed and wired for hanging (no sawtooth hangers). Accepted work is dropped off 29 November to 1 December, or shipped to arrive by 30 November at the artist's cost with a return label included. The juror's notification is emailed 19 November and the artists' reception is 12 December, 3–5pm. The gallery is free, open to the public and accessible.
