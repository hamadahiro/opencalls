Atlanta Art Fair 2026

Prize Exhibition

Atlanta Photography Group is selecting the work for its own booth at the Atlanta Art Fair, which returns to the historic Pullman Yards campus for its third edition on 1–4 October 2026. There is no theme and all photographic genres and processes are welcome. Up to 24 works will be chosen by Andi McKenzie, PhD, Curator of Works on Paper at the Michael C. Carlos Museum; APG's booth at last year's fair was picked by guest curator Melissa Messina as the best curated booth at the fair. Selected works must be no longer than 30 inches on the long side and presented in white frames — no exceptions, and no saw-tooth hangers — and are delivered to the APG Gallery by 25 September. Entry is $70 for up to six works and is open to current APG members only; membership starts at $25 a year for students and $55 for individuals. Notifications go out on or about 28 August 2026.
