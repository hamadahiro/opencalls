Fine Art Awards 2026

Dodho Magazine's fine art competition, open worldwide, selects 100 finalists for a 30×30 cm printed annual edition. First, second and third prizes plus two honorable mentions receive a certificate and editorial visibility, and the overall winner is named Photographer of the Year; there is no cash prize. Entry is by paid image pack of three, six or twelve photographs, with VAT added for European entrants.
