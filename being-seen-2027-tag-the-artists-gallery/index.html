Being Seen 2027

Prizes $500 / $250 / $125 Three $50 honorable mentions Exhibition

A regional juried exhibition at TAG/The Artists Gallery, an artist-run gallery at 501 N Market Street in Frederick, Maryland, hung in the gallery itself from 5 to 28 March 2027 with a reception on 6 March, 5–8 pm. The gallery's premise for the show: "If a tree falls in a forest, does it make a sound? It does only if there is an observer. Art also needs an observer to truly Be Seen because art is not complete until it connects with the heart and mind of the viewer." The juror is Florencio Lennox (Lenny) Campello — an artist, curator, critic and author of 100 Artists of Washington, DC, who has juried or curated over 200 fine art shows and recently assembled the Women Artists of the DMV survey across twenty venues in the capital region. Photographs in both analog and digital formats are explicitly eligible, alongside original paintings, drawings, prints, mixed media including digital collage and manipulations, and sculpture; work must have been made in the last three years, must not have been shown at TAG before, and no reproductions of pre-existing artwork or video are accepted. Awards, chosen by the juror, are $500 First Prize, $250 Second, $125 Third and three Honorable Mentions of $50 each. The eligibility bar is tight and worth reading twice: open to artists 18 and over living in Maryland, DC, Virginia, West Virginia, Pennsylvania or Delaware, within easy driving distance of Frederick, and not currently represented by TAG. That driving-distance clause is enforced by the logistics rather than by a map — there is no shipping option in either direction, and accepted work must be hand-delivered to the gallery on 28 February or 1 March 2027 (2–6 pm) and collected on 28 or 29 March, with $5 a day charged for storage after that and disposal possible after thirty days. All wall-hung work including the frame must stay within 48 inches in any direction and 6 inches deep, under 30 lbs, framed and ready to hang with wire or a cleat — no sawtooth hangers. All work must be for sale with no price-on-request, and the gallery takes 30% commission. Judging runs 13–24 January 2027 and the gallery aims to notify every applicant by 29 January.
