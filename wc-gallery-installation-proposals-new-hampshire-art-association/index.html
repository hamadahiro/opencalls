WC Gallery Installation Proposals

Prize Solo exhibition

The oddest room in the New Hampshire Art Association's Portsmouth gallery is its bathroom, and the association runs it as an exhibition space. In the association's words the WC Gallery is "an irregular installation space" and it is looking for artists "exploring innovative ideas and taking creative risks to create a visually and mentally stimulating experience". Installations run in three intervals a year — February–May, June–August and September–December — and the brief is to keep the bathroom functional while making it immersive and actively engaging for visitors: a mural or wall work, found objects, or any idea that transforms the room. NHAA publishes a photograph and a dimensioned diagram of the space and encourages artists to come and see it in person. Proposals are accepted on a rolling basis and artists may submit more than one. A proposal is a one-page plan with a diagram showing images or drawings of what will go where, the materials involved (paint, hung work, 3D objects), what the artist will need from the gallery (pedestals, labels), a title, preferred months of installation, and a timeline of what is already made and what will be. Open to regional artists, NHAA members and non-members alike, and free to propose.
