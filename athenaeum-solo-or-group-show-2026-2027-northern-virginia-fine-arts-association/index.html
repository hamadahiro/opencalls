Athenaeum Solo or Group Show 2026–2027

The Athenaeum Gallery, a Greek Revival landmark on Prince Street in Old Town Alexandria run by the Northern Virginia Fine Arts Association, sets aside part of its programme — an average of eight shows a year — for exhibitions proposed by artists and curators rather than chosen by the gallery director. This is the 2026–2027 review window, the successor to the cycle that closes on 30 September 2026: the portal opens the same day and accepts proposals through 30 September 2027, and the gallery describes the review as rolling rather than batched. Artists living or working in Virginia, Maryland or the District of Columbia may propose a solo show; a group of artists from the region may propose a group show; and a curator based anywhere may propose a show of work by artists from the area. Proposals are evaluated by gallery director Twig Murray on what the artist is trying to achieve or communicate, what makes the work distinctive, technical quality, and how recently the artist or the work has been shown locally. All visual media are welcome, photography included, but every work must be wall-mounted, less than nine inches deep and framed or fully finished, wired and ready to hang on picture hooks; nothing floor-mounted or free-standing is considered, and work requiring electricity is excluded. Sculpture may be entered only if it is under nine inches deep and lighter than 30 pounds. An application takes six to twelve images and up to three proposals per applicant are allowed. The artist, group or curator is responsible for getting work to the gallery and for collecting unsold work at the close. The Athenaeum insures everything in the show for the full retail price for the run, takes 40 per cent commission on sales and pays the artist within thirty days of closing; a curator-proposed show can negotiate a different split. Work does not have to be for sale to be included. Founded in 1964, the NVFAA is a registered 501(c)(3) supported by donations, member contributions, building rentals and sales commissions. The Athenaeum Gallery retains a 40 percent commission on works sold and pays the artist 60 percent of the sale price within thirty days.
