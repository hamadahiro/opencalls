SOMETHING NEW — October 2026 Open Call

Prizes Online publication Artist profile Archive placement

Free monthly open call from Thirdplat, an independent online platform that publishes work against a rolling monthly theme and keeps it in a permanent public archive. Photography is named outright among the welcomed forms: "Whether manifested as moving image, photography, writing, craft, sound, performance, or cross-disciplinary work, Thirdplat welcomes original, respectful creations directly to our open global platform." THE THEME is an inquiry into consequence rather than novelty — "SOMETHING NEW is not simply a celebration of novelty, but an inquiry into impact: when something new enters our lives, what does it change?" The platform asks for work reflecting on "one new presence in your life, community, or practice, and the trace it has left behind", and lists the pressures it has in mind: pandemics shifting human distance, displacement turning home into a portable memory, climate rewriting landscapes, AI redefining authorship. The "new" may be "a place, relationship, technology, loss, habit, identity or unfamiliar way of seeing", and responses "may be personal, social, documentary, fictional or experimental". THIS IS NOT A COMPETITION and the platform says so plainly: "Thirdplat is not a conventional competition or juried exhibition. We treat submission as publication rather than audition." Accepted work is published directly, subject to the platform's content guidelines, and becomes part of that month's online exhibition and the archive. WHAT AN ARTIST GETS: a public page for the work, an artist profile "that can develop into an ongoing record of their practice", a direct shareable link, and placement alongside other readings of the same theme — the platform's stated aim is to "give emerging and independent creative work a place where it can remain visible beyond a single post, exhibition or deadline". Entry is free, up to three works, one entry per artist, files up to 50MB each as JPG, PNG, TIFF, MOV or MP4, and it is open worldwide: "Creators from around the world are invited to show us what entered their world, what it changed and what it left behind." AI IS ACCEPTED WITH A DUTY TO DISCLOSE, in the Terms of Use rather than on the call page: "Thirdplat accepts thoughtful engagement with artificial intelligence, digital systems and emerging creative tools. If artificial intelligence has materially generated or transformed a submitted work, you should describe its role honestly." THE RIGHTS CLAUSE IS CLEAN: the artist keeps copyright, and the licence granted to THIRDPLAT LTD is "non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free" and scoped "only for operating, presenting, archiving and promoting Thirdplat, its open calls and participating artists", with removal available on request. TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE SPENDING TIME ON IT. The terms qualify the no-audition promise — "Submission does not guarantee selection, publication, exhibition, promotion or any other particular outcome", and Thirdplat "may decide which works are selected, featured, published, archived or displayed". And the archive is nearly empty: the platform’s own content index returns two published artworks in total across its whole life, not two this month, alongside three posted open calls. TWO FURTHER THEMES ARE ALREADY POSTED on the platform's own open-calls index — 2026-11 THE THIRD SPACE: CULTURAL HYBRIDITY and 2026-12 REFLECTION: AI AND US — so a photographer who misses this window has a monthly one behind it.
