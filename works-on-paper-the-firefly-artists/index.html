Works on Paper

Prizes Gallery exhibition 1st-3rd place awards

National juried works-on-paper exhibition from Long Island artist cooperative The Firefly Artists, hung at the Darcy Arts Center in Northport, NY, July 21 – August 21, 2026. Photography is explicitly accepted alongside drawing, painting, printmaking, mixed media and digital media — original, human-made 2D works on paper up to 36"×36". First through third place and honorable mention awards; all work for sale with a 35% gallery commission. Reception August 6.
