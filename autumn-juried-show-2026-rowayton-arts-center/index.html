Autumn Juried Show 2026

Prize $250 Best in Show

Rowayton Arts Center is a non-profit gallery on Rowayton Avenue in Norwalk, Connecticut, and its Autumn Juried Show is an open-call, open-theme, all-media exhibition. Photography is named outright in the accepted-media list in both colour and black and white, and the centre's blanket ban on giclee reproductions is explicitly waived for photography and digital art. The juror is Kimberly Henrikson, executive director of the Center for Contemporary Printmaking, a non-profit print organisation that has run for over thirty years in Norwalk with a regular exhibition schedule, instructional workshops, an artist-in-residence programme, studio rental space and edition printing for artists. There is a $250 cash award for Best in Show. Entry is $35 for a first piece and $10 for each additional piece up to five for non-members, $25 and $10 for members, and $2 and $1 for the centre's Art Angel life members; a maximum of two pieces per artist can be juried into the show. Work must have been created in the last three years and not previously exhibited at RAC, must be two-dimensional and no more than 40 inches in height or length including the frame, and must be ready to hang on braided wire — no sawtooth hangers. Artwork generated by AI is not acceptable. Accepted pieces have to be hand delivered on October 12 or 13; the centre will not accept work that is mailed in, which in practice limits this to artists who can get to Norwalk. The show runs October 18 to November 14 2026 with an opening reception on the afternoon of October 18, and RAC takes a 30% commission on sales.
