SE Center for Photography — The Portrait

Prize Exhibition

The SE Center's open call on the portrait, classic to contemporary. The organisers frame it around what portraits are for: objects of remembrance and reverence, of seduction and glorification — from the keepsake in a locket to the likeness of a leader meant to inspire, images that can stir and confront and drive us to action just as they can lull us into longing for a time since passed, acting as a mirror in which we find our own inward experience or as a window onto the virtues of another. Analog and digital manipulation in all its forms is welcome, monochrome or colour, any subject, analog, digital or antique processes, and photographers of every skill level and location may enter. The juror is Aline Smithson, the Los Angeles visual artist, educator and editor best known for conceptual portraiture that uses humour and pathos to explore the performative potential of photography; she has had over forty solo shows at institutions including the Griffin Museum of Photography, the Santa Barbara Museum of Art and the Center for Fine Art Photography, her work is held in public collections, and in 2007 she founded Lenscratch. Between thirty-five and forty selected images hang in the SE Center's virtual gallery for about a month, with the selection also carried across the Center's social channels, an archived online slideshow and a video walkthrough. Entry is $35 for up to five images and $6 for each additional image to a maximum of fifteen; members pay $28 and $5. Work exhibited at the SE Center within the previous two years is not eligible, and there are no refunds for images not selected. The Center takes no commission — the artist keeps 100 percent of any sale. Submissions opened 6 September 2026 and close 15 November; selections are announced 1 December and the exhibition runs 15 December 2026 to 30 January 2027.
