Where I Sleep

Forum 301 is a gallery and discussion space at 301 W. Ohio Street in Chicago's River North, built around open-ended forums that use exhibitions as a starting point for conversation between artists, scholars and students. For its September exhibition the gallery is seeking artists experimenting in the photographic medium. Where I Sleep presents artists who investigate the nuances of “home” through various photographic practices, each examining the complexities of connecting to a physical space, with the works drawing viewers to consider the isolated opportunities seated inside an individual's sense of home, including the potential to encourage memory, seeing and story. Accepted works include photographic prints, and the gallery explicitly encourages artists to think beyond a photo on a wall, whether that means projections, sculptural work, work in book form or something else. Four submissions are allowed for a single $20 fee, and applications are made through a form on the gallery's own site that asks for an artist statement or bio plus materials, dimensions and year for each work. Applications close August 23, 2026 and the exhibition runs September 4 to October 4, 2026.
