Numéro+ Flux et Forma

Prize Publication

Numéro Switzerland's open call on the theme Flux et Forma — flow and transformation, where constant change shapes creativity. Open worldwide to creators 18+, including photographers, videographers and multidisciplinary artists; submit a single project of images, video or both. The winner receives a print article and project cover feature in Numéro, digital promotion, a Nanlite lighting package and a copy of the magazine.
