Jazz Fest Volunteer Photographer Program 2027

The New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Foundation Archive selects photographers to document the 2027 New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival — the music, but also the food and art vendors, the cultural pavilion and the crowd — for permanent inclusion in the Foundation's historic collection. Selection is by a three-person blind jury of photographers scoring on artistic merit (60%) and experience photographing live cultural events or professional reliability (40%); no more than 150 applications are accepted. Photographers keep full ownership and copyright of their images, granting the Festival and Foundation permission to use them for non-commercial and educational purposes only, under a separate Terms of Agreement issued to those invited. Selected photographers are assigned one of the festival's two weekends (not both) and receive complimentary entry on assignment days, access to the Foundation hospitality lounge and free parking in designated lots, and may shoot the festival at leisure once the assignment is done. In return they must attend a compulsory photographers' meeting the day before the festival opens — that is where pit passes and tickets are issued — bring their own gear, deliver at least 18–24 photographs per act on their assigned stages with the selection left to the photographer, and be able to stand and walk long distances in heat, mud or dust. This is unpaid volunteer work: there is no fee to apply and no stipend — what comes back is credentialed access to a world-famous festival and a permanent place in a major cultural archive. Open internationally to student, amateur and professional photographers aged 18 or over; the application runs through CaFE and asks for six images, watermark-free. Two cautions about the Foundation's own page: the apply link in its How To Apply block points at a dead CaFE listing (ID 16128 — the live one is 18184, linked here under Submit via), and the same block dates the deadline November 13 while the page's own application-period header and CaFE both say November 14. Apply by November 13 to be safe. Festival dates are April 22–25 and April 29 – May 2, 2027.
