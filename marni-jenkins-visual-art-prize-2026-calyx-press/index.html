Marni Jenkins Visual Art Prize 2026

CALYX Press, the oldest feminist press in the United States, has launched the inaugural Marni Jenkins Visual Art Prize, a blind competition with a rotating final judge for work by women and nonbinary artists. The winner receives a $400 cash prize and publication in an upcoming issue of CALYX, A Journal of Art and Literature by Women+, the press's biannual print publication, whose every issue carries a glossy full-colour insert of visual art. The winner and up to two runners-up are published on calyxpress.org and receive a complimentary one-volume subscription of three issues - though the press's own contest page words this more broadly, saying the winner and all finalists get the subscription and the website publication. This year's final judge is Betty LaDuke, an Ashland, Oregon painter whose shaped and painted wood panels address social justice, food security and cultural diversity, and whose work is held by the Portland Art Museum, the Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art, the Hallie Ford Museum of Art and the Oregon State Capitol. The prize is named for Meredith 'Marni' Jenkins, a founding member of CALYX Press and an accomplished photographer and painter, and it exists to encourage bold new visual art that challenges convention and explores the broad experiences and perspectives of women and nonbinary artists. The submission window runs 1 July to 30 November 2026. Submit up to six pieces of visual art; photographs of three-dimensional work such as sculpture and textile pieces are welcome, and the CALYX editorial collective reads everything first before sending twenty finalists to the judge. Files must be JPG or PNG, at least 300 dpi and at least five inches wide at that resolution, in RGB or CMYK. Work must be unpublished, and simultaneous submissions are discouraged. CALYX operates a strict anti-AI policy and asks that no work generated with AI tools be submitted. Entry is through Submittable at $15, which the press's own page itemises as a $14 consideration fee plus a $1 PayPal processing fee. Winners are announced in January 2027. Note that CALYX also takes free year-round art submissions on a separate track, considered by its Art Collective each May and December.
