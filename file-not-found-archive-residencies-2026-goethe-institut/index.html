FILE NOT FOUND — Archive Residencies 2026

Prizes €4,000 Exhibition

International open call selecting ten artists for residencies at cultural archives worldwide, run by Goethe-Institut in collaboration with Gerador. Each residency lasts 2 weeks to 2 months between August 2026 and May 2027, on-site at one of ten participating archives: Acervo Bajubá (São Paulo), The ArQuives (Toronto), China Modern Art Archive (Beijing), documenta archiv (Kassel), Arsenal Filminstitut (Berlin), Kurdistan Center for Arts & Culture (Erbil), Imagine IC & The Need for Legacy (Amsterdam), VEHA Archive (Podlasie), Museu Nacional de Arte Contemporânea (Lisbon), and South African History Archive (Johannesburg). Each selected artist receives €2,000 artist fee, €2,000 production budget, and up to €2,000 for travel and accommodation. Final works exhibited at BLMK (Brandenburgisches Landesmuseum für moderne Kunst) in Cottbus, Germany, in Fall 2027. Open to artists worldwide, all disciplines including photography. Working language English.

DeadlineJune 7, 2026
ResultsJuly 15, 2026
Entry feeFree
LocationCottbus, Germany
Submit viaWebsite

Jury: One representative each from the host archive (deciding vote), BLMK, Gerador, and the Goethe-Institut FILE NOT FOUND project team

Organized by Goethe-Institut
