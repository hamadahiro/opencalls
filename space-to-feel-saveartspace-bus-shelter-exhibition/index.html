Space to Feel — SaveArtSpace Bus Shelter Exhibition

Prize Bus shelter exhibition in Miami

SaveArtSpace is a non-profit that has placed artists' work on commercial billboard and transit advertising space since 2015 — turning public ad space into open-air exhibitions seen by far larger audiences than gallery shows reach.

"Space to Feel" is curated by Leah Guzman, a board-certified art therapist and professional artist specializing in creative wellness. The theme invites work about emotional honesty, tenderness, healing, and human connection, celebrating vulnerability and mental health.

The call is open to all mediums, including photography, and to artists of all ages and backgrounds. Selected works are displayed on bus shelter advertising space in Miami, Florida for at least one month beginning August 14, 2026.

Submission is $35 for one to three images and $10 for each additional image, structured as a tax-deductible donation supporting exhibition production. The deadline is June 29, 2026.
