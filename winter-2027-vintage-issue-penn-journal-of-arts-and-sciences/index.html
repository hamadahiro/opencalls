Winter 2027 'Vintage' Issue

Prize Publication

The Penn Journal of Arts and Sciences is an independent publication founded in 2024 that presents contemporary creative work across disciplines, with a primary focus on visual art and poetry alongside experimental and cross-disciplinary practice. Submissions made before this deadline are considered for the Winter 2027 'Vintage' issue, published both online and in print within the journal's curated editorial framework. Selected contributors may also be included in editorial features, interviews and wider PJAS releases. The call is open internationally to artists and writers at all career stages, working in any discipline, with individual and collaborative submissions accepted.
