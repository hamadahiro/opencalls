Flourish Fund 2026–27

Prize $10,000

The Flourish Fund is CultureSource's regranting programme for Southeast Michigan, run in partnership with the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts as part of its Regional Regranting Program. It awards six grants of $10,000 each, every year, to artist-organised projects that are experimental and risk-taking, collaborative, shared with the public, and rooted in a visual arts practice. Projects can be interdisciplinary but must have a strong foundation in the visual arts, and the fund states that proposals may span mediums and disciplines from sculpture to projection mapping, printmaking to immersive technology, and beyond; recent grantees include a Detroit community-archiving project built on pop-up digitisation events for family photographs and home movies, paired with contemporary artists' work in galleries and installations. Applicants must be independent, unincorporated artists at any career stage, and the lead organiser must live in one of the seven Southeast Michigan counties - Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Washtenaw, Monroe, Livingston or St Clair - with the project taking place and being publicly shared in one of them. Groups and collectives may apply with a single lead applicant, who receives the funds. Projects run by incorporated entities including LLCs and 501(c)(3)s, by universities, schools, religious institutions or municipalities, or led by currently enrolled high school or university students, are not eligible, and the fund does not support retroactive or reimbursement requests. The application asks for artist and project information, work samples, a proposed budget and several short answers, and is free to enter through Submittable. Applications opened on 13 August and close on Friday 11 September 2026; note that the portal stays open until 11.59pm but technical assistance ends at 5pm. An independent committee including previous Flourish Fund grantees reviews and ranks proposals, then meets to agree the six awards, with notification to all applicants in mid-October. Grantees receive $6,000 on signing and $3,000 at the interim report stage.
