The Eyes are Bigger Than the Stomach: Food and Contemporary Art

Prizes $300 $150 ×2 $100 ×2 Exhibition

A national juried exhibition at the Indy Art Center built on a subject the organiser calls universal: “There are few things so universal and central to the human experience as food. It has shaped cultures, customs, geography, politics and much more across centuries.” The question put to artists is how we reflect on food through contemporary art in 2026 — including the contemporary edges of it, which the call names explicitly: food scarcity, factory farming, climate change. The brief is deliberately open on medium: this is a call “open to any artist working within the theme or concept of food with no specified medium required”, and the Art Center states that all artworks which connect to food in some way, through media, concept or theme, will be considered, with jurors approaching the theme under an encompassing definition covering both traditional techniques and contemporary methods of making. For a photographer that is a wide door — documentary work on agriculture and the food supply, still life, studio and alternative-process work all sit inside it — and the eligibility clause confirms it: entries are open to artists 18 or older residing in the United States, working in any media, with work made within the last two years that has not been shown at the Art Center before. Entry is two works for $35 with $5 for each additional piece up to a maximum of fifteen, and awards are cash: $300 Best in Show, $150 Best in 2D, $150 Best in 3D and two honourable mentions of $100 each. The exhibition is a long one — it opens 5 April 2027 and closes 13 June, with the reception on 16 April, 5 to 8 pm — and it runs in six galleries at an institution that mounts more than a dozen contemporary art exhibitions a year. Practical terms worth knowing before applying: all work may be offered for sale and the Art Center retains a 35% commission on sales; the artist pays shipping, insurance in transit and return shipping, and work must arrive in a reusable container by 21 March 2027 (hand delivery opens 20 February); work must not exceed 250 pounds or ten feet in any direction; and installation artists are asked to make contact before applying. The deadline is 17 February 2027 with notifications by end of day on the 19th.
