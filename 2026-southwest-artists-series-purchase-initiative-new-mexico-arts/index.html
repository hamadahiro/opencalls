2026 Southwest Artists Series Purchase Initiative

Prize $5,000–$50,000 purchase

New Mexico Arts' Art in Public Places programme buys artwork outright for publicly accessible buildings across the state — amphitheatres, community centres, courthouses, educational institutions, fire stations, healthcare facilities, libraries, recreation complexes, rest areas and senior centres among them. The 2026 Southwest Artists Series Purchase Initiative aims to diversify the state collection by purchasing work from artists at every stage of their careers, and all media in all genres are considered; New Mexico Arts names photography explicitly among the mediums it accepts, alongside drawing, painting, sculpture, mosaic, graphic arts, etching, lithography, silkscreen, textile, fibre, wood, metal, glass, mixed media, collage and assemblage. Work must be durable, permanent, low-maintenance and compliant with ADA guidelines once installed, and suitable for interior or exterior display. The programme seeks artwork in the $5,000–$50,000 range, and the price must be all-inclusive: materials, labour, museum-quality framing under Plexiglas, security hangers, delivery, installation, insurance, taxes, commissions and both written and professional photographic documentation of the work. Since its inception in 1986 AIPP has placed more than 3,000 works of art across all 33 New Mexico counties, amounting to over $21 million paid directly to artists. Open to artists who reside in New Mexico, Arizona, Utah, Colorado, Oklahoma or Texas, and to galleries located in those states, which may submit on behalf of any artist they represent regardless of that artist's residency. A statewide Submission Review Committee of artists, arts professionals and community leaders scores applications, and the selected pool is then shown to local committees for purchase consideration.
