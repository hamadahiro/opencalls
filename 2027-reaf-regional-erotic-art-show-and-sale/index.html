2027 REAF Regional Erotic Art Show and Sale

Prizes $100 Best in Photography $100 Best in Fine Art 3 × $50 Producers Choice

The juried art show at the heart of the Rochester Erotic Arts Festival, an adults-only weekend of art, performance, workshops and vending that moves to Ithaca for 2027 after losing its Auburn hotel. REAF is looking for high-quality original work that is "creative, erotic and thought-provoking", and photography is not a tolerated extra here — it is named in the accepted-media list alongside drawing, painting, printmaking, mixed media, collage, digital art, film and sculpture, and it has its own top award: Best in Photography, $100, matched by Best in Fine Art at $100 and three Producers Choice Awards of $50. Accepted artists get a free weekend Artist Pass to the whole festival and can buy a plus-one pass for $30. Work is offered for sale and REAF takes no commission — artists supply their own buyer contact details, which are displayed with the work, and deal with buyers directly. One entry is $30 and covers up to five pieces; an artist may file up to three entries, so fifteen pieces is the practical ceiling at $90. Work must not have been shown at REAF before. THIS IS A HAND-DELIVERY SHOW AND THAT IS THE REAL GATE: accepted work has to be brought in person to Ithaca, New York on the evening of Thursday 8 April or the morning of Friday 9 April 2027, ready to hang — matted, framed, signed and labelled — and cannot be collected before the exhibition closes; there is no shipping option, and the organiser describes the show as regional without naming a region. Prints other than photographs must be limited editions on archival paper, numbered and signed. Artists must be 18 or over, must hold a model release for any identifiable person photographed, and where a model is nude or in sexual activity must hold a legible copy of ID proving they were 18+; work depicting minors is refused outright. REAF carries no insurance on displayed work and recommends artists check their own. Submissions are open now; the deadline is 1 February 2027, decisions go out by 8 February, and accepted artists must respond by 15 February. The festival itself runs 9–11 April 2027 across the Ithaca Downtown Conference Center and The Hotel Ithaca, with ASL interpreters available all weekend.
