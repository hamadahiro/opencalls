Foodways

Prize Exhibition

The Cultural Council for Palm Beach County invites artists to interpret foodways — “the eating habits and culinary practices of the many cultures represented in the region” — for an exhibition running January 15 to March 6, 2027. The Council suggests three broad directions: growth and production practices and the people who cultivate, package and move food through the community; the role food plays in shaping identity, unity and tradition; and the environmental context of farming, fishing and food production. The submission guidelines enumerate media plainly — painting, sculpture, photography, works on paper, installations, digital art and video, crafts and textiles are all welcome — and each artist may submit up to five works with high-resolution images (two views for 3D) and a brief statement on how the work relates to the theme. Only artists who live in Palm Beach County, Florida are eligible. Applications are taken only through Artwork Archive, where a free account is enough to apply, and no entry fee is listed on either the Council's call page or the Artwork Archive listing.
