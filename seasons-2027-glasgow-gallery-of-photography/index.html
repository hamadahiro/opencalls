Seasons 2027

Prizes Exhibition Publication

The brief is the simplest kind and the hardest to do badly: the gallery wants the seasons as a change in palette, not as a calendar. Its own framing — "from the greens of Spring to the blues and whites of Winter" — points at how a season changes the way the world looks rather than at seasonal subject matter, and it asks specifically for work that shows how that change affects the environment around us. A single strong frost picture will read; eight unrelated pretty months will not.

This is one of two March 2027 shows at the gallery and the money is structured the way the house series always is: submitting is free, selection happens first, and only photographers who are selected pay the £50 participation fee. That fee covers printing and installation — prints go up at 16"x12", 18"x12" or 12"x12" in frames the gallery owns — and a discount is available if you are selected for both of the month's exhibitions. The other March show is Women in Photography 2027, same deadline.

What comes back is a printed wall, an online gallery page, and inclusion in the exhibition catalogue, plus a digital certificate, photographs and video of your work in situ for the gallery's channels, and automatic entry for Photographer of the Month. Submission is 1 to 8 JPEGs at 300ppi and at least 3000 pixels on the shortest side, sent in one go by Smash, WeTransfer or Dropbox — the gallery explicitly asks you not to attach images to an email, because that compresses them.

Dates worth putting in a calendar: submissions close 30 October 2026, acceptances go out by 4 November, and selected photographers must pay by 18 November. The gallery notifies only the photographers it selects and asks everyone to check their spam folder, so silence after 4 November is the answer.
