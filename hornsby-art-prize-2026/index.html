Hornsby Art Prize 2026

Prizes AU$10,000 AU$1,500 Photography Award

Established in 2009 and run by Hornsby Shire Council with the Hornsby Art Society, this non-acquisitive prize celebrates Australian contemporary art across five categories — Painting, Drawing, Printmaking, Photography and Sculpture — each carrying its own $1,500 award, so photographers compete for a dedicated Photography Award as well as the $10,000 major prize. The overall pool is $23,000, which also covers a $5,000 Hornsby Shire Local Artist Award and a $500 People's Choice. There is no set theme. Artists may enter up to two works; 2D pieces are capped at 125cm in height and width including the frame, and nothing may exceed 15kg. Finalist works must not have appeared in any other art prize exhibition and must be for sale, with a 20% commission going to the Hornsby Art Society. Finalists are announced by 5pm on 1 September and the exhibition runs at Wallarobba Arts and Cultural Centre from 16 October to 1 November 2026, with the prize announced on opening day.
