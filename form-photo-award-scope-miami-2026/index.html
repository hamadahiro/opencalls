Form Photo Award / SCOPE Miami 2026

Prizes Exhibition Publication

Form is a Basel-based photography platform founded by Dimitri Bogachuk, spanning a gallery, publishing house and magazine. Alongside its photo basel edition each spring, Form runs a second annual award tied to the SCOPE Art Fair in Miami.

The premise is again positioning: the top four winners exhibit at SCOPE Art Fair Miami in December 2026, during Miami Art Week, reaching the collectors, curators and gallerists who travel for the fair.

Shortlisted artists are featured in Form Magazine, extending visibility beyond the fair. The theme is open, and both single images and series are accepted.

Eligibility is global, open to photographers 18 and over. Submission is through Picter with 1–8 images and a €35 fee. The deadline was extended to August 8, 2026.
