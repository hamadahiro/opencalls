Beyond the Image Art Prize 2026

Prize Solo exhibition

Via Gluck 62 AIR, a non-profit cultural association with its own space on via Gluck in Milan, has launched the first edition of the Beyond the Image Art Prize, an international open call for contemporary photography, video art, experimental film, moving images and hybrid or installation-based practices that use photographic and audiovisual language. Its full title is "Who Are You Beyond the Image? Art, Neuroscience and Algorithmic Narration of Experience", and it grows out of SatisFace, a research project on the face, self-image perception and the neuroscientific, psychological and cultural implications of representing identity. The prize wants work that reflects on identity, image, body perception, digital technology and the algorithmic narration of experience, read through documentary, conceptual, poetic, critical or installation approaches. Two projects are selected, one by an emerging artist and one by an established artist, and each gets a solo exhibition in the association's Milan space plus a place in the official programme of the Milano Centrale Festival 2026, curatorial and organisational support, promotion, and a possible contribution towards production costs agreed with the artist. Entry is open to any nationality with no age, education or career requirements, and collectives may apply. Applicants send a project proposal of up to 2,500 characters with up to five supporting images, a CV and a portfolio, all under 25 MB total. The €19 fee is paid by bank transfer. The exhibition runs 2–30 October 2026, opening during the festival.
