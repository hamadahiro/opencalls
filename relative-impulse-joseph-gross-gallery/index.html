Relative Impulse

Prize Group exhibition

A free open call from the Joseph Gross Gallery, the 2,000-square-foot contemporary space inside the University of Arizona School of Art in Tucson, next door to the University of Arizona Museum of Art. Relative Impulse features artworks for which the making — or destroying — of family archives is material and metaphor: "Funny, heart-wrenching, or bizarre, using the ephemera of memory itself or a copy of a copy, each artwork reminds us that everything's relative." Artists of any career stage and any media or material process are eligible, and work made at any time qualifies; up to three artworks may be submitted, each on its own form with two to four images, and there are no size restrictions. Curated by gallery director lydia see, with jurying, preparation, installation and marketing handled by the School of Art's Gallery Praxis class and its student gallery managers. There is no entry fee, but selected artists ship or hand-deliver their own work and provide a return shipping label — the gallery says shipping assistance may be available to artists for whom the cost would be a barrier to exhibiting. Notifications go out 8 September, work must arrive by 29 September, the exhibition runs 13 October 2026 to 28 January 2027 with a reception on 22 October, and packages are mailed back by 10 February 2027. Current University of Arizona students, staff and faculty may also submit. No living or organic matter may be exhibited, and selected works cannot be withdrawn before the exhibition closes.
