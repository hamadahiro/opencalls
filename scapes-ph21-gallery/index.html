–scapes

Curated international group exhibition of photographs of natural, built and staged scenes — landscapes, cityscapes, streetscapes, seascapes, waterscapes, cloudscapes, desertscapes, treescapes, bodyscapes — where the gallery is looking past the recording of a place to the interpretive insight of the photographer, work that asks us to contemplate a scene as meaningful rather than merely re-present it. The theme is broadly construed and independent of generic or stylistic constraints, and all creative photographic interpretations are welcome. Curated by Zsolt Bátori (PhD in Philosophy, Rutgers; founder, director and curator of PH21 Gallery, invited to Photolucida’s board in 2026) and Borbála Jász (art historian and philosopher of art, PH21 vice-director, also invited to Photolucida’s board in 2026). One curator’s choice and up to three honourable mentions are announced; the curator’s choice receives three free entries to any upcoming PH21 themed group call, each covering up to 15 images. Entrants may submit 3, 5, 7, 10 or 15 images, individual works or a series of at most five photographs. The standard ladder now runs €30 for the first 3 images, €35 for 5, €40 for 7, €45 for 10 and €50 for 15, and a reduced rate of €20 covers up to five images for students, unwaged applicants and those from low-income economies — the gallery asks for no proof of eligibility. The early-bird window closed on October 5, when those rates were €25–€45 and €15; the final deadline is October 12, and all entrants are notified 10–15 days after it. The exhibition runs December 12, 2026 – January 5, 2027 at PH21’s venue in Barcelona’s Gothic Quarter. Selected photographers pay a print and installation fee, from €45 for prints up to 40cm on the longest side, or may ship their own exhibit-ready prints; work need not be for sale, and the gallery commission on any sale is 25%.
