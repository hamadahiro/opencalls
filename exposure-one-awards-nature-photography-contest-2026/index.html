Exposure One Awards - Nature Photography Contest 2026

Prize $4,000

Exposure One's black-and-white nature contest, running for the first time as a separate cycle alongside the platform's Photographer of the Year and One Shot contests. Thirteen categories — Abstract, Aerial, Animals, Earth's Textures, Humans & Nature, Land, Light & Shadow, Minimalism, Ocean, Pattern & Form, Underwater, Wild Portraits and Other — and the organisers encourage an expansive reading of each; one image may be entered in several categories and is judged independently in each. Black and white only, with sepia and other single-tone images accepted. There is no cap on how many images you may submit, and a series is 2–5 images working within one concept. Prior publication, prior awards and work of any age are all fine; the contest does not require exclusivity. Over $4,000 in cash awards, plus an online winners' gallery, a profile in the Exposure One artist directory, a published interview in Exposure One Magazine, a custom press release, badges and certificates, and the option to buy a trophy. Judged by five institutional jurors. AI-generated images are not admissible in any Exposure One contest, and the organisers reserve the right to ask for proof of original files. The entry ladder runs from $15 for a student single image to $50 for a professional series, with 15% off during early bird (to 11 November) and 10% off in the regular round (to 8 December); additional categories are half price, and 5- and 10-credit bundles cut the per-entry cost further. The published schedule runs early bird to 11 November and regular entry to 8 December 2026, with winners announced 20 January 2027 — 8 December is the last date the organiser prints anywhere on the page, and it is the date stored here. The page's underlying schedule data also carries an extended round to 5 January 2027, but the organiser keeps that round hidden on this contest where it publishes it on its sister contests, so treat 8 December as the deadline and do not plan around a later round that has not been announced.
