VI Premio Editorial de Fotografía Documental Galega

Prizes Photobook published by Alauda Negra €1,000

The sixth edition of the Galician Documentary Photography Publishing Prize, run by the non-profit Asociación Recreativo Cultural Acolá — the association behind FFoco, the A Coruña photography festival, which marks its tenth edition at the Fundación Luis Seoane between October and December 2026 — and financed in full by the Fundación Marta Ortega Pérez. The prize exists to turn a documentary project into a book: entrants are not expected to arrive with a finished, print-ready dummy, and the organisers describe the call as accompanying a project until it acquires new value in book form. Open to photographers and collectives whose work is connected to Galicia, satisfying at least one of three conditions — born in Galicia, resident in Galicia, or presenting a project centred on or thematically and conceptually linked to Galicia. Subject matter is free, but the work must be documentary, original and previously unpublished in book form with no edition committed to a third party; earlier showing in exhibitions, magazines, digital publications or social media does not disqualify a project as long as the author still holds the rights needed to publish it as a book. Entry is a single PDF of at most 20 MB containing an essay or maquette of 30 to 60 photographs, plus a project description and a biography of up to 300 words each. An independent jury of publishing professionals and representatives of established imprints picks up to eight finalists and then one winner, judging technical quality, originality, narrative capacity, coherence and editorial viability; the jury's composition is published with the result and its decision is final. The winning project is published by the Galician imprint Alauda Negra with Acolá covering every editorial and production cost — image editing and selection, editorial direction and design, layout, pre-press, paper, printing and distribution — and the winner also receives €1,000 for the publishing rights under an editorial contract. Non-winning projects keep all their rights. The five previous winners are Ana Paes, Ana Núñez Rodríguez, the Cadela Negra collective, Iván Nespereira and Jesús García. Submissions close Sunday 18 October 2026 at 23:59 peninsular Spanish time.
