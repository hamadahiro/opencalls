2027–2028 Gallery Season

Prize Exhibition

Annual juried call that fills a whole exhibition year at the Pump House Regional Arts Center, a non-profit arts centre in an 1880 city water pumping station in downtown La Crosse, Wisconsin, converted to a cultural centre in 1977. Accepted artists are scheduled into one of three galleries for a show somewhere between 1 September 2027 and 31 August 2028; the largest, the Kader Gallery, has about 100 linear feet of wall split across two facing walls so that two 2-D artists are often featured at once. The call is open to all styles and to 2D and 3D work, and photography is named among the accepted media alongside painting, sculpture, printmaking, fibre and mixed media. A jury committee of art professionals, educators, artists and patrons — volunteers serving staggered three-year terms — selects from the applications and tells the gallery curator which opening to offer each artist. Conditions worth reading first: work must have been made in the past two years, be unified in theme, and must not have been shown within a 50-mile radius of La Crosse in the previous twelve months; the artist must not have exhibited in the same medium at the Pump House in the previous five years; all work must be for sale, and the Pump House handles sales and retains a 40% commission, so the artist receives 60%; and artists must be available to deliver and collect their own work. The $25 jury fee is waived outright for current Pump House members (membership starts at $35 a year, $15 for students), and a limited number of jury-fee scholarships are available on request.
