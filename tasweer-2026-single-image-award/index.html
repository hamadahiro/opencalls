Tasweer 2026 Single Image Award

Annual single-image competition from Tasweer, Qatar's photo festival organised by Qatar Museums. Open to photographers aged 18+ currently residing in the Western Asia and North Africa (WANA) region. Each photographer submits one image representing their personal narrative and experience. Winners receive 2,000 QAR per awardee, with selected images showcased in an online exhibition and featured as part of Tasweer Photo Festival Qatar's public and digital programme. Submit one JPEG or TIFF, 3000 pixels max on longest side, 3–8 MB.
