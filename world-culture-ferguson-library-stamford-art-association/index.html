World Culture: Art at The Ferguson Library

Multimedia juried exhibition from the Stamford Art Association on the theme of world culture — food, regional clothing and customs, ceremonies, cultural patterns and abstractions, open to interpretation by artists of all backgrounds and experience levels. Photography, painting, drawing, printmaking and any medium welcome; up to 5 works. Up to $400 in awards for excellence and conformity to the theme, with a 30% commission on sales. Work must be wired for hanging and hand delivered. Shown at The Ferguson Library, 3rd floor, Stamford, September 10, 2026 – January 7, 2027, with an opening reception September 10.
