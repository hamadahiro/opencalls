Richards Family Prize 2027

Prizes $4,000 Griffin Museum Press catalogue Exhibition

The Richards Family Prize is the Griffin Museum of Photography's award for photographers past the emerging stage. It is $4,000, described by the museum as a cash prize to assist in the production of the work, and it comes with a catalogue published by Griffin Museum Press and an exhibition at the museum in Winchester, Massachusetts.

The museum sets out what it means by its own terms. Professional is any photographer who earns the majority of their income from photography. Mid-career is any photographer with some visibility in exhibitions, gallery representation, and scholarship or residency success. The prize is open worldwide.

The brief is broad on medium and on approach: fine art through documentary and photojournalism, digital through film-based work and cameraless images, and the eligibility text extends to individual works of photography, installation, moving images and sculpture, whether finished or in progress.

The juror for the 2026/2027 cycle is Alison Nordström - an independent scholar, writer and curator who was founding director and senior curator of the Southeast Museum of Photography and senior curator of photographs and director of exhibitions at George Eastman House. She has curated more than 150 exhibitions in nine countries, among them Truth/Beauty: Pictorialism and the Photograph as Art, 1845-1945, and is the author of over 100 books and essays.

Entry is free and runs through CaFE, with 5 to 10 images accepted. The application period for the 2027 cycle opens Monday October 5, 2026 and closes Friday November 6, 2026 at 23:59 Mountain Time, and the museum publishes no notification date. The two previous winners were Izabella Demavlys (2024, Without a Face) and Lee-Ann Olwage (2025, The Right to Play).
