ADEX Dugong Photo of the Year 2027

Prize Exhibition

Conservation photography campaign from Asia Dive Expo (ADEX) and Asian Geographic spotlighting dugongs and their threatened seagrass habitats. Submit up to three images of dugongs in the wild or the underwater and coastal environments linked to them, each with a short story of 80–150 words; entrants also post their submission on Instagram with @AsiaDiveExpo added as collaborator. Free to enter. The grand prize winner is hosted at ADEX 2027 in Singapore with accommodation covered, receives a feature showcase, and is presented at the Blue Legacy Awards on April 11, 2027; shortlisted photographers are exhibited at ADEX 2027, featured online, and receive digital recognition. Public voting begins January 11, 2027.
