The Fine Odd

Prize Publication (print)

The Fine Odd is a fine-art publication built around a condition rather than a theme. HFP sets “odd” as the premise, leaves its meaning to the artist — a fetish, an obsession, an object, a gesture, a visual language — and the curator decides which responses share a frame. The FAQ is explicit that work does not have to be explicitly odd and does not have to be 18+, though 18+ themes, sexuality and desire are welcome. It publishes as two volumes: 01 Photography, open to conceptual, documentary, staged, experimental, analogue, digital, fashion and manipulated practices, as a single image or a series; 02 Visual Art & Print-Based Work for two-dimensional practices.

Selected artists get two dedicated spreads, 2–5 works sequenced into the edit, a bespoke layout with full credits, and permanent archiving in the HFP repository. The terms add that included artists typically receive a complimentary digital copy plus a discounted price on physical copies and a wholesale discount for bulk orders. Worth reading before submitting: the licence is non-exclusive and artists keep full copyright, but it also lets HFP produce and sell derivative retail products — prints, postcard sets — from selected work. Single-artist products pay the artist 55% per unit sold; multi-artist bundles split revenue evenly among that bundle's artists for six months, after which HFP may sell remaining stock without further payment.

Entry is $15 for up to three pieces or $25 for up to five, non-refundable once a submission enters review, and the higher tier does not affect the odds of selection. Initial uploads are preview files only — JPG or PNG, 2 MB maximum, 2000 px on the long side — with production files requested from selected artists. Entrants must be 18 or over, with no education or country restriction, though HFP notes artists in Vietnam may currently be unable to pay because of cross-border PayPal limits. No curator is named and no deadline or notification date is published. This is HFP's first open call carrying a submission fee: the free founding issue closed in July 2026 and its artbook has not appeared yet.
