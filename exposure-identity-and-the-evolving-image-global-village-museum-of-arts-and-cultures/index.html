Exposure: Identity and the Evolving Image

Prize Museum exhibition

International juried photography exhibition at the Global Village Museum of Arts and Cultures in Fort Collins, Colorado, inviting photographers to explore identity through self-portraiture and photographic imagery — asking how we see ourselves and what it means to be seen. The brief connects contemporary photographic practice with the medium's technical and cultural history, including the legacy of Kodak in Northern Colorado. Open to photographers 18+ worldwide; work must have been created between 2016 and 2026. Selected works are exhibited and professionally printed at 16×20 inches. Exhibition runs October 2, 2026 – January 22, 2027.
