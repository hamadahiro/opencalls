Book Artist in Residence 2028

Prizes $350/week stipend $500 materials Housing

A four-week residency on Maine Media's campus in Rockport, Maine, running April 1-29, 2028, for artists who combine word and image in book form. The resident has unlimited access to the Charles Altschul Book Arts Studio - a Vandercook Universal I press, two etching presses, a photopolymer platemaker and bindery equipment - which sits next to the school's alternative-process photography lab. The programme is open to emerging and practising artists, book artists, letterpress printers and photographers who combine word and image, and is designed to help the resident complete a proposed, editioned project. Residents needing housing receive a $350 weekly stipend, up to $500 for materials and travel, campus meals when the cafeteria is open, and an apartment with a kitchen within walking distance; an applicant within driving distance who needs no housing receives $1,000 plus $500 for supplies. Collaborations are considered, with the same funding regardless of the number of participants. Selection is in two rounds - a written proposal, then an invited physical maquette of the proposed book, actual size, with at least two spreads. The resident donates two pieces from the edition to the Maine Media Gallery, gives a public lecture or other public engagement, and hosts an open studio. Applications are sent by email with an artist statement and CV, a one-page project description, an outline budget and ten images of recent work.
