AUTOPORTRAIT 2026: PLAY

Third interim edition of Kuala Lumpur Photo Awards' AUTOPORTRAIT open call, run this year with FORMAT International Photography Festival and taking FORMAT's 2027 festival theme, PLAY, as its brief. KLPA frames self-portraiture as always grounded in experimentation, trial and error and creative playfulness, and reads PLAY widely - fun, joyful, exploratory, experimental, deceitful, mischievous, subversive or rule-bending - from camera play and questioning the medium itself to documenting the act of play. Prizes are €1,000, €500 and €300, and the top 35 finalists have their portraits promoted online and screened at FORMAT27 in Derby, United Kingdom (11 March to 11 April 2027) and at future KLPA exhibitions in Kuala Lumpur. The jury is Jodi Kwok (curator at QUAD and FORMAT), Enrico Stefanelli (founder and artistic director of Photolux Festival, Lucca), D. M. Terblanche (South African self-taught artist, 2026 New Eyes Award recipient), Mari Katayama (Japanese artist whose self-portraits use her own body as sculpture and lens) and Sean Cham (artist, historian and RCA tutor). Open to professional and amateur photographers from all countries aged 16 or over on the deadline date; each submission takes up to 10 single images with captions, priced €12 for one, €45 for three to six and €65 for seven to ten, with the two-image tier quoted as €25 twice in the body of the Picter submission page a submitter reads before paying, and as €24 on the KLPA site and inside Picter’s own structured callInfo.costs field (this listing stores €24, the figure on the KLPA site and in Picter's structured costs field; the €25 in Picter's prose body is unexplained), with a maximum of two submissions per photographer, and full-time students under 25 may enter one image free with proof of enrolment, judged alongside the main category. All photographs must have been taken between 2024 and 2026 (proof may be requested at verification), and entries are judged blind. The rules bar commercial or advertising images, heavily manipulated, HDR or content-altered work, and AI prompt-generated images in whole or in part. The call opens on 25 September 2026 - entry will be through the KLPA site's ENTER link or its Picter page, with payment by card via Picter - and closes at 23:59 Malaysian time (UTC+8) on Saturday 28 November 2026, the organiser having extended it from 25 November. Jury selection in early December, shortlist in mid-January 2027 and prizewinners announced in February 2027.
