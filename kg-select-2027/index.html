KG+SELECT 2027

Open call from KG+, the public art festival KYOTOGRAPHIE organises in Kyoto alongside its own International Photography Festival, for a solo exhibition proposal. Applicants must propose a solo show featuring photography or work incorporating photographic elements — moving image, installation and related media all count — and duos, collectives and collaborative groups are welcome. There is no theme, and fine art, conceptual, contemporary and documentary practices are all invited. Applications are open regardless of age, profession, nationality, experience, or whether an applicant is self-nominated or nominated by others; previously exhibited and unpublished work are equally eligible. A jury, to be announced, selects roughly ten finalists, each of whom receives a ¥200,000 production grant with no receipts required, around 20 square metres in a shared venue, and a place in the group exhibition running 17 April to 16 May 2027. During the festival the jury visits the exhibition and names one KG+SELECT Award winner, who receives a ¥500,000 production grant, a solo exhibition in the official programme of KYOTOGRAPHIE 2028, venue and production support, inclusion in the official catalogue and PR support; the jury may decline to give the award. Applications close 10 October 2026 at 23:59 JST and finalists are announced on the KG+ site in early December. The ¥10,000 fee is non-refundable and includes one KYOTOGRAPHIE 2027 festival passport per application. Applicants plan for a roughly 4 by 5 metre white cube and submit up to 20 images or a presentation video, a floor plan, an artist portrait and texts in both Japanese and English. Printing, framing, equipment rental, projector use, transport and installation are all at the artist's expense, and supervising the exhibition during the festival is the artist's responsibility, though one or two venue staff oversee the shared space and artists are not required to be present throughout.
