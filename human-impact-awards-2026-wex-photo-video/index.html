Human Impact Awards 2026

Prize £5,000

Wex Photo Video's annual documentary award, run in partnership with Sony, asks for a 5–10 image series exploring how people shape, affect and respond to the world around them. The brief is deliberately open — community and belonging, identity and representation, climate and land, urban change, protest and social change, care and resilience, ritual, technology, inequality, displacement or simply everyday humanity. Human impact does not have to mean crisis; the judges are looking for thoughtful visual stories with a clear point of view that hold together as a body of work rather than a set of strong singles. Entry is free with no purchase necessary, and images made on any camera, phone or system are welcome. The open Professional Award pays £5,000 for first place, £3,000 for second and £1,000 for third. The Phil Smith Student Photography Award, named in memory of a member of the Wex business team, awards Sony camera and lens kits to its top three students, who enter with a school or university email address. Entries are reviewed by an independent panel of photographers and industry professionals. Photographers retain full copyright; AI-generated imagery and stock photography are disqualified. Open to UK residents aged 18 or over.
