What Would You Do With $1000

Prizes $1,000 One-year NMSAF membership

A microgrant from the No More Starving Artists Foundation, a Palm Beach County 501(c)(3) whose stated mission is to help artists build a sustainable practice and a lasting legacy. The $1,000 award is deliberately open-ended: it is meant to fund the creation of new work, the completion of a project already under way, professional development, or a new experimental concept that pushes the artist’s work forward. It is open to professional artists over 18 who live in Palm Beach County only, at emerging, mid-career or established stage, and across every discipline the foundation lists — visual artists, musicians, vocalists, literary artists, designers, dancers, culinary artists, poets, writers and filmmakers — so photographers apply as visual artists. Applicants must not be currently enrolled in an active educational programme, and may only apply as a solo artist: no collaborations and no teams. All submitted works, concepts and accompanying materials must be original and, in the foundation’s words, conceived and executed solely by human hands, minds or voices — generative AI is explicitly barred. The application asks for an artist statement (not a bio or résumé), a detailed written description of the specific idea the artist wants to realise and why the funding matters for it, a short 30-to-60-second proposal video to accompany that narrative, and three work samples. The entry fee is $10 and non-refundable; the number of awards is not fixed and jurors are to be announced. Recipients also receive a one-year NMSAF membership. The award is not an unconditional grant: the foundation's legal agreement binds every recipient to a Pay It Forward commitment to volunteer in the community within 12 months and to file documented proof of it — a signed letter from the organisation served, or five photographs, or a video — and states that if the proof is not provided, “the entire grant will revert to a loan and must be repaid to NMSAF”, with a 1.5% monthly finance charge on any delinquent balance. Recipients also file a W-9.
