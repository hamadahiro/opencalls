No-Type Issue No. 03 — America

Prize Publication prize

Themed open call from No-Type, an independent Chicago photography publisher, for the third issue of its magazine. Guided by Langston Hughes' poem "Let America Be America Again," the issue invites photographs exploring American identity and life — community, joy, and resilience as well as inequality and social challenge — and what America is, has been, and might become. Guest-edited by Chicago-based photographer Kenn Cook Jr. Selected photographers announced September 15, 2026; high-resolution files due September 22.
