ProfiFoto New Talent Award 26/2

Prizes Share of €3,000 Publication Exhibition

Second 2026 round of the New Talent Award from German photography magazine ProfiFoto, run in cooperation with Canon, WhiteWall and Hahnemühle for aspiring photographers who want to present their work to a professional audience. Submit a project concept (max. 2,000 characters) with 5 to 10 sample images reflecting your personal visual language — the call is open theme and open to any photographer. Winning works are published in ProfiFoto and shown as WhiteWall prints in a group exhibition; from the ten winning works across the year's two rounds, the jury selects the three best for cash prizes totaling €3,000.
