Susan McLaughlin Risograph Photobook Award 2026

Award from New York's Penumbra Foundation honouring risograph-printed photobooks. Open to artists, publishers and printers worldwide for risograph photobooks made between July 2024 and July 2026, where photography gives the context of the narrative or content. Submit one physical copy of the book by mail along with a printed entry form; entry is free, with applicants covering shipping costs (international entrants declare the book as a donation with no commercial value). The winner receives a $5,000 award and is presented at Penumbra's In The Fold photobook forum, with additional online features. Books produced through the Penumbra Risograph Residency are not eligible. Juror to be announced.
