Photographic Exploration Project: ENTANGLEMENT

Prize Exhibition prize

Photographic Exploration Project (PEP) is a Berlin-based photography platform founded by Bénédicte Blondeau. The 2026 themed open call ENTANGLEMENT asks photographers to engage with the intricate webs of connections that hold things together — from intimate relationships through ecological systems to global networks.

Selection is by Rüdiger Lange (Artistic Director, Loop – Raum für aktuelle Kunst, the Berlin contemporary art space) and Bénédicte Blondeau (PEP Founder & Curator).

Selected work is shown in a group exhibition at Loop Raum in Berlin in July 2026.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through JotForm with 1–10 images. Fee is tiered €5–€15 by submission scale. The 2026 deadline was March 31; exhibition opens July 2026.
