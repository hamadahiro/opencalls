Small Art Show 2026

The Paseo Arts Association's annual small-works juried exhibition, held in Gallery II of the Paseo Arts & Creativity Center in Oklahoma City's historic Paseo Arts District through November and December. The brief is deliberately collector-friendly: original work no larger than 15 inches in any direction including frame, priced between $25 and $199. Open to all mediums of visual art in two and three dimensions, and the PAA's own preparation guidance names photography directly — "photos printed on metal do not need wire, if the hanging hardware is provided" — while the association also runs a dedicated annual photography show, Paseo Photofest, and a photography studio at the PACC. Reproduction prints are not eligible. Awards are cash: one Best of Show at $150, two Merit Awards at $75 each, and four to six honourable mentions, announced by the juror at the opening reception during the Paseo Arts District's First Friday Gallery Walk on November 6. The 2026 juror is Erica Nkechi, Executive Director of Heart Studios. The flat fee covers up to three works and is $15 for PAA members, $20 for everyone else; membership starts at $40 a year. Two things to plan for: artwork must be dropped off in person in Oklahoma City on Saturday, October 31 and collected on Saturday, December 19, and all work must be for sale. The association's commission on this show is stated inconsistently on its own site — the entry form and the call page both say 30%, while the juried-shows overview page says 30% generally but 20% for the Small Art Show — so confirm the rate before pricing. Entries close October 5 and selections are announced October 10.
