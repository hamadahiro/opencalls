Graduate Artist Residency

A free one-month studio residency at OVADA's warehouse on Osney Lane in central Oxford, running Friday 23 October to Thursday 19 November 2026. It is open to artists living in Oxfordshire who studied Fine Art - in Oxfordshire or elsewhere - and who graduated from university within the last 24 months; joint applications from two artists are welcome, and OVADA says it is particularly interested in practitioners who would benefit from dedicated studio space and a sustained period of time to develop their practice. The residency provides a central Oxford studio with 24-hour access, free Wi-Fi and power, communal facilities, a community of artists working in the studios, and a connection to the exhibitions and events programme across the warehouse; an open studio during the residency can be arranged with OVADA. Accommodation and materials are NOT provided, and the space must be fully vacated by 5:00pm on 19 November. Practical limits worth noting for process-based work: heavy machinery, welding tools and loud equipment are not permitted, and practices involving solvents, aerosols or materials requiring specialist ventilation cannot be undertaken indoors. Applicants submit a link to their website and/or artist Instagram, a PDF portfolio and a statement of up to 300 words on what they would like to gain from the residency, by Sunday 27 September at 12:00 PM. Two things to know before applying: the residency page names NO accepted media at all, so photography is neither named nor excluded - OVADA's own gallery open call that closed on 21 September did list photography among the media it welcomes - and although the page says an application form follows, no form or application link is published on it, so contact@ovada.org.uk is the only route OVADA gives. OVADA is a non-profit company limited by guarantee (no. 5138370).
