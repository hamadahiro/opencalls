All About Women 2026

Prize $500 / $300 / $100

The Marin Society of Artists has run a gallery and artist community in San Rafael, California since 1927, and All About Women is its annual online exhibition about women's lives - past, present and future - and the issues that concern them. It is open to all artists aged 18 and over regardless of gender, and photography is named in the accepted-media list alongside painting, sculpture, printmaking, mixed media, fiber art, ceramics and all types of fine art craft; film and video are the only exclusions. The juror is Nancy Roberts, a professional artist and educator based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Selected work is presented in two forms: an online show in the MSA Virtual Gallery, which carries each artist's price and contact information, and a video version of the whole exhibition that stays available on YouTube after the show closes. A virtual Zoom reception is held on 11 October 2026 at 5pm PST. Awards are $500 first prize, $300 second and $100 third, plus several non-monetary honourable mentions, and MSA takes no commission on work sold. Entry is through CaFE: $35 for three pieces for non-members and $30 for MSA members, then $7 for each additional piece up to a total of ten; diptychs and triptychs count as one piece and should be submitted as a single image. Work previously juried into an MSA exhibition is not accepted. Entries close 15 September 2026 at 11:59pm Mountain Time and the exhibition runs 29 September to 30 November 2026.
