God Is Dead — Premio BìFoto Fest 2026

Prize Exhibition

Themed award of BìFoto Fest, the international photography festival of Sardinia, whose 16th edition opens in Mogoro on October 10, 2026. The theme draws on 'Dio è morto' (God is dead) by Italian band I Nomadi — a song that moves through disillusionment and loss while leaving room for hope. Open worldwide to photographers 18+, with projects of 8 to 12 photographs in any genre relating to the theme. Three winning projects are printed and exhibited at the festival, with accommodation and meals covered for the opening day and long-term visibility of the work.
