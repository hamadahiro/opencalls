Hasselblad Foundation Nature Photography Grant 2026

Grant for photographic explorations of nature, landscape, and environment. Emphasis on originality, environmental issues, biodiversity, and human impact on nature and climate. Open to citizens of Nordic countries or permanent residents of the Nordic region. Projects should have a 1–3 year timeframe.

Prize 100,000 SEK prize
DeadlineMay 8, 2026

Entry feeFree
EligibilityNordic only
