BBA One Shot Award - Winter 2027

Prizes €300 Berlin exhibition

BBA Gallery's single-image prize, the Berlin gallery's most accessible open call: "We are looking for that one definitive frame that stops viewers in their tracks." One photograph, one €19 entry, and the gallery's own summary of the rules is "1 shot - 0 rules (almost)" - "You do not need a massive portfolio or an expensive series to make an impact in the art world", "We welcome artists of any background, nationality, and ability, our only rule is that you must be over 18 to apply", and "You can submit work in absolutely any photographic medium or genre." A shortlist of 20 images is professionally printed and hung at BBA Gallery with all production costs covered by the gallery, and a longlist of 50 is shown on a large digital display in the space; there is no shipping cost to the artist. First prize is €300 in cash plus the exhibition, an Artsy profile and promotion through the gallery's social channels and newsletter, and selected images are also featured weekly online as Shot of the Week. BBA runs these prizes as an entry point into its gallery programme rather than as a one-off: "We built these open calls not just to hand out titles, but to foster real connections between emerging talent and the established art world", and it says prize artists go on to its Berlin events, group shows, art-fair representation and solo exhibitions. Previous One Shot winners include Michael Bulgrin (Germany), Rik Roos and Peter Alfred Hamelinck (Netherlands), Pervin Donmez (Latvia) and Calogero Cammalleri (Italy). Two conditions sit in the terms rather than the pitch: submitted work "must be available exclusive for sale throughout the exhibition and three months after if sales are made through BBA Gallery", and the gallery takes 50% commission on anything it sells. Entry is one .jpg, .png or .heic file up to 3 MB, uploaded after the fee is paid.
