Collectives' Hub 2026

Prize Group exhibition

Photo Fringe is a volunteer-led registered charity in Brighton that has run a biennial open-platform photography festival since 2003. The Collectives' Hub has been part of the festival since 2014 and is consistently one of its most visited and critically discussed exhibitions; for the twelfth edition, up to eight photography or lens-based collectives will be selected to build their own shows inside the main gallery at Phoenix Art Space, on view 2 October to 22 November 2026. Work should respond to Future Histories, the theme curating the Main Gallery this year — an invitation to consider how the past keeps configuring and sometimes overshadowing the present, and how what we do now is already writing tomorrow's history. Collectives interpret that freely. A collective needs at least three people, with no upper limit; spaces run between 3.5 and 5 metres wide and are allocated by Photo Fringe producer Ricardo Reverón Blanco and Phoenix curator Laurence Hill. Work can be wall-hung or freestanding, collectives install and de-install themselves with a Photo Fringe technician on hand, and every selected group also makes an online exhibition on photofringe.org. Entry is free: apply by emailing up to 200 words on the proposed exhibition, up to 200 words on the collective, up to 12 JPEGs and a website or Instagram link — video applications in BSL or English and audio applications of up to three minutes are equally welcome. If selected, each collective pays the subsidised Photo Fringe 2026 group registration fee of £150 plus £130 towards production by 2 September, produces its own exhibition, and covers travel and transport; Phoenix Art Space takes 35% commission on sales. Submissions close 11.59pm on Sunday 9 August 2026 and shortlisted collectives hear back on 26 August.
