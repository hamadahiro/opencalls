BBA One Shot Award - Spring 2026

Prize €300 prize

BBA Gallery is a Berlin-based contemporary art gallery that runs several international prizes across photography, painting, and sculpture. The One Shot Award is the gallery's single-image photography call, running multiple editions per year — the Spring 2026 edition closes May 25.

The format is intentionally minimal: one image, one entry, €19 fee. Twenty selected images are printed and exhibited at BBA Gallery in Berlin; thirty additional images are shown on digital display. The first-place winner receives €300 cash, the exhibition, social media promotion, and an Artsy profile listing — Artsy being the major online art marketplace.

BBA covers all production and shipping costs for selected work. Eligibility is open globally to any artist 18+, any photographic medium or genre. Submission is through the BBA Prizes platform: JPG, PNG, or HEIC up to 3MB, with name, country, image title, and year of creation.
