Hayama Artist Residency 2027

Prizes Funded residency $800 stipend Exhibition

Hayama Artist Residency sends artists to Hayama, a coastal town of 33,000 on Japan's Miura Peninsula facing Sagami Bay, for four weeks from 1 to 30 June 2027. Founded and directed by curator Dexter Wimberly, the residency is fully funded: a roundtrip flight to Narita or Haneda, a private bedroom in shared accommodation at hôtel ami hayama for the month, and $800 USD toward meals and local transport. Artists are not required to make new work — the programme is built around rest, research and reflection, immersion in Japanese culture, and meeting artists, curators and gallery owners in Japan. A key component is a group exhibition during the stay, hosted with KOKI ARTS, a contemporary gallery in Tokyo's Bakurocho art district, whose director works with the residency's curatorial team on selection, consignment, shipping and insurance. Open to any visual artist over 21 working in any medium, based anywhere in the world; photography sits squarely in scope — past residents include Joiri Minaya, whom the residency describes as working in photography, digital media, film, performance, sculpture, textiles and painting, and Asim Waqif, who has worked in sculpture, site-specific public installation, video and photography. Applications are reviewed by the director and a selection committee, which for 2026 comprised Alexander Berggruen, Francesca Bertolotti-Bailey, Hilary Burt of SOCO Gallery, Kimberli Gant of the Brooklyn Museum and the artist Yoshua Okón. Submit up to 10 samples in any combination of JPEG, audio or video, with links accepted on a one-page PDF. The $95 application fee is waived for first-time applicants who apply again the following year. Applicants are notified by 15 December 2026.
