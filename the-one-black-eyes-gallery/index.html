The One

Prize Exhibition

Black Eyes Gallery opens with The One, a call for the single strongest photograph an artist has made: the frame they obsess over, the one that opens a portfolio, the one where everything aligned. Any genre is welcome, documentary through portrait, landscape, abstract and conceptual, provided the work is photographic or lens based, and anything created entirely or substantially with artificial intelligence is disqualified. Twenty-five images are selected by juror Douglas Fur, a nationally exhibited Tucson photographer with a 2006 BFA from Cornish College of the Arts who spent two years at Decode Gallery and is now Artist Liaison and Gallery Manager for Tucson Erotica. Selected photographers send a file and nothing else: the gallery prints, mats and frames every accepted image at its own expense for the show at 485 S. Stone from September 26 to October 31, then holds the single exhibition print in its archive for two years before destroying it. Accepted work also enters a permanent online archive carrying the artist's name, image title and social link, and is promoted across the gallery's social channels, email list and website with an individual feature on the Black Eyes Instagram. Entry is $40 for up to five images or $50 for up to ten, with a reduced $20 fee held for two artists per call on a first come first served basis for anyone who writes in before submitting, and any entry that produces no selection earns $5 off the next one. Submissions are reviewed blind where possible, on creativity, originality, interpretation of the theme and how an image works against the exhibition as a whole, with CV and bio largely set aside. Work may optionally be offered for sale at a price the artist sets, the gallery retaining 50 per cent, and photographers keep full copyright throughout.
