Come In! Galerie Waidhofen/Ybbs — Open Call 2027

The call names no medium, asking only for "Deine Werke" and a "Werkübersicht für die geplante Ausstellung (Fotos oder Links)" — and "Fotos" there is the format of the portfolio, not the medium of the work, so it settles nothing on its own.

The programme does settle it. The organiser's own Come In! Galerie page names the standing selection panel, and one of the six is Verena Mayrhofer, described by the organiser as an artist in "Medienkunst, Installation, Fotografie" and holding its Coordination Office for Visual Art. The rest span sound art and performance, curating and art history, illustration, provincial visual-art administration and stage design — a panel built to read across media rather than to score one, which is what an unrestricted first-show call needs.

That same page states the shape of the offer independently of the open call text — "Es finden 6 Ausstellungen pro Jahr statt" and "€ 500,- Honorar + bis zu € 500,- Materialkostenersatz" — so the six slots and the money are each stated twice, on two surfaces. Two conditions are worth knowing before applying: artists are responsible for installing their own show ("Die Künstlerinnen und Künstler sind für den Aufbau der Ausstellung selbst verantwortlich"), with support available but by arrangement, and the gallery expects the artist in person at the opening as well as across the first exhibition weekend.
