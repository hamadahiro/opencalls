2027 Exhibition Open Call

PS122 Gallery, the not-for-profit alternative exhibition space that has run out of the city-owned 122 Community Center in New York's East Village since 1979, invites proposals for its 2027 season. A three-person jury of arts professionals will select six curator- and/or artist-led exhibitions, each running for a month in the gallery's roughly 1,000 square foot space, with exhibitions scheduled across February, March, April, June, September and October 2027. Proposals must involve at least two artists working in any medium, and either the curator or the artists must live in one of the five New York City boroughs; collectives are encouraged, as are experimental uses of the space, in which case public programmes with an aesthetic component are welcomed but not required. Currently enrolled students are not eligible and each applicant is limited to one submission. The application is free and asks for a 500-word exhibition proposal, the names of the confirmed participating artists, a combined PDF of up to ten images of work, installation shots or a proposed layout with captions, and a combined PDF of CVs for everyone involved. Held annually since the 1986-87 season and restarted in 2023, the open call helped define the gallery's history: it gave early exhibitions to Peter Halley, Keith Haring and Amy Sillman, and the programme has shown Nan Goldin, Barbara Kruger, Louise Bourgeois, Glenn Ligon, Alfredo Jaar, Kiki Smith and David Wojnarowicz among hundreds of others. The gallery is a programme of Painting Space 122 Inc, an artist-run cooperative, and is supported in part by public funds from the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs. Applicants are notified in October 2026.
