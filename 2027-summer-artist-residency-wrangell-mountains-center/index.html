2027 Summer Artist Residency

Two artists receive a free three-week residency at the Wrangell Mountains Center in McCarthy, inside Wrangell-St. Elias National Park; six to eight more take subsidised stays of two to six weeks at roughly $100 a day. All residents get a private cabin, studio space and shared meals, but travel and stipends are not covered. Open to artists and writers at any career stage.

DeadlineJanuary 17, 2027
PrizesFree residency, Cabin, Meals
Entry fee$15 (waiver on request)
LocationMcCarthy, AK
Requirements5-10 images
