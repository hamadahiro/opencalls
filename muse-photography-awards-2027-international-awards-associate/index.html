MUSE Photography Awards 2027

Prize $3,000

The 2027 MUSE Photography Awards are open. International Awards Associate — the group behind several geographically titled photography and design competitions — runs the awards across 12 main categories (Advertising, Architecture, Black & White, Commercial, Editorial, Fashion, Film, Fine Art, Minimalist, Nature, People and a Special Category) subdivided into 345 subcategories, with separate Professional and Amateur/Student divisions carrying the same categories. Entry closes in four stages: early bird 16 October to 19 November 2026, regular 20 November to 17 December 2026, final 18 December 2026 to 14 January 2027, and a final extension 15 January to 25 February 2027. Each stage costs more than the last. A professional single image runs $30 early bird to $45 at final extension and a professional series of two to eight images $40 to $55; an amateur or student single runs $25 to $40 and an amateur or student series $35 to $50. Additional categories are $20 to $35, and a Multi Entry Pass is $185 for ten credits, one credit per category entry. Photographer of the Year takes $3,000 in the Professional division and $2,000 in Amateur/Student, with the MUSE statuette; Category Winner of the Year takes $100. The Platinum, Gold and Silver tiers below that are scored bands (85–100, 75–84, 65–74) on the jury's 1–100 scale and carry no cash — winners receive winner pages, interviews, e-certificates and badges, and may buy a personalised statuette. Cash prizes do not stack, so each winner receives only the highest one they qualify for. Results are announced on 16 April 2027. The competition is open to entrants aged 18 and over worldwide, professional, amateur and student alike. Entry is a single image or a series of two to eight images; work must have been made within the last five years (2023–2027), and previously published or previously awarded photographs are allowed. Judging is blind. Entering means creating an account on the awards site. The Special Category carries a dedicated AI-Generated Photography subcategory (SP2701); no surface we could read states a policy on AI use in the other eleven categories.
