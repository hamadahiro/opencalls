World Food Photography Awards 2027

Prizes £5,000 Trophy and Mall Galleries London exhibition 4-week residency in Scotland

The 2027 edition of the long-running international food photography competition run by Food Photographer of the Year Ltd, with entries open since 8 September 2026 and closing 7 February 2027. Twenty-seven categories cover the genre broadly — Food at the Table, Food for Sale, Food for Celebration, UNIDO Future of Food, A Feast of Flowers (a one-year-only category marking the 2027 Awards coinciding with the RHS Chelsea Flower Show), the Award for Innovation, the Newcomer Prize for first-time entrants, World Food Programme Food for Life, London Museum London Tastes, the Claire Aho Award for Women Photographers and a dedicated Louis Jadot Wine Photographer of the Year, plus On The Phone for smartphone work. The overall winner takes £5,000 and a trophy; all category winners receive a trophy and the opportunity to have their work displayed at the Mall Galleries, London in a free-to-enter public exhibition. Category finalists are invited to the VIP Awards Evening Reception at the Mall Galleries (travel and accommodation not included); the published exhibition offer is to winners rather than to all finalists. The Award for Innovation winner, supported by the Hugo Burge Foundation, gets a four-week residency in Scotland from 23 August to 19 September 2027 plus a £1,400 stipend; the Louis Jadot category winner takes £1,000. Standard entry is £35 per adult entrant covering up to six images, with further images at £7 each and no cap on the number submitted; the Food Stylist category is £35 per portfolio of five, and single entries to On The Phone are £7 (£2 of each going to Action Against Hunger). Three categories are free to enter, each capped at five entries: the Jamie Oliver Youth Prize for under-18s; the RPS Student Food Photographer of the Year, open to anyone currently attending — or within the previous twelve months having completed — a photography course at a recognised college or university, whose winner and two runners-up receive RPS membership, with the winner published by the RPS Journal and given two one-to-one mentoring sessions with a senior agent at At Collective; and UK for UNHCR Food as Home, open only to entrants with a refugee background or experience of forced displacement. Annual membership at £50 includes six free entries. The 2027 panel is chaired by the food photographer David Loftus and runs past fifty names across photography, publishing and food, among them Jamie Oliver, Deborah Willis (Chair of Photography, Tisch School of the Arts, NYU), Robin Hammond, Fiona Shields (Head of Photography, Guardian News & Media), Simon Hill (President, The Royal Photographic Society), Michael Pritchard (author of British Photographic History) and Karin van Gilst (Director, Foam Photography Museum). Shortlisted entrants are notified by the end of February 2027, judging takes place in March and the winners are announced on 18 May 2027. Generative AI is barred outright: it "must not be used to create, replace, extend or materially reconstruct any visual element of the entered image", entrants must retain original capture files, and breach risks disqualification and permanent exclusion; non-generative masking and retouching that introduces no invented food, props, surfaces, steam, garnishes, textures or backgrounds is allowed. Sponsors and partners include the World Food Programme, UK for UNHCR, UNIDO and Action Against Hunger.
