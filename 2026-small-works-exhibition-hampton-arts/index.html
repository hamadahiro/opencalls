2026 Small Works Exhibition

Small-works show at Hampton Arts' Charles H. Taylor Visual Arts Center, open to all Hampton regional and Virginia artists aged 18 and over. The call is open to all media; two-dimensional work must be no larger than 8 x 10 inches including the frame and three-dimensional work no larger than 8 inches on any side, with 2D pieces under 20 square inches and 3D pieces under 5 inches cubed judged separately as Miniatures. Work must be original, made within the past two years and not previously shown at the center, and accepted pieces must arrive dry and ready to hang (no sawtooth hangers). One entry per artist covers up to six artworks for $40, or $35 for current Hampton Arts League members. Awards go to First ($200), Second ($150) and Third ($100) Place plus three Awards of Merit across the two categories, and a new $100 Mighty Miniature award for the strongest miniature as chosen by the judge. Notification is by email the week after the deadline; accepted work is delivered to the center November 10-14 and the exhibition runs December 5, 2026 to January 16, 2027, with an artist reception and awards on Saturday, December 5 from 3 to 5 pm. Because the show runs through the holidays, work that sells may be removed at the time of purchase. A new award has been added this edition: the Mighty Miniature award of $100, given by the judge to the strongest miniature.
