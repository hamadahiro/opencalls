Art Comes Alive 2026

Seventeenth edition of ADC Fine Art's annual exhibition and awards gala, held in the gallery's 13,000 sq ft space in Cincinnati's Over-the-Rhine and long nicknamed the 'Academy Awards for Artists'. Around 250 living artists are shown across all mediums, photography included. What distinguishes it from a typical juried show is the award structure: ADC puts the total at over $450,000, though almost all of it is representation, exhibitions and services valued in-house rather than cash — eighteen gallery representation contracts at $12,500 each (ten with ADC, three with Art Leaders Gallery, two with Gallery V Fine Arts and one each with bG Gallery, Idaho Art Gallery and Twisted Track Gallery), two solo shows and six group shows valued together at $26,000, five $1,000 credits towards an art fair of the winner's choice ($5,000 in total), six ADC Fine Art Magazine features, ten SmartSell memberships, a Studio El publishing contract and a one-to-one consultation. Two awards are cash: the Eric Boyer Artist Grant and the Carol Pfetzing Artist Grant, $500 each, both named for artists ADC represented. The jury pairs ADC's own staff — president Litsa Spanos, gallery director Annie Kautz, marketing director Amy Scalia and assistant gallery director Chance Smith — with Redwood Art Group CEO Eric Smith, Art Leaders Gallery partner Candice Gorman, bG Gallery partner David Gorman, Gallery V Fine Arts owner Valerie Phillips, Idaho Art Gallery owner Nelli Gribyan, Twisted Track Gallery curator Cynthia Lou, Studio El art director Todd Haile and Blue Crow Gallery owner Jodi Wheeler. Entry is $25 per work, with the last entry free on applications of four or more. Results are announced October 2, work arrives at the gallery by October 16, and the opening night and awards ceremony fall on November 7.
