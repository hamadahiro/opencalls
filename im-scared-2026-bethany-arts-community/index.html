I'M SCARED 2026

The third edition of Bethany Arts Community's I'M SCARED series, born out of the questions of what it truly means to be scared, what shadows haunt your thoughts and what we should genuinely fear — and how you make space to show frightening work at all. The team describes what it wants through a list of guiding words: shadow, sorcery, dystopian, witchcraft, haunting, doom, nightmare, foreboding, grotesque, phantom, morbid curiosities, oddities. 2D work, 3D and sculpture, music and soundscapes, and film under five minutes are all accepted, so photographs and lens-based work sit squarely within scope. There is no entry fee. Artists must be 18 or over and able to hand-deliver and collect work themselves at the Ossining campus; 2D work must be wired for a STAS hook system, and the arts centre takes a 20% commission on sales. Notifications 4 September, drop-off 30 September and 1 October, gallery 10 October – 6 November 2026 with an opening reception on 10 October and a curator walk and talk on 21 October.
