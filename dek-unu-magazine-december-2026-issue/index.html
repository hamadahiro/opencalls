Dek Unu Magazine — December 2026 Issue

International not-for-profit journal publishing one accomplished artist per monthly issue in print and online paperback. All fine art projects in all photo genres and techniques are welcome — each feature pairs 11 images with per-image commentary and an in-depth interview, and there is no word limit on the artist's own account of the work. The jurors read the documents as well as the images. The submission window for the December issue runs October 1–20, 2026; email submissions. Entrants must be at least 18, and submission is limited to artists not previously published by Dek Unu.
