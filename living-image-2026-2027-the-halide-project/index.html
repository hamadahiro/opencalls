Living Image 2026/2027

The Halide Project is a nonprofit darkroom and gallery on North 2nd Street in Philadelphia, and Living Image is its annual juried exhibition of traditional and historic photographic processes — the show it builds its year around. The 2026/27 edition is juried by Amanda Smith, who founded A Smith Gallery in Johnson City, Texas fifteen years ago and has run more than 200 juried and curated exhibitions there, alongside jury work for All About Photo, Analogue Forever, Dodho, Texas Photographic Society, the International Photography Hall of Fame and Critical Mass. Selected work hangs at The Halide Project gallery from 5 December 2026 to 17 January 2027 and is published in a printed catalogue; every selected artist receives a copy. One Grand Prize winner takes $250 and a slot to show a body of work in The Halide Project's annual Spring Invitational, with further sponsored prizes announced later. The medium gate is strict and specific: work must be made using traditional, pre-digital photographic printing — silver gelatin, c-prints, instant film, platinum/palladium, cyanotype, gum bichromate, wet plate collodion and other alternative and historic processes. Analog prints made from digital negatives are eligible; inkjet prints and digital c-prints are not, even if shot on film. Open to artists 18 or older in any country. Entry is $40 for up to three works and $10 for each additional, to a maximum of ten, with a $5 discount off the total for members. Accepted work must arrive framed and wired to professional standards by 7 November and must not exceed 30 inches in any dimension including the frame; artists cover shipping both ways, and the gallery retains a 25% commission on sales.
