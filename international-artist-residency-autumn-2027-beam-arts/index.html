International Artist Residency — Autumn 2027

One- or two-month studio residency in Thessaloniki, Greece, taking up to five artists from different disciplines per period. Applications for the autumn 2027 cycle close 30 October 2026, with some availability still open for the winter/spring 2027 periods, and decisions are confirmed by 9 November 2026. The 2027 periods run monthly from January to July and again from September to November, and artists staying longer than a month can remain in the studios between periods; the application form also still lists a November 2026 period (4–30 November). Each resident gets an individual studio in a shared space with their own key, continuous one-to-one mentorship and assistance, a well-equipped communal workshop, critiques, visits to museums, galleries and artist studios in Thessaloniki, the chance to propose site-specific projects, presentations or workshops, the chance to exhibit with local venues, promotion through the organisation's social media, and a group exhibition at the end of each residency where work may be sold. Accommodation is optional. bEam arts is an independent, self-funded organisation that receives no external financial support, so the cost sits with the artist: €1,850 for one month with accommodation or €3,300 for two, €1,000 for one month without accommodation or €1,800 for two, with duo artists paying €250 or €150 extra per residency month. There is no application fee; selected artists pay a deposit of 50% of the residence fee, refundable if cancelled 30 or more days before the start date. The organisers encourage applicants to seek funding from national arts councils, foundations or universities, maintain an artist funding directory, and will supply letters of invitation and acceptance and tailored wording for funding applications. Open to artists working in any medium; the application asks for an artist statement and a project proposal of up to 250 words each, plus a CV and 5 to 10 portfolio images with dimensions, technique, title and date.
