Fusion: Strathmore's 35th Annual Juried Exhibition

Strathmore is a multi-disciplinary arts centre on a 16-acre site in North Bethesda, Maryland, and its Annual Juried Exhibition reaches its 35th edition with Fusion. The theme is growth through combination: from stars creating the elements that make up the universe to harmonising flavours from different cultures in a dish, fusion represents not only the new but the accomplishment of creation. The call asks for work that mixes multiple mediums, abstract or representational — the organisers say this can include but is not limited to drawing, painting, mixed media, assemblage and sculpture, and work may be of any style and combination of mediums, including pieces that invite the viewer to touch them. Artists from the DMV region and beyond are encouraged to apply. One submission fee of $36 covers up to eight works, is non-refundable and is tax-deductible, and there is no cap on how many works from a single artist's entry may be accepted — each is considered individually on technical mastery, connection to the theme, and original and aesthetic approach. The jurors are Adah Rose Bitterbaum and Lesley Morris. All work must be exhibition-ready and available for sale for the full run; the Mansion uses an Arakawa cable hanging system, so hanging works need ¾-inch or larger d-rings or screw eyes with a ¼-inch or larger opening, and cleats, saw-tooth hangers and anything requiring a nail in the wall are refused. Anything previously exhibited or offered for sale at Strathmore is ineligible. Jury results land 1 December 2026, accepted work is dropped off 11 to 13 January 2027, and the exhibition opens to the public on 23 January 2027 with a reception on 4 February, closing 27 February. Artists arrange and pay for their own delivery and pickup. Strathmore takes a 40 percent commission on all artwork sold from the exhibition.
