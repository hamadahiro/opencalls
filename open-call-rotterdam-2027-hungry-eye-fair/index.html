Open Call Rotterdam 2027

Prize Exhibition at the fair

Hungry Eye Fair — the Rotterdam photography fair founded in 2015 as Haute Photographie — opens its twelfth edition to independent photographers. The 2027 Rotterdam edition runs 17–21 March, and the open call is the route for photographers outside the fair's invited roster to show work alongside the established names it exhibits. The call is open to all photographers regardless of where they live. Applicants send a résumé and a portfolio as a PDF (the fair explicitly refuses WeTransfer links) to the fair's own submissions inbox, with the subject line the page's apply button fills in. The deadline is 1 November 2026. Applying is free. The fair states plainly that taking part is not: "Applying for the open call is free, but participation at Hungry Eye Fair requires a contribution." No amount is published anywhere on the call page, so selected photographers should ask what the contribution is before committing.
