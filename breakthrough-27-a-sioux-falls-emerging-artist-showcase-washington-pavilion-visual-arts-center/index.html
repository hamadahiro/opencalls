Breakthrough ’27: A Sioux Falls Emerging Artist Showcase

Prizes $900 in cash awards Exhibition

Free, no-jury showcase for emerging artists in the Everist Gallery at the Visual Arts Center inside the Washington Pavilion, Sioux Falls — eight galleries and South Dakota's largest art museum. Presented by Sioux Falls Stage & Gallery, which “invites emerging Sioux Falls-area artists to submit work for Breakthrough ’27: A Sioux Falls Emerging Artist Showcase, on view Dec. 18, 2026–March 28, 2027.” PHOTOGRAPHY IS NAMED, not implied: “all artistic mediums are welcome, including drawing, painting, sculpture, ceramics, photography, digital art and more.” THERE IS NO ENTRY FEE AND THERE IS NO SELECTION STEP — the museum states “there is no cost to participate, and all eligible artwork that meets the submission and content guidelines will be accepted while space remains available,” so the only competitive element is the awards. CASH AWARDS, presented at the Sioux Falls Stage & Gallery event and artist reception on Feb. 18: Best in Show $500, Breakthrough $200, Sioux Falls Spirit $200 — $900 in total, a figure summed here from the three the museum lists; no total is advertised. FOUR BINDING BARS: the artist must live within a 10-mile radius of Sioux Falls city limits; must never have had a solo exhibition at the Visual Arts Center; submits exactly one artwork; and that work must be no larger than 50 × 50 inches, no heavier than 75 pounds, and delivered ready to install. SUBMISSION IS IN PERSON ONLY — “artwork must be submitted in person. Online submissions will not be accepted.” Work and a completed paper form go to the Visual Arts Center front desk on the second floor on one of three Fridays: Oct. 23, Nov. 13 or Nov. 27, each 10 a.m.–4 p.m., with Nov. 27 marked “final opportunity.” NOTE THE CUTOFF IS 4 P.M., NOT MIDNIGHT. CONTENT GUIDELINES BIND because the show is open to visitors of all ages: work “must be appropriate for a general audience, including young children” and “should avoid explicit nudity or sexual content, graphic violence or disturbing imagery, profanity or overtly mature themes and overly political or religious content.” The museum publishes no AI policy for this call.
