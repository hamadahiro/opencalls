PowerLines Documentary Photography Fellowship

Three-month documentary photography fellowship from PowerLines, a national nonprofit focused on modernizing public utilities. The fellow documents the human experience of rising utility costs across the United States. $30,000 stipend distributed over three months (May 15 – August 15, 2026). Joint ownership of all images produced. Fellows may accept non-conflicting external assignments during the fellowship. Applicants must have at least three years of professional documentary or photojournalism experience.
