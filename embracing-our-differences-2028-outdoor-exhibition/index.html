Embracing Our Differences 2028 Outdoor Exhibition

Prize $2,000 × 3

International outdoor juried exhibition, running annually since 2004. Fifty selected artworks are reproduced as billboard-size panels — 16 feet (4.88 m) wide by 12.5 feet (3.81 m) high — each paired with a quotation, and displayed from mid-January to April 2028 in Sarasota and St. Petersburg, Florida. Three cash awards of $2,000 each: Best-in-Show Adult, Best-in-Show Student and People's Choice, the last voted by exhibition visitors. Work must reflect the theme Embracing Our Differences — inclusivity, freedom of expression, individuality, resilience in the face of bullying, discrimination or societal barriers, and building bridges between groups. Artwork may be created in any size or medium: the guidelines name no medium and exclude none, and one judging criterion rewards work that adds to the variety of media in the final selection, so photography competes on the same terms as any other medium. The file must be horizontal at a 16:11 aspect ratio, at least 300 dpi at 12.8 by 8.8 inches (3840 × 2640 pixels or larger recommended, 600 dpi preferred) and under 400 MB, in JPG, PDF or PNG. A 150-word artist statement is required; the guidelines cap titles at five words in one line and at "less than 5 words" in another, so four words is the safe maximum. Entry is free with no limit on the number of submissions, each submitted as a separate entry — online through Zealous, or by post with a signed form, colour print and statement per artwork. Open worldwide to professional, emerging, student and amateur artists; online entrants must be 13 or over, younger artists with a parent or guardian's consent. Selection is by a blind judging panel of professional artists, curators and art professionals, judging in early fall 2027; only artists whose work is selected are notified individually, by email, and all winning entries are posted on the organisation's website by early January. AI-generated artwork is not accepted and may be disqualified; the organisers may ask for a process description or work-in-progress images. Artists keep ownership of their work and grant the organisation a non-exclusive worldwide licence for exhibition and promotional use.
