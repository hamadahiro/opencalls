Fresh Legs 2026

Prize Exhibition

Fresh Legs is the annual group exhibition initiative from Galleri Heike Arndt DK, running since 2013. The exhibition is multidisciplinary by design: painting, sculpture, illustration, drawing, street art, photography, short film, animation, and related media all eligible.

The 2026 exhibition runs across two Berlin venues: Galleri Heike Arndt DK (June–October 2026) and INSELGALERIE Berlin (June–July 2026). Selected works span both venues across different runtimes.

Selection terms include some longer-tail commitment: selected works must remain available for 12 months for potential follow-up exhibitions, and all works must be for sale. Gallery takes 30–50% commission on sales.

The fee structure is staged: free to submit. A €40 exhibition fee is charged only if accepted.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through JotForm with 3–6 works. The 2026 deadline was March 31.
