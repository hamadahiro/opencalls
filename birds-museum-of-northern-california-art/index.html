Birds!

Prize Exhibition

The Museum of Northern California Art (monca) in Chico seeks original artworks for its annual bird-themed exhibition, staged during Chico's 2027 Snow Goose Festival — 'work must showcase birds, of all kinds, in all media'. All media are accepted; the museum bars giclée prints of original non-photographic art and states 'No AI please'. Entry is limited to artists residing in Northern California, defined as 41 named counties from Alameda, Butte and Humboldt to Sacramento, San Francisco, Santa Clara, Sonoma and Yuba. The entry fee is $15 for the first work and $10 for each additional, up to eight, non-refundable whether or not work is accepted; the museum's exhibition committee of professional artists and collectors makes the selection, with results emailed on or before 19 November 2026. Accepted work is delivered 17–20 December 2026 or 7–10 January 2027 (the artist pays shipping both ways), must be wired for the museum's cable-and-hook rail (no sawtooth hangers), must not have been shown at monca before, and stays for the whole run: the show opens 14 January 2027 with a reception on 29 January and closes 7 March 2027, though the entry agreement text still carries an earlier 10 January 2027 closing date. There is no cash award; the return is a museum group show. Entries opened on CaFÉ on 22 September 2026 and the museum's own calls page had not yet posted this call at the time of listing.
