SAGA Grants 2026 — Round 3

Prize Up to $10,000

The Spokane Arts Grant Awards fund arts and culture projects, programs and individual artists in the Spokane region, with requests of any amount up to $10,000 accepted three times a year; Round 3 of the 2026 cycle closes 1 October at 11:59pm and awards are announced 1 December. Spokane Arts will grant $166,000 across 2026 and has made more than a million dollars in awards since 2017. All arts disciplines are encouraged, 'including but not limited to, visual, performing, literary, audio, media arts, humanities, culinary, fashion, architecture, crafts and folk/traditional arts', and a past SAGA grant funded a twelve-page full-colour photographic insert on sixteen African Americans in Spokane for Black Lens News. Individuals apply as a working artist on a solo project - defined as someone trained in a discipline and/or self-identifying as a professional artist - and must hold permanent residency in the Spokane region through the award period; collectives and organisations apply in two separate categories. Applications are free and submitted through SmarterSelect, and each round's portal opens a month before its deadline. Projects must directly impact Spokane; reimbursements for past work, tuition that generates credit, pass-through grants and residency, travel or production costs outside the Spokane area are not funded, and applicants funded in the two previous consecutive years must sit a year out. A selection panel of five to six community members - one city arts commissioner, one Spokane Arts board member and three or four community members with arts experience - weighs artistic merit, effectiveness and community impact; the panel stays anonymous through the granting year, and past rosters are published by year. At least one work sample is required. Spokane Arts also offers one-to-one grant-writing appointments and free information sessions.
