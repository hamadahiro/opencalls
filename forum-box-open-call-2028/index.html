Forum Box Open Call 2028

Forum Box is an artist-run cooperative gallery on the Helsinki waterfront with roughly 300 square metres of exhibition space, and it is now taking proposals for its 2028 programme. Artists, curators and working groups may apply for the whole space or for one or two of its three rooms — Permanto (about 65 m², three continuous walls, 5.6 m ceiling), Monttu (about 70 m², divided by stairs) and Parvi (about 90 m², 4.2 m ceiling and the most wall space). The standard slot is five weeks including installation, and for 2028 shorter project-based exhibitions and performances can also be proposed. Selection is made by an exhibition working group of member artists together with the executive director, and every selected artist is offered a member of Forum Box as a peer contact through the process. Applying is free: a single PDF of no more than 4MB containing the gallery's application form, an exhibition or project plan of up to two A4 pages, CVs and around ten images of recent work, emailed by 1pm Finnish time on 7 September 2026. Exhibiting artists currently pay a space rent — €280 for four weeks in the whole gallery or €100 for one room, dropping to €125 and €70 for a single week — which covers communication, marketing, invigilation and sales handling, and the cooperative states it aims to be entirely rent-free by 2028 and has reduced rents every year since 2020; a 40% commission (plus VAT) applies to work sold. Two conditions are worth reading before applying: solo exhibitions carry a five-year minimum interval between shows at Forum Box, and artists may not hold a solo exhibition elsewhere in the Helsinki metropolitan area in the twelve months before their own. A separate, rent-free strand in the same application window seeks a proposal for the Lux Galleries programme of the Lux Helsinki light art festival, on view 30 December 2027 to 9 January 2028, for which the five-year interval does not apply. All applicants hear by the end of October 2026.
