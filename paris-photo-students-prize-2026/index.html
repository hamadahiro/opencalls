Paris Photo Students Prize 2026

Annual prize supporting photography and visual arts students from European schools. A jury of art professionals selects 20 students from roughly 600 applications, and four laureates are chosen. Laureates present their projects in a dedicated space at Paris Photo (Grand Palais, November 12–15, 2026) and in an exhibition at the Gare de Lyon train station. All selected students join a professional support program, with partner awards including a FUTURES residency and grants from La Saif.
