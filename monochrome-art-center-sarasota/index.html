Monochrome

The winter juried show in Art Center Sarasota's Atrium Gallery asks for work made in a single colour or a single tonal range. The call frames the constraint as the point: reduction and a limited palette are meant to push bold, nuanced and inventive approaches to value, texture, form and composition, and the organiser notes that subtle shifts in tone and surface can build depth, atmosphere and unexpected complexity. It is open to all artists and all media, and the call names photography explicitly alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, mixed media, digital arts, sculpture and video. AI-generated art is not allowed. The juror is Simone Friend, co-founder of the artist agency Friend & Johnson, who has spent more than forty years representing illustrators, photographers, directors and animators and who is described as drawn to originality, emotional depth, technical mastery and a clear point of view. Jurying is blind — the juror sees everything except the artist's name and the price — and the awards are chosen in person after the exhibition is installed: $500 first place, $300 second, $200 third, plus two merit awards and four honourable mentions. Up to three works may be entered, and a diptych or triptych counts as one. Two-dimensional work may not exceed 72 by 65 inches after framing and three-dimensional work 60 by 36 by 36 inches. Work must be original, completed within the last three years and not previously shown at ACS; editioned prints, including photographs, are allowed if they are labelled as prints and numbered. All work must be offered for sale — no NFS or price-on-request — and the artist receives 60% of the selling price as a member or 50% as a non-member. Artists are notified by 21 December 2026, accepted work is received in person on 12 and 13 January 2027 or by post between 11 and 13 January, and the show runs 21 January to 27 February 2027 with an opening reception on 21 January, 5–7pm.
