Call for Artists: 12-Month Gallery Representation

Prize 12-month gallery representation

Art in the Berkshires Gallery and Creative Studio opened at 8 Castle Street in Great Barrington, Massachusetts, and is now assembling the core group of artists it will represent from December 2026 through November 2027. Photography is named explicitly in the call alongside painting, printmaking, drawing and sculpture, and limited-edition photography is permitted; reproductions and AI-generated artwork will not be considered, and work must have been created in 2024 or later. The gallery asks for work that is Berkshires-minded — it offers paintings of Hawaiian beaches as an example of what would not fit — and for a genuinely distinct perspective; all styles are welcome, but artists are expected to have a large enough body of work that sold pieces can be replaced quickly. Up to thirty artists are selected. Rather than a single show, the selected group works together across four to five seasonal exhibitions through 2027, with all represented artists shown year-round and the first group exhibition opening 3 December 2026 and running to 31 January 2027. Selection happens in two parts: a jury reviews submissions and announces first-round results on 27 September, shortlisted artists are interviewed between 1 and 23 October, and final acceptances are issued on 30 October 2026. Representation carries exclusivity in Southern Berkshire County. The call is open to artists who live or work in Berkshire County or within fifty miles of it, which in practice reaches into eastern New York, northwest Connecticut and southern Vermont. Entry is $30 and submissions run 25 July to 20 September 2026; the gallery publishes no commission rate for represented artists, so that is worth asking about before applying. The gallery describes itself as community-focused and also invites represented artists interested in running workshops at its Creative Studio or teaching as paid instructors to get in touch separately.
