Beyond the Surface: A Mixed Media Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

The Art Center in Dover, New Hampshire is running a national juried exhibition on what happens when artists combine materials, techniques and ideas in a single piece. Photography is named outright in the organiser's own list of things artists may incorporate, alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, collage, fiber, found objects, digital elements, assemblage and sculpture — so lens-based work that is layered, printed onto unusual substrates, cut, stitched or built into an object belongs here. The juror is Dennis Cardalisco, a contemporary artist with more than forty years in the visual arts who studied at the Museum School at Tufts University and holds a BFA from the Massachusetts College of Art and Design. Awards are Best in Show, a Juror's Choice selected by Cardalisco, and a People's Choice voted by exhibition visitors. Entry is open to artists living in the United States aged 18 and over, with up to three original works per artist — works submitted together should come from a series or complement one another, and both the art and the framing must be consistent. Work must have been made in the last five years and not previously shown at The Art Center. Both 2D framed and 3D work are accepted; 3D pieces needing a pedestal must stay under 36 inches high and within a 16-inch footprint, while larger floor-standing work is encouraged and exempt from those limits. The exhibition runs 8 September to 31 October 2026 in the gallery and online, with an artists' reception on 3 October. Accepted work is delivered or shipped by 1 September and must arrive ready to hang, packed for re-use, with a prepaid return label. All work must be for sale; the artist keeps 70% and The Art Center retains 30%. Entry is $30 for up to three submissions and free for current Art Center members.
