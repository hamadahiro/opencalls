I Can Buy Myself Flowers

HAZEGALLERY, the curatorial platform of PURPLEHAZE Magazine, invites artists and image-makers to participate in a curated pop-up exhibition in Amsterdam alongside the launch of PURPLEHAZE Print Issue 012. Theme explores autonomy as identity, rituals of independence, intimacy turned inward, beauty chosen rather than granted, solitude as clarity, and desire as self-directed. Accepts fashion, fine art, and documentary photography, works on paper, fine art prints, and short looping video works. Selected artists participate in the June 2026 Amsterdam exhibition and may be published in the print issue.
