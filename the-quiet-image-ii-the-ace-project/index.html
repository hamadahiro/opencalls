The Quiet Image II

Second edition of The Quiet Image at The Ace Project, a contemporary photography space on Zaagmolendrift in Rotterdam. The call asks photographers based in the Netherlands and Belgium for work exploring the relationship between nature and the human body. Up to five works may be submitted by email for review; submission is free. Selected photographers are invited for a personal conversation at the gallery, and their work must be professionally printed and framed at their own cost — participation also carries a contribution fee towards exhibition costs, which the gallery does not publish in advance. The exhibition runs 25 September to 18 October 2026.
