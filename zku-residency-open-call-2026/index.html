ZK/U Residency Open Call 2026

ZK/U's general open call selects practitioners for two- to six-month residencies in a former railway depot in Berlin-Moabit, with a studio, living space and a public programme of open studios and dinners. Residencies are self-funded: the monthly fee is €750 to €1,200, and successful applicants have two years to take up a place.
