Artotéka Open Call 2027

The Artotéka is the Municipal Library of Prague's art department — a lending library of pictures and art literature that also runs a contemporary art programme in the entrance hall of the library's Opatov branch in Jižní Město, the large panel-housing district on Prague's southern edge. For 2027 it is building a linked exhibition programme and invites short-term exhibition projects from individual artists, author duos and artist groups, explicitly looking for a season in which different generations, media, approaches and themes meet. What the library asks for is work that reckons with its audience: the space is a public library foyer, not a gallery, so the call is for projects that take account of a varied readership and can enter the Jižní Město setting with some sensitivity — the organisers frame it as a chance to reach people who do not normally walk into a gallery. What is on offer is concrete rather than monetary: a vitrine-type exhibition space in a hall the library counts at up to 15,000 visitors a month, a run of six to eight weeks, transport of the work within Prague, help with installation, curatorial framing of the project, promotion by the library, and the option of building an accompanying programme around the show — the Artotéka regularly pairs its exhibitions with workshops, lectures, guided conversations and demonstrations of artistic technique, and treats that public-facing side as part of its purpose of opening up visual art in a living, shared space. Applications are free and are sent by email to the Artotéka's own departmental address with the downloadable application form and its required attachments; results are announced by the end of 2026.
