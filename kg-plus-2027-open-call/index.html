KG+ 2027 Open Call

Prize Exhibition

KG+ is the open-call satellite programme of KYOTOGRAPHIE, the Kyoto International Photography Festival. Launched in 2013 to discover and support emerging artists and curators, it reaches its fifteenth edition in 2027 and runs alongside the main festival from 17 April to 16 May 2027, with exhibitions spread across venues throughout Kyoto.

The call asks for a solo or group exhibition proposal featuring photography, or work incorporating photographic elements such as moving image, installation or related media. There is no theme. Both indoor and outdoor venues are eligible, but the model is important to understand before applying: applicants must find and reserve their own venue inside Kyoto City first, and submit a written venue usage permission form bearing the venue manager's name, official seal and address. The exhibition must be open to the public for at least nine days of the festival period, with at least one opening day falling on a Saturday, Sunday or Japanese national holiday; the organisers publish worked examples of eligible and ineligible date patterns.

Applications are open regardless of age, profession, nationality or experience, and applying to both KG+ and KG+SELECT is permitted, with the same proposal or a different one. The submission comprises exhibition title and statement (600 Japanese characters with an English translation, or the reverse), artist biography, an exhibition plan with venue photographs, a dimensioned floor plan and a layout plan (hand-drawn accepted), up to 20 artwork images at 300 dpi, one promotional image and an artist portrait. Photographs made for advertising or commercial promotion are not accepted.

The KG+ Executive Committee reviews applications on the quality and concept of the work, the scale and originality of the exhibition, its dates and its accessibility, and sorts selected exhibitions into KG+ PICK UP (images and text in print and online) or KG+ (text in print, images online). Participants receive free promotional signage such as vertical banners, discounted places on KYOTOGRAPHIE masterclasses and portfolio reviews, and eligibility for the KG+ Discovery Award, which goes to one artist or group chosen from the KG+ exhibitions.

The application fee is JPY 18,000, non-refundable regardless of the outcome, paid by credit card, bank transfer or PayPal through a separate payment form; each applicant receives one KYOTOGRAPHIE 2027 Passport. Participants are responsible for every cost of their own exhibition — venue rental, printing, framing, projector rental, transport, installation and deinstallation — and for supervising the show during the festival. Applications run 1 November to 31 December 2026, closing at 23:59 JST. Results reach applicants by late January 2027 (by the end of 2026 for anyone applying before 30 November), and final category assignments are announced in early February 2027.
