Xposure International Photography Awards 2026/27

Xposure's International Photography Awards recognise outstanding photography from across the world, celebrating work that demonstrates originality, narrative depth and technical excellence. The awards are free to enter and open to photographers at all stages of their careers, and are presented within the wider Xposure programme in Sharjah, where shortlisted and winning work is introduced to international audiences through exhibition, publication and professional engagement during the festival's annual gathering of photographers, editors, curators and industry leaders. The organiser describes nine categories spanning contemporary practice, among them Architectural Photography, Nature & Landscape, Night Photography, Portraiture, Sports Photography, Street Photography, Mobile Photography and Photo-manipulation & AI; entries may be single images or cohesive series. You may enter up to ten images or films per user distributed in any ratio across the categories, only one image may be uploaded at a time, and work must have been taken or produced in the past three years. Each entry carries a title, caption and place taken, and all entry information must be submitted in English. Judging runs in three stages: a first round scoring individual submissions for visual strength and originality, a second round assessing composition, technical execution and coherence in a series, and a final collective deliberation that names category winners, runners-up and an Overall Winner. There are seven nominees per category. The Overall Winner receives USD 6,000, flights and accommodation to the awards ceremony in Sharjah and inclusion in the Xposure festival exhibition; each Category Winner receives USD 3,000 with the same travel and exhibition, each Category Runner-up USD 1,500 with travel and exhibition, and nominees receive an online exhibition and an image displayed during the festival. Beyond a category specifically designed for photo manipulation, entries must not add or remove content or stage a photograph to mislead the viewer, though adjustments of levels, colour, contrast and brightness are permitted; watermarks or names that are not part of the original capture are disqualifying, and nominated finalists must supply high-resolution TIFF and RAW files for verification. Entrants to the AI & Creative Expression category must have exercised primary creative control and must retain prompts, revisions, seeds, model and version information or layered working files as evidence of authorship. Entry is open to all nationalities over 18; there is a separate Junior category for UAE residents under 18. A separate Visual Storytelling Award runs alongside for six-image stories. An entrant cannot hold a cash placing in more than one Xposure programme in the same festival year, with the Independent and Freelance Photojournalist Award taking precedence over these awards. Xposure is the Sharjah platform for photography, filmmaking and visual storytelling, powered by the Sharjah Government Media Bureau.
