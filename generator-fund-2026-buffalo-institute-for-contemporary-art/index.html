The Generator Fund 2026 — Kinetic & Potential Grants

The Generator Fund is the Buffalo Institute for Contemporary Art's regranting programme, launched in August 2023 as part of the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts' Regional Regranting Program, and it funds artists living and working in Erie and Niagara Counties, New York. It has awarded over 50 grants since 2023, supporting exhibitions, performances, workshops, publications, gardens, sound works and new artist-run spaces. Cycle 4 offers two grant types on one deadline. Kinetic Grants of up to $5,000 fund an artist's production for a new short-term project with a publicly accessible component - exhibitions, performances, videos or film screenings, books, zines or brochures. Potential Grants of up to $10,000 act as seed funding for longer-term, platform-building work: exhibition spaces from formal to informal, podcasts or radio stations, zines or forums for arts writing, collective spaces, community gardens, public lecture series. You may apply as lead applicant for both, as long as each proposal is distinct. Photography is named outright in the fund's own list of eligible media, alongside painting, sculpture, installation, printmaking, video, performance art, social practice and zines; work rooted primarily in music, theatre, dance, creative writing or film without a clear visual arts framework is not eligible. Applicants must live and work in Erie or Niagara County and may apply as an individual artist, an informal group or an unincorporated collective - 501(c)(3) organisations, BICA employees and anyone receiving a BICA solo exhibition honorarium in the same fiscal year cannot. There is no application fee. A venue does not need to be locked down at the point of applying, and the fund actively encourages artist stipends: you can pay yourself, collaborators, assistants, materials, travel and rentals out of the grant. Funded projects generally cannot take place at BICA itself, and must be publicly accessible somewhere in Erie or Niagara County. Projects cannot start before 1 January 2027 and must be completed by 31 December 2027. The Cycle 4 jury is H Boone, Megan Lee, Zack Boehler and Julio César Morales, who are asked to read applications with generosity and curiosity rather than looking for the most polished grant writers. Applications go through Submittable with a project description, a budget on the fund's own template, work samples and a CV; grantees are notified in December 2026 and first cheques go out in January 2027.
