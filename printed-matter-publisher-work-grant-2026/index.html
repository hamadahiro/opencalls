Printed Matter Publisher Work Grant 2026

Prizes 3 × $10,000 Art Book Fair table

Printed Matter's annual programme of unrestricted grants for artists' book publishers, now in its third year and supported by the Wagner Foundation. In 2026 three grants of $10,000 each go to independent presses with a publishing history of one or more years, alongside a featured exhibitor presence at a forthcoming Printed Matter NY or LA Art Book Fair, with the table provided free of charge. The award is unrestricted: funds can cover production and development costs, artists' fees, equipment, studio rent or hired assistance, with no obligation to deliver a specific project. Eligible imprints should be small in scale and published with an independent spirit, without major ongoing distribution support; commercially run studios that only publish client work, and imprints attached to well-resourced institutions, are generally not eligible. The jury is broadly interested in presses that treat the book as a site for experimental artwork, that engage social and political themes with nuance, that give voice to marginalised perspectives, and that back emerging and underrepresented artists regardless of sales prospects. The grant is not aimed at artists seeking to fund a book of their own work, though artists who actively publish others are welcome. Open to publishers based anywhere; international applications encouraged. Past Publisher Work Grant winners cannot apply a second time.
