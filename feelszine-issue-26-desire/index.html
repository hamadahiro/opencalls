FeelsZine Issue 26 — Desire

Prize Publication prize

FeelsZine is a Canadian creative zine. Issue 26 is themed around desire — the brief is left open for interpretation across approaches.

The publication is multidisciplinary: photography sits alongside illustration, collage, typography, and comics. Each issue features submissions across these categories rather than restricting to a single medium.

Submission terms: 1–2 works per applicant, sized 6.5×9.5 inches with 0.125-inch bleed for the print layout. The publication is unpaid — contributors are not compensated, but the zine's print distribution carries the work to a small but committed audience.

Submission is by email. Application is free. Eligibility is open globally. The Issue 26 deadline was March 31, 2026; selections published July 2026.
