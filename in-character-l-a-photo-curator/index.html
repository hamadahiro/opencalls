In Character

Prize Solo online exhibition

L.A. Photo Curator runs online by design and says so on the page, which makes the $25 easy to price. Every entrant goes into the group exhibition with an artist statement, biography and website link, so nobody pays and gets nothing; the competitive part is first place, which brings a solo online exhibition, an article with a Q&A built from the juror's responses, and a month on the platform's splash page. Selected work also stays in play for the Top 40 Images of the Year and the Photographer of the Year title. Judging is Laurie Freitag, who has run the platform for eleven years, was named one of ten Incredible Women Photographers to Follow in 2026, and has sold work recently through Duncan Miller Gallery.

The brief is one of the broadest this platform has posted and that is the point: character can arrive through a face, a gesture, clothing, posture, surroundings, relationships, or the small details that give a person away. Freitag asks for the expressive, the eccentric, the quiet, the guarded, the theatrical, the ordinary and the unforgettable, and explicitly allows the subject to look away or stay partly out of frame — "sometimes character is revealed through what surrounds us, what we choose to show, or what we leave behind." Self-portraiture is admitted on the same footing as portraits of other people, which widens this to anyone with a body of work about their own life.

Mechanics are low-tech and strict about counts. Hit the Buy Now button to pay $25 by PayPal or card for either three or six images, then email exactly that number of JPEGs as a ZIP — 1024 pixels on the long side, 72 DPI, under 1 MB each, named First_Last_Title.jpg — with the statement and biography in the body of the message ("No Word documents or PDFs"), website and Instagram written out in full, and the PayPal transaction ID and payer name included. Listing images for sale is optional and all proceeds go to the artist; the platform handles no part of a sale and takes no commission. Fees are non-refundable and late work is not eligible, though a limited number of fee waivers can be requested. One entrant is invited to guest-jure a future call, which is a real CV line. Deadline 1 November 2026.
