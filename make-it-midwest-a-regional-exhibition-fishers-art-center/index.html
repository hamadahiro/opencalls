Make it Midwest: A Regional Exhibition

Prizes $300/$150/$150/$150 Exhibition

Fishers Art Center, the new public art centre inside the Fishers Arts & Municipal Complex outside Indianapolis — free to visit, and programmed and managed by the 90-year-old Indy Art Center — is calling for entries to a juried regional exhibition of contemporary Midwest makers. The call is explicitly open to every medium: "The Fishers Art Center is calling for entries for artists in any medium 18 or older living in the Midwest", and again, "Artists working in any media or processes are eligible for entry. The intention of this exhibition is to feature works in a variety of media ranging from traditional processes to contemporary and new media processes" — so photography, analog and digital alike, is in scope. Eligibility is by residence: artists currently working in Indiana, Ohio, Illinois, Michigan, Kentucky, Missouri, Iowa, Wisconsin, Kansas or Nebraska, aged 18 or over. Work must have been made in the last two years and must not have been shown at the Art Center before; collaborative works between artists are eligible; nothing may weigh more than 250 pounds or measure more than 10 feet in any direction, and installation artists are asked to make contact before applying. Entry is $35 for two pieces with $5 for each additional piece, up to a maximum of fifteen. Four awards are given: Best in Show $300, Best in 2D $150, Best in 3D $150 and Curator's Choice $150. Accepted work hangs in the Art Center's galleries, which are free and open to the public, from 12 February to 11 April 2027, with a reception on opening night from 6 to 8pm; the Art Center takes a 35% commission on any sales and work may also be entered not for sale. The schedule the organiser publishes runs: applications close 16 December 2026, acceptance notifications by end of day 17 December, artwork drop-off 4–31 January 2027, installation 1–11 February, exhibition 12 February to 11 April, pick-up 12–16 April. Artists hand-deliver or pay shipping and insurance both ways, and work must arrive in a reusable container. Entry is through the CaFÉ / Creative West portal. The Art Center names no juror on either surface, though the Curator's Choice award implies one; it also runs a separate year-round proposal route for solo and group exhibitions by email, reviewed on a rolling basis.
