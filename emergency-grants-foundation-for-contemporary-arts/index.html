Emergency Grants

Created in 1993, the Foundation for Contemporary Arts' Emergency Grants provide urgent, project-based funding to visual and performing artists and poets who have sudden, unanticipated opportunities to present their work to the public with insufficient time to seek other funding, or who incur unexpected or unbudgeted expenses for projects close to completion with committed exhibition dates. It is a year-round, multi-disciplinary programme open to artists living and working anywhere in the United States, for projects taking place in the US and abroad. FCA receives around 100 applications a month and makes roughly 15 to 20 grants of $500 to $3,000 each; applicants may request any amount in that range and the average grant is currently $2,200. The panel is made up of established practising artists from the disciplines FCA supports and meets monthly, weighing the urgency of the request, the significance of the opportunity, the impact the money would have and the strength of the work samples. Photographers apply under Visual Arts, and the foundation's mission is explicitly to support experimental and contemporary practice. Applicants must have a US Tax ID, must be individual artists rather than curators, producers or organisations, must have a confirmed presentation with specific dates at the time of applying, and must not be enrolled in any degree-granting programme. FCA asks that artists apply eight to ten weeks before the public presentation date. Grants may cover artist fees, airfare, accommodation, materials, shipping, fabrication and production; work-in-progress showings, projects presented by commercial galleries or art fairs, and any opportunity that requires the artist to pay to participate are not eligible.
