Nocefresca Open Call 2027

Seventh edition of a rural artist residency in Milis, a village of citrus groves at the foot of the Montiferru range in western Sardinia, 19 km from the coast. Month-long residencies run March–June and September–December 2027, with 42 places across the year and up to seven residents at a time; the residency closes in January, February, July and August. Photographers are named among those it is for, alongside visual artists, designers, writers, composers, videomakers, illustrators and architects, and no final outcome is required. Each group is accompanied by a guest curator across two group meetings and three one-to-one sessions, with a public open studio at the end of the month. The residency is self-funded and cannot offer fellowships: studio-and-housing fees run €1,650–€1,880 a month depending on studio and room, and selection weighs artistic quality, feasibility of the project, length of stay, impact on the group and the local community, and secured budget. Applying is free; a €450 confirmation fee falls due within two weeks of selection.
