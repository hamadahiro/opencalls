Glasgow Photography Zine

Prize Publication

The Glasgow Gallery of Photography invites photographers worldwide to submit work for a forthcoming zine celebrating Glasgow — its people, streets, architecture, music, communities, weather, industry, nightlife, green spaces, rituals, and everyday moments. Work must relate to or have been made in Glasgow, but photographers need not be Glaswegian. All styles and processes are welcome, including digital, analogue, documentary, conceptual, portraiture, landscape, street, and experimental. Submit up to five images, individually or as a short series; selected work appears in the printed zine (£10 to buy), promoted across the gallery's social media and network. Free to submit.
