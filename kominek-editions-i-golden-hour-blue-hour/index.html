Kominek Editions I — Golden Hour / Blue Hour

Prize Exhibition

Kominek Gallery — the Berlin gallery and photobook publisher behind books with Alec Soth, Rinko Kawauchi and Viviane Sassen — invites submissions for Kominek Editions I, a group exhibition built on the concept of 100 photographers, 100 images, on the theme Golden Hour / Blue Hour. Email 1–5 images for consideration to info@kominek-gallery.com. Selected photographers are announced August 10; the exhibition opens at the gallery on September 10, 2026.
