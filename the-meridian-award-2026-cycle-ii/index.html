The Meridian Award 2026 — Cycle II

An independent international award granting a $1,000 fully unrestricted cash prize to one contemporary artist working in any medium, founded in New York City and open to artists worldwide aged 18 and over, with no restriction on nationality, location, education or professional status. There is no theme; portfolios are judged on artistic merit, clarity of vision, coherence of the work presented and the strength of a distinct artistic perspective. Photography is named in the award's own discipline list alongside painting, drawing, digital art, sculpture, print-based media, textile-based media, performance, sound art, video art and interdisciplinary practice; short and feature-length films are the only stated exclusion. Each cycle selects one winner and two finalists, and all three are invited to record an online interview published on the award's website and Instagram — but only the winner receives the $1,000. Apply through the award's own site with a short bio, an artist statement, a work description and up to five files, images and video links combined, with a $25 non-refundable fee; a discounted early-bird rate opens each cycle and fee waivers are not offered. Collaborative teams may apply as one. Artists keep full copyright, and the award takes only promotional reproduction rights on selected work. One thing to weigh: the jurors are described as invited contemporary-art professionals but are not publicly named. The inaugural 2026 cycle went to Brooklyn sculptor Wen Liu, a 2026 AIM Fellow at the Bronx Museum with fellowships from Yaddo, MacDowell and Bogliasco, with painter Matt Bollinger and interdisciplinary artist Andrius Alvarez-Backus as finalists. Cycle II closes October 15, 2026 and the winner is announced in mid-November.
