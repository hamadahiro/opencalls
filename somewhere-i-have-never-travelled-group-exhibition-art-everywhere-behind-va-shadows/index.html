Somewhere I Have Never Travelled — Group Exhibition

Prize Exhibition prize

Somewhere I Have Never Travelled is a group exhibition organized by Art + Everywhere and Behind VA Shadows. The brief works through the title's e.e. cummings reference to explore place as memory and memory as place — both directions of the relationship.

The exhibition is multidisciplinary by design: all mediums are welcome, including performance and installation alongside photography and traditional visual art. The exhibition runs July 12 – September 12, 2026 in Harvard Square, Cambridge, MA — a long two-month run.

Practical note: artists are responsible for shipping their work to and from the exhibition.

Selection is by curators Brenda Melgoza Ciardiello and Stéphanie Williams.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the organizers' website with up to 5 images. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 31; results announced May 15, 2026.
