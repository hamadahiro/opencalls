Neoteric Abstract XV

Prize Exhibition

SlowArt Productions' fifteenth Neoteric Abstract exhibition at Limner Gallery in Hudson, New York is built for the abstract artist alone, and sets out to represent the full range of abstraction — from the geometric to the lyrical, from the precise to the raw. Photography is named in the organiser's own list of eligible media alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, graphics, digital and installation art and video, and the only content restriction is that the work be abstract; entrants must be 18 or older, and the call is open nationally and internationally. Wall-mounted work must be no taller than 72 inches and no wider than 120 inches, and sculpture must fit through a 36-inch door. Video may be entered by supplying a link to a file hosted on Vimeo, YouTube or a similar platform, with each link counting as one image. Entry is by email, with four works covered by the fee and further works at $5 each; details of a two-dimensional work count as an additional entry, though sculptors may add one extra view per piece at no cost. Artists accepted to exhibit pay nothing further. Selected artists are shown at Limner Gallery from 15 April to 8 May 2027, with the exhibition also carried on the gallery's website and promoted on social media. Work may be exhibited for sale or not for sale at prices the artist sets, and the gallery takes a 35% commission on sales. Results are posted at slowart.com/results no later than 28 February 2027, and that posting — not the email — is the official notification.
