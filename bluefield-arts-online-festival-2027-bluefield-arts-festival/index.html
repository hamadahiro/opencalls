Bluefield Arts Online Festival 2027

The ninth Bluefield Arts Online Festival shows one work per artist in its online gallery from 21 to 28 February 2027, keeps it up for up to eight months and takes no commission on sales. Awards are honorary and non-monetary, and the form names no jury. Entering grants the festival use of your images for publicity, the gallery and education in perpetuity.
