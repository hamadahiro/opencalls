Chase Gallery 2027 Season

Spokane Arts is taking portfolios for the 2027 exhibition schedule of the Chase Gallery, the city-owned space on the lower level of Spokane City Hall next to the council chambers, free and open whenever the building is. The gallery's stated mission is to show work that engages the public, brings people to a new outlook on contemporary issues and gives people of all backgrounds space to share a perspective; two-dimensional, sculptural, installation, video, audio, traditional and experimental work are all encouraged, and the Chase has given over its walls to photography before, including a forty-three-year retrospective of Rick Singer's Spokane pictures. Individual artists, artist teams and guest curators proposing a themed group show are all eligible. The gallery mainly curates group exhibits out of individual applications, but exceptionally strong work may be considered for a solo show. Applicants must live in Washington, Oregon, Idaho or Montana, with priority given to residents of the Intermountain Northwest. There is no entry fee. The application asks for an artist statement, a CV, a numbered works list with title, dimensions, year and medium, and between five and ten work samples; video may be linked instead, up to ten minutes in total. AI-generated, AI-prompted and AI-assisted work is barred outright and digital submissions are run through an AI image detector. The gallery takes 30 percent on sales and artists arrange their own shipping and delivery.
