Dream of Home Away

The next cycle of Van Der Plas Gallery's year-round Open Call Program, for a group exhibition titled Dream of Home Away at the gallery's Lower East Side space at 156 Orchard Street, on view 12–25 February 2027 with an opening reception on 12 February, 6–8 pm. Founded in New York City in the 1980s, the gallery shows emerging and established contemporary artists with a focus on downtown culture, outsider art, street art, painting, sculpture and experimental practices, and photography is named explicitly among the media it wants — alongside painting, sculpture, printmaking, mixed media, digital art and experimental practices. The brief is not thematic in any strict sense: the gallery says it looks for "originality, conceptual clarity, and a strong artistic vision" and work that reflects the diversity of contemporary practice, and the exhibition is meant to bring together different practices from artists at different career stages inside a professional gallery setting. Applying is open to artists at any stage, internationally, with no age or residence bar: $35 buys the review of up to four artworks (JPG), plus installation images for any framed or otherwise applicable work, an artist bio and an artist statement of 100–200 words each. Submissions are read on a rolling basis by the gallery's own curatorial team, selection is not guaranteed, and work submitted now is considered for Dream of Home Away first and then stays under consideration for later exhibitions in the programme; artists may apply again at any time and each submission is read independently. Selected artists are contacted directly by email, with no published notification date. The commercial terms are worth knowing before you pay: the gallery states the $35 covers submission review, administrative coordination, exhibition planning and the running costs of a physical gallery; all shipping, delivery and return arrangements and their costs fall on the artist, including for international applicants; every accepted work must be available for sale for the run of the show, with pricing agreed with the gallery after acceptance, and work not for sale may be declined or excluded; and the framing specification is enforced — work on paper must be framed, with a recommended minimum two-inch mat, a museum-style frame in natural wood, black or whitewashed finish and plexiglass rather than glass, and work that does not meet it "may be excluded from the exhibition, even after delivery". The gallery will frame to spec for an additional fee on request. The binding document is a Terms and Conditions PDF linked from the call page, last updated 25 October 2026, and it carries five things the page itself does not: sales made during or as a result of the exhibition are split 50% artist / 50% gallery; submitted work must not exceed 48 by 60 inches, and anything over 36 by 48 inches has to be shipped unstretched; artists must supply a prepaid return shipping label and email a copy to the gallery; unsold work left past the collection date incurs a $250 storage fee after three days and may be donated or discarded after a month, with no compensation; and every applicant, selected or not, is told the outcome in the first week of January. The same document sets no age, residence or AI condition.
