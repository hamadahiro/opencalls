Group Show II — Guest Curator Jesse Lenz

Prize Exhibition

Second group show from Hypam Editions, the Louisville, Kentucky photo curatorial project, hosted at Misc. Goods Co., Hypam's home base. Guest curated by Jesse Lenz — photographer, founder and director of Charcoal Book Club, Charcoal Press, and the Chico Hot Springs Portfolio Review. No theme: submit up to 10 images that best represent your photographic practice or a cohesive body of work; emerging and established photographers both encouraged. Selected artists print, frame and ship their work (max 36 inches on the long side). Acceptance notification September 4; the show opens October 17 with a reception featuring a Charcoal Press/Book Club booth.
