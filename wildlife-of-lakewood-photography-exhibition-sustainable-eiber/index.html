Wildlife of Lakewood Photography Exhibition

A free neighbourhood-run show at the James J. Richey Gallery in Lakewood's Civic Center for photographs of local wildlife, open to Lakewood residents of any age or experience and to images made in the city since January 2025. A panel of Lakewood curators and guest jurors reviews up to four framed, ready-to-hang works per photographer.

DeadlineJanuary 31, 2027
PrizeExhibition
Entry feeFree
EligibilityLakewood, CO only
LocationLakewood, CO
RequirementsUp to 4 works
