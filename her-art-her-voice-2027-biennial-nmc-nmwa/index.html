Her Art, Her Voice — 2027 Biennial Juried Exhibition

Prizes Exhibition Best of Show award

Biennial juried exhibition of the New Mexico State Committee of the National Museum of Women in the Arts, co-hosted by Turner Carroll Gallery and GF Contemporary on Canyon Road, Santa Fe, March 5 – April 3, 2027. Open to women-identifying artists residing in New Mexico who are NMC-NMWA members with an Artist Registry page (or registry information submitted by August 31, 2026). All mediums and subject matters considered, including photography. Awards include Best of Show, Award of Excellence, Award of Merit, and two Honorable Mentions. $50 for up to three artworks.
