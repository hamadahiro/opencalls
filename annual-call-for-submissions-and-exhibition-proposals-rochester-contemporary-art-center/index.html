Annual Call for Submissions and Exhibition Proposals

Prize Exhibition

Rochester Contemporary Art Center, a not-for-profit founded in 1977, keeps a standing annual open call for general and video submissions toward future exhibition seasons, with a December 31 deadline every year. General submissions take a letter of intent saying what you want to do and what you are open to, whether a curatorial project, a solo show or inclusion in any project the gallery is planning, plus a CV, an artist statement, up to 20 images and an image checklist giving title, date, medium and presentation size for each. Site-specific installation and curated project proposals should carry as much detail as possible in the letter. RoCo names immigration, housing, and artists' rights and censorship as special topics of interest for this cycle. A separate video programme welcomes proposals from curators, artists and organisations worldwide, preferably programmes of several single-channel works curated around a theme, in any form including experimental, documentary, animation, narrative and virtual reality; curators may propose two to five works of up to 20 minutes each, and curated video projects from around the Great Lakes are especially welcome. Materials go through a single online form.
