You'll Covet Your Eye Out — Holiday Show

Prize Exhibition

A holiday group exhibition at Covet Art Gallery, a storefront at 1922-A South Coast Highway in Oceanside, north of San Diego, open since 2023, built around a single constraint: every work must be priced at $500 or less. Size is explicitly irrelevant — a tiny treasure and a statement piece compete on the same terms — and the gallery is after work that is covetable, collectible and giftable, whether someone is buying for a person they love or for themselves. All mediums and styles are welcome, all career levels, and the call is open nationally and internationally. Work must be original and must be for sale; the sale price given at entry is final and sales split 50/50 with the gallery, while artists are also invited to list further work on the gallery's own online marketplace, where the split is 70% to the artist. Artists retain full copyright and cover delivery both ways. Entry is $25 plus $5 for each additional image, up to twenty images. The show runs November 7 to December 12, 2026, with an opening party on November 7 from 5 to 9pm and a closing party on December 12. There is no cash award and no juror is named, though the gallery's own artist agreement calls this a Juried Group Exhibition — what is on offer is a selected place on the wall of a real gallery over the selling season.
