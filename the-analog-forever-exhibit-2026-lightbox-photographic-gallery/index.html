The Analog Forever Exhibit 2026

Prize Exhibition

Fourth annual partnership exhibition between LightBox Photographic Gallery and Analog Forever Magazine, celebrating photography's analog roots. Open worldwide to any style or medium — the sole requirement is that the work includes at least one analog element in the creative process, whether it starts on film, passes through a hand process, or ends as an analog print. Platinum prints, cyanotypes, lumen prints, gelatin silver, three-dimensional objects and assemblage all welcome; the artist's hand must be evident. Juried by Michael Kirchoff, Editor-in-Chief of Analog Forever Magazine. Exhibition runs August 8 – October 7, 2026 at the gallery in Astoria, Oregon; accepted works due August 1. Fee $40 for up to 5 submissions, $60 for up to 10.
