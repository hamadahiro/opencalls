The Yunghi Grant 2026

Yunghi Kim's photojournalism grant, in its twelfth year, gives seven working freelance photojournalists $4,000 each, to start, continue or finish a project or simply to cover living costs. Jeffrey Smith of Contact Press Images and CNN Digital's Bernadette Tuazon judge; you email a 300-word case, one image and a portrait. Staff photographers, full-time educators and students are not eligible.
