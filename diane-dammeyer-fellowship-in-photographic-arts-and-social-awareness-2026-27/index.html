Diane Dammeyer Fellowship in Photographic Arts and Social Awareness 2026/27

Fellowship supporting socially engaged photographers in creating work within a community. The fellow partners with a Chicago-based community organization to address human rights, social, economic, or cultural issues through photography. September–June commitment with flexible hours. Culminates in a public exhibition and/or publication at MoCP. Preference for Chicago-based projects. Requires MFA in photography or related field, or BA with 5 years post-degree creative practice. Two professional references required.
