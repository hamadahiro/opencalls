Dyonyzine Issue 06 — Second Helpings

Prize Publication prize

Dyonyzine is an Asian-American-founded creative arts magazine published as a volunteer-run independent project. Issue 06 — Second Helpings is themed around food: the working premise is that a meal is never just a meal.

The magazine is multidisciplinary by design. Submissions accepted include photography, visual art, drawings, paintings, digital illustrations, multimedia works, and a literary side: poetry, prose, and essays. Photography is one part of a broader publication.

Eligibility is open to all ages, backgrounds, locations, and experience levels — no restriction by career stage. AI-generated work is not accepted.

The magazine is volunteer-run and submission is completely free. Each applicant may submit up to 3 entries. Submission is via Google Form.

The Issue 06 deadline was April 28, 2026; selections communicated May 5–8, 2026.
