The Book Project: Landscape 2026

Prize Publication in printed photobook

Juried call for a published hardbound photography book on landscape photography, part of Black Box Gallery’s Book Project series of focused hardbound books each built around a single theme. From grand landscapes to intimate details, the gallery is looking for work that demonstrates the photographer’s own connection to nature. Ten photographers will be selected with five photographs each. The 12 × 12 inch hardbound book is printed by Blurb and sold through the Black Box Gallery website. All rights to the photographs remain with the photographer and files are used only for the book printing. Photographers are notified 7–10 days after the deadline. Apply by paying the entry fee via PayPal and emailing your entry.

DeadlineOctober 25, 2026
ResultsEarly November 2026
Entry fee$40
LocationPortland, OR
Requirements5–10 images
Submit viaEmail

Jury: Todd Johnson, Founder & Director, Black Box Gallery
