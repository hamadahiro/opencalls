Pet Project

The Bedford's annual juried exhibition returns with Pet Project, a whimsical spin through the joy of the animal companions who captivate us with their larger-than-life personalities and quirks — a playful menagerie of muses and mischief-makers who slither, flutter, scamper and pounce their way into our lives. The call is open to all media and to US-based artists 18 and over; only artwork about pets will be juried. Artists may submit up to three works, with no size limit in principle though the gallery reserves the right to decline exceptionally large or cumbersome pieces, and 2D work must arrive framed where applicable and ready to hang. Jurors are to be announced, and $2,000 in cash prizes will be given as juror awards. The $40 entry fee is waived for anyone who donates $50 or more to the Bedford before applying, using a donor code entered at checkout. Entries close at 12 pm Pacific on 24 March 2027 — a midday cut-off, not end of day. Notice of participation goes out 23 April 2027, work is delivered 14–18 June and the exhibition runs 10 July to 12 September 2027 at the Bedford Gallery in the Lesher Center for the Arts, opening with a party on 10 July. Artists are encouraged but not required to list work for sale; the gallery's commission structure is 60 per cent of the sale price to the artist and 40 per cent retained.
