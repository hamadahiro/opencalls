Pride Photo 2027

Pride Photo has run an annual international contest since 2011 and it remains one of the few genuinely free, genuinely international open calls built entirely around LGBTQIA+ stories. There are two ways in: STORIES, for a project series of 3 to 15 images, and SINGLE IMAGES, for up to five standalone works that carry a story in one frame. You may submit two projects per application and apply more than once. The brief is deliberately wide on form — documentary, portraiture, collage, photographic intervention — and specific on intent: work that broadens perspectives on the range of LGBTQIA+ experience rather than illustrating a single idea of it. Minimum file size is 3000 pixels on the long side.

What comes back is money and sustained visibility. Selected photographers receive EUR 500 for each work taken into the Pride Photo Exhibition 2027, which opens in Amsterdam and then tours nationally and internationally from spring 2027 to spring 2028, reaching gallery professionals, press and general audiences; work is also carried across Pride Photo's own print and digital platforms with credit. Copyright stays with the photographer; the licence granted is non-exclusive and limited to the exhibition, archive and the promotional, educational and research activity around it, and any commercial use beyond that needs separate permission. One clause worth reading if you photograph other people: the rules require that depicted subjects cooperated voluntarily, know about the submission and have consented to public display.

The AI position is unusually well argued rather than simply stated. All use of AI must be declared with a clear description of how it was applied in the making or editing of the work, and AI-assisted submissions are then assessed case by case — but images created entirely through generative AI are refused, on the stated ground that pictures representing LGBTQIA+ lives should be grounded in lived reality and that image datasets reproduce dominant assumptions about marginalised communities. Submissions opened 16 September 2026 and close 31 October 2026 at 23:59 CET, through Picter only; entries sent by email are not accepted. Winners are announced in June 2027 and the 2027 jury is chaired by Supranav Dash.
