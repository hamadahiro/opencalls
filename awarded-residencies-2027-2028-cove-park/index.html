Awarded Residencies 2027 & 2028

Cove Park funds two-week to three-month residencies in rural Argyll between May 2027 and December 2028, paying £775 a week in 2027 plus travel and, where needed, materials, access and visa costs. For 2027-28 applicants must be based in the UK, Central or South America, or from that diaspora in Europe or North America. Any art form and any career stage, but anyone in formal education at the deadline is not eligible.
