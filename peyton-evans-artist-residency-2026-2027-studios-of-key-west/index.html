Peyton Evans Artist Residency (PEAR) 2026–2027

One-month residency program on Key West, Florida. Photographers apply under Media Arts. Residencies run the 2nd day of the month to the last day, between October 2026 and August 2027. Residents receive accommodation at the PEAR House (live/work space), a bike to get around the island, and access to shared kitchen facilities. No residency fee once accepted; artists cover their own travel, living expenses, and materials. Founded in 2006, The Studios has hosted hundreds of writers and artists; selection committees are comprised of working artists and professionals across Visual Arts, Literary Arts, Media Arts, Musical Arts, and Performing Arts.
