In and Out of Balance

Drawing Rooms, the Jersey City gallery run by the non-profit Victory Hall Inc. inside the Topps Building at the Mana Contemporary complex, invites work for a group show about visual weight, tension and spatial harmony. The gallery asks how balance is managed inside a picture: symmetrical arrangements that read as calm and ordered, asymmetrical ones where unequal elements hold equal visual weight through colour, size and negative space, compositions that radiate from a central axis, and deliberate weight shifts that make a work feel precarious. Its own reference list runs from Richard Serra and Malevich to Agnes Martin, Sarah Sze, Wangechi Mutu and Cecily Brown. Acceptable media are named as painting, drawing, printmaking, collage, photography, sculpture or any other fine art medium, and work must be original - the gallery accepts no AI and no reproductions. Anne Trauben, the gallery's curator and director, selects. Entry is $35 for up to three images, jpegs at least 1200 pixels on any side, and applicants must be over 18. The exhibition runs 29 October to 21 November 2026 with an opening reception on 30 October, Meet the Artists on 8 November and a closing reception on 22 November. Sales split 60% to the artist and 40% to the gallery, and artists chosen here are also considered for the gallery's other programmes, including the Art Project JC lobbies and its Artsy exhibitions.
