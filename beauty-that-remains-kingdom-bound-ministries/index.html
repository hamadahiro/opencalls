Beauty That Remains

Kingdom Bound Ministries and Project Lab are gathering work for a one-day community art exhibition in Buffalo's Sheridan Parkside neighbourhood, built around faith, restoration and what endures through hardship. The call is for work that speaks to rebuilding — restoration, redemption, peace, resilience, compassion, healing and the beauty that survives difficult seasons — and especially to community: the ways people come together to support one another and carry hope forward. Work may be symbolic, abstract, deeply personal or narrative-driven. Accepted media are listed explicitly and include photography alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, mixed media, digital art, printmaking and textile/fibre work; AI-generated art is not accepted. The exhibition is a fundraiser tied to the conversion of a long-closed school building into the Sheridan Parkside Life Center, a space for connection, care and creativity, and proceeds also support the centre's literacy initiatives for local children. Artists may submit one to ten images for a $25 fee; selected work is shown on 19 September 2026 from 11am to 4pm at 169 Sheridan Parkside Drive, Tonawanda, New York, with a raffle of donated pieces running through the day and artists able to sell their work on a 60/40 split. Notification is 26 August 2026.
