HUMOROUS: The Art of Laughter

Prizes Awards Exhibition

The Armory Art Center's juried gallery exhibition for Florida-based artists, hung alongside the tenth annual Creative Market on its historic West Palm Beach campus in Howard Park. The theme is humour in all its registers: playful portraiture and caricature, visual puns and wordplay, absurd and surreal scenes, comics and graphic narratives, everyday life with a twist, pop-culture and nostalgia parody, and animal antics. The Armory argues that humour in art does more than amuse, that it shifts perspectives, crosses language and cultural lines, and gives satire a way to critique politics and social norms. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside oil, acrylic, water media, pastel, drawing, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, fibre, wood, ceramics and digital art; AI-generated work is not accepted, and diptychs and triptychs must be uploaded as a single image. Entry is $43 plus processing for up to two pieces, each judged individually, so the jury may take neither, one or both. The show is curated and juried by Armory staff with an anonymous juror, and first, second and third awards are given. Open to amateur and professional artists eighteen and over living in Florida. Notification is by October 16 and the exhibition runs November 2 to December 26, 2026 with a reception on November 19. All work must be for sale, with a 60/40 split in the artist's favour, and artists cover shipping both ways.
