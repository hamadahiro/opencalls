Big Sky

Prizes $250 $100

The theme is wide enough to be genuinely open — Webster Arts puts "vast, natural geographies" and "the layouts of our living rooms" in the same sentence — and the medium rule is an affirmative with a single carve-out, all media except video. For a photographer that is a clearer invitation than most all-media calls manage.

Read the fee as a ladder, not a flat price. The gallery page prints "Entry Fee: $25.00" and the portal record carries a $5 additional-media rung on top of a three-image minimum, so a full ten-image entry costs $60, not $25.

The shipping clause is the real cost for anyone outside Missouri: accepted work has to reach the gallery by 23 December 2026 and stay up until 8 February 2027, framed and wired to spec. The awards total $350 across two prizes and no overall total is advertised.

The published notification date is impossible — the dates block reads "Wednesday, July 8, 2026: All artists notified by email", four months before the deadline, which reads as a year carried over from an earlier cycle's template. Treat the notification date as unpublished until the gallery corrects it.

A second date in the portal record is wrong by a year, and it is the one an out-of-state artist plans shipping around. The record’s opening paragraph says selected work is expected at the gallery “before the exhibition opening on Friday, January 8, 2026”, while its own dates block and the gallery page both say “Friday, January 8, 2027”. 8 January is a Friday in 2027 and a Thursday in 2026, and the same paragraph calls this “our opening show of 2027”, so 2027 is the real year; the 2026 string is a leftover.
