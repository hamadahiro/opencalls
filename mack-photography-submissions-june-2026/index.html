MACK — Photography Submissions

MACK's annual photography submission window. One of the world's most prestigious art and photography publishers, founded by Michael Mack in 2010 and based in London. This window seeks photobook projects that expand visual vocabulary and challenge expectations of photography's potential, with a special interest in voices and perspectives that are often underrepresented in the photobook landscape. Submit a PDF draft sequence and completed submissions form via email during the June 1–30 window only. No WeTransfer, Dropbox, or download links accepted. Email subject line: 'Submission: Photography'. Response within one month after the window closes.
