MCAD–Jerome Foundation Fellowship for Early Career Artists 2026/27

Prizes $10,000 Exhibition Catalogue

Four unrestricted $10,000 fellowships for early-career visual artists living in Minnesota, administered by the Minneapolis College of Art and Design and funded by the Jerome Foundation, which has run this programme since 1981 and has backed more than two hundred artists through it. Photography is named outright in the eligible media, alongside painting, sculpture, drawing, printmaking, sequential and comic art, public and social practice, conceptual, installation, graffiti, textile, ceramics, glass, bead, metal, paper, and digital video or film presented in an exhibition or installation context; the programme is aimed at artists who can present work in a gallery. Each fellow receives the $10,000 in unrestricted funds plus $1,000 in reimbursement-based professional development money, three studio visits from professional critics (a local curator, a catalogue writer and a national curator), an exhibition at the MCAD Gallery in January to March 2028, a catalogue with a critical essay on their work, a place on a public panel, access to MCAD's facilities and library, and half-price Continuing Education tuition. The fellowship period runs 1 December 2026 to 15 March 2028. Eligibility is tight and worth reading before applying: you must be a legal Minnesota resident for at least a year before the deadline and stay in the state for the whole fellowship, and you must have between two and ten years of generative practice outside any degree programme. Artists with significant recognition may be too established even inside that window. Applications go through Submittable and ask for proof of residency, ten work samples in any mix of image, audio, video and PDF, a resume, an artist statement and a fellowship goals statement — the guidelines ask that you not use AI or ChatGPT to write either statement. An independent three-person jury scores every application, shortlists twenty-five to thirty, then ten finalists who get twenty-minute online studio visits on 12 and 13 November. Staff take no part in the selection, and MCAD-affiliated applicants can never be more than half of any cohort.
