Surreal Salon 19

The nineteenth edition of Baton Rouge Gallery's annual international exhibition celebrating pop-surrealism and lowbrow art, held in partnership with the LSU School of Art and open to artists over 18 anywhere in the world. The guest juror is Lauren YS, who hand-picks the show through a blind process — the juror sees neither the artist's name nor their home city. Judging criteria are stated as conceptual and aesthetic quality, feasibility of display, and overall artistic merit. Photography is not listed among accepted media because no media list is published; what the gallery does say is that “for more than a decade, Surreal Salon has featured paintings, photography, ceramics, textiles, mixed media works, and more.” Work must be physical, tangible and non-perishable, and two-dimensional work must arrive ready to hang in a professional gallery — no bull clips, clip frames, magnets, push-pins or poster frames. $2,000 in cash prizes is awarded: $1,000 Best in Show, $600 second, $400 third. The gallery states in one place that the Best in Show artist is profiled in an online editorial by Beautiful Bizarre Magazine and in another that the editorial runs on juxtapoz.com; the two sentences sit in the same document, so expect one of them. Entry is $35 for up to three works. All work must be for sale and the gallery takes a 50% commission on anything sold, which is steep and worth pricing for. Selected work is delivered by 6pm on December 19, 2026; the show runs January 2 to 24, 2027 at the gallery on Dalrymple Drive, and shipping both ways is the artist's cost. Work left uncollected after February 14, 2027 is charged $10 a day in storage. The gallery reserves the right to disqualify submissions it deems pornographic, explicitly violent, obscene or hateful.
