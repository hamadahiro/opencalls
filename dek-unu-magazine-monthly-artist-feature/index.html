Dek Unu Magazine - Monthly Artist Feature

Prize Publication prize

Dek Unu Magazine is an international not-for-profit photography journal that publishes monthly. The structural premise is unusual: each issue features one accomplished artist rather than a multi-photographer group format. Issues are published as both print and online paperback.

Eligibility is open across all fine art photography genres and techniques. Selection criteria pair technical excellence with artistic vision and conceptual intent — the magazine is looking for resolved, mature bodies of work. The Monthly Artist Feature is the magazine's rolling submission stream — applications can be made at any time, with editorial review on a continuing basis.

Submission is by email with 11 images. Application is free. Eligibility is open globally.
