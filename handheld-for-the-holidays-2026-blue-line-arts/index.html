Handheld for the Holidays 2026

Prize Exhibition

Blue Line Arts in Roseville invites artists to submit smaller-scale handmade work for a holiday selling show, naming mugs, bowls, coasters and prints as examples of what it is after. Work is sold directly off the wall or shelf and there may be a chance to restock during the run. The gallery encourages pricing at or below $100 but will take pieces above that, and asks specifically for handmade items rather than commercially printed or pre-manufactured goods - prints are named among the wanted examples, so photographers should read both clauses before deciding what to send. Open to artists everywhere aged 18 and over; work must be original, made within the last two years, never previously exhibited at Blue Line Arts, and individually for sale. The gallery handles all sales, paying non-members 50% and members 60%. Entry is $25 for non-members covering up to 10 artworks and free for current Blue Line Arts Artist Members at the $75 level or above via a discount code from the gallery. Applications close 25 September 2026 at 5pm Pacific, notifications go out 16 October, and the show runs 24 November to 19 December 2026 with a reception on Saturday 19 December.
