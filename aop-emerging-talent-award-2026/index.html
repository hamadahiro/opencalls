AOP Emerging Talent Award 2026

Prizes AOP membership London billboard Exhibition

AOP Project Award celebrating the new wave of photographers and image-makers who have been practicing professionally — full or part-time — for under three years. No subject restrictions: enter in three formats — Stills (a single image or a 2–5 image series), Moving Image (up to 7 minutes), or Innovation (creative use of AR, VR, CGI or other techniques). Open to AOP members and non-members alike, of any age or background. Judged by established professionals and industry representatives. The Gold winner receives a tailored AOP Associate Membership package built around their career path: 1:1 folio reviews with industry representatives, an AOP Business Practice Review worth £500, and a 12-month professional mentorship — plus a BUILDHOLLYWOOD digital billboard display across London worth £8,520. Gold and Silver winners and selected finalists are exhibited in late 2026 and featured in an Awards publication.

DeadlineJuly 20, 2026
Entry fee£10 / £15 single (member / non-member)
EligibilityEmerging artists
LocationLondon, UK
Requirements1 image or 2–5 series

Jury: Jack Eames, Photographer & Director · Jason Baron, Head of Photography, BUILDHOLLYWOOD · Kate Congreve, Producer, Mother London · Kim Pappas, Photographic Agent & Producer
