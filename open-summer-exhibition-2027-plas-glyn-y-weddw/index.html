Open Summer Exhibition 2027

Prizes £1,500 Solo exhibition

Plas Glyn-y-Weddw is an 1857 mansion at Llanbedrog, on the Llŷn Peninsula in north-west Wales, that has housed an art gallery since 1896 and has been run by an independent charitable trust since 1996. The trust's own description of it is "an arts and heritage centre of national importance". Its annual Summer Exhibition has grown to the point where the gallery has rebuilt it from 2027 as a genuinely open submission: everyone who wants to exhibit now applies, and an independent selection panel decides. This is the first edition under that format.

Photography is explicitly welcome, though you have to open the guidelines PDF to find it: the call page says only "mixed-media showcase". The guidelines are unambiguous - the exhibition is for visual art only, and the named disciplines are painting, drawing, printmaking, photography, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, mixed media and other visual art practices. Film, video, animation, sound, performance and written work are all excluded, which puts a photographic print firmly inside the brief and a moving-image piece firmly outside it.

The gate is age, not standing. Applicants must be 16 or over; beyond that the gallery welcomes professionals, emerging practitioners, students and people who make work in their spare time, and there is no geographic restriction despite the Welsh framing. The caveat is commercial: while the opportunity is open to everyone, selected works are expected to meet a professional standard suitable for exhibition and sale, and the gallery says it is looking for well-resolved proposals with a clear artistic intent and strong potential for commercial viability.

Costs and returns are both worth doing the arithmetic on. Entry is £15 for up to three works, with additional works at £5 each and a ceiling of six, so the maximum entry fee is £30. Against that: a Judges' Choice Award of £1,500 plus a future solo show at the gallery, and a Young Artist Award of £500 for an entrant under 25. If your work sells, the gallery takes 43% including VAT, deducted from the final sale price - a figure that appears only in the guidelines FAQ, not on the call page, and one to weigh before you price anything.

Practicalities. The deadline is 1 March 2027 at 17:00 - a 5pm cutoff, not midnight, and the call page is the one surface that does not say so - the time is in the guidelines FAQ and on the CuratorSpace listing. Wall-based work is capped at 1m² and plinth or floor work at 60cm³. The work you submit images of must be the work you exhibit: substitutes will not be accepted, and pieces that differ from the submission in appearance or size may be rejected. This is an "off-the-wall" hang - buyers take work away during the run, so the show changes as it sells. It runs 25 July to 3 October 2027, 9:30 to 17:00 daily with free entry, and the gallery tells applicants it expects 30,000 visitors across it. There is a free public opening with refreshments. You do not need to be present. Applications are made through a free CuratorSpace account; the gallery's own address, post@oriel.org.uk, handles questions.
