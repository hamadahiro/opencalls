Adler Gallery Exhibition — January–February 2027

Prize Two-month solo exhibition

The Karen and Ed Adler Gallery at Port Washington Public Library gives one artist at a time a two-month solo exhibition in a dedicated gallery space, and there is an opening in the schedule for January-February 2027. Submissions are read by the library's Art Advisory Council: a preview committee makes the first cut and the full council then votes on whether to extend an invitation, with preview meetings held three times a year. During the run the artist is offered a two-hour public reception to talk about the work. Entry is free. Applicants fill in the library's own request form with a current resume and ten high-quality images of individual works, each labelled by title in the filename, tightly cropped so only the artwork shows, with no borders or backgrounds, and photographed straight on rather than at an angle; an image list giving title, medium and dimensions for each work is asked for, and a bio or promotional material can be attached as optional additional information. The library asks for a cohesive selection that reads as a curated exhibit rather than a survey. Only artists currently living in the tri-state area - New York, New Jersey and Connecticut - will be considered. Photography is part of the gallery's programme: past Adler Gallery shows include Steve Lampasona's The Light and Textures of New York City, a Holocaust survivor photography exhibit mounted around International Holocaust Remembrance Day, and Alan Richards's Let Me Show You Something, which built on archival and vintage photographs enhanced with drawing and digital painting. The exhibition runs 29 December 2026 to 28 February 2027.
