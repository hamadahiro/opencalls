Colorscapes

Global online exhibition from Photo Artfolio exploring the expressive power of colour in photography - bold saturated hues, subtle palettes and quiet tonal relationships, from the unexpected collision of colours on a city street to the shifting tones of a landscape, a portrait defined by its palette, or an abstract composition built entirely around colour, light and form. Open to all photographic genres and approaches, including documentary, portraiture, street, landscape, nature, architecture, still life, conceptual, fine art and experimental work. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers; all work must be original and made by the submitting photographer, and AI-generated imagery is not eligible. Up to 20 images may be entered, at $20 for the first image and $5 for each additional one. Cash prizes of $500, $350 and $150, and all accepted photographs are shown in the online exhibition, which opens on 11 November 2026. Photo Artfolio now carries the full Colorscapes prospectus on its own site, at photo-artfolio.com/colorscapesprospectus-1, and entries are made through CaFE. Submissions are reviewed by Photo Artfolio's creative director Julia Cumes with an anonymous committee of jurors.
