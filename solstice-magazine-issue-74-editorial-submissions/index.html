Solstice Magazine Issue 74 - Editorial Submissions

Prize Publication

Submit fashion and editorial photography for publication in Issue 74 of Solstice Magazine, a London-based fashion publication. Submissions close 11:59pm EST on 21 November 2026 and the issue is released at 10pm EST on 21 December 2026. Editorials must be unpublished and exclusive to Solstice - work found published elsewhere is pulled and the contributor blacklisted, and accepted work must stay offline until the issue is out. Fashion submissions need a minimum of five outfit changes (beauty submissions, five hair and make-up looks) with at least three finished, retouched photographs of each outfit, and every garment must carry a real brand credit; stylist's own and model's own credits are rejected, including for vintage and second-hand pieces. Website editorials are open year-round and usually publish within two or three working days. There is no submission fee and no payment for editorials; the magazine does not send complimentary print copies but will supply digital tear sheets on request.
