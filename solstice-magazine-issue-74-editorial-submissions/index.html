Solstice Magazine Issue 74 - Editorial Submissions

Submit fashion and editorial photography for publication in Issue 74 of Solstice Magazine, a London-based fashion publication. Submissions close 11:59pm EST on 21 November 2026 and the issue is released at 10pm EST on 21 December 2026. Editorials must be unpublished and exclusive to Solstice - work found published elsewhere is pulled and the contributor blacklisted, and accepted work must stay offline until the issue is out. Fashion submissions need a minimum of five outfit changes (beauty submissions, five hair and make-up looks) with at least three finished, retouched photographs of each outfit, and every garment must carry a real brand credit; stylist's own and model's own credits are rejected, including for vintage and second-hand pieces. Website editorials are open year-round and usually publish within two or three working days. There is no submission fee and no payment for editorials; the magazine does not send complimentary print copies but will supply digital tear sheets on request. Two paid routes do exist, and they are the only money on the page: an editorial with fewer than five outfits or looks is normally rejected, but the magazine says a contributor “can opt to pay a penalty fee of USD280.00 to have the work published”, by mutual agreement and only if the work is good enough; and if a set repeats a brand across more than two outfits or “focuses on a particular brad/designer” (sic), “a fee starting from USD380.00 would have to be paid for it to be published”. Both are optional, and neither is a condition of a conforming submission.
