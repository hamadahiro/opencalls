Windows on the World

Click! Photography Festival turns downtown Raleigh into a month-long photography city every October, spreading exhibitions, talks and screenings across Raleigh, Durham and Chapel Hill. Windows on the World is its storefront show: selected photographs are printed by the festival and installed in and around the shop windows of City Market, the historic brick market district in downtown Raleigh, so the work is met by people walking past rather than by gallery visitors. The theme is JOY, and the call asks what joy looks like to you — a fleeting smile, a quiet landscape, a family celebration, an unexpected encounter, a moment of personal triumph. All genres are welcome, from documentary and street photography to portraits, landscapes, abstract and experimental imagery, and all photographic processes are accepted: alternative, film or digital. The call is open to photographers of all levels, from emerging talents to seasoned professionals, and international submissions are accepted. Entry is $45 for up to five images. Artists do not ship work: everything selected is printed and displayed by the festival, with the possibility of a projection event on First Friday. The exhibition runs 1–31 October 2026 with an opening reception on Friday 2 October, and images not selected for the outdoor Plein Air exhibition are eligible for a travelling slide show presented around the Triangle through October. AI-generated images are not eligible.
