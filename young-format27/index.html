Young FORMAT27

Prize Exhibition at QUAD, Derby and Photofusion, London

FORMAT, the UK's leading festival of international photography, established in 2004 and run by QUAD in Derby, opens its strand for photographers aged 25 and under for the FORMAT27 Biennale. QUAD/FORMAT's Young Advocates and Photofusion's Young Curators — Photofusion is described by the call as "London's largest all-encompassing photography centre" — are "seeking photographs from children and young people, aged 25 years and under, to exhibit as part of the FORMAT27 Biennale in Derby and at Photofusion in London". The two young curatorial teams each curate a physical exhibition of selected works, one at QUAD in Derby and one at Photofusion in London, both shown during 2027; FORMAT27 itself runs across Derby venues from 11 March to 11 April 2027 on the festival theme of PLAY. The brief asks for your interpretation of PLAY and is explicit that no special equipment or experience is needed: "Use what you've got… There's no right or wrong way to do this, so enjoy yourself and feel free to experiment." It suggests thinking about angles and viewpoints, composition, shape, light and colour, abstract and conceptual readings of the theme, experimenting with new techniques and unconventional methods. SUBMISSION: up to five images with a short description of how the work responds to the theme, through the festival's Tally form; JPEG or JPG, each file no larger than 10 MB, named FIRSTNAME_AGE (the call's own example is AMY_22). Work selected but supplied at too low a print quality will be shown on a digital display instead, and depending on volume other submissions may be shown as a collage or on digital display. Teachers and lecturers submitting on behalf of students indicate this on the form. Under-18s must have their work submitted by a parent or legal guardian, and any image featuring a person under 18 needs a completed consent form, downloadable through the submission form, or the work cannot be exhibited; images of under-18s will not be used in promotional material. DEADLINE CAUTION: the call page prints two different dates — "Deadline for submissions: Sunday 13th December 2026" at the top and "Submission deadline: Sunday 6 December 2026" lower down. The submission form itself states Sunday 13 December 2026, which is the date stored here; if you want to be safe, submit by 6 December. Outcomes are announced in February 2027.
