Art in the Atrium 26

The 26th installment of the City of Frisco's Art in the Atrium, a free rotating exhibition in the atrium of the George A. Purefoy Municipal Center. The city seeks original two-dimensional work from artists in Frisco and the surrounding area, loaned for display from 30 September 2026 to 1 February 2027. Photographs are explicitly provided for and must be printed on quality paper at a resolution of at least 300 dpi; work must have been made within the past two years, be framed and ready to hang with wire securely attached — unframed canvases with finished edges and wire are also accepted — and measure no more than 60 by 60 inches. Three-dimensional work is not considered. Each artist may submit two images through CaFÉ at no cost, with a description explaining motivation, themes or process, and pieces may be offered for sale. Submissions are reviewed by the Arts and Culture Advisory Board and city staff on criteria including uniqueness and originality, visual impact and demonstrated excellence of technique, and every artist is notified in writing. Work must be delivered and collected in person, with drop-off on 29 September 2026 — shipped work is not accepted and return shipping is not provided. The entry deadline is Wednesday 6 September 2026.
