Fotografiska Emerging Artist Platform

Prize Exhibition

Fotografiska's emerging program is structured around continuous open calls plus rotating themed cycles. The "Foto of the Day" track runs weekly: seven images selected from open submissions, featured on Fotografiska's website and social channels. Themed calls run alongside it, with regional eligibility for some — Sweden-only for the Stockholm shows, Baltics-and-Finland for Tallinn — and global eligibility for others.

Selected work has been shown at Fotografiska's museum locations in Berlin, Stockholm, and Tallinn. The platform doesn't publish guaranteed compensation; outcomes vary by call. Submissions are free and limited to three per cycle. The AI rule is set per call rather than platform-wide: the Home open call states that prompt-based or generative-AI images are not accepted, while the platform terms allow work created or modified with AI provided it is labelled as AI-generated or prompt-based at submission.

Each themed call names its own selectors: Home was curated by guest curator James Nachtwey with Pauline Benthede, Fotografiska's Global Vice President of Artistic Direction and Exhibitions, and Sophia Emmerich, while Emerging Global is judged by the Fotografiska Berlin team. Emerging Global — the first Emerging Global group exhibition, at Fotografiska Berlin — is open until 15 October 2026 with notification 15 November 2026; "Foto of the Day" remains continuous.
