Blank Wall Gallery — Moments of Color

Open call on colour — the bold contrast of urban life, the soft pastels of a sunset, the vibrant hues of nature — asking how photographers capture the essence of colour in their work and turn ordinary moments into something extraordinary. Free to submit; selected photographers pay a participation fee only if exhibited (€35 if the photograph is printed, €15 if it is shown on the gallery's 43-inch 4K digital screen). Over 100 printed photographs are shown in each exhibition, with printing on 260 gsm paper, framing, hanging, invitations and the opening night all covered by the gallery, and no commission taken on sales. Selected work is shown at Blank Wall Gallery in Athens, 23 October – 4 November 2026.
