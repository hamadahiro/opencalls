Weathered & Fired: Barnstormers and Atmospheric Fired Ceramics

Prize Exhibition

Artspiration Frederick invites original work inspired by barns, farms, rural landscapes and the passage of time in Frederick County, Maryland — from weathered wood and rusted metal to atmospheric fired ceramics that embrace the elements. All media are accepted and photography is named explicitly alongside 2D, 3D, ceramics, sculpture and mixed media. Artists may submit up to three pieces and up to three may be selected; all work must be original, exhibition-ready and for sale, with 2D work properly framed and wired (no sawtooth hangers). The exhibition runs 2 October to 1 November 2026 with an artists' reception on 10 October, in a gallery that is free to enter, open to the public and wheelchair accessible. Accepted work is dropped off 29–30 September, or shipped to arrive by 29 September at the artist's cost with a prepaid return label. Jurors are potter Tony Clennell, a Royal Canadian Academy of Arts member with an MFA from Utah State who has taught across four continents, and Kimberly Yourick, founder of Lockhouse Gallery in Brunswick, Maryland and an oil painter specialising in plein air landscapes. Notification is emailed the week of 21 September.
