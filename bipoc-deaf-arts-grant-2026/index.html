BIPOC Deaf Arts Grant 2026

Prize $1,000

Deaf Spotlight, the Seattle organisation working to advance Deaf arts, runs the BIPOC Deaf Arts Grant annually to provide financial support to artists sitting at an intersection that most open calls never address: artists who are both from an underrepresented racial background and Deaf, DeafBlind, DeafDisabled or Hard of Hearing.

Photography is explicitly eligible. The grant accepts work across five discipline categories — Literary, Media, Performing, Tactile and Visual — with photography named under Visual Arts alongside printmaking, painting, drawing and sculpture. The award is unrestricted, and applications are evaluated on three equally weighted factors: the applicant's vision for the grant, their experience and potential, and the artistic growth they have demonstrated over time.

For 2026, five recipients each receive $1,000, which can be taken as a single payment or as two instalments of $500. The geographic spread is deliberate: at least one recipient will reside in the Pacific Northwest (Washington, Oregon or Idaho), at least one in California, and at least one will be a national selection from outside the Pacific Northwest region.

To apply you must identify as BIPOC, identify as Deaf, DeafBlind, DeafDisabled or Hard of Hearing, live in the United States or a US territory — citizenship is not required — and be at least 18 by the deadline. Past recipients of the grant are not eligible, nor is anyone serving Deaf Spotlight or Urban Jazz Dance Company as a board member, grant judging or planning committee member, or staff member during 2026.

Applications opened 1 June 2026 and close Friday 28 August 2026 at 11:59pm PST, submitted through Submittable. There is no application fee, and notification is sent by email in October 2026. Deaf Spotlight invites applicants to get in touch about any accommodations the organisation can provide.
