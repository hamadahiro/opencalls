This Land Is Your Land

Prize Exhibition

Community photography exhibit at the Library Arts Center gallery in Newport, New Hampshire, marking America's 250th anniversary. The call asks photographers to share images of the places, faces, traditions and stories that make up the fabric of America — sweeping landscapes and quiet hometown streets, everyday moments and the people who shape communities, natural beauty alongside resilience and heritage. Open to photographers of all experience levels who live in New Hampshire or Vermont, or who have a distinct connection to the area; up to two pieces per photographer. Entry is free for Library Arts Center members and $5 otherwise. Accepted artists are notified by July 28 and deliver framed, ready-to-hang work to the gallery on August 5–6. The exhibit runs August 15 – October 9, 2026, with an opening reception on August 14.
