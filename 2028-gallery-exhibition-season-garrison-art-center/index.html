2028 Gallery Exhibition Season

Garrison Art Center is a 501(c)(3) on Garrison's Landing, directly across the Hudson from West Point, and it programmes its two galleries a full season ahead. This call is for the 2028 season and takes proposals for solo shows, two- and three-person shows and small curated group exhibitions; proposals are paired so that both rooms are filled at once, with each accepted proposal placed in either the 625-square-foot Gillette Gallery or the 360-square-foot Balter Gallery, while the Visiting Artist gets both. Photography is one of the seven media the centre lists on its own prospectus, alongside mixed media, sculpture, painting, drawing and printmaking; the current ROOTS exhibition in the galleries includes cyanotype, which the centre describes as a camera-less photographic process. Applicants are US residents aged 18 or over with a studio within a 90-minute radius of Garrison, NY - the centre is candid that this is about logistics rather than prestige: artists deliver and collect their own work, must attend the opening reception, and the gallery coordinator and committee prefer to curate the show from an in-person studio visit, though Zoom is possible. Group proposals pay once and apply once, with individual CVs but a single unified proposal and the images divided between the artists. Five to ten work samples are required. A jury of ten reviews everything over several rounds, a deliberate process the centre says takes several months, so expect to hear back no earlier than May 2027. Applications opened August 15, 2026 and close January 15, 2027; the application fee is $30.
