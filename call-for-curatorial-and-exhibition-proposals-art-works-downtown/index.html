Call for Curatorial & Exhibition Proposals

Prizes Up to $5,000 Exhibition

Art Works Downtown, the 501(c)(3) non-profit arts organisation whose 40,000-square-foot building at 1325-1337 Fourth Street in downtown San Rafael houses galleries and 33 artist studios, takes proposals on a rolling basis for Gallery 1337, its primary exhibition space. The invitation is deliberately wide: emerging and established curators, independent curators, artists, and collaborative teams may propose solo or group exhibitions, and AWD says it is looking for "projects with a clear vision, strong artistic quality, professionalism, originality, and a compelling concept suited to the gallery and our public audience." What makes this worth a photographer's or curator's time is that AWD budgets for the work the proposal implies rather than simply lending a wall: selected projects may receive up to $5,000 in curatorial or exhibition support, use of Gallery 1337, exhibition planning and production support, advertising and promotion, gallery staffing during public hours (Wednesday to Saturday, 11am to 5pm), two exhibition receptions, and support for related public programs. There is no application fee. A proposal should carry the exhibition title; a curatorial or exhibition statement setting out concept, themes and goals; curator and/or artist bio and CV; participating artists with brief bios and portfolio links if they are already identified; representative artwork images with artist, title, medium, dimensions, date and price where applicable; the proposed number and type of works; an installation concept with any technical or equipment needs; preferred dates and timeline; public programming ideas; audience, outreach or partnership ideas; and a preliminary budget naming the intended use of any requested stipend. Proposals are weighed on strength and originality of concept, quality of artwork, professionalism and completeness, feasibility, appropriate scale and quantity, ability to engage a broad public audience, inclusivity, diversity, equity, accessibility and representation, and the potential for meaningful public programs. Photographic work is squarely inside the artwork guidelines — AWD primarily exhibits original artwork and permits signed and numbered limited editions, while un-editioned reproductions and print-to-order works are generally not accepted; video and new media are welcome, though most equipment must come from the artist or curator, audio normally needs headphones, and projection may be limited by ambient light. Explicit sexual content and graphic violence are not eligible. Artists should generally reside within 100 miles of San Rafael unless otherwise approved. At least half the exhibited work should be available for sale; AWD processes sales and the tax, keeping 40% of the retail price and paying 60% to the artist unless something else is agreed in writing. Proposals go by email to the exhibitions contact, with no phone calls; AWD warns that the volume of submissions means it may not answer every applicant and cannot give individual critiques, and that it will reach out directly where a proposal fits an upcoming slot.
