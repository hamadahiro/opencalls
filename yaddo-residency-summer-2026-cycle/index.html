Yaddo Residency — Summer 2026 Cycle

Two-week to two-month residency at Yaddo's 400-acre estate in Saratoga Springs, NY, founded in 1900. Open to professional and emerging artists across literature, visual art (painting, drawing, sculpture, printmaking, photography, mixed media, installation), music composition, performance, and film & video. Residents receive room, board, and a private studio; modest access grants available to offset travel. The Summer cycle covers residencies November 2026 through June 2027. Portal opens June 1; application fee waivable in cases of financial hardship. Yaddo encourages applications from artists from backgrounds underrepresented in arts and culture.
