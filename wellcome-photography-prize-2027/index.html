Wellcome Photography Prize 2027

Prizes £10,000 per category 22 × £1,000

Wellcome's biennial photography prize returns for 2027, open to everyone, everywhere, and free to enter. The prize brings photography and scientific imaging together around health and science: discovery research, mental health, infectious disease, and climate and health. There are three categories — Striking solo photography (a single photograph), A storytelling series (3–5 photographs), and The marvels of scientific and medical imaging (a single image made with a scientific or medical imaging technique). Each category winner receives £10,000, and a further 22 entries across the three categories are awarded £1,000 each; the prize draws extensive international media coverage and image makers may be invited to showcase their work at events. Eligible disciplines include documentary, clinical, studio and still-life photography, close-up photography and photomacrography, microscopy, and medical imaging such as CT, PET, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound and thermography, as well as new and emerging biomedical imaging technologies. Illustration, moving image and AI-generated images are not accepted. Images must have been taken after 31 December 2021 and submitted at print resolution. Judges weigh creativity and visual impact, storytelling and relevance, and technical excellence — the story behind an image counts as much as the image. Wellcome also publishes ethical storytelling guidelines developed with Eh!woza. Entries opened 14 July 2026; the top 25 entries and the winners are announced in summer 2027.
