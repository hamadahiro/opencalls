42nd Annual International Exhibition

Prize Purchase awards (cash)

The Department of Art and Art History at The University of Texas at Tyler runs one of the longest-running juried competitions in the region, now in its 42nd year, shown at the university's Meadows Gallery. The 2027 edition is juried by Diana Roberts, an arts writer, curator and arts administrator in San Antonio who has been Director of The Center for Spirituality and the Arts, Curator of the Airport Art Spaces at San Antonio International Airport, and Gallery Director for Galeria Ortiz Contemporary. The call is explicitly open to two-dimensional media, and photographs are named alongside paintings, drawings, prints and fiber art; work must have been made in the last three years. Awards take the form of purchase awards: the department buys three to six works from the exhibition each year and adds them to its permanent collection, much of which hangs across the UT Tyler campus as a teaching resource. Entry is $35 for up to three works, judged from digital files; the CaFE entry window opens on 24 August 2026. Framed works must use Plexiglass rather than glass and weigh under 50 pounds; artists cover shipping both ways and must include a pre-paid return label. Notification is 14 December 2026, work is due at the gallery by 8 January 2027, and the exhibition runs 19 January to 5 March 2027 with a reception and juror's presentation on 4 March.
