Postmark Arts & Cultural Programming Grant 2027

The City of Auburn and the Auburn Arts Commission fund arts and cultural programming at the Postmark Center for the Arts, the city's arts venue at 20 Auburn Avenue, and at the adjoining outdoor Auburn Arts Alley. Grants of $500 to $2,000 go to the strongest proposals for programmes that take place there during 2027 and are free to the public - exhibitions, screenings, workshops, readings, performances and celebrations are all named as qualifying public benefit, so a photography show, a documentary screening or a darkroom workshop all fit. Individuals, community groups and arts organisations may apply and 501(c)(3) status is not required; no residency restriction is stated, but the programme must happen at the Postmark Center, and projects that charge admission, serve mainly an organisation's own membership, support religious activities or political organisations, or fund scholarships and ticket subsidies are excluded. The flexible-use hall seats about 90 theatre-style, with two glass-walled meeting rooms alongside. Applications are scored on artistic quality, community impact and accessibility, planning and capacity to deliver; the granting committee meets in November 2026 and payment is made on completion, against an invoice and final report. There is no application fee.
