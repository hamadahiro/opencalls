The Shape of Memory

The IU Kokomo Art Gallery's juried summer exhibition asks artists to treat memory as the foundation of identity and expression rather than simple recollection, sharing their own stories through colour, light and distortion and translating how lived experience has shifted over time. Open to all artists over 18 who reside in the United States; work must be original, completed within the last two years, and works created by artificial intelligence are not eligible. Up to three works, 2D, 3D and video all eligible. The gallery cannot take payment through CaFE: after an entry is received, staff email an invoice from the gallery within roughly three to five business days, and the $25 fee must be paid before the entry is shown to the juror. Photographs and works on paper must arrive framed with a wire for hanging; the gallery walls take 2D pieces up to 9.5 feet tall and 9 feet wide through the doors, with two long walls that can carry pieces up to 25 feet. Accepted work is reproduced in an exhibition catalog. Entries close 2 April 2027, notifications 26 April, work received 18-21 May, exhibition 1 June - 7 July 2027 with a closing reception on 7 July, 4-6pm. The call names no juror.
