Journey 2026 — Stirling Photography Festival Photo Book

Prize Publication

The Stirling Photography Festival's eighth annual commemorative photo book, themed Journey — the festival calls it “a vital part of our programme and an important fundraiser”. One picture per photographer, literal or interpretative responses to the theme, and images taken in the past are welcome; anything after a first submission is excluded. Open to photographers of all ages, skill levels and abilities, on any camera or camera phone, with no stated residency requirement — the festival runs in Stirling, Scotland, and was established in 2018. Composites are allowed provided every component image is the photographer's own; work must be camera-taken and not created with AI programs, AI-altered images are discouraged, and AI tools in the editing process are “acceptable within reason”. Selection is made by the festival's creative team during October 2026, and the book is published by the end of 2026. Submit one image with your name, an optional title and a description of up to 70 words, either by email or through the Google form — the form requires a Google sign-in, so the email route is the open one. No payment is made for the images, and entering grants the festival permission to use them beyond the book, which is sold to fundraise: in the festival's exhibition in print and online, and in future PR, advertising and fundraising. Credit to the photographer is always given.
