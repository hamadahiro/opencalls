Best of the Beach

A juried show at Gallery of Hermosa on Pier Avenue in Hermosa Beach, presented with the local Easy Reader newspaper, and the gallery is blunt that this is not a beach-art show: 'Artistic excellence is the standard, and there is no required subject. Subject matter, style, and medium are open.' The title is meant as an invitation rather than a theme — submit the work you believe is your best and say why it belongs. A short artist statement of up to 100 words is strongly recommended with each entry, and the gallery offers several readings to work from: your strongest piece, the beach as literal subject (coastline, water, light, wildlife, surf culture, life by the sea), the beach as metaphor (shoreline, tide, horizon, edge, impermanence, the meeting of two worlds), a personal connection to a place or community, or your own take. Open to local, national and international artists in any medium: 2D work in all media plus smaller 3D sculpture. Best in Show takes $500 and an article in Easy Reader, whose length, format and timing the paper decides; second place takes $200, third $100, and four further awards $50 each, announced at the opening reception, where winners need not be present. Entry is $36 for up to three images, with detail shots counting toward that total, and the jury's decision does not guarantee acceptance. The CaFE portal now takes entries until 11:59pm Pacific on 24 November 2026, two days later than the gallery's own at-a-glance line, which still reads “Submission deadline: Sunday, November 22, 2026 (CAFE applicaiton closes 10PM PST, please note the time)” — the platform record was moved after 18 November, so send early and do not count on the extra days. Decisions are emailed November 30, accepted work is dropped off December 7–9, the reception runs Saturday December 12 from 2 to 5pm, and the exhibition hangs December 12, 2026 to January 10, 2027, with pick-up on January 11–12. All accepted work must be offered for sale, at the gallery and online, from the time of acceptance — work marked not for sale is not eligible — and neither the gallery nor the CaFE prospectus states a commission on a sale. The artist is responsible for delivering or shipping work to and from the gallery. Two-dimensional work may be up to 48 inches wide by 72 inches high including the frame and 25 lbs, must arrive ready to hang on braided picture wire (no sawtooth hangers), and anything over 20 inches on a side must be glazed with acrylic rather than glass; photographs must be framed or mounted on board, and must be unique or part of a limited edition, with the series and edition noted on the work.
