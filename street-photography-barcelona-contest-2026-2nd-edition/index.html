Street Photography Barcelona Contest 2026 — 2nd Edition

Prizes €500 / €250 / €250 Exhibition

Second edition of the Street Photography Barcelona Contest (SPBC), run by the team behind Ibizacinefest under the theme 'The Street Is Art'. Open worldwide to photographers 18+, with 1, 3 or 5 single images (€10/€25/€40) of spontaneous street photography taken in public spaces within the last two years — no AI or manipulated work, no journalistic photos or artificially lit portraits. Twenty-five finalists are exhibited at Galerie Art Nomade in Barcelona's Born district, November 6–9, 2026, with printing, framing and installation fully covered, and screened at Ibizacinefest in March 2027. Cash prizes of €500 (overall winner, including a flight and hotel to present in Ibiza) and two €250 special mentions are paid before the festival. Jury led by Xavi Herrero and Olivier Verdier.
