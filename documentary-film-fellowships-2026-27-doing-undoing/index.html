Documentary Film Fellowships 2026–27 — Doing/Undoing

Prizes Up to INR 3,00,000 (~$3,400) production budget × 3 Mentored fellowship

Rough Edges and the Mrinalini Mukherjee Foundation are running a new round of their Documentary Film Fellowships, framed as support and mentorship for visual arts practitioners who want to expand an existing practice into documentary filmmaking rather than for established filmmakers. The 2026–27 theme, Doing/Undoing, asks for feminist enquiries into the everyday encounters between gender and language — how each shapes, transforms and disrupts the other — and the call explicitly asks for unconventional, interdisciplinary and experimental treatments rather than conventional documentary. The foundation states it is open to practitioners working in painting, sculpture, mural making, textile, printmaking, installation, performance and lens, media-based and mixed media art, so a photographer with no prior film credit is within scope. Three projects will be commissioned. Proposed films should run 40 to 60 minutes with a maximum budget of INR 3,00,000 per project, though the organisers say budgets will vary by project; the fellowships are mentored, developed in dialogue with Rough Edges with feedback from MMF, and selected artists commit to finishing in eight to nine months. Films may be in any language but final cuts must carry English subtitles. The fellowships are open to Indian women, non-binary, trans and queer visual artists aged 18 or over who are currently resident in India, applying as individuals or as teams; co-applicants and collaborators may identify any way, and the organisers especially encourage voices from communities marginalised by caste, class, religion, dis/ability, ethnicity, work or region. Projects developed in response to this call, or at a very early stage, are eligible; work already at rough-cut stage or needing only post-production money is not, and co-productions are excluded. Applications are free and may be sent as an online form, a downloadable form emailed as PDF, or a recorded video proposal in English of up to 15 minutes. The selection jury comprises Rough Edges, MMF and an independent juror who is not named. The deadline is the end of 15 November 2026; no time of day or time zone is published. The foundation's archive shows one earlier round, on the theme The Other Side, which drew 160 applications and commissioned three films at Rs. 3 lakhs each, mentored by the Rough Edges team.
