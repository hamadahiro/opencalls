Small Works, Big Impact

The 203 Art Space, a contemporary gallery and creative hub that opened in March 2026 on the 5th floor of the Stamford Town Center, invites submissions for its next monthly exhibition, "Small Works, Big Impact." The brief is bold pieces no larger than 24 x 24 inches that leave a lasting impression despite their scale — work where detail, colour, vibrancy and concept carry the memory. Photography is a named accepted medium alongside mixed media, digital, sculpture and painting. Artists may submit one to three pieces. All work must be wired and ready to hang; if stretcher bars are less than an inch and a half deep, the piece must be framed. Selections are announced 21 August and accepted work is dropped off in person at the gallery on 22 and 23 August. The show runs 26 August to 26 September 2026, with an opening reception on Saturday 29 August, 3–5pm. The gallery retains a 30% commission on work sold during the exhibition; the artist receives 70%. Entry is $40 for the first piece and $10 for each additional piece. Note that drop-off and pick-up are in person in Stamford, and no cash award is offered — the exhibition itself is the opportunity.
