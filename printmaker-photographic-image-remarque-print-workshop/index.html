The Printmaker and the Photographic Image: Dissolving Media Boundaries

International juried exhibition for printmakers and photographers working in both traditions. Seeks hand-printed imagery that weds photographic elements with printmaking techniques — cyanotype, photogravure, photopolymer, photocollage, and other alternative processes especially welcome. Final image must be a hand-pulled print; no solely digital output. Successful images should display technical sophistication while conveying emotional valence. Exhibition June 19 – August 1, 2026 at Remarque Print Workshop & Gallery in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Maximum paper size 16×20". Accepted works must arrive by June 4, 2026. 50/50 commission on sales.
