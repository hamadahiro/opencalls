Art + Science Competition 2026: States of Change

Prizes $500 $300 $200

Lenscratch's annual Art + Science Award, now in its 2026 edition under the theme States of Change. The brief asks for photography that examines transition, transformation and instability within lived experience — change that may be gradual or abrupt, visible or difficult to perceive, ranging from emotional shifts to environmental conditions — and invites artists to consider how photography can reveal states where boundaries are not fixed and conditions keep shifting. Lenscratch's own submissions page frames the wider competition as looking for new work made within the last five years that shows an intersection of art and science, with topics as broad as climate change, environmental challenges or artificial intelligence. Awards are $500 for first place, $300 for second and $200 for third, plus two honourable mentions. The jurors are Linda Alterwitz, the Las Vegas interdisciplinary artist whose work integrates scientific inquiry with photographic processes and whose project Injection Site showed at FORMAT in Derby, and Ben Hickey, Executive Director of the Center for Exploratory and Perceptual Arts in Buffalo and previously curator of exhibitions at the Hilliard Art Museum. Entry is via a Google Form; Lenscratch states no entry fee on either of its two pages for this award, and every Lenscratch call in this directory is free. Winners are published on Lenscratch, which has run a daily photographer feature since 2007 and carries an archive of past Art and Science winners. The deadline is October 9, 2026.
