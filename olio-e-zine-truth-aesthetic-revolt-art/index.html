olio e-zine — Truth

olio is a new e-zine and this is its first issue, themed “truth”. It is the project of Marie Magnetic, a visual artist who works across collage, drawing, painting, prints and mixed media and sculpture, and who publishes calls under the name Aesthetic Revolt Art. Her own site carries her CV, exhibition history and previous publications; it does not carry a page for this call.

Entry is free. One piece per artist, JPG or PNG, up to 50MB. The medium brief is an affirmative that includes photography — the call is open to “all mediums that can be digitally published” rather than to a named list.

Submission runs only through a Google Form, which is gated behind a Google sign-in, so a Google account is needed to apply. No fee, payment, prize or jury is named, and no rights or usage terms are published either; what a selected contributor gets is publication in the issue. Because this is the first issue there is nothing published yet to judge the result against — worth weighing, though the free entry means the only cost is the time.
