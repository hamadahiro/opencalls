Untitled 2026 — A Photographic Exhibition

Prizes $500 cash Camera gear Adobe Creative Cloud subscription Exhibition

Untitled is the annual juried competition and exhibition of APA San Diego, the Southwest regional chapter of American Photographic Artists, and the chapter describes it as open to anyone, from students to professional photographers, with no categories. There are no restrictions on genre or subject matter - portraiture, landscape, street, fine art, documentary, abstract and everything in between - and work may be titled or untitled. The 2026 jurors are Karen E. Haas, Lane Senior Curator of Photographs at the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston, where she looks after a collection of more than 6,000 prints spanning the history of western photography; Leah Ollman, who has written about art for more than 35 years for the Los Angeles Times, Art in America, Photograph, The Brooklyn Rail and ARTnews; and Amanda Smith, founder and co-director of A Smith Gallery in Johnson City, Texas. The jurors select the top 30 images for an in-person exhibition, and the top three are awarded prizes; the organiser states those prizes go to photographers living in the U.S. The 2026 prize list gives Best in Show $500 cash, a ThinkTank FocusPoint Rolltop 30L, a full one-year Adobe Creative Cloud membership, a Nine Volt Powerbloc Flight and a George's Camera gift card; second place takes a ThinkTank FocusPoint Crossbody 13L with the Powerbloc and gift card, third place a ThinkTank Turnstyle 11L with the Powerbloc, gift card, a photography book library and a signed copy of Jason Lee's Galveston, and there is a People's Choice Award as well. A single image entry is one image and you may upload as many as you like, each charged at $10 for APA members or $20 for non-members; membership starts at $50 a year. Files must be JPG, no smaller than 1200 and at most 1800 pixels on the longest side, 72 ppi, sRGB, with no visible identifying text or symbols - entries are assigned an arbitrary ID for blind judging. No AI generative works are accepted. Entries close at 11:59 pm PST on October 13, 2026, and the chapter is donating 10% of entry fee proceeds to The WOO, a gallery and arts organisation in Barrio Logan working to expand affordable collaborative creative space for San Diego artists.
