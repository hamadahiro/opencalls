E Pluribus: From Many

Prizes $1,000 / $800 Exhibition

Latino Arts marks forty years of programmes rooted in "Cultura y Corazón: Culture and Heart" with a juried exhibition at its Milwaukee gallery, 5 March to 4 June 2027, made with the Milwaukee Institute of Art & Design and the Richard and Ethel Herzfeld Foundation. E Pluribus means "from many," and the show is about how many voices, histories and traditions can sit together while staying distinct: work is invited through personal, cultural, community-based or cross-cultural perspectives on the mix of cultures, practices and lived experiences that shape Hispanic and Latino identities. The call is open to artists from within the Latino/Hispanic community and to artists who are allies of it through their practice, and the organiser is explicit about how that is read — "The process does not ask whether a work is Hispanic or Latino enough." Applicants must be 18 or over and live in Wisconsin, Iowa, Minnesota, Illinois or Michigan. Up to two original works per artist, no collaborations, completed in 2025 or later, nothing that has already won an award in another juried show, and the work must be two-dimensional, within 6ft high by 8ft wide by 2in deep — which photographs satisfy on the organiser's own criterion. "Digital art is welcome, though strictly NO AI generated images will be allowed." Artists choose one of two tracks for both submissions, Emerging or Established, each defined by practice rather than by age, schooling or sales: the Jurors' Choice recognition is $1,000 in the Established track and $800 in the Emerging one. Required with every entry, beyond the images: an artist statement of 100–250 words and a biography of no more than 150 words. One content rule applies to the work itself — it "must not contain explicit language or sexual imagery." Entry is $40 per artwork, so two works cost $80. Selected artists arrange delivery and transport of the work to the Milwaukee gallery themselves, on set dates in late February 2027. Submissions opened 30 October 2026 and close Sunday 10 January 2027 at 11:59pm CST, with selection notices on 20 January.
