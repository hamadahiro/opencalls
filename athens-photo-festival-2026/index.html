Athens Photo Festival 2026

Prizes Exhibition prize Publication prize Residency prize

Athens Photo Festival is the major international photography festival held annually in Athens, Greece. The festival's main exhibition runs at the Benaki Museum / Pireos 138 — Athens's principal venue for contemporary art programming.

Approximately 80 artists are selected for the festival exhibition across 3,000+ square meters of exhibition space. Production costs are covered by the festival, plus two-night accommodation in Athens for selected artists.

Beyond the main exhibition, selected artists access additional opportunities: portfolio reviews, Talent Lab (the festival's mentorship program), and an artist residency component with a €600 honorarium.

All genres welcome — fine art, conceptual, documentary, installation. Eligibility is open globally.

Submission is through the festival's website with up to 20 images. Fee is €25. The 2026 deadline was March 17; results announced March 30, 2026.
