2027 Galleries in City of Auburn

Prize Exhibition

The City of Auburn, Washington is seeking artists and artist groups working in two-dimensional or small three-dimensional media for eight-week exhibitions in its city-operated gallery spaces during 2027, including the Cheryl Sallee Gallery at the Auburn Senior Activity Center — fitted with an Arakawa hanging system and professional gallery lighting — and the Community & Event Center. Photography is explicitly among the media invited, alongside printmaking, painting, drawing, mixed media, textiles, mosaic, glass, collage, fibre art, ceramics and metal sculpture. Shows run roughly 5–12 works. There is no entry fee and no location restriction, but selected artists must deliver and collect work in person in Auburn.

DeadlineSeptember 2, 2026
Entry feeFree
LocationAuburn, WA
Requirements3–10 images
Submit viaWebsite

Jury: Selection panel of City of Auburn Arts Commissioners, artists and community representatives

Organized by City of Auburn Parks, Arts & Recreation
