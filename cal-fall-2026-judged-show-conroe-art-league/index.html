CAL Fall 2026 Judged Show

Prize Cash awards

The Conroe Art League's autumn judged show is run virtually on CaFE, with winning work by adult CAL members invited into the league's Madeley Fine Art Gallery in downtown Conroe for the month of October — no hanging fee, but one docent shift per artwork. Photography is a category of its own: the league asks that photographic images be the artist's own, made on film or digitally on a lens-based camera and produced by photographic processes, minimally altered with standard post-processing. The AI line is drawn unusually tightly — no stock or AI images in any form, nothing substantial imported from another image, and no AI sharpening or resolution tools such as Adobe super resolution or Topaz Photo AI. Judging is by Diann Zimmerman, an artist and educator who founded the Watercolor — Beginners and Beyond community and was Education Director for the Watercolor Art Society-Houston. Cash awards, scaled to the number of paid entries, go to Best of Show, Best Floral, Best Landscape and Best Student; the amounts are not published in advance, and the Best of Show winner also receives a year's CAL membership. Winners qualify to enter the Lone Star Art Guild's 2027 annual show, though that step requires membership of CAL or another LSAG league. Entry is open to any artist living in Texas, member or not; entries from outside Texas are declined. Work must be original, not painted on by anyone else, and completed in the last two years. Two-dimensional work is capped at 48 inches including the frame and 30 pounds; three-dimensional work at 30 x 30 x 48 inches and 40 pounds. Subject matter must be family friendly and no nudes are accepted. Adults pay $20 per artwork — the league states no maximum, though the CaFE form takes up to 20 images per artist — while artists 18 and under enter up to three works free using the coupon codes published with the call. Awards are announced electronically on Friday 2 October, with gallery take-in for eligible winners on 6 October.
