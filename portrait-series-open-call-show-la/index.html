Portrait Series Open Call

SHOW LA — the Los Angeles photography fair that runs 10–13 December 2026 at The Reef, 1933 S. Broadway — has partnered with LensCulture and Polaroid on an open call for portrait photography. Photographers of all categories and levels may submit a portrait series of up to ten images, which should sit within the same body of work or series; entries opened 6 October and the deadline has been extended to 6 November. The grand prize winner takes a $1,000 cash prize, a feature on SHOW LA's Instagram account and an exhibition at SHOW LA this December; three finalists are exhibited at the fair alongside the winner, and the winner and all three finalists each receive a Polaroid camera with film. The call is open internationally and is judged by three jurors — Paris Chong, gallery director of the Leica Store & Gallery Los Angeles, Ann M. Jastrab, executive director of the Center for Photographic Art, and Matthew Martin, director of the Peter Fetterman Gallery. Entry is $45 and submissions are handled on LensCulture, whose entry route requires a signed-in account.
