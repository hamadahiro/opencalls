Arts to Hearts Magazine Issue 14 — The Summer We Remember

Prize $500

Themed issue of Arts to Hearts Magazine inviting work on 'The Summer We Remember' — memories, moments and metaphors of summer. Open to artists 18+ worldwide across painting, drawing, photography, sculpture, printmaking, digital art, collage, textile, mixed media, installation and illustration. The winner receives a USD 500 cash prize, a 4-page spread and a glass trophy; the runners-up receive 4-page spreads and trophies, and all selected artists receive a 2-page spread in the internationally distributed magazine.
