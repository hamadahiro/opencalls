Color: Emotion. Story. Metaphor.

Prize Exhibition

The January show at Black Eyes Gallery, a submission-based photography and fine-art gallery at 485 S. Stone Avenue in Tucson, is about colour doing the work: "The tone of an entire photograph can be changed simply by the color of the lighting: the blue-green of fluorescent, or the warm glow of incandescent. Nothing in the frame moves. Only the color does." Any genre — landscape, street, portrait, experimental, conceptual — and any colour process, film, digital or alternative. Images with selective colour are eligible; black-and-white and monochrome work is not, and belongs in the gallery's separate monochrome call. Twenty-five images are selected and the gallery prints, mats and frames every one at its own cost, so there is nothing to produce or ship. One exhibition print is made; the gallery holds it in its archive for two years and then destroys it. Selected work hangs 2–30 January 2027 with an opening reception on 2 January, 5–8pm, enters the gallery's permanent online exhibition archive with the artist's name, title and social link, and is promoted across the gallery's channels including a feature on its Instagram. The juror is Douglas Fur, a Tucson photographer with a BFA from Cornish College of the Arts who has worked at Decode Gallery and is gallery manager and artist liaison for Tucson Erotica. Review is blind wherever possible — the gallery says a CV and bio will most likely not factor into selection. Open to photographers worldwide aged 18 and over, except where US sanctions or payment-processing rules stop the gallery accepting a submission. Entry is $40 for up to five images or $50 for up to ten, paid on the JotForm entry form; a reduced $20 fee is available to two artists per call, first come, if you contact the gallery before submitting, and an entry with nothing selected earns $5 off the next. Listing work for sale is optional and encouraged, with the gallery suggesting $200–$500 and retaining 50 per cent on gallery sales — the entry form asks you to acknowledge that split. Work made entirely or substantially with AI is disqualified. Files are JPEGs, at least 1200px on the long side, named lastname_firstname_title. Artists keep full copyright; the gallery takes a non-exclusive right to reproduce selected images for jurying, the exhibition, its archive and promotion.
