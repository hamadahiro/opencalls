Reel Pride 2026 Art Show

Prize Exhibition

The Reel Pride Art Show is the gallery arm of Winnipeg's Reel Pride International Film Festival — a space for 2SLGBTQI+ artists to show work alongside the festival at The Gas Station Arts Centre. The organisers welcome work in all forms and mediums and are open about what they are after: personal expression, a statement, or a celebration, and the many ways queer and Two-Spirit people experience and build community. There is no entry fee. If work sells the artist keeps the full sale price — the festival takes no commission — and artists retain full copyright, with images used only to promote Reel Pride and its events. Submissions run entirely through the call site, and accepted work is dropped off in Winnipeg during the week of 7–11 September. The show runs 15 September to 2 November 2026 alongside the festival.
