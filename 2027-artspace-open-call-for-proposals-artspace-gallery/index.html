2027 Artspace Open Call for Proposals

Artspace, a 501(c)(3) artist-run nonprofit gallery in Richmond, Virginia, opens its annual call for exhibition proposals for the 2027 season. The Exhibition Committee reviews proposals for shows in all genres, and proposals may be for individual, group or joint exhibitions, or for curating the work of others. Four slots are available in 2027: 26 March–17 April, 23 April–22 May, 27 August–18 September and 24 September–16 October. Selection criteria are published in full and cover artistic merit and track record, how the exhibition contributes to Artspace's mission of building stronger communities through contemporary arts, how it fosters opportunities to gather with and learn from artists, how it coordinates with other shows in the same period, and how it broadens awareness of contemporary art in and around Greater Richmond. Applicants submit an art resume or CV, a written proposal, preferred dates, any planned programming or workshops, and five to ten image, video or sound files. The proposal fee is $35 and goes to operations and programming, but the committee will waive it — email them before submitting if the fee is a hardship. Work should have been made within five years of the exhibition date unless the proposal is a retrospective, and everything must fit through a door 33 inches wide by 82¾ inches high. Full-time students are not eligible to apply for individual or joint shows. Selected exhibitors are notified by 13 November 2026 and the full slate is announced by the end of December.
