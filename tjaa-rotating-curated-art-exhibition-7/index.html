TJAA Rotating Curated Art Exhibition #7

The Texas Jewish Arts Association hangs the seventh in its rotating series of curated exhibitions at the Dallas Jewish Community Foundation on Merit Drive. There is no theme and no entry fee, and the call is open to all genres, all mediums, all sizes and all styles for both two- and three-dimensional work, so photography is eligible on the organisers' own terms. Members may submit up to five pieces; the curators choose on the basis of what fits and hangs well together in the available space rather than on a competitive jury, and not being selected is explicitly not a judgement on the work. The Foundation offers a $180 Visitor Choice Award. Entry is a membership benefit, so a current TJAA membership is required - membership is open to all, costs $18 a year for students up to 26 and $36 for adults, and TJAA offers a no-questions-asked complimentary membership to anyone who cannot afford the dues; new members joining before the deadline are welcome. All work must arrive gallery-ready with D-rings and wire, and accepted pieces must be delivered in person in Dallas on Thursday 17 September, with the shipping address listed only as 'to be determined'. The exhibition opens on Monday 21 September and closes 22 October 2026, with collection on 25 October and a $10-a-day storage charge after that. Sales are handled directly between artist and buyer, with TJAA requesting a 20% donation that funds cash awards at its future shows.
