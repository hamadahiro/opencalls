Project Nexus Supported Residencies 2027 — Visual Arts and the Environment

Prizes ₦3,175,000 stipend Accommodation & studio at the G.A.S. Farm House

The first of three residencies in the 2027 round of Project Nexus, run by the Guest Artists Space (G.A.S.) Foundation and the Yinka Shonibare Foundation with the support of Deutsche Bank — an eight-week, fully supported residency at the G.A.S. Farm House in Ikiṣẹ, Ogun State, 11 January – 26 February 2027. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside ceramics, sculpture, painting, drawing, film and moving image, installation, textiles and fibre, printmaking and interdisciplinary or material-led practice, so a photographer working in the rural landscape can apply here as well as to the Lagos photography residency on the same deadline. The residency is addressed to artists whose work connects with the environment and material conditions of the farm: ecology, agriculture and food systems, landscape and biodiversity, natural or locally available materials, craft and making traditions, sustainability and material cycles, the relationships between land, labour and culture, and the ways environmental knowledge is carried through communities and generations. Proposals are read against two of the Foundation's strands of inquiry — Ecology, Farming and Food, and Materiality, Craft and Identity. The award is ₦3,175,000 towards food, living expenses and materials, plus internal travel and airport transfers with the G.A.S. driver, a private bedroom with a shared bathroom, a weekly cleaning service, seasonal fresh produce, on-site team support Monday to Friday, event production support, and books taken out from the G.A.S. Library and Picton Archive in Lagos; trips outside Lagos and Ijebu are at the resident's own cost. Applicants must be Nigerian nationals currently residing in Nigeria, aged 23 or over at any career stage with an existing body of work, available for the fixed dates, proficient in spoken and written English (Yoruba is not required and translation support is offered), and able to name a referee. Nothing finished is expected — there is no requirement to produce an exhibition or completed artwork, only to share an aspect of the practice in a format agreed with the team and to leave a short reflection in the G.A.S. archive. Applications close at midnight WAT on 3 November 2026 and no fee is charged; a selection panel is announced in November 2026 and final selections on 7 December 2026.
