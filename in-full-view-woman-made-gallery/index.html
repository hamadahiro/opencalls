In Full View — Woman Made Gallery

Prize Group exhibition

In Full View is the autumn exhibition at Woman Made Gallery, the Chicago feminist arts space founded in 1992 to champion women and nonbinary artists. The 2026 edition narrows eligibility deliberately: only artists aged 65 and over may apply. The brief is built around visibility — how older artists are seen, remembered, and represented — and around the texture of a fully lived life.

The exhibition is juried by Jerri Zbiral. Work is shown in person at the gallery's space at 1332 S. Halsted Street in Chicago from October 10 through November 7, 2026. Selected artists ship or hand-deliver finished work ready to install rather than producing in-house.

All media are welcome, photography included. Submissions are made through EntryThingy with one to three artworks per applicant. The entry fee is $24.60. WMG's standard terms apply to packaging, insurance, and return shipping.

The deadline is August 12, 2026. WMG has presented more than 475 exhibitions across three decades; In Full View sits squarely within their mission of putting underrepresented artists into a space where their work is genuinely seen.
