46th Annual Western Spirit Juried Art Show & Sale

Prizes $1,000 Best of Show $500 Best of Each Media

The Cheyenne Frontier Days Old West Museum runs one of the Rocky Mountain region's larger juried shows, and photography sits in it as a full medium with its own Best of Media award rather than as an afterthought: the 2026 edition hung more than 240 works by over 100 artists across acrylic, drawing, mixed media, oil, pastel, photography, printmaking, sculpture and watercolour, with a sponsored Best Photography award among the prizes. The show wants original work interpreting the American West, and the prospectus draws a firm line around the photography category - images should represent reality with only minor adjustments, meaning exposure, colour correction, cropping and light retouching, and anything edited further has to be entered as mixed media. For the 46th edition an independent jury of professionals from the Western art community selects the show, which runs 6 March to 18 April 2027. Awards are $1,000 Best of Show, $500 Best of Each Media, $250 Best Student Artist and $100 Best Miniature, and there is a separate student category. Entry is $20 per work for non-members and $10 for members, up to four works; the member rate needs a discount code from the museum before you submit. Entries opened on 1 September 2026 and close on 14 November, with acceptances announced 11 December - the prospectus contradicts itself on that date, its calendar giving 14 November and a line on page two saying 2 November, so enter early. Work must be original, made in the last three years and not previously shown for sale at the museum, with 2-D limited to 47 by 60 inches including frame. Everything accepted must be for sale for the run of the show and the museum takes 35% commission, which also applies to work sold within 30 days of closing. AI-generated artwork and installation art are not eligible.
