Outdoor Photography Magazine — Environmental Wildlife

Submit up to 5 images on the theme of environmental wildlife — animals shown in the context of the landscape they live in — for the 'One Thing This Month' reader competition (OP 333) in Outdoor Photography Magazine (UK). The winner receives a Cumulus Moonrain Jacket (RRP £325). Entries need captions of up to 75 words covering where, why and how each image was taken, plus full technical details in the magazine's stated order (camera and lens, focal length, ISO, shutter speed, aperture, tripod or handheld). Free to enter, uploaded in bulk through the magazine's Google Form. The magazine publishes work taken outside in the landscape, wildlife, nature, adventure and travel genres, and asks that images be made ethically and with sensitivity to environmental and conservation issues. Entry is restricted to readers aged 18 or over, with one entry per person per competition, and the publisher's competition terms state that winners are notified by email within 30 days of the closing date and have 14 days to claim the prize.
