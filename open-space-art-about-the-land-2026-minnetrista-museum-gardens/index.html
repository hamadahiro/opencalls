Open Space: Art About the Land 2026

Prizes $1,500 in cash awards ($100 photography merit award) Museum exhibition at two venues

Since 2001 Open Space: Art About the Land has asked Indiana artists to draw creative inspiration from the land and celebrate the idea of open space. It is an annual juried competition and exhibition developed by Minnetrista Museum & Gardens in Muncie and the Anderson Museum of Art in partnership with Red-tail Land Conservancy, and the 2026 show hangs at both museums from November 7, 2026 to January 3, 2027, with a portion of proceeds going to the conservancy's habitat work. Any artist 18 or older who is a present or former resident of Indiana may enter up to three works made in the last three years for one $35 fee; eligible work is listed as ceramics, digital art, drawings, fiber, mixed media, paintings, prints, sculptures, metalwork and photography (digital work must be printed; video and audio are not eligible), it must address 'the spirit of open space with reference to the land', may not have been made as part of a class, and may not exceed 50 pounds; the two surfaces disagree on size - the org's prospectus PDF caps entries at 60 inches in any direction while the ShowSubmit entry page says 48 inches. The ShowSubmit entry page also states that no use of artificial intelligence, in the conception or development of the artwork, is permitted (the PDF carries no AI clause). The juror is India Cruse-Griffin, a Muncie-trained mixed-media collage artist and Harrison Center studio artist who won the Indy Arts Council's DeHaan Artist of Distinction in 2025. Awards total $1,500 at her discretion: $500, $350 and $250 for first to third place, $100 merit awards for photography, three-dimensional work and the Jim Faulkner landscape award, and the $100 Stefan and Joan Anderson Prize for the best piece representing a Red-tail nature preserve. Results are emailed October 2; accepted work is dropped off by appointment on October 16 or 17, the awards reception is November 6 at Minnetrista, and both venues take 30% on sales. Entries close at 11:59pm on Monday September 28, 2026 through ShowSubmit.
