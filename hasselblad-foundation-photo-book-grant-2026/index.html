Hasselblad Foundation Photo Book Grant 2026

Prize SEK 100,000 prize

The Hasselblad Foundation Photo Book Grant supports the development and publication of photographic books. Two grants of SEK 100,000 each (approximately $9,500 USD) are awarded each cycle.

The grant is staged: projects should be largely edited but not yet published. The funding is structured to push books over the finishing line — editing is mostly done, what's needed is production funding.

AI-generated mockups in book dummies must be disclosed.

The 2026 jury is Jacob Birch (Spine Studio), Maryam Fanni (HDK-Valand, the Gothenburg art school), and the Hasselblad Foundation's own Andréas Hagström and Dragana Vujanovic Östlind.

Eligibility is restricted to professional photographers. Submission is through Picter with a digital book dummy. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was April 1; results announced June 2026.
