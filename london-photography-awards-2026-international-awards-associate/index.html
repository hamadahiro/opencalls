London Photography Awards 2026

Prize $3,000

London Photography Awards is an international photography competition run by International Awards Associate, the same group that operates several other geographically-titled photography awards. The 2026 edition runs across 13 categories: Architecture, Fine Art, Nature, Minimalist, Fashion, Black & White, and others.

The structure is split: separate professional and amateur/student divisions, with the same categories on each side. Top prize is $3,000.

The 2026 jury is large — over 20 photographers, art directors, and educators across multiple countries, ranging from independent practitioners to ambassadors and creative agency directors. Submissions are reviewed across the full jury rather than category-by-category.

Eligibility is open globally to photographers 18+. Submission is through the awards website with 1–8 images per entry. Fee is tiered $25–$50. The 2026 deadline was April 30; results announced May 15, 2026.
