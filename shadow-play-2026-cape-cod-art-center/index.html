SHADOW PLAY 2026

Prize Exhibition prize

Cape Cod Art Center is a community arts organization on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, with a regular schedule of themed online juried exhibitions throughout the year. "SHADOW PLAY 2026" is the gallery's themed call exploring shadow and light — work that engages with the interplay between presence and absence, illumination and obscurity. Any interpretation of the theme is welcome.

Cash prizes are awarded for first place, second place, and honorable mentions. Selected work appears in the online juried exhibition with promotion through the gallery's communications channels.

Entry fee is $15 per submission of up to 2 images. Submissions go through Smarter Entry. AI-edited work is permitted; fully AI-generated images are not accepted. The 2026 deadline is May 17, with results announced May 29.
