Non-Verbal Co-Learning Engagement — Spring 2027

Prize $2,000 CAD

A six-week paid engagement at grunt gallery in Vancouver for artists whose practice runs on gesture, text, signed languages or silence rather than speech. Two artists are selected and each receives $2,000 CAD, spendable entirely at their own discretion with no reporting to grunt; no completed artwork is required and no materials or artwork have to be handed over. The commitment is up to eight hours of co-learning: six weekly silent check-ins online, one interpreted or hybrid staff meeting, one silent accessibility committee meeting, a two-hour closed roundtable with other non-verbal artists, and a visit to a grunt exhibition, with travel and access supports paid by the gallery. One deliverable is required — taking part in an interview, video or other response that is archived and shared publicly, due March 5, 2027 — and artists also meet grunt's program director, curator and/or exhibition manager for feedback on presenting their work to galleries. Two hard gates: applicants must live in the Greater Vancouver Regional District, and must have a silent or non-verbal art practice. The gallery is blunt that working in a quiet visual medium is not itself such a practice — the program is about navigating professional arts spaces without relying on speech, whether by choice, necessity or circumstance — and that it expects a long-standing practice (participants have typically had four years of focused work; hobbyists are told it is not a match). Priority goes to artists who self-identify as Deaf, deaf, Hard of Hearing, Non-Verbal or Neurodiverse, to Trans and Queer artists adapting to silence through voice-changing transitions, and to Indigenous artists from the Host Nations (Musqueam, Squamish, Tsleil-Waututh). This is neither an exhibition nor a production residency and no maker or studio space is provided. No medium is named anywhere in the call, and grunt's own programme includes a lens-based photography residency, so photographers are not excluded — but the subject here is how you communicate, not what you shoot. Free to apply, by Google Form (a Google sign-in is required) or by emailing a plain-text or Microsoft Office application to aep@grunt.ca. Spring 2027 engagement runs February 2 – March 12, 2027, with a free ASL-interpreted info session on January 6, two days before the deadline.
