Cosmic Pop

Prize Exhibition

Juried spring exhibition at the Alpharetta Arts Center, presented by Arts Alpharetta in partnership with the City of Alpharetta Cultural Services. Cosmic Pop asks for work celebrating bold colour, expressive linework and the imaginative spirit of Peter Max, whose own retrospective is shown alongside the selected exhibition — vibrant palettes, cosmic themes, pop-psychedelic energy, or a modern reading of that style in the artist's own terms. The organisers are explicit that this is not an imitation show: work must be original in concept, composition and execution and must not copy Max. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside drawing, painting, mixed media, printmaking, sculpture, kinetic art, ceramics, fiber and folk art. Artists 18 and over may submit up to three works at $15 per entry, one artwork per entry; reproductions of original works and pieces previously shown in the Arts Center's main gallery are not eligible. Accepted work must arrive gallery-ready, wired and ready to hang. The show runs March 10 to May 1, 2027, with a reception on Friday, March 12, 5-7pm.
