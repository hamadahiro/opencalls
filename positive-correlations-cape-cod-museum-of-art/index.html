Positive Correlations

Prizes $500 / $200 / $100 Exhibition

An international juried exhibition for the Hope / McClennen Gallery at the Cape Cod Museum of Art in Dennis, Massachusetts, a museum founded by artists in 1981 and open year-round. The theme is deliberately loose: artists may read Positive Correlations as connections, relationships, patterns, coincidences, evidence, or cause and effect, and the museum says outright that alternative, unexpected and interdisciplinary interpretations are encouraged. The call is open to artists of all ages from the United States and anywhere in the world, and the media list names photography directly — drawing, painting, printmaking, photography, collage, assemblage, sculpture, textiles, multimedia, digital art and video. Photographers get an unusually clear ruling on prints: limited-edition prints and photographic editions are permitted, and digital photography and limited-edition digital printing are acceptable when used to make original photographic work; only reproduction prints of original artworks are refused. AI-generated artwork is not accepted. Each artist may enter up to five works at $25 per work. Two-dimensional pieces must arrive ready to hang, framed or on a gallery-wrapped canvas at least 1.5 inches deep, and Plexiglas is strongly preferred over glass for anything shipped. Work shown previously at the museum is not eligible. Selling is optional rather than a condition of entry; where work does sell, the artist keeps 60%. The juror awards three cash prizes of $500, $200 and $100 — the juror has not yet been named, which is worth weighing before you pay per image. Entries close at midnight Eastern on Monday 8 February 2027, notifications go out by the end of 19 February, delivery and shipped work are due 12 March, and the exhibition runs 19 March to 6 June 2027, with a gallery talk and reception on 26 March.
