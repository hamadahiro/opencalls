Works on Paper 2027

Prizes Exhibition Sales opportunity

Green & Stone has been selling paint, pigment and paper to artists on the Fulham Road in Chelsea since 1927 and opened its own gallery there in 2018; in 2027 the business turns 100, and the sixth edition of its annual Works on Paper exhibition opens that centenary year. Photography is named in the accepted media on every surface — drawing, painting, watercolour, printmaking, collage, photography, illustration, mixed media, sculpture, book arts and experimental approaches are all welcomed, provided the work is made on or of paper — but the binding rules narrow that more than the call page suggests, and a photographer should read them before paying: "only original prints are accepted", defined as hand-made work using traditional printmaking techniques in a limited, numbered edition; "reproduction prints (e.g. Giclée prints of original artworks) are not accepted"; and the FAQ adds that "digital-only artworks are not eligible". Hand-coated and alternative-process work — photogravure, cyanotype, salt, platinum, photopolymer — along with photo-collage, zines and book objects, sits plainly inside that; anyone planning to send a standard inkjet or c-type print should ask the gallery first, because the rules never say which side of the line a photographic print falls on. Work must also have been made within the last three years, measure no more than 100 cm in any direction including the frame, not have been exhibited in a London gallery in the past three years (the call page states that restriction more strictly still, as never), remain available until 7 February 2027, and be the work of one artist — collaborative entries are excluded. The selection is the real draw: every artwork is considered anonymously by an independent panel, and the gallery states plainly that an artist's name, qualifications and exhibition history do not determine selection — "You don't need an impressive CV. You need work you believe in." It is open to anyone aged 18 or over anywhere in the world, amateur or professional, self-taught or formally trained, including students, recent graduates and artists returning to practice after a break; a third party such as a gallery or agent may enter on an artist's behalf with the artist's full authorisation. Selected work hangs at 251–253 Fulham Road from 11 January to 3 February 2027, with a private view on 12 January. Every selected work must be for sale — nothing may be marked not for sale — and the price the artist sets must cover the gallery's 40% commission, any VAT and the framing. Framing is specified rather than suggested: simple black, white or natural-wood frames, white or off-white mounts, low-reflective conservation glazing recommended, ornate, brightly coloured and metallic frames accepted only by prior agreement, and the gallery reserves the right to request reframing or decline to exhibit work that falls short; work not yet framed at the deadline can be submitted with an image of another piece framed in the same style. Up to three artworks may be entered, at £20 for the first, £15 for the second and £10 for the third, dropping to £15 / £12 / £8 for artists aged 30 or under on the closing date. Entries close at 12 noon GMT on Wednesday 25 November 2026 and results are notified on 2 December; selected work is delivered unwrapped to the gallery on 4–5 January 2027 and unsold work collected 4–6 February, both journeys at the artist's own cost, and the details entered on the form are used verbatim for the exhibition catalogue and labels.
