Woven Together

A juried group show at Blue Door Art Center in Yonkers, curated by Co-Executive Director Luis Perelman and Gallery Director Michele Amaro, built around weaving taken both literally and as metaphor — fibre, paper, metal, wire and rope alongside photography, painting, sculpture and mixed media. The curators describe the theme as connection, layering, community, memory and "the transformation of individual strands into something larger", which leaves room for photographic work about textile and structure as much as for work whose own making is layered or stitched. Entering costs nothing: submission is free and the gallery pays attention to process, noting it may ask for more detail about materials or method during jurying. Money only changes hands if the work is selected — an in-gallery installation fee of $25 per piece for members and $35 for non-members, or $10 and $15 per piece respectively for the online exhibition, so a photographer can place a print in the physical show for $35. The gallery's terms add two costs they do not price, though: the artist pays shipping or delivery, and insures the work against loss or damage for the time it is in the building. Nothing may exceed 24 by 36 inches or 100 pounds, and the work must be the artist's own original. The show hangs 21 November to 19 December 2026, opening with a reception on 21 November from 1 to 6pm. Sales carry a 25% gallery commission for members and 35% for non-members. The same terms take permission, with attribution and no payment, to reproduce selected work in Blue Door's publicity and exhibition books; copyright stays with the artist. Blue Door is a small 501(c)(3) art centre on Riverdale Avenue, exhibiting since 2002 and an ArtsWestchester grantee; its ArtCall listing tags eligibility as local, while the gallery's own call page states no geographic restriction at all.
