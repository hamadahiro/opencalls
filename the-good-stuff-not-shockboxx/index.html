The Good Stuff

Marking Suicide Prevention and Awareness Month, Not Shockboxx asks artists to notice what makes life worth staying for — not milestones or picture-perfect moments but the smaller, deeply personal things: the first quiet hour of the morning, a perfectly made cup of coffee, the particular way sunlight enters a room, a dog asleep at your feet, an inside joke, a song played a thousand times. The gallery is clear that a submitted piece does not need to address suicide or mental health directly; this call is about the other side of that conversation. Work can be joyful, tender, funny, strange, nostalgic, imperfect or almost impossible to explain, and the organisers suggest you may already have made it without realising where it belonged. All media and approaches are welcome, and the exhibition runs online through Artsy from 21 September to 20 November 2026 so audiences beyond the Hermosa Beach gallery can see it. Entry is $20; gallery artists and Backboxx Artist Society members apply free. Not Shockboxx welcomes work that is provocative, political, sexual, unsettling, profane or experimental, and it does not accept work made wholly or partly with generative AI. Accepted work must be priced for sale, since Artsy will not list NFS pieces.
