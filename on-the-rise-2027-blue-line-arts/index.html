On the Rise 2027

Prize Exhibition

Blue Line Arts, a nonprofit gallery in Roseville, California celebrating sixty years in 2026, brings back On the Rise, its emerging-artist exhibition for artists aged 14 to 24 at the time of applying. The gallery pitches it as a first gallery show and a first-hand education in how a juried exhibition works from beginning to end, and photography is named in the accepted media alongside painting, sculpture and digital art. The entry fee is $25 and covers one to five images, but Blue Line Arts members at the College Student and Artist levels, and high-school students in Placer, Sacramento, El Dorado, Yolo, Yuba, Sutter and Nevada counties, submit for free; 50% discount codes are offered to high-school students outside those areas and to community-college and university students regardless of region. Codes are issued by hand during gallery hours, Tuesday to Saturday, so request one well ahead of the deadline. Note that the gallery calls this a regional exhibition and its eligibility text says artists must reside in qualified regions, while the fee structure explicitly provides for applicants outside those counties and the entry portal lists the call as national - worth an email to the gallery before assuming you are out of range. Work must be original, completed within the last two years, not previously exhibited at Blue Line Arts, and offered for sale; 2-D work must arrive ready to hang. The gallery handles all sales, with non-members receiving a 50% commission and members 60%. Artists under 18 apply through a parent or guardian, who signs the consignment agreement and receives any sale proceeds. Notification is 9 April 2027; the exhibition runs 8 May to 19 June 2027, with an opening reception on 15 May and a closing reception on 19 June.
