Annual Members Art Exhibition 2026

The Philadelphia Sketch Club — founded in 1860 and the oldest artists' club in the United States — runs its annual members' exhibition at its Camac Street galleries, 8 November to 20 December 2026, with a reception on Sunday 6 December. Juror Ryan O'Farrell selects the show. Any 2D or 3D medium of any subject is permitted, so photography, alternative process and photo-based work all qualify; the club runs a separate annual photography exhibition as well. Work must be original and not previously shown at the PSC, framed and wired for hanging (no hooks, brackets, sawtooth hangers or holes), no larger than 44 inches in any dimension including the frame and no heavier than 30 lbs; the gallery cannot take large free-standing sculpture. Artists may submit up to ten works, with a maximum of two accepted per artist. Works created using generative AI are not eligible in any PSC exhibition. Prizes are $300, $200 and $100 with Contemporary Sketch Club Medals and certificates, plus at least three honourable mentions, presented at the reception at 2pm. All pieces must be for sale and the club takes 25% commission; accepted work is hand-delivered or shipped to Philadelphia on 25, 28 or 30 October and picked up 20, 21 or 23 December. THE MEMBERSHIP GATE: the show is open only to PSC members in good standing, and the club states that artists who join and are accepted before the deadline may enter — but membership is $175 a year ($90 for under-35s) and applications are approved at the board meeting held on the third Tuesday of each month, which for this deadline falls on 20 October, after entries close. Practically, this round is reachable by existing members.
