The Last Supper (The Last Casa Bonita Art Show)

Prizes Cash prizes Exhibition

The tenth and final edition of NEXT Gallery's Casa Bonita Art Show, and the brief is gloriously specific: "ALL SUBMISSIONS MUST DIRECTLY RELATE TO THE CASA BONITA RESTAURANT IN LAKEWOOD, COLORADO" — the place with an 85-foot pink stucco tower, a 30-foot indoor waterfall, cliff divers, mariachi, puppet shows and a haunted cave. This year's theme is The Last Supper, which the gallery is careful to frame as "a jumping off point, not a religious assignment. Think: meals, indulgences, gatherings, futures, pasts, endings, chances… The interpretation is up to you." A national juried call, open to all, one work per entry; alongside visual art the gallery is taking literary submissions on the same theme (flash fiction to 400 words, poetry to one page, previously published work accepted with permission). Cash prizes and Casa Bonita swag will be awarded, though no figures are published. Jurors are Casa Bonita Art Show founder Dolla B, Casa Bonita aficionado Andrew Novick and fashion designer Mona Lucero. The show runs 12 to 28 February 2027 at NEXT Gallery in the 40 West Arts Hub in Lakewood, with accepted work dropped off on 7 or 8 February and mailed work received by 8 February; out-of-state artists make specific arrangements with the gallery. Sold work carries a 33% commission and prices cannot be changed after acceptance. AI is barred twice over: "No AI Art will be accepted" in the eligibility criteria and "Absolutely NO AI/ChatGPT work will be considered" for the literary half. Entry is $35, or $15 for entrants under 13, and closes at midnight MST on 18 January 2027.
