ELEVEN: Be There

Prize $333 / $222 / $111 / $111

ELEVEN is Viewpoint Photographic Art Center's end-of-year juried exhibition, and one of four group open calls the Sacramento non-profit runs annually — the November/December themed slot, which the centre states is open to all and does not require membership. The 2026 theme is Be There, taken from the reply Weegee is said to have given when asked the secret of his success: "F8 and be there." Viewpoint is after photographs that came out of preparation, seeking, opportunity and actually being present, whether that is a child's first step, light thrown by a skyscraper's windows, or the quietude implied by new snow. Juror Jeff Redman founded and ran Lightwork, a photographers' supply shop and gallery in Sacramento, from 1980 to 1997, curating exhibits there for Linda McCartney, Ruth Bernhard, Roy DeCarava and Morley Baer among dozens of others; he went on to found Viewpoint itself in 1991 with the support of other charter members, and learned carbon printing during Covid. Awards are $333 for first place, $222 for second, $111 for third and $111 for Curator's Choice, plus two honourable mentions of a Viewpoint workshop valued at $95. Up to six images may be entered, and a multiple-image set such as a diptych or triptych counts as one image and is submitted as a single file. Judging is anonymous, so files must not be signed, watermarked or named after the entrant. Each entry also has to declare the print's photographic medium (inkjet, gelatin silver, alt process, metal or other), image size, mat size and price. Accepted prints are delivered between September 30 and October 24; overall size including mat may not exceed 18 by 24 inches, or 20 by 20 for square images. The exhibit runs November 11 to December 19 2026 at the gallery on J Street, with a second-Sunday reception on November 15, and the online exhibit continues to January 20 2027. Prints offered for sale need a minimum price of $80, and Viewpoint splits sales 50/50 with the artist.
