Arts to Hearts Magazine Issue 15 — WHERE I AM

The 15th edition of Arts to Hearts Magazine, published in collaboration with StudioToGallery, will feature 100 selected artists from around the world under the theme WHERE I AM — the places, experiences, memories, environments, relationships and cultures that shape an artist's perspective, including where an artist stands within their own practice. Work already made is welcome; no new work is required. The magazine takes painting, drawing and illustration, photography, sculpture, ceramics, textile and fibre art, printmaking, mixed media, collage, digital art, installation and new media, and is distributed internationally through Amazon, Barnes & Noble and other channels. Each of the 100 selected artists receives a dedicated two-page feature carrying their selected artwork, a professionally prepared interview, artist information or biography and links to their website and social platforms, plus an official certificate. From the 100, a Top 10 receive official recognition and an exclusive mentorship session with Arts to Hearts founder Charuka Arora and StudioToGallery founder Sonia Borrell; from those, a Top 3 are named Cash Award Winner, 1st Runner-Up and 2nd Runner-Up. The Cash Award Winner receives $500 plus a four-page feature, one-year StudioToGallery membership, physical trophy and certificate; both runners-up receive a two-page feature, membership, trophy and certificate. Open to artists of all genders, nationalities, backgrounds, career stages and disciplines, emerging and established. The entry fee is tiered by the number of works and is non-refundable: the organiser publishes only the entry point, "Submission starts at $21 for one artwork", with options to submit 3, 5, 10 or 20 artworks whose prices it does not state. The organiser states there is no additional mandatory publication package if selected. AI-generated artwork is not eligible. Applications go through the Arts to Hearts portal; payment and final submission are separate steps. Results are expected about three weeks after the deadline.
