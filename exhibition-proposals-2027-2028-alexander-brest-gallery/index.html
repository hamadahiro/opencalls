Exhibition Proposals 2027–2028

Prize Solo or group exhibition

The Alexander Brest Gallery is the exhibition space of the Linda Berry Stein College of Arts & Sciences at Jacksonville University, on the university's riverfront campus across the St Johns River from downtown Jacksonville, and it programmes its season from an annual open call rather than from invitations alone. For the 2027–2028 season the gallery, under director Zakriya Rabani, is taking proposals for its 2,300 square foot space in 2D, 3D, installation and video work — photography sits inside the 2D remit, and the call is explicit that proposals are welcome from established and emerging artists in all mediums. Both solo and group exhibitions are considered, as are curatorial proposals, and a team may apply with one shared résumé of no more than a page per member. Applications are free and go in as a single PDF of no more than 50MB, named LastName_FirstName_ExhibitionTitle_Proposal.pdf, holding three things: an exhibition narrative that opens with a clear thesis statement (project proposals for unfinished or installation work also need a timeline, and any on-site making has to be installable in under three days); a CV of one to two pages per person with a preferred contact method; and an image list of up to 12 high-resolution images for a solo show or three per person for a group show, each image on its own page at large size with artist, title, medium, dimensions and year underneath. Video and audio work is submitted as streaming links plus stills, and an all-video application needs no image PDF at all. One requirement sets this call apart from a straight exhibition application: every proposal must include a student engagement activity at JU — a studio visit, a lecture, a tool or material demonstration, student participation in the exhibition narrative, or a collaboration with another college on campus. The gallery publishes its floor plan and installation photographs for applicants to plan against, and states that it is committed to showing artists across race, colour, creed, gender, gender variance, sexual orientation, national origin, age, religion, marital status, political affiliation and mental or physical disability. The call is open to artists anywhere in the continental United States and closes on 14 December 2026.
