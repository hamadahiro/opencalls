In The City 2027

Prizes $1,000 Exhibition

Skylines, crosswalks, alleyways and shops; bright lights, buses, taxis and crowds. Maryland Federation of Art's third In the City exhibition asks for work that carries the sense of an urban experience — the parts of city life that are universal and could be anywhere from a metropolis to a small town, and the symbols and iconic places that mark out one particular community, its restaurants, festivals, music halls and graffiti. Urbanite or visitor, the brief is a visual story of the city. Any original 2D or 3D work meeting MFA's guidelines is considered, with no dimension exceeding 72 inches including frame; artists may be asked to help install work over 60 inches or 40 pounds. Photographic prints must have been analogue or digitally processed by the artist, who must have controlled cropping, colour balance, contrast and the final printing, and any limited-edition photographic print must be signed and numbered; work made with image-generative AI is not accepted and no reproductions are. All MFA exhibitions are blind juried, with names and identifying information hidden from the juror. This edition is selected by Rob Pointon, a British painter who works primarily en plein air with an interest in wide-angle perspective and the depiction of movement; born in 1982, he took a first-class honours degree in fine art at Aberystwyth and studied at the Royal Drawing School in London, is a full member of the Royal Institute of Oil Painters and of the Manchester Academy of Fine Arts, and since 2021 has held residencies with the Household Cavalry and the wider Household Division. Awards designated by the juror total $1,000, presented at a virtual ceremony at 5:30 PM EST on 3 February 2027. Selected work hangs at MFA's Circle Gallery in Annapolis from 3 to 27 February 2027, with an in-person reception on the closing day. Entry is open to artists residing in any state or territory of the United States, Canada or Mexico and costs $45 for one or two works, $30 for MFA members, plus $6.75 each for up to four more ($4.50 for members); up to six original works made in the past three years may be entered, and work previously exhibited at Circle Gallery is not eligible for re-entry. Entry runs on ArtCall, where one "application" means one artwork; a second or third image of the same work may optionally be attached as a detail photograph. Artists are notified at 5 PM on 17 December and keep 70 per cent of sales, 75 per cent for members.
