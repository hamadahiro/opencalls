In The City 2027

Skylines, crosswalks, alleyways and shops; bright lights, buses, taxis and crowds. Maryland Federation of Art's third In the City exhibition asks for work that carries the sense of an urban experience — the parts of city life that are universal and could be anywhere from a metropolis to a small town, and the symbols and iconic places that mark out one particular community, its restaurants, festivals, music halls and graffiti. Urbanite or visitor, the brief is a visual story of the city. Any original 2D or 3D work meeting MFA's guidelines is considered, with no dimension exceeding 72 inches including frame; artists may be asked to help install work over 60 inches or 40 pounds. Photographic prints must have been analogue or digitally processed by the artist, who must have controlled cropping, colour balance, contrast and the final printing, and any limited-edition photographic print must be signed and numbered; work made with image-generative AI is not accepted and no reproductions are. All MFA exhibitions are blind juried, with names and identifying information hidden from the juror. Caution, as of 9 November 2026: the organiser's two pages disagree about whether non-members may enter, and the entry portal contradicts itself: its own FAQ answers "Who is this exhibition open to?" with "We invite all artists residing in any of the states or territories of the US, Canada, or Mexico to enter this exhibition", while its Terms of Entry on the same page still say members only. The entry portal's own Terms of Entry (mdfedart-itc2027.artcall.org) read “Entry is open to MFA members residing in the any of the states or territories of the United States, Canada, and Mexico”, and the linked prospectus PDF prices the call for members only — “1 or 2 entries for MFA Members is $30. Up to 4 additional entries $4.50 each for MFA Members. You must be a member to be elligible for entry” — while MFA's own call page still prints the open sentence (“Entry is open to artists residing in any of the states or territories of the United States, Canada, and Mexico”) above the two-tier fee block of $45 for 1–2 entries ($30 members) plus $6.75 each additional ($4.50 members). An MFA membership is $70 a year. If you are not a member, settle this with the gallery before paying: the portal is where you enter, and it currently says members. This edition is selected by Rob Pointon, a British painter who works primarily en plein air with an interest in wide-angle perspective and the depiction of movement; born in 1982, he took a first-class honours degree in fine art at Aberystwyth and studied at the Royal Drawing School in London, is a full member of the Royal Institute of Oil Painters and of the Manchester Academy of Fine Arts, and since 2021 has held residencies with the Household Cavalry and the wider Household Division. Awards designated by the juror total $1,000, presented at a virtual ceremony at 5:30 PM EST on 3 February 2027. Selected work hangs at MFA's Circle Gallery in Annapolis from 3 to 27 February 2027, with an in-person reception on the closing day. Entry is open to artists residing in any state or territory of the United States, Canada or Mexico and costs $45 for one or two works, $30 for MFA members, plus $6.75 each for up to four more ($4.50 for members); up to six original works made in the past three years may be entered, and work previously exhibited at Circle Gallery is not eligible for re-entry. Entry runs on ArtCall, where one "application" means one artwork; a second or third image of the same work may optionally be attached as a detail photograph. Artists are notified at 5 PM on 17 December and keep 70 per cent of sales, 75 per cent for members. MFA retains a 30 percent commission on any work sold through it (25 percent for MFA members), so artists keep 70 or 75 percent of a sale.
