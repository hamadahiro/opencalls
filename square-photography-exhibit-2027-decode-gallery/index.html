SQUARE Photography Exhibit 2027

Prize Exhibition

The next edition of Decode Gallery's square-format show, challenging photographers to compose within the confines of the perfect square — from analog Hasselblads to digital Instagram feeds, the most persistent format in photography, where symmetry, balance, focus and narrative are all transformed by equal sides. Submissions are welcome from every photographic genre, including portraiture, landscape, documentary, architecture and conceptual work; the call is open to all photographers worldwide, amateur and professional alike. Twelve images are selected by juror Holly Hart, a conceptual fine art photographer who founded Decode after a BA in Photography from the Art Institute of Seattle, and hang for an unusually long three months — February 6 to April 24, 2027 — at the gallery in Tucson, opening February 6. Selected photographers pay nothing further: the gallery handles all printing itself on an Epson printer with Ilford Pearl paper and presents work in museum-quality 14 x 14 inch frames with mats cut to 7 x 7 inches. One print is made per accepted image file and archived in a flat file for six months after the show. Entry is a flat $40 per entry, which the gallery's guidelines describe as covering up to ten photographs; the live entry form itself accepts fifteen files before it refuses more, so the ten is the entitlement the gallery states rather than a limit the form imposes. Photographers are notified by email within two weeks of the deadline. Work may optionally be listed for sale, with Decode taking a 50 per cent commission; all rights to the image file and the print remain with the photographer. The gallery states plainly that a paid entry does not guarantee a place in any exhibition.
