Small Works 2026

The Winterville Cultural Center Gallery, in the renovated 1950s high school just outside Athens, Georgia, invites artists across the United States to submit small-scale work for its National Juried Small Works Exhibition 2026. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, ceramics, sculpture, fiber and mixed media; no reproductions or giclees, and AI-generated artwork is not eligible. Work may not exceed 14 inches in any direction including the frame, and three-dimensional work may not exceed 14 inches in height, width or depth. Yu-Kai Lin, founder and director of Kai Lin Art in Atlanta, selects the exhibition and all three awards: Best in Show $500, Juror's Award $250 and a People's Choice Award of $100, $850 in cash in total. Up to three works may be entered for a single fee. Acceptances are sent September 12, shipped work is due September 23 and hand delivery runs September 24 to 26; the show opens to the public on October 7 with a reception on October 9 and closes December 15, 2026. All accepted work must be for sale for the run of the exhibition, with 65 percent of any sale to the artist and 35 percent to the gallery, and artists cover shipping both ways. Two-dimensional work must arrive framed and wired, with no sawtooth hangers. Entries are JPEG only, cropped to the artwork edges, 2 MB maximum.
