College Photographer of the Year 81

The 81st edition of the student photojournalism competition founded by Cliff and Vi Edom in 1945 and administered by the University of Missouri and the Reynolds Journalism Institute. Entry is free: CPOY credits co-sponsor Nikon Inc., “whose generosity allows CPOY to offer free entry to students in educational institutions worldwide.” Nineteen still-photography and multimedia categories are open to undergraduate and graduate students at any college, university or educational institution worldwide who enrolled for at least one semester, trimester or quarter between September 2025 and December 2026; anyone with two or more cumulative years of paid professional experience as a photographer, photo editor or videographer, including paid internships, is ineligible. Entries must have been created between August 23, 2025 and August 22, 2026. This is a journalistic competition — digital, analog/film or print alteration is prohibited outside the Illustration category, and AI-generated images are barred from every category, Illustration included. Awards include Nikon Z-series mirrorless camera and lens kits, a Missouri Photo Workshop fellowship, National Press Photographers Foundation monetary awards, Mountain Workshops and MediaStorm workshop tuition and a True/False Film Fest pass; the Alexia/CPOY Project Grant adds $1,000 to continue the proposed project, $500 for travel and lodging, and a place at The Alexia Fall Workshop. Judging runs October 17–24 and is livestreamed.
