Prémio NARRATIVA – FUJIFILM 2026

Prizes €3,500 Solo exhibition

Second edition of the photography award run by NARRATIVA, a non-profit association and Lisbon photography space, together with Fujifilm Portugal, its main patron. The award is open to all fields of photography and looks for original, unpublished projects that build visual narratives around contemporary issues; the jury weighs the relevance of the theme, the project's impact, conceptual and narrative coherence, and originality. The winner receives €3,500 and a solo exhibition at NARRATIVA in Lisbon, scheduled to open in January 2027, and the jury may name up to two honourable mentions whose projects are publicly presented at NARRATIVA. Applications run from 1 to 30 September 2026 through a form on NARRATIVA's own website and ask for a biography of up to 1,000 characters, a project title, a synopsis of up to 2,000 characters, and 15 to 20 JPEGs (3000px on the long edge, 72dpi, 2MB maximum) renamed 001, 002, 003 in reading order and stripped of the author's name and project title, so the reading is anonymous. Entry is free. Eligibility is narrow: applicants must be 18 or over, resident in Portugal and with tax domicile there, at either an emerging or established stage. Projects made in educational or academic contexts are welcome, but work developed in NARRATIVA's own workshops and masterclass is not, and current or former NARRATIVA members, Fujifilm X-Photographers and Fujifilm employees may not enter. Only basic adjustments to colour, contrast and framing are allowed and AI tools are barred outright. Two conditions are worth reading before entering: the winner must travel to and attend the exhibition opening and an accompanying talk or guided tour, and any honourable mentions must attend the public presentation of their projects on a date still to be set, and under the regulations one work from the exhibition enters NARRATIVA's collection while the remaining prints are stored for a year for possible showings at partner venues and destroyed after that. The jury for this edition is named — Yael Martínez, the Magnum photographer and W. Eugene Smith Grant and two-time World Press Photo winner; Ana Rocha, a designer at the photobook publisher GOST Books; and Mário Cruz, NARRATIVA's founder and director and himself a two-time World Press Photo winner.
