Cabin Fever 2027

Prizes $125 / $50 x2 Exhibition

Read the delivery clause before anything else: entries are hand-delivered only, across four dated windows in January 2027, and there is no shipping route in the prospectus. If you are not within driving distance of Fayetteville this is not a call you can enter.

It is non-juried, so there is no juror to weigh and no selection to lose — everyone who meets the specifications hangs. What is competitive is the award: $125 for first place and two $50 honorable mentions, handed out at the reception.

"All media accepted" is stated outright rather than implied, which is what makes this listable for photographers; the binding constraints are physical instead — 40 x 40 inches maximum including frame, properly wired, and priced for sale at 30% commission.
