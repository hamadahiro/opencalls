2027 Corinne Adams Emerging Artist Fellowship

Prizes $750 artist fee 3-month solo exhibition

Atlanta Celebrates Photography was founded in 1998 as an artist-run organisation, and its annual festival grew to become the largest community-based photography festival in the country. On its 25th anniversary it rebranded as the Atlanta Center for Photography (ACP), and as of 1 July 2024 it has worked from a four-storey building at 546 Edgewood Avenue SE; its programmes include commissioned exhibitions, a reading room and bookshelf residency, a teaching studio artist programme, portfolio reviews, a digital media lab and the Corinne Adams Emerging Artist Fellowship. The 2027 fellowship cycle is open, with an application and fee deadline of 3 November 2026 at 11:59 PM EST.

One Fellow is selected by jury to complete and exhibit an individual project over an approximately nine-month period. The Fellow receives a $750 artist fee plus production and framing financial support, 1:1 mentorship from Atlanta-based artist and educator Constance Thalken, professional development opportunities, exhibition layout and installation assistance from mentor and ACP staff, participation in the 2027 ACP Virtual Portfolio Reviews, the opportunity to give an artist talk and suggest additional programming, and a three-month solo exhibition at the ACP Project Lab with an opening reception on 20 May 2027.

ACP additionally selects four to six artists for the Emerging Artist Fellowship Cohort. Cohort members get a full schedule of monthly, concentrated cohort meetings and group critiques, a $325 artist fee, inclusion in a cohort group exhibition hosted by Day and Night Projects in summer 2027, and recognition across ACP's newsletter and social channels.

Eligibility is specific: an emerging lens-based artist working in photography, video art or mixed media, aged 18 or older, living or working in the metro-Atlanta area, and not enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate programme at the time of submission or over the course of the fellowship. ACP asks for a biographical statement including past art experience (250 words max) and a brief proposal of the exhibition (350 words max); the application page notes that a successful proposal considers the unique architecture of the Project Lab and ACP's installation-based approach to lens-based media, and reference images and a floor plan are provided.

The application fee is $15, paid separately — any application with an unpaid fee by 3 November will not be considered. ACP states that if the fee is a barrier, applicants should email programme staff. Finalists are interviewed by ACP staff and jurors on 10 and 11 November 2026, and the selected Fellow is notified by 13 November 2026. Note that ACP's own standing Emerging Artist Fellowship programme page still displays the previous cycle's "APPLICATIONS CLOSED" text and 2024/2025 dates; the live 2027 application form and the ACP front page are the current surfaces.
