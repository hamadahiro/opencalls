Open Call — New Perspectives

Prizes Solo exhibition €500 production grant Group exhibition

New & Abstract, the Berlin gallery that began in 2019 as an Instagram platform and has since shown over a hundred artists across roughly 100 square metres in the centre of the city, is looking for “distinctive, innovative, and exciting artistic positions” on the theme of New Perspectives — “fresh, individual ways of seeing”. The media list is wide and names photography explicitly, alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, digital art, sculpture, installation, wall objects and mixed media: “whether figurative or abstract, minimalist or expressive, conceptual or intuitive — surprise us with your unique artistic vision”. Thirty artists are selected for a curated group exhibition at the gallery, running 4–18 December 2026, and receive a feature on the New & Abstract Instagram account, professional photographs of the exhibition, and joint promotion through the gallery’s website, social channels and invitation materials. An independent jury then picks one winner from those thirty for a solo exhibition at the gallery in spring/summer 2027, with the gallery taking care of the concept, communications and opening reception, plus a production grant of up to €500; the winner is announced publicly at the 4 December opening, where attendance is “strongly encouraged but is not a requirement for winning”. Twenty honourable mentions are featured on the gallery’s Instagram feed and stories. The call is “open to artists aged 18 and over from anywhere in the world”; one image is uploaded per application, work for the group show must not exceed 80 × 80 cm, and you must be the creator and rights holder. Practicalities worth pricing in: a non-refundable €20 processing fee applies per artwork submitted, and the gallery states that “no additional costs will arise — not even if you are selected for the exhibition, the online gallery, or Instagram promotion”; the artist keeps 60% of any sale price and is responsible for getting work to Berlin and arranging its return. Jury selection is on engagement with the theme, artistic originality, quality and coherence, and the potential of the practice to grow into an independent solo show.
