Circuito OFF 2026

Prize Festival exhibition

The free and independent section of NARNIMMAGINARIA, the photography festival staged in the historic centre of Narni, Umbria, and run by the Associazione culturale SATOR. Now in its 11th edition, the 2026 theme is "Through human landscapes": the traces people leave in the places they inhabit and reshape, and the invisible territories of their stories and emotions. Documentary, narrative, conceptual, artistic and experimental readings are all admitted, and entry is open to photographers of any age, nationality or background. Three categories: Single Shot, a single photograph, with fine-art printing and installation handled entirely by the organisers; Triptych, three photographs or a single panel supplied already printed and hung in the bars, restaurants, shops and other businesses of the centre rather than in galleries, with installation optionally delegated to the organisers for €35; and Stories, a printed sequence of 8 to 18 images at 30×40 cm, framed by the organisers. Entrants can apply to one, two or all three categories and submit more than one work in each, paying a €15 contribution per category. Everything goes by email as a single ZIP folder. The exhibition runs 17 October to 2 November 2026; printed works are due 8 October.
