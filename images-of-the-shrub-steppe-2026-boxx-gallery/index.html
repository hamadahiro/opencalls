Images of the Shrub-Steppe 2026

Prizes Three $100 awards Exhibition

Annual juried exhibit presented by Boxx Gallery and Cowiche Canyon Conservancy celebrating the shrub-steppe lands of Central Washington — the tenth Conservancy show at the Tieton gallery. Work in any medium made within the past five years and inspired by the shrub-steppe landscape is eligible, as long as it hasn't appeared in a previous edition; there are no location restrictions on entrants, but all work must be delivered to and picked up from the Tieton gallery. Accepted work may be shipped to arrive by August 27 or hand-delivered September 1, and must be for sale (35% gallery commission). Juried by photographer and 2022 Guggenheim Fellow Nancy Floyd. On view September 5–27, 2026, with Juror's, People's Choice, and Cowiche Canyon Conservancy awards of $100 each.
