2026 Betsy Meyer Memorial Exhibition & Meyer Family Award

Prizes $1,000 Exhibition

Main Line Art Center's annual Betsy Meyer Memorial Exhibition and Meyer Family Award for Contemporary Art honours three artists a year with a show in the Center's gallery in Haverford, Pennsylvania, and an award of $1,000. The programme exists to support and honour contemporary artists and to encourage excellence and experimentation in artistic practice, presentation and community involvement. The 2026 exhibition runs 14 November to 18 December, opening with a reception and artist talks on Saturday 14 November from 6 to 8pm. All fine artists are eligible to apply, all mediums are welcome - the organiser's own Artwork Archive listing names photography among the accepted categories - and artists are encouraged to submit work that pushes the boundaries of their medium. The practical conditions are strict and worth reading before applying. Everything you apply with must be available for the exhibition, and the Center reserves the right to rescind an invitation and award if it is not. At least three of the works chosen for the show must be for sale, on a 60/40 commission split in the artist's favour. Artists must attend the opening reception and artist talks on 14 November and be available to help install between 11 and 13 November; anyone who would rather the Center installed their work must drop it off on 9 or 10 November between 9am and 4pm. The Center does not ship or transport work under any circumstances, so delivery and collection are the artist's responsibility - in practice this is a call for artists who can get to the Philadelphia Main Line in person. Entry is $35 and applications go through SlideRoom. Winning artists are notified by Monday 5 October 2026. Main Line Art Center has been running since 1937 and also operates Accessible Art programmes for children and adults with disabilities; it notes separately that it does not accept unsolicited exhibition proposals and does not rent its galleries.
