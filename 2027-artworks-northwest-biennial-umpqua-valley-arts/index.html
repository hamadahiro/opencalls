2027 Artworks Northwest Biennial

Prizes $500 / $250 / $150 $100 J. Lynn Peterson Award

Umpqua Valley Arts' regional biennial: a juried and curated showcase of contemporary work by artists living in the Pacific Northwest, with space for more than 100 works. The organisers invite artists at all stages of their careers — established, mid-career and emerging — residing in Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Northern California, Alaska, British Columbia, Western Montana or Western Wyoming. All themes, genres and media are eligible except video; fine art prints including c-prints and digital prints are explicitly eligible but must be signed, numbered and in a limited edition of 125 or fewer, and no reproductions of original artworks are accepted. Work must have been made in the last three years, not shown at UVA before, and must stay within 60 inches on its longest side, fit through a standard three-foot door and weigh under 50 lbs. Each artist may enter up to three works; on CaFE every image counts as a submission, so the organisers recommend one image per piece to leave room for more works. Selection is by a single invited curator who lives and works in the region — the list of past curators runs through Grace Kook-Anderson, Steven Hayes, Marie Watt, Andries Fourie, Scott Malbaurn, Arvie Smith and Charlene Liu — and the 2027 curator is the Portland artist Brenda Mallory, a citizen of the Cherokee Nation whose sculptures sew reclaimed materials together with industrial hardware to explore dominion, disruption and repair, and who has held Joan Mitchell, Hallie Ford and Bonnie Bronson Fellowships. Accepted works are then juried again for cash awards: $500 first place, $250 second, $150 third and the $100 J. Lynn Peterson Award. Entry is $35 and UVA members get 10% off with a coupon code from the exhibitions coordinator. Artists are notified no later than 5 May 2027, work is delivered on 7 June, and the exhibition runs 11 June to 21 August 2027 in Roseburg, Oregon.
