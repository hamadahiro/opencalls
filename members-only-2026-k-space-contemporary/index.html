Members Only 2026

Juried, non-thematic exhibition of new work by members of K Space Contemporary, the 501(c)(3) contemporary art space in downtown Corpus Christi. Held only in even-numbered years, it exists to put a member's work in front of a panel of Coastal Bend art professionals: the Best of Show winner is awarded a solo exhibition at K Space in 2027 or 2028, with cash awards for second and third place. All visual art media are accepted, including photography - the gallery bars only reproductions such as giclee or inkjet copies of original works, and video is exhibited on a 58-inch screen. Each artist may submit one or two works completed in the last two years and not previously shown at K Space; 40 to 50 top-scoring works are hung. Two-dimensional work must be no wider than 60 inches and wired to hang, with Plexi rather than glass. Entry is $10 plus K Space membership, which can be bought inside the entry system ($20 student or senior 65+, $35 individual). All work must be for sale, with a 40% gallery commission. Notification November 12; the show runs November 28, 2026 to January 22, 2027, with an ArtWalk reception and awards on December 4.
