Flowers — Carlotta Gallery

Carlotta Gallery's themed virtual exhibition on floral life — fleeting, fragile and fierce — from the harsh elegance of a single blooming stem to petals fading to dust. The gallery is after the raw, the romantic and the forgotten: high fashion studio setups, wild overgrown fields, deep macro textures or moody analog decay. Free to apply, pay only if selected; selected work is shown in a virtual exhibition for 6–12 months with a free PDF catalogue, permanent Collections-page listing, Instagram feature and certificate of recognition.
