6th Annual Listen to Women Exhibition

Prize $200 Best of Show; $150 second; $100 third

A free juried show for teenage artists in metro Detroit, sponsored by the American Association of University Women, Grosse Pointe Branch, and hung by the Grosse Pointe Artists Association in its gallery at The War Memorial on Lake Shore in Grosse Pointe Farms. The brief is narrow in who it is for and wide in what it will take: artists aged 14 to 18 living in Wayne, Oakland or Macomb counties, making work that shares “their thoughts on issues affecting women”. Entry is free — AAUWGP covers the fee outright, so a 14-year-old photographer can enter a juried gallery show at no cost — and there is real money at the end of it: $200 for Best of Show, $150 for second place and $100 for third. Elizabeth Gauthier, a painter, muralist and adjunct professor at the College for Creative Studies, is the juror, with final acceptance resting with her and AAUWGP. Up to three pieces per artist, submitted as JPGs of at least 600 by 600 pixels and no more than 50MB; images must honestly show the nature and quality of the work, and the organisers ask that pieces be photographed evenly lit and without reflections. There is no stated size cap, though gallery space is weighed during jurying and large installation pieces are out, as is anything built from commercial kits, prefabricated models or hobby crafts. Past winners of the exhibition may enter again. Results go out by email on 3 December; accepted work is delivered in person to The War Memorial on Saturday 5 December between noon and 3pm, with two-dimensional pieces mounted on a rigid backing such as foam board or framed and wired for hanging — clip-on and poster frames are refused. The show runs 9 December to 22 January 2027 and work is collected on Saturday 23 January. Work need not be for sale.
