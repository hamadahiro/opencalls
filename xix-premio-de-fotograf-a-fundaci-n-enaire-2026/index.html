XIX Premio de Fotografía Fundación ENAIRE 2026

19th edition of this prestigious photography prize, running since 2005, in collaboration with PHotoESPAÑA. Open theme — submit up to 3 artistic photographs (digital or analog). Two-phase process: digital submission, then 30 finalists present physical prints (1x1m to 3x3m). Winning works join the ENAIRE Contemporary Art Collection. Finalists and winners exhibit at Naves de Gamazo in Santander during PHotoESPAÑA 2026. AI-generated images not allowed.
