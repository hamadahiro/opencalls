Chemins de photos 2027 — 14th Edition

Chemins de photos returns for a 14th edition across some twenty villages of the western Aude, showing selected series as large open-air prints the association pays to produce. No theme, no entry fee, open to any photographer or collective; €30 membership only if selected, and a €300 Jury Prize can reward an unexhibited series. Selected work is licensed non-exclusively for two editions, may be loaned or rented to partners, and is archived online indefinitely.
