Open Call — Il Reportage in Italia

Open call for the 6th edition of Festival del Reportage in Ascoli Piceno (June 26–28, 2026). Seeking reportage photography projects made in Italy — completed or in progress, 15–25 images. At least some photos must be taken after January 1, 2025. Submit via Google Form with a file-sharing link (WeTransfer, Google Drive, etc.). Winner receives €500 cash prize, projection of work at the festival, and full hospitality including travel within Italy, accommodation, and meals. No restrictions on nationality, age, or experience.
