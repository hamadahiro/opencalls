2028 Artist-in-Residence Program

The Factory on Willow, a reimagined mill complex in Manchester, New Hampshire, gives emerging artists a live/work studio apartment for up to three months, a $1,600 food and materials stipend, $350 towards travel and a closing solo or duo exhibition. Applying is free and applications are read as they arrive; each resident donates one work made there to the building's collection.

DeadlineOctober 17, 2027
PrizesResidency, $1,600 stipend, Exhibition
Entry feeFree
