Photobooks in Dialogue

Centrum för fotografi (CFF), Sweden's member organisation for photographers, is calling for photobooks and zines for an exhibition in Gothenburg staged with Fotobok Gbg as part of Fotobok Fest. The curators are looking for photo-based publications in which dialogue, collaboration or exchange are central: an exchange of ideas between two photographers, between an artist and an archive, between image and text, or the close interplay of artist, designer and publisher. They describe it as a ping-pong match, where ideas bounce from one side to the other in fine balance without ever falling off the table — books and zines where the exchange between several voices is fundamental and has shaped the work from the ground up. The exhibition runs 25–27 September 2026 at the CFF gallery on Bangatan 10 in Gothenburg, with support from the City of Gothenburg and the Swedish Photographers' Association. Publications from anywhere in the world are welcome, but shipping to Sweden is at the applicant's own cost; books must arrive by 22 September. Applications are free and consist of a single PDF of no more than 10 MB containing a CV of up to two pages, a presentation of the publication of up to 300 words in Swedish or English, and 10–15 pages from the book. Selected participants are notified at the end of August. After the exhibition you can either donate your publication to the Hasselblad Foundation Library in Gothenburg or have it returned at CFF's expense.
