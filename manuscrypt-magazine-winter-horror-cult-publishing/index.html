Manuscrypt Magazine — Winter Horror

Cult Publishing is a small horror press that describes the publishing world as "a sucking void of unanswered and untapped talent" and itself as "the void answering back", existing to platform emerging voices whose work is undeservedly unseen. Its magazine, Manuscrypt, is built around a binding theme each issue, and art submissions are open for the next one: Winter Horror. The press asks for work that encapsulates the horror of the winter season — its own prompts are blood on fresh snow, figures in the gale, bodies frozen mid-scream in ice, trees devoid of green. Fitting the theme is not mandatory, but themed work is favoured for acceptance. Photography is named first among the accepted media — "art submitted to the magazine can be photography, digital art, and drawings, but we are not limited to these mediums" — and the magazine's FAQ repeats it. Both original and reprinted art are accepted for this issue. There is no submission fee and no payment step anywhere in the route, and every accepted submission is paid $50 — but the organiser's two surfaces disagree about reprints. The website FAQ says "at this time, we do not pay for reprints", while the submission form itself promises "$50.00 for all successful original and reprinted art submissions"; the form asks each entrant to mark the piece Original or Reprint, so a reprint may still fall under the FAQ's exclusion. Original work that is accepted carries three months' exclusivity from publication and First Publication Rights pass to the magazine, a point Cult Publishing spells out at length and links to Clarkesworld editor Neil Clarke's guide and the Society of Authors' contract guidance rather than burying. Simultaneous submissions are accepted; the FAQ asks for one story per writer per issue, and the art form takes a single title and one file per submission. Work goes through a Google Form as a PDF or image file of up to 100 MB, at a minimum of 300 dpi; the magazine page is 8.5 by 11 inches but the art does not have to match that. The form uploads the file to Google Drive, so a Google account is needed to attach it, and it heads itself "Winter Edition" where the call page names the theme Winter Horror. AI is refused without qualification: "We do not publish work generated by AI in any form. Whether it is text, images, or otherwise." Submissions opened on 28 October 2026 and close at 5pm EST on Friday 20 November 2026, with every entrant answered after the window shuts.
