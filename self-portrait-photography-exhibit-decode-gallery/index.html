SELF PORTRAIT Photography Exhibit

Decode Gallery invites photographers worldwide to submit their best self portrait work for a themed gallery show in Tucson, Arizona. The gallery frames self portraiture as an exploration of identity and an intimate glimpse into the life of the artist, and is looking for work that reveals a story rather than simply records a likeness. At least twenty-six images are selected by juror Holly Hart, a conceptual fine art photographer who founded Decode Gallery after a BA in Photography from the Art Institute of Seattle. Selected photographers pay nothing further: the gallery handles all printing itself on an Epson printer with Epson Ultra Premium Luster paper, and presents work in museum-quality 22 x 22 inch white frames with precut white mats in a range of sizes. Submitted files are never cropped, only scaled for the best fit in the mat. One print is made per accepted image and archived in a flat file for two years after the show. Entry is $40 for up to five photographs, open to amateurs and professionals alike. Photographers are notified by email within two weeks of the deadline and high-resolution files are due two days later. The exhibition runs November 7 to 28, 2026, opening on November 7. Work may optionally be listed for sale, with the gallery taking a 50 per cent commission and all rights to the file and print remaining with the photographer.
