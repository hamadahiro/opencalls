Solo Exhibition April 2026

Prize Exhibition prize

All About Photo runs a recurring Solo Exhibition program — a monthly featured photographer slot. The April 2026 cycle selects one photographer for a full-month online solo exhibition running April 1–30, 2026.

What's offered: a permanent gallery presence on the All About Photo platform, an exclusive interview, social media promotion, newsletter feature, international press coverage, and commission-free sales.

Selection is by Sandrine Hermand-Grisel, photographer and founder of All About Photo. Single-juror selection.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the All About Photo website with a cohesive project of 6–14 images. Fee is $45. The April 2026 cycle deadline was March 20; selection announced April 1, 2026.
