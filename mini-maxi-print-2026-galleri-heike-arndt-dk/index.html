Mini Maxi Print 2026

Open call for professional graphic artists worldwide from Galleri Heike Arndt DK's Berlin space in Friedrichshain, for an exhibition of small and large-format print editions opening October/November 2026. Eligible techniques are editioned printmaking — lithograph, screen print, woodcut, etching, drypoint, linocut, photogravure and similar — in editions of at least 3; monoprints, inkjet and digital prints, and photo-based images without a physically worked plate are excluded. Submit 3–6 prints per section. Free to apply; selected artists pay a €40 exhibition fee plus shipping both ways, and works must remain available for the exhibition period plus 12 months. The gallery produces flyers and a poster featuring each artist.
