Howard Chapnick Grant 2026

Prize $7,500

The Howard Chapnick Grant sits alongside the W. Eugene Smith Grant in Humanistic Photography and the Smith Student Grant, but it works differently from both: it recognises programs, not individuals. The Fund is looking for initiatives that make a significant contribution to the field of documentary photography through education, and applicants must have been operating for at least one year. There is no geographic restriction.

For 2026 the award is $7,500, dispersed as $6,000 on receipt of the grant and $1,500 on submission of a final report. Funding may go toward research, direct learning, online resources, publication, archives, exhibition, or dissemination through social media. It explicitly does not fund the making or production of photographs — this is money for the educational infrastructure around documentary photography rather than for a shooting project.

Unlike the Fund's other grants, applications do not go through Picter. Entries are accepted by the Smith Fund directly by email, with "Chapnick Grant" in the subject line, and consist of a description of no more than 500 words on one page covering the program's activities, its constituency and its contribution to its community, together with at least three reference links so the Fund can verify the work.

A $25 donation accompanies the application, but it is explicitly voluntary: the Board of Trustees states that it understands some programs can afford to donate while others cannot. Entries opened on 1 July 2026, the deadline is 12 October 2026 at 11:59pm EDT, and recipients are announced on 15 December 2026.
