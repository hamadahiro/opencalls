W. Eugene Smith Grant for Student Photographers 2026

Prize Two $5,000 grants

The W. Eugene Smith Memorial Fund's grant for student photographers, awarded to fully matriculated students whose work continues Smith's tradition of humanistic, concerned photography. Two grants of $5,000 each are awarded — $4,500 on selection and $500 on delivery of twelve images within 18 months. Open to students 18 or older of any nationality (high-schoolers excluded); international applicants are welcome and a valid student ID is required. Applicants submit a bio, project description and proposal with up to 40 images from an ongoing project. The call opened July 1, 2026 and closes 12 October 2026 at 11:59pm EDT; recipients are chosen by a three-member international jury.
