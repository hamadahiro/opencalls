National Open Call — Young Photographers

GRAIN Projects is commissioning one photographer aged 18–26 and living in England to make new work about young people's lives and the challenges they face. The commission pays £2,000, with a further £500 towards a public sharing of the work, plus mentoring. Entry is free, but there is no exhibition and the project runs just eight weeks.
