BINGO 2026

Prizes Exhibition Print swap

A pilot “give two, get one” open call from Candela, the Richmond photography gallery and book publisher that became a 501(c)(3) in 2026 after fifteen years as Candela Books + Gallery. Fifteen small photographic works are selected for a show running October 2–24 that ends in a bingo fundraiser: each framed print on view goes home with a winner on closing day, while a second print from each artist enters a randomised swap that sends another participant's work back to them, with Candela covering the postage. There is no theme other than the use of photography and no entry fee; artists may submit up to three prints, each with at least two available editions and none larger than 12 inches on the longest side. Candela frames and mats the exhibited works and prints are due by September 22. The gallery is candid that the show is philanthropic and community-focused and “may not be a great fit for artists looking for big sales opportunities”.
