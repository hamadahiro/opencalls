Winter Small Works Juried Show 2026

The winter exhibition at Rowayton Arts Center, a non-profit gallery on Rowayton Avenue in Norwalk, Connecticut, is a small-works show with no theme — the centre asks only that the work not be holiday themed. It is framed frankly as a selling show: RAC says it is presenting commercially viable work, appealing in subject matter and price and accessible to the public, so the sum of all four sides of a piece must come to 80 inches or less including frame, and everything must be marked for sale at $500 or less. All media are welcome, two-dimensional work only, and the centre's ban on giclee reproductions is waived for photography. The juror is Sandra Pelletier, founder and owner of Sorelle Gallery, a fine art gallery and art advisory firm featuring traditional and contemporary work with a stated commitment to client service, fine art education and collection diversity; she holds a bachelor's and a master's degree in art education and has worked as a part-time art teacher and in technology and sales. Best in Show receives $250. Entry is $35 for a first piece and $10 for each additional piece up to five for non-members, $25 and $10 for members, and $2 and $1 for the centre's Art Angel life members. The call is open to all artists 18 and over. Artwork generated by AI is not acceptable, work must have been created in the last three years and not previously exhibited at RAC, and accepted pieces are hand delivered on November 16 or 17 — mailed work is not accepted. The submission link goes live on the centre's website on October 5 and closes at midnight on October 30; artists are notified on or before Friday November 6, and the show opens with a reception on the afternoon of November 22 and runs to December 24 2026. RAC takes a 30% commission on sales.
