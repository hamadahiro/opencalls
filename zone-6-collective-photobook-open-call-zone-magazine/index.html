ZONE 6 — Collective Photobook Open Call

Prize Publication (print)

ZONE is an independent book publisher and photography-based visual arts platform started in 2018 and run by its founder Ali Beşikçi as a one-person operation; this is the open call for its sixth collective book, after five earlier volumes that between them published more than 650 artists from around the world. There is deliberately no theme. The interest is in what happens when images from different contexts are placed together and begin to act like parts of a larger organism, so the call is open worldwide to all visual arts - photography first, but also painting, illustration, sculpture, design, film stills, anything printable in a book - with no restriction on age, accomplishments or approach. You may send images from one or more projects; they are considered individually, and selection is said to rest solely on the images, not on CV or status, with a stated preference for giving visibility to lesser-known artists. Selected artists are published in the sixth collective book in offset print, with print run, size and design decided by the character of the work; the curator selects, edits and sequences the images and places them alongside other works, but will not crop or alter them without consent. Favourite works are also shared on ZONE's social channels during and after the call, whether or not they make the book. Selected artists do not receive a free copy but may buy any number at 50% off. There are two ways to enter: pay a €10 submission fee for up to 10 images, or pre-order the book at the early-bird price of €30, which includes one submission of up to 10 images; both are non-refundable and neither guarantees inclusion, and every additional submission needs its own fee or pre-order. Payment through the page redirects to an upload form; files are JPEGs under 2 MB named First Last Number.jpg, with your Instagram username in the message, and submissions that ignore the naming rules may be excluded without refund. High-resolution files are requested only if selected. Deadline 30 November 2026; featured artists are announced on 1 February 2027 on the website, social media and newsletter, and the book is due in spring 2027.
