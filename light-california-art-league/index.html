LIGHT

Prizes $800 Best of Show $500/$200/$100 places Exhibition

A live juried exhibition on the theme of Light, presented by the California Art League at bG Gallery in Bergamot Station, Santa Monica, and hung there from 3 to 18 November 2026. Om Navon Bleicher, who founded bG Gallery in 2009 and directs it, is the sole juror and picks both the show and the awards from the digital images submitted. Awards are $800 Best of Show, $500 first place, $200 second place, $100 third place, three honourable mentions and the Theodore Herman Award for Photography. The League - founded in 1948 as the Valley Artists Guild by the sculptor Henry Van Wolf and renamed in 2012 - says painters, sculptors, photographers and collage artists are welcome, and this call accepts drawing, painting, photographs, digital art, mixed media, collage and sculpture; no performance art or AI is accepted. Work must be original and created by the artist from conception to completion, and must not have been shown in a CAL exhibit before. The call is open to CAL members and non-members alike: one or two entries cost $35 for members with dues current and $55 for non-members, the $20 difference being a one-time charge that also gives the new artist a discounted CAL membership; further works are $5 each up to a total of six. There is a 30-inch maximum frame width for wall art. Entries close 11 October at midnight PST and artists are notified on 19 October. Accepted work must be hand delivered to the gallery on 2 November; all work must be for sale and must not be offered for sale anywhere else until 30 days after the show closes, with bG Gallery taking a 50% commission on sales in that period. The opening reception and awards presentation is on Saturday 7 November, with an artists' talk on 15 November and pick-up on 19 November.
