Field Notes from the Middle

Inaugural juried exhibition at Filter Space, Filter Photo's Chicago gallery, surveying contemporary photography across the Midwest. The call is open to photography, photographic materials and other lens-based artwork that engages with the region, from artists making work in Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, South Dakota and Wisconsin. Juror Nathaniel M. Stein, Curator of Photography at the Cincinnati Art Museum, will select five artists to each exhibit five to seven works from a single body of work, and all five are eligible for the 2026 Filter Photo Museum Purchase Award of $2,000, with the selected work acquired by the Cincinnati Art Museum for its permanent collection. The other four exhibiting artists each receive a $250 stipend towards shipping and production costs, though artists otherwise cover their own production and shipping. Applicants submit an artist statement of 500 words or less, a current bio and 15–20 sample images. Accepted artists are notified in mid-to-late October, work is due at Filter Space by November 25, and the exhibition runs December 4, 2026 – February 13, 2027 with a gallery reception on opening night.
