Forms of Being

International group show at CICA Museum in Gimpo, South Korea, on portraits, figures and living forms across photography, printmaking, painting, video, sculpture and installation. Submission is free by email - up to five JPEGs and/or three video links plus a bio. Accepted artists contribute $78 per work toward exhibition processing and maintenance, and the show runs April 28 to May 16, 2027.
