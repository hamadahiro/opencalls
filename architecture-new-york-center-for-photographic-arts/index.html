ARCHITECTURE

NYC4PA's international juried call ARCHITECTURE asks photographers for their vision of the built world - farm houses, split-level ranches and skyscrapers, but equally bridges, private houses, skylines, cathedral interiors, the pyramids of Mexico or the main hall of Grand Central. Whole structures and architectural detail are both welcome, and the organiser is explicit that a building need not be world famous. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers working in any medium, style or school of thought - print, image transfer, emulsion transfer, encaustic, black and white - with experimental and mixed techniques welcome; only 2-D work is eligible and jurying is blind, so no signatures or watermarks on images and no artist names in titles. $4,025 in cash awards go to ten prizewinners - the organiser's own summary line rounds this to $4,000, but the itemised ladder on the same page sums to $4,025: a $1,100 Grand Prize, whose image is featured on the NYC4PA home page, three First Prizes of $400, three Second Prizes of $325 and three Third Prizes of $250, alongside 20 Juror Selections and 20 Honorable Mentions, all included in the NYC4PA online gallery and winners catalog with an award certificate. Images awarded an NYC4PA prize within the past two years are not eligible; images submitted and not selected may be resubmitted at any time. THE CUTOFF IS TIMEZONE-SPECIFIC: NYC4PA prints "DEADLINE: November 22, 2026 (Midnight Pacific Time)" - the end of 22 November on the US west coast, which is already 23 November in Europe, so it is not end of day where you are.
