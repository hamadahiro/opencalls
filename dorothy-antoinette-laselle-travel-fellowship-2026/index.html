Dorothy Antoinette LaSelle Travel Fellowship 2026

Prize $5,000

Two awards of $5,000 for Texas-based visual artists to fund a research trip that pushes their practice forward. The fellowship takes its cue from its namesake: Dorothy Antoinette “Toni” LaSelle (1901–2002), a pioneer of modernism in Texas, travelled to Europe in 1927–28 after finishing her MA at the University of Chicago, studying the art of England, Italy and France — a trip made possible only by a study fund she won for work shown in the First Student Art Exhibition at the Renaissance Society in 1926. She went on to live in Denton and teach art and design for over four decades at what is now Texas Woman's University. The guest juror this year is Lana Meador, Associate Curator of Modern and Contemporary Art at the San Antonio Museum of Art, who co-curated Texas Women: A New History of Abstract Art and has juried awards for the City of San Antonio's Department of Arts and Culture and the Texas Sculpture Group. Applicants must currently live in Texas and have done so for at least a year, hold a degree in the arts, and have had at least three solo or group exhibitions in the past three years — BFA and juried shows count. There is no application fee. Applications ask for up to four work thumbnails, a CV, two references, proof of Texas residency and a project proposal of 2,000 characters or less covering why the fellowship matters to your practice, your goals for the trip and when you would travel. Awardees are notified on 2 November 2026 and must complete their travel between 1 December 2026 and 31 December 2027.
