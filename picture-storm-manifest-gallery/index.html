PICTURE STORM

Prizes $50 honorarium $1,000 Grand Jury

Manifest, the artist-run 501(c)(3) creative research gallery in Cincinnati, calls for works about image overload. "Our lives are loud with pictures": the prospectus asks how we slow the stream, how we process too much information, how we tell a lie from the truth, and how we cope with having to take part, willingly and unwillingly, in making images - "as loving as exchanging a picture with a friend, as essential as producing and revealing an artwork or scientific discovery, as discomfiting as being photographed in public". PICTURE STORM is a call for work about multiplicity, reproduction, excess, archives, records, overload and navigating a world overfull of visual information, from a gallery that runs its own darkroom and photo facility and an international photography annual - though Manifest states that its juries "have no collective predisposition towards any particular kind or style of art". Open to everyone internationally in all traditional and non-traditional media, with no size limit beyond what fits through the gallery door and no restriction on when the work was made. Selection is two-part: "an anonymous several member panel of professional and academic advisors" scores the entries and passes its recommendations to the exhibit curator, who makes the final selections from the jury-approved pool. Every exhibiting artist receives a $50 honorarium with no strings attached, and one work in each of the season's nine exhibition periods takes a $1,000 Grand Jury Award decided by re-jury. The exhibition runs 22 January - 19 February 2027 and selected work appears in the season annual. Work must arrive ready to install and artists pay shipping both ways; sale is optional, and the gallery takes 30% on anything sold. The entry form takes up to twelve works, at $45 for the first three and $5 for each one after.
