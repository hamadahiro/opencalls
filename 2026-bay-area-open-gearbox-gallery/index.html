2026 Bay Area Open

GearBox Gallery is an artist-run space on West Grand Avenue in Oakland, and the Bay Area Open is its call for a two-person featured exhibition in the Main Gallery. Two artists are chosen to show together from November 5 to December 19 2026, with an opening reception on Saturday November 7. Entry is open to artists living in the nine Greater Bay Area counties — Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano and Sonoma — who have a current, cohesive body of work made within the last five years. Applicants submit 8 to 12 images along with an artist statement about the work rather than about wanting to show at GearBox, and the gallery says image quality will be an important factor in jurying. There are no media or size limitations. The curatorial team selects which of the submitted pieces to hang, so not everything entered is necessarily shown; the aim is to present a substantial body of work from each of the two artists. The processing fee is $35 per application rather than per image. Notification goes out by email on September 28 2026, and accepted work is delivered in person on November 1 or the morning of November 2 — the gallery does not accept mailed work. Submitted artwork must be for sale, and GearBox pays the artist 60% of the sale price within 30 days of the exhibition closing. Pick-up is scheduled between December 20 2026 and January 3 2027. Entries go through CaFE at callforentry.org, where the 2026 Bay Area Open listing is now live.
