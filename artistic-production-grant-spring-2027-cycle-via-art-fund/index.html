Artistic Production Grant — Spring 2027 Cycle

Prize $25,000–$100,000

VIA Art Fund's Artistic Production Grants pay for the production of newly commissioned works of visual art shown outside museum or gallery walls, in the public realm or in non-traditional exhibition environments. Awards run from $25,000 to $100,000, with the upper levels reserved for permanent installations or works to be gifted to a US public collection, and the fund has awarded more than $10 million since 2013. The Spring 2027 cycle starts with a letter of inquiry due 5 November 2026; invitations to submit a full application go out 15 December 2026, invited applications are due 25 January 2027, awards are announced in April 2027 and projects must open to the public after 15 June 2027. Individual artists, nonprofits and cultural institutions may apply, in the US and internationally, but the project needs both a confirmed artist and a confirmed exhibition venue or presenting partner at the time of the letter of inquiry: if the project takes place in the US the artist may be based anywhere, and if the venue is outside the US the artist must be based in the US. VIA does not fund museum or gallery exhibitions, curatorial projects, residency programmes, performing arts or youth education. VIA names no medium in its guidelines, and its own grantee roster answers the question directly: photographer Wendy Red Star and film-and-photography artist Amie Siegel both hold 2025 Artistic Production grants. There is no application fee, and the letter of inquiry is filed through VIA's own eligibility form.
