REWIND. PIVOT. REPEAT.

The Shemer Art Center & Museum in Phoenix devotes this juried group show to the idea that life rarely follows a straight path — that we revisit old ideas, rediscover forgotten memories and adapt, and that looking back is often how transformation starts. Artists are invited to work from nostalgia, history, personal milestones, cultural evolution, adaptation, resilience and renewal, drawing on specific eras, family histories, traditions, architecture, fashion, music or the pivotal moments that redirected a life. Original works in all visual media are welcome and photography is named explicitly alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics, printmaking, fiber, mixed media, glass and digital art; photographic entries must state how the work will be printed and displayed, whether framed under glass, on metal or otherwise. The juror is Bentley Calverley, who founded Bentley Gallery in 1984 and spent a career introducing leading-edge contemporary art to Scottsdale and Phoenix. Entrants must be Arizona residents aged 18 or over, work must have been made within the last three years and cannot have been shown at the Shemer before, and nothing may exceed five feet high by four feet wide. Up to three works may be entered, at $40 for one rising to $50 for three, with member rates of $30 to $40. Notification is on 2 October, hand-delivered work is due on 23 October between 1 and 4 pm, and the exhibition runs from 28 October 2026 to 7 January 2027 with an artist reception on 29 October from 5:30 to 7:30 pm. All work is insured by the Center, artists keep 70% of any sale, and accepted artists agree to help with installation and promotion.
