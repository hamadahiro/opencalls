No! Wahala Magazine Issue 5: Interconnectedness

Prize Publication

One of Africa's first contemporary photography magazines — a non-profit founded in 2020 by Anne and KC Nwakalor, dedicated to African photographers telling African stories on African terms. Issue 5 invites African photographers, on the continent and across its diasporas, to explore 'Interconnectedness': Pan-Africanism and shared histories, community and collective life, our relationship to land and environment, mind, body, and place. Documentary, conceptual, and experimental approaches all welcome. Submit a series of 10–15 individually captioned images with a written narrative as a Story on Visura. Window runs 1 July – 31 August 2026 (11pm GMT). AI-generated images are not accepted.
