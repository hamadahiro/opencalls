Tomorrow Will Be Yesterday

Prize Exhibition

A photo slam presented by the German Photographic Society (DGPh) — a non-profit founded in 1951 with around 1,200 members — at Fotografiska Berlin to mark the society's 75th anniversary. Photographers worldwide, aged 18 and over, submit an existing photographic series of 10–20 images on the theme 'Tomorrow Will Be Yesterday': work that reads the present as raw material for tomorrow's memory — transition, emergence, transformation. Submissions include a short project description (max 600 characters) and a brief artist bio; files as JPEG, sRGB, 3000 px on the long side. Selected works are shown as a sequenced slideshow on the large LED wall at Fotografiska Berlin on 19 September 2026, and in an online slideshow the following day. Non-competitive — there is no cash prize. €15 per project; DGPh members submit free of charge via voucher code.
