Gathered VII: Georgia Artists Selecting Georgia Artists

Prizes Up to $5,000 in awards Exhibition

The seventh instalment of MOCA GA's biennial exhibition built on a single idea: Georgia artists choose Georgia artists. The museum describes the project as the one in which “the MOCA GA mission is fully realized … as artists are involved in all phases of the exhibition, including selection and review”, and the series has run since 2006 under three successive names — Within State Lines (2006, 2009), Georgia Artists Selecting Georgia Artists (2013, 2015) and, since then, Gathered. This round is the first to be staged in MOCA GA's new building by Mack Scogin Merrill Elam Architects. The jury is three Georgia artists named on the call, each with a bio on the museum's own jurors page: Hannah Israel, gallery director and professor of art at Columbus State University; Craig Drennen, a painter and 2018 Guggenheim fellow who teaches at Georgia State University; and Kelly Taylor Mitchell, an artist and educator working between Atlanta and San Juan. Photography is part of what this series selects on the museum's own account of the last round — the 2023 jurors “selected a diverse spectrum of artwork including sculpture, painting, photography, textiles and new media”, a show of 46 artists — and the call itself names no medium restriction at all: artists submit three recent works, each produced within the last three years and all of which must stay available for the full run, since a juror may take one piece or several. Images go as jpeg or png under 4 MB and no more than 4800 px on the longest side, new media (video, animation, motion graphics) as mov or mp4 under 25 MB or as a link, with a bio of up to 150 words and a statement of 50–100 words. Work must arrive installation-ready, in what the museum asks be professional-quality frames or mounting. Entry is $35 and up to $5,000 in cash awards is given out. Eligibility is strictly residential: home and studio address both within Georgia, and 18 or over. MOCA GA provides installation staff, pedestals, shelving, projectors and monitors, interpretive materials, press and social marketing, and a public opening reception; artists deliver and collect their own work and sign a museum loan form. Submissions close at midnight on November 1, 2026, selections are notified by December 11, work is dropped off January 7–9 and the exhibition runs January 23 – March 27, 2027, opening with a reception on the 23rd.
