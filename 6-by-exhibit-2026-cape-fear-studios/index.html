6 by Exhibit 2026

Prizes $450 / $200 / $100 Exhibition

A small-works call with one governing rule: one dimension of each piece must measure six inches, before framing. Cape Fear Studios in downtown Fayetteville opens it to visual artists in any medium, photography included, with up to three original works per artist — no prints or mass-produced work — and a minimum age of 18. The juror is Silvana Foti, previously executive director of the David McCune International Art Gallery at Methodist University, where she was instrumental in the gallery's opening and the founding of its advisory board. She holds an MFA in printmaking and ceramic sculpture from the Art Institute of Chicago and the University of Notre Dame and a BFA from St Mary's College, and has curated regional, national and international exhibitions including Warhol, Chagall, The Story of the Exodus; Picasso: 25 Years of Edition Ceramics; Rodin: Portraits of a Lifetime; and Impressionism to Modernism: Masterworks of Early Photography, all meeting American Alliance of Museums standards. She taught at college level for more than twenty years and chaired Methodist University's Department of Art. Awards are First Place $450, Second $200, Third $100 and two Honorable Mentions of $50 each. Entries close 10 August 2026 through CaFÉ and the exhibition runs 25 September to 24 November 2026 in the main gallery.
