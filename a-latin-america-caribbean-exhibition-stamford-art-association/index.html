A Latin America & Caribbean Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

The Stamford Art Association's second annual Latin America and Caribbean show, presented at the Townhouse Gallery in Stamford and online on the SAA website. The theme is open to all media and all interpretations, and the association is explicit that you do not need to be of Latin American or Caribbean descent to enter: submissions are open to anyone whose work engages with or is inspired by these regions and themes. Up to nine works may be submitted. The returning juror is Valeria Ochoa, SAA's gallery manager and board secretary, a literature and language educator with an MA in linguistics and an MA in curriculum and instruction who works in Norwalk Public Schools; a first-generation Colombian, she writes and photographs around ancestry, roots and lineage and their ties to land and belonging. Work must be original and not previously shown at the SAA gallery, no larger than 48 by 60 inches framed, wired for hanging and hand delivered — there is no shipping option, with receiving on September 18 and 19 and pickup on October 23 and 24. Photographs are exempt from the rule requiring prints to be signed and numbered as a limited edition; giclees are not accepted. The gallery takes 30 percent commission on work sold. The show runs September 24 to October 22, 2026, opening Thursday September 24, 5:30 to 7 PM.
