Pure Macro & Close-Up Photography Awards 2027

Pure Photography Awards' macro and close-up award, open worldwide and deliberately pitched at anyone who has looked closer — "You don't need to be a professional photographer to enter. You don't even need to call yourself a photographer." Insects, flowers, plants, textures, patterns, water, everyday objects and the smallest details of the natural world are all in scope, shot with a dedicated macro rig, a standard camera or a phone; a macro lens is not required. The Macro & Close-Up Photographer of the Year receives a US$500 cash prize and the official trophy, and winners, placed photographers and finalists are exhibited at the Pure Photography Awards exhibition in India, interviewed on the organisation's own site and carried through its press channels. Judging is by Marit van Ekelenburg, an OM SYSTEM Ambassador and Dutch macro photographer. Two rules carry weight here: AI-generated or AI-altered photographs are not accepted, though normal editing and focus stacking are allowed, and any photograph made by killing, harming, drugging, freezing, restraining or deliberately distressing an animal or insect is strictly refused. RAW or supporting files may be requested to verify any shortlisted or winning photograph. Copyright remains entirely with the entrant, and no further fees are charged after submission or selection.
