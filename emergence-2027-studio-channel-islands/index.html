Emergence 2027

Studio Channel Islands Art Center's annual exhibition for students and recent graduates tied to Ventura County, California, running February 6–26, 2027 in the Camarillo gallery. The visual-art application is open to media of every kind — the page states "Open to all media" — and asks for up to six pieces. Eligibility runs on two legs: residents of Ventura County who are currently enrolled at a college or university or graduated from one in the past five years, and non-residents who are currently enrolled at, or graduated in the past five years from, a Ventura County college or university. The jury for the Thomas and Patricia Dodds Award for Excellence picks three winners from the student work — $1,000 for first place and $500 for each of two runners-up — and only artists currently enrolled at a college or university located in Ventura County are eligible for those; a further $1,000 award goes to Best in Show. There is no entry fee. Accepted work must be delivered ready to hang by February 2, 2027 and stay in the gallery until the exhibition closes; sales are split 60% to the artist and 40% to the art center, and the artist sets the price inclusive of that commission. The wider Emergence programme spans visual arts, poetry, film, music and dance, with separate applications on the same page for poetry (also due January 17) and for musicians (due January 6, 2027), and winners of the Dodds award are announced during the February 6 opening reception.
