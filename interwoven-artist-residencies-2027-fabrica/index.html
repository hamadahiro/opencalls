Interwoven Artist Residencies 2027

Prize Residency

Two month-long residencies across three partner organisations — Cow House Studios in Co. Wexford, Ireland (3–30 May 2027), Le Shed / Association Cybèle in Normandy, France (10 May–6 June 2027), and Fabrica in Brighton, UK (3–30 July 2027) — for contemporary visual artists willing to take their practice across borders. Interwoven starts from the textile legacies of the Norman era, the Bayeux Tapestry and the Sicilian Tristan quilts, read as evidence of inter-regional blending of technique, material, design and storytelling "through the creative skill of the female hand", and asks not for illustration of the past but for work that engages those European histories in ways relevant to now. The partners say they are particularly interested in artists bringing feminist, social or decolonial approaches. Each selected artist receives accommodation and travel costs, desk space plus room to produce or test work, support from the host organisation, and a connection into the project's cross-border network of artists and organisations; the open call document names no fee, bursary or production budget. Eligibility is geographic: the open call document limits the residencies to "visual artists based in France, the United Kingdom, Ireland, the Channel Islands, Flanders, Southern Italy (specifically Sicily, Puglia, Campania, Calabria), Norway and Denmark, with an interest in textile history, feminist histories, and/or with a textile-based practice". Applicants cannot apply to the residency in the country where they live, and must be able to stay up to 28 consecutive days. The call itself enumerates no media; photography is named in the Irish host's own provision - Cow House Studios lists a black-and-white darkroom printing 35mm up to 4x5in, film and digital cameras, studio lights and darkroom chemistry among its facilities, and its own open residency call names photographers among the practitioners encouraged to apply. The application is an expression of interest of up to 500 words about how the opportunity meets your practice rather than a defined project, a two-page statement and CV, and a five-page portfolio. Shortlisted artists are interviewed on 22 January 2027 and selections are announced by 5 February. The project forms part of the European Year of the Normans, initiated by the Région Normandie.
