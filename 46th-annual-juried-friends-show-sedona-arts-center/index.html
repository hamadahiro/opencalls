46th Annual Juried Friends Show

Prizes $500 / $250 / $150 Exhibition

Sedona Arts Center's Juried Friends Show and Sale reaches its 46th year in 2027, hanging at the centre's gallery at 15 Art Barn Road in Uptown Sedona from March 5 to April 18, 2027, with an opening reception on March 5, 4–6pm. The theme this year is The Face of Friendship. It is open to artists of all levels working in all mediums — the centre's own exhibition listing names painting, sculpture, photography and digital media — but it is gated by support rather than by residency: every applicant must be a current Sedona Arts Center donor at the Friend level, which starts at $70, at the time of applying. On top of that the entry fee is $40 for one submission of up to three JPEGs at a minimum 300 DPI, cropped to the artwork without mat or frame, with title, dimensions, medium and price listed on the entry form. Artists must be 18 or older, the work must have been made within the last three years, and it cannot have been shown in any previous Sedona Arts Center exhibition. Awards are First Place/Best of Show $500, Second $250 and Third $150, with honourable mentions at the jurors' discretion and a People's Choice award announced at the end of the run. Notifications go out by February 12, 2027 and accepted work must reach Sedona, shipped or hand-delivered, by March 3. Everything in the show must be for sale at a price the artist sets, and the centre retains 40% commission, paying out in the month following a sale. Shipping runs at the artist's expense in both directions: artists pay to get work to Sedona and must supply a USPS, FedEx or UPS prepaid label for the return of unsold work. One eligibility caveat to settle before paying the $40 and the $70 donation: the CaFE record for this call states only '18 years and older', while the centre's own Call to Artists page states eligibility for its juried exhibitions as 'U.S. artists, 18+' — the two surfaces disagree and the broader one is the call's own application document, so non-US photographers should confirm with the centre first. Selected artists are asked to help promote the exhibition; the centre states plainly that it cannot guarantee any particular level of visibility despite its own press, mailing and social-media promotion. Sedona Arts Center has been a creative home in Sedona since 1958 — more than 68 years, by its own count — and its gallery, in its own words, 'represents over 100 professional local and regional artists'; its 2026–27 programme that also includes the Vision and Sound African American experience exhibit, the First Peoples Exhibit and the Big Gay Art Show.
