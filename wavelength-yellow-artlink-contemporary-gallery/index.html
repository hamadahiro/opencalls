Wavelength: Yellow

Prize Exhibition

Wavelength is Artlink's colour-specific open call exploring the physical spectrum of visible colour, changing hue with each iteration; this edition is devoted entirely to yellow. Work need not be only yellow but must have a primarily yellow focus, and photography is named explicitly among the welcome media alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramic, installation, video and fibre. Juried by Ellen Mensch, the Fort Wayne artist, curator and educator who has run Artlink's exhibitions and education programming for more than six years. Artlink is a nonprofit contemporary gallery in the Auer Center for Arts & Culture in downtown Fort Wayne. Open to artists 18 and over residing in the United States at $12 per entry; accepted work must arrive ready for installation, shipped work framed with plexiglass rather than glass, and a 35% sales commission applies. Exhibition 22 October – 15 November 2026, accepted work due 11 October.
