Midwest Regional 2027

Artlink's annual juried group exhibition for artists living in the American Midwest, open to any medium and any size and shown across the gallery's main space. Artlink is a 501(c)3 nonprofit contemporary gallery in the Auer Center for Arts & Culture in downtown Fort Wayne, funded in part by Arts United and the Indiana Arts Commission. Eligible states are Indiana, Ohio, Michigan, Illinois, Wisconsin, Minnesota, Iowa, Missouri, North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska and Kansas; entrants must be 18 or over, and work must have been made in the last five years and not shown at Artlink before. Reproductions are not accepted. Entry is $12 per work with a limit of ten entries, and the juror gives three cash awards for first, second and third place — Artlink does not publish the amounts. Accepted work must arrive ready to install, framed pieces included, and anything damaged or differing from what was accepted may be turned away. A 35% sales fee applies to work sold, with artists paid by cheque after the show closes; work is insured while at the gallery but not in transit. The exhibition runs 7 January to 7 February 2027, opening with a reception on 7 January from 5 to 8pm.
