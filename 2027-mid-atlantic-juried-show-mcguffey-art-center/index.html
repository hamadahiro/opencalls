2027 Mid-Atlantic Juried Show

Prize Exhibition

The third annual Mid-Atlantic Juried Show at McGuffey Art Center, one of the oldest artist-run cooperative art centres in the United States, housed in a historic elementary school building in downtown Charlottesville, Virginia. The show runs April 2 to May 1, 2027, with a First Friday opening reception on April 2 from 5:30 to 7:30 pm and a panel discussion on the closing day. Any artist aged 18 or over resident in Virginia, West Virginia, Maryland, Washington DC or North Carolina may enter; photography is one of the seven named categories, alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, fibre, sculpture and mixed media. Work must be original and made within the last three years, and AI-enhanced or AI-generated art and photography is prohibited from entry, as is work copied from other artists or from published material. Two-dimensional entries may not exceed eight feet in any direction and nothing requiring the artist to be present for moving or assembly is accepted. Entry is $35 for up to three submissions, reduced to $25 for McGuffey Art Center Association members via a coupon code from the front office, and only one piece per artist is taken into the exhibition. Accepted artists pay a $10 handling fee and arrange delivery themselves — hand-delivered March 25–27 or shipped prepaid March 22–26, in reusable containers with a return label — and the centre states it does not insure work in transit or on exhibit. Work is for sale unless marked otherwise, with the artist keeping 65% and McGuffey 35%. The 2027 juror is Jack Rasmussen, Director and Curator of the American University Museum at the Katzen Arts Center in Washington DC since it opened in 2005, previously Executive Director of Maryland Art Place and of di Rosa, and a past president of the Maryland State Arts Council; he judges on artistic voice and vision and on technical execution, and his decisions are final. Artists are notified on February 15, 2027.
