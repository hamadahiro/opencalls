Small Wonders 2026

Prizes $1,000 Exhibition

The 22nd edition of Maryland Federation of Art's annual small-works exhibition, for artists who dream big but work small. Everything must fit under 11 inches in all directions including the frame and display, but there is no limit on creativity or media. MFA exhibitions are blind juried — names and identifying information are hidden from the juror — and this year's selections are made by Abby Daggett, Gallery Director and Marketing Director at Maine Art Hill in Kennebunk, whose background spans commercial galleries and museums in Boston, Venice and Italy. Selected work is shown at MFA's Circle Gallery in Annapolis and its new second location on Maryland Avenue from 25 November to 22 December 2026, with a virtual awards ceremony on 30 November, an opening reception on 3 December and two further Midnight Madness receptions. The juror presents cash awards totalling $1,000. Entry is open to artists residing in any state or territory of the US, Canada or Mexico; up to 6 original works made in the past three years may be entered, and work previously shown at Circle Gallery is not eligible. Artists are notified 29 October and keep 70% of sales (75% for MFA members). Founded in 1963, MFA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit supported by the Maryland State Arts Council.
