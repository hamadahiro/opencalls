Call for Exhibitions — Spring 2028

Prizes Exhibition Artist fee

Photographic Gallery Hippolyte in Helsinki, run by the Association of Photographic Artists, is taking applications for its Spring 2028 exhibition season. The call is open to all forms of contemporary art, but photography carries the programme: proposals are expected to connect to photographic art or the extended field of photography more than superficially, and the application text must spell that connection out when it is not obvious. Professional practitioners, artists, artist groups and curators may all apply with a ready-made exhibition concept, and the gallery states plainly that applicants need not be based in Finland or be members of the association, and that it welcomes both artists at the start of a career and long-established ones. There are two spaces - the gallery itself at about 72 square metres and Hippolyte Studio at about 13 - and one application may request the gallery, the studio or the whole space. Exhibition slots run January to June 2028 and last about five weeks including installation, deinstallation and opening. Applying is free, and since 2025 the exhibition space has been rent-free for every artist, with the association also paying an exhibition fee; it is explicit that rent-free status is decided annually on funding, and that the maximum rents that would otherwise apply are 650 euro for the gallery, 280 for the studio and 930 for both for association members, and 1,000, 280 and 1,280 for non-members. Rent, when charged, covers invigilation, visitor tracking, staff support with planning and installation, the opening reception, documentation, press and publicity. The exhibitor installs the work and pays for transport, insurance, travel and any AV equipment, which the gallery does not own, and the association takes a 30 percent commission on works and associated material sold, with selected works also carried in its online shop for up to three months after the show. Applications go through an online form linked from the gallery's Apply for an exhibition page, as a single PDF of no more than ten pages and 5 MB holding a one-page exhibition plan, up to ten supporting images and a condensed CV; each applicant can submit only once, nothing is accepted by email or post, and late applications are not considered. A three-to-five-member exhibition committee drawn from the association, plus two visiting assessors including one expert from outside it, peer-review every application, and all applicants hear personally, by email or text message, before Midsummer. The gallery hopes to programme work not previously shown in the Helsinki metropolitan area, and a selected artist agrees to hold no solo show of the same content there in the twelve months before the exhibition or during it. An artist may be granted a new Hippolyte slot once five years have passed since their previous one. The call opens twice a year, in April and October, about eighteen months ahead of the season; this round runs 1-31 October 2026, and the gallery receives around 250 applications a year.
