SCAN Full Contact 2026

Prizes 2027 solo exhibition prize €900 prize

First edition of Full Contact, a professional photography meeting organised by SCAN, the international photography festival hosted by Tarragona City Council. Twelve photographers are selected for a portfolio-review and networking day at Teatret del Port in El Serrallo (Tarragona) on 17 October 2026, with accommodation, meals, and curatorial conversations covered. One project is selected as the Full Contact Award winner and receives a solo exhibition during SCAN's 2027 official programme along with a €900 fee (incl. tax) for exhibition and communication rights. Open to photographers of legal age residing in any country of the European Union or the United Kingdom.

DeadlineMay 10, 2026
Entry feeFree
Eligibility18+, Europe only, united-kingdom
LocationTarragona, Spain
RequirementsUp to 16 images
