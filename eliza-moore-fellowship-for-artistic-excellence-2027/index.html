Eliza Moore Fellowship for Artistic Excellence 2027

Annual fellowship for one outstanding early-career artist developing new works that address plants, gardens, or landscapes in the broad sense. Open to visual artists (including photographers), literary artists, dancers, and musicians. Award includes a $10,000 individual grant and a required 2–5 week residency at the Oak Spring Garden Foundation's 700-acre estate in Upperville, Virginia, with access to staff, the rare book library (over 19,000 botanical art objects), and the working landscape. Eligible applicants must be early-career artists with some recognition through exhibitions, commissions, performances, grants, residencies, or publications, and not enrolled in an undergraduate program. Established artists with extensive solo exhibitions or published books are ineligible. 2027 residency start dates: March 1, April 25, May 24, July 12, August 29, or October 4. Application portal opens February 14, 2026.
