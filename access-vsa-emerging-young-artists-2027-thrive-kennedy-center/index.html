Access/VSA Emerging Young Artists Program 2027: Thrive

Prizes $3,000 stipend Exhibition

Thrive is the 2027 edition of the Kennedy Center's Access/VSA Emerging Young Artists Program, a Jean Kennedy Smith Arts and Disability Program that runs as a juried career development programme and exhibition for visual artists with disabilities aged 16 to 25 who live in the United States. The theme asks disabled artists to consider how their life and their art are flourishing: how the work is evolving and changing, and how the person is developing and growing. Fifteen artists are selected by a jury of industry professionals; each receives a $3,000 stipend and has one of their submitted pieces included in an exhibit in Washington, D.C. Winners are required to attend three virtual workshops (3 April, 1 May and 5 June 2027) and an all-expenses-paid series of career development workshops in Washington from 14 to 18 June 2027. Entry is free. All visual art forms and media are eligible if they meet the handling rules — 2D work no larger than 60 inches in height or width, 3D no larger than 48 inches in any direction when packed, nothing perishable — and work must have been completed in the last three years and after the onset of disability. Applicants submit images or video of at least three different pieces, and are told to show a portfolio rather than several views of one work, alongside four 300-word answers on themselves, their process, the perspective they would bring and their future plans. Previous programme winners cannot reapply, group projects are not considered, and artwork created with generative AI is ineligible. Selected applicants are asked to document their disability, age and right to work in the US before the final round, and must be willing to publicly disclose that they have a disability in all materials and public appearances associated with the programme. Accommodations and alternate application formats can be requested until 30 October 2026. The submission portal opens on 24 August.
