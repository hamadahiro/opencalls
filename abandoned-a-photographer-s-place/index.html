Abandoned

Prize Exhibition

Themed call exploring abandonment — physical, emotional, environmental, social, or symbolic — through images of absence, decay, isolation, memory, transition, and what remains. Both traditional and experimental photographic processes welcome. Juror: Marty Evans. Exhibition July 8–31, 2026 at A Photographer's Place in Raleigh, NC, with an opening reception July 10, 7–9 PM. Selected artists may optionally have work printed in-house on the gallery's Canon ImagePROGRAF Pro-1100.
