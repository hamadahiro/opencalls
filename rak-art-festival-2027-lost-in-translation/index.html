RAK Art Festival 2027 — Lost in Translation

Prizes AED 12,000 grand prize prize Category awards prize

14th edition of the Ras Al Khaimah Art Festival, organized by the Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi Foundation for Policy Research at the Al Jazeera Al Hamra Heritage Village in the UAE. Theme: Lost in Translation — exploring displacement, migration, cultural adaptation, and the search for belonging across linguistic and cultural boundaries. Open to international artists across four categories: Emerging Artists, Established Artists, Filmmakers, and Students (ages 6–19). All nationalities welcome. Photography eligible alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, installation, video art, and short film. Submit up to four artworks across two mediums maximum, with a 100-word artist statement and 200-word biography. Grand Jury Prize: AED 12,000 + trophy. Photography category prizes: AED 6,000 (1st) and AED 4,000 (2nd). Notifications: July 30, 2026; festival opens January 22, 2027.
