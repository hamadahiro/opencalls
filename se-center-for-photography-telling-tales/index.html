SE Center for Photography — Telling Tales

A single photograph can carry a whole story, and the SE Center is looking for the images that manage it unaided — pictures that stand on their own without a supporting sequence, the signature frame or motif that ends up standing for an artist or a project. Analog and digital manipulation in all its forms is welcome, monochrome or colour, any subject, analog, digital or antique processes, and photographers of every skill level and location may enter. The juror is Brian Clamp, owner and director of ClampArt in Chelsea, New York, a gallery specialising in modern and contemporary art with an emphasis on photography that mounts ten to fifteen shows a year of emerging and mid-career artists; Clamp took a master's in critical studies in modern art at Columbia and has curated photography shows and sat on portfolio review panels across the United States. Between twenty-five and thirty-five images hang in the SE Center's virtual gallery for about a month, with the selection also carried across the Center's social channels, an archived online slideshow and a video walkthrough of the exhibition. Entry is $35 for up to five images and $6 for each additional image to a maximum of fifteen, with member rates of $28 and $5. Work exhibited at the SE Center within the previous two years is not eligible. The Center takes no commission — the artist keeps 100 percent of any sale. The exhibition runs 15 November to 31 December 2026. The prospectus Calendar closes submissions on 15 October 2026, but the Smarter Entry portal that takes the entry was still counting "3 days left" on that date, so a late entrant should try the portal rather than assume it has shut.
