BEERS London Group Exhibition 2026

Prize Group exhibition at BEERS London prize

BEERS London is a contemporary commercial gallery in the City of London that runs an annual open call for a curated group exhibition. The 2026 cycle selects approximately 12 artists working across all media — painting, sculpture, photography, video, mixed media — for a show in October 2026 (autumn).

Selected artists consign their work for the duration of the exhibition plus up to three months afterward, during which the gallery actively promotes the work to its collector base. Sales split commission 50/50. Insurance is provided by the gallery during the exhibition window.

The application is structured to allow combining tracks: the group exhibition application alone is £10, and the combined residency-plus-exhibition application is £25 (£5 less than applying separately). Selection is by the BEERS London team alongside guest curators. Eligibility is open globally to artists at any career stage.

Inclusion in the group exhibition does not guarantee gallery representation or sales — the format is closer to a curated open submission than to a representation pitch. Selected artists are notified approximately two months before the exhibition opening. The deadline has been extended to August 1, 2026.
