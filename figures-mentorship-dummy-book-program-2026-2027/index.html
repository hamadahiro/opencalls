FIGURES Mentorship & Dummy Book Program 2026/2027

FIGURES, the Slow & Contemporary Photography platform behind the FIG001 book, is running its mentorship and dummy book programme for a second year, and is looking specifically for projects at the very earliest stage of creation — you do not need to show a single finished picture of the project to apply, and projects that are already too advanced will not be considered. Program winners get twelve monthly 1.5-hour one-to-one online sessions with founder and editor-in-chief Nicolas Blanchadell, covering every phase from deepening the initial concept through production, picture editing and sequencing; a renowned guest artist/curator joins for one mid-programme session, and a professional art-book graphic designer steps in at the end to finalise the cover and the more demanding pages. Each winner leaves with a printed dummy book plus the ready-to-print PDF and all supporting material. The number of selections scales with entries: for every 150 valid submissions FIGURES picks one program winner plus ten further projects for a one-hour individual review. All photographic practices are welcome — documentary, portrait, street, experimental, fashion — as are mixed-media projects where photography is central to the process or makes up at least half the final work. Entry is €10, or free with the discounted purchase of the FIG001 issue at €25; no further fees are asked of selected artists, and applicants facing financial hardship are invited to write to FIGURES directly. If fewer than 150 valid applications arrive, the programme is cancelled and €10 entrants are refunded in full.
