Freezer Festival 2026 — VJ Books

Prizes Festival exhibition Performance slot

Freezer Festival Internacional de Fotografía — now in its third edition, curated by photographer and teacher Luciana Demichelis — is looking for photographers and musicians to build a VJ book for its November 2026 programme. A VJ book, also called a bookjockey, is an experimental performance device: a video camera pointed at a table of objects, photographs, fanzines and photobooks, projected live, with a music set played alongside it. The festival has staged three of them so far — Federico Paladino with songs by Seba Rulli at Casa Unclan, curator Luciana Demichelis for the CEF Córdoba visit to the Centro de Arte UNLP, and photographer Amparo Fernández with photographs intervened live. This is the first year it is opened as a call. You can apply as a solo VJ (making the visuals and playing the music set yourself) or as a duo (one on live visuals, one on music). The festival supplies a webcam connected to a projector, a tripod and sound; the application form asks what else you need. Freezer runs from Thursday 5 to Sunday 15 November 2026 across La Plata, Berisso, Ensenada and Buenos Aires, with the Centro de Arte UNLP and the Edificio Karakachoff as its main venues, alongside photographic exhibitions, photobook presentations, open talks, workshops and photographic brigades. Proposals submitted here are also considered for the festival's presentations and curatorial projects through 2027. There is no entry fee. Selected artists are confirmed by email and on the festival's social channels. Applications close 31 October 2026 at 23:59.
