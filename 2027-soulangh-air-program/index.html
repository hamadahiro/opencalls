2027 Soulangh AiR Program

Prizes Residency NT$72,000 living grant NT$150,000 production budget

Soulangh Cultural Park is a Tainan City Government cultural site built into the former Jiali Sugar Factory, founded in 1906 as the Meiji Sugar Corporation's first modern refinery. The plant closed in 1995, the county leased the land in 2003, and the renovated warehouses opened as a cultural park in 2005. The artist village has run an international residency since 2013.

The 2027 theme is sugar, salt and local cultural heritage. Jiali District is the geographical centre of Tainan's Saline Land and was an essential place of habitation for the Siraya people; the surrounding area is rich in Taoist temples and centipede parades. Artists are encouraged to use Tainan's cultural assets and to incorporate the park's own environment into outdoor work.

The programme is open to all artistic disciplines and to artists worldwide. Applicants need basic communication skills in English or Chinese and must submit a residency project plan built on the theme, a CV and at least one recommendation letter with a handwritten signature. Applications are accepted online only, through the AIR in Tainan portal run by the Tainan City Government Cultural Affairs Bureau.

Selected artists receive an accommodation suite (room and utilities free; personal items such as towels and toothbrushes are not provided), a bicycle, an exhibition space with lighting rails, wooden partitions and air conditioning, a shared studio, and free access to multimedia equipment and hardware tools. Living expenses are granted at up to NT$800 per day for up to 90 days (NT$72,000) and exhibition production at up to NT$200 per day for up to 90 days (NT$18,000), both subject to 6-20 per cent withholding tax for foreigners, plus a separate NT$150,000 production budget for materials, woodwork, printing, 500 flyers, translation, insurance and an opening press conference. A stamp duty of 0.1 per cent of the grant is paid by the artist in cash and is not covered.

Each resident must hold one achievement exhibition and at least one parent-child workshop or open studio day at the park, complete a residency report and questionnaire, and take part in the exchange activities the organisers arrange. Copyright stays with the creator; Soulangh retains the right to exhibit and publish the work for educational and promotional purposes. Artists who do not reside for at least two-thirds of their contracted term are disqualified.

The residency period runs 1 March to 30 October 2027 (tentative) and applicants choose three preferred terms in the application system; opening and exhibition dates are agreed after selection. The review period is September 2026 and results are announced in November 2026 on the websites of both the Cultural Affairs Bureau and Soulangh Cultural Park. The deadline was extended from 31 August to 10 September 2026.
