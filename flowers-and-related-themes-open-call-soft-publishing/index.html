Flowers and Related Themes Open Call

Soft Publishing, the Treviso-based independent publishing house producing contemporary photography books in limited editions, invites work on flowers read as broadly as possible. The organiser's own examples run from a garden or a living room to fresh-picked or withered stems, flowers painted or printed on a dress, a flower-shaped vase, a chair whose shape evokes a blossom, a bloom held by a person or a cat playing in a flower-filled meadow — offered as inspiration rather than a list to follow literally. The call states that flowers have long been associated with femininity and asks artists to move beyond that convention and approach the theme from as many perspectives as possible. Photography is the first of eleven media the entry form lets an artist declare, alongside drawing and painting, video, collage, embroidery, sculpture, patchwork, paper art, clay, set design and design, with a free-text field for anything else; if the submissions are diverse enough the publisher says it would like to structure the book into chapters by interpretation. Up to ten images may be submitted through the Tally form, with higher-resolution files requested if the work is selected, plus a biography of at most 20 words, a project description of at most 50 words written in the third person, and a caption list matching the number of images uploaded. The entry carries a €7 submission fee, which the publisher says supports the project and the production of the publication. Finalists are selected by 15 March 2027.
