Experimental Photo Festival 2027 — Exhibition Open Call

Prizes €150 artist fee Solo exhibition (Barcelona) Publication

There is no theme and no single definition of experimental photography here: the jury weighs artistic strength, experimental approach, originality and conceptual clarity, the project's potential to become an engaging exhibition, and the artist's openness to dialogue and adaptation. The festival is explicit that a proposal does not need to arrive resolved — scale, materials, installation and the relationship to the assigned venue are developed afterwards with the curatorial and production teams.

One €20 application is considered for all three routes at once, so there is nothing to submit twice: twelve solo exhibitions, a Group Exhibition Mention with its own €600 shared production budget and a run that lasts until December 2027, and an online feature for twenty artists in Analog Forever Magazine. The magazine selection carries no fee, production budget, diploma or festival access.

Read the money in both directions before applying. The €150 artist fee is real cash but needs a valid invoice; the €200 production budget is administered by the festival, is never handed over as cash and covers only pre-approved expenses; the €999 Ágora Diploma is an in-kind education benefit that cannot be exchanged for money. Against that, selected artists must travel to Barcelona for installation, the opening, the festival and dismantling, must install and dismantle their own exhibition with the team's help, and pay their own accommodation — the Col·legi Major Penyafort-Montserrat booking is priority access, not a free room.

The licence is broad and worth reading: submitting grants the Association a worldwide, irrevocable, perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use the submitted images, texts and materials for promotional, exhibition, educational and commercial purposes connected with the festival, IPFA and Ágora, with credit where reasonably possible and no further payment. Artists keep copyright and ownership, and work shown in the festival is not offered for sale during the event.

Neither the festival's own call page nor its Picter prospectus and terms state any policy on AI-generated or AI-assisted work.
