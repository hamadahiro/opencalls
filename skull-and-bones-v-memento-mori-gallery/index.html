Skull & Bones V

Prize Exhibition

The fifth edition of Memento Mori's annual group show for all things skeletal. The gallery is a lowbrow/outsider contemporary space and tattoo studio in Lakewood's 40West Arts District, on the First Friday art-walk circuit. Open nationally to 2D and 3D work; photographic work is welcome but must be printed and framed or mounted and arrive ready to hang from a hook or nail, and the gallery will not accept photographs depicting another artist's work, street art included, without permission. Up to two pieces at $30 each. All work must be for sale with 40% of the sale going to the gallery, and out-of-state artists are responsible for shipping in both directions. Drop-off 26 September, show 2–24 October 2026, First Friday reception 2 October.
