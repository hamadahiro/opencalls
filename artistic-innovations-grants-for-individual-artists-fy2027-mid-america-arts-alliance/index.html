Artistic Innovations Grants for Individual Artists FY2027

Prize Up to $15,000

Grants of up to $15,000 for individual artists to make and present new, original work — and, as of the August 2026 cycle, with no cost share or match required, a change funded by Mid-America Arts Alliance's reinvestment of a gift from Yield Giving. Photography is listed as a discipline in its own right in the eligibility guidelines, separate from the Visual Arts category. Applicants must be at least 21, be a US citizen, permanent resident or hold non-resident status, and be a full-time resident of the six-state M-AAA region — Arkansas, Kansas, Missouri, Nebraska, Oklahoma, Texas, and the Native Nations in this geography — both before the deadline and throughout the grant period. Projects must introduce a new original idea or artwork at any stage of development and include at least two public-facing engagements in the region between January 1 and June 30, 2027: a feature or premiere presentation of at least 60 minutes, and an outreach or educational activity of at least 30 minutes with direct artist-audience interaction. Applications are adjudicated October 1 to November 30 by a panel of arts professionals scoring against criteria adapted from the National Endowment for the Arts' Artistic Merit and Artistic Excellence rubrics. M-AAA intends to award at least one individual artist grant per state, with no fewer than 18 grantees in the FY2027 cycle. Applicants new to M-AAA are explicitly encouraged, as are artists serving underserved communities. The deadline is 5:00 pm CT on September 24, 2026; selections are notified December 18, 2026 and announced publicly in January 2027.
