Blossom

Blossom is a thematic juried exhibition at Midwest Center for Photography (MWCP), the photography gallery in Wichita, Kansas. The 2026 edition runs June 5–22, 2026.

The brief is broad inside its category: photographs of flowers and plants across all approaches — nature photography, macro, still life, and any other treatment of botanical subjects. All photographic media and stylistic registers are welcome.

Selected artists' work is physically printed and framed by MWCP for gallery display — production costs are absorbed by the gallery rather than passed to the photographer.

Eligibility is open internationally. Submission is by email with 5+ images. Fee is $50 for 5 images. The 2026 deadline was May 1.
