Lay of the Land: A Focus on Nature

Artlink's juried group exhibition of work exploring the natural world, open to any artist 18 or over living in the United States. The gallery asks for artworks that engage the natural world through subject matter or materials — realistic depictions of flora and fauna, abstract interpretations of geological textures, or, in its own phrase, “the complex, evolving dialogue between humanity and the environment.” The call is explicit that it is open to all media, and it names photography and video alongside painting, ceramics, sculpture, printmaking, installation, fiber, assemblage and drawing. There is no size limit. Entry is $12 per work with a limit of ten entries, submitted online only; jury notifications go out after November 16, 2026, accepted work is delivered by December 13, 2026, and the show runs January 7 to February 7, 2027 at the gallery. Artlink is a 501(c)3 nonprofit contemporary gallery in the Auer Center for Arts & Culture in downtown Fort Wayne, funded in part by Arts United and the Indiana Arts Commission. Accepted work must arrive ready to install and professionally presented.
