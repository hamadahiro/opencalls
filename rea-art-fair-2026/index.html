ReA! Art Fair 2026

Prize €1,500

ReA! Art Fair is a Milan contemporary art fair focused on emerging artists. The 2026 edition runs October 22–25 at Fabbrica del Vapore, the city's converted industrial cultural complex.

Fifty artists are selected through the open call. The fair is multidisciplinary — all visual art is accepted, including photography. Selected artists exhibit without additional booth or production costs beyond the application fee.

Eligibility is restricted to emerging artists. The €1,500 prize is awarded to one of the selected artists. The 2026 jury is five curators: Milena Zanetti, Valeria Conti, Vittoria Martinotti, Erica Massaccesi, and Maria Myasnikova.

Application is via the fair's website with a portfolio PDF. The fee is €55. The 2026 deadline was May 6; results announced June 15, 2026.
