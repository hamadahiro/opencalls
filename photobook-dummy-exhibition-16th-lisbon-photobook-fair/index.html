Photobook Dummy Exhibition — 16th Lisbon Photobook Fair

Prize Exhibition

Open call for the photobook dummy exhibition of the 16th Feira do Livro de Fotografia de Lisboa, held November 27–29, 2026 at the Arquivo Municipal de Lisboa – Fotográfico. Dummies are displayed for consultation throughout the fair, where past editions (72 projects by 65 authors in 2026) have drawn the attention of attending publishers and booksellers — a real chance to move a book project forward. Pre-register with up to 6 photographs of the dummy (72 dpi, max 2 MB each) via the Google Form by October 12, 21:00 GMT; confirmations October 23, dummies delivered by November 16. Participation is free. The fair also runs parallel calls for self-publisher/small-press tables and presentations of authorial projects.
