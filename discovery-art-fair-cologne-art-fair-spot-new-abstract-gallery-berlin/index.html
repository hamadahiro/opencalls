Discovery Art Fair Cologne - Art Fair Spot

Prize Exhibition prize

New & Abstract Gallery is a Berlin-based gallery focused on abstract and contemporary art. The 2026 open call selects artists for an exclusive art fair spot at the Discovery Art Fair Cologne — work shown at the gallery's booth during the fair rather than as a stand-alone presence.

Eligibility is restricted to abstract and contemporary artists.

Submission is through the gallery's website. Fee is €20. The 2026 deadline was March 18; results announced March 27, 2026.
