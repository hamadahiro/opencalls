Josephine Ulrick and Win Schubert Photography Award 2026

Prizes A$25,000 acquisitive A$5,000 People's Choice

Queensland's richest acquisitive photographic prize returns to HOTA Gallery on the Gold Coast: A$25,000 and acquisition into the HOTA Collection for the winner, plus a A$5,000 People's Choice Award decided by public vote. Submissions are invited across the traditional and expanded medium, including photocollage, sculpture, projection, installation and cameraless photography. Finalists are chosen by a panel of 2018 award winner Tamara Dean, UNSW Galleries director José Da Silva and independent writer and curator Mikala Tai, and are shown in the award exhibition at HOTA Gallery from 28 November 2026 to 7 March 2027. Previous winners include Polly Borland, Merilyn Fairskye, Shaun Gladwell, Polixeni Papapetrou, Darren Sylvester and Justine Varga. Entrants must be 18 or over and Australian citizens or permanent residents; individuals and groups may enter, but nobody may appear in more than one submission. Entry is limited to one artwork, created on or after 11 May 2025 and no larger than 300 cm in any dimension including frame or mount, and work must be available for the full exhibition period. AI may be used provided the extent of that use is clearly described in the submission. The entry fee has been reduced to A$15, with A$10 Mob Tix for First Nations applicants. Finalists are notified by email or phone and have seven days to accept; the winner is announced at the exhibition opening.
