Fundación Enaire Photography Prize 2026

19th edition of Spain's prestigious photography prize in collaboration with PHotoESPAÑA. Open theme — submit original artistic photographs not previously awarded. Jury selects 30 works for a material phase (June 3–5), then picks three winners, 14 finalists, and a PHotoESPAÑA Special Mention. All selected works exhibited at Centro de Arte Naves de Gamazo, Santander, as part of PHotoESPAÑA 2026. Public vote prize via social media. Foundation covers 50% of production costs (max €500) for material phase.

Organized by Fundación Enaire
