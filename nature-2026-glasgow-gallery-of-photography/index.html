Nature 2026

Prizes Exhibition prize Publication prize

The Glasgow Gallery of Photography runs themed international juried exhibitions on a recurring schedule. Nature 2026 is the gallery's call for work engaging with how we interact with nature — including beauty, power, cruelty, and nature's influence on daily life. The framing is broader than landscape or wildlife; it's the human-nature relationship.

The gallery's fee structure is unusual: free to submit, with a £48 fee charged only if work is accepted for exhibition. Selected work is shown both in a physical exhibition in Glasgow and in an online gallery, with an accompanying exhibition catalogue.

The exhibition runs July 3–31, 2026.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is by email via file transfer with 1–8 images. The 2026 deadline was March 27; results announced April 1, 2026.
