The Art of the Automobile

A juried show at the Sacramento Fine Arts Center in Carmichael, California, open to all art media with an automobile theme, with events around the exhibition sponsored by the United British Sports Car Club Sacramento. The judge is Kandi Thompson, a Sacramento-area watercolour painter of thirty-five years who has studied with nationally and internationally acclaimed artists, been a member of several art groups including the California Watercolor Association, and been published in Watercolor Magic and the Artists Magazine calendar. Photography is specifically accommodated: the rules exclude giclées and works made by any commercial printing process, but state the exception for photography. Work must be original, finished in the last three years, not previously shown in a juried show at Sac Arts, and available for sale, with a maximum size of 4 × 8 feet; hanging pieces must be wired with D-rings, and works over 29 × 35 inches must use plastic rather than glass. Up to three pieces per artist may be submitted at $25 each or three for $60, dropping to $20/$50 for members and $15/$40 for youth entrants. The centre retains a 50% commission on sales for non-members and 30% for members. Jury decisions are sent on 21 August, and the exhibition runs 8 September to 3 October 2026.
