59th Annual Newport Beach Art Exhibition

59th annual juried exhibition and sale held in and around the Newport Beach Civic Center Community Room — a one-day show on August 15, 2026 with an awards reception at 4 p.m. Photography is its own award category alongside paintings, drawings and 3D art, with $300/$200/$100 prizes per category plus Juror's Choice and People's Choice. Up to two recently created original works per artist, hand-delivered; all work must be for sale with a 20% donation to the Newport Beach Arts Foundation on sales.
