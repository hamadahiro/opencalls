Open Call for Emerging Artists

Elliott Gallery in Amsterdam is shifting its open-call programme away from exhibition proposals and towards early-career photographers: recent graduates of universities and photography courses, current students, and self-taught photographers in the early stages of their careers, worldwide. The gallery is explicit that it is not looking for flawless, finished portfolios but for fresh voices, raw talent, authenticity and potential. There is no set theme; the gallery looks for a distinct perspective and a genuine passion for the medium, and accepts photography only. The prize is a personalised mentorship with the Elliott Gallery curatorial team — three 60-minute one-to-one sessions over three months covering how to refine an artistic identity and conceptual approach through intensive portfolio reviews, how to navigate the fine art market, price work, write statements and approach galleries, and how to curate work for digital and physical spaces, with the distinct potential for further collaboration such as digital exhibitions, group shows and print sales. Shortlisted artists also receive exposure across the gallery's website and social channels to its base of fine art photography collectors. Applicants submit a single public link to a folder or online portfolio plus a statement of up to 500 words, via Google Forms. Applications opened 24 July 2026 and close 9 August; the shortlist is announced 14 August and the winner on 21 August 2026. The €25 fee covers administration costs. Elliott Gallery is a physical gallery at Tussen de Bogen 91 and complies with the Gallery Fair Practice Code.
