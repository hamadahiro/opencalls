Contradiction

Prizes $300 / $200 / $100 Two $50 coordinator awards Group exhibition

A juried exhibition at Sebastopol Center for the Arts built on paradox. The call asks for work that lives in the tension between opposing forces rather than resolving it: "Consider how things that shouldn't belong together insist on being together. Consider an enigma, a paradox. Bitter/sweet, organized chaos, strange bedfellows. What happens when fire meets ice, big meets little, or grief meets joy?" SebArts offers a list of starting points - bitter and sweet, fire and ice, grief and joy, dark and light, sun and moon, logic and imagination, life and death, opposites on the colour wheel - and states that the work "doesn't need to resolve the tension. It needs to inhabit it." The show runs 27 February to 11 April 2027 at the centre's gallery in Sebastopol, with an opening reception on 27 February, 2-4pm. SebArts invites "artists working in any medium", so photographic work is eligible, and the organisation adds that it welcomes "artists of all backgrounds and perspectives". Awards are $300 for first place, $200 for second, $100 for third, and two $50 coordinator awards. Work sold during the exhibition splits 60% to the artist and 40% to SebArts. Jurors are listed as TBA; jurying runs 7-14 January 2027 and artists are notified by email on 15 January 2027. Entry is online only, at $35 for a first application and $10 each for a second and third - a maximum of three separate submissions, each entered one at a time, with up to three JPEG images of 5MB or less per entry. Work must not have been shown previously in the SebArts gallery and should have been made in the last five years. Accepted work is delivered at the artist's expense: shipped pieces must arrive by 19 February 2027 with a prepaid return label and a reusable container, or be hand-delivered on 21 or 23 February, and picked up on 12 April. Wall-hung work is capped at 50 lbs and free-standing work at 300 lbs, with a maximum width of 64 inches for a 10-foot wall. Selected work must stay up for the whole run, and anything left 30 days after the close becomes SebArts property.
