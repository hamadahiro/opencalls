2026 Booooooom Photo Awards

Booooooom's annual photography awards, run by the long-standing art and photography platform founded by Jeff Hamada. Five category winners are named across Portrait, Nature, Street, Student and Series — the last of these new for 2026 and built for cohesive bodies of work rather than single frames. Each category winner receives $1,000, a WANDRD camera bag (the PRVKE 21L for Portrait and Series, the ROGUE for Nature, Street and Student), an interview and feature on Booooooom, promotion across its social channels and a place on the awards mini-site. Three shortlisted photographers additionally receive a Manfrotto ONE Photo aluminium tripod with XPRO ball head, and sixteen participants drawn from across the whole entry pool — not only the winners — receive a hardcover photo book designed and printed by foto foto. A shortlist of twenty and a longlist of two hundred are published on the awards mini-site and across Booooooom's social channels. The jury is Katy Hundertmark, managing editor of Foam Magazine; Pat O'Rourke, founder and curator of Rental Magazine; the photographer Jennilee Marigomen; and Elena Rebecca Rivolta, editor-in-chief of PHROOM. Entry is free for a single image in one category; $25 at the early-bird rate covers up to ten images, which may span several projects, in one category; and an Awards Pass covers unlimited images across every category. Work must have been made in 2025 or 2026. The Student category is open to anyone who was enrolled in a school of any kind when the work was made, and students may enter the same work in the other categories as well.
