7th AOYF Human Rights Photography Prize

Prizes CHF 10,000 CHF 1,000 ×4 Exhibition

The first fully international edition of a prize the Geneva foundation Act On Your Future has run since 2015 with the Centre de la photographie Genève — until now open only to students and recent graduates of six European art schools, and from this seventh edition open to artists anywhere. The 2027 theme is Post-Truth: Reality in Question. The call asks what can be believed and who shapes the reality we see at a moment when images circulate instantly, algorithms decide what reaches us, AI can generate content hard to tell from a photograph, and authentic images and victims' testimony can themselves be dismissed as fabricated. Projects may engage evidence and testimony, manipulated or AI-generated imagery, contested histories, propaganda, the circulation of images, or personal experience of a reality others dispute; documentary, conceptual, experimental and interdisciplinary approaches are all invited and no photographic style is privileged. Five finalists are chosen by an interdisciplinary jury over three rounds — two online rounds in January and February 2027, notification in February, final deliberations in Geneva on 14 June 2027 — and show together at the CPG from 16 June to 21 August 2027. The winner, announced at the opening on 16 June, receives CHF 10,000: CHF 7,000 unrestricted plus a CHF 3,000 project development grant. The other four finalists each receive CHF 1,000. All five get curatorial guidance from the CPG team, up to CHF 1,500 of production support produced in Geneva under CPG supervision, and travel and accommodation to attend the final jury round, award ceremony and opening from 14 to 16 June 2027, subject to budget. Each finalist also donates two works made with that production support to the AOYF Collection, chosen by mutual agreement: copyright stays with the artist, but ownership of those physical works passes to the foundation, which may exhibit, reproduce, lend or sell them from its collection with the artist credited. Works shown in the exhibition may be offered for sale by agreement with the artist, and proceeds from any sale are shared equally between artist and foundation. Being in Geneva from 14 to 16 June 2027 is an undertaking finalists give, with remote participation only exceptionally authorised, and visas are the artist's own responsibility, their fees covered subject to approval and budget. Entry is free and open to artists aged 18 and over, individuals or collectives, with no restriction on nationality, residence or formal art education. One project per applicant: 8 to 25 images (or a moving-image work or excerpt up to 10 minutes), a project statement of up to 300 words, a 150-word biography with contact details, and captions. Work may be new, ongoing, completed, previously exhibited or published. Generated, composited, staged, appropriated and archival material is allowed provided the methods are disclosed in the statement — passing manipulated or generated material off as documentary evidence can disqualify. AOYF and the CPG may ask for raw files, metadata, prompts or reference material to verify authorship, and failing to supply what they reasonably request can disqualify too. Applications close 20 December 2026 at 23:59 CET and are accepted only through Picter. CAUTION: the foundation's own prix-de-photographie page says nothing about this edition — re-read on 28 October 2026 it still presents the 2023 prize, open only to students and graduates of six partner art schools and paying each of the four non-winning finalists CHF 500, under a 2023 copyright line — so the organiser's prospectus and terms and conditions on Picter are the surfaces that bind here.
