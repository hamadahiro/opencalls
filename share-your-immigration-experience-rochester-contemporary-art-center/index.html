Share Your Immigration Experience

Part of RoCo's larger exhibition Sanctuary: Borders and Belonging in 2026, this call invites immigrants of all ages, backgrounds and geographies to submit one visual artwork expressing or documenting their personal experience of immigration. Artwork may be in any artistic medium or craft. RoCo suggests working from personal narrative and lived experience: stories of arrival, hope, aspiration, belonging, loss, escape or resilience; encounters with systems that criminalise and exclude or that welcome and support; family histories, cultural inheritance and everyday rituals; and responses to border politics, detention or racialised policing. The gallery is explicit that it wants the full spectrum of experience, including immigrations that were straightforwardly positive, and it also welcomes work made in collaboration between immigrants and non-immigrants. Every eligible entry will be displayed, though the submission process still has to be completed so the installation can be planned, and work may be presented anonymously even though the entry form asks for a name. One piece per artist, no larger than 24 by 24 inches and ready to hang, dropped off at RoCo on September 11 or 12. The exhibition runs October 2 to November 14, 2026, with a reception on October 2. RoCo particularly wants voices living or working in the Rochester area.
