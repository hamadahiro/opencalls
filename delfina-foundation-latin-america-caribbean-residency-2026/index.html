Delfina Foundation — Latin America & Caribbean Residency 2026

Delfina Foundation@delfinafdn

Twelve-week fully funded residency at the Delfina Foundation in London, supported by Jorge M. Pérez and El Espacio 23. Open to visual artists based in Latin America, the Caribbean, and related diasporic communities in the United States. Artists from Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Brazil, and Argentina are not eligible for this cycle (these countries received dedicated residencies in prior years). Two artists will be selected from this call: one for the autumn 2026 residency (September 21 – December 13) and one for the winter 2027 residency (January 5 – March 25). Apply via Cognito Forms with completed application form — read the Guidance Notes document before applying.
