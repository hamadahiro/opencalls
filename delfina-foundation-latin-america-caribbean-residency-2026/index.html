Delfina Foundation — Latin America & Caribbean Residency 2026

Prize Residency

Delfina Foundation is a London-based contemporary art residency. The 2026 Latin America & Caribbean Residency is fully funded with support from Jorge M. Pérez and El Espacio 23, the Miami-based collection.

Eligibility is restricted to visual artists based in Latin America, the Caribbean, or related diasporic communities in the United States. Notably, artists from Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Brazil, and Argentina are not eligible for this cycle — these countries received dedicated Delfina residencies in prior years.

Two artists are selected from this call: one for the autumn 2026 residency (September 21 – December 13) and one for the winter 2027 residency (January 5 – March 25). The residency is twelve weeks in each case.

Application is free, made through Cognito Forms with the completed application form. Applicants are expected to read the Guidance Notes document before applying.

The 2026 deadline was April 13; results announced June 1, 2026.
