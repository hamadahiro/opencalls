2026 AUTOMAT Open Call

The annual open call from AUTOMAT, an artist-run co-op gallery in Philadelphia's Crane Arts Building, for a group exhibition curated by the collective's own members. Open to emerging and mid-career artists working in any medium in the Philadelphia region. This year's call is interested in transformation, facade, growth, movement, generations, re/pavement, transmogrification and private/public space — "However, we will accept all submissions from Philadelphia artists." Up to three works, up to two images per work; time-based work goes in as a public YouTube or Vimeo link of ten minutes or less, and experimental and installation proposals are explicitly encouraged with an installation plan. The show runs December 10, 2026 to January 23, 2027, opening with a Second Thursday reception from 6 to 9pm and open Saturdays through the run. AUTOMAT is candid about the fee: "The $25 open call entry fee supports the general operation of AUTOMAT and the production of this show, which is otherwise funded by our artist members. We acknowledge the barriers that fees create for artist submissions" — artists who need it may select a $10 tier instead. Shipping both ways is the artist's cost; hand delivery is to Suite 105 on December 5.
