Bi-Annual Open Call 2027

Prize Exhibition

GALLERY jENSEITS is a contemporary gallery that opened in May 2026 at 1101 Saint Gregory Street in Mt. Adams, Cincinnati, and describes itself as showing "both the renowned and the unknown, the alluring and the absurd". Its first bi-annual open call selects work for the 2027 exhibition programme, which runs from February to September 2027 in shows of six to ten weeks. There is no application fee. The call is open internationally to emerging and established artists working in contemporary art, and the gallery's media list is explicit: "All media are accepted, including (but not limited to) painting, sculpture, installation, ceramics, printmaking, photography, fiber arts, digital, and new media." Applicants must be 18 or over, submit high-quality images with descriptions, pricing, dimensions, creation dates and materials, and be able to deliver and retrieve work by shipping or in person; up to 18 pieces may be submitted. Selected work must be available for the full six- or ten-week run and arrive ready to install. Artists are responsible for shipping costs and insurance in both directions, and the gallery makes final curatorial decisions.
