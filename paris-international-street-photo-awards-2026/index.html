Paris International Street Photo Awards 2026

Prizes $1,000 / $700 / $500 Exhibition Publication

An annual street photography award run from Paris, open to amateur and professional photographers worldwide aged 18 or over. Entries are made as single images or as series of 5 to 15 photographs, judged separately in each of about forty street-photography categories — from classic street and black & white to urbex, night shots, film photography and street humour. Street Photographer of the Year takes $1,000, Best Street Photo Project $700 and Street Photo of the Year $500, all paid by PayPal. Winners, jury nominees and finalists are exhibited at the organisers' expense — the 2026 edition will be shown at an international photo festival in New York in late 2027 — and grand-prize winners get a monograph published with partner Snap Collective plus features in FotoSlovo and OPENEYE magazines. Entry is €18 for a single image up to September 30 and €21 from October 1, with series and multi-image packages priced up to €75; each additional category costs €6. Photographers keep copyright; previously published and previously entered work is accepted. Winners are announced December 26, 2026.
