2027 Saltonstall Residency Fellowships

Saltonstall hosts five people at a time on its Ithaca property, one of them a photographer or filmmaker, for a free two-, three- or four-week residency with a private apartment, its own black-and-white darkroom, $100 a week and up to $1,000 in need-based support. Seven-night residencies are reserved for parents and caregivers. Only year-round New York State residents aged 21 and over may apply.
