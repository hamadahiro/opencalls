Residency for Artists Based in Argentina 2027

Gasworks' residency programme brings artists from all over the world to London for up to three months, and this round is open to artists based in Argentina. The eleven-week residency runs 7 April – 22 June 2027 at Gasworks in Vauxhall, is fully funded, and is supported by the ERA Foundation and developed in partnership with URRA. Gasworks describes its residencies as self-led, non-prescriptive and process-based: there is no required outcome, and the point is time and space to research, develop new work, engage with the city and build networks, with administrative, pastoral and curatorial support from the staff. The selected artist receives 24-hour access to a fully accessible private studio alongside the other residents and Gasworks' community of London-based studio holders; a single room in Gasworks' Residency House, a two-storey, five-bed house with three communal bathrooms and a kitchen shared with four other resident artists; economy return flights and taxi transfers from the artist's home country, including one cabin and one hold bag; all visa costs and support with the visa application; a weekly stipend of £175 for living expenses; a materials budget of up to £800; a prepaid TFL zones 1+2 travel card for the whole residency; studio visits with Gasworks' staff, external curators, artists and educational groups; an Open Studio event and presentation of the work and research developed during the residency; access to Gasworks' basic tools and AV equipment; a dedicated residency page on the Gasworks website with a short video interview filmed and edited by Gasworks; and at least two individual studio visits with other artists, curators and arts professionals. The open call is for early-career contemporary visual artists based in Argentina who have at least a moderate level of spoken English and are comfortable discussing their work in English; the medium is not restricted, and Gasworks' recent residents include photographers and lens-based artists. The residency FAQ adds that an artist currently studying outside Argentina but keeping a permanent address there is still eligible to apply. Because the funds and the shared accommodation are for one person, the residency cannot be offered to duos or collectives, and partners or children cannot be accommodated — artists wishing to bring family may still apply if they arrange their own travel and accommodation. Applications are free and digital only, made through the form at the end of the opportunity page as a single PDF of no more than 15MB, and they close at 1pm UK time on 16 November 2026, not at midnight. A selection panel of Gasworks representatives and external advisors with specialist knowledge of the contemporary art scenes in Argentina and the UK makes the choice, prioritising artists making high quality work who have not had the opportunity to work in London before; a shortlist of three to five artists is interviewed online in the week beginning 30 November 2026, and the successful applicant is notified in the week beginning 14 December 2026.
