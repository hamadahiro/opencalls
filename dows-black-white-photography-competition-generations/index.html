Dow's Black & White Photography Competition — Generations

Prizes Exhibition for the top 15 Douro Valley photography trip (1st) Dow's Vintage Port and a copy of '100 Leica Stories' (2nd, 3rd)

Dow's, the Douro Port house promoted by Symington Family Estates - five generations of Symington winemakers have lived and worked at its vineyards at Quinta do Bomfim and Quinta da Senhora da Ribeira since 1912 - runs this black-and-white competition out of Vila Nova de Gaia, and the fourth edition asks for a single idea: Generations. The brief is deliberately wide - human relationships and the passage of time, memory and inheritance, tradition set against modernity - and the only fixed requirement is that the picture be monochrome and your own. Entry is free and open worldwide to anyone 18 or over, up to three images per person, with no restriction on when the photograph was taken, so archive work is as welcome as something made this month. The fifteen strongest entries are printed and hung in a Porto gallery in February 2027, with the placed winners announced at the end of that exhibition: first prize is a trip to Quinta do Bomfim in the Douro Valley and the company's Vila Nova de Gaia lodges, with access to private grounds and viewpoints to photograph the valley and the Port trade; second and third receive a bottle of Dow's 1994 and 2003 Vintage Port respectively, each with a copy of '100 Leica Stories'. There is no cash prize. The terms are unusually specific about the photograph itself: files must be maximum-quality JPEG at a minimum of 4,000 pixels on the longest edge at 300 dpi because entries are printed large for the exhibition and for book publication; EXIF metadata must be intact; and shortlisted entrants have seven days to supply the original RAW or straight-out-of-camera file for verification. Post-processing is limited to traditional monochrome darkroom moves - exposure, contrast, tonal balance, dodge and burn, cropping, minor sharpening, dust-spot removal - while compositing, added or removed elements, texture overlays, digital painting, heavy HDR and generative AI are all grounds for disqualification. Two things to weigh before entering: the judging panel is appointed by the promoter and named only as an external panel of top national photographers, so you cannot see who will read your work; and by entering you grant Symington Family Estates a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual licence to use, reproduce, adapt and distribute your submitted images across any channel tied to Dow's Port or the competition, including print, social media, exhibition displays, trade marketing and promotional packaging. You keep copyright, and the company credits the photographer where reasonable and practical, but that licence does not expire. A separate tick-box opts you into marketing email; it is not a condition of entry. Submissions close 2 January 2027 at 23:59 WEST, and the competition is governed by Portuguese law.
