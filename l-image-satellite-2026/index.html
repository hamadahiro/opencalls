L'Image Satellite 2026

Open call for photographers and visual artists working with contemporary still image practices including documentary, experimental, vernacular, and hybrid image forms. Selected artists exhibit at L'Image Satellite festival in Nice, September–October 2026. Artists receive €500 presentation fee, round-trip artwork transport, accommodation, and travel. Submit up to 20 JPEG images via online form and email.
