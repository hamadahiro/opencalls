Focus #7 — The Pursuit of Happiness

Annual open call for Les Mesnographies, the first free open-air photography festival in the Yvelines, staged in the municipal park at Les Mesnuls west of Paris. For its 7th edition the festival invites series responding to the notion of happiness, quoting Oxford's Department of Psychology — happiness as a positive emotion arising not only from doing or receiving something but from a sense of connection between people or of wellbeing — and asking what happiness is: a goal, a state to be reached, something within us or something built over time. The festival hangs work outdoors in the park for three weeks each year, brings together photographers of different nationalities, and runs alongside a free programme of guided visits, talks, a picnic and an opening concert; past editions have shown photographers from Argentina, China and France, and the festival has been covered by M Le Monde, Fisheye, Télérama, Polka, Beaux Arts, France Culture and Arte. It also runs a hors-les-murs strand placing work in nearby communes with the Parc naturel régional de la Haute Vallée de Chevreuse. Applications are a single PDF of no more than 20 images with the series text and a biography, sent by email — the organisers ask that you do not send WeTransfer links. Because the work is shown outdoors in a park open to all audiences including children, the festival asks applicants to keep that in mind when building a series.
