Slide Night 2026

Prizes Projection at Rotondes Exhibition at Light Leaks Festival, May 2027 for first place

The Luxembourg Streetphoto Collective — Street Photography Luxembourg a.s.b.l., based at 3 Place des Rotondes in Luxembourg City, organiser of the Light Leaks Festival and of the Streets Unplugged podcast, and described in its own Instagram bio as a non-profit organisation — runs Slide Night as its annual projection evening at Rotondes. The 2026 announcement invites "street and documentary photographers to submit their work" and states that "selected photographs will be projected on the big screen on 9 December 2026", with a submission deadline of 25 November 2026. Asked in the comments what the prize was, the collective answered from its own account: "First price gets to exhibit during the Light Leaks Festival in May 2027" — so the winning photographer carries over into the collective's own festival exhibition. THIS YEAR'S RULES ARE ON THE ENTRY FORM, NOT THE WEBSITE: streetphoto.lu still reads "Streetphoto.lu is under reconstruction!" and its newest Slide Night page is the 2025 edition, but the Google Form linked from the collective's Instagram bio as "SlideNight2026" is live and titled SLIDE NIGHT SUBMISSIONS 2026, and it carries the 2026 terms in full. The evening runs 19:00–21:00 on 9 December 2026 at Plateforme / Rotondes. The deadline is 25 November 2026 at 23:59 and the jury replies on 1 December. Work must fall under street or documentary photography, must be original and made by the submitting photographer, and may have been taken anywhere in the world. The entry itself is a slideshow that must include music and run no longer than three minutes, uploaded as a high-quality MP4, and selected photographers are required to be present on the night. The form asks for name, birthday, email, phone, Instagram handle and a title for the slideshow, and it records the name and email on your Google account when you upload the file — so you need to be signed in to Google to submit, which is also why the form is unreadable without an account. The collective's standing Slide Night terms and conditions require participants to be of legal age in their country or to have parental consent, state that late entries will not be accepted, leave ownership with the photographer while granting the collective a non-exclusive royalty-free licence to use the work for the event and related promotion, and are governed by Luxembourg law. No entry fee appears on the announcement, on those terms, or anywhere on the 2026 form, which has no payment step — that is the basis for the free fee stored here. The form says only that "our jury will curate all submissions"; no jurors are named.
