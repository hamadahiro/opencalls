Interesting: An Art Exhibition

Prizes Cash awards Exhibition

The Gallery at Arts Illiana gives its spring slot to an open-theme show about what makes a work hold your attention, open to any medium from artists anywhere in North America; the prospectus names inkjet prints of digital photographs among its model entries. Cash awards go to select categories, and all work hung must be for sale, with the gallery keeping 30%.
