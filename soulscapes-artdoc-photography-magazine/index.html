SoulScapes — Artdoc Magazine Open Call

An international open call from Artdoc Photography Magazine on spirituality, inner awareness and the invisible dimensions of human experience. The magazine frames spirituality broadly — found in nature, silence, contemplation, personal rituals and moments of heightened awareness — and asks for work about the search for meaning and our relationship with experience beyond the visible world: inner experiences, intuition, memory, emotion, and the importance of feeling, observing, questioning and reflecting. Art and documentary photography, visual diaries and visual storytelling are all welcome, as is work that reaches the invisible through light, atmosphere, symbol, abstraction and experiment; the editors say they are interested in the relationship between inner and outer worlds rather than in one register of image-making. Selected work is shown in Artdoc's international online exhibition or published in the magazine, and selected photographers may be invited for a portfolio interview. Submission is through a member-only form on the call page: entry itself costs nothing, but you must hold an Artdoc membership, €35 a year (the magazine's membership page currently offers a first year at €24 with the coupon ARTDOC50%), which also carries the six digital issues and the member-only submission forms. The call page itself prints “This Open Call is Now Closed” to visitors who are not signed in — the same line appears word for word on Artdoc’s past calls, while the magazine’s own open-calls page lists SoulScapes as its current open call with this deadline, and the submission form only appears once you are logged in as a member. Photographers keep copyright; submitting grants Artdoc permission to publish the images in its online exhibitions, features, issues, social channels and promotional material. The magazine states it may refuse entries that fall outside its ethical and aesthetic parameters, naming explicit nudity and overtly submissive postures as likely refusals.
