The Present of Presence: Live the Moment

The Cape Cod Museum of Art's annual members' juried exhibition, held at the museum's building on Hope Lane in Dennis, Massachusetts. The brief is about attention: in a world of relentless headlines, buzzing devices and climate anxiety, the museum asks what it means to truly inhabit this moment — to see, listen, feel, notice and connect without reaching for the next distraction. Work might celebrate a fleeting encounter, reveal something overlooked, embrace stillness, or capture the intensity of being alive in an uncertain world. Photography is named first in the museum's own list of what will be considered: photography, printmaking, textile, multi-media and three-dimensional artwork. The exhibition is open only to CCMoA members, but membership is bought at the point of entry rather than earned — the call says outright "Not yet a Member of the Cape Cod Museum of Art? You can join today to enter!", and membership runs $30 for students, $50 individual and $85 dual/family, purchasable online. It must stay current through 31 March 2027. Members may submit up to five artworks at $20 for the first and $10 for each additional one, plus a ticket service fee of $0.50 on the first entry and $0.25 on each additional one added at checkout; entry fees are non-refundable. Work must be original, new to CCMoA and not previously exhibited there; any prints must be limited edition and signed. There are no size limits — scale is decided by gallery space. Work arrives framed or on gallery-wrapped canvas at least 1.5 inches deep, ready to hang. AI-generated artwork is not accepted. Juror's Awards of $300, $200 and $100 are presented at the opening reception and gallery talk on Thursday 14 January 2027; the museum has not yet named the juror. Selected artists are notified by end of day on 17 December 2026 and the exhibition runs 8 January to 21 March 2027; work may optionally be offered for sale, on a 60/40 split in the artist's favour. Entries close at noon Eastern on Monday 14 December 2026.
