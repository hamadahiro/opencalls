Outdoor Photography Magazine — Spring Landscapes

Prize Publication prize

Outdoor Photography Magazine is a UK monthly print magazine focused on landscape, wildlife, and outdoor photography, published by GMC Publications. The magazine runs themed reader-submission features in each issue. Spring Landscapes is the seasonal landscape feature.

The brief is narrow: spring-season landscape photographs only. Selected work appears in the magazine's print pages — readership skews toward serious amateurs and active landscape photographers in the UK and elsewhere.

The winner receives Komperdell Carbon CXP Pro walking poles (RRP £150). Selection is for publication, not cash prize.

Submissions are up to 5 images at JPG 300ppi, with captions. Eligibility is open globally, but the magazine's editorial focus skews UK and European. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was April 30; selections published within 10 weeks of deadline.
