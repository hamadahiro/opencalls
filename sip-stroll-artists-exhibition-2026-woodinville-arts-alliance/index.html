Sip & Stroll Artists' Exhibition 2026

Prize $250 first prize

Juried members' exhibition presented by the Woodinville Arts Alliance with The Winemakers Reserve, on display in Washington wine country on 13 and 14 November 2026. Photography is one of eleven named categories, alongside acrylic, ceramic, digital, mixed media, oil, pastel, pencil, watercolour, fibre/textile and 3-D. Judging takes place on 13 November, followed that evening by the Sip and Stroll event from 5 to 8 PM, where visitors meet the artists over wine and live music and the awards are presented; the show stays up for the weekend. Each artist may enter up to three pieces made within two years of the show date. Work generated with artificial intelligence is explicitly excluded, as are giclée prints, art prints and copies. Pieces must measure at least 144 square inches and no more than 2,304 square inches including framing, be wired to hang — no glass clips, easel backs or saw-tooth hangers — and any matted work must be covered with glass or plexiglass. All entries must be priced to sell and remain on display for the run; the Alliance retains 30 per cent commission on sales of original art and pays artists within two weeks of the event. Accepted work is hand-delivered to the venue between 10 and 11 AM on 13 November, or shipped with return postage enclosed, and collected on the Saturday afternoon; the Alliance states that artists must be present at the Friday evening event. Entry is open only to Woodinville Arts Alliance members, but membership can be bought to enter and costs $44 a year. The judge is visual artist Anna Macrae.
