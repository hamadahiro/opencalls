2027 Pacific Northwest Drawers

Blue Sky, Oregon Center for the Photographic Arts, marks twenty years of its Pacific Northwest Drawers programme — a juried public archive of original prints by up to 60 regional photographers, held in accessible flat-file drawers at the gallery for a full year. This is the rare exhibition where visitors are encouraged to handle the art, and the drawers draw students, curators and collectors as well as daily visitors. Submit exactly ten unwatermarked prints from a single series or body of work, ideally made in the last five years; accepted work must arrive matted or mounted and must not exceed 20 x 16 x 1.5 inches when stacked. Shana Lopes of SFMOMA juries. All accepted work is featured on Blue Sky's website and stays in the digital archive indefinitely; prints are eligible for sale with the artist's permission at a 50% gallery commission. Free to enter, one entry per artist. The cutoff is not end of day: the CaFE record states “The deadline for submissions is 10:59 PM (PT), Friday, December 18, 2026” — 11:59 pm Mountain time, an hour before midnight where the gallery is — and adds that no deadline extensions are given.
