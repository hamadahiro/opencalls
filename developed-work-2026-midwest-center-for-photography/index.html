Developed Work 2027

Prize $200 fellowship

Developed Work is the Midwest Center for Photography's annual national fellowship competition, focused on thematically developed bodies of work rather than individual standout images. The brief is explicit: selection emphasizes a cohesive conceptual framework — submissions are evaluated on the body of work as a whole rather than its strongest image.

The Wichita, Kansas gallery exhibits selected work in person, with the 2027 exhibition running in March 2027. The winner receives the $200 Developed Work Fellowship Award and one year of online representation by MWCP, which functions as ongoing visibility for the work beyond the exhibition window.

Submissions are limited to U.S.-based photographers and require 5 to 14 images plus a brief artist statement explaining the work's intent. Entry fee is $50 for up to 5 images. The 2027 cycle deadline is December 31, 2026.

The competition runs alongside MWCP's other open calls, including the international Ten x Ten Small Works exhibition. Where Ten x Ten samples individual 10×10 inch prints across many photographers, Developed Work selects a single fellow per cycle whose practice exemplifies sustained thematic exploration.
