Developed Work 2027

Developed Work is the Midwest Center for Photography's annual national fellowship competition, focused on thematically developed bodies of work rather than individual standout images. The brief is explicit: selection emphasizes a cohesive conceptual framework — submissions are evaluated on the body of work as a whole rather than its strongest image.

The Wichita, Kansas gallery exhibits selected work in person, and both MWCP surfaces now date the 2027 exhibition October 1-25, 2027. The winner receives the $200 Developed Work Fellowship Award and one year of online representation by MWCP, which functions as ongoing visibility for the work beyond the exhibition window.

Submissions are limited to photographers based in the USA and require a cohesive body of work with a brief artist statement outlining its intentions; no image cap is published, with $50 covering five images and each additional image $10. Selected photographers also rent a standard frame from the gallery at $30 per frame, payable in advance. The 2027 cycle deadline is July 1, 2027 on both the entry page and the calls index.

The competition runs alongside MWCP's other open calls, including the international Ten x Ten Small Works exhibition. Where Ten x Ten samples individual 10×10 inch prints across many photographers, Developed Work selects a single fellow per cycle whose practice exemplifies sustained thematic exploration.
