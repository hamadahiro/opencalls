sƛ̓x̣etkʷ Artist-in-Residence Program — Round 3

Prize $9,000 stipend

Three-month winter residency (January 5 – March 26, 2027) at Spokane Falls Community College for emerging Native American artists who are enrolled members of a US federally recognized tribe, with priority for Pacific Northwest and Washington tribes, particularly the Schitsu'umsh (Coeur d'Alene). Offers a $9,000 stipend paid monthly, furnished off-campus housing, up to $500 travel reimbursement, access to fine-art facilities including ceramics, printmaking, digital art, and sculpture, and optional mentorship. Residents commit 35 hours weekly on campus; 'emerging' means without gallery representation.

DeadlineOctober 20, 2026
ResultsBy November 10, 2026
Entry feeFree
EligibilityNative American artists, Emerging artists
LocationSpokane, WA
Requirements5–10 images

Jury: SFCC faculty, students & regional Tribal community committee
