Times Square Collective Showcase — February 2027

Prize Exhibition

Arrival Gallery is selling places in a one-day digital billboard showcase on a screen at 1568 Broadway in Times Square, New York, on 15 February 2027. The work is shown as a curated rotation of artists, each piece in a 1:1 square format throughout the day. Each artist enters one artwork as a JPG or PNG (the upload step also accepts MP4), and work that is not square is centre-cropped. Work showing smoking, violence, nudity, vulgarity or offensive content is not accepted. The application form charges £100 including VAT as its last, required step, and says that only completed payments guarantee a place on the billboard; it describes the showcase as 20 artists. Arrival Gallery's own digital showcases page gives different terms: 10 artist slots at £160 per slot, applications reviewed before places are confirmed, and no payment collected through the application forms. The form is branded Art Seen New York and, once submitted, sends applicants to art-seen.com. The deadline is 31 January 2027, 23:59 GMT.
