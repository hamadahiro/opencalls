True Blue

Prizes $300 / $200 / $100 Exhibition

The Cultural Center of Cape Cod, a 501(c)(3) arts centre on a historic campus at 307 Old Main Street in South Yarmouth, Massachusetts, is running True Blue as an online juried exhibition: from the vastness of the sky to the depths of the sea, from loyalty and friendship to sorrow and melancholy, it asks artists to explore the colour, symbolism and emotional resonance of blue, and it wants interpretations ranging from the literal to the figurative. Photography is named in the eligible media alongside painting, drawing, digital art, mixed media, sculpture, fibre art, printmaking, glass, wood, ceramics and signed, numbered limited-edition prints; video is the only exclusion. Entry is $20 for the first work and $5 for each additional work up to a maximum of 20 images, non-refundable and no guarantee of acceptance. Awards are $300 for first place, $200 for second and $100 for third, and the first-place winner also has the option of showing the winning image — and possibly more, depending on size — for a month during 2027 in the Center's physical Showcase Gallery on the Cape Cod campus, which turns an online prize into a real wall. The virtual exhibition stays online for three months. Jurying is by the Cultural Center's directors, who give no individual feedback. Sales inquiries are routed to the artist, whose own link sits beside the image in the online gallery, and the Center takes no commission on an online sale; in exchange, entering grants the Cultural Center copyright in the image for display and promotional use, while the physical artwork stays the artist's property. Work that is copied, produced by AI, or made under an instructor's guidance is not eligible. Winners are notified and the online gallery opens by December 15, 2026.
