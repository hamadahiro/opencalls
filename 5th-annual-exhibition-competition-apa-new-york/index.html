5th Annual Exhibition & Competition

The New York chapter of American Photographic Artists reaches the fifth edition of its annual juried competition, and the offer is specific: 80 photographs selected for a one-week open-gallery exhibition at Soho Photo Gallery from February 2 to 7, 2027, with a reception on February 4 and a fully illustrated show catalogue. Soho Photo was founded in 1971 by a group of New York Times photojournalists and avant-garde photographers, is New York City's longest-running co-op gallery, and now sits in Chelsea. The chapter is explicit that no genre is out of scope — personal or assignment work, commercial or fine art, reportage, fashion, street, still life, landscape, macro or portraits, “digital or old-school alternative processes”, colour or black and white, and “any photographic genre we forgot to mention”. Around the show the chapter names a feature on the APA website, social media, the catalogue, and an opening reception intended to bring in “the industry professionals who buy photographs and hire photographers”. The curator is Elizabeth Renstrom, a New York photo editor, curator and photographer whose own work deals in nostalgia, femininity and consumer culture; she was Senior Photo Editor at The New Yorker and held editorial positions at VICE, Marie Claire and TIME. She has judged or reviewed for LensCulture, the Lucie Photo Book Awards, APA-Los Angeles, Red Hook Labs New Talent, Photoville, Belfast Photo Festival and the Daylight Photo Book Awards, and has taught at Parsons School of Design and the International Center of Photography. Curating is blind: submitted files are assigned an arbitrary ID and the photographers' names reach the curator only after selection is complete. Eighty photographs are chosen in all, one photographer cannot have more than five images in the show, and the curator may take all or any part of a series entry. Entry is priced per image and membership halves it: $25 per image for APA members and $50 per image for non-members, the same rates for single images and for series entries of two to four images. APA membership runs across five levels starting at $50, and the competition is open to photographers whether or not they join. There is no cash prize — what is awarded is the wall, the catalogue and the reception. Files are JPEGs of up to 2MB at 1800 pixels on the long side, with no watermarks, named Lastname-Firstname-Title (series entries add -Series-XofY) and no special characters. There is no restriction on when the work was made, but an image already selected in a previous APA New York annual exhibition will not be considered, and winning images from past APA competitions are barred. AI is excluded twice over: the submission guidelines say work created using AI-generated prompts is “not considered photography for this contest”, and entrants separately warrant that their submission contains no elements created by AI. One date needs care. The Important Dates block — on the call page and on the entry page both — gives December 3, 2026 as the submission deadline, while the Contest Terms further down the call page state that “the deadline for entries is December 1, 2026”. The chapter has not reconciled the two, so a photographer who wants certainty should be in by December 1. Winners are notified no later than December 21, 2026 and supply a high-resolution file for the book and the exhibition print by December 30. Entry fees are not refundable, entrants keep full copyright, and the permission granted to APA covers exhibition and promotion of the work with credit.
