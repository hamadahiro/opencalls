Open Theme 2027

Prize Exhibition

An all-media open call from The Lab on Santa Fe, the gallery at 840 Santa Fe Drive that sits in the middle block of Denver's Art District on Santa Fe, where the gallery says the First Friday Art Walk pulls the largest crowds in the state. “Open theme” means no theme: the organisers want artists to take control and test what the space can hold, and they name photographers alongside painters, illustrators, printmakers, sculptors, video artists, installation artists, collage and assemblage artists, textile and fibre artists and ceramists. Nature, humanity, politics, chaos, transcendence — whatever pulls at you. They ask to feel the rigour inside your process, saying risk, intensity and the possibility of failure are all part of the atmosphere, but are emphatic that this is not meant to exclude anyone: all mediums, all styles, all subject matter, the only rule being to show you are open to your muse rather than trying to please. Entry is $34 for one or two works and one application per artist. Location eligibility is listed as national rather than Colorado-only. All submitted art must be for sale, measurements on applications must be accurate, and work can be turned away at drop-off for improper wiring; sales are split 58% to the artist and 42% to the gallery. The exhibition runs 5 to 27 February 2027.
