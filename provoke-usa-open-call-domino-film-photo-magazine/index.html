Provoke USA — Open Call

Prize Publication

Provoke USA is a photography zine published by Domino Film Photo Magazine in Chicago. The brief is explicitly historicized: the zine takes its name and approach from the Japanese Provoke movement of the late 1960s — Daido Moriyama, Takuma Nakahira, Yutaka Takanashi, and the others associated with destabilizing certainty and revealing a world beyond conventional language.

The zine accepts work that engages with that lineage: rough, blurred, contrasted, are-bure-boke, or other approaches that resist clean documentary description.

AI-generated images are not accepted.

International submissions welcome. Submission is by email with the photographer's Instagram handle. Files must be named with the photographer's name (e.g., JohnDoe 1). Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 28.
