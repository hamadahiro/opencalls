SAGE Magazine 2027 Print Edition — Marginalia (in nature)

Prize Publication

SAGE Magazine is a student-run environmental publication of the Yale School of the Environment in New Haven, Connecticut, in print since 2006. What began as a small internal publication for environmental graduate students has become, in the magazine's own words, a sought-after publication for poets, writers, visual artists, comic book creators, graphic designers and environmentalists expressing themselves through art; its stated goal is expanding environmentalism through provocative conversation and the arts. The digital magazine publishes year-round and an Annual Print Issue appears each spring, alongside events, speakers and workshops on environmental writing, journalism and the arts. Submissions are now open for the 2027 print edition on the theme Marginalia (in nature). Marginalia as traditionally defined refers to notes, comments or artwork written or drawn in the margins of a book or manuscript, and in these margins, the editors write, "we emphasize, interpret, argue, daydream"; the issue asks what is written, argued, archived and dreamed in the margins of environments, landscapes and ecosystems big and small, inviting photographers and artists to "take us to these edges and peripheries". SAGE seeks provocative, critical, beautiful, captivating work from emerging and established environmental writers and artists, welcomes pieces ranging from playful to pensive, and describes its throughline as strengthening and exploring the relationship between humanity and the environment. Four categories are accepted: prose (personal essays, narrative journalism and short stories up to 3,000 words), poetry, photography and photo essays, and visual art. Contributors may submit up to three pieces. Work must be previously unpublished; simultaneous submissions are welcome, but a submission must be withdrawn if it is accepted elsewhere. Rolling gallery submissions are no longer accepted. There is no entry fee, and submission is through the magazine's own Google Form. The deadline is 14 December 2026. The editors note that response times vary because the publication is student-run, and ask that contributors not email about the status of a submission. The current masthead is Editor-in-Chief Sara Sherburne and Managing Editor Olivia Bayne. The submission form carries three requirements the call page does not: each piece needs its own form submission, only one file may be uploaded per form (PDF, document, drawing or image, maximum 10 MB), and visual work should be a .png, .raw or high-quality .jpeg of at least 300 dpi. The form's Type of Submission list offers only visual art, poetry and prose, so a photograph is entered as visual art, and its required affiliation question reads "university or independent", so no Yale connection is needed. Contributors receive no confirmation email. One thing to read past: the same page also advertises a compensated designer position for the 2027 print edition with a 13 November 2026 deadline, applied for through Yale Student Employment - that is a job posting, not part of this call for work.
