Color Unleashed

A juried all-media show at Arts Center East in Vernon, Connecticut, on color as the subject itself — bold hues, unexpected combinations, vibrant patterns, subtle shades. Photography is a named accepted medium; artists 18 and over may submit up to five pieces, with awards at the January 30 reception. Work must arrive wired and ready to hang, and anything not collected within 30 days of the close becomes the gallery's property.
