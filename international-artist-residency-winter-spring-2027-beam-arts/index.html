International Artist Residency — Winter/Spring 2027

Prize Residency

One- or two-month studio residency in Thessaloniki, Greece, taking up to five artists from different disciplines per period. Applications for the winter/spring 2027 cycle close 24 August 2026 and decisions are confirmed by 4 September; the 2027 periods run monthly from January to July, and artists staying longer than a month can remain in the studios between periods. Each resident gets an individual studio in a shared space with their own key, continuous one-to-one mentorship and assistance, a well-equipped communal workshop, critiques, visits to museums, galleries and artist studios in Thessaloniki, the chance to propose site-specific projects, presentations or workshops, and a group exhibition at the end of each residency where work may be sold. Accommodation is optional. bEam arts is an independent, self-funded organisation that receives no external financial support, so the cost sits with the artist: €1,850 per month with accommodation or €1,000 per month without, duo artists €250 or €150 extra. There is no application fee; selected artists pay a deposit of 50% of the residence fee, refundable if cancelled 30 or more days before the start date. The organisers encourage applicants to seek funding from national arts councils, foundations or universities and will supply letters of invitation and acceptance and tailored wording for funding applications. Open to artists working in any medium; the application asks for an artist statement and a project proposal of up to 250 words each, plus a CV and 5 to 10 portfolio images with dimensions, technique, title and date.
