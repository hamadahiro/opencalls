About the Hopper Prize Grants

The Hopper Prize is an unrestricted artist grant open to visual artists working in any medium — photography, painting, sculpture, video, installation, and mixed media all qualify. Unlike awards tied to a single discipline or theme, applicants submit work freely chosen from their existing practice, with no requirement to fit a curatorial brief.

Prize structure

The $13,000 total prize pool is split across six recipients in two tiers. Two artists receive $4,500 each as primary grant winners, and four artists receive $1,000 each as secondary recipients. Beyond the cash, all six winners — plus an additional 24 artists making up a 30-person shortlist — are featured in The Hopper Prize's online journal, providing editorial visibility for selected work.

How to apply

Submissions are made through the Hopper Prize website with a $40 entry fee. Applicants must be 18 or older and may submit up to 10 images per application. The current cycle deadline is May 12, 2026, with winners and shortlist announced on August 28, 2026. The juror for this cycle is Misa Jeffereis, Assistant Curator at the Contemporary Art Museum St. Louis (CAM), bringing curatorial perspective from one of the leading non-collecting contemporary art museums in the United States.

Why apply

Open-medium grants of this size with no thematic restriction are uncommon — most art grants restrict by discipline, residence, career stage, or theme. The Hopper Prize's combination of cash, online publication, and curated shortlist makes it useful for artists building their grant CV. The prize runs on a recurring cycle, with multiple application windows per year.
