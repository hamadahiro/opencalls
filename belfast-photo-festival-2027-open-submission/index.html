Belfast Photo Festival 2027 Open Submission

Prizes £2,000 Spotlight Award Exhibition

Belfast Photo Festival is the largest annual photography festival in the UK and Ireland, and its Open Submission is the route in: no set theme, no membership, no career stage required. The jury takes work of any subject, concept or approach, and the festival is explicit that previously exhibited, published or awarded work is eligible, as is work in progress and even installation shots — though those count towards the image limit. Entries must be photographic or lens-based, but may be combined with other forms such as performance, painting, sculpture, film, music or literature. Photographers, artists, curators, archives and collectives worldwide may enter, aged 16 or over, with a parent or guardian's consent at 16 and 17. Each entry is either a photography project of 1 to 15 images or a photobook as a PDF proof, and an entrant may enter as many as they like — but each is a separate entry carrying its own fee. What selection buys is scale rather than a cheque alone. Ten to fifteen artists are chosen; their work is produced as large-format prints by the festival at no cost to the artist and shown across the city of Belfast from 3 to 27 June 2027, with a partner exhibition produced by Photo Schweiz 2027, a contribution towards travel to the opening, a year of Royal Photographic Society membership, three one-to-one sessions with the curatorial team, international press coverage, and promotion to the festival's 30,000-plus social following and 13,000-plus subscribers. One selected artist takes the £2,000 Spotlight Award, which in 2026 the jury split between two photographers. The one cost that can land on a selected entrant is in the photobook route: a selected book must reach the festival professionally printed and bound by 1 May 2027, and shipping to Belfast, insurance and the cost of return are the entrant's responsibility. Shortlisted artists are contacted on 18 March 2027, results are announced in April, the festival opens on 3 June 2027, and the international portfolio reviews — which entrants get a discount on — run from 18 June. The fee is staged, and the organiser's own surfaces disagree on when the early rung closes: the call page and the Picter listing both say £22 per entry until 11:59pm GMT on 11 December 2026, while the festival's terms and conditions say 27 November 2026 in two separate clauses. Those same terms still give 23 October 2026 as the opening date although the call in fact opened on 11 November, so 27 November is the date to beat. After the early rung it is £26 per entry until the 5 February 2027 close, and the terms reserve the right to extend that deadline. The festival states where the money goes — it is a registered charity in Northern Ireland (NIC102819) and says entry fees fund exhibition production and printing, artist travel, jury fees, the submission platform and promotion, alongside core funding from the Arts Council of Northern Ireland and Belfast City Council, with more than £100,000 invested in artists' fees since 2024. Every eligible entry is scored independently by every juror before the full jury shortlists and selects together, and the jury is published in full: Charmaine Toh of Tate Modern, Roxana Marcoci of MoMA, Alessia Glaviano of PhotoVogue, Mikko Takkunen of The New York Times, Florian Ebner of the Centre Pompidou, Laura Ligari of the International Photography Festivals Association, and festival founder Michael Weir.
