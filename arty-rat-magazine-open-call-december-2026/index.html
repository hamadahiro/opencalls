Arty Rat Magazine Open Call — December 2026

Arty Rat is an independent art journal run from Vienna by curator Oleksandr Dyvnich, showcasing contemporary visual art, illustration and “bold creative voices from around the world”; the open call page addresses itself to “artists and photographers”. This is the next rolling cycle after the 1 November one, at the same standing call page. Submission is free, open worldwide to applicants 18 or older, and up to six works may be entered as JPG or PNG — either through the form on the call page, or by pasting a Google Drive or Dropbox link into it, or by emailing the files to the journal. Selected artists receive a free digital copy of the magazine as a PDF, photographs of their printed magazine pages and a feature on the journal's Instagram; two selected artists have their work on the magazine covers, and one selected artist takes part free of charge in a group exhibition at The Names Gallery in Vienna — a show the call page still dates only as “the end of August” with no year. A participation fee applies only if the work is selected, and the organiser lists the options openly: €35 for publication in the magazine, €65 for publication plus an artist's page on the website with biography, statement and photographs of works, €100 for a curatorial review covering magazine and website, and €100 for the Vienna exhibition. The fee is non-refundable. The most recent issue, Issue 5, was published in September 2026.
