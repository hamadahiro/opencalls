CITY Photography Exhibit

Prize Exhibition

Dusk Gallery is a Santa Fe, New Mexico photography gallery — a sister gallery to Decode Gallery in Tucson, both founded by fine art photographer Holly Hart. CITY is the gallery's call for work engaging with personal urban experience: quiet, unnoticed moments through the bustling energy of metropolitan life.

The brief is open in approach. Selection is by Holly Hart.

Selected work is shown in a physical exhibition at Dusk Gallery in Santa Fe. The gallery's production model mirrors Decode's — the gallery typically covers professional printing and framing for selected work.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the gallery's website with 5 or 10 images. Fee is $40–$45. The 2026 deadline was April 16; results announced approximately April 30, 2026.
