MY MICHIGAN: Our Rivers, Our Lakes

Prize Exhibition

A juried themed exhibition at the Grosse Pointe Artists Association's gallery on The War Memorial campus in Grosse Pointe Farms, Michigan, asking artists to "prove the truth behind the slogan 'Water Wonderland' that was coined in 1963" — Michigan's rivers and lakes, however they are read. The juror is Alex Gilford, an artist and park ranger the association describes as noted for his efforts to strengthen the relationship between the arts and nature. Photography is one of the media the organisers accept, and the call's own banner image is a photograph, Donald Cronkhite's "Nankin Lake, After the Storm". Open to all artists over 18 and to alumni of GPAA's Promising Artists exhibitions; original work only, nothing made under an instructor, nothing previously exhibited with GPAA, and no large installation pieces. One to six pieces per artist, with the entry fee covering the first two and a $3 transaction fee added at checkout. There are no size limits, but 2D work must arrive framed and wired ready to hang; accepted work is delivered in person or shipped with a prepaid return label to the gallery on 26 September. Note two things before entering: the organisers publish no awards or prizes of any kind for this show — the return is the two-month hang — and work priced to sell carries a 35% gallery commission for members, 45% for non-members. Exhibition runs 1 October to 4 December 2026, with a gallery talk on the opening evening.
