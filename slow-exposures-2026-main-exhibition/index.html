Slow Exposures 2026 — Main Exhibition

Annual juried exhibition celebrating photography of the contemporary American South. Work must be taken in one of 16 Southern states: Alabama, Arkansas, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Mississippi, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, and West Virginia. Selected photographs shown first at the historic R.F. Strickland Building in downtown Concord, GA, then move to the Cochran Gallery in LaGrange, GA. 30% commission on sales. Festival weekend September 18–20, 2026 in Zebulon, Georgia. Submit up to 6 images via Formsite. 23rd edition.
