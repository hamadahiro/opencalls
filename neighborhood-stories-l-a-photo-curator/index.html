Neighborhood Stories

Open call juried by Laurie Freitag, founder of L.A. Photo Curator, on the stories that make a neighbourhood, town or city its own — a local celebration, a holiday tradition, a street festival, a family business, a favourite gathering place, or the quiet rituals of everyday life. Portraits, street photography, documentary, travel, fine art, conceptual work, mobile photography and mixed media rooted in photography are all welcome. First place receives a solo online exhibition, a featured Q&A article with the juror's responses, and a month on the L.A. Photo Curator homepage; every entrant's work appears in the online group exhibition with artist statement, bio and website, and every entry is considered for Top 40 Images of the Year and Photographer of the Year. One participating photographer is also invited to serve as guest juror alongside Freitag on a future L.A. Photo Curator competition. 5% of entry fees go to No Kid Hungry and a further 5% to the winner's chosen charity. Submit 3 or 6 images (not 4 or 5) by email after PayPal payment; a limited number of fee waivers is available on request. Open to amateur and professional photographers worldwide.
