Blank Wall Gallery — Best Portfolio 2026

Prize Solo exhibition prize

Blank Wall Gallery is an Athens, Greece photography gallery (55 Fokionos Negri Street, 11361 Athens). The Best Portfolio competition is the gallery's annual international portfolio prize — structured to identify a single photographer whose body of work merits a solo exhibition.

One winner is selected. The prize is a two-week solo exhibition with 40 professionally produced prints — production and financing of the prints handled by Blank Wall Gallery — plus international promotion through the gallery's website and social channels. The total prize package is valued at over €2,200.

Submissions are made by email and require a portfolio of 10 to 20 images. The format favors a coherent body of work rather than a sampler of strong individual images. Entry fee is €50.

Eligibility is open globally to photographers aged 18 and over. The 2026 deadline is May 31; results announced June 2.
