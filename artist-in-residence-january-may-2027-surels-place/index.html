Artist-in-Residence — January–May 2027

Prizes 1-month residency Stipends

A month-long live-work residency in Garden City, Idaho, in the house built by the late artist Surel Mitchell. The residency is free to apply to and free to hold: rent, utilities and wi-fi are covered, and residents receive a $100-per-week living stipend, a $300 travel stipend, use of the house bicycles, free or discounted tickets to local art events, marketing through the organisation's channels, and logistics and installation help for their public events. Each resident is assigned a host who introduces them to the local arts community. The programme is open to professional visual, literary and performance artists — the org describes it as "any artist who needs a place to focus" — though the property cannot accommodate loud or messy processes such as welding or blacksmithing, and work that is predominantly conceptual may find less enthusiasm with the jury. Residents must live at the house for the residency (no studio-only stays), take no other employment during it, give at least one public workshop of two to three hours, present a show or lecture on the residency work, and donate one piece to Surel's Place for future fundraising. The commission on sales arising from the residency is 35%. Applicants must be 25 or older and non-smokers; partners may join but children and pets cannot. This September 1 deadline covers residencies running January to May 2027; applications for playwrights and screenwriters are paused until the March 2027 round. Visual artists apply by email with a cover letter, a proposal including a workshop outline, a CV, and 4 to 6 images with an image list.
