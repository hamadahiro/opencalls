Haus-a-rest — Issue 77: All About Me, Using Metaphors to Explore Identity

UK-based independent online zine, edited by Jenna Fox and Nichola Rodgers, calling for visual art and writing on identity told through metaphor. The brief asks artists to look past the literal self-portrait and consider how objects, animals, landscapes, colours, patterns, symbols and everyday things might stand in for different parts of who they are — "show us what represents you" — and says the work may be playful, reflective, abstract, humorous, challenging or deeply personal, since identity is layered and constantly evolving. Media are open and photography is named explicitly alongside painting, drawing and illustration, digital art, sculpture and 3D work, textiles, collage and mixed media, printmaking, ceramics, installation and conceptual art; writers may send short stories, essays, poetry, experimental and cross-genre work. Open internationally to artists and writers at any stage, including students, recent graduates and the self-taught — the editors say they are interested in ideas and practices rather than credentials. Free to enter and free if selected. Submit one to three artworks with title, year, medium, dimensions and a statement explaining the metaphor behind the work; a public Instagram handle is a stated requirement, because selected work is tagged across the zine's 11,000-follower platform, and the editors say they will not select artists without one. Fully AI-generated work is excluded: the work "must be resolved, not generated by AI". There is no cash prize and no payment to artists — selected works appear in a curated online group exhibition on haus-a-rest.com and in the December issue, typically online for about six months, and artists retain all rights.
