Fall 2026 - Spring 2027 Solo Exhibitions

Prize Solo exhibition prize

SRO Photo Gallery is the photography gallery within Texas Tech University's Landmark Arts program in Lubbock, Texas. The annual Solo Exhibitions open call selects programming for an academic year — six to eight solo exhibitions plus four moving image exhibitions across the Fall 2026 – Spring 2027 schedule.

Selected artists receive a full solo exhibition slot rather than inclusion in a group show — the structural premise is dedicated wall and time for each artist.

All styles, techniques, and subjects are welcome. Eligibility is open globally.

Submission is by email with 20 images representing the proposed exhibition. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 31; results announced early May 2026.
