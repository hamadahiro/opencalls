Open Call Pop-up-Galerien Herbst 2027 — Waldviertel/Industrieviertel

Prize Four-week exhibition

No medium is named anywhere in this call, in either direction. The organisers address "Künstlerinnen und Künstler sowie Kunsthandwerkerinnen und Kunsthandwerker" and ask for "konkrete Arbeiten, bewusste Raumnutzung und Einblicke in die künstlerische Arbeitsweise" — concrete works, deliberate use of the room, and insight into how the artist works — so a photographer competes on the same terms as a painter or a ceramicist.

What settles the medium question is the programme these two rooms actually ran, and both gallery pages publish it in full — 18 dated slots at Bad Fischau-Brunn for 2026 and 20 at Horn, with Horn noting "Derzeit sind alle Ausstellungstermine für 2026 vergeben". Horn's 2026 list includes "Fotoclub VHS Horn" over 27 June to 5 July, so photography has already hung in one of the two galleries this call is for. The organiser's remit points the same way: Verena Mayrhofer, who holds Kulturvernetzung's Coordination Office for Visual Art, is described by the organiser on its own Come In! Galerie page as an artist working in "Medienkunst, Installation, Fotografie".

Two things narrow this one hard. The regional gate is not Lower Austria at large but the Industrieviertel for Bad Fischau-Brunn and the Waldviertel for Horn, each region tied to one venue; and membership of Kulturvernetzung Niederösterreich is a condition of entry, €25 a year for artists and individuals. There is no entry fee and no fee for the room.

What is asked of a selected artist is heavier than the listing's bullet list suggests. The call's own two-page PDF adds "Selbstständiger Auf- und Abbau der Ausstellung" and makes the artist the organiser, not the guest, of the vernissage and the finissage — "Zu jeder Ausstellung sind eine selbst zu organisierende Vernissage und Finissage vorgesehen" — with attendance on both weekends compulsory at fixed hours: Saturday and Sunday 17:00–19:00 in Bad Fischau-Brunn, Saturday 17:00–19:00 and Sunday 15:00–17:00 in Horn. A further event is wanted but explicitly not required, while an idea for a self-run mediation format is a required part of the submission itself. A strong portfolio with no idea for the space, no capacity to install and run the show single-handed, and no willingness to appear on camera is the wrong submission for this one.
