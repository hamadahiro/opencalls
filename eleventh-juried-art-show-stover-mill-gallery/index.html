Eleventh Juried Art Show

Prize Over $3,000 in prizes

Eleventh juried exhibition at the Stover Mill Gallery in Erwinna — Bucks County's longest continually running art gallery, now in its 66th season, owned and maintained by the nonprofit Tinicum Civic Association. Open to all media including photography (no giclée reproductions); one work per artist, maximum 36 inches framed. More than $3,000 in prize money awarded through a blind jury process judging artistic excellence and aesthetic vision. Shown over four weekends October 17 – November 8, 2026, with a reception October 18; accepted artists may also offer two unframed portfolio pieces for sale. All works must be for sale; 30% commission supports the gallery.
