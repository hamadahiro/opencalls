Fellowship Program 2027–2028

Prizes $1,500 stipend Exhibition

Gallery Route One, the artist-run nonprofit in Point Reyes Station on the Marin coast, is offering two 24-month Fellowships running January 2027 to December 2028 to artists aged 21 to 40. Each Fellow receives a minimum of ten exhibition opportunities — including a solo exhibition in the Annex Gallery in 2027 and a two-person exhibition in the Center Gallery in 2028 — plus a $1,500 stipend to support the production and presentation of that work, mentoring and assistance producing the exhibitions, publicity and promotion, ongoing exposure on GRO's website and social media, and access to all GRO-sponsored professional development. Fellows also become eligible to be hired as Teaching Artists in GRO's Artists in the Schools programme, and work alongside the gallery's practising artist members curating and installing solo and group shows; GRO's call page adds that Fellows “do some volunteer work” during the fellowship period. Applicants must be aged 21 to 40, live and work in the greater Bay Area within commuting distance of Point Reyes Station, have a serious fine arts practice built on training, education and/or experience, and hold a cohesive portfolio of work completed within the last five years together with a developing focus or current area of inquiry and experimentation. GRO has run the Fellowship since 2014, and photography sits comfortably inside it — one past Fellow describes using the two years for a large-scale photography and oral history project, and the gallery separately runs a standing Latino Photography Project. The application asks for 8 to 10 images with dimensions in the filenames, an artist statement referencing two or three of them, a description of your studio practice, a CV and one reference, and it carries no entry fee. Applications are accepted only between 1 and 30 September 2026.
