2026 Architectural Photography Competition (APC26)

The biennial architectural photography competition of the American Institute of Architects' Long Beach/South Bay chapter, open to photographers as well as architects and designers — the chapter's own announcement invites "photographers, architects, designers, and lovers of the built environment". Entries are accepted 31 August to 31 October 2026 in three categories: Exterior (form, materials, context, light, landscape and the relationship between a building and its surroundings), Interior (composition, materiality, light, scale, detail and the experience of being within a space) and, new for 2026, Regional Focus, for architecture inside the chapter's own Long Beach/South Bay boundaries. All entries automatically qualify for a People's Choice award. Enter one category per image; multiple entries are welcome. The chapter states plainly that judging focuses on the photographic image rather than the architecture itself. No cash prize is published: winners are announced in early December 2026 and asked for a print-quality high-resolution file, and Regional Focus selections may additionally be redrawn as sketches for the 2027 Color Your City architecture coloring book with the photographer credited. In the 2024 edition the chapter published each winning photograph alongside written comments from all three jurors — Nirav Solanki, Janna Ireland and Paul Turang — plus an online gallery of every submission; the 2026 jury has not been announced on either the announcement or the entry guidelines.
