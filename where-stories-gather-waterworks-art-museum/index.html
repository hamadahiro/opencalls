Where Stories Gather

Prizes $300 Best in Show Exhibition

The WaterWorks Art Museum sits on the banks of the Yellowstone River in Miles City, Montana, in a 1910 water plant listed on the National Register of Historic Places - its galleries are the renovated basins that produced the town's drinking water for sixty years - and it has run an annual juried show since at least 2014 (the 2018 edition was titled Photography Plus). The 2026-27 edition, Where Stories Gather, invites artists working in two- and three-dimensional media to explore the places, memories, objects, traditions and encounters where narratives take shape, from landscapes, homes and communities to personal histories, cultural symbols and quiet moments of reflection. The prospectus names no media list beyond 2D and 3D, so photographers should note that eligibility is unenumerated; the museum's own juried-show history includes a photography edition. The juror is Jenn Hall Tooke, a museum artist and paleontology-trained community advocate who has worked at WaterWorks and the Carter County Museum in Ekalaka. Six cash awards: $300 Best in Show, $200, $150 and $100 honourable mentions, a $150 Director's Choice and a $100 People's Choice. Entries are open to artists nationwide; work must be original, made within the last two years, at least 12 inches on one side and no more than 60 inches in any direction, and for sale (35% commission). A $40 fee covers up to three entries, judged from digital images, with results emailed by December 22; accepted work is due January 14, the show runs from the January 29, 2027 reception to March 18, and a committee picks about 25 pieces for a smaller collection that tours galleries in eastern Montana for the rest of 2027. Shipping both ways is at the artist's expense, and an artist who withdraws an accepted piece is barred from the museum's juried shows for three years. Deadline Wednesday December 16, 2026 through OnlineJuriedShows.
