Hounds in the Hulse

Prize Group exhibition

A free community exhibition in the Hulse Gallery at Art Center Waco built entirely from work about pets and companion animals — furry, feathered, scaled or otherwise. The centre addresses the call to pet lovers throughout McLennan County and the surrounding area, and is explicit that you do not have to be a professional artist to take part: it welcomes submissions from artists at every level, from community members, and from pet owners who commissioned a portrait of their animal. Every medium is eligible and photography is named alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, mixed media, fibre art, sculpture and ceramics, so long as the work features or is inspired by a pet. Each person may submit up to three artworks through the centre's Google Form, with no entry fee. Work must arrive ready to display safely in a gallery: there is no minimum size, nothing may exceed 72 inches in any direction, wall pieces are capped at 60 pounds, and framed work needs secure hanging hardware. Gallery space is limited, so submitting does not guarantee inclusion — Art Center Waco says it will show as many community submissions as it can while keeping the hang safe and considered. Selected participants are sent drop-off, exhibition and pickup details afterwards. Submissions close 31 August 2026.
