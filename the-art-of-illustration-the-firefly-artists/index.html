The Art of Illustration

Prizes $150 $75 $50

The Firefly Artists, an artist cooperative founded in Northport, New York in 2011 and now running a gallery at 90 Main Street, asks for work that tells a story: book covers, movie posters, magazine spreads, comic pages, illustrated passages, political cartoons, articles or poems, with type incorporated or sent separately. Pieces are judged "not only on artistic merit but also how well it tells and sells the story". The framing is illustration-led - the gallery's own calendar heads it "Illustrator Show" - but photography is not a guest here: it is named in the accepted-media list on both the eligibility and the requirements pages ("drawing, painting, photography, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, collage, fabric work, glasswork, jewelry and digital media"), and the gallery's no-reproductions rule carries an explicit carve-out, "No reproductions will be accepted, with the exception of photography and digital art". Editorial, book-cover and photo-illustration work is squarely in scope. Entry is $35 for the first image and $5 for each of up to three more; artists nationwide may apply and there is no age bar. Awards are First Prize $150, Second Prize $75, Third Prize $50 and Honorable Mention, and nothing in the awards block narrows the eligible pool by medium. Work must be original and human-made - AI-generated art is not eligible, nor is anything made under close instruction in a workshop or class. Maximum frame size is 36 inches on the longest side, sculpture no larger than 12 inches in any direction; accepted work must arrive framed, wired and ready to hang, with plexiglass rather than glass on anything 16x20 inches or larger. All work must be for sale and the gallery takes a 35% commission; artists insure their own work while it is on view. Acceptance letters go out 22 October, selected work drops off 1, 4 and 5 November, the show hangs from Saturday 7 November to Sunday 6 December 2026 in the gallery's Darcy Arts Center exhibition space with a reception on Thursday 12 November, and pickup runs 7, 9 and 12 December - after which the gallery charges $5 a day in storage, and work still unclaimed 90 days after the show closes becomes the property of the gallery.
