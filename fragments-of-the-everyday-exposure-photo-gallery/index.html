Fragments of the Everyday

Prizes $500 Solo exhibition (Gold Award)

Exposure Photo Gallery's third open call invites photographers to submit images that find meaning in the world around us — places, people, gestures, scenes and moments, from the extraordinary to the seemingly ordinary. There is no single way to interpret the everyday: it can be found on a street or in a remote landscape, in a face, a piece of architecture, an object, a spontaneous scene or a deliberate composition; what matters is the photographer's perspective. The juror is Steve McCurry, the American photographer whose Afghan Girl is among the most recognisable photographs in the world and who has spent more than five decades portraying cultures, people and scenes of everyday life. Selected work is shown in a group exhibition at the gallery in Río Tercero, Córdoba: a Gold, a Silver and a Bronze Award, ten Honorable Mentions whose prints hang in the physical show, thirty-seven Juror's Selections shown digitally, and special recognitions reserved for photographers from Córdoba Province. The Gold Award winner also takes US$500 and a solo exhibition at the gallery, on a date arranged with the artist. Selected and winning works appear in a print-on-demand exhibition book, and an Online Gallery Special Recognition marks roughly the top 7.5 per cent of entries. All awards are issued as digital diplomas. Fees are tiered by image count and there is no cap on how many photographs may be submitted. Entries close 31 March 2027 at 11:59 PM Pacific time; winners are announced 3 May 2027 and hi-res files are due 14 May.
