HNDL Magazine — PRIDE Issue

Prize Publication prize

HNDL Magazine is an indie publication by and for neurodivergent, disabled, and chronically ill creatives. The PRIDE Issue is themed around Pride from within the publication's editorial frame — work by creatives who hold both disability and queer identities, and the layered experience that produces.

The publication is multidisciplinary: art, poetry, creative nonfiction, prose, photography, and zines all eligible. The editorial preference for this issue is candid photography and creative nonfiction. Written pieces are capped at 4 pages; no PDFs accepted.

AI-generated work is not accepted. Eligibility is restricted to neurodivergent, disabled, and chronically ill creatives.

The publication is unpaid — contributors receive a printed copy of the issue. Submission is by Google Form with up to 3 pieces. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 30.
