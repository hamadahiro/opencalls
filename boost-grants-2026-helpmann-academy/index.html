Boost Grants 2026

Prize A$500

A$500 micro-grants for emerging South Australian creatives, made possible through the generosity of John Phillips. The money is meant to cover the ordinary costs that stall a young practice: purchasing, hiring or upgrading technology and equipment, where the Academy names cameras and associated equipment among the eligible buys; training that helps you develop or adapt your practice; workshops and seminars; materials and tools, including printing and framing; operational costs such as studio rental, website hosting and insurance; and audience-development costs such as paid advertising, posters and social media strategists. Photography is one of the Visual Arts specialisations on the application form, sitting between Installation and Moving Image. Applications are invited from graduates of supported creative degrees at Adelaide University and Flinders University, and from graduates of the former University of South Australia and University of Adelaide, for up to five years from 31 December of their final year of study; honours students qualify only where honours followed directly from the undergraduate degree, master's and PhD candidates only through their earlier study, and final-year students are not eligible for this round. There is no application fee. The form asks for a two-page artist CV, an official academic transcript, a budget on the Academy's own template and four to six images or one to two audio-visual demos, alongside written answers on your practice and on how the funds would boost it. Assessment weighs alignment with career development, feasibility and value, potential impact, and capacity to build new networks, collaborations or partnerships. Funded activity must take place between 1 January and 31 December 2027, the panel may award less than the sum requested, and there is no retrospective funding for work already begun.
