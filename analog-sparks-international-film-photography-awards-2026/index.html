Analog Sparks — International Film Photography Awards 2026

International awards discovering and promoting analog photographers worldwide. Categories: Architecture, Fine Art, Human, Lifestyle, Nature, Photo Journalism, Technique, Zines & Photo Books. Accepts color/B&W film, instant film, collodion, alternative techniques, and hybrid analog-digital (no purely digital). Winners exhibited at House of Lucie in Athens and Ostuni.

Jury: Barna Erdesz, Art Director & Gallerist · Danial Shah, Documentary Photographer · Eva Szombat, Visual Artist · Hossein Farmani, Founder · Ibrahim Mursal, Visual Artist & Writer · Kenro Izu, Photographer · Kira Krasz, Visual Artist · Mikkel Horlyck, Photojournalist · Mo Laudi, Curator · Wanda Martin, Canon Ambassador
