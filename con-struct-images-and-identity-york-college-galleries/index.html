CON/STRUCT: Images and Identity

Prize Exhibition

Free, photography-only juried exhibition at Gallery Hall, Marketview Arts — York College of Pennsylvania's venue in the Downtown York arts district — running 13 November 2026 to 30 January 2027, with an opening reception on 13 November at 5pm. CON/STRUCT examines photography's dual power to actively build identity and enforce social expectations, and the curators read the word for its own tension: the "con" of image fabrication against the rigid "structures" of gender, status and belonging. The show pairs photographs from Ryan Scott Oliver's musical 35mm with a juried selection in the main gallery and an uncurated community wall alongside it, so featured artists and public participants appear together. There are two levels of participation and applicants say which they are applying for: framed, install-ready physical artwork in the main gallery space, or digital images for a repeating slideshow projection in the space adjacent to it — the digital route means no shipping. Curated by Ashley Moog Bowlsbey and Matthew Clay-Robison, director of the York College Galleries. Open to all artists over 18 residing within the United States who work with the photography medium in any form; up to three artworks each, submitted as JPG or JPEG files up to 10MB, sRGB recommended for both black-and-white and colour, named LastName_FirstName_ArtworkTitle_Submission#.jpeg. Submission is free and artists pay only to ship accepted work, which is accepted 6–9 November and must arrive by 9 November; drop-off times and the shipping address are sent on acceptance. Artists are notified by 30 October 2026. The venue is 37 W Philadelphia St, York, PA 17401. One practical warning: the organiser's Google Form is sign-in-gated, so you will need to be logged into a Google account to open it at all — the full terms above are on the call listing, which is readable without one.
