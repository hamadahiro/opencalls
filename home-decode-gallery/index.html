HOME

Prize Exhibition

Decode Gallery is a Tucson, Arizona photography gallery founded by conceptual fine art photographer Holly Hart, who also juries the gallery's open calls. HOME is the gallery's call for work engaging with the multifaceted nature of home — as feeling, memory, sanctuary, physical space, heritage, or identity.

The brief invites domestic spaces, loved ones, landscapes, or personal belonging. The thematic framing is broader than literal residence — what counts as home is left to the photographer.

Selection structure: at least 26 images selected for exhibition at Decode Gallery in Tucson, June 6–27, 2026. The gallery covers production: complimentary professional printing on Epson printers with Ilford Pearl paper, museum-quality 22×22 white framing, and custom matting.

Eligibility is open worldwide to all skill levels. Submission is through the gallery's website with 5 or 10 images. Fee is $40 for 5 images. The 2026 deadline was April 18; results announced approximately May 2, 2026.
