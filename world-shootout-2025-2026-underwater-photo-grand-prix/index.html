World ShootOut 2025–2026 — Underwater Photo Grand Prix

Now in its twentieth year, an underwater photography competition running since the Eilat Red Sea Shootout in 2005, with 884 winners and more than $284,000 in cash awarded alongside dive travel prizes. Images must be made underwater or half-in, half-out of the water, and taken between 2 November 2024 and 1 November 2026. Nine categories are open: Best 5 Images, Wide Angle, Macro & Super Macro, Amateurs, UW Fashion, Black Water, BigAnimals Behaviour, Video Clips of the World and Best Picture of the Year, the last of which may reuse images entered elsewhere. Fees are charged per category and per set — €25 for a single image in most categories, €15 in Amateurs, €35 for a first set of five in Best 5 Images and €45 for a video clip — with the per-image rate falling as sets are added. Awards include €2,000 and the Andromeda statuette for Best Picture of the Year, chosen by the director of the boot Düsseldorf dive show; €1,000 cash each in Black Water and Amateurs, a €2,000 SEACAM voucher in Wide Angle and a Papua New Guinea liveaboard trip for two worth about $11,640 in Macro; and a Papua New Guinea dive package for two worth about $18,900 for the Best 5 Images winner, plus resort packages and equipment from the competition's dive-industry sponsors. The Amateurs category is restricted to entrants who have never won a World ShootOut prize and to GoPro, phone and fixed-lens compact cameras. Minor editing is allowed — colour and contrast, backscatter removal, up to 15% cropping, minor flaw fixes — but moving or removing subjects, compositing and any use of AI are prohibited, and semi-finalists must supply original RAW and JPEG files on request. Judging is by an international panel including National Geographic's David Doubilet: semi-finalists are named 25 November 2026, category finalists 25 December, and the winners at the boot Düsseldorf show on 30 January 2027.
