43rd Center Annual

Houston Center for Photography's annual group exhibition, now in its 43rd year. Seeks to highlight current themes, technologies, and practices in photography. Features diverse works from HCP members worldwide — emerging, mid-career, and established photographers. Two artists selected for $500 HCP Juried Membership Honoraria. This year juried by Asha Iman Veal, curator for globally collaborative exhibitions of contemporary art. Exhibition runs June 4 – August 16, 2026.
