Palermo Takeover 2027

Palermo Galerie is handing over its programme. From June to December 2027 the Stuttgart project space is opening its rooms to artists, collectives and curatorial teams and inviting independent proposals for exhibitions, performances, screenings, discussions and other experimental formats — “Your programme. Your ideas.” The space itself is the story: Palermo occupies the vacant former Schwabenbräu-Passage in Stuttgart-Bad Cannstatt, repurposed as a temporary gallery and project room for contemporary art until the owner modernises or demolishes the site, and it is run by Kunstverein Dialekt e.V., which has been supported by the City of Stuttgart's Kulturamt since 2005, with the gallery project additionally backed by the city's Liegenschaftsamt and Wirtschaftsförderung, the Bad Cannstatt district council, Dinkelacker Schwabenbräu, Werkstatthaus and Stuttgarter Jugendhaus gGmbH. The association states its position plainly: exhibitions are developed through cooperative and solidaristic processes, the focus is not the economic valorisation of artistic practice but the recognition of its intrinsic value and social dimension, and artists are treated not as entrepreneurial agents producing for a market segment but as producers of work that exists independently of exchange-value logic. It also writes the possibility of failure into its ethos as a productive force that enables experimentation. Be clear about what is and is not on offer, because the call is. What you get: rent-free space, existing technical equipment by arrangement and subject to availability, and 70% of food and drink revenue for your team with 30% going to the association. A total of €5,000 in funding has been applied for — and the call states that support will only be available if that funding is approved, so it cannot be counted on. What you carry: financing, organisation, publicity and any additional event permits required. This is a venue and a platform rather than a funded commission, which makes it a fit for a photographer or collective that already has a show in mind and wants a real room and an engaged audience rather than a prize. Applications run from 1 November 2026 to 15 January 2027 and consist of your concept, team information, a preliminary programme schedule, a funding strategy and work samples or links, sent by email. The call is published in German and English and sets no restriction on where applicants are based.
