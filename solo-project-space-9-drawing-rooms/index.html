Solo Project Space #9

Drawing Rooms, the Jersey City gallery run by the non-profit Victory Hall Inc. inside the Topps Building at the Mana Contemporary complex, hands its Project Space and up to four further solo gallery areas to artists with a cohesive body of work or an installation - any theme, any concept, any size or medium. The accepted-media list runs painting or drawing on paper and canvas, collage or textiles, photography, prints and sculpture; work must be original, with no AI and no reproductions. One artist takes the main Project Space and up to four more are given solo areas for the autumn slot, 29 October to 22 November 2026, with an opening reception on Friday 30 October. Installation artists are welcome and are asked to supply a full description of materials and install time. Entry is $35 for up to 15 unframed images at least 1200 pixels on the short side, applicants must be over 18, and all exhibited work must be for sale with 60% of proceeds to the artist. Accepted work is dropped off in Jersey City on 16, 17 or 18 October, or shipped to arrive by Monday 19 October, with shipping arranged directly with the director. Artists selected for any 2026 show get preferred status for the gallery's end-of-year Big Small Show, and may also be picked up for other Drawing Rooms programmes including The Art Project JC lobbies and the gallery's Artsy.net exhibits.
