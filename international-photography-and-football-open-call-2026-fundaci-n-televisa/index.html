International Photography and Football Open Call 2026

International open call for photographers to capture football/soccer in any form — packed stadiums, dirt fields, color or black and white, cameras or phones. Winners exhibited alongside the Annie Leibovitz Futbol 2026 exhibition at the National Museum of Anthropology in Mexico City. Submit 1–3 images. Judged on originality, impact, and narrative quality.

Jury: Annie Leibovitz, Photographer · Pablo Ortiz Monasterio, Photographer & Editor · Carlos Cuarón, Filmmaker · Ana Hop, Photographer
