Experimental Photo Festival 2027 — Artists Open Call

Prize €200–€500

The eighth Experimental Photo Festival runs in Barcelona from July 21 to 25, 2027, and this is the call for the artists who build its programme — distinct from the festival's exhibition call. It is international and aimed at photographers, visual artists, multidisciplinary artists and collectives who explore, question or transform photography through technique, materials, concept, technology or interdisciplinary practice; the festival says you do not have to call yourself an experimental photographer to apply. A proposal is not a portfolio submission: each applicant proposes two workshops — one of four hours, built on a technique, process or methodology they know well, and one of eight hours that lets participants go deeper into a process, research or experimental approach — plus one discursive activity, either a conference, a roundtable or a project or research presentation, lasting 20 to 40 minutes. Each workshop carries a materials budget of up to €50 covered by the Experimentalphotofestival Association, and an exhibition proposal may optionally be included. Applying is free: the proposal form is a document downloaded from the festival's own call page and submitted through its online form together with a biography of up to 250 words in the applicant's own language, a PDF dossier, ten JPGs of the work and a portrait. SELECTED ARTISTS ARE PAID, AND CHOOSE BETWEEN TWO COMPENSATION OPTIONS: six nights in the festival's official accommodation (stated value €300) plus €200 in cash, or €500 in cash with accommodation arranged and paid by the artist. Either option also includes meals during the festival, full artist accreditation, official 2027 merchandise, and a place on the twelve-month Diploma in Experimental Photography at Ágora, School of Experimentation — more than 60 techniques and processes, specialisation, mentoring and a final personal project — which the festival values at €999. Travel to and from Barcelona is not covered, and payment requires an invoice issued in the artist's name. The festival publishes a financial transparency page stating that 94 people receive compensation through it — 50 artists, 14 staff and 30 volunteers — while 150 people pay a registration fee to attend. Selection is by the Experimentalphotofestival Association team against three criteria: connection to the festival's three challenges (combining knowledge and processes, building meaning beyond technique, and treating creation as a space for exchange), contribution to gender balance in the programme, and originality; applications are read individually and then weighed collectively against the whole 2027 programme. Selected artists must be in Barcelona from July 20 to 26, 2027, with July 20 given to workshop preparation, material review and technical checks, and sign an agreement covering activities, compensation, production, cancellation and image rights. Artists retain copyright and ownership of their work; the Association takes a non-exclusive right to reproduce submitted images, always credited, for promotion, exhibition and editorial use, and to record the festival itself. The call opened October 12 and selected artists are announced in December.
