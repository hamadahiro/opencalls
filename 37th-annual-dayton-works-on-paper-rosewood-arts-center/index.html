37th Annual Dayton Works on Paper

Prizes $1,100 in awards People's Choice Award Exhibition

Rosewood Arts Center, the City of Kettering's arts facility just outside Dayton, Ohio, runs its 37th Annual Dayton Works on Paper, a juried exhibition “created for artists living within a 40-mile radius of Dayton, Ohio” — a radius the centre's own prospectus notes “excludes Columbus and Cincinnati”. The show “features two-and three-dimensional works of art on or of paper”, so the support rather than the medium is the test and a photographic print on paper qualifies. The organiser says so itself in its last published prospectus (2025, the 35th annual): “Entries must be original works in any medium on or of paper”. This edition's entry form asks each entrant to type the “Medium of Work” as free text and names no accepted or excluded media, and photographers have been selected repeatedly — a Dayton landscape photographer's CV records selection at the 32nd, 33rd and 34th Annual, “1 of 60 selected from 241 prints” at the 34th, and a Merit Award at the 32nd. The juror distributes “artist awards totaling $1,100”, and gallery visitors choose a People's Choice Award funded through the Joan W. McCoy Memorial Art Fund. The juror for this edition is not named on the organiser's page. Entering is a two-part, in-person process rather than an upload: you complete the Works on Paper 2027 Digital Entry Form (one form covers all three works, asking title, year, medium, dimensions, insurance value and price for each), then bring the physical work and the printed Entry Form/Prospectus to Rosewood Arts Center, 2655 Olson Drive, Kettering, on Saturday 2 January 2027 (the page prints both 12pm–3pm and 1–3pm for this slot) or Monday 4 January 2027, 12–8pm. The entry fee is $20 for up to three artworks. The 2027 prospectus is not online — the page says it can be collected at Rosewood or at playkettering.org/gallery, and it is not on that page today — and the 2025 one carried conditions this edition's page does not repeat: jurying from the actual work rather than from files, a 40-inch width cap without the frame, work completed within the last four years, and nothing previously exhibited at the Gallery. Jury results go out by email by 9pm on Monday 11 January 2027; declined work is collected 12–16 January; the exhibition runs 19 January – 27 February 2027 with a closing reception on Saturday 27 February, and exhibiting work is picked up after the reception or on Monday 1 March, 12–8pm. Work is offered for sale and the price you enter must include the City of Kettering's 30% commission. Every entrant ticks agreement to the Rosewood Gallery Group Exhibition Agreement, a four-page contract linked from the entry form, and it is worth reading before you enter: the artist arranges, pays for and insures framing, packing, shipping and delivery in both directions; Rosewood covers damage only while the work is on display, to a maximum of $20,000; and work left more than fifteen calendar days after the exhibition closes becomes “Abandoned Artwork”, which waives the artist's Visual Artists Rights Act rights of attribution and integrity and transfers ownership to the City, which may then sell, alter or destroy it.
