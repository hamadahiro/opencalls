MUSE Photography Awards 2026

Prize $3,000 prize

MUSE Photography Awards is run by International Awards Associate, the same group that operates several other geographically-titled photography competitions. The 2026 edition runs across 14+ categories including Fine Art, Commercial, Nature, People, Minimalist, Editorial, Black & White, Architecture, Fashion, and Mobile.

The structure is split: separate Professional and Amateur/Student divisions, with the same categories on each side.

Top prize is $3,000. Winners are featured on Muse.World, the platform's annual digital showcase.

The 2026 jury is large — over 20 photographers, art directors, and creative professionals from multiple countries.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through the awards website with single image or series (2–8 images). Fee is tiered $25–$55. The 2026 deadline was March 26; results announced April 10, 2026.
