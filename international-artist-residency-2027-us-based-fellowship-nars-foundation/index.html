International Artist Residency 2027 — US-Based Fellowship

Prizes Residency Fellowship

The NARS Foundation's International Artist Residency Program supports early- to mid-career artists working across all disciplines with three- and six-month residencies in Sunset Park, Brooklyn, alongside an international cohort. This is the autumn open call for US-based artists, covering 2027 Season I (January-March), Season II (April-June) and the two seasons together. Residents get a 24/7 studio of 250-300 square feet, three to four studio visits a month from curators, critics, art historians and gallerists, weekly A.S.A.P. gatherings, artist talks, panels, screenings and salons, and the season closes with a group exhibition in the NARS gallery and an open-studio event. NARS subsidises the cost for every selected US-based artist through a Partial Fellowship: for 2027 the fellowship is $7,292-$7,792 against a one-season programme fee of $9,962-$10,742, leaving the artist responsible for $2,950-$3,450, payable in full or in monthly instalments. One US-based artist per season also receives a Full Fellowship covering the entire programme fee, awarded by a jury of NARS staff and invited art professionals on artistic quality, level of need, studio practice and likely benefit from the NARS community; applicants must tick the relevant section of the form to be considered. The studio is a workspace only - housing, travel, materials and living costs are the artist's own, and there are no specialist facilities, though residents get access to Materials for the Arts. Photography is explicitly among the practices NARS expects, though it supplies no darkroom or equipment. Open to US-based visual artists with an active professional practice of at least three years, including international artists resident in the US for three years or more; students may apply if their programme ends before the residency starts. Applications ran 31 August to 21 September 2026, 11:59pm ET. Materials: a CV, a 400-word residency proposal, a 300-word bio and up to ten work samples. NARS is a 501(c)(3) supported in part by public funds from the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs.
