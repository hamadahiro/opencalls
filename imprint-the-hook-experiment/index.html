IMPRINT

A juried international group exhibition about the impressions that shape us — the visible and invisible traces of memory, identity, history and human experience, and the stories we inherit, rewrite or leave behind. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside drawing, painting, printmaking, assemblage, wood, glass, ceramics and collage; video, electronics and anything needing power are excluded, and every finished work must measure exactly 10 x 10 inches. The Hook Experiment is a 4,500-square-foot gallery and creative space in a partially renovated building on Oxford, Pennsylvania's main street, founded to give artists room for risk and imperfection outside commercial pressure. Selected work hangs November 20, 2026 – January 16, 2027 with opening and closing receptions plus school and community engagement events; artists keep 70% of sales and may mark work NFS. Artists ship both ways with a prepaid return label or hand-deliver on November 7–8.
