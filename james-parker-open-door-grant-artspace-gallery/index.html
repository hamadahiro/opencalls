The James Parker “Open Door” Grant

Prizes Up to $2,000 Solo exhibition

Artspace, the artist-run 501(c)(3) gallery in Richmond, Virginia, has opened a new grant aimed squarely at the artists most open calls price out. The James Parker “Open Door” Fund was established in 2026 in memory of a former Artspace member, through a donation from his widow Beq Parker, and the gallery says the grant is there to “amplify the voices of Richmond, VA artists from all backgrounds, particularly those identifying with underrepresented races and ethnicities, early-career creators, or under-resourced artists”, by removing financial and institutional barriers. The package is worth over $2,500: up to $2,000 in cash towards project development, equipment, materials and execution; a one-year Artspace Artist Membership with dues waived (valued at $480); at least three one-hour mentorship sessions with a seasoned Artspace artist member covering project management, budgeting and exhibition planning; a place on the Artspace Exhibition Committee in an advisory capacity; and a culminating public event in 2027–28 — a solo exhibition, performance, workshop or open studio hosted or supported by the gallery. Entry is free and open to artists and creators working in any medium, photography included, with the application deliberately built to avoid gatekeeping: there is no CV or résumé requirement, and selection turns on project potential instead. Applicants send up to five images, media files or web links of recent work, a brief proposal for a project or body of work to be completed in 2027, a resource and support plan, and two narrative answers — how the $2,000 and the gallery's resources would supply what their practice is currently missing, and what next step the year of support would make reachable. THE ONE HARD BAR IS GEOGRAPHIC: applicants must live in the Richmond, VA metropolitan area, and the selected artist has to be local enough to take part in gallery operations, including gallery sitting and Fourth Friday support. Applications close 27 November 2026; portfolios and proposals are reviewed 28 November – 11 December, the top three finalists are interviewed 18–25 December, and the winner and an alternate are announced on 6 January 2027.
