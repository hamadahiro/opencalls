Celebrate Colorado: Show Us Your Downtown

Colorado Photographic Arts Center juried online exhibition celebrating downtowns across Colorado — historic landmarks, civic spaces, contemporary developments and architectural details, with interpretations of 'downtown' intentionally open. Open to Colorado photographers 18 and over; film, digital, mobile and screen-capture work are all accepted. Thirty images are selected by juror Darrin Alfred, Curator of Architecture and Design at the Denver Art Museum, for an online exhibition on CPAC's website from August 21 to December 31, 2026. Best in Show and honorable mentions receive CPAC memberships.
