MONOCHROME

NYC4PA's international juried call MONOCHROME celebrates the special nature of the monochrome print — black and white, sepia, or shades of a single color — where emotion, texture and beauty are expressed in gradations from light to dark. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers; any photographic process is welcome (2-D work only) and judging is blind. $4,000 in cash awards go to ten prizewinners — a $1,100 Grand Prize, three First Prizes of $400, three Second Prizes of $325 and three Third Prizes of $250 — alongside 20 Juror Selections and 20 Honorable Mentions, all featured in the NYC4PA online gallery and Prize Winners catalog with an award certificate. Images awarded an NYC4PA prize within the past two years are not eligible.
