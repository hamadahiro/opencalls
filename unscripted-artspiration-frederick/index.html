Unscripted

Open-themed juried exhibition at Artspiration Frederick, a nonprofit community art center in Frederick, Maryland, welcoming artwork that is free from prescribed narratives. Accepted media include painting, drawing, photography, printmaking, sculpture, fiber art, ceramics, mixed media, and digital art. Open to visual artists 18+ residing in the continental US; 1–3 images at 300 dpi for jury review. Exhibition runs August 1–30, 2026 at the Artspiration Frederick Gallery. Deadline 11:59pm EST.
