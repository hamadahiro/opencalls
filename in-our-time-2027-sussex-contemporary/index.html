IN OUR TIME 2027

Prizes Exhibition Catalogue

Sussex Contemporary's contemporary response to the Bayeux Tapestry: a collective portrait of life in 2027, made by UK and French artists and shown at The Atrium, Marine Workshops, Newhaven, from 21 August to 12 September 2027 with free public admission. The brief is deliberately open — a personal experience, a public event, a community, a concern, a celebration or an everyday observation; the organisers state the work need not depict the Tapestry or Norman history at all, and "we're leaving the narrative to you". Like the organisation's PASSAGE call this is what Sussex Contemporary calls a democratic exhibition: the FAQ states plainly "There is no selection panel or competitive judging", and entry guarantees inclusion provided the finished work meets the published requirements and arrives by the delivery deadline. The format is the constraint, and it is tight: every contributor works on the same supplied A5 canvas-covered board (148 × 210mm, portrait orientation), unframed, with a maximum total depth of 1cm including any raised elements, and the works hang in one continuous line with no gaps between them. Three-dimensional work is excluded for that reason. Participation is £40, which covers the board, guaranteed inclusion, a listing in the printed and online catalogues and a private view invitation for two on 21 August 2027; board collection from Seawhite's showroom in Partridge Green is free, and delivery and return postage are optional extras of £3 to £14.50 depending on whether the board goes to a UK or French address and whether unsold work is posted back. Sending the finished artwork to Sussex Contemporary is arranged and paid for by the artist. All work must be for sale and stays up until the exhibition closes; artists set their own price, receive 70 percent and the organisation retains 30 percent commission including VAT, with payment for sold work made on 1 October 2027. Entries close 9 July 2027 at 23:59 UK time, catalogue information is due by 22 July and completed artwork must arrive by 30 July. The guarantee has a limit the call page does not print. The linked terms and conditions, which are the binding document, reserve to Sussex Contemporary the right to accept or decline any work submitted entirely at its discretion, with its decision final, and to withdraw a work whose display could expose the organisation to reputational or legal risk. The same terms extend the 30 percent commission to any sale made within three months of the exhibition closing as a result of the display, charge £15 per week per work for late collection, and treat a work not removed within 28 days of the final collection date as abandoned, which allows Sussex Contemporary to sell or dispose of it without notifying the artist. Unsold work can be collected at Marine Workshops on Monday 13 September 2027 between 10am and 3pm. Submitting grants the organisation an irrevocable, royalty-free consent in perpetuity to reproduce the work for promotion, press, signage and catalogue use. French entrants should note that return postage covers a packed work of up to 500g, above which the organisers ask to be contacted before entering, and that any import taxes are the artist's.
