The Woman Artist Award — 7th Edition

Prizes $500 Trophy Publication

The Woman Artist Award is the annual international call through which Arts to Hearts Project selects women artists for Women Artists on the Rise, its hardcover coffee-table book series. The Award is in its 7th edition; the book will be its 5th, because the two have separate publication histories.

The theme is open. The organisers list photography among the eligible mediums by name, alongside painting, drawing, illustration, sculpture, printmaking, collage, ceramics, textiles, fiber art, installation, performance, digital art, mixed media and interdisciplinary practice, and state there are no geographical restrictions.

Eligibility is women artists aged 18 and above, emerging and mid-career, worldwide. All work must be original and created by the applicant, and the same artwork may not be submitted twice in one application.

The Main Prize Winner receives US$500, a bespoke engraved glass trophy with a certificate, a full-length interview in Women Artists on the Rise - 5th Edition, an extensive interview on the Arts to Hearts website with social features, a one-year Women in Arts Network portfolio feature valued at US$196, and a one-year subscription to the Arts to Hearts print and digital magazine. The first runner-up receives the trophy, certificate, website interview, a one-year network feature and the magazine; the second runner-up the same with a six-month network feature and the digital magazine. The top three are offered mentorship calls and a Fiber Art Now subscription.

Entry is by package and non-refundable: the FAQ lists US$21 for one artwork, $35 for three, $47 for five, $62 for ten and $100 for twenty. The entry form’s own first rung disagrees with that FAQ and reads $12 for one image, the same price the 6th edition charged; the other four rungs match. A $27 optional add-on buys a customised artist interview, published only if you are selected. Images must be high-resolution JPG or PNG, and the form asks for titles, dimensions, mediums, year of creation and an artist biography.

The final deadline is November 30, 2026. The page states no closing time, but the organiser’s own submission record timestamps the close at 23:45 India time that day - 18:15 UTC, early afternoon in the Americas - so this is not a local-midnight cutoff. Selection is by an independent jury whose size the record gives as five and whose members it does not name, and no notification date is published. The previous edition, the 6th, closed on March 27, 2026 and was won by Elen Bezhen with a US$500 cash award; the 5th edition’s winner, Anastasia Pavlovskaya, received US$1,000.

Applying requires an Instagram handle: thirteen of the application form’s fifteen fields are mandatory, including Instagram, phone number, date of birth and full postal address. Only the website field and the question "What would receiving this award mean for you and your career as an artist?" are optional.
