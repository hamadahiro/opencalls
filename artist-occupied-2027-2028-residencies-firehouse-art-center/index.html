Artist Occupied 2027/2028 Residencies

Prizes Residency Solo exhibition Project stipend

The Firehouse Art Center in Longmont, Colorado runs two Artist Occupied residencies a year, each working in the public South Gallery before a culminating solo exhibition in the Main Gallery, with the option to guest curate the accompanying South Gallery show. One application covers both cycles: the summer residency runs 1 July to 11 September 2027 with its exhibition opening 11 September 2027, and the winter residency runs 1 November 2027 to 6 February 2028 with its exhibition opening 11 February 2028. The centre frames the programme as professional development for emerging and experienced visual artists, connecting visitors to working artist environments and processes. The studio is 350 sq ft with natural light and tall ceilings, a full sink, limited basement storage, wi-fi, 24-hour building access and use of the kiln. Residents are asked to work in the space at least ten hours a week — five hours on each of two days during open gallery hours, Wednesday to Sunday 12–5pm — so that the public can watch the work happen, and to schedule at least three public events such as a workshop or artist talk, with workshop fees paid to the artist. One piece of original work must be donated to the Firehouse for its annual Gala auction. A materials budget may be submitted with the application; the stipend is project-specific and set by the Exhibits Committee. Applying is free: complete the centre's form and email a PDF with a site-specific proposal for the work to be made during the residency, a statement of why you want the residency, an artist statement, an account of how your work or process will engage the community during studio time, 8–10 images of recent work, a materials budget if applicable, an artist resume, three references and contact details. Selection weighs the merit of past work, readiness to benefit from the residency, a demonstrable interest in interactive, participatory or socially engaged practice, and the potential to develop an innovative practice in public. Applicants must currently live and work in Boulder County or the surrounding area and be able to commute to the centre for the whole residency period. Short-listing is announced 15 February 2027, interviews are the week of 27 February 2027, and the final selection is announced 6 March 2027. Past residents include Heather Kegel, Kaitlyn Tucek, Kenzie Sitterud, Jessica Forrestal and Mollie Jackson.
