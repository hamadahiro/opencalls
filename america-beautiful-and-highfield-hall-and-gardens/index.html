America, Beautiful and…

A juried exhibition for the United States semiquincentennial at Highfield Hall & Gardens, the restored Beebe family estate in Falmouth on Cape Cod, now a nonprofit cultural centre with a gallery programme. The call asks artists to reflect on the American experience — how America is lived, remembered, celebrated, questioned and reimagined — and the title sets the terms: not only what is beautiful about America, but what comes after the 'and'. The jurors are Jackie Reeves and Lauren Wolk, jointly named the Arts Foundation of Cape Cod's 2026 Artists of the Year and co-founders of The InkLine Project; Wolk is a visual artist represented by Larkin Gallery in Harwich Port and Provincetown as well as a New York Times bestselling author for young readers, a published poet and an award-winning filmmaker, and the organiser frames the pairing as a dialogue between visual art and storytelling. Fine Art Photography is named in the accepted categories, alongside ceramics, collage, drawing, fiber, mixed media, mosaic, oil and acrylic painting, pastel, printmaking, sculpture and watercolour; giclées and similar prints that are copies of original art are not allowed. Awards are $200 for Best in Show, $100 each for four judge's awards, and four honourable mentions. Artists may enter any number of original works — the entry form caps that at twenty — but only one per artist will be accepted and displayed. Work must not have been shown at Highfield before, must be by a living artist and must be made independently rather than under instruction in a class or workshop; the call adds that art must be made from your own photo references and clarifies that no copying of other art is allowed, including art generated by AI — a rule about copy work rather than a ban on AI tools. Two-dimensional work must be framed and wired, with acrylic glazing rather than glass above 30 inches, and work over 40 inches in either direction is less likely to be accepted. Entry costs $20 for the first work and $15 for each after, non-refundable whether or not the work is selected. Files are due by midnight EST on 20 November 2026, acceptances are posted on the Highfield website and emailed between 8 and 11 December, shipped work must arrive by 1 February 2027 with a prepaid return label, hand-delivered work is received on 8 February, and the exhibition runs 4 March to 30 May 2027. Accepted work must stay up for the whole run; withdrawing it brings a two-year ban from Highfield's juried exhibitions. Highfield publishes the call on its own site as well, at highfieldhallandgardens.org/blog/call-for-art/ (measured 23 October 2026: "Digital entry only, submissions due by November 20, 2026" and the same $20/$15 fee ladder), with the prospectus linked there and entries taken through EntryThingy.
