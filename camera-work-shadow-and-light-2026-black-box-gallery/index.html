Camera Work: Shadow and Light 2026

Prizes Gallery exhibition prize Free printing/framing prize

Juried photography exhibition at Black Box Gallery in Portland, Oregon, exploring the dialogue between shadow and light. Both black-and-white and color work welcome — formal, conceptual, expressive, minimal, intricate, literal, or abstract approaches all considered. Juried by Todd Johnson, gallery founder/director, with an MFA in Photography from the San Francisco Art Institute and 25 years in arts education and administration. 26 photographs selected for the physical exhibition (June 1–25, 2026); 50 additional images included in the online gallery; all 76 published in a purchasable catalog. Black Box provides free archival printing, matting, and framing for accepted work. The gallery retains 50% commission on sales. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers; submission via PayPal payment + email with up to 5 JPEGs.
