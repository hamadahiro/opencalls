Santu Mofokeng Foundation Photography Prize 2026

Prize R100,000

A new national award from the Santu Mofokeng Foundation, established in honour of one of South Africa's most influential photographers and dedicated to recognising and supporting outstanding emerging photographers working in the country. Through a national open call the Foundation seeks artists whose work shows a thoughtful and compelling engagement with the photographic medium, reflecting the depth, sensitivity and critical awareness that characterised Mofokeng's own practice, and aims to provide recognition, visibility and professional support at a pivotal stage in a career. The inaugural award carries a R100,000 cash prize together with editorial support, an artist profile and opportunities for future exhibition presentation through the programmes of Latitudes, the Foundation's promotional and platform partner. Entry is open to independent practitioners living and working in South Africa; the application form sets an age ceiling of 45 and asks for a cohesive body of ten to fifteen photographs made within the past five years, alongside a portrait image, a recent biography and statements describing the applicant's practice, the submitted body of work and why they would be a strong recipient. The Foundation was established in 2015, when Mofokeng donated his photographic work and copyright to it, and works to preserve, research and share his legacy while supporting contemporary photographic practice through exhibitions, publications and public programmes. Applications close on 15 October 2026.
