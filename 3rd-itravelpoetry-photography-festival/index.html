3rd iTravelPoetry Photography Festival

The third edition of iTravelPoetry's Athens photography festival, on the theme of Christmas, holidays and winter. Selected pictures are printed A3 and hung at the organisation's Chalandri venue for two days, where the public — not a jury — votes the winner a free solo show there plus a place in the Beyond Borders tour. Applying by email is free; €20 per selected photo covers its print.
