Delfina Foundation — India Residency 2026

Prize Residency

Delfina Foundation is a London-based contemporary art residency. The 2026 India Residency is fully funded with support from The Charles Wallace India Trust and The Inlaks Shivdasani Foundation — both organizations long associated with India-UK cultural exchange.

Eligibility is restricted to contemporary visual artists based in India. The residency is twelve weeks; the 2026 cycle runs January 5 to March 28, 2027 (winter season at the Delfina Foundation).

Application is free, made through Cognito Forms with the completed application form. Applicants are expected to read the Guidance Notes document before applying.

The 2026 deadline was April 13.
