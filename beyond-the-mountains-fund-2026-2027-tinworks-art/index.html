Beyond the Mountains Fund 2026–27

Beyond the Mountains Fund is Montana's arm of the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts' Regional Regranting Program, run by Tinworks Art, a non-profit arts organisation in Bozeman; the fund joined the network as a regional partner in 2026. Each year the fund awards one grant of $10,000 and ten grants of $5,000 to Montana-based artists for independent, public-facing projects that encourage experimentation and collaboration, with priority given to Indigenous, emerging and mid-career artists; established artists are eligible when seeking support to explore new, experimental or previously underdeveloped areas of their practice. Projects must take place in Montana, be artist-organised and self-initiated, and operate within a visual arts context the fund defines broadly to include visual art, performance art, digital art and mixed media. They must happen outside a conventional studio or commercial gallery setting, be accessible to the public in community spaces, and charge no admission or participation fee; 501(c)(3) organisations and for-profit businesses cannot apply. One proposal per person per cycle, and past awardees may reapply two years after their award date. The application asks for contact details, an artist bio, a 250-character project summary, a 3,000-character project description and a basic budget, plus information on collaborators, the intended audience or site and any outreach plans; supplementary imagery and sketches are welcome, and timeline and budget templates are provided. Applications are reviewed by a panel of three to five compensated artists and arts professionals with strong ties to the local and regional arts community and expertise in nontraditional, community-centred art practices. Half the award is disbursed up front and half on completion of an interim project report. The 2026 window opened August 1 and closes October 1, 2026 at 11:59pm MT; grantees are announced in December 2026, initial project activities run 1 January to 30 June 2027, an interim report is due 30 June 2027, project activities complete over July–November 2027 and final reports and documentation are due 31 December 2027. Free to apply, through Submittable.
