Art in Transit Open Call for Artwork

Prizes Artist fee from a project budget Temporary exhibition

Metro's Office of Community Engagement is seeking artists, designers, photographers, poets, illustrators and interdisciplinary creatives for temporary public art activations across the transit system, on a rolling basis. The call builds a pool of artists and artworks that may be selected for future temporary installations and digital presentations at Metro stations, facilities and customer-facing spaces.

Five formats are listed: barricade and construction-wall art (vinyl wraps or printed panels, so files must be supplied in a modifiable digital format); digital screen activations, where artwork, animation, photography and other digital media appear on station and facility screens, capped at twenty seconds and reformattable for custom screen sizes; station and facility installations, two- or three-dimensional temporary exhibitions on public view for up to three years; community and special-event activations; and emerging opportunities as new spaces and technologies arrive.

The call is open to artists and creatives residing in the District of Columbia, Maryland and Virginia, aged 18 or over, individually or in teams, at emerging, mid-career and established levels, with priority to artists inside Metro's registered service area. Photography is one of the named disciplines. Metro states that artists from historically underrepresented communities are strongly encouraged to apply.

The form asks for contact details, city, state, ZIP, ward or county, a 50-word bio, a 100-word artist statement, discipline(s), five work samples as JPEG or PNG, a link to a website or portfolio, and a CV of up to two pages. Submission does not guarantee selection. A Community Advisory Group may be convened for final selection of a multi-year temporary project. If selected, the artist submits a quote for services and Metro provides a budget covering the artist fee and any labour associated with file preparation.
