Movements

International group exhibition at CICA Museum in Gimpo, South Korea, gathering work on movements of nature, bodies and man-made structures as well as artistic and social movements. The museum asks for work on the fluidity of identity, cultural and sociopolitical boundaries, shifting social phenomena, and physical and physiological alterations of the body. Photography is named first among the welcomed media, and "Photographs & 2D Digital Art" is one of four submission categories alongside video and interactive art, physical works, and performance. Submission is free and by email: up to five JPEGs and/or three links to video works, plus a bio. Selected photographic and digital work is printed at 10x15 or 10x10 inches, framed and installed by the museum's team; artists sending physical work ship at their own cost, with 2D pieces capped at 39 inches on the longest side and wall-mounted work at 10 kg. Accepted artists contribute $78 USD (110,000 KRW) per piece toward exhibition processing and maintenance. The show runs November 11-29, 2026, and the museum notes the dates are subject to change.
