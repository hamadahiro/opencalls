Call for Solo Exhibition Proposals

Prize Solo exhibition

The St. Louis Artists' Guild has been showing regional work since 1886 and runs a free gallery on North Jackson Avenue in Clayton, just west of the city. Its open call for proposals is for solo exhibitions, from emerging and established artists alike, working in all media — a rare thing to find with no submission fee attached. Selected artists show in either the Guild's curated gallery or its ramp gallery; floor plans for both are published alongside the call so proposals can be pitched to the space. Applicants must live within a 150-mile radius of St. Louis, be 18 or older, and have a website of their own (Instagram and Facebook pages do not count). One proposal per artist. Two or more artists may collaborate on a proposal or pitch a group exhibition, but it must be submitted under a single name. Anyone who has already had a solo exhibition at the Guild must wait five years before showing again. Proposals go in through the Guild's Google Form by midnight on 5 September 2026.
