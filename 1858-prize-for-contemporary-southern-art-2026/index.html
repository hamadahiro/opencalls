1858 Prize for Contemporary Southern Art 2026

An annual award from the Gibbes Museum of Art and its Society 1858 auxiliary, given to an artist whose work contributes to a new understanding of art in the South. The prize welcomes applications from artists working across any media, and photography has a long history here — past winners and finalists include Sally Mann, William Christenberry, Deborah Luster, Birney Imes, Stephen Marc, Jeff Whetstone, Susan Worsham, Jennifer Shaw and 2025 finalist Tommy Kha. Alongside the monetary award, a work chosen by the Gibbes curatorial team in collaboration with the artist goes on display in the museum's Mary Jackson Modern and Contemporary Galleries for the year following the announcement. Previous winners have gone on to Joan Mitchell Fellowships, Guggenheim Memorial Foundation awards and Pollock-Krasner grants, with work entering the collections of the National Gallery of Art, the Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Art Institute of Chicago. Eligibility is geographic rather than medium-based: artists must reside in, work in, or be from Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, West Virginia or the District of Columbia. Applications go through SlideRoom with a registration form, an artist statement of 150 words or less, a CV, and a portfolio of up to ten images or videos of recent original work at 300dpi or better. Submissions are reviewed by a panel of visual arts professionals, Society 1858 representatives and Gibbes staff; the winner and finalists are notified in October 2026, with a public announcement later in the year.
