Solo Exhibition October 2026

Monthly featured photographer showcase from All About Photo, open theme and open to professionals and enthusiasts alike. The selected photographer receives a month-long online solo exhibition running October 1-31, 2026 across the site's home page, competition gallery and exhibition directory, then permanent access in the past-exhibitions archive, plus a dedicated portfolio page carrying 20 images in the Photographers Directory. The showcase also includes an in-depth interview, a special-edition newsletter, promotion across the platform's social accounts and press coverage; past winners have been picked up by outlets including The Times UK, National Geographic Espana and The Eye of Photography. Work may be sold directly to buyers with no commission taken. Entry is a flat $45 for a cohesive project or portfolio of 6 to 14 images; only cohesive bodies of work are published as a Solo Exhibition. Selection is made by All About Photo founder and editor Sandrine Hermand-Grisel; the winner is notified by email shortly after 26 September and entries close on Friday 25 September 2026.
