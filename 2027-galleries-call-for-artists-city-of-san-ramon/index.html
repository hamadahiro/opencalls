2027 Galleries Call for Artists

The City of San Ramon, 35 miles east of San Francisco, programmes five gallery spaces and this call fills their month-long exhibition slots for the whole of 2027. The venues vary in scale and in what they can take: the Dougherty Station Community Arts Center hangs 30-plus pieces across up to 115 linear feet of 2D and 3D; the Lindsay Dirkx Brown Gallery takes 25 to 30 pieces over 100 linear feet, also 2D and 3D; San Ramon City Hall holds 30-plus 2D pieces across 125 linear feet; the Alcosta Senior & Community Center takes 20 to 25 2D pieces; and the San Ramon Library shows 10 to 15. The city says the programme is deliberately open to artists at any age and any stage, and accepts both solo and group proposals, so long as the applicant has a body of at least ten completed works. Accepted media are two-dimensional or three-dimensional work with the sole exception of film, so photography qualifies; AI-generated work does not, work already shown in a San Ramon gallery in 2025 or 2026 cannot be repeated, and the city bars nudity, violence and what it calls otherwise inappropriate imagery or themes. There is no application fee — selected artists pay a $50 exhibition fee, due 30 November. Applications are made through CaFE with five image samples, an artist statement and a resume; the portal window runs 10 August to 27 September. The Arts Advisory Committee reviews on 21 October and notifications go out 2 November. Work may be sold during the run, with a 20% sales commission payable to the city.
