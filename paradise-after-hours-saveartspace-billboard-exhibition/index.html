Paradise After-Hours — SaveArtSpace Billboard Exhibition

Prize Billboard exhibition in New York City

The curator is the reason to read this one closely: Olivia "LIT LIV" Morgan came up documenting underground nightlife under that name, works primarily in analog, and is a queer Black photographer looking at identity — so the person selecting the work has been inside the rooms the call is about.

What comes back is not a gallery wall but a billboard in New York for at least a month, which is a different kind of audience: people who did not choose to look at art that day. SaveArtSpace has been doing exactly this since 2015 and the fee is a tax-deductible donation that buys the ad space.

"Artists of all ages and talents" is the stated eligibility, so there is no career-stage gate here. The window is short — it opened 8 October and closes 2 November.
