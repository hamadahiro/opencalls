In Good Company

A new juried exhibition at The Studio Door, the gay-owned and operated gallery in San Diego, running August 13 – September 24, 2027, that asks San Diego County visual artists to portray each other: "Who should represent San Diego's working art community today?" Each selected artist makes a new portrait of another San Diego County artist, so the show adds up to a collective portrait of the region's art community seen one practitioner at a time. Portraiture here is not limited to painting or realism — photography is named in the accepted media alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, assemblage, collage, mixed media, fibre art, artist books and sculpture, and expressive, symbolic and experimental interpretations are explicitly invited ("think beyond the familiar image of an artist holding a brush"). You apply with three to five existing images that show your approach to portraiture, not with a finished piece: either name three preferred subjects, with a note on why each artist's work deserves recognition, or ask the gallery to assign you one from a list built on recommendations from local art organisations and galleries. Selected artists are notified by April 9, 2027 with their confirmed subject, then have more than three months to make the work — progress images due July 1, final portrait and catalogue materials July 30, local delivery August 7. Participants are asked to keep their involvement confidential until opening night. Three artists each receive a $100 Juror's Award; all accepted portraits appear in an exhibition catalogue introducing the artists portrayed, and a copy is donated to the San Diego History Center as a record of this moment in the city's cultural life. Work must be original and available for sale, with sales split 50/50 with the gallery and commission cheques mailed within 30 days of the close. Performance art is not accepted; video, film, installation and anything needing external power require gallery approval first. Open to San Diego County artists only.
