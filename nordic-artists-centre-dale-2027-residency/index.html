Nordic Artists' Centre Dale 2027 Residency

Two-to-four-month residency in 2027 at NKD, a non-profit centre funded by the Norwegian Ministry of Culture and Equality in the village of Dale, Fjaler on Norway's west coast. Established 1998. Residents receive a monthly grant of NOK 11,000, up to NOK 5,000 in travel reimbursement, and free accommodation in a private residence house plus a 50m² studio with 6-metre ceilings and north-lit panorama windows. Open to professional visual artists, designers, and architects (photographers welcome). Not available to students or practitioners in music, writing, dance, or theatre. Residents give a presentation and participate in an open studio day.
