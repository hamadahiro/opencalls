Communication Arts Photography Competition 2027

Prizes Publication Trophy

The 68th year of the photography competition run by Communication Arts, the Californian design and visual-communication journal whose annuals are a standing reference for art directors, designers and art buyers. An international jury of creative directors and photographers selects the winners, and every winning entry is published worldwide in the Communication Arts Photography Annual in print and digital editions and on commarts.com, with a contact index for the photographers represented — the point of the thing is to be found by people who commission work. There is no cash prize: each winner receives an Award of Excellence milled from solid aluminium plus printed certificates for all creative contributors. Nine categories are judged — Advertising, Books, Editorial, For Sale (posters, prints, greeting cards, licensing/stock, gallery and online-store sales), Institutional, Motion/Cinematography, Self-Promotion, Unpublished and Student Work — and the Unpublished category explicitly takes commissioned-but-unpublished, personal and experimental work, so this is not only for photographers with an ad-agency client list. Any photograph first published or produced between March 2026 and March 2027 is eligible, from any country; entry fees are $40 for a single image and $80 for a series of up to five in most categories ($90/$180 for time-based media, $20–$90 in the student categories), and descriptions in English matter to the judges. Entries registered after March 12 and up to March 26, 2027 cost $10 extra per submission. The competition states it will not accept AI text-to-image generated submissions. Accepted entrants are notified within two months of the final deadline.
