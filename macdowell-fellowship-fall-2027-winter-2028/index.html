MacDowell Fellowship — Fall 2027/Winter 2028

MacDowell's Fall/Winter 2027–2028 cycle, for residencies running September 1, 2027 to February 29, 2028 at the 450-acre estate in Peterborough, New Hampshire. A Fellowship consists of exclusive use of a private studio, accommodations and three prepared meals a day for two to eight weeks; artists may request up to eight weeks and the average stay is 30 days, with no minimum length. There are no residency fees, and Fellows may afterwards apply for need-based stipends and travel reimbursement grants. About 300 artists in seven disciplines are awarded Fellowships each year and MacDowell states the sole criterion for acceptance is artistic excellence. Photographers apply under Visual Arts, which MacDowell describes as considering makers of three-dimensional and two-dimensional art 'from photographers and printmakers to sculptors and creators of large-scale installation as well as everything in between'; the campus photography studio houses a private black-and-white analogue darkroom, but MacDowell does not provide digital photography equipment and chemicals and paper must be supplied by the artist. Applications are welcomed from artists of all backgrounds and all countries and from artists at any stage of practice, but applicants cannot be enrolled in a degree-seeking programme during the residency season they are applying for — doctoral candidates who have completed all coursework may apply — and artists may submit only one application every 24 months. Visual Arts applicants submit eight examples of work completed within the last four years. The reference-letter requirement remains suspended. Note the application is not yet open: it goes live on January 19, 2027 at macdowell.org/apply/apply-for-fellowship and closes February 10, 2027. A nonrefundable $30 processing fee is charged with each application, payable by card in the online form, with waivers available on request for significant financial hardship or where economic sanctions prevent payment; waiver requests are only considered from the opening of the application to three business days before the deadline. Applicants are notified roughly 2.5 months after the deadline, at the end of April.
