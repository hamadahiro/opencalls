101 Art Book: Garden Edition

Prizes $500 Publication 4-page feature

Arts to Hearts Project's 101 Art Book series reaches its sixteenth volume with the Garden Edition, a hardcover publication built around a single theme interpreted by 101 artists from around the world. The series began in 2023 and has published fifteen editions and more than 1,300 artists so far, with volumes on Photography, Food, Collage, Landscape, Abstract, Portrait, Animal, Floral, Nature, Dogs, Pattern, Colors, Faces and Texture; this one asks for work drawn from the natural world. The organisers frame a garden as more than a place where things grow - a world of colours, forms, patterns, textures, movement and transformation that can be found in a single flower, the shape of a leaf, the flight of a butterfly, an overgrown landscape or an imagined world filled with life - and they open the theme to each artist's own reading of it. Flowers, plants, trees, leaves, grass, fruits, seeds, insects, butterflies and birds are all named, as are landscapes, forests, wild gardens, botanical forms and seasonal change, and so is the unexpected beauty of weeds, tangled branches, fallen leaves and decaying flowers; symbolic, conceptual and wholly abstract interpretations are explicitly welcome. Photography is named twice in the call's own words - nature photography sits in the list of eligible work, and the FAQ answers a photographer directly: work exploring flowers, plants, landscapes, wildlife, botanical details and natural patterns is eligible. AI-generated artwork is not. Entry is open worldwide to artists 18 and over at any career stage, including self-taught and unrepresented artists, with no requirement of formal art education, gallery representation or exhibition history, and individual artists only. The fee is a tiered image ladder: 21 US dollars for one image, 35 for three, 47 for five, 62 for ten and 100 for twenty, with a separate optional 22-dollar add-on for a virtual studio-visit interview published on the organisation's website if selected, which is not required to enter. Every selected artist is published in the book alongside the other hundred and receives an official certificate of recognition; the book also carries interviews with the guest curator, collectors, galleries, industry figures and the cover artist, and selected artists are included in the publication's marketing campaign across an audience the organisation puts at over 190,000. One artist receives a 500 US dollar cash award, a four-page spread and a trophy. The books are produced as premium hardcovers and sold at 120 dollars each, distributed through Amazon and Barnes & Noble. Applications close on 13 December 2026; the organisers aim to announce results within ten days and commit to announcing them within three weeks of the deadline, with the clock restarting if the deadline is extended.
