The Wellspring of 1000 Eyes

The Art Department, Paisley marks the first year in its new permanent home — a former Argos store on Causeyside Street — with an open call built on the phrase "Keep your Eye on Paisley", which the organiser attributes to Benjamin Disraeli, and on Argos Panoptes, the hundred-eyed giant of Greek myth set to watch and never sleep. Artists are invited to respond to Vintage Paisley (any photograph or artwork of the town's past or present) and to the broader themes of Power & Guardianship, The All-Seeing Eye, The Eyes of the Truth, The Third Eye, The Public Eye and Private Eye. Photography is named among the media the exhibition will feature, and the entry guidance is explicit that works may be of any medium or genre. There is no entry fee and no hanging fee — the project is supported by Renfrewshire Council's Culture, Heritage & Events Fund — and up to six works may be submitted per artist, all through the online form. Work is graded by a selection panel using an average-score system; artists are notified on 8 November 2026, hand-in runs 16–30 November, and the exhibition opens 1 December 2026 and closes 30 January 2027. Most work is expected to be for sale, with a 30% commission plus VAT on the commission element supporting the charity, and remittance by 31 March 2027. The cutoff is not end of day: the organiser’s own status line reads "CALL FOR ENTRIES CLOSING AT SAT 31ST OCT, 4pm" and its key-dates list repeats "Call for Entries CLOSES 31ST OCTOBER 2026 at 4pm", so entries shut at 16:00 UK time, not at midnight.
