Winter 2026 Residency

Prizes $3,000 CAD stipend Residency

Two three-month residencies at SITE Toronto, a non-profit launched in January 2026 with a crowdfunding campaign and built around funded time rather than exhibition deadlines. One place goes to a Toronto-based artist or curator, who receives funding to travel abroad for research or professional development; the other goes to a resident from elsewhere, who is flown to Toronto and housed in a private apartment for the full term. Both receive a CAD $3,000 stipend, up to CAD $1,500 in travel support, a private studio, one-to-one mentorship with an artist, curator or other cultural practitioner of their own choosing, the chance to produce a SITE Edition and to conceive a public programme, and access to SITE's network. The Toronto place is delivered with XinResidence and prioritises practitioners at a formative stage and from communities that have historically faced barriers to space, resources and institutional support. Photographers are squarely in scope: Spring 2026 resident Yantong Li made a photographic edition out of the residency, an archival print on Hahnemuhle photo rag, with Beichen Zhang. The session runs November 16, 2026 to February 19, 2027. Applications are free and go through a Google Form linked from the residencies page; the deadline is noon EST on Monday September 14, and shortlisted applicants may be invited to an online interview the week of September 21.
