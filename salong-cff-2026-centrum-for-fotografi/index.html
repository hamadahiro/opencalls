Salong CFF 2026

Prize Exhibition

Centrum för fotografi is Sweden's member organisation for photographers — state-supported, with galleries in Stockholm and Gothenburg — and Salong CFF is its annual open salon, running for the 25th time. It is not a juried competition: every member who registers is hung, simultaneously at three venues, Galleri CFF in Stockholm, CFF in Gothenburg and Galleri kran in Malmö, 20 November to 12 December 2026, with a vernissage at all three on 20 November. The organisation calls it one of the year's most visited exhibitions. Registration is free of charge this year and the only condition is CFF membership. One work per member, a maximum of 50 × 70 cm, registered through a Google Form with a high-resolution JPEG of up to 5 MB that is printed in the salon catalogue — so the image you attach is the image that gets published. CFF also selects one or more of the photographs to promote the salon, asking the artists concerned for approval first. Every work must carry a price and be for sale, with prices including 25% Swedish VAT; CFF takes no commission on sales to private individuals and leaves those sales to the artist, and charges 25% on sales to companies and public institutions. Two things to weigh. You must be able to deliver and collect the work yourself at your chosen venue on the set days — there is no shipping route — so in practice this is for photographers who can get to Stockholm, Gothenburg or Malmö twice. And the membership gate takes time: CFF membership is aimed at professionals working with photographic images, which it defines broadly enough to include photo editors, photo historians and curators, but an application is reviewed before it is approved, and if you have no CV or work online CFF will ask for them separately "in order to make an assessment of your professional experience". Dues are SEK 500 a year, SEK 250 for students, payable after approval, and you can join at any point in the year. No turnaround time for that review is published, and registration closes 18 October — so if you are not already a member, apply well before then. Membership itself carries the usual benefits: open calls, member exhibitions, courses at member rates, a portfolio page and the industry guide.
