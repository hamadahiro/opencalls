Zari Art Prize 2026

Prizes £1,000 Best in Show Exhibition

The Zari Art Prize returns for 2026 from Zari Gallery, a Fitzrovia gallery in London's West End founded in 2016 to show new and established artists united by mixed cultural heritage. The prize is awarded across three categories — painting, sculpture and photography — from a £4,000 prize pool, with Photography taking £550 and Photography Runner-up £350 alongside a £1,000 Best in Show and a £300 People's Choice. There is no set theme. Artists worldwide may submit up to ten works; entry is £20 for a single work, rising on a sliding scale to £87.50 for ten. Judges select an online shortlist between 23 and 31 August, a public vote opens 2 September, and the shortlist is announced 9 September, with shortlisted work delivered 11–17 September for the gallery exhibition in central London. Photographs must arrive framed and fitted with hanging hardware; images are submitted as JPEGs under 10 MB, ideally 300 dpi and at least 1600 pixels wide. Work is offered for sale to the gallery's collector audience.
