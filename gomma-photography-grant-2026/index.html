Gomma Photography Grant 2026

The Gomma Grant returns for its thirteenth edition, run by Gomma Publishing Ltd - the imprint behind Gomma Magazine, publishing since 2004 and, on its own account, having worked with Roger Ballen, Anders Petersen, Michael Ackerman, Momo Okabe, Keizo Kitajima, Araki and Daido Moriyama. The organisers say they look for photographers who develop their own language instead of following trends, and that they judge the work rather than the CV, reputation or number of followers. Entries are welcome across documentary, portrait, landscape, conceptual, black and white, colour, analogue and digital, from a project that may be unfinished, ongoing or completed, and it does not matter how long ago the work was begun. Each entry is 8 to 24 images at 72 dpi, minimum 1,500 pixels on the long edge, with a short description plus an optional artist statement and biography. Multiple entries are allowed provided they come from different bodies of work, and two photographers may submit a collaborative project jointly. The fee depends on which deadline you make. Only the final deadline is still open, and the organiser has extended it: €35 to Friday 20 November 2026, with the submission clock set to Hawaii Standard Time (UTC-10). The €25 deadline closed on Thursday 15 October 2026 and the €19 early-bird on Friday 25 September, and the entry portal no longer offers either — Submittable now offers only “Gomma Grant 2026. EXTENDED Deadline.”, whose guidelines read “FINAL Deadline: Friday 20th November 2026 = €35 for 24 images”. As of 12 November 2026 the organiser’s page gives the 20th throughout: the banner reads “THE 2026 GOMMA GRANT EDITION: EXTENDED DEADLINE : 20 NOV 2026”, the Guidelines block “EXTENDED Final Deadline: Friday 20th November = 35.00 euros” and the Deadline & Fees block “EXTENDED Deadline: Friday 20 November - €35”; the 21 November that the entry form’s FAQ prints is the window for editing a submission you have already made, not for entering. At €35 the entry covers 24 images, with additional images allowed at an extra fee the organiser does not price. Seven awards are listed. First Prize takes €1,000 and a potential publishing deal with Gomma Publishing and its network; the Gomma New Flavour award takes €300 and the same publishing offer; Second Prize €200 and Third Prize €100; the Honorable Mention, Best Documentary Award and Best Black and White Award carry no cash. Every award adds promotion through Gomma's own channels and media partners, and the organisers say winning work is put to international galleries and curators. AI-generated images are not accepted. No jury is published for this edition - the only answer the FAQ gives on the subject is scoped to 2025, which it says was judged in secret. One conflict worth knowing before you plan around it: the guidelines block says winners are announced in Spring 2027 while the Deadline & Fees block on the same page says February 2027, and the entry form sides with Spring 2027.
