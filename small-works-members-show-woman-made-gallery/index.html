Small Works Members Show

Woman Made Gallery's end-of-year small-works exhibition, open to all current WMG members of any gender working in any medium — photography included. Each artist submits JPEGs of three works, one of which is guaranteed a place in the show. Work must measure 20 inches or under on its longest side, be priced at $400 or less and listed for sale, and must not have been previously shown at WMG; recent work is encouraged but not required, and a general artist's statement is needed. Selected by the WMG Program Committee. The exhibition runs November 21 – December 19, 2026 at the gallery on South Halsted Street in Chicago. Entry is free with membership; WMG has presented hundreds of exhibitions since 1992 and remains one of the longest-running feminist art spaces in the United States.
