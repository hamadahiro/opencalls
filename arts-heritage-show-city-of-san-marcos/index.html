Arts & Heritage Show

Prize Exhibition

The Walkers' Gallery is a City of San Marcos exhibition space in the San Marcos Activity Center at 501 E. Hopkins Street, central Texas. The call is published on the City's own arts hub, visitsanmarcos.com, under the category the City reserves for its own calls; the hub's closed-calls list shows the gallery runs themed shows regularly, among them Got the Blues and Lines, Loops & Layers.

Arts & Heritage is the gallery's first annual show on the theme, and its premise is a pairing: artists' work hangs alongside informational displays from local heritage and cultural organisations — registered nonprofits based in San Marcos or Hays County that educate the public about the area's history and culture. Artists are told to work in any style, literal or symbolic or abstract, but to be mindful that work with cultural flavour and colour will sit especially well in this company.

All 2-D and 3-D media that can be hung safely on the walls or in the large windows are eligible, which admits photography. The content restrictions are firm and worth knowing before you submit: everything must be apolitical, non-violent and G-rated for a family-friendly public venue, with no nudity even if tasteful and artistic.

What comes back is the hang and the audience, not a prize. The show is explicitly not juried; the gallery reserves the right to cap entries so as to include as many artists as want in, and placement is first-come, first-served. There is no entry fee, the gallery takes no commission, and any sale is settled directly between artist and buyer — though sold work must stay on the wall until the show closes. Each artist may bring one large work 36 inches wide or more, or up to two medium or smaller works, to a limit of two entries and 30 lb per piece, ready to hang with hardware fitted near the top.

The call is dated inconsistently on its own page: the entry-form bullet says entries close Friday October 22, 2026, while the Important Dates list says Friday October 23, 2026 at 11.59pm Central. October 23 is the Friday; the earlier date is stored here so nobody misses the window. Delivery is in person at the San Marcos Activity Center on Saturday October 24 between 10.30am and 1.30pm, the artists' reception is Friday November 20 from 5.30 to 7pm with music and refreshments and is open to the public, and work is picked up on Saturday January 9, 2027.
