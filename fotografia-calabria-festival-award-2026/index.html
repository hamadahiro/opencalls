Fotografia Calabria Festival Award 2026

Fifth edition of the award run by the Associazione Pensiero Paesaggio alongside Fotografia Calabria Festival, Calabria's first widespread photography festival (first edition 2022, grown out of the earlier Fiumefreddo Photo Festival). For the first time the competition sets no theme, asking instead for projects with strong conceptual coherence and a recognisable artistic vision; entries are judged on the quality and originality of the photographic research, narrative coherence and the strength of the proposal. Each photographer may enter one project of 10 to 25 images, shot digitally or on film (analogue work scanned), with diptychs allowed; post-production is accepted as an expressive choice, but in documentary projects removing or adding people or objects is not. The winner receives €2,000 in cash from the Festival plus a paid €1,000 commission to publish a project in Internazionale magazine — the two prizes may be combined or awarded separately — along with an exhibition or screening at a forthcoming edition of the Festival and twelve months of promotion. Shortlisted projects go into the permanent online gallery on the Festival's website and its social channels. Copyright stays with the photographer.
