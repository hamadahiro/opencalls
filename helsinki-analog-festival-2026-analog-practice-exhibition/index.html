Helsinki Analog Festival 2026 - Analog Practice Exhibition

Prizes €500 prize Exhibition prize

Helsinki Analog Festival is held annually in Helsinki, Finland. The Analog Practice Exhibition is the festival's international open call for artists working with analog processes — celebrating materiality and craftsmanship in image-making.

Eligibility is process-restricted: analog photographic practices only. The festival is organized by Finnish Darkroom.

Top prize is €500 plus exhibition during the festival. Submission is through ForPhotographersOnly. Fee is €20.

The 2026 edition deadline was December 14, 2025.
