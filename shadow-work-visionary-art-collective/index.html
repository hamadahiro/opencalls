Shadow Work

A curated virtual exhibition with an accompanying print catalog, selected by Etta Harshaw for Visionary Art Collective, the Brooklyn-based art and career-development organisation behind New Visionary magazine, the New Visionary Podcast and a programme of virtual and in-person shows. The collective takes submissions from artists at every career stage and across all mediums, photography included, and its exhibition calls have run as virtual shows paired with a printed catalog. Submissions are handled on the organisation's Submittable page, which gives the title, the curator and the closing time and nothing more: the call closes at midnight Eastern between December 6 and 7, 2026, and the entry form itself sits behind a Submittable account, so the image count and any submission fee could not be read. On the organisation’s Submittable board, read 6 November 2026, the concurrent Artist Spotlight form carries a $25.00 price label and this call carries none — that label is where Submittable prints a form’s entry fee, so the call reads as free to enter. The organisation’s own submit page says only that submission fees fund the platform and are non-refundable, without pricing any call, and the form itself sits behind a Submittable account, so check the price on the form before paying anything.
