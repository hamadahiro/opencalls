Vehicles & Voyages

Prize Exhibition

Juried exhibition at Arts Center East in Vernon, Connecticut celebrating the many ways we move through the world — cars, trucks, bicycles, motorcycles, boats, trains, aircraft, and the journeys they make possible. Historic vehicles, scenes of travel, personal road trips, maritime exploration, or imagined voyages into the future: movement, connection, freedom, and discovery. All media and artistic styles welcome, including photography; open to artists 18+. Entry $35 for one piece ($30 for ACE members), $5 each additional piece up to five. Shown August 8 – September 12, 2026, with reception and awards August 22.
