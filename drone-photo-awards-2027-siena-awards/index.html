Drone Photo Awards 2027

Prizes €500 gear €400 accommodation Exhibition

Siena Awards' aerial photography contest returns for 2027 with nine categories and a prize package that helps cover the stay in Italy. The rules open with the eligibility line — “Drone Photo Awards competition is open to professional and amateur photographers”. Seven single-shot categories are published (Wedding, Nature, People, Urban, Animals, Abstract and Sport), alongside a Videos category and a Series category, where “You may enter from 5 to 9 images” on “a set of images that are presented together on the same theme, edited in the same style, and with a common link.” Six of the single-shot briefs define the work as “captured from the sky” and Abstract as “Aerial photography”, though neither the Videos nor the Series brief states an aerial requirement of its own. Entry is free for one image; beyond that the rules' Entry Fees table reads “3 images (including the free one) € 30”, “Each additional image € 10”, and € 35 for a Series or a Videos entry, with “no refunds provided” once paid. The entrant “with the highest scoring image will be endowed the title of ‘Drone Photographer of the Year’” and there is also a winner in each category; the overall winner receives “€ 500,00 worth of photography equipment”, the “Pangea Prize” crystal trophy, and “a fixed fee of 400 euros to assist with accommodation expenses for two overnight stays in Siena during the Awards Ceremony weekend”, reimbursed after the winners are announced. Two conditions attach to the awards: the trophies and parchments “will only be given to winners attending the Awards Ceremony”, and equipment “not collected during the ceremony will be the responsibility of the winners”. Winners are honoured on stage at the ceremony in Siena, and “the winning images will be showcased in Siena at the ‘Above Us Only Sky’ exhibition during 2 months”, with advertising in the international press. Photographers keep their copyright — “Siena Awards recognizes you retain full ownership of the copyright in each photograph that you have submitted” — and grant only promotional use of entered images. AI is barred outright: the rules prohibit AI generation software “for the creation or enhancement of images”, including AI-generated elements added to camera-captured frames and generative fill used to extend an image beyond its original boundaries. No 2027 juror is named — entries are “reviewed and pre-screened by our editors and an international group of industry professionals”, then judged by “our international jury”, and the award's jury route serves only the roster of previous editions. Entries opened on 11 October 2026 and close on 1 June 2027.
