OD Photo Prize 2026

Sixth edition of Open Doors Gallery's annual open call for emerging photographic artists working within the first ten years of their practice. Open-themed and international — from documentary and street photography to collage, mixed media, and experimental lens-based work; collectives and duos welcome. Submit a project of 6–15 images with an artist statement. At least 20 shortlisted artists join a group exhibition in London in November–December 2026 (venue TBC), with works produced by the gallery and sold on a profit-share basis. The winner receives £2,000, an interview in 1000 Words Magazine, long-term mentorship, and an OD artist listing with an Artsy profile for at least a year. Additional awards include Judges' Picks, the InCadaqués Award — a solo presentation at the festival in Spain — and the AJ Page People's Choice Award.
