Flora & Fauna

South x Southeast's Flora & Fauna is curated by gallery director Nancy McCrary and framed by E. O. Wilson's line that we should preserve every scrap of biodiversity as priceless while we learn to use it and come to understand what it means to humanity — Wilson being the American biologist and naturalist often called Darwin's natural heir. The call sets no further brief than the title, and the gallery illustrates it with two of McCrary's own pictures: tick weed and water lilies in the Okefenokee Swamp in late winter, and early morning in the Maasai Mara National Reserve in Kenya. Selected photographs hang in the SxSE Gallery through November and December 2026 — a virtual gallery whose own tagline is "making the virtual a reality" — and a Zoom group review is held with the exhibiting photographers during that run, on a date to be announced. Entry is by email to the gallery director: images should be JPEG only, 72dpi and 1000 pixels on the longest side, named lastname-firstname-title (the gallery's own example is mccrarynancybigredhouse), zipped into a single file and attached to an email with the subject line "Flora & Fauna". The entry fee is $40 for the first four images and $10 for each additional image, paid separately through the gallery's shop.
