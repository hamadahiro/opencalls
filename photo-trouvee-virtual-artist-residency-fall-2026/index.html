Photo Trouvée Virtual Artist Residency — Fall 2026

Prize Residency

10-week virtual residency (September 4 – November 6, 2026) for artists who work with or are inspired by found/family photographs. Weekly Zoom calls, bio/statement workshops, artist talks, group feedback, and accountability groups in a cohort of 6–9 artists. Open worldwide to artists at any career stage and in any medium, as long as found or family photography informs the work; participants commit to attending at least 80% of weekly calls. Partial scholarships available.

DeadlineAugust 20, 2026
Entry fee$650
LocationOnline
Requirements1–3 images
Submit viaWebsite

Jury: Juliana Naufel, Co-founder · Twiggy Boyer, Co-founder

Organized by Photo Trouvée Magazine
