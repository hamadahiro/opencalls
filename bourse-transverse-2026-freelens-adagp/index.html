Bourse Transverse 2026

Prizes €7,000 Publication

Joint grant from photographers' association Freelens and ADAGP, the French visual-arts authors' society, supporting a duo — a photographer and an artist from another visual discipline — in creating a work together and publishing it as a book. €12,000 in total: €7,000 to the winning duo and €5,000 towards the book's edition, with the work made within a year and the book published within two. Open to duos from the French scene (working or residing in France for at least five years, or French nationals living abroad) with professional track records of exhibitions or publications; a publisher may already be attached. Applications in French via the ADAGP candidature portal by 3 September 2026, 2pm; the winning duo is announced at Salon Polycopies in Paris on 12 November 2026.
