Defiance Zine Open Call

Prize Publication prize

Revoke Collective is an emerging indie photography collective. The Defiance Zine is the collective's first publication, themed around defiance — work that challenges, disrupts, or simply refuses.

The brief reads defiance broadly: quiet resistance, bold rebellion, personal freedom, cultural pushback, or moments of quiet nonconformity. The editorial register favors raw and unpolished work — the brief states the collective is not chasing polish, it's chasing pulse.

Selection results in inclusion in the first Revoke Zine.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is by Google Form with up to 3 images. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was April 6; selections communicated mid-April 2026.
