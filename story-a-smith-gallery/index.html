Prizes $100 Solo exhibition

A Smith Gallery's next juried online show takes narrative as its subject — "story: adventure, tale, narrative, myth, drama, fable, saga, fiction, novel, fantasy…." — and the gallery makes good on the theme itself: co-director Kevin Tully, writing as Frank Cincinnatus, "will write a short tale to go along with the Juror and Director Award images", and the prospectus prints a sample of his fiction in full. Tully is also the juror; he is a photographer, designer, writer and artist who "has curated and installed hundreds of exhibitions for the gallery" and has juried for All About Photo, Analogue Forever, Dodho Magazine, the International Photography Hall of Fame and Critical Mass. The call is open and unlimited: "The competition is open to all photographers both professional and amateur working in all photographic mediums and styles. International entries are welcomed", at $40 for the first five images and $5 for each additional one, with "no limit to the number of images you may submit"; work previously shown in an A Smith Gallery competition is not eligible. Jurying is blind, with images renamed and numbered. Up to 47 images are selected for an online exhibition that is permanently archived, featured image by image on the gallery's Facebook and Instagram, discussed in a recorded GalleryTalk, listed for sale in the gallery's online store and published in a purchasable exhibition catalogue; 27 of the 47 also go into "The 27", a hardbound fine art book. The Juror and Director Awards each bring "a online solo exhibition, 'The 27' exhibition book and feature story on the gallery blog", and the Visitors Award, decided by Instagram likes, pays $100. Photographers submitting 13 or more images get a free 30-minute review with the gallery directors; fewer than 13 costs $20 for the same. Photographers keep full rights, the gallery takes 30% of any sale, and a sold print may be shipped by the artist or printed by the gallery. The exhibition runs 8 January to 18 February 2027.
