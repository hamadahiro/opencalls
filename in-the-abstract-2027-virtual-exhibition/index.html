In The Abstract 2027 — Virtual Exhibition

SFVACC's annual abstract-art open call, in its virtual edition: a juried online exhibition running 24 February to 30 April 2027. The geography is worth reading twice before paying: the prospectus body says the show "is open to all artists" and the CaFE record's location bucket reads International, but the binding Eligibility Criteria field on the same page narrows it — "In The Abstract a Virtual Exhibition is open to all artists residing in North America." Outside the US, Canada or Mexico, ask SFVACC before entering. The brief is the broad one — "Abstract art uses shapes, forms, color, line and texture as opposed to representing an accurate depiction of visual reality" — and the organiser says it "seeks artists who can provide a well-rounded range of 2D and 3D abstract art." Photographers are in scope on the prospectus's own terms: up to six original works per artist, submitted as JPEGs, "in all media except video." Juror Genie Davis, a writer, art curator and the owner of Diversions Fine Arts Gallery in Manhattan Beach, selects an $800 Best of Show (plus a listing on SFVACC's Best of Show page), $400 first place, $300 second place and up to four Honorable Mentions. Entry is $40 for the first work with up to five more at $5 each, and SFVACC is running an early-bird code (SaveVirtual) that takes $10 off until 30 November 2026. The sales terms are unusually clean for an online show: buyers contact the artist directly through the exhibition page, SFVACC takes no commission at all, and prices are fixed at the figure given at submission. Three conditions to read first: work made under an instructor's direct supervision is not eligible, a submitted work cannot be substituted or withdrawn, and work previously shown in any SFVACC exhibit should not be entered. Accepted work stays online until the exhibition closes. Entries close 24 January 2027 at 11:59pm PST, with acceptances emailed by 31 January.
