Call for Entries 2027

ROY G BIV Gallery for Emerging Artists in Columbus, Ohio runs its exhibition programme from one annual call for entries, and this is the round for the 2027 season. Selected artists are given a month-long small group exhibition during the 2027 calendar year, curated by the gallery director. Submissions come from artists local and global and are reviewed and voted on by a panel of jurors from the contemporary arts community; the 2027 jurors are filmmaker and visual artist Yusef Ferguson, a 2025 Greater Columbus Arts Council Visual Arts Fellow currently exhibiting at the Columbus Museum of Art, and sculptor Dana Lynn Harper, who holds an MFA from Penn State and makes ritual votive works about racial identity and cultural heritage. ROY defines emerging artists as those early in their careers, not represented by a gallery, and not yet substantially celebrated by the media, funding circles or the public; artists who have shown at ROY before are ineligible apart from ImageOHIO and the annual small works member show. Entry requires a ROY G BIV membership costing $25 for the calendar year. Submissions go by email with up to 10 images (JPG or PNG, under 1.5 MB each) or up to 5 video URLs, plus an artist statement, CV and list of works. Two-dimensional work must arrive ready to hang, and artists are responsible for transport and installation.
