Echoes of Fall 2026

Prizes Cash awards Exhibition

All-media juried exhibition on fall-inspired work at the Medford Arts Center, a community arts centre on North Main Street in Medford, New Jersey. Michael Schweigart is juror of awards; chairs are Diane Emerson and Susan Barnes. Open to artists 18 and over, up to three original pieces each, none previously shown at MAC. Entry is $20 for the first work for members and $30 for non-members, plus $10 for each additional piece. Every accepted work goes into the online exhibition and into a brochure and YouTube video; artists who can hand deliver also have the option of showing their pieces in the gallery at 8 N. Main St. The show runs 4 September to 1 October 2026; artists are notified by email on 1 September, and the awards and online exhibit go up on 5 September. Cash awards for first place, second place and three honourable mentions, though the centre does not publish the amounts. All work must be for sale and Medford Arts takes 30% commission; artists ship sold work to the buyer themselves.
