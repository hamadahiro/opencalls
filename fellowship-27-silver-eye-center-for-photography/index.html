Fellowship 27

The 27th edition of Silver Eye's annual open call, the Pittsburgh photography centre's programme for recognising "exceptionally talented artists working with photography, particularly those who have not yet received significant recognition or support". Two awards are made: the International Fellowship Award, open to a photographer anywhere in the world, and the Keystone Award for a photographer living in or making work in Pennsylvania, each carrying a $1,000 prize, with two Honorable Mentions of $500 in each category. All six selected artists are included in the Fellowship 27 exhibition at Silver Eye in May 2027 and receive curatorial support to develop the show plus free production support from the Lab @ Silver Eye. Jurors this cycle are Harlan Bozeman, an artist and educator from Central Arkansas whose research-driven practice confronts the erasure of Black legacies, and Dr. Paula V. Kupfer, an art historian and curator from Panama specialising in the history of photography in the Americas, a former managing editor of Aperture now teaching at the University of Pittsburgh. Submissions are a work sample of up to 20 images with a one-page statement, bio or CV and image checklist; photographers featured in a Silver Eye solo or small group exhibition between January 2022 and December 2026 are not eligible, and anyone shown there before 2022 must submit a significant creative departure. Awardees are announced in January 2027. The cutoff is NOT end of day: the call page states it twice as "NOV 19 2026, NOON ET" and "Deadline: NOV 19, 2026, NOON EST", so entries close at midday Eastern.
