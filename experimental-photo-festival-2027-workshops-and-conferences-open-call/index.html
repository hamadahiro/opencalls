Experimental Photo Festival 2027 — Workshops and Conferences Open Call

Prizes €500 compensation Accommodation €999 diploma

Barcelona's experimental photography festival is looking for artists to lead workshops and give talks at its eighth edition in July 2027. Selected artists choose six nights of festival accommodation plus €200, or €500 compensation, and receive meals, accreditation and the €999 Ágora diploma. International, free to apply.
