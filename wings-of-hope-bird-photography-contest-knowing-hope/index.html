Wings of Hope Bird Photography Contest

Prize Photography gear for 8 winners

Knowing Hope, a US non-profit that funds medical care for children with chronic and catastrophic illness in under-resourced communities across Asia, has launched its first annual bird photography contest as a fundraiser. Photographers of all levels may enter four categories — Birds in Flight, Perched & Portrait, Behavior & Interaction, and Juvenile & Nesting — across two divisions: a novice division for those who have not sold prints, won photography awards or worked professionally, and an advanced division for everyone else. Entries are open to photographers aged 16 and over, with up to ten entries each and one to four photographs per entry; photographs may be spread across any combination of categories. AI-generated images are barred and basic adjustments are allowed, but elements may not be added, removed or substantially altered. Eight category and division winners receive equipment prizes including a Cotton Carrier harness, Vortex binoculars, a Delkin CFexpress card, a Wimberley Monogimbal head, a Think Tank backpack and a KelbyOne Pro membership. Judging is by Duade Paton, an Australian bird photographer and BirdLife Australia contributor; Marina Scarr, whose avian work has appeared in National Wildlife, Nature's Best Photography and Living Bird; and David Lindo, The Urban Birder, a judge for Bird Photographer of the Year and the British Wildlife Photography Awards. Each photograph is scored 1–5 on technical quality, composition and artistic merit, moment and impact, and overall impression. Photographers keep full copyright, granting the organisers a non-exclusive licence for promotional and fundraising use with credit. Winners are announced between 1 and 14 November 2026.
