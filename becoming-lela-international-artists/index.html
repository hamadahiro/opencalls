Becoming

A juried group show on change and transformation at The Los Angeles Makery, 4–28 March 2027, open to LELA members and non-members aged 18 and over. Painting, sculpture, photography, mixed media and digital art are accepted; video, AI-generated work and digital reproductions are not. Accepted work is hand-delivered in Los Angeles on 3 March, the gallery keeps 30% of sales, and no awards are offered.
