Photo-Solo Exhibition Proposals — Season 23

Prizes Solo exhibition $100 honorarium

Manifest Gallery invites proposals for photography-related solo exhibitions to be held during its Season 23 opening period, September 25 – October 23, 2026, as part of the 2026 FotoFocus Biennial. Open to all artists working in photo-based media, including experimental and mixed-media approaches. Submit a proposal form, up to 12 images, and a resume; extended deadline June 21, 2026. Solo exhibitors receive a $100 honorarium, a free copy of the season publication, and automatic entry into the $1,000 Grand Jury Award pool; gallery commission on sales is 30%. A parallel all-media call for solo proposals across the full 2026–27 season shares the same deadline. Notifications by early August.
