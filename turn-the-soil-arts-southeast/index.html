Turn the Soil

ARTS Southeast's spring Supporter Exhibition in Savannah asks for new work tied to the natural world, including work made with natural materials. Current Supporters, from $25 students or $50 artists, may submit up to four wall-hanging works, capped at 30 inches in every direction, for a juried show running 21 January to 15 May 2027. ARTS Southeast takes no commission, so artists keep 100% of sales.
