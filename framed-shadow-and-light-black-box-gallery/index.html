Framed: Shadow and Light

Black Box Gallery runs one of the more photographer-friendly models in the American juried-show circuit, and this call is a good illustration of why. Todd Johnson — the gallery's founder, an SFAI photography MFA and a reviewer for Photolucida, Critical Mass and Blue Sky — picks 26 photographs for the physical walls and 50 more for the online annex, and the gallery then prints, mats and frames every selected work itself at no cost to the artist. You send a high-resolution TIFF or JPEG; they print it on an Epson Stylus Pro with archival inks on Premium Luster in a colour-calibrated room, into 18 × 24 inch black frames with pre-cut white mats. For a photographer without a framing budget, that removes the single biggest cost of showing work.

The theme is the oldest one photography has. Shadows give definition and dimension and set the relationships between subjects; the call asks equally for the hard graphic shadow of black and white and the moodier, more atmospheric shadow of colour work. All 76 selected photographs — gallery and annex together — go into a catalogue available through Blurb. Exhibition prints stay in the gallery's flat files for two years afterwards and are shown to collectors, curators and critics; the file agreement is explicit that only one exhibition print is made, that the photographer is notified and must consent before any sale, that edition information is agreed with them, and that all rights to the file and the print remain theirs. Work need not be for sale at all. If it sells, commission is 50 percent.

Entry is $40 for up to five photographs and the mechanics are old-fashioned: pay the fee through PayPal, then email the gallery with your name, the call title, the image titles and the files attached — JPEGs at 1200 pixels on the long side, 72 PPI, under 5 MB each, named firstname_lastname1.jpg and so on. Open to all photographers worldwide, amateur and professional. The deadline is 9 October 2026 and notifications go out seven to ten days later; the show hangs 1–25 November 2026.
