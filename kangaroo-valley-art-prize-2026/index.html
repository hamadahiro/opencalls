Kangaroo Valley Art Prize 2026

Prizes AU$10,000 AU$4,000 Young & Emerging

The headline prize of the Visual Arts in the Valley festival, held in the Kangaroo Valley Hall and venues around the valley in the NSW Southern Highlands. The terms of entry are deliberately wide: any original work of visual art by an Australian artist or an artist residing in Australia is eligible, expressly including photographic art, digital art, new media and video alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, textiles and collage. Work must have been made within two years of the closing date and must not have been shown by Visual Arts in the Valley before; maximum hung size is 115cm wide by 180cm high including the frame. Alongside the $10,000 main prize there is a $3,000 Highly Commended, a $2,000 Commended, a $4,000 Young & Emerging Art Prize for artists aged 30 or under (with a $1,000 Commended), a $1,500 Salon of Local Artists Prize, a $1,000 People's Choice and a $500 Packing Room Prize. Judged by painter Steven Harvey, senior lecturer at the National Art School, with Olivia Welch of Ngununggula Regional Gallery curating. The exhibition runs 2–5 October 2026.
