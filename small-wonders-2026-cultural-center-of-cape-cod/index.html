Small Wonders 2026

Prize Exhibition

Small Wonders is the Cultural Center of Cape Cod's juried small-works holiday exhibit, filling the Great Hall of its South Yarmouth campus from November 18 to December 19, 2026. There is no theme: the only rules are size and price. Work may be no larger than 12 inches on its longest side, though a frame may extend to 16 inches, and the Center suggests pricing at no more than $350 so that the show works as a place to buy art as a gift — buyers take pieces home immediately rather than waiting for the exhibit to close. Many media are welcome and photographs are named explicitly, alongside paintings, textiles, glass, sculpture, ceramics, collage, assemblage and mixed media; video is excluded. Entry is $10 per piece for up to five pieces, non-refundable even if the work is not accepted, and there is no online route: artists print the submission form from the Center's Call For Art page (or fill one in on arrival) and hand-deliver the work with payment on Monday, November 16, 10am to 4pm. Jurying and notification happen the next day, Tuesday, November 17, and work not taken into the show is collected on November 18, the day the exhibit opens; the reception is Friday, November 20, 5–7pm, and unsold work is picked up on December 21. All work must be for sale, with the Center taking 40% of the sale price and the artist 60%. Everything must be original, prints must be signed and numbered, wall work must be ready to hang (no saw-tooth hangers), and no AI-generated work is accepted. One clause lives only on the printed submission form rather than on the web page: work left beyond 90 days becomes the property of the Cultural Center unless alternate arrangements have been made, and the Center is not responsible for work left after the posted pickup date. The Center is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with a year-round programme of exhibitions, classes, residencies and events on Cape Cod.
