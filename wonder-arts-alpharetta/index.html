Wonder

Prize Exhibition

Juried winter exhibition at the Alpharetta Arts Center, presented by Arts Alpharetta in partnership with the City of Alpharetta Cultural Services. Wonder invites work that captures seasonal awe and enchantment — shimmering light, quiet reflection, the joy of discovery, and the extraordinary hidden within the everyday. Photography is among the accepted media, alongside drawing, painting, mixed media, printmaking, sculpture, kinetic art, ceramics, fiber and folk art. Artists may submit up to three works, one artwork per entry. All work must be original in concept, composition and execution; reproductions of original works are not eligible, and pieces previously shown in the Arts Center's main gallery cannot be re-entered. Accepted work must arrive gallery-ready and wired to hang, and may be delivered in person or shipped with a prepaid return label. The exhibition runs November 24, 2026 to January 9, 2027, with a reception on Friday, December 4.
