Artist Grant 2026

Ten unrestricted grants of €10,000 from the Dutch foundation The Supporting Act, paid in ten monthly instalments of €1,000 from January to October 2027 and intended to cover living costs and artistic development. The grant goes to emerging artists from underrepresented groups whose practice involves collaborative, collective or community-benefiting creative processes; the application form's own discipline picker names photography outright, alongside architecture, dance, design, digital arts, film and video, literature, music, multidisciplinary practice, painting, performing and sculpture, and the independent selection committee is drawn from artists, curators and former grantees. To apply you must have two to ten years of professional experience, have been born in or before 2005, be based in Belgium, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal, Spain or the United Kingdom with a bank account in your own name in one of those countries, identify as belonging to an underrepresented group or as someone facing systemic barriers in your country's creative industries, and have a website or public social profile where your work is visible. The application is a form plus links to three publicly available works and a video of no more than four minutes; only finished work is accepted, not project proposals, and one application per person. The call opens 1 September 2026 and closes 1 October at 11:00 CET, all applicants hear back in late October 2026, and the announcement and first instalment follow in January 2027. There is no application fee, no budget required and no spending report afterwards. Current and former TSAF grantees are not eligible.
