Photographers Without Borders Revolutionary Storyteller Grant

Prizes $5,000 prize Adobe CC prize

Photographers Without Borders (PWB) is an international nonprofit supporting impact-driven photography. The Revolutionary Storyteller Grant is the organization's annual grant program for projects with explicit environmental and social-justice framings.

The brief is thematically specific: projects focused on environment, land/water protection, decolonized conservation, queer ecology, and climate change. The framing is rooted in non-extractive, community-rooted approaches rather than traditional photojournalism.

Three recipients worldwide receive $5,000 USD each plus Adobe Creative Cloud subscription.

Selection is by photographers Eman Mohammed, Sophia Nasif, and Natalia Ortiz Mantilla.

Eligibility requires PWB membership. Submission is through the PWB website with up to 12 images. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 30.
