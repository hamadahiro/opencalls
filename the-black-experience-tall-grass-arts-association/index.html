THE BLACK EXPERIENCE

Prize Group exhibition

Tall Grass Arts Association, a seventy-year-old non-profit gallery in Park Forest, Illinois that keeps its exhibitions free to the public, opens its 2027 season with THE BLACK EXPERIENCE, a Black History Month exhibition for African American artists. The brief is wide and deliberately personal: works may share images or interpretations of the artists’ own lives, experiences, lessons, influences, challenges, fears, struggles and victories. The gallery asks artists to consider negative and positive events and the quality of daily life, injustices and rewards, decisions made and the people who inspired them, histories of lost hope and long-held dreams for the future; images of people who made a difference, texts of important speeches or writings, situations of changing conditions and the struggles to make those changes; and works that refer to events, relationships, talents and other art forms that educate, document or inspire. Photographers are squarely inside this one — photography and digital works are named in the accepted-media list alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, graphic design, mixed media and collage, calligraphy, books, sculpture, fibre, glass, ceramics and metal, and any medium except video is acceptable in all sizes, styles and formats. Selection is by a named panel: curators Claudia Craemer and Deb Craemer, joined for the choice of works by photographer Perry Slade and painter Eurnice Harris. They weigh the traditional criteria — compositional elements, use of media, technical skill — together with each artist’s own vision and the impact and lasting impression of the piece, and say that for this show in particular the power of a work’s message will also count. Up to five works per artist, submitted through EntryThingy. The prospectus itself prints no price: EntryThingy’s record for the call carries a single $25 entry fee and publishes no per-work ladder above it, so treat $25 as a floor until the entry form shows a total — it can be paid on EntryThingy, by card over the phone, or in person at the gallery in cash, by check or by card. Note what this show does not offer: works will not be judged for awards, though visitors’ votes are collected for the most outstanding pieces. Shipped work is accepted if the artist supplies a pre-paid return shipping label; two-dimensional work must be wired and ready to hang, with no sawtooth hangers and no wires showing above the frame, and anything needing special installation must be installed by the artist. All work stays up for the full run. Artists are notified by 8 December 2026, required forms are due by 18 December, work is delivered to the gallery on 3–6 January 2027, the exhibition runs 16 January – 19 February 2027, and work is returned 20–23 February. Unless an artist asks otherwise, Tall Grass takes permission to use images of exhibited work for publicity or educational purposes.
