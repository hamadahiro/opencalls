FORECAST 2026

SF Camerawork's annual juried exhibition of contemporary photography, presenting work by emerging and under-recognised artists. The jurors for this edition are Janet Delaney, the Berkeley-based photographer of South of Market and a 2020 Guggenheim Fellow; Omar Jason Farah, Curatorial Assistant at BAMPFA, previously at the Leslie-Lohman Museum and the Whitney; and Jonathan Mark Jackson, Assistant Professor of Photography at UC Santa Cruz and a 2026 Hellman Fellow, whose practice joins still photographs and prose around historic Black memory. They will consider all work within the expanded field of photography, from traditional to experimental, with a focus on recently completed, thought-provoking projects. Up to five artists are selected for a group exhibition at SF Camerawork's gallery at Fort Mason Center for Arts & Culture in San Francisco, running November 21, 2026 to January 9, 2027. One selected artist receives the $1,500 Juror's Choice Award and up to four more receive a $500 artist fee; selected work is featured across SF Camerawork's media channels, can be sold through the gallery on a 70/30 artist/organisation split, and brings opportunities for artist talks, interviews and audience engagement. Submit up to ten samples of finished, gallery-ready work (images up to 5MB; video up to 5 minutes or 250MB per sample, or a YouTube/Vimeo link), a brief project or artist's statement, and one of a CV, artist résumé or professional biography. Read the access route before you plan: there is NO application fee, but you must be a current SF Camerawork member, with membership valid through the closing date. The Community Membership artist rate is a sliding scale with a suggested contribution of $100 and options starting at $50, open to working artists including students (the full programme value is $250). Join first; the organisation then emails a link to the SlideRoom application, and current members have already been sent theirs. The cutoff is not end of day — applications close Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 5:59 PM PST. Selected artists are notified October 20, 2026 and gallery-ready artwork is due November 14, 2026.
