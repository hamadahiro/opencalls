Tarot and Oracle: The Art of Divination

Perry Lawson Fine Art, a contemporary gallery in a historic building in downtown Nyack, New York, invites paintings, drawings, mixed media, collage, photographs, digital art, prints and sculpture related to tarot and oracle for a juried exhibition running November 21, 2026 to January 10, 2027. Photography is named twice in the gallery's own eligibility text. The show treats these works as artworks in their own right rather than as decks, and the gallery says it is looking for creativity, technical skill and compelling imagery and symbolism, with an eye to work that will appeal both to divination enthusiasts and to a wider public. Selection is on technical skill, originality and the ability to convey meaning through symbolism while still functioning as a compelling work of art. Participating artists may additionally offer their own corresponding tarot or oracle decks for consideration for sale during the run. Open to artists 18 and over residing in the United States, and the gallery will review a maximum of 200 submissions before closing the call. The juror is the artist Dannielle Tegeder. The $22 application fee covers up to five pieces and goes toward the juror's honorarium, the entry platform and marketing. Artists ship accepted work to the gallery at their own cost, though shipping is deducted from the gallery's commission if the work sells. The gallery takes 50% commission on sales.
