Portrait 2026 — Atlanta Photography Group

Prize Exhibition

A juried exhibition celebrating portraiture and the stories carried in the human face and body, open to photographers worldwide. Juror Alan K. Avery, president and owner of Alan Avery Art Company, Atlanta's longest-running gallery, selects up to 24 works. Themes include identity and self-expression, emotion and psychology, cultural diversity and narrative portraiture. Selected prints (24 inches maximum on the longest side, white or black frames and mats) hang at the APG Gallery in Atlanta from November 3 to December 4, 2026.
