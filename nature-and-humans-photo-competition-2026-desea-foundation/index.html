Nature and Humans Photo Competition 2026

Fourth edition of the DESEA Foundation's international nature and conservation photography competition (Madrid, Spain). Fifteen awarded categories: four conservation photojournalism categories (Series, Single Image, Spain, and 'Conservation Told by Women'), two WildWatchingSpain special categories, six thematic categories (Mammals, Birds, Oceans, Other Animals, Landscape, Creative), plus Young, Child and People's Choice awards. Around €17,000 in total prizes including cash awards plus camera gear from Sony and OM System. Open to amateur and professional photographers of any nationality; up to 30 images, JPG minimum 3000px on the longest side. FONAMAD members pay €15; under-18s enter free. The window was extended three days past the original 28 September close: natureandhumans.com now carries an extension banner and a countdown timer set to 1 October 2026 at 23:59, while the rules page's own calendar still prints 28 September — the rules also say an extension "will be sufficiently publicised on the Competition web platform", which is what has happened.
