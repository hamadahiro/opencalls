TOKAS Research Residency Program 2027

Prizes 6–12 week residency Accommodation

Research-focused residency at Tokyo Arts and Space for international and local creators — visual artists (photographers welcome), designers, architects, curators, critics, and writers. Five places for 6–12 week stays within one of three periods between May 2027 and March 2028, for conducting research on arts and culture in Tokyo. TOKAS provides a single-room living space at TOKAS Residency in Koto-ku; no studio or workspace is included. No age limit; applicants must not be students at the time of participation; duos may apply. Application package PDF plus online form with two letters of recommendation and a portfolio. Deadline June 16, 2026, 18:00 JST.
