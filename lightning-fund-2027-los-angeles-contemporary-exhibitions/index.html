Lightning Fund 2027

Prize $6,000

LACE, Los Angeles Contemporary Exhibitions, has opened the seventh edition of its Lightning Fund, an annual regranting programme supported by the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts. This cycle it awards ten artist project grants of $6,000 each to artists and artist-run collectives, projects and publications in Los Angeles County. The same application also decides the Jacki Apple Award, a $10,000 grant to one LA-based mid- or advanced-career artist for work resulting in a performance, media project, exhibition or publication within a year; no separate application is needed. LACE describes its definition of visual art practice as expansive, listing drawing, painting, printmaking, sculpture, installation and similar mediums alongside film and video, new media, sound art, performance art and social practice; practices firmly rooted in dance, theatre, music or industry, feature and documentary filmmaking are not eligible, nor are nonprofit organisations and LLCs set up for commercial enterprise. Applicants must live in Los Angeles County, be at least 18, and not be currently enrolled in a college programme. Proposals should be innovative and socially relevant and accessible to the public outside traditional museum or gallery settings, and the review prioritises presentations in Los Angeles County; awarded projects are not intended to be shown at LACE. Previous winners may reapply after waiting two cycles. The application asks for contact details, a 300-word account of your practice and past projects, and a 500-word project description covering timeline, collaborators, materials, location and needs. Entry is free, through Submittable. Applications opened on 12 August and close on 23 September 2026 at 11.59pm PST, a deadline LACE describes as firm; decisions and funds follow by the end of January 2027. A recorded information session is posted on the LACE website.
