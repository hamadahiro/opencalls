BLUE Photography Exhibit

Decode Gallery's juried exhibition on the colour blue, in every hue and shade — landscapes and seascapes, portraits and abstracts, and whatever the gallery calls the calm or melancholy the colour stirs up. The call is open to all photographers worldwide, amateur and professional alike, and there is no restriction on genre or process. At least 26 images are selected by juror Holly Hart, a conceptual fine art photographer who founded Decode after a BA in Photography from the Art Institute of Seattle. Selected photographers pay nothing further: the gallery handles all printing itself on an Epson printer with Ilford Pearl paper and presents work in museum-quality 22 x 22 inch white frames with precut white mats (11 x 11, 10 x 15, 11 x 15, 12 x 15 inches and others), scaling files for the best fit in the mat without ever cropping them. One exhibition print is made per image and archived in a flat file for six months after the show. Entry is $40 for up to five photographs or $45 for up to ten. Photographers are notified by email within two weeks of the deadline, and high-resolution files are due two days after the acceptance email. The exhibition runs January 2 to January 30 at the gallery in Tucson, opening January 2. Work may optionally be listed for sale, with Decode taking a 50 per cent commission; all rights to the image file and the print remain with the photographer.
