Prix Mustaqbal 2026 — Momentum

Annual competition promoting emerging contemporary creation. Open to artists aged 18–35, Moroccan or foreign residents in Morocco, working in drawing, painting, installation, photography, or sound art. No thematic constraints. Approximately 15 finalists exhibited at Artorium in Casablanca. Winner receives artist residency at Jardin Rouge and duo-shows in Paris and Casablanca.
