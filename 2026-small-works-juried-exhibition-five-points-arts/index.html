2026 Small Works Juried Exhibition

An international juried small-works show at Five Points Gallery, the exhibition arm of Five Points Arts — a 501(c)(3) that describes itself as one of Connecticut's largest and most respected arts non-profits. Open to all artists 18 or older working in any style or medium, with a single size rule: no dimension may exceed 24 inches, frame included. Up to three works per artist for a flat $35 CaFÉ fee. Only work that has not previously been exhibited at Five Points Gallery is considered. The one award is a solo exhibition in Five Points Gallery in 2027/28, announced at 7 pm during the opening reception. Juror is Patricia Carrigan, a Connecticut painter and mixed media artist who has exhibited nationally and internationally since 1990 and has taught visual fine arts at Manchester Community College for over twenty years. Submissions open September 1, 2026 and close October 11 at 11:59 pm; jurying runs October 13–23, notification is October 25 on CaFÉ, and accepted work is delivered between October 27 and November 13. The exhibition runs November 20, 2026 to January 9, 2027 at 7 Water Street, Torrington, with the opening reception on November 20, 6–8 pm. All work must be for sale and the gallery retains a 50% commission; accepted pieces are also shown on the Five Points website.
