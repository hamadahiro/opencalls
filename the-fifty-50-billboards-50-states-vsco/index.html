The Fifty: 50 Billboards 50 States

Prizes Billboard exhibition Paid licensing

The Fifty is VSCO's nationwide recognition programme for photographers working in the United States: one photographer from each of the fifty states is selected to have an image printed on a billboard in their home state. The framing is deliberately anti-metropolitan — the pitch is that you can build a photographic career where you already live, documenting your own community.

There is no entry fee and no separate application portal. Photographers submit by posting work to a VSCO account with the hashtag #TheFifty and the hashtag for their state, adding a short caption about the image or series and putting their state in their profile bio. VSCO recommends three to five images, particularly if they form a cohesive series. A free VSCO account is sufficient to enter.

Beyond the billboard, VSCO states that selected work may be carried into editorial features, curation and licensing opportunities, and that selected photographers are licensed and compensated for the use of their images. Anyone selected will be asked for a high-resolution file suitable for large-format printing, and images containing recognisable people require a model release. VSCO's fine print adds two qualifications: billboard formats vary by state, so selected work may instead appear as an alternative large-format display, poster or public installation, and submitting grants VSCO the right to review the work and, if selected, use it for the campaign and on social media, with a release or licence agreement possible on request.

The call is open to photographers aged 18 and over residing in the United States; VSCO says international opportunities are planned separately. The deadline is September 10, 2026. The curation team reviews every submission and notifies selected photographers on a rolling basis rather than on a single announcement date, with selections also published on the VSCO journal.
