Barcelona Showcase

A three-day group show at Carrer de la Flor del Lliri 4, Ciutat Vella, 24–26 September 2027, in the venue or on screen. Emerging and independent artists can apply without previous exhibitions, from anywhere, without attending. Applying is free but selected artists pay for their place, and the Apply link is a mailing-list signup that emails the form.
