Grand Prix de la Photographie Paris Match 2026

Prizes Exhibition Paris Match feature

For the Bicentenary of Photography, Paris Match launches its first national photography Grand Prix on the theme 'Un regard sur notre époque' (A Look at Our Time). Open to everyone across France, amateurs and enthusiasts alike, it invites photographers to capture contemporary France in all its diversity and contradictions. Entrants submit a series of four original photographs across thematic categories — among them creativity, daring, family and transmission, each backed by a partner (Google France, Bpifrance, VistaPrint, CCF | KOREGE). A jury selects the finalists, revealed in autumn 2026, with an exhibition and awards ceremony on 16 December 2026. Each grand-public category winner receives a prize from Paris Match and its partners plus featured visibility to the magazine's readers. Submissions run 11 June – 19 July 2026.
