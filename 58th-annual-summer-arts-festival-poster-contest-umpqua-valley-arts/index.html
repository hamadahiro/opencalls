58th Annual Summer Arts Festival Poster Contest

Prize $500

Umpqua Valley Arts has run a poster contest for its Summer Arts Festival since the festival began, and the 58th edition is open: the winning image becomes the face of the whole event. There is no theme. UVA asks for work that embodies the spirit of the festival — a gathering of artists, food vendors and entertainers — and says reflecting Douglas County or Central Oregon is welcome but optional, adding that it "welcomes artwork that represents the artist's own style/subject matter of choice." The entry form's own rule is the binding one: "Open to all artists to submit up to (3) entries of two dimensional artwork/s. Open media." Both traditional and digital work are accepted, and the only format bar is that the work be two-dimensional and reproducible — photographs qualify on the organiser's own terms, with files wanted as JPEGs at 300dpi, 1920px on the longest side. A panel of UVA staff with local art professionals and community representatives judges from the digital files on craftsmanship, creative ability, ingenuity and design. The winner receives a $500 prize, a free Summer Arts Festival T-shirt, free entry to the festival, credit on the poster itself and credit in the visitor guide; the selected artwork is then converted to a digital file and used across the festival's website, social media, posters, apparel, postcards, programme, press releases and signage, and may be slightly altered, cropped or edited to fit those formats and carry festival information. Two clauses are worth reading before entering: the rules reserve UVA the right to reproduce or edit artwork from the submitted digital images for publicity purposes, and an accepted work cannot be substituted or withdrawn. AI tools are barred outright — the criteria require that "the work is not created with the use of AI programs or tools." Entry is free and closes 12 December 2026, and the form does name a notification window: the winner "will be selected and will be contacted directly by late December 2026."
