Photo Trouvée Virtual Artist Residency — Spring 2027

10-week virtual residency led by Juliana Naufel and Twiggy Boyer, founders of Photo Trouvée Magazine, for artists who work with or are inspired by found or family photographs. Artists are invited to set goals, carve out time for their practice, focus on a project and connect with like-minded artists around the world; 6–8 slots are available. The programme runs weekly 60–90 minute live Zoom calls on Fridays at 1pm EST, a Writing About Your Work mini workshop on the artist bio and statement, an artist talk with a guest speaker, six group feedback calls (each artist gets live group feedback at least twice), small accountability pods, networking for future projects, lifetime access to call replays and a one-year digital subscription to the magazine. Open to artists and creatives from anywhere in the world and at any stage of their career, working in any medium as long as found or family photography informs the work; the calls are in English, but the organisers say they will work on solutions rather than let that limit participation. Participants commit to attending at least 80% of the live calls. The investment is a one-time payment of $650 or two payments of $330, non-refundable and payable in full before the first live call, and partial scholarships of 15%, 25%, 30% or another amount are available to a couple of artists. Applications are accepted until all spots are filled; the cohort's call dates are listed as TBD.
