Hale Master of Fine Art Residency 2027

The Artists Association of Nantucket's fully funded Hale Residency is a month-long stay on Nantucket for artists who are currently enrolled in an MFA programme or who graduated from one within the past five years. The residency is described by AAN as designed for an artist “whose work explores aesthetic beauty”, and it exists to give that artist uninterrupted time: there is no expectation to teach, exhibit or present work, though exhibition and community engagement are encouraged. Hale residencies run only in January, February and March. The selected artist receives $500 toward travel and a $2,000 stipend for materials and living expenses, and stays in the Kaaren and Charles Hale apartment for Artists in Residence — a furnished studio apartment inside AAN's Visual Arts Center on Amelia Drive — with a private studio equipped with tables, easels, rolling carts and a large sink. AAN's printmaking and ceramics studios are also available to residents around the regular class schedule. Applications go by email as a single PDF and ask for a CV of up to three pages, a 200-word artist statement, a 200-word bio, 12 digital images with an image list, a 300-word cover letter and contacts for three references. The deadline is 31 August 2026 and announcements are made by 21 September 2026.
