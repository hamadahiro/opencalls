ABSTRACT

NYC4PA's international juried call ABSTRACT asks for photographs that are not instantly identified as that person or that thing. The organiser defines the brief as art that does not attempt to represent external reality but seeks its effect through shapes, colours and textures - sometimes a distortion of everyday items like tables, staircases or windows, sometimes an artistic close-up of a small section of a wall or a piece of art that reads as no recognisable object. It does not matter whether you photograph something abstract or create the abstraction through photographic processes: macro shots, close-ups of graffiti, fanciful post-processing manipulations or simply photographing something at an angle that disguises it all work. Open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers using any photographic process - print, image transfer, emulsion transfer, encaustic, black and white - with experimental and mixed techniques welcome; only 2-D work is eligible and the jury is blind, so no signatures or watermarks on images and no artist names in titles. Ten cash prizewinners share $4,025 - the organiser's summary line rounds this to $4,000, but the itemised ladder on the same page sums to $4,025: a $1,100 Grand Prize whose image is posted on the NYC4PA home page, three First Prizes of $400, three Second Prizes of $325 and three Third Prizes of $250 - alongside 20 Juror Selections and 20 Honorable Mentions, all included in the NYC4PA online gallery and winners catalog with an award certificate. Two surfaces give different closing dates: the organiser's own call page states February 7, 2027 at Midnight Pacific, while the SmarterEntry portal card reads "Closes Mar 7"; the earlier of the two is published here, so prepare for 7 February. Images awarded an NYC4PA prize within the past two years are not eligible; images submitted and not selected may be resubmitted at any time.
