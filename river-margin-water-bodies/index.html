River Margin — Water Bodies

Prize Online publication

Water Bodies is an online segment of River Margin, the ecology-and-botany journal founded in 2026 by Melbourne-based artist, musician and writer Lana Eileen, whose first issue is still assembling separately. This call is narrower and faster: work about bodies of water - rivers, streams, lakes, the ocean, wetlands and more - and about water's relationship to plants, animals and people, water histories and water stories, to be featured online. Photographers may send up to seven photographs, digital or film, as email attachments or through a Google Drive link (the journal asks entrants not to use WeTransfer, as the links expire before it can open them). The same call takes writing up to 10,000 words, sound art and music, film, and visual art; for visual art the journal accepts only painting, drawing, sculpture and traditional media, ruling out digital and AI-generated work - the no-AI rule is written under visual art, while photography is described simply as digital or film. Work must be in English but is welcome from anywhere in the world, and you may submit to more than one category as long as each follows its own rules; a short third-person bio and a link to your website or social media are helpful but not required. There is no fee and no payment: the journal says plainly that it is a volunteer-run project by one artist and cannot yet pay contributors, so the return is publication online. Submissions go by email with the subject Water Bodies Submission by 27 September 2026; because of the volume received, only successful entrants hear back, within two months of submitting, and the journal asks that nobody email to confirm receipt.
