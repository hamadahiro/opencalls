14th Annual International Juried Print Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

Remarque Print Workshop & Gallery in Albuquerque - founded in 1996 as New Grounds and, in its own words, the only non-toxic professional print workshop in the city - invites printmakers from around the world to its 14th Annual International Juried Print Exhibition, on view at the workshop's new Edith Boulevard space from 11 December 2026 to 30 January 2027. The brief asks for innovative prints drawn from all printmaking traditions, 'including, but not limited to: relief, etching, monotype, collagraph, photopolymer intaglio', and says the gallery is particularly interested in combinations of traditions. Read the gate carefully before entering, because this is a printmaking show rather than a photography show: straight and digitally output photographs are out - 'No digital images; no giclee reproductions' - but photopolymer intaglio is named in the organiser's own list of welcome processes, and that list is open-ended - 'all printmaking traditions, including, but not limited to' - so photographic intaglio work made as a print is inside the brief; Remarque's 2026 programme included an exhibition called The Printmaker and the Photographic Image: Dissolving Media Boundaries. Each artist submits three prints, original, two-dimensional and made within the last three years, on paper no larger than 16 by 20 inches. Entry is $35 through CaFE, and the show is juried and curated by the team at Remarque rather than by a named outside juror. There is no cash award: what comes back is the hang, and Remarque archivally frames every selected work at its own cost, so beyond the entry fee the artist pays only shipping in both directions and must supply a prepaid return label. All work must be for sale; the gallery retains 50% of the retail price and pays the artist within 30 days of the close. Entries must be posted by midnight MDT on 1 November 2026, the jury result comes back through CaFE by 9 November, and accepted prints must reach Remarque by 27 November.
