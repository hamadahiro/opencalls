NEXT! Festival 2027 — Wir sind doch keine Maschine

The youth strand of Cologne's photography festival invites anyone aged 6 to 21 living in Cologne to answer the theme 'Wir sind doch keine Maschine' — we are not machines. Up to five photos, staged, documentary or experimental, no experience needed; selected work hangs at the Rautenstrauch-Joest-Museum from 14 May to 13 June 2027.

DeadlineMarch 1, 2027
PrizeMuseum exhibition
Entry feeFree
EligibilityCologne only, Ages 6–21
LocationCologne, Germany
RequirementsUp to 5 photos
AI policyNot accepted
