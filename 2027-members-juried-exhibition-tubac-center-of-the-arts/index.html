2027 Members' Juried Exhibition

Tubac Center of the Arts opens its annual members' juried exhibition to artists across the United States, with all media accepted including video. Members may enter up to three 2D or 3D works completed in the last 24 months and not previously shown at TCA; giclee reproductions are not permitted. Entry is restricted to TCA members, but membership is bought directly from the art centre (Artist and Individual tiers are $50 a year) and is not itself juried - non-members are disqualified without refund, so join before applying. Juror Mary-Beth Buesgen is Lead Curator at the Mesa Contemporary Arts Museum and spent fifteen years at the ASU Art Museum as Curator and Collection Manager of its Ceramics Research Center. Awards total $900 - Best of Show $300, Award of Excellence $200, the Virginia Hall Contemporary Award $100, the Ursula Suess Abstract Award $100, an Award of Merit $100 and two Honorable Mentions at $50 - and are presented at the opening; the awards judge had not been appointed when the call was published and TCA says the prospectus will be updated once one is found. Best of Show also receives a solo exhibit in the Studio Gallery, 2 April - 16 May 2027. Entries open 21 September 2026 and the exhibition runs 8 January - 14 February 2027. All work must be for sale with a 40% commission, artists cover shipping both ways and must supply a prepaid return label. TCA's own timeline labels the 16 November deadline a Wednesday; 16 November 2026 is a Monday, and both the timeline and the CaFE portal give the same date. The cutoff has a time: the portal calendar block for this exhibition runs "September 21, 2026 12:00 AM" to "November 16, 2026 11:59 PM", in the US/Arizona zone the same block names.
