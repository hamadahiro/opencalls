FoMe 2026 International Open Call — INTUITION

Prizes MXN 120,000 (~US$6,000) Exhibition

A first edition, which cuts both ways, but the backing is unusually solid for one: FoMe's inaugural fair sits inside Mexico City Art Week (3–7 February 2027), opens with a tribute exhibition to Manuel Álvarez Bravo made with Casa MAB, and presents the INTUITION show with BMW. The theme is handed over rather than prescribed — photographers are invited to treat intuition "as a subject or as part of their creative process" — and every genre and approach is admissible.

The economics are the reason to look twice. Entry is free, fifty artists exhibit one photograph each, and FoMe pays for printing and mounting outright — no participation fee, no production levy on the selected. Work is offered for sale with the artist keeping copyright and 65% of the sale price net of indirect taxes. The FoMe Prize is MXN 120,000, around US$6,000, awarded to one of the fifty and explicitly not an acquisition: the organisers state it "does not involve acquiring the work or transferring copyright". There is a second, Photographers' Prize, voted by the exhibitors themselves.

Submission is light at the front and demanding in the detail. Send one standalone photograph, or two to three images from a single project where the extra frames exist to give context — only one picture per artist goes on the wall. Alongside it goes a single PDF with a portfolio (images, technical details, artist statement, your most significant projects of the last five years) and a CV, plus a project description of up to 250 words tying the work to INTUITION. Nothing can be edited once submitted, and replacement entries are not accepted. Two conditions sit in the terms rather than on the fair's own page, and both can disqualify you: you must be over 18 (any nationality) and not represented by a gallery in Mexico at the time of entry, and the picture must not be AI-generated or AI-modified, already commercialised or part of a commercial edition, or tied up in a representation, exclusivity or sales agreement.

The jury is six-strong and worth reading before you choose a frame: Pablo Ortiz Monasterio, the editor behind México Indígena, Río de Luz and Luna Córnea; Yvonne Venegas, Magnum Expression and Guggenheim; the aerial documentarian Santiago Arau Pontones; the portraitist Ana Hop; Manolo Márquez, an analogue printer who directs Leica Gallery Mexico City; and Greta Rico, whose Leica Women Foto Project award went to work on the families femicide leaves behind. They vote in two rounds — the Official Selection of fifty first, the prize winner second. Selected artists must then supply a 300 dpi TIFF or JPG that is identical to the file the jury saw — "No substitutions or modifications will be accepted after the jury's decision" — so submit the frame you actually want printed. The call closes 2 December 2026, results land on 13 December, confirmations and final files are due by 15 December, and the FoMe Prize winner is announced on 7 February 2027.
