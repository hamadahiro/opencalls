Rotterdam Photo 2027 — Projects

Prize Exhibition

The international projects strand of Rotterdam Photo's open call for its 2027 edition, which runs 17–21 March 2027 during Rotterdam Art Week. The theme is 'VIBRANCE – The Pulse of a Living World', an exploration of energy in its many forms, from the intimate to the collective and from physical presence to digital intensity; the festival asks photographers to read vibrance freely as a social condition, emotional state, visual rhythm or conceptual framework. Selected photographers each receive a dedicated exhibition space inside the festival's container village in the centre of Rotterdam, where the work becomes part of a large-scale public exhibition seen by more than 10,000 visitors. Artists keep curatorial freedom over their own space, may sell work during the festival, and Rotterdam Photo takes no commission on sales. Participants also join the Rotterdam Photo Talks and networking programme, appear in the official festival programme and publications, and are promoted through festival and partner platforms including LensCulture, Dodho Magazine and All About Photo. The category is open worldwide to emerging and established practitioners working across documentary, conceptual, experimental and interdisciplinary image-based practice; entrants must be at least 16. Submissions are reviewed over three rounds by a named selection committee and judged on artistic strength and originality, conceptual depth and relevance to the theme. Photographers may apply to more than one category, with each submission reviewed separately.
