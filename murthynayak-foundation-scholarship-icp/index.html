MurthyNAYAK Foundation Scholarship

Prize 3 × $10,000 tuition scholarship

Offered by ICP with support from PhotoSouthAsia, the MurthyNAYAK Foundation Scholarship is a $10,000 tuition award for artists from the South Asia region who show exceptional achievement and potential in photography. Three scholarships are given, each covering $10,000 of the $12,500 tuition for one of ICP's online One-Year Certificate programmes: Curatorial Practice in Photography, Writing and the Photographic Image, or Documentary Practice: Visual Storytelling. Eligibility is limited to applicants from and currently residing in South Asia - Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, the Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka - who demonstrate strong artistic ability, engagement with contemporary photographic practice and a commitment to impactful projects. Selection is based on the same portfolio, curatorial project proposal or writing sample required for the programme application, and both the programme application and the scholarship form must be in by 18 October 2026. The programme application carries a $60 fee. Scholarships cannot be combined, so an applicant may hold only one award, and the scholarships are open only to students enrolling in the One-Year Certificate programmes rather than part-time courses or Open Education workshops. Applications are made through SlideRoom.
