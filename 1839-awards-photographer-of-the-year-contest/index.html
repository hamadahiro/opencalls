1839 Awards Photographer of the Year Contest

Flagship open-genre cycle of the 1839 Awards, recognizing creators working with the camera as an art form across 22 categories — from Abstract, Aerial and Architecture to Film/Analog, Photojournalism, Street and Wildlife. Over $4,000 in cash prizes plus international media exposure, a profile in the 1839 Artist Directory, published interviews in 1839 Magazine, online exhibition space, and entry into the People's Vote Awards. Early bird pricing through July 22, regular entry through August 20, extended final deadline October 19; winners announced November 18, 2026.
