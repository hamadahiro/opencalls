London Showcase

Prize Exhibition

This call is for HUGE Gallery's London showcase at The Factory, 21–31 Shacklewell Lane, London E8, from 27 to 29 November 2026. HUGE Gallery runs three-day group showcases in several cities and describes itself as an open exhibition platform for emerging artists. It is open to emerging, independent, self-taught and formally trained artists working in any medium, photography included, and artists do not need to live locally or attend. Applying is free, but places are paid for by the artists who are selected. Physical places start at £150 and digital places at £50. A physical place includes presentation in the venue, an artist page on HUGE's website for two years, documentation and a social media feature; a digital place covers on-screen and online presentation. HUGE's own pitch is "If it fits, you're in". Applying starts with a mailing-list signup: the application link asks for a first name and email address, says applications are not public, and sends access to the form by email.
