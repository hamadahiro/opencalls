SE Center for Photography — The Members Show 2026–27

Prize Exhibition

The SE Center's annual members' exhibition, open to any photographer who joins the Center. Anything goes: all subjects, any photo-based imagery, analog and digital manipulation in all its forms, monochrome or colour, analog, digital or antique processes, and member photographers of every skill level and location are welcome. The juror is Michael Pannier, an award-winning fine art and commercial photographer based in Greenville with thirty years in photography and forty-five in the gallery and art world, who founded the SE Center for Photography as an exhibition and education venue after relocating from Maryland; he is represented in galleries across the United States and works regularly as an exhibition juror, curator, portfolio reviewer and speaker on the business of fine art photography. Between forty and fifty selected images hang in the SE Center's virtual gallery for about a month, with the selection also carried across the Center's social channels, an archived online slideshow and a video walkthrough. Entry is $5 per image and you may submit up to fifteen. Membership is bought directly from the Center and is not itself juried: $25 a year for secondary-school and college students and educators of any age, $50 for over-60s, $60 for everyone else, with couple and supporter tiers above that; it also brings discounts on the Center's other calls and workshops. Images exhibited at the SE Center within the previous two years are not eligible. The Center takes no commission — the artist keeps 100 percent of any sale. Submissions close 6 November 2026, selections are announced 15 November, and the exhibition runs 1 December 2026 to 31 January 2027.
