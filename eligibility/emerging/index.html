Open Calls for Emerging Artists

Open calls, grants, and awards specifically for emerging, early-career, and student photographers and visual artists.

Pioneer Works Visual Arts Residency 2027

5-month Brooklyn residency at Pioneer Works. $5,000 award, free studio & darkroom access. Free to apply; photographers eligible.

Art Shapes the Future · Fotografiska Emerging

Fotografiska Emerging × IFA Berlin × Volkswagen R call on the future. €1,500 each to 3 artists + IFA Berlin show. Free; video/AI eligible.

AOP Emerging Talent Award 2026 · Association of Photographers

AOP award for image-makers working professionally under 3 years. Gold prize: membership package, mentorship + £8,520 London billboard.

EMERGE 2026 · Midwest Center for Photography

Fellowship for emerging photographers. 20 selected for exhibition, one awarded $200 + year-long online representation.

Envision: The Michigan Artist Initiative 2027 · Stamps Gallery, University of Michigan

Michigan artists compete for a Stamps Gallery show with $2,000 finalist stipends and a $5,000 top prize. Free to enter.

Source Graduate Photography Online 2026 — MA/MFA

MA/MFA graduate showcase from Source magazine for UK/Ireland 2026 grads. 8 images. Featured online + in printed supplement.

OD Photo Prize 2026 · Open Doors Gallery

Open-themed prize for emerging photographers. £2,000, London group show, mentorship, InCadaqués solo presentation. 6–15 images.

One in Eight · Fotografiska Emerging

Fotografiska Emerging × Estée Lauder call: emerging image-makers on the breast-cancer experience. €1,500 + exhibition in Berlin & Stockholm.

Are Men OK? · Fotografiska Emerging

Free Fotografiska Stockholm × MÄN call on masculinity in Sweden today; selected emerging work shown in a group exhibition.

Talent of the Year 2026 — Tsvetan Tomchev Award · NOISE Foundation

Award for young Bulgarian photographers (up to 26) in documentary and visual storytelling. Winner gets a Sony camera and NOISE mentorship.

Liu Shiming Artist Grants 2026

Foundation grants of $5,000 to five early-career visual artists (2–10 years) for projects bridging tradition and contemporary practice.
