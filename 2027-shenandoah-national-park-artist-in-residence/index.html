2027 Shenandoah National Park Artist in Residence

Prizes $1,200 stipend 3-week residency

Shenandoah National Park gives one artist at a time three weeks in the Blue Ridge with a rustic, furnished house to themselves, and photographers are among the disciplines it names: the park's CaFE listing runs them in with writers, composers, painters, sculptors, needleworkers and poets, while the park's own page says simply that artists of all disciplines are encouraged to apply. Six residency windows are offered across 2027 - 27 April to 18 May, 1 to 22 June, 29 June to 20 July, 27 July to 17 August, 7 to 28 September and 7 to 28 October - and the park asks applicants to be ready to work largely independently in a semi-remote setting where the cellular signal is very limited, though Wi-Fi reaches the house. The residency pays a 1,200 US dollar stipend plus up to 100 dollars for materials used in public programming, and is funded by a donation from the Shenandoah National Park Trust. In return the resident presents at least two public programs during the stay and donates one original work to the Trust within six months; the park may show donated work in local galleries, visitor centres and community outreach. The park's own account of the programme makes clear that photography is a real part of it: the 2026 cohort of seven included a night photographer who gave a public programme on painting with light at Big Meadows. Practical conditions matter here. Artists must provide their own art supplies and must have their own transport to and within the park for the whole residency, and proposals or finished works may not manipulate or disturb the park's natural and cultural landscape except as the Superintendent's Compendium allows. The house is for the artist alone: joint applications from collaborating artists are considered, but family members, friends and partners may not stay in it. The application is a single submission through CaFE with a 5 dollar fee and up to five files - still images, audio or video, five in total, of which at least two work samples are required - plus answers to several questions, a resume, the applicant's availability and a reference contact; the park asks for demonstrated proficiency and a strong commitment to the chosen medium, and applicants must be at least 18. One practical note on where to apply: the park's own page has not been updated for this round, still presenting the 2026 residents and an email alert for when applications are announced, so the CaFE listing is the live application. The published timeline runs from a panel review between 13 January and 14 February 2027, through notice of non-selection or an interview request on 19 February and finalist interviews from 22 February to 5 March, to decisions shared with interviewed applicants on 22 March 2027. Applications close at 11:59pm Eastern on 12 January 2027, a deadline the park states it will not extend under any circumstances.
