Travel Photographer of the Year 2026

Long-running international travel photography award, founded in 2003 and judged by a panel of professional photographers and editors. Enter four-image portfolios across Adventures & Journeys, Cultures Traditions & Celebrations, Landscapes (Natural & Urban) and Wildlife/Sealife/Birdlife, or single frames in the One Shot categories: In Nature, People of the World, Street, and Travel in Black & White. Free Young Travel Photographer categories for under-18s and a Rising Talent Award round out the programme. Winners share cash prizes, imaging software and extensive media exposure, with the overall Travel Photographer of the Year title as the headline honour. Both discount windows have closed: the 10% early-bird ended 1 July 2026 and the £99 unlimited-entry TPOTY Pass ended 14 July 2026, so entries now go at the standard ladder — £14 for a One Shot single image, £28 for a four-image portfolio, £30 for the six-image Rising Talent entry, with £20 for two One Shots, £36 for two portfolios and £59 for either the ten-One-Shot or the four-plus-two twelve-image combination. Entries close 12 October 2026, finalists are announced 21 November, the People’s Choice vote runs 20 December to 10 January 2027 and winners are announced 17 January 2027.
