X ROID Cologne 2027

Prize Exhibition

The Stage Gallery's international instant art expo returns to Cologne, the city where it began as InstantCologne in 2019, after editions in Cologne, Bonn, Vienna and Basel. X ROID Cologne 2027 runs 21–23 May 2027 at the gallery's new Cologne location on Gottesweg, a three-day festival of instant photography with a Pola Walk through the city and an international gathering of instant artists. Only original instant photographs are eligible — no prints. Accepted formats are SX-70, 600, Spectra, Packfilm, Square, Wide and Mini, from Polaroid, Polaroid Originals, Impossible Project and Fuji Instax. One exhibition space holds up to 4 Polaroids (SX-70/600/Packfilm) or a maximum of 2 8x10-inch Polaroids, and artists may book multiple spaces — the fee is charged per space, so a second space doubles it. The participation fee covers professional framing of the work by the gallery (own frames by arrangement) and inclusion in the exhibition catalogue, and — a change from the Bonn edition, which took 25% — the gallery takes no sales commission at all, so artists keep 100% of any sale. Return shipping is not included. The exhibition is juried and curated by The Stage Gallery itself; no outside juror is named, and there is no cash prize. Applying is a binding commitment: the organiser states that acceptance makes the participation fee due and non-refundable even if the artist then cancels. Copyright stays with the artist throughout, with a promotional-use licence for the gallery and the catalogue. Application is by email to submissions@thestagegallery.com with the subject line “Application X roid Cologne 2027”, artist and contact details, and JPG files at 1000px on the longest side.
