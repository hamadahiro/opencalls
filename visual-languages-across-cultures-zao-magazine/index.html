Visual Languages Across Cultures — ZAO Magazine

Prize Publication

A guest-edited special issue of ZAO, the independent art magazine published in Glasgow by Molend Art and carrying an ISSN assigned through the British Library. The issue asks how contemporary art becomes a shared language across cultures, and looks for work reflecting on cultural diaspora, identity, memory and cross-cultural communication. All mediums are accepted — painting, sculpture, photography, installation, ceramics, mixed media, digital media, performance documentation, textile and experimental practices — from artists 18 and over worldwide at any career stage, up to six original works each. Guest editor Mengyi Grace Wang, a contemporary artist and researcher, works with editor-in-chief Bowen Wu. ZAO favours practices that are still unfolding and builds a cultural passage between East Asia and the UK. Free to enter; AI-generated work is strictly prohibited.
