RC50: A City in Focus

Prize $200 stipend

A free citywide photography exhibition marking Rancho Cucamonga's 50th anniversary, run by the city's own RC Public Art programme, "showcasing the people, places, and stories that define our community". An Art Selection Panel chosen by the City picks 50 photographs to be displayed in public spaces across the city — digitally applied to display banners by a third-party vendor, shown at community locations and events through the anniversary year and on the exhibition's own website. Each selected photographer is paid a $200 stipend. The city invites photographers of all experience levels; themes are left to the photographer within four prompts — community (people and everyday moments, neighbourhoods and landmarks, celebrations and traditions), the city's history, the topography of the land, and nature native to the region — and applicants also write a 100-word community connection statement and complete the prompt "My Rancho Cucamonga is…" in three words. One entry per applicant, free to enter. Open to all ages with parent or guardian permission for minors, but the application's eligibility block requires that the artist reside in the Inland Empire region (the call summary words this more loosely as a preference for applicants who work, reside or attend school there); current City employees are not eligible. Entries close December 3, 2026 at 11:59pm PDT, or earlier if 200 applications are received — whichever comes first. Judging runs January 4–25, 2027 and selections are announced by February 1, 2027. READ THE RIGHTS TERMS: the artist retains copyright, but "the City owns all installed artwork and reserves the right to copy, reproduce and adjust the artwork as it sees fit, in perpetuity", and no guarantee is given for the location or longevity of the display.
