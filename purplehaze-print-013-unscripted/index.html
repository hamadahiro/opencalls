PURPLEHAZE PRINT 013 — Unscripted

Free open call for the Autumn–Winter 2026 printed edition of PURPLEHAZE, an independent Berlin magazine published by HAZE ART & MEDIA GROUP. The theme is UNSCRIPTED, and the editors put it like this: the image does not always begin with intention — sometimes it forms in the space where control loosens, where timing overtakes composition, and where the frame captures more than it can contain. They are looking for work that holds tension, presence and unpredictability, and say they are drawn not only to the captured moment but to work where the process, the material or chance itself leads the artist toward a result that could not have been planned in advance. Staged or not, what matters is whether the work carries the feeling of something uncontrolled. Photographers, painters, performance artists, installation artists and image-makers of all kinds are invited; the call is open to artists worldwide at any stage of their career. Submit up to 12 works or one cohesive series as JPEGs at a minimum of 2000px on the long edge and/or a link to a portfolio or website, with a project statement of up to 150 words and a short artist bio, by email to the magazine. Selected projects appear in the Autumn–Winter 2026 printed edition. On rights: submitting grants PURPLEHAZE non-exclusive rights to reproduce selected work in the issue, and all other rights remain with the artist. Work is reviewed by the PURPLEHAZE editorial team on a separate timeline from the magazine's general submissions, and selected artists are notified by October 15, 2026. Read what does and does not come back before you submit: the magazine's stated policy is that complimentary print copies are not provided for independently selected editorial contributions, and contributors selected through the editorial process receive a 20% discount on one preordered copy of the issue; the magazine also states that publication is never conditional on payment. Previously shared work is welcome for art and photography projects, including work already posted on the artist's own social media — the magazine states that a post on your own account does not count as prior publication. One scope limit to read before applying: the call is for independent editorial and artistic submissions, and projects centred on a single designer, brand, collection, campaign or commercial identity are handled separately through the magazine's partnership formats.
