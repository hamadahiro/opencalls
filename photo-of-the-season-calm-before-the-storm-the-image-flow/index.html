Photo of the Season — Calm Before the Storm

The autumn cycle of The Image Flow's quarterly Photo of the Season competition, themed Calm Before the Storm — quiet images full of tension, where what counts as a storm is left to the photographer: extreme weather, a high-stakes performance, a protest, a party, or something unnamed. The Image Flow is a photography gallery, print lab and education space at 328 Sir Francis Drake Boulevard in San Anselmo, California, and judges in house on composition, colour, contrast and overall quality alongside subject interest, point of view and whether the image tells a story. Open to photographers worldwide, amateur and professional. $15 for up to three images. First place $150, second $100, third $50; the call page promises three honorable mentions where the terms page still says two. All winners and honorable mentions are featured in the gallery's newsletter, website and social media, and selected images from all four 2026 seasons are shown together in a group exhibition at The Image Flow in 2027 — if an exhibited print does not sell, the artist collects it or pays return shipping, and unclaimed prints are recycled after 30 days. Eligible media are analogue film, digital, alternative process, printmaking and digital art; movies/video and AI-generated images are excluded, and the two surfaces disagree on mixed media — the call page excludes it outright while the terms page admits photography-based mixed-media. The terms also bar overt sexual content or graphic nudity, political imagery and religious iconography, and violent, sorrowful or disturbing subjects including images of weapons — a restriction worth reading before paying, since the call's own prompt offers a protest as one possible storm. Submissions opened September 22, 2026 and close Monday December 21, 2026 at 11:59 PM; artists are notified by email and no notification date is published.
