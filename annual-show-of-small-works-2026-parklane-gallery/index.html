Annual Show of Small Works 2026

Parklane Gallery's Annual Winter Show of Small Works is a seasonal favourite in Kirkland, Washington, timed to the holiday shopping season so that visitors can buy work and take it home immediately rather than waiting for the show to close. Parklane is a co-operative gallery whose programme covers painting, photography, sculpture, glasswork and jewellery, and the call is open to any 2-D or 3-D artwork not previously shown at the gallery. Awards are $500 for first place, $300 for second and $250 for third, plus honourable mentions, additional special recognition awards, and separate miniature awards for pieces that meet the definition and quality of a miniature. The juror is to be announced. Hanging work must be no larger than 13 inches in height or width and 3-D work no more than 6 inches in any dimension; there is no lower size limit, and accepted pieces with an image area under 25 square inches may be shown in a separate Miniatures section. Entry is $25 plus $5 per piece for up to seven pieces per artist. The show is both hung and online: the juror chooses prizes from all accepted pieces, every accepted work goes into the online store when the show starts, and because wall space is limited some pieces hang in November and others in December. Notifications go out 28 September, accepted work is due 16 October, and the exhibition runs 4 November 2026 to 2 January 2027.
