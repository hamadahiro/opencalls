34th Annual Open Juried Show

The Guild of Creative Art's all-media October show, running at its Shrewsbury gallery October 4-28, 2026. Photography is one of three entry categories and is defined tightly: all entries must have originated with a camera, composites may use no one else's frames, stock photography is barred, and any image containing AI-generated content, including Photoshop's generative tools, is disqualified. Up to three works, none over 48 inches on any side including the frame; diptychs and triptychs count as a single entry. The jurors are Jim LaSala, a fine art photographer and managing partner at Xact Studios in Hillsborough, and James Yarosh, artist and owner of James Yarosh Associates Fine Art Gallery in Holmdel. Around $2,300 in cash awards is on offer and six of the named prizes go to photography: $200 Best in Photography plus $100 awards for macro, landscape, nature, people and action work, alongside ten Bronze Achievement medals. Work is hand-delivered on September 30 and the Guild takes a 33 1/3% commission on sales.
