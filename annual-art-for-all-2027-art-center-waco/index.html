Annual Art For All 2027

Art Center Waco, a Texas arts centre running since 1973, opens its second Annual Art for All juried exhibition to emerging and established visual artists living in the state of Texas and aged 18 or over. The framing is deliberately wide - the centre's mission is that art is for everyone - and all 2D and 3D media are eligible, with sculpture, painting, drawing, printmaking, photography, mixed media and ceramic fine art named in the prospectus. Read the media rule to its end before ruling yourself out: it says "no digital prints", but the same paragraph clarifies that what is barred is reproductions of original works, giving giclee prints of paintings as the example, "while photographic prints are allowed", and a later hanging clause confirms it by requiring works on paper, photography and collage on paper to be framed and ready to hang. Up to three pieces may be entered for $25, each made within the past five years and not previously shown at Art Center Waco. There is no minimum size and a 72-inch maximum in any direction, with wall works capped at 60lbs; sawtooth hangers, clip frames, keyhole slots, loose eye hooks, adhesives, velcro and command strips are all refused. Three awards go to work in the show - $500 Best of Show, $300 second and $200 third - chosen from the hung exhibition and announced at the opening reception. Selected artists are notified on or before Friday 13 November 2026 and must get their work to the gallery between 1 and 18 December, by drop-off or shipping; shipped work carries a $15 handling fee per box or crate, billed separately after acceptance. Everything hangs in the Beard Gallery at 701 S. 8th Street from 7 January to 5 March 2027, priced for sale with a 30% commission to the centre and 70% to the artist, and the centre says proceeds from sales support both the participating artists and its own exhibitions, education and community programmes. No juror is named for this edition.
