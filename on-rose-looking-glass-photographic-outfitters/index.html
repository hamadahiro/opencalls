On Rose

Prizes Exhibition at Abrams Claghorn Gallery Trophy

Looking Glass, the independent photographic outfitters in Berkeley, launches On Rose to mark its move to a new home on Rose Street. Photographers are invited to interpret 'rose' as broadly as they like — as a flower, a colour, a symbol, a place, a story, an act of resistance or a moment of renewal. A rose does not need to appear in the picture, but the connection should be visible and meaningful. Each photograph is assigned to one of six categories: The Botanical Rose; Symbol, Story and Metaphor; Rose as Colour; Celebration, Honor and Community; On Rose Street; and They Rose Up. Entry uses a sliding-scale contribution of $5, $10, $15, $20, $25, $30, $40 or $50 — the amount chosen has no effect on judging, selection or awards — and one entry covers up to three photographs. Work is judged anonymously by Bay Area photographers Becky Jaffe, Sarah Deragon and Brandon Ruffin on interpretation of the theme (40%), visual voice and emotional resonance (40%) and photographic execution (20%). The overall winner, category winners and honourable mentions are exhibited at Abrams Claghorn Gallery & Shop in Albany, California in October 2026, with an opening reception; the overall winner also receives a commemorative trophy. Selected photographers are expected to be notified in October 2026. Photographs may be made on film, digital cameras or phones, and older work is welcome, but every entry must begin as a real-world capture by the entrant.
