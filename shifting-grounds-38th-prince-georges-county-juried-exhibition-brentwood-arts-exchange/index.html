Shifting Grounds: 38th Annual Prince George's County Juried Exhibition

The 38th annual Prince George's County Juried Exhibition, run by the Arts and Cultural Heritage Division of M-NCPPC's Department of Parks and Recreation and shown at the Brentwood Arts Exchange, the anchor institution of Maryland's Gateway Arts District, from 16 November 2026 to 9 January 2027. This year's theme, Shifting Grounds, asks artists to reflect on transformation across personal life, community, technology and environment - how we adapt, resist or redefine ourselves as identities and ideologies reconfigure - and the organisers say it should be interpreted broadly. The juror is Chris Combs, a Washington DC and Mount Rainier artist whose sculptural work incorporates and questions technology; he was 2025 Artist in Residence at the Sanford Underground Research Facility, a five-time DC Commission on the Arts and Humanities fellow, and a former photo editor for National Geographic. Photography is named in the eligible media, alongside painting, drawing, assemblage, fiber, video, printmaking, encaustic, glass, metal, collage and mixed media; work made with generative AI will not be selected, and reproductions are out. Open to artists 18 and over who live, work, study or keep a studio in Prince George's County, Maryland, with up to three artworks each, no size limit, and no work previously shown in this exhibition. There is no entry fee, but every participant must complete M-NCPPC's one-time online vendor registration (a W-9 is needed) so that the county can pay them: each artist selected receives a $150 participation stipend after the show closes, and on the juror's recommendation the Division may purchase works for the M-NCPPC Permanent Art Collection. M-NCPPC insures consigned work for 75% of its sale value and takes a 25% commission on sales. Submissions close at 11:59pm on 23 October 2026; selected artists are notified by 28 October, work is dropped off on 7 or 9 November, and the reception is Saturday 21 November, 3 to 5pm.
