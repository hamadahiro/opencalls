"Art Is: South Dakota" Juried Exhibition

Prizes Five-venue touring exhibition Catalogue

Five South Dakota institutions have put together one statewide juried show and a two-year tour for it. Artists currently living in South Dakota, and enrolled citizens of any of the state's federally recognized tribes wherever they live, may submit up to three works to Art Is: South Dakota, built around the open prompt "Art Is…" — each application carries a short first-person statement (1,000 characters, roughly 160 words) answering what art means to you. The organisers describe the exhibition as "designed to broaden inclusion and access, creating opportunities for artists at every stage of their careers". Photography is named in the eligible media alongside painting, drawing, original prints, sculpture, ceramics, mixed media, textiles, fine crafts, traditional and folk arts, video art and artist books; reproductions are not eligible and work must have been completed within the last three years. Entry is $25 for up to three works — the same price whether you send one or three — and fee waivers or scholarships are available on request from the Washington Pavilion's Visual Arts Center. Payment is by card, PayPal or cheque. Employees of any of the exhibiting institutions may not enter. Selected work tours five venues over more than two years: South Dakota Art Museum in Brookings (30 January – 23 May 2027), USD Art Galleries in Vermillion (28 June – 6 August 2027), the Dahl Arts Center in Rapid City (8 October – 30 November 2027), the Washington Pavilion in Sioux Falls (17 December 2027 – March 2028) and Čhatkú Arts Center at Pine Ridge (March – May 2028). Accepted work is a loan for the full tour and must stay available for it, so a sale-ready print is not what this call wants. Works are reproduced in an exhibition catalogue and used by the institutions for documentation, publicity and education. The jury is two invited jurors — one from outside South Dakota and one an enrolled citizen of a South Dakota federally recognized tribe, both to be announced — plus a representative of each exhibiting institution: Taylor McKeown (South Dakota Art Museum), Amy Fill (USD Art Galleries), Erin Castle (Washington Pavilion Visual Arts Center), Ashley Pourier (Čhatkú Arts Center) and Jeannie Larson (Dahl Arts Center). Applications close 11:59pm CST on 6 December 2026, acceptances go out 11 December, loan agreements are due 16 December and work is delivered between 16 December 2026 and 8 January 2027 to Brookings, Rapid City or the Akta Lakota Museum in Chamberlain. Practical limits worth reading before you frame: images for the application must be JPEGs no more than 1,200 pixels on the longest side at no less than 300 dpi; works on paper must be framed with acrylic glazing rather than glass; D-rings or screw eyes with wire or a cleat system, no sawtooth hangers; and packaged work may not exceed 50 lbs or 60 inches. Artists insure work in transit and the institutions cover it once received. No cash awards are advertised — what is on offer is the tour and the catalogue.
