Trois Points Mag Vol 16 — The Future

Prize Publication

Trois Points Mag is an independent contemporary photography magazine made by an international team of artists based in Paris. It was founded in 2016 as an online-only publication, paused through the covid years and came back in December 2022 both online and as a high-quality printed issue; its stated concept is to promote new contemporary photography, analog and digital, from photographers anywhere in the world, always around a proposed theme. Vol 16, the Winter 2026 paper issue, is themed The Future, and the magazine's question is "What does the future look like before it happens?" The editors are interested in visions of what is yet to come - imagined, anticipated, feared, desired or remembered - and say the future may be technological or primitive, intimate or collective, utopian or dystopian, real or impossible, emerging through landscapes, bodies, architecture, objects, rituals, altered realities or everyday moments: "There is no single future. Show us yours." Submissions go by email with the subject line "Vol 16 / The Future": up to 15 images per project, each file titled with the photographer's full name, at 300 dpi in a large file format and under 50 MB in total. A short text of three or four sentences about the theme is mandatory and is what gets printed alongside the work if selected - the magazine explicitly does not want a general artist statement - together with a brief four-or-five-sentence bio rather than a CV, website and social links, and the photographer's current city and country. The camera used must be named, and captures made with an iPhone or other smartphone are not accepted. There is no entry fee; the magazine suggests a voluntary 5 euro donation to help sustain the project. The deadline is 30 November 2026. CAUTION: the magazine's own website has not been updated for this issue — www.troispointsmag.com/submissions still presents Vol 11 (Autumn 2025, "The Consuming World") as its newest call, with a 12-image cap, so the brief above is the one the magazine published on its own Instagram on 5 October 2026 and the website's guidelines page does not match it. The magazine's standing terms add that every image must be the artist's own original work, that files are accepted in digital format only, that there is no payment for publication — a contributor buys a copy of the magazine only if they want one — and that submitting grants permission to publish the image together with the photographer's name and city of residence. SECOND CAUTION, MEASURED 10 NOVEMBER 2026 AND THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE PASS TO FAIL: that Instagram post no longer serves its caption, so none of the terms above can be re-confirmed at source. The post page still loads with its like count and its 5 October 2026 date, but the caption element renders empty and Instagram’s own embed endpoint for the same post answers “The link to this photo or video may be broken, or the post may have been removed”. Captions on the magazine’s other posts do still read and the account is active — it posted on 9 November — so nothing says the call has closed; but the deadline, the voluntary donation and the fifteen-image cap all rest on one reading of a caption that is now unreadable. Confirm with the magazine before sending work.
