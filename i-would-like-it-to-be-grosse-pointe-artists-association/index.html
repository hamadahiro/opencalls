I would like it to be

Prize Solo exhibition

A juried show at the Grosse Pointe Artists Association's gallery on the War Memorial campus in Grosse Pointe Farms, on the Lake St Clair shore north-east of Detroit. The theme asks artists to share a dream or creative spark — to picture the world ahead for themselves and for others as they would like it to be. The juror is Sabrina Nelson, a Detroit artist of more than thirty-five years and a Kresge Artist Fellow, who teaches studio art at the Detroit Institute of Arts, where she lectures and gives artist demonstrations, and is on staff at the College for Creative Studies. First prize is a solo show at the gallery in 2027. The call is open to any artist over 18, plus alumni of the association's Promising Artists exhibitions — there is no geographic bar, but accepted work has to be in the gallery's hands for intake between noon and 2.30pm on Saturday 5 December, so travel or shipping arrangements are the artist's problem to solve. Work must be original: anything made under an instructor's direction, commercial kits, prefabricated models and hobby crafts are all ineligible, as are large installations and pieces already shown with the association. There is no size limit, though gallery space is a factor in final acceptance. Entry runs $30 for members and $40 for non-members for the first two pieces, with up to four more at $10 each and a $3 transaction fee on top; images must be JPGs of at least 600 × 600 pixels, evenly lit and free of reflections. Two-dimensional work should arrive framed and ready to hang, with acrylic rather than glass. Not everything has to be priced to sell, but anything that is and does carries a commission of 35% on member work and 45% on non-member work, so price accordingly. Entries close at midnight on Monday 16 November 2026, results are emailed by Friday 20 November, the exhibition runs 9 December 2026 to 22 January 2027 with a gallery talk at 2pm on Saturday 12 December, and work is collected between 10am and noon on Saturday 23 January.
