Hodder Fellowship 2027-28

Prize $93,000

The Hodder Fellowship is awarded by Princeton University's Lewis Center for the Arts to artists and writers of exceptional promise — composers, choreographers, performance artists, visual artists, writers, translators and humanists with, in the fellowship's own phrase, "much more than ordinary intellectual and literary gifts". Candidates are chosen more for promise than for performance, which makes it one of the rare major fellowships explicitly aimed at where a practice is going rather than what it has already accumulated.

The award is a ten-month appointment carrying a stipend of $93,000. Its stated purpose is to give recipients "studious leisure" — time to pursue an independent project and undertake significant new work. There is no formal teaching requirement, distinguishing it from the Princeton Arts Fellowship, which asks fellows to teach a course each semester.

Eligibility rotates by discipline. For this cycle Princeton is accepting applications from the Program in Dance, the Program in Theater and Music Theater, the Program in Visual Arts, and the Department of Music — so photographers and other lens-based artists working under the Visual Arts umbrella can apply this round. US citizenship is not required.

Applications consist of a résumé, a 500-word project proposal and work samples, which may be images, writing excerpts or performance video depending on the discipline. Previous Princeton Arts Fellowship recipients are not eligible, and an applicant may apply for the Hodder no more than twice in a lifetime. The deadline is 8 September 2026 at 11:59pm EDT, and there is no application fee.
