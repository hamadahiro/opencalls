Small Works 2026

The Georgetown Art Center, run by the City of Georgetown's Arts and Culture programme in the town's historic district north of Austin, holds an annual juried exhibition of work no larger than twelve inches in any direction. Each piece has to distil a full artistic vision into a compact format, and the hang is designed for close looking. Entries may be in any media, 2-D or 3-D, abstract or representational; photography and digital entries are explicitly invited, and camera-generated photographs, computer-enhanced digital images, digital collage or montage and digital and graphic design may all be entered, so long as the output is framed or mounted with hanging hardware and stays inside the twelve-inch limit. Only original work is accepted, and reproductions and giclee copies of other artworks are not. Artists may enter up to three pieces, of which one will be shown if selected. Work must have been made between January 2023 and the closing date and must be for sale; the centre keeps a 30 per cent commission. Entries are scored online by the Georgetown Art Center Exhibits Committee, with the guest judge choosing the awards. Selected work may be hand-delivered or shipped with a prepaid return label. The show runs 20 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, with a reception and prize-giving on 21 November.
