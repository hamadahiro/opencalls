McClure Gallery 2027–28 Exhibition Programme

Prizes Exhibition CARFAC exhibition fee

The McClure Gallery is the exhibition space of the Visual Arts Centre in Westmount, on the western flank of Montreal, and it is now taking proposals for its 2027–28 season. The call is open to contemporary artists of all media and to curators with an exhibition proposal; the only technical warning the gallery gives is that its A/V equipment is limited and may not support certain new-media works.

Two things separate this from the run of gallery open calls. It costs nothing to apply, and the gallery pays — selected artists receive CARFAC-based exhibition rights fees, at the A.1.1 rate for a solo show and A.1.3 for a group show. Selected proposals may be presented in one or both of the gallery's two adjoining spaces, and a proposed floor plan is welcome but entirely optional.

Proposals go to a committee of peers that is renewed every year, so the eyes on your dossier change from season to season. The gallery is explicit about what it is looking for beyond the work itself: your proposal should say why you want to show here specifically, in keeping with the Visual Arts Centre's mandate.

The dossier is precisely specified and the gallery enforces it. You complete an online submission form, then send everything else as a single WeTransfer: 10 to 20 JPGs at no more than 1.5 MB each and/or links to video excerpts of eight minutes maximum, plus three separate PDFs — an image list with artist name, title, year, medium and dimensions in centimetres; a CV of up to three pages; and a two-page maximum description of your practice and the proposed project. Do not put images inside a PDF; a proposal submitted as one PDF document will not be evaluated. Do not email the files as attachments, do not zip them, and do not send cover letters or press material, which are not passed to the jury. The one wrinkle: the entry form itself is softer than the page, asking for the documents "via WeTransfer or as one compiled PDF". Send one WeTransfer and you satisfy both.

The gallery prioritises artists living in Quebec but does not exclude applicants from elsewhere in Canada or abroad. Anyone who has had a solo exhibition at the McClure within the last five years is asked to wait before submitting again.

The deadline is October 15, 2026 at 11:59 pm, and the committee's decision reaches applicants by email in roughly three months.
