Erbleichen

Prize Group exhibition

NEU! is an artist-run space on Untere Vogelsangstrasse in Winterthur with a curated programme of a new show every two months. For a group exhibition in November 2026 it is looking for work from a world that flows thick and slow and is losing its colour — spaces where time seems to stand still and the future and its promises go unfulfilled, time loops, system errors, ghosts, doppelgängers of decay. The great bleaching. Proposals are welcome across all mediums and may be unfinished, unproduced, in process or complete, and are sent by email. There is no entry fee. NEU! notes that it is not able to cover shipping or similar costs.
