Changes: Nothing Stays

The Ludington Area Center for the Arts asks what happens when something becomes something else: personal growth, cultural shifts, environmental transformation, political upheaval, emotional evolution, physical metamorphosis. Photography sits among the eleven accepted media, alongside collage, video, installation and sculpture. International, 18+, $30 for two works, with the show running 5–27 March 2027.
