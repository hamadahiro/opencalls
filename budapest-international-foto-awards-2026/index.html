Budapest International Foto Awards 2026

Annual international photography award promoting professional, amateur, and student photographers to Budapest's growing art community, from the team behind the Lucie Awards and IPA. Categories include Advertising, Analog/Film, Architecture, Book, Editorial, Fine Art, Nature, People, Portfolio, and Special, with single images and series of 2–11 photographs accepted. Top titles are Photographer of the Year and New Talent of the Year, with cash prizes, the BIFA trophy, and a winners' exhibition — historically at the House of Lucie Budapest. Jury includes Hossein Farmani and BBC Wildlife's Sheena Harvey. Final deadline August 5, 2026.
