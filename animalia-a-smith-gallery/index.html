animalia

A Smith Gallery in Johnson City, Texas announces "animalia", a juried online photography exhibition on animals - "fauna, beasts, birds, zoological, mammals, wildlife, amphibians, creatures, reptiles, fish, insects, critters" - juried by Traer Scott, the Providence-based photographer and author of fifteen books whose series Natural History has been shown at the University of Maine Museum of Art, the Griffin Museum of Photography and the American Museum of the Americas. Entries are due 2 November 2026, results are emailed 13 November 2026, and selected work is shown in the gallery's online exhibition from 27 November 2026 to 7 January 2027, with a reception on 26 December 2026 at 3pm CST and a GalleryTalk video posted in ASG Conversations. The entry fee is $40 for the first five images and $5 for each additional image, with no limit on how many you may submit; images should be 1800 pixels on the longest side, JPEG at 72 ppi, unwatermarked. The juror selects up to 47 images in total, 27 of which also go into "The 27", a hardbound fine-art book of the exhibition. The Jurors Award and Directors Award each carry a month-long online solo exhibition, a copy of "The 27" and a feature story on the gallery blog; a Visitors Award of $100 goes to the most-liked image on Instagram. Photographers submitting 13 or more images get a complimentary 30-minute review by the gallery directors; below that a review costs $20. Jurying is blind. The gallery lists all selected work in its online store and retains 30% of the sale price. Open to professional and amateur photographers worldwide in all photographic mediums; work previously exhibited in an A Smith Gallery competition is not eligible, and the gallery states it prefers not to accept images created with artificial intelligence "due to the lack of clarity concerning copyright issues".
