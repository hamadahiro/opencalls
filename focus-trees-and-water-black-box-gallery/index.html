Focus: Trees and Water

Prizes Exhibition Catalog

Black Box Gallery's next themed juried show takes trees and water as its subject, "both subjects in a dynamic and diverse exhibition on contemporary photography", and the juror is Todd Johnson, the gallery's founder and director, who holds an MFA in Photography from the San Francisco Art Institute and has worked as a photography portfolio reviewer. The selection has two tiers: 26 photographs are chosen for the physical show in Portland, Oregon from 1 to 25 January 2027, and a further 50 for the gallery's online annex, with all 76 reproduced in a catalog available for purchase on Blurb Books. The economics are the reason this gallery is worth the entry: Black Box provides free printing, free matting and free framing for every selected work — accepted photographers send a high-resolution TIFF or JPEG and the gallery prints it, in 18" x 24" black frames with pre-cut white mats (mats for other aspect ratios and a mat cutter are on hand). Only one exhibition print is made, and if a sale inquiry comes in the photographer is notified and edition information discussed before anything is confirmed; the gallery takes 50% on sales, and work does not have to be offered for sale at all. Exhibition prints are kept in the gallery's flat files after the show for future sale and promotion of the photographer. Entry is by email with a PayPal fee payment: up to five JPEGs, 1,200 pixels on the longest side at 72 PPI, 5 MB or less each, named firstname_lastname1.jpg and so on, with titles and file names listed in the email body. Open to all photographers worldwide, amateur and professional; all rights to the photographs remain with the photographer. Selections are announced 7 to 10 days after the deadline.
