FLOAT Magazine — Open Submissions

Year-round, free submissions to FLOAT Photo Magazine, an independent photography publication that runs printed issues alongside online features, online exhibitions, artist portfolios, book reviews and Instagram takeovers. All photographic styles are accepted; the editors are looking for cohesive, thought-through bodies of work, for both the online content and the magazine issues. The online-portfolio route asks for 10–25 images from a single body of work as JPEGs at 1200 pixels on the long side, plus an artist statement or relevant text with a title and description of the work and a link to your website — files must be attached, not sent via WeTransfer, which the team says often expires before they download it. Separate routes exist for an Instagram takeover (send your Instagram link, username and a self-portrait; if selected you supply up to 12 images with captions, though the waiting list is long) and for a book review (send the full book PDF with 1–5 images; if a contributing writer picks it up you mail them a physical copy, which is not returned). Everything goes by email to the address FLOAT publishes on its submissions page. Work not selected can be resubmitted later with a different body of work. FLOAT is volunteer-run and says it cannot always reply to every email, and it publishes no notification window. This is the magazine's standing submissions policy, separate from its occasional themed printed-issue calls, which charge a fee.
