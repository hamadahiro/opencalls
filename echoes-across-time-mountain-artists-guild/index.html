Echoes Across Time

Prize Exhibition

The Mountain Artists Guild's winter juried show in Prescott, Arizona is mounted as a concert partnership with the Arizona Philharmonic, whose “Echoes Across Time” programme — Handel's Violin Sonata in D Major, Fazıl Say's Four Cities and Tchaikovsky's Piano Trio in A Minor — is the brief. Artists need not illustrate a particular composition; the brief is what the music awakens — a memory, a place, a person, a culture, a moment in history. The Guild suggests memory and nostalgia, childhood and family tradition, cultural heritage, remembrance, changing landscapes and cities, connections between generations, rhythm and movement, and music translated into colour, shape and texture. Photography is one of the two judged categories in its own right (“all types of photography mediums”), alongside original paintings; the show is 2D only, with no three-dimensional work, and reproductions, giclées and printed merchandise are excluded, as are nudes and work unsuitable for family viewing. CAUTION, TWO CONTRADICTORY ELIGIBILITY LINES ON ONE PAGE: the prospectus header reads “Open to members of Mountain Artists Guild only” and the fee table lists a member rate and nothing else, while a later paragraph reads “this exhibition is open to all artists”; the two sibling shows the Guild ran this year both printed a non-member rate as well, so the members-only reading is the one to plan around. Up to three works may be entered for the base fee, and members also agree to volunteer a minimum of four hours during the run or pay a $20 buyout. Entries are juried for the gallery show and then judged again in the gallery; the juror was still “TBA” when this entry was written. All artwork must be for sale, priced to carry the Guild's 25% member commission, and delivered in person — no shipping — with gallery intake on January 11, 2027. Canvas or frame size must be at least 154 square inches and no more than 1,728. The exhibition runs January 13 to February 14, 2027, award-winning work is shown at the February 14 concert at the Ruth Street Theater, and accepted entries stay in the online Web Gallery for three months. Note the cutoff is not end of day: entries close at 9 PM on January 2, 2027.
