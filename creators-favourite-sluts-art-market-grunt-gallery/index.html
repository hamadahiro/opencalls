Creators’ Favourite Sluts: A Two-Spirit/Indigiqueer Art Market

grunt gallery is seeking 2S and/or Queer Indigenous artists from Turtle Island and Palestine to join the roster of tabling artists for “Creators’ Favourite Sluts”, a Two-Spirit/Indigiqueer art market returning to the gallery on Saturday, December 12, 2026, from 1pm to 7pm. The market had its first run in February in celebration of Valentine’s Day.

The market takes over two rooms of the gallery, with space for 13–15 artists. Most artists will be two to one table, so applicants are asked to consider how much table space they will realistically need and to note in the application if they need more — the more artists are willing to share space, the more can be included. grunt provides chairs and tables; artists are responsible for handling their own transactions.

There is no fee to apply and no fee to table, and artists keep 100% of their proceeds. The deadline to apply is October 31, 2026, via grunt’s Google Form. Load-in starts at 12:30pm; grunt asks anyone for whom those times do not work to get in touch at vance@grunt.ca. Information on accessing grunt’s space is published at the foot of the events and calls page, and specific access concerns can be raised at access@grunt.ca.

From the intake form itself (Artist Intake Form — 2S/Indigiqueer Art Market 2026, created inside grunt.ca): “Vance Wright and grunt gallery are excited to present the second iteration of Creators’ Favourite Sluts”; “Submitting your application is not considered being selected for the market, official selections will be made end of October. Artists will be contacted on the status of their selection then.”; “Applications will be accepted until October 31st, at 11:59 PM.” Set-up runs 12:30–1pm and break-down 7–7:30pm, and submitting the application is taken as a commitment to that time frame. Required of every applicant: email, name, pronouns, “a short description of the artwork or wares you plan on selling. (I.e. beadwork, paintings, prints, stickers, etc.)”, whether half a table is workable, three to five images of the work, a bio of up to 100 words, and permission to use the materials in promotion. Optional: Nation and connection to that community, phone number, access needs, a portfolio or social link, and a headshot.

grunt gallery is an artist-run centre at 350 East 2nd Avenue in Vancouver, operating since 1984.
