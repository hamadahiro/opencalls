Benjamin Hall Photography Award 2026

Prizes £1,000 Exhibition

New award from Holt Art Projects — the team behind the Sir John Hurt Art Prize — celebrating photography as a way of seeing and engaging with the world. Open worldwide to any genre, analogue or digital; editing is allowed as long as the camera-made image remains central, but works built primarily in software are not eligible. Shortlisted works are exhibited in Holt, North Norfolk, July 20 – August 1, 2026, and the £1,000 winner is announced at the awards reception on July 19.
