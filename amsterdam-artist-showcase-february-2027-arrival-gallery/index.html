Amsterdam Artist Showcase — February 2027

Prize Exhibition

Arrival Gallery's three-day group showcase in Amsterdam, 12 to 14 February 2027, at Zesde Vogelstraat 40 in the city's west. The gallery takes submissions from painters, photographers, sculptors, illustrators, textile, mixed-media and digital artists, and describes itself as working with emerging, independent and mid-career practitioners. Photographic, moving-image and screen-based work is explicitly catered for: alongside hung physical work, Arrival presents digital pieces through curated display screens, digital exhibition formats, online artist features and city activations, which makes the digital route a realistic option for a photographer who does not want to ship and frame prints. Submitting work for review is free and there is no entry fee. The cost comes after selection, and Arrival publishes the floors rather than hiding them: the Digital Showcase route starts from £65 and the Physical Showcase route from £165, with the full format, timeline and preparation details sent to selected artists before they confirm a place. Applicants pick their preferred route — digital, physical or featured — when they submit, or ask the gallery to recommend one. Places are sold from a limited release, so the gallery's own framing is that confirmation secures a slot from the current batch rather than that selection alone guarantees a wall. Anyone considering this should read it for what it is: a paid-participation showcase, free to enter, where the real decision comes once a price is quoted.
