2027 Creative Arts Grant

The Bothell Arts Commission funds innovative projects by individual artists and by community and arts organisations that hire artists, with awards between $500 and $10,000. Applicants do not have to live in Bothell, but every funded project must take place in the city and culminate in a public event or presentation that is free or low-cost and open to Bothell residents and visitors - the commission's own examples of public benefit include exhibitions, screenings, workshops and freely accessible published or recorded material, and grant funds may cover equipment for public benefit such as 2D hanging devices. Only new or reimagined projects qualify; work already shown in Bothell is ineligible unless a new component is added, and permanent public art tied to capital construction, private events and content opposing a specific business, political or religious viewpoint are excluded. A panel scores applications on artistic innovation, public access and benefit, and project feasibility, with priority but not a requirement given to Bothell-based artists. There is no application fee. An optional grant workshop is held on 23 September 2026, applications close 16 October at 11:59pm Pacific, the commission approves selections on 19 November and notifies applicants on 23 November; funded projects must be completed and reported by 1 December 2027.
