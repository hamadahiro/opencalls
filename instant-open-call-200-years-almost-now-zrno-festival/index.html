Instant Open Call — 200 Years: Almost Now

ZRNO, the Macedonian international festival of alternative photography, is selecting 22 artists for "200 Years: Almost Now", a group exhibition of contemporary instant photography that travels across Europe. Work made with any instant process is eligible, with no restriction on format, camera, film or technique - Polaroid, Instax, integral and peel-apart film, expired film, manipulated instant photographs, transfers, lifts, emulsion experiments and hybrids are all welcome. The curatorial frame takes the 200th anniversary of Niépce's first permanent photograph as its starting point and asks what defines photography as an artistic practice today, with particular emphasis on work that expands, manipulates or recontextualises the instant image through physical intervention. The exhibition is confirmed for Analogmania in Timișoara (October 2026), the Rotlicht Festival in Austria (November/December 2026), ZRNO in Macedonia (spring 2027) and Novi Sad in Serbia (2027), with further international venues possible. Submission is free and open to artists worldwide at any career stage, and the festival explicitly encourages women, queer, ethnic-minority and non-European applicants. Selected artists pay a participation fee of €250 — the €200 early-bird rate has closed, and the rules PDF gives two different dates for that boundary — covering press and media relations, promotional design, exhibition setup and dismantling at every venue, transport of the works between venues, gallery rental in Skopje and Vienna, opening events and a printed catalogue with a curatorial text; artists cover the cost of sending their work.
