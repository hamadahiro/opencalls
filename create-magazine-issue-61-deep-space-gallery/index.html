Create! Magazine Issue #61 — Juried by Deep Space Gallery

Prizes Cover feature Publication

No theme this round, which for a photographer is either an invitation or a problem: with all subjects and media welcome, the work has to carry itself rather than answer a prompt. The jurors are the reason to bother. Jenna Geiger and Keith VanPelt run Deep Space out of a two-level space on the industrial edge of Bergen Hill, in Jersey City's Bergen-Lafayette neighborhood, and their exhibiting record — Spring/Break during Armory Week, Artsy's first FOUNDATIONS showcase, PERSONAL STRUCTURES at the European Cultural Centre during the 2024 Venice Biennale — is a working gallery's record, not a résumé assembled for a call.

One artist takes the cover of the print and digital issue, and the cover is a real package: a feature spread inside, a long interview, a slot on the Create! Podcast and a print copy. Everyone else selected gets a four-page spread in print and digital, website and social features, and a digital issue.

The money is plain. $35 covers three images, and the magazine says outright that extra images or an online interview cost more — worth reading the Submittable form before you pick how much to send. Submit up to 10 works with a 300-word bio and statement; cohesive series are what they ask for. Nothing further is charged if you are published, and copyright stays with you.

Jurying runs through December, winners are announced in January 2027 and the issue lands in Winter 2027. If the fee is the obstacle, Create! points to its free Studio Sunday submissions as the no-cost route onto the platform — a different thing from the print issue, but it exists.
