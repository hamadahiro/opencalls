The Art Advocacy Project 2D (TAAP 2D) 2027

The City of Loveland's Art in Public Places programme hangs a year-long, on-loan exhibition in the Central Galleria of the Loveland Public Library, and the 2027 call is open to Colorado artists working in any two-dimensional medium — the call names fiber, charcoal, mixed media and painting, and the prospectus deals with photography directly: selected work must stay up for the full year, but photographs that are part of a limited edition may be sold and the print replaced within the first six months of hanging. Selected artists are paid a $350 honorarium and may offer work for sale, though sales are not required; if a piece does sell, Art in Public Places retains 20% of the purchase value as commission. Since 2022 the programme has paired exhibiting artists with the Loveland Poet Laureate committee, so a selected artist may spend time talking through their work with a poet whose poem is then shown alongside it; selected artists should plan to attend the Artist Talk and Poetry Reading Reception on Saturday 27 March 2027. One entry per artist, free to apply, open to anyone 18 or older at any career stage. Work must be valued at $2,500 or less, no larger than 36 x 36 inches, professionally framed with protective glass where applicable and ready to hang on an art rail with D-rings fitted before delivery; the corridor is high-traffic, so fragile or unprotected work is unlikely to be selected. A jury panel of Art in Public Places representatives and citizen volunteers makes the selection. Contracts are signed by 5 March 2027, work is delivered to the library on 18 March 2027, and the display runs from late March 2027 to late February 2028.
