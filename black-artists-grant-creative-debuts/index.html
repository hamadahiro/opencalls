Black Artists Grant (BAG)

A no-strings £500 grant for Black artists based in the UK, awarded monthly by the artist-support platform Creative Debuts. Recipients may spend it on whatever they want — making new work, buying equipment or materials, travel, research, visiting exhibitions or conferences, or simply covering some life expenses — and the grant expects no outcomes and no reporting. Creative Debuts is explicit about why it exists: Black artists are systematically under-supported by the art world, by institutions, curators, the artist-led scene, major and minor funding bodies, the market, art schools and audiences too, and while the organisation says a full institutional overhaul is what is actually required, it hopes the grant helps recipients keep working in the meantime. It currently supports one recipient a month and is sponsored by Creative Debuts itself, whose grants page says more than £130,000 has gone to creatives through the programme. Eligibility is narrow and stated plainly: applicants must be based in the UK and must be Black — the page specifies Black heritage and Black identity, 'not politically Black, not POC or BAME'. There is no age limit and no deadline, since selection is a monthly rolling cycle; apply once only, because all applications are kept and reviewed in later months, and unsuccessful applicants are not notified. One thing to know before applying: the grant is described as open to all creative disciplines, with painters, musicians, directors, jewellers, set designers, sculptors and choreographers given as examples — photography is not among the examples named, and it does not appear on Creative Debuts' grants, featured-artists or open-calls pages either, so a photographer applies under the general all-disciplines wording rather than an explicit invitation. The application form sits on the grant page itself.
