MANIFEST 2026: Reflecting on Collaboration

Prize Exhibition

Third annual juried exhibition from fstop504, a volunteer-run New Orleans nonprofit that puts on free and low-cost photoshoots, workshops and exhibitions for photographers, artists and models out of its community studio, Lapin's Workshop. MANIFEST 2026 is part of the PhotoNOLA festival and hangs at the Contemporary Arts Center, New Orleans, from 5 December 2026 to 14 January 2027, opening on First Saturday with a photoshoot alongside the reception. This year the call widens beyond photography — immersive installation, sculpture, video installation, painting and mixed media are eligible alongside it — in recognition of the people who make the organisation's programming. The theme is collaboration, read broadly: work made with another artist or person, work made with skills learned at an fstop504 or Lapin's Workshop event, a photograph taken at one of those events, or collaboration in a more abstract sense — a muse, another artist, nature, society. Applicants answer at least one question about what collaboration means to them. One condition to read before applying: entry is open to anyone who has attended at least one fstop504 or Lapin's Workshop event, and the organisers point out that there is still time to attend one; photographers who have left New Orleans may still submit if they can arrange shipping or delivery of the work. The $35 fee covers installation materials and marketing, and partial or full waivers are available on request if cost is a barrier. Jurors are drawn from the city's institutions: a gallery director, NOMA's curator of photographs, the CAC's executive director and fstop504's founder.
