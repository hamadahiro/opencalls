We Are the Ones

Prizes $200 / $100 Exhibition

Willow Street Gallery, the exhibition project of DC Arts Studios in the Takoma neighbourhood, is looking for work about community — what it means to hold and be held by other people, and to build something better together. The theme takes its title from the closing line of June Jordan's Poem for South African Women, and the gallery is explicit that it is a starting point rather than a literal brief: the call frames healing as a communal endeavour and resilience as something rooted in giving of yourself and receiving the care of others. Photography is named directly in the accepted media, alongside painting, drawing, mixed media and textile art; the gallery can take 2D work in any physical medium but cannot accommodate video or other digital media this round. Open to artists 18 and over living and working in the greater Washington DC region — DC, Maryland and Virginia. Two cash prizes are awarded, $200 for best in show and $100 for the runner-up, and the show runs 12 September to 3 October 2026, with an opening reception on 12 September and a closing reception on 2 October. DC Arts Studios has served the DMV since 1979 and keeps its gallery commission at 20% rather than the usual half.
