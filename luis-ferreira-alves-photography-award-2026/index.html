Luis Ferreira Alves Photography Award 2026

Prizes €10,000 / €4,000 / €1,500 voucher Exhibition Publication

Luis Ferreira Alves photographed the buildings of Álvaro Siza and Eduardo Souto de Moura for decades, and the award made in his name asks for the same thing his pictures did: a considered relationship between photography, architecture and the experience of space. The rules are specific about form — a series of 9 to 11 images, read as a whole, held together by sequence, montage or cumulative effect, with a statement of no more than 2,000 characters. The subject can be one building, a group of works, or a wider architectural context; analogue or digital, colour or black and white, all accepted so long as the files arrive digitally. What the jury is looking for is a clear authorial position rather than documentation.

The money is real and so is the afterlife. First prize is EUR 10,000, second EUR 4,000 from the “la Caixa” Foundation / BPI, third a EUR 1,500 equipment voucher. New for the second edition is a Portuguese Emerging Photography Award, a further EUR 1,500 voucher reserved for Portuguese artists and awarded independently of the main ranking. Up to ten finalists are shown in the official exhibition and published in a bilingual catalogue, with up to two special mentions possible; the shortlist is required to include at least two Portuguese authors where the quality is there. Judging runs in three stages on the platform, configured for blind review, with a final in-person assessment of printed copies. Artists keep full copyright and grant only a non-exclusive licence limited to the award's own purposes.

Entry is free, which is unusual at this prize level. Submissions open on 25 September 2026 and close on 1 January 2027 at 23:59 mainland Portugal time; finalists are selected and announced in February–March 2027, the prize winners on 25 April 2027, and the exhibition and prize-giving ceremony follow in July. The award's own /edition/ page now carries the 2026 cycle in full — timeline, 9 to 11 images, the four prizes and the partners — and the rules live on the Picter prospectus's terms tab and in the award's news post; one loose end at the time of listing is that the Picter entry form's image window still read 5 to 7 against the rules' 9 to 11. The first international edition drew 1,495 submissions from 99 countries, so expect company.
