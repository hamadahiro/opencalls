Local Lens: Analog Photography Showcase

Open-house showcase in the Kimbell's Piano Pavilion lobby, presented with Courtesy of cofounders Katherine Stephens and Jesse Jackson IV as a Second Saturday event alongside the exhibition Photography's First Century: Masterworks from the Bibliotheque nationale de France. Six photographers working in analog and traditional processes - film, wet plate, cyanotype and other historic techniques - are selected to display finished work together with the negatives, plates, equipment and other artifacts of their process on a 72 x 30 in. table, and to talk with museum visitors about their methods. Each selected artist receives a $150 honorarium. Open to amateurs and professionals at all levels; artists need not be based in the DFW area but must attend the showcase in person on Saturday, December 12, 2026, noon-3 pm. Fully digital photographs and AI-generated images are not permitted. Free to apply: a form plus 3-5 images emailed to the education team. Not a commercial event - no sales are permitted, though artists may share contact information with visitors.
