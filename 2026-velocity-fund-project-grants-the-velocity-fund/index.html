2026 Velocity Fund Project Grants

Prize $5,000 (12–14 grants)

The Velocity Fund's 2026 cycle, open from 22 September to 22 November 2026, giving Philadelphia-based artists project grants of $5,000 each. It is one of 41 Regional Regranting Programs of the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, hosted by Asian Arts Initiative in Philadelphia's Chinatown North after earlier stints at Philadelphia Contemporary and at Temple Contemporary in the Tyler School of Art and Architecture; the Foundation set the programme up in 2007 to reach independently organised, public-facing, artist-centred activity that animates local art scenes but lies beyond the reach of traditional funding. Applying is free — "There is no application fee to apply." Individual artists living in Philadelphia may apply, as may artist-run spaces and collectives whose lead applicant lives in Philadelphia; incorporated 501(c)(3) nonprofits, for-profit businesses, artists living outside Philadelphia, senior Asian Arts Initiative staff and any lead applicant who received a Velocity grant the previous year may not. The project must involve visual arts components, take place between February 2027 and February 2028, and reach a public outcome in Philadelphia inside that window. The fund will not reimburse or resolve work already underway, and says plainly that applications only for travel, studio rent or equipment, with no public outcome, are not encouraged; it favours projects rooted in their communities, led by artists, mindful of their impact, and staged in those communities rather than in commercial galleries and museums. The fund defines what it supports as work using visual arts "including (but never limited to) printmaking, performance, video, puppetry, sculpture, and curation" — photography is not on that list and does not need to be, since the most recent cohort includes Uriah Bussey's Disabling the Queer Lens, a film and digital photography series of Black and Brown queer disabled people made with COVID-safer portraits, ending in a photobook, cyanotype prints and a public exhibition. Apply through Submittable with a biography of the lead applicant and collaborators, a description of your audience, a description of the visual arts involved, a project proposal in writing or on video, a timeline, the fund's budget form and five to ten work samples; a lead applicant may submit one proposal per cycle, and may reuse last year's application because each year has a new panel. That panel is independent of Velocity, Asian Arts Initiative and Warhol Foundation staff, includes one former grantee and one administrator from another Regional Regranting Program, scores on artistic vision, connection to publics, capacity to realise the project and personal impact, then meets over the 30 top-scoring applications to choose the final 12 to 14 grantees; the panellists are named after the awards are made. Grantees are notified in early February 2027 and receive 80 percent of the money up front, the rest after a midterm check-in. Applications are also offered in Vietnamese.
