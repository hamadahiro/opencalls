The Mini Masters Show

The Mini Masters Show is a juried group exhibition of small works at the Arts Center in Orange, Virginia, from 4 December 2026 to 30 January 2027. Every work in it measures 8 by 8 inches. Entry is free. Artists propose three original works, each 8 × 8 inches not counting the frame. The page's opening describes the show as one of 8-inch paintings, but its media section says any medium that can be framed and exhibited on a wall is welcome, which takes in photographic prints. Work must arrive framed and ready to hang, with mats and frames kept minimal because they add to the size. Only canvas may hang unframed, and only with clean, wrapped edges. There are no prizes. Every accepted work is offered for sale at a fixed $100, and sales are split evenly between the artist and the centre, so the artist receives $50 for each work sold. Delivery is the artist's job. Work is dropped off on Saturday 28 November 2026 and unsold work is collected on Saturday 30 January 2027. Unlike the centre's other calls, this page offers no shipping arrangement. Applicants email 5–10 images or time-based samples of recent work, an artist statement of 300–500 words, a CV and a short biography, with "Mini Masters" in the subject line. The three 8-inch pieces don't need to be finished at the time of applying. An optional reflection on scale, intimacy or time in the work can be added. The centre says it is looking for originality, craftsmanship and a compelling visual voice, in minimal or layered, narrative or abstract work.
