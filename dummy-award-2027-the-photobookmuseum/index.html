Dummy Award 2027

Prizes Publication Production grants

The international competition for the best unpublished photobook dummies of the year, now in its 17th edition. The Kassel Fotobook Festival began inviting photographers to the Kassel Dummy Award in 2010; since 2023 The PhotoBookMuseum in Cologne has run it, with MAS Matbaa in Istanbul — the Turkish photobook printing and binding house — as printing and publishing partner. The organisers describe the rules as easy: all photographers worldwide are invited, and from all entries around 50 books are selected for a shortlist that is exhibited at international photography events. Each exhibiting venue holds a vote, decided either by public vote or by curator's choice, and those votes are combined with the shortlist jury's to select five winning books. The book that wins the PBM/MAS Prize is produced and published by MAS Matbaa in cooperation with The PhotoBookMuseum with costs fully covered; the other four winning books receive a production grant towards printing at MAS Matbaa. Every submitted book enters the Dummy Award Library and becomes part of the permanent book collection of The PhotoBookMuseum in Cologne. The organisers note that participating photographers have been able to establish contacts with galleries, publishers and festivals and to present their books to an international audience, and that hundreds of shortlisted books have been exhibited at photography events and exhibition spaces worldwide. An unpublished book, per the FAQ, is one not published by a publisher, normally without an ISBN and without distribution — a book self-published in a print run of up to 100 copies, or published digitally, still counts as unpublished and is eligible even if it has an ISBN. The definition of a photobook is given as a nonperiodic collection of photographs printed or reproduced on photographic paper, with any amount of text or even empty pages, and some form of binding. Registration runs from 3 November 2026 to 31 January 2027 through the award's own site, with a €44 entry fee paid at registration, where entrants also supply the information about their dummy. The €44 includes 7% VAT and the registration page states it is "plus shipping": entering means posting a physical book to Cologne at the entrant's own cost, and the dummy has to arrive by 17 February 2027 — a second and harder date than the registration deadline. The surfaces disagree on that deadline: the call page banner and the FAQ both close registration on 31 January 2027, while the linked terms say it "closes on the 30th of January 2027", so treat the earlier date as the real one. There is no limit on the number of entries per person, at €44 per entry and book. Three further costs fall on the entrant after submission. Books are not returned by default — every registered dummy enters the Dummy Award Library for good, and a return has to be requested when registering and paid for by the entrant 6 to 8 weeks after the March 2027 jury session, at the organiser's own estimates of €15 within Germany, €22 in the EU, €36 elsewhere in Europe and €52 worldwide for a parcel up to 2 kg. A shortlisted entrant must send a second copy of the dummy to Cologne within 21 days of the shortlist announcement, again at their own cost, or the book is left out of the overlapping exhibitions. And the PBM/MAS Prize winner waives any claim to royalties from sales and to licence revenue from the first trade edition in return for production, project management and sales being covered, instead receiving 10% of the print run to use freely and the option to buy more copies at production cost plus shipping; MAS Matbaa and the museum also reserve the right, in close consultation with the photographer, to set the print run and to change size and features to optimise production. The other four winners' grant is 25% of production and only applies if they print at MAS Matbaa. The museum says it handles dummies with the utmost care but is not liable for damage, loss or theft during the transports and exhibitions: a registration-number sticker goes inside the cover or sleeve, and a small hole may be punched in the back or cover so a cord can be attached at venues that have to secure the books. Entrants transfer a right of selective publication in the museum's and MAS Matbaa's online media with no obligation to remunerate, and cannot influence how their work is reproduced in the catalogue, the shortlist exhibitions or online; copyright otherwise stays with the author. The shortlist jury — 5 to 7 international experts, brought to Cologne for a two-day session — is announced in mid-December 2026 and meets in early March 2027, with the shortlist announced shortly after and the world tour of around 18 venues and festivals in 15 countries starting in April 2027; the five winners are announced in February 2028 on the terms page and March 2028 in the FAQ. The PhotoBookMuseum gGmbH is a non-profit, part-funded by the city of Cologne, and reports around 385 paying registrations last year.
