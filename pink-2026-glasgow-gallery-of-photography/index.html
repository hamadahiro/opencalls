Pink 2026

Prizes Exhibition prize Publication prize

Pink 2026 is one of Glasgow Gallery of Photography's color-themed open calls — the gallery runs a roughly monthly schedule of themed group exhibitions, with single-color themes like Pink, Yellow, and Red appearing in rotation. The brief is the color pink across any genre, setting, or style: nature, urban environments, portraits, still life, abstraction.

The exhibition runs September 4 through 30, 2026 in the gallery's Glasgow space, sharing the program with Urban 2026. The submission model is free to apply, pay-only-if-selected (£48 participation fee). The £48 covers printing on Fujifilm photographic paper, gallery-supplied framing, and installation/de-installation. Photographers retain all copyright.

Selected work also appears in the gallery's online gallery and is included in the exhibition catalogue. All participants are put forward for the gallery's Photographer of the Month consideration, which functions as ongoing visibility beyond the show itself.

Eligibility is open globally — emerging and established, professional and amateur. Each photographer submits up to 8 JPEGs at minimum 300ppi and 3,000 pixels on the shortest side, named as Firstname-Surname_# and submitted via Smash, SwissTransfer, WeTransfer, or Dropbox. The 2026 deadline is May 22, 2026; acceptance notification May 27; payment due June 10.
