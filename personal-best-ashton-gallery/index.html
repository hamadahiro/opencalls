Personal Best

Prize Exhibition

A San Diego regional juried show at Ashton Gallery inside the Art on 30th art centre in North Park, on view October 3 - 30, 2026 and juried by painting instructor and professional artist Tracie Fearing. The brief is deliberately open rather than thematic: most artists have one piece they consider their best, and the gallery wants that work as a deeply personal measure of growth and achievement. Photography is explicitly welcomed alongside all 2D work and any 3D work that fits on a pedestal, and fabric and journal pages are accepted if prepared for hanging; the gallery does not take AI-created artwork, digital art, installations, reproductions or giclee prints, with an exception that keeps limited-edition photographic and printmaking editions eligible. Enter online or drop work off in person - the jurors often prefer to see submissions in person, and photographic entries should be printed by the deadline because accepted online entries must reach the gallery on Monday September 28 between 11am and 2pm. Work must be wired with two D-rings and ready to hang, signed and labelled on the reverse with title, size and medium, and offered for sale; anything marked NFS is juried out and the gallery takes the standard 50% commission on sales. Opening reception Saturday October 3, 4-7pm.
