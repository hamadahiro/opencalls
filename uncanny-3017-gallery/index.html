Uncanny

Prize Exhibition

Uncanny explores the eerie, the mysterious and the unexplainable, and welcomes artwork that embraces the strange, surreal and unsettling — haunting figures, dreamlike landscapes, cryptic symbolism, eerie atmospheres and imagined creatures. Whether psychological, supernatural, grotesque or quietly unsettling, the gallery is after work that evokes curiosity, tension and wonder and invites viewers into worlds that defy explanation. Photography and digital media are named in the accepted media alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics, fiber, woodworking, mixed media and printmaking. 3017 Gallery is a small contemporary gallery in the lower level of ArtisanABQ in Albuquerque, New Mexico, running a monthly themed open call with an opening on the second Saturday of every month. Entry is free; sales are processed through the gallery on a 30% commission. Between three and seven works are required for consideration, delivered ready to hang — two-dimensional work such as framed photographs properly wired, works on paper framed. Applicants send an artist bio of up to 150 words in the third person along with the title, medium, dimensions and price of each piece, and selected artists are notified shortly after submissions close. The exhibition opens on 10 October 2026.
