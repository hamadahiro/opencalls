XVI Concurso Internacional de Fotografía sobre Humanización Asistencial

Prize €600 prize

The Concurso Internacional de Fotografía sobre Humanización Asistencial is an international photography competition organized by Hospital San Juan de Dios de León, in Spain. The 2026 edition is the 16th cycle.

The brief is thematically narrow: photography reflecting humanization, solidarity, and care — in healthcare settings or in everyday life. Subjects can depict professional healthcare environments, family, personal, sports, or cultural settings where someone helps a person in need. The unifying thread is the act of care.

Cash prizes are €600, €400, and €200, plus a weekend hotel stay for the top winner. AI-generated work is not accepted.

Selection is by Secundino Pérez, Mauricio Peña, and Javier Casares — all photographers.

Eligibility is open globally to adults 18+. Submission is by email with up to 3 photographs per participant. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was April 13; results announced April 24, 2026.
