Put a Pin(hole) In It

Prize Group exhibition

Group show of pinhole photography at Blue Moon Camera & Machine in Portland, Oregon, open to long-time pinhole enthusiasts and recent converts alike. Each photographer may submit up to 5 images (series welcome; the jury may select single images from a series). 25 works are curated from all submissions. Selected images must be delivered matted — not framed — in 16×20 mats (horizontal or vertical), and can be any size within the mat. Submitted files should be at least 1000px and no more than 3000px on the long edge, and include the photographer's name, contact info, and which pinhole camera was used. The show opens August 1, 2026.
