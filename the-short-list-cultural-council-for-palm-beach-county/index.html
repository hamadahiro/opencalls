The Short List

Prize Exhibition

The Cultural Council for Palm Beach County fills its Lake Worth Beach gallery with small work once a year and sells it, and The Short List is built around the idea that a first purchase should be possible: an exhibition of tiny pieces pitched at collectors of every kind, from people buying their first artwork to people adding to a collection. Artists working in all styles and media, two- and three-dimensional, are welcome, and the Council's suggested directions — collage, abstract, whimsical, flora and fauna, food — are subjects rather than a medium list. Every work must be no greater than 12 inches framed in its longest dimension, with no minimum size, and the Council asks artists to price at or below $500 because that is what sells in this show, while explicitly not excluding higher prices. Up to three completed individual works may be submitted, one entry per artist, and sets such as diptychs and triptychs are not accepted — pieces may complement each other but must be priced and sold separately. Entry is free and applications are taken only through Artwork Archive, where a free account is enough to apply. Artists are accepted on a rolling basis up to a cap of 150 participants, so applying early matters, and everyone hears by 5 November. CAUTION: the Council prints two closing times for the same day — the headline deadline line says 29 October 2026 at 11:59 PM EDT while the Application Requirements paragraph says "by October 29, 2026, at 5:00 pm" — so treat 5:00 pm as the cutoff. Selected artists deliver and collect their own work on dates coordinated with the Council, sign a memorandum of understanding and a loan agreement, and the Council retains 25% of sales to fund its artist services staff and the building. The exhibition itself opens on 12 March 2027, five months after the deadline. Work is insured on the premises but not in transit.
