Blank Wall Gallery — Abandoned Buildings

International open call on abandoned buildings for Blank Wall Gallery in Athens. "Life is always evolving," the gallery writes: people migrate, drawn to modern spaces that better accommodate their needs and desires, and the buildings of the past are left behind — "silent sentinels of history, empty and forgotten", once vibrant with life, now narrating their stories only to those with the patience or curiosity to listen. The gallery wants such buildings from different parts of the world, each telling its own story, and reads them as glimpses into a bygone era that invite reflection on the lives, choices and moments that shaped them. All means accepted. Free to submit, up to five files per photographer; selected photographers pay a participation fee only if exhibited (€35 if the photograph is printed, €15 if it is shown on the gallery's 43-inch 4K digital screen). Over 100 printed photographs hang in each exhibition, with printing on 260 gsm paper, framing, hanging, press releases, invitations and the opening night all covered by the gallery, and no commission taken on sales. Selected work is shown at Blank Wall Gallery in Athens, 15–27 January 2027.
