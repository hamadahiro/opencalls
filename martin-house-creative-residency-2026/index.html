Martin House Creative Residency 2026

2–4 week project-based residency at Frank Lloyd Wright's Darwin D. Martin House in Buffalo, New York. Residents live in the Gardener's Cottage on campus and create new work inspired by the architecture, design, landscape, and social history of Martin House. Open to artists working in any discipline including photography, writing, and research. Residents receive $5,000 stipend paid in two installments and up to $1,000 travel allowance for non-local applicants. Residency term runs June–October 2026. Must deliver a free public program, performance, or exhibition sharing work with the community. Requires US social security number. Application window March 30 – May 13, 2026.
