IMPRINT

Prizes $50 honorarium Cash prize

Manifest Gallery establishes IMPRINT as an annual international exhibition of printmaking, launched alongside the new printmaking studio that opened at the Cincinnati non-profit's permanent home in May 2026 and that operates beside its drawing, painting and darkroom photography programmes. The gallery frames the two disciplines together, writing that “like photography, printmaking is a genre of creative work that is underscored by its processes” and calling printmaking an important checkpoint on the road map between the earliest cave markings and the ubiquitous photography in everyone's pocket today. The call is open to artists worldwide, has no size limit beyond what fits through the gallery door and no restriction on when the work was made, and states no predefined expectation for type or style, with submissions ranging from traditional to conceptual or experimental; the media clause reads “open to all traditional and nontraditional genres and media within the expansive bounds of printmaking”. The one hard limit is that printmaking must be the work's primary medium, which makes this a show for photogravure, photopolymer, photo-screenprint and other photographic print processes rather than for straight photographs. Selection runs in two stages: an anonymous multi-member panel of professional and academic advisors juries the entries, then passes its recommendations to the exhibition curator, who makes the final selections from the jury-approved pool. Every exhibiting artist receives a $50 cash honorarium, and one work from this exhibition period wins a $1,000 Grand Jury Award decided by a second-round jury drawn from the top-scoring works; Manifest runs that award in each of the nine exhibition periods of its 23rd season. Selected work is shown December 11, 2026 to January 8, 2027 and documented in the season annual. Entry is $45 for up to three works and $5 for each additional work, with no cap on the number of entries, and artists receive 70% of any sale.
