Future Figurations Residency

A five-week faculty-led thematic visual-arts residency (January 25 – February 26, 2027) exploring speculative futures, science fiction, myth-making and world-building, using futurity as an anti-oppressive, restorative and decolonizing force. Artists receive individual studio space, faculty mentorship and studio visits, weekly talks and workshops, open studios, and access to specialized production facilities and the Paul D. Fleck Library and Archives. Eligible mediums explicitly include photography and digital media. All participants receive the Banff Centre Arts Scholarship (100% tuition plus 25% of meals and accommodation).
