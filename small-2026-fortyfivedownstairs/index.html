SMALL! 2026

Prizes Exhibition Photography Prize

fortyfivedownstairs — a not-for-profit theatre and gallery established in 2002 by Mary Lou Jelbart and Julian Burnside, on the lower ground floor at 45 Flinders Lane in Melbourne's CBD — is taking entries for the sixth edition of SMALL!, its community-sourced exhibition of small works. The premise is flat and deliberate: every artwork that meets the requirements is hung, nothing is juried out, and all of it is shown anonymously, so visitors choose on the work alone and the artist's name is revealed to the buyer only once a piece sells. Photography is one of the accepted media alongside painting, drawing, prints, textiles and small sculptural work, and there is a dedicated Photography Prize among the nine awards given on closing night — the others being Under 18, Painting, Drawing, Printmaking, Sculpture, Textile, Mixed Media and a People's Choice Award. Prizes are decided by a selection panel of artists, curators and arts workers; the gallery says the values will be confirmed closer to the exhibition and will likely be vouchers from Melbourne art-supply stores in the A$50–A$150 range, so entrants should treat the cash value as unsettled. Everything is priced identically at A$160 regardless of medium or the artist's standing, of which the artist keeps A$125 and the gallery takes A$35 — a commission far below the usual gallery cut. Open to all artists living in Australia with no age restrictions; under-18s can opt into the Under 18 Prize, and the gallery states that discounted entry fees are available to First Nations artists on request. Each artist may enter a maximum of two artworks, one per entry form, at A$25 each, and SMALL! caps the show at 1,000 entries — once the limit is hit the form closes, so the practical deadline can arrive early. Work must not exceed 20 × 20 × 20 cm including any frame or display apparatus and must be ready to hang; unframed works on paper are pinned. Everything entered must be available for sale, and work may be delivered in person or posted; unsold work has to be collected at the gallery across two days in December unless return postage is bought, which costs A$25 added to the entry or A$40 arranged afterwards and is available within Australia only. AI is excluded outright: "Artworks must NOT be created with the use of AI, in any part or form." Entries close 5pm on Monday 9 November 2026, accepted work must reach the gallery by 5pm on Friday 20 November, and the exhibition runs 3–17 December 2026 with a ticketed preview on 2 December, an opening on 3 December and the prize announcement at the closing event on 17 December.
