40th Annual Sears Invitational Art Show & Sale

Prizes Exhibition Catalogue

The 40th Annual Sears Invitational Art Show & Sale at the Sears Art Museum, Utah Tech University in St. George — the museum's premier event, founded by Robert N. Sears (1915–1995), a retired vice-president of Phillips Petroleum who worked to establish a quality art invitational at the university, and now one of Utah's longest-running and most popular juried museum exhibitions. Around 100 artists and roughly 200 works are selected by jury for a six-week run, 13 February to 27 March 2027, opening with a gala on 12 February. Entry is free: "There is no fee to enter!" The museum answers the media question outright in its own FAQ — "We welcome visual art of all subjects and mediums—including watercolor, oil, acrylic, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, photography, mixed media, 2D digital art, and more." Any artist aged 18 or older based in the western United States may submit, with priority given to artists from Utah, Idaho, Colorado and Nevada. You may submit up to three individual pieces, each as a separate ArtCall entry and each editable until the call closes, and a maximum of two will be accepted; recent work is asked for and everything submitted must be available for sale. Each entry needs a high-quality image, the title, dimensions, sale price and medium, your location and a brief artist bio. Selected artists are included in a professionally designed full-colour exhibition catalogue available for purchase by museum patrons, and gain the show's audience of collectors, curators, visitors and fellow artists. Sales split 60 percent to the artist and 40 percent to the museum, which funds exhibitions, educational programmes and part-time student positions, and the museum's share is tax-deductible for the buyer. Local artists drop off and collect in St. George, and the museum runs a complimentary van service to and from Ogden, Salt Lake City and Orem; artists outside Utah, or unable to use those points, are responsible for round-trip shipping. Acceptances are posted at searsart.com and emailed on 16 November 2026. One caution: one FAQ answer dates the run "February 13 through March 27, 2026" while the call's own timeline block and the ArtCall prospectus both say 2027 — the exhibition is in 2027.
