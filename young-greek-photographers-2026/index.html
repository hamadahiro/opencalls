Young Greek Photographers 2026

Annual exhibition organized since 1987 by the Hellenic Centre for Photography as part of Athens Photo Festival. Open to artists up to 35 years old residing permanently in Greece or Greek artists residing abroad. Selected works exhibited at the Benaki Museum / Pireos 138, Athens (June 10 – July 26, 2026), then traveling to Thessaloniki. Production costs covered, festival catalogue inclusion, portfolio reviews with international professionals, and nominations for international festivals. Two finalists join the Photoxenia artist residency program in Crete. Up to two projects per applicant, separate forms required.
