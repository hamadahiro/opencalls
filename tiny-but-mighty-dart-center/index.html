Tiny But Mighty: d'Art's Annual National Exhibit of Small Artworks

Prizes $300 Exhibition

Annual national juried exhibition of small artworks at d'Art Center, the nonprofit art center in Norfolk, Virginia's NEON District (est. 1986). All mediums and all styles are welcome, 2D or 3D, as long as work measures no more than 8 inches in its longest dimension (12 inches for framed hanging pieces). Open to US artists 18+; 3 to 10 works. Exhibition in The Vault gallery August 22 – September 19, 2026.
