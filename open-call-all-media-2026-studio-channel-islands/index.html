Open Call: All Media 2026

Prizes $1,000 $750 $500

International juried exhibition in Studio Channel Islands' 3,000-square-foot gallery in Camarillo, California, August 1 – September 12, 2026. All media and styles welcome — including photography — for 2D/3D, video, film, performance, sound and installation work completed in the last three years; the prospectus asks for 'breakthrough pieces' that mark a turning point in your practice. Juried by Patricia Sweetow of Patricia Sweetow Gallery (Los Angeles). Cash prizes of $1,000/$750/$500 plus three juror's awards; 60/40 split on sales.
