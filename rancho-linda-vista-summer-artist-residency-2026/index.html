Rancho Linda Vista — Summer Artist Residency 2026

Prize $1,000 stipend

Rancho Linda Vista is a historic arts community in Oracle, Arizona, on 100+ acres of high-desert wilderness north of Tucson. The Summer Artist Residency program offers one-month residencies for visual artists during July or August.

Residents receive a furnished one-bedroom house, separate studio space, and a $1,000 stipend. Access to the community's swimming pool, library, and wilderness trails. The residency concludes with an open studio exhibit.

Practical requirements: residents must provide their own transportation and materials. The location is rural and remote — applicants should plan for that context.

Eligibility is open globally; MFA is preferred but not required. Application is by email to the residency coordinator. Application is free.

The 2026 deadline was April 17; results announced May 2026.
