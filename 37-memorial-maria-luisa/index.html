37 Memorial María Luisa

Prizes €5,000 €300 per category

The Memorial María Luisa is not a brand exercise. It is run from Infiesto, in the Asturian interior, by a group of friends of María Luisa Álvarez, who died in a mountain accident on 18 November 1990, and the FAQ answers the question of who the organisers are in one line: some of María Luisa's friends, who are very keen on mountains, and who finance the contest. Thirty-seven editions later it still opens in September and closes on 1 December, and photographers and filmmakers from more than a hundred countries have entered it.

The structure is unusually generous for a contest this size. The first photograph is free. Everything after that is covered by a single €22 payment, VAT included, that unlocks all fourteen photographic categories and the video section together - and participation is free outright for entrants under 19 and, for the first time this year, for photographers with disabilities, who also have a section and an award of their own. The categories run from Mountain Landscape, Alpinism and Climbing through Adventure and Extreme Sports, Man in the Mountain and/or Nature, Animal World, The World of Birds, Vegetal World, Submerged World, Biodiversity and Geodiversity, Nature Landscapes, Creative Photo and Macro, to a Topic of the Year that is The Universe for this edition. You may enter five photographs in each. Multiple exposure counts if it was registered in camera in a single primary file; HDR and panoramas are fine.

Judging is genuinely blind - the system assigns each photograph an alphanumeric code the moment it arrives, and the panel, drawn by the Founders Board from mountain and nature photographers of recognised prestige, never sees who made it. The overall winner receives €5,000, a trophy, a diploma, a book portfolio and two invitations to the gala; each of the fourteen category winners receives €300 and the same trophy, diploma, portfolio and invitations. There is a Samsung 5G tablet for the Young Photographers Prize and another for the Award for the person with a disability, a Citizen watch for the Topic of the Year, and a €3,000 FotoRuano voucher for the best video, film or documentary. Results are announced on 1 March 2027 and the prize gala runs 23-24 April 2027 in Infiesto and Oviedo, with accommodation provided free for the winner and a companion. Selected work then joins the Memorial's touring exhibition, which the organisers lend - framed, labelled and crated - to non-profit mountain and environmental groups.
