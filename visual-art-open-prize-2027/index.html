Visual Art Open Prize 2027

Prizes £10,000 fund — £2,000 Solo exhibition in London

The 12th edition of the Visual Art Open, a UK-based international art award with a £10,000 prize fund and a dedicated Photography & Digital Art category alongside Painting, Sculpture, Drawing & Illustration, Mixed Media/Collage/Printmaking, Student Artist and Art Educator. The overall winner takes £2,000 in cash, a solo exhibition at a London venue, a place in the London finalists' show at the Minster Building, a dedicated article on their practice, and a year of Visual Artists Association premium membership with its mentorship and Momentum business-development programmes. Each category winner receives £250, the same VAA membership and course, a place in the finalists' exhibition, an article and extra online promotion. All finalists are invited to exhibit in central London and receive a certificate; every entrant is put forward for promotion across the VAA's social channels and its 60,000-subscriber mailing list. Entry is open worldwide to artists at every career stage, from first-time entrants to established practitioners, and the organisers make a point of discounted rates for students and art educators, which stay fixed across the whole entry period. Work is submitted as JPEG or PNG images up to 5MB, up to five per entry, and the number of works has to be chosen before checkout because the price changes with it. Judging is by a panel drawn from commercial galleries, museums, art media and academia — recent panels have included the creative director of Mall Galleries, the senior fair manager of The Other Art Fair, and the critic Tabish Khan — though the organisers have not yet published the panel under the 2027 label. The prize is funded by entry fees, donations, art sales, advertising and sponsorship rather than by Arts Council money, and entries cannot be refunded once submitted. Early-bird pricing runs to 17 December 2026; standard pricing applies from 18 December 2026 until the entry period closes on 25 March 2027. The terms page adds bars and costs the entry pages never mention: entry is open from age 14, with written authorisation from a legal guardian for anyone under 18; work must have been made within the last three years and must not already have been sold; and a 40% commission is taken on anything that sells through the VAO exhibition. AI work is barred outright — the rules require that every submission be “created manually and entirely by the artist”, and state that “AI-generated or AI-assisted artwork is not eligible for entry”. One number is worth doing yourself: of the advertised £10,000 fund the published cash is £3,750 — £2,000 to the overall winner and £250 to each of the seven category winners — with the balance made up of the VAA memberships, courses, London exhibition places and certificates listed above.
