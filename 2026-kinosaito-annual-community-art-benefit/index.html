2026 KinoSaito Annual Community Art Benefit

Prize Exhibition

KinoSaito is a real arts centre, not a rented room: the renovated former St. Patrick's School in Verplanck — the parish school that opened in 1891 and ran for exactly a century before closing in 1991, and later Kikuo Saito's own studio — now holding two galleries, a theatre and performance space, two resident-artist studios, a classroom, a library and a coffee bar. It runs exhibitions, performances and a residency programme, already appears on this directory for its Artist-in-Residence call, and is a grantee of the New York State Council on the Arts and of ArtsWestchester.

The reason to read this one is the price. Entry is free, the $10 is explicitly a suggestion, and the only money that changes hands is on a sale — half of it. For a photographer who has never shown in a gallery, a salon-style hang with an opening reception costs nothing to try.

The gate is geography, and it is strict in a way a facet cannot capture. The organiser does not ask where you live; it asks whether you can drive your own print to 115 7th Street and come back for it. "Therefore, only artists within commuting distance to KINOSAITO … are eligible to submit." There is no shipping route offered.

What comes back is the wall, the reception on 12 December and the chance of a sale under $3,000 at a 50/50 split. No juror is named, there is no cash award, and the show is openly described as a fundraiser for the artists and for KinoSaito together. One practical trap: the page says up to two works, but the Google Form makes both image uploads required, so a single print will not submit.
