Open Call for Exhibition Proposals 2026 (3rd Edition)

Prize Exhibition

Elliott Gallery is a physical fine art photography gallery at Tussen de Bogen 91 in Amsterdam, and complies with the Gallery Fair Practice Code. It publishes monographs as well as exhibiting — Matthew Murray's Saddleworth: Responding to a Landscape, which took First Prize at the 2018 International Photography Awards, came out under the gallery's imprint.

This is the third edition of the gallery's exhibition-proposal call, which it describes as having grown over two years into one of its most rewarding initiatives. It sits alongside a separate open call for emerging artists, whose prize is mentorship rather than wall space.

The ask is a single coherent series of up to fifteen photographs with a statement of up to 200 words, submitted as a link — a portfolio site, a Google Drive or Dropbox folder, or a WeTransfer or SwissTransfer link. Fine art photography only; the gallery states other mediums will not be considered. There is no theme, and the terms open it to amateurs, professionals and recent graduates worldwide who are over 18.

The three winners share a six-week slot planned for March or April 2027. Two show together in the main exhibition room with roughly 20 m² of wall each; the third gets a solo showcase in the Green Room, the gallery's separate back room, with about 10 m². All three also get a curated digital exhibition, curatorial guidance on the hang, and a feature in the gallery's newsletters and social channels. The gallery hangs the show and pays for labels, the catalogue, promotion and an opening reception; the artists pay for producing, framing and shipping their own work, and must collect or transfer it within 14 days of the closing. Sales during the run are split 70/30 in the artist's favour, and winners or finalists may be offered a 12-month representation contract.

Matthew Murray and Leticia Valverdes join three of the gallery's own on the jury: founder and curatorial director Addie Elliott, who began as a curator at the V&A and The Photographers' Gallery before founding Elliott Gallery in 2003, associate consultant Eirini Alexandrou and curatorial projects manager Eszti Bakos. Valverdes was one of the gallery's own 2025 open-call winners; Murray's work is held by MoMA, the Tate and the V&A. Judging is on creativity, originality and presentation, and on how a proposal would sit in the gallery's programme; a shortlist is interviewed online and the decision follows the interviews, with no date published for it. The application fee is €35 per proposal (the gallery quotes roughly $40 or £30), is non-refundable, covers administration costs, and must be paid again for each further submission. Applications close 30 November 2026.
