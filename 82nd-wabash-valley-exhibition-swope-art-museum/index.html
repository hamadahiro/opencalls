82nd Wabash Valley Exhibition

Eighty-second edition of the Swope Art Museum's annual juried exhibition in downtown Terre Haute, running since 1944. Open to current or former residents of Indiana, Illinois, Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, and Tennessee with original work in any medium completed 2024–2026 and not previously shown at the Swope. Juried by Mark Pascale, curator in the Department of Prints and Drawings at the Art Institute of Chicago. More than $10,000 in awards, from $100 Awards of Recognition to the Grand Prize, announced at the October 2 opening reception. On view October 2, 2026 – January 10, 2027.
