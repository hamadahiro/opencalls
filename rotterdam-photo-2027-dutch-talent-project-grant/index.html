Rotterdam Photo 2027 — Dutch Talent Project Grant

A production grant for emerging photographers based in or trained in the Netherlands, run by Rotterdam Photo with Amarte Fonds as part of the festival's 2027 open call. Five selected artists each receive €3,000 to develop new work responding to the festival theme 'VIBRANCE – The Pulse of a Living World', together with curatorial guidance and artistic coaching throughout the development process. The resulting work is shown as a work-in-progress exhibition at Rotterdam Photo 2027, held 17–21 March 2027 during Rotterdam Art Week, with a dedicated exhibition space and inclusion in the group exhibition and the official festival programme. Grantees also take part in the Rotterdam Photo Talks and networking events, are documented in video profiles of the artists and their process shared on YouTube and social media, are published in and included in the official festival programme, and receive long-term promotion by Rotterdam Photo across its website, social channels and future festival communications beyond the 2027 edition. Eligibility is open to photographers based in the Netherlands, currently studying there, or who completed studies there, specifically third- and fourth-year art academy students, alumni of photography or related visual arts programmes since 2022, and self-taught photographers with up to five years of practical experience. Projects begun during study qualify if they are extended or deepened beyond the academic context. Entry is free.
