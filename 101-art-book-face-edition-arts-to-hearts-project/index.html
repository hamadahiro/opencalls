101 Art Book: Face Edition

Prizes $500 4-page magazine feature

Fourteenth edition of the 101 Art Book series, a curated hardcover publication that gathers 101 contemporary artists around a single theme. Face invites work that reads the human face in every register — portraiture and self-portraiture, faces glimpsed in clouds, architecture or machines, faces as icons, and the face as data in an age of facial recognition, surveillance and deepfakes. Realistic, abstract, conceptual, symbolic, surreal and experimental approaches are all eligible, and photography is explicitly named among the accepted disciplines. One artist receives the Face Edition Award: a USD 500 cash prize, a four-page editorial feature in Arts to Hearts Magazine, a custom glass trophy and a Certificate of Excellence; two further artists receive Jury Recognition Awards with the same feature, trophy and certificate. Previously exhibited, published or sold work is accepted so long as the artist holds the rights, and artists retain full copyright throughout. Open internationally to emerging, mid-career and established artists 18 and over; results are announced within three weeks of the deadline.
