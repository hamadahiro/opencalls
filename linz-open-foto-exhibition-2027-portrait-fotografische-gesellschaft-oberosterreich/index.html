Linz Open Foto Exhibition 2027 – Portrait

A portrait-only open call from the Fotografische Gesellschaft Oberösterreich: 150 finalist images hang for 30 days at the OÖ-Fotogalerie in Linz's Kulturquartier, go online and appear in an accompanying booklet. Files must be 2:3 and print-ready at 60x40 cm. Open worldwide from age 16; no AI-generated images.

DeadlineJanuary 7, 2027
PrizesExhibition, Booklet
Entry fee€12 for 1 image, €19 for 2, €25 for 3
Eligibility16+
LocationLinz, Austria
Requirements1–3 images
