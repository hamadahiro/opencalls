FeelsZine Issue 28 — Play

Prizes Publication $60 honorarium

FEELS is a Canadian independent zine that has published two issues a year, in January and July, since Issue 01 in January 2017. Issue 28 is themed "Play" and is open for submissions now; the editors note that the mission statement for the theme is still coming, so the brief is wide open.

The publication is multidisciplinary. Visual art — photography, illustration, art, collage, comics, typography — sits alongside writing: poetry, personal essays, fiction and interview proposals. Proposals for forms not on that list are invited by email.

Each accepted submission receives a $60 honorarium and a free copy of the issue. Entry is free. Art submissions need a short artist statement; FEELS prints at 6.5 × 9.5 inches with a 0.125-inch bleed, accepts low-res proofs at submission and requests 300dpi files if the work is taken. Longform writing is capped at 1,800 words; poetry is limited to two pieces, or four within a single collection.

Submissions go by email with a short bio, a description of the work, the country you are submitting from, and a statement of your personal connection to the topic. Previously published work is welcome unless it will appear in another Canadian independent magazine during the same six-month window. The zine states it is anti-AI and accepts no work made with generative AI. Deadline 15 March 2027; the issue is released in July 2027.
