Artist Residency Program

The Art Center in Dover, New Hampshire gives three artists a four-month studio residency in its space at the Washington Street Mills, and thanks to a local benefactor it costs the selected artists nothing — the gallery values the opportunity at $2,500. This round covers 1 October through 31 January. Residents get their own studio with a shared utility sink and are asked to be there twenty hours a week, drawing on downtown Dover, the Cocheco river and the Seacoast arts scene, and to build a body of work that goes up in the Artist Residency Exhibition in the gallery at the end of the term. Residents also help coordinate two artists' receptions and hang one exhibit during the term. This is studio space only; housing is not included, so it suits artists already within reach of the Seacoast. Applicants must be 18 or older and pay a $25 application fee, plus a $100 damage deposit returned at the end of the residency. The gallery takes 30 percent commission on work sold from the exhibition, and artists keep full rights to their images. Applications go through a form on the gallery's own site, with five examples of work, a CV and two character references sent by email.
