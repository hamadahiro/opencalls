The Food We Share

Prize Exhibition

Schack Art Center in Everett, Washington runs an annual Arts Education in Action exhibition, and for 2027 it wants work about food in its broadest sense: culture, family, connection, recipes, ritual, aesthetics, agriculture, nutrition, nostalgia, evolution, insecurity, comfort, entertainment and art. The prompt is unusually well built out, with strands the gallery lists as cooking and eating, science, connection and symbolism, harvest, industry, survival and power — the last of which points at food insecurity, food deserts, food waste, gender roles and class divisions. Photography is named explicitly among the media sought, next to painting, drawing, sculpture, furniture, printmaking, fiber, glass and video, and installation proposals are encouraged. Out of respect for those facing food insecurity the gallery discourages work made of food, and every accepted artist is asked to supply a favourite recipe for a booklet visitors take home. The call is open to artists living and working in the Pacific Northwest; entry is $5, with up to six pieces per artist, and work must not have shown at Schack before. Accepted pieces hang in the Main and Mezzanine Galleries from 25 March to 22 May 2027. All work must be for sale for the run of the show and Schack takes 40 percent. Nothing made in whole or part by AI is accepted.
