Vivid

Prizes $800 Exhibition

A colour-driven juried exhibition at the Kavanagh Gallery of Fine Line Creative Arts Center in St. Charles, Illinois, in the Fox River Valley about an hour west of Chicago. Work in this show needs unmistakable energy and a lively, dynamic composition that elicits a strong reaction — bold, vibrant, saturated hues. All media are accepted, with a maximum width of 48 inches for wall work. Jurors select on creativity, quality of artistry and originality as represented in the submitted images, and $800 in prizes will be awarded. The show runs 17 December 2026 to 12 February 2027, with an in-person opening reception on 17 December from 6 to 8pm; the gallery is closed to the public from 20 December to 3 January. Entry is open to any artist 18 or over residing in the United States, with up to 3 pieces per application and one image per piece; work previously shown at Fine Line is not eligible. Artists are notified by 14 November. All work must be for sale and the gallery takes a 30% commission; artists are responsible for shipping to and from the centre, and hand-delivered work is checked in on 12 December.
