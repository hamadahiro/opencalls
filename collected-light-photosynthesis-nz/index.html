Collected Light

Prize Group exhibition

Open call for "all art made with light" from Photosynthesis, a fully volunteer-run non-profit in Ōtautahi Christchurch that operates the only gallery in the city dedicated to photography, alongside a community darkroom, studio and digital workspaces for hire. Collected Light is the second edition of a regular mega group show: the first, Open 2026, ran in February and March 2026 with more than 80 artists and over 180 pieces, and the organisers describe the format as "a cross-section of the state-of-our-arts". Artists of all ages and experience levels may enter one to three still works made with light — photographs, prints, photo books and zines are all named. Each entry needs a title, a short description, an image and a display plan. There is no size limit, but you must give the finished outside height and width in millimetres including any frame or mount (and the depth if it matters), measured as planned if the work is not yet printed; later changes need the gallery's approval so it can plan the layout. A panel reviews every entry after submissions close and selects the show; the organisers say they cannot include every entry but hope to include at least one submission per artist. Work may be offered for sale with a koha commission of 20% to Photosynthesis on any sale. Print workshops run through November for anyone who needs help producing their piece. Installation begins 28 November and the show opens on 4 December 2026 at the Photosynthesis gallery on Battersea Street in Sydenham; the call page says the exhibition end date is still to be confirmed, but the gallery’s own exhibitions calendar already dates the run 4–20 December 2026. Selection, delivery and collection dates and the opening time are still to be announced.
