Emerging Stockholm

Local open call from Fotografiska’s Emerging programme, running since 2023 and dedicated to supporting and showcasing early-career image-makers from across Sweden. Roughly three artists a year are invited to present their work within Fotografiska Stockholm’s exhibition programme, alongside opportunities for professional dialogue and development. Material is reviewed on an ongoing basis throughout the year, and only artists whose work moves forward in the internal review are contacted. Applicants must be 18 or over and based in Sweden. An emerging artist here means a photographer or image-maker whose work has not yet been presented in an institutional museum exhibition but who has already developed a recognisable visual language — not tied to age, years of experience or formal education, and including self-taught artists. The platform's Open Calls index gives the call a closing date of 11 March 2027. The cutoff is not end of day: Fotografiska's own Open Calls index prints this call as "Closes Mar 11, 2027, 9:00 AM", a clock time the platform renders in the reader's own timezone — 09:00 UTC, so about 10:00 in Stockholm — which leaves only the morning of the last day.
