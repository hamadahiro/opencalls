New/Now: Pondering Change

Prize $500 / $250

The South Haven Center for the Arts sits on the Lake Michigan shore in south-west Michigan, and its annual national juried exhibition is the show it opens to artists from across the country. New/Now: Pondering Change takes as its premise that change is the one fact nobody escapes — losing a first tooth, the birth of a grandchild, a natural disaster, the graying of one's hair — and that the only variable is our reaction to it.

The call asks for a piece made in response to a deviation in your life, your work or your world: a change you have lived through, one you anticipate, or one you set in motion yourself. Applicants write a short note on how that change informed the work, which is part of what the juror reads.

Juror David Baker is a poetic landscape painter working largely en plein air near Lake Michigan and around the Ox-Bow campus, a professor emeritus at Southwestern Michigan College who still teaches at Ox-Bow and at the Center itself. Awards are $500 for first place and $250 for second.

Entries are open to artists residing in the United States and the Tribal Nations that share these geographies, in any visual medium, with a maximum of two works. Two-dimensional pieces top out at 72 by 55 inches; three-dimensional work must pass through a standard doorway and weigh under 50 pounds. Work must be original and made within the last five years. Entry is $25, or $10 for students and artists economically challenged by unforeseen events, and the Center takes a 35 per cent commission on anything sold during the run. Submissions close 31 August at 11.59pm ET, notifications go out by 5 September, and the exhibition runs 26 September to 7 November 2026.
