Open Call for Exhibition Proposals 2027–2029

Second Street Gallery — founded in 1973 and the oldest nonprofit contemporary art space in Central Virginia, mounting 10 to 14 exhibitions a year — is taking proposals for its Main Gallery and smaller Dové Gallery for the seasons running October 2027 to January 2029. This is a rolling call. Proposals are read periodically by the gallery's Executive Director and Chief Curator, Kristen Chiacchia, as the calendar fills, and the call closes once every slot is taken; the gallery says applying early matters because fewer dates remain as the calendar is built. Individual artists, collaborating artists and curators can all apply, for solo, two-person or group exhibitions. There is no geographic restriction and artists at every career stage are invited, though full-time students can only be included in group shows. Applicants choose which gallery they want to be considered for and which of five exhibition windows they are free for. Artwork should generally have been made within five years of the exhibition date unless the proposal is a retrospective or has a clear curatorial reason for earlier work. An application takes 7 to 15 images, an image list, a proposal, an artist or curatorial statement, CV, an estimate of scope, installation requirements, and a sketch of one or more public programmes — an artist talk, workshop, screening, tour or whatever suits the practice — which the gallery develops with the artist and pays for separately, with programme honoraria starting at $100. Selected exhibitions receive a $1,000 honorarium towards production, packing, shipping, travel and lodging; for two-person and group shows that sum is per exhibition, split as agreed. Artists arrange and pay for transport of the work. At least 75% of the work shown must be for sale, with exemptions possible for installation, video, performance, site-specific and time-based work, and the gallery takes 50% commission on sales. If selected, artists must attend the opening in Charlottesville and take part in at least one public programme. Work created or substantially generated with generative AI is not accepted. Entry is $35, or $20 for current gallery members.
