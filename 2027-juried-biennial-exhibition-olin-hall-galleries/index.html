2027 Juried Biennial Exhibition

Prizes $1,000 / $800 / $600 $400 Community Choice Exhibition

Olin Hall Galleries at Roanoke College in Salem, Virginia has run its Juried Biennial every two years since 1990, and the 2027 edition is free to enter. The gallery frames the show as a survey of work by artists within a 200-mile radius of the college, and its media line is unusually explicit: "All fine arts media are accepted for submission, including, but not limited to, painting, drawing, sculpture, graphic design, photography, ceramics, printmaking, mixed media, textiles, video, and film." The juror is Betsy Johnson, Assistant Curator at the Hirshhorn Museum and Sculpture Garden, where she oversees the photography and works-on-paper collections and has curated Feel the Sun in Your Mouth: Recent Acquisitions (2019), Toyin Ojih Odutola: A Countervailing Theory (2021), One with Eternity: Yayoi Kusama in the Hirshhorn Collection (2022), A Window Suddenly Opens: Contemporary Photography in China (2022), Basquiat x Banksy (2024) and the co-curated Revolutions: Art from the Hirshhorn Collection, 1860-1960. The awards are $1,000 first place, $800 second, $600 third and a $400 Community Choice, announced at 7 p.m. during the opening reception on 22 January 2027. Artists may submit up to three works in any medium; everything must be original and completed within the last three years. Olin Hall Galleries is a non-profit and takes no commission on sales from the exhibition, forwarding interested buyers' details straight to the artist. Two-dimensional work needs proper hanging hardware and sculpture must be portable and under 150 lbs. Accepted work is hand-delivered to the gallery 6 to 9 January 2027, the show runs 22 January to 14 March 2027, and pieces are collected 15 to 17 March.
