Fotosalongen 2027

Sweden's largest photography salon returns for its second edition at the Hasselblad Center in Gothenburg, and the open call is deliberately wide: amateur and professional photographers working across every genre are invited to submit, with no set theme. The foundation welcomes documentary photography and photojournalism alongside nature, family, street, science, politics and art photography, and says the salon is built to create unexpected encounters between established artists, emerging talents and photographers whose work has never been shown publicly. The jury looks for compelling images and photographic narratives with depth, originality and a diversity of expression, so that the selected works together offer a broad snapshot of photography today. Submissions are still photography only — no video — as up to five single images, or an installation without electronic equipment; one project per person or group, and groups may apply jointly. Entry is free. Selected photographers exhibit at the Hasselblad Center, receive compensation of SEK 3,200, appear in the exhibition catalogue and may sell work; participants cover transport of works to and from the Center. The jury is chaired by the foundation's chief curator Dragana Vujanović Östlind and includes Alexandra Ellis of the Sune Jonsson Centre for Documentary Photography at Västerbottens museum, film director Tarik Saleh and photographer Elisabeth Toll. Applications run from 1 August to 18 October 2026 via Picter; the exhibition itself runs 23 April to 26 September 2027, in a venue that draws around 70,000 visitors. Open to anyone who turns 18 during the application year or is older, and who is either a Swedish citizen or has a residential address in Sweden.
