The Petrides Residency for a Greek Artist in NY

Prizes $9,000 stipend Residency

A fully funded three-month residency in New York for one artist based in Greece, newly established by the Athens- and New York-based sculptor George Petrides with the International Studio & Curatorial Program (ISCP) in Brooklyn and administered by NYFA. Photography is one of seven named eligible disciplines, alongside craft/sculpture, digital/electronic arts, interdisciplinary work, painting, printmaking/drawing/book arts and video/film. The inaugural residency runs 1 October to 31 December 2027 at ISCP, 1040 Metropolitan Avenue, Brooklyn. The selected artist receives 24/7 access to a private furnished studio of 334 square feet, a monthly stipend of $3,000 for accommodation, living expenses and materials ($9,000 across the three months), and a one-time payment of $1,000 toward airfare and local travel. The programme includes at least one hour-long meeting a month with visiting critics, field trips, gallery walks and Fall Open Studios, and the option to join NYFA's professional development programmes. The artist arranges their own travel and housing; ISCP expects roughly 20 studio hours a week. To apply you must be a practising artist in one or more of the eligible disciplines, hold a valid Greek passport, be a current full-time resident of Greece and have been for at least two years at the time applications opened, be 30 or older by 20 October 2026, have working English, and not be enrolled in a full-time degree programme. Artist duos of two may apply together on one shared stipend and studio. Proof of residency and age is required of the selected artist. The application asks for a CV or bio of up to five pages, up to 10 images (or a mix of 5 images and one audio/video link), a 150-word work-sample note and three 250-word statements on the proposed project, the practice and why New York. An independent three-person panel scores applications, shortlists three finalists for virtual interviews and makes the final choice; all applicants hear in February 2027 and the recipient is announced publicly in March. Applications must be in English and go through Submittable; there is no application fee.
