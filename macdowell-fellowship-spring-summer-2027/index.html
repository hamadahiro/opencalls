MacDowell Fellowship — Spring/Summer 2027

Prize Funded residency up to 8 weeks

MacDowell's Spring/Summer 2027 cycle, for residencies running March 1 – August 31, 2027 at the 450-acre estate in Peterborough, New Hampshire. A Fellowship consists of exclusive use of a private studio, accommodations and three prepared meals a day for up to eight weeks; there are no residency fees, and Fellows may apply for need-based stipends and travel reimbursement covering direct travel, rent, lost income and childcare. Photographers apply under Visual Arts, which MacDowell describes as spanning "photographers and printmakers to sculptors and creators of large-scale installation". The sole criterion for acceptance is artistic excellence and artists at any stage of practice may apply, but applicants cannot be enrolled in a degree-seeking program during the residency season and may submit only one application every 24 months; the reference-letter requirement is temporarily suspended. Applications open August 17, 2026. A $30 processing fee applies, with waivers available on request for financial hardship.
