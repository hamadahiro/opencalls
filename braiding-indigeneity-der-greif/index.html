Braiding Indigeneity

Der Greif, the Munich non-profit for contemporary photography, invites photographers, artists and visual storytellers to revisit how images construct identity, land and relational memory. The call was developed within Der Greif's online exhibitions series and grounds itself in the ethos of Issue 19, "I Am, Because of You, Mother Earth", guest-edited by Zanele Muholi, carrying that relational ethic forward in conversation with Indigenous visual sovereignties. The organisers are looking for creatives engaging with indigeneity through genuine collaboration, consent, and accountability to the Indigenous individuals, knowledge-holders, or communities whose ways of framing life and relationality inform the work; the title pays tribute to Robin Wall Kimmerer's Braiding Sweetgrass, a reading that inspired the call's take. Submissions are one project of up to 20 images, cohesive and not previously featured by Der Greif. Selected participants join a hybrid workshop - online and in person in Paris on 14 November 2026, three hours, time and location to be announced - integrated into the FUTURES Hub programme, with collaborative image analysis, reciprocal critique and public auditory sessions; the selected work is then shown in an online exhibition in November. Twelve to sixteen artists are selected. The call is open to photographers internationally, particularly emerging and mid-career artists, with no age or regional restrictions, and the eligibility line says only that it is preferable to be available for the Paris workshop - but the call page carries a firmer note in bold before submitting: you must already be in Paris on 14 November 2026 to apply, as Der Greif cannot compensate travel or accommodation costs. Der Greif describes the 15 EUR as a voluntary donation, and its Picter entry page agrees: it reads "Open Voluntary" and "Submit for free or pay a voluntary donation of 15€", so submission is free and the donation optional. Artists unable to contribute financially can email the voucher address given on its Picter entry page for a 50% or 100% discount code.
