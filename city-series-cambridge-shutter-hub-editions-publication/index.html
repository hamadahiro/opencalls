The City Series: Cambridge — Shutter Hub Editions Publication

Open call for The City Series: Cambridge, the inaugural edition of Shutter Hub's ongoing series of photobooks exploring the people, places, and cultures that shape cities worldwide. Photographers are invited to approach Cambridge openly — through people, atmosphere, details, routines, abstractions, or unexpected moments that feel personal — showing what the city feels like rather than what it looks like. Selected images featured in a printed publication available at shutterhub.org.uk and selected stockists. Published in collaboration with Velvet Magazine. Pay-what-you-can entry with £10 minimum; free submissions available on request for photographers with limited income. Open worldwide.
