2027 Lighthouse Works Fellowship

A six-week fellowship on Fishers Island, New York, from an organisation devoted both to developing artists and to the year-round cultural life of the island. Fellows get a private bedroom in a three-storey Victorian house shared with the other fellows, all meals — Lighthouse Works staff cook and eat dinner with fellows most nights, and all dietary needs are accommodated — a private studio about a mile and a half from the house, flooded with light and facing the ocean, and $1,750 in stipends. There is a wood and metal fabrication shop and a kiln on site. The primary obligation is simply to pursue your work; the only two commitments are an Artist Talk and an Open Studio, which bookend the stay. The organisation runs three fellowship tracks — Visual Art, Fiction and Composer — and states that artists at any stage of their career are encouraged to apply, with artistic excellence the primary criterion for acceptance. It publishes no list of accepted media, so a photographer applies under Visual Art, but its own roster of past fellows settles the question: Genesis Baez, whose photographs the Museum of Modern Art has collected, Peng Ke, a RISD photography graduate and Magnum Foundation fellow, and Widline Cadet and Lachell Workman, both photography graduates, have all held fellowships here. Applicants must be at least 21 and must not be currently enrolled in a BFA or MFA programme; Lighthouse Works primarily considers US-based artists, though international artists with a demonstrated history of living and working in the United States are also invited, and collaboratives, duos and collectives are not accepted this cycle. Applications for the 2027 cycle are open September 1 to October 1, 2026 (11:59pm ET) through SlideRoom, with a $25 application fee — a limited number of waivers are granted first come, first served if you email before you submit. The visual-art work sample is up to ten files, and only the first five are read in the opening round. Selection panels — a jury of experts in each applicant's field — meet from mid-October through December, interviews are scheduled in early January and results go out in mid-January. The 2027 calendar runs five sessions between March 9 and November 30, 2027. This year's programme is supported in part by a grant from the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation.
