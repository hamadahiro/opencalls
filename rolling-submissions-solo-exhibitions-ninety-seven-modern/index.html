Rolling Submissions: Solo Exhibitions

Prize Solo exhibition

Ninety-Seven Modern is an online contemporary gallery founded in Detroit in 2025 by Sierra R. Aguilar, on the premise that the art world is needlessly difficult and exclusive to break into. Its exhibitions are, for now, entirely virtual, which lets viewers anywhere see work by emerging artists, and they rotate on a schedule covering all mediums, processes and origins; the gallery's group shows and its own shop have leaned photographic. Alongside those themed shows it takes rolling proposals for online solo exhibitions, open to "all artists of emerging status, of any age, race, denomination, and demographic". A proposal is up to fifteen high-quality images with title, year, medium and place of creation, a third-person bio attached as a PDF, an artist statement about the work, and your preferred artist name plus any social media and website links, all sent by email with "Solo Exhibition" in the subject line. Selected artists are featured on the gallery site and across its social media. There is no deadline - applications are open on a rolling basis - and the $25 non-refundable application fee is paid through the gallery's own Squarespace shop rather than on the call page.
