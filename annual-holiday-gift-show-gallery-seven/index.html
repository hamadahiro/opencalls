Annual Holiday Gift Show

Gallery Seven's annual selling show in Lockport, Illinois, hung from 27 November 2026 to 9 January 2027 and built around the town's Christmas in the Square weekend — the gallery opens with an artists' reception and holiday open house on Saturday 28 November, 12–5pm, and holds a second open house on Thursday 3 December, 5–7pm. The submission form is a one-page PDF on the gallery's own site and asks only for your details, your medium, the number of pieces and a price range; selection is made from three example images emailed to the gallery, and there is no entry fee. The medium line on the form is a blank to fill in and nothing excludes photography — the gallery's own list of what it shows opens with Photography, ahead of pen and ink, oil painting, assemblage, watercolour, woodturning and jewellery. Work sells on a signed consignment contract with a 30% commission to the gallery, which leaves the artist 70%. This is a hand-delivered show: everything must be priced, labelled and photo-inventoried by 21 November, with drop-off on 21 November 12–5pm or 22 November 12–3pm and pick-up on 9 or 10 January. Distance, not medium, is the real gate — the gallery asks for work by local artists and its stated mission is to give local artists somewhere to show and sell. Gallery Seven began in 2007 as a group of seven fine-art photographers in Joliet and moved into the historic Gaylord Building in downtown Lockport in 2018; it runs two juried shows a year, one of which, Open Lens, has been its fine-art photography exhibition every June since 2010.
