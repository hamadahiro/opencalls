Black Whimsy — SaveArtSpace Billboard Exhibition

Public art exhibition on billboard advertising space in New York City, curated by Adesewa Adekoya, opening October 16, 2026. Black Whimsy asks what happens when Black imagination has no limits, inviting artists to picture the ethereal, fantastical and otherworldly possibilities of Black life and creativity across the diaspora — the many surreal and dreamlike worlds in which Black people can exist — and to escape reality for a while. Open to artists of any age, skill level or background; all art is accepted except corporate logos, personal advertising and video, and still JPEGs only, so photographic work is eligible. Selected works are displayed on NYC ad space for at least one month; artists are notified after September 21, 2026. SaveArtSpace is a non-profit that has placed artists' work on commercial ad space since 2015, and fees are tax-deductible donations supporting exhibition production.
