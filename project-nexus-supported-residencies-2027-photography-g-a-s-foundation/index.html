Project Nexus Supported Residencies 2027 — Photography

Prizes ₦2,660,000 stipend Accommodation & studio in Lagos

A fully supported four-week photography residency at G.A.S. Lagos, 8 February – 5 March 2027, from the Guest Artists Space (G.A.S.) Foundation and the Yinka Shonibare Foundation with the support of Deutsche Bank. It is one of three residencies in the 2027 round of Project Nexus, and the only one addressed specifically to photographers: applications are invited from photographers living and working anywhere in Nigeria for whom photography is a principal part of their practice, across analogue and digital work, documentary, conceptual and constructed image-making, portraiture, experimental processes, photographic archives and interdisciplinary practices rooted in photography. The award is a ₦2,660,000 residency stipend towards food, living expenses and materials, plus internal travel, a private en-suite bedroom, a shared workspace and access to the G.A.S. Library and Picton Archive; trips outside Lagos and Ijebu are at the resident's own cost and specialist materials should be brought along. There is no requirement to arrive with a finished proposal and no requirement to produce an exhibition or completed artwork — residents are asked to share an aspect of their practice in a format agreed with the team (a talk, workshop, open studio, screening, field session or shared meal) and to contribute a short reflection to the G.A.S. archive. Applicants must be Nigerian nationals currently residing in Nigeria, aged 23 or over at any career stage with an existing body of work, available for the fixed residency dates, proficient in spoken and written English, and able to name a referee who can speak to their practice. The application asks for a single PDF portfolio of images with research examples or publications (20MB max), a CV, a short biography (150 words), referee contact details and six written answers in one section — previous exchanges or community engagement, why G.A.S., and which strand(s) of inquiry the work engages, at 200 words each; a proposal for a public-facing community event and how the residency would be documented, at 150 words each; and a sixth, optional 200-word answer on practice and research. Responses in that section may be given as a video statement with a link instead. Pronouns, phone and address are required fields; up to three video links, a reference letter and an access-requirement note are optional. Applications close at midnight WAT and no fee is charged. A selection panel is to be announced in November 2026, with final selections on 7 December 2026. The two sibling residencies, both on the same deadline and form page, are Visual Arts and the Environment at the G.A.S. Farm House in Ikiṣẹ, Ogun State (8 weeks, ₦3,175,000, photography among the accepted media) and a part-funded residency for an artist of the global Nigerian Diaspora (4 weeks in Lagos, ₦2,660,000, travel not covered).
