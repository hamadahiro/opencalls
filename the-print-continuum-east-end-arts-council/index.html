The Print Continuum

Prizes $1,000 Exhibition

East End Arts Council in Riverhead, New York invites printmakers from across the United States to submit to The Print Continuum, a national juried exhibition celebrating the intersection of printmaking's historical traditions and its expanding contemporary practices. A continuum is a spectrum, the council writes, where distinct points are linked by gradual change rather than clear divisions; printmaking is not divided between old and new but is a medium in which history, innovation and experimentation stay in dialogue. Eligible work includes screen printing, relief printing, woodcut, linocut, intaglio, etching, lithography, monotype, collagraph, gel printing, and mixed-media works incorporating hybrid processes on paper, fabric, sculpture or other substrates. Read the medium clause carefully before applying: photography and 3D printing alone are not eligible, but works incorporating those processes within original printmaking or mixed-media pieces will be considered — so photogravure, photopolymer, screenprints made from photographs and photo-based collage are in scope while a straight photographic print is not. Selections are made by artists Ellen Wiener and Bill Albertini, with the exhibition curated by Mich Miller, Head of Printmaking at East End Arts Council. Applications go through CaFE and ask for an artist statement of up to 500 words on creative intent, process and how printmaking contributes to your vision, three to five images with optional detail or installation views, a website or social media link, and an image list. The cap is three submissions of work, each original and not previously shown at East End Arts Council; the three to five images may include multiple views, details or installation shots of those same pieces. There is no size limitation. Selected work hangs at the council's 11 West Gallery from 24 October to 13 December 2026, with an opening reception on 24 October. Awards are $1,000 for first prize, $500 for second and $250 for third. Council members get early access and a 10 percent discount code on the application fee. Read the terms before entering: accepted work must stay available for the whole run and is sold exclusively through the council, which keeps a 50 percent commission from non-members and 30 percent from members, and selected artists arrange and pay for delivery and return themselves.
