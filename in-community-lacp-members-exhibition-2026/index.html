In Community: LACP's Annual Members' Exhibition

Prizes $500 cash Exhibition

The Los Angeles Center of Photography's annual members' exhibition, open to current LACP members — membership can be purchased online before submitting. Juror Chandra Glick, Photo Director at Artforum, selects works for the show at LACP's downtown Los Angeles gallery, October 1–November 5, 2026, with an opening reception on October 1. Prizes: 1st place $500 cash; 2nd place $250 LACP gift card plus a one-year membership; 3rd place $250 LACP gift card; honorable mentions also awarded. Entry fees are tiered from $18.75 for a single image to $150 for 31–35 images. Images and texts created by AI are not accepted.
