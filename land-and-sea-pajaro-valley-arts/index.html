Land and Sea: Celebrating Our National Marine Sanctuary

Prize Exhibition

Pajaro Valley Arts, the Watsonville nonprofit, is running an invitational and juried exhibition FOR PHOTOGRAPHY AND DIGITAL ART ONLY, presented alongside Photo Carmel Month in April 2027, on the Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary — which “spans from Rocky Point in Marin County to Cambria in San Luis Obispo County, reaching 25 miles offshore and inland to the ridges of the Coast Range.” The brief is deliberately wider than a nature show: PVA “invites photographers and digital artists living and/or working in the greater Bay Area to explore this territory in full — its waters, wildlife, working harbors, towns, industry, and people,” and welcomes “images of any subject connected to this region: marine life, coastal landscapes, architecture, industry, and the everyday people who live and work here. The goal is a fuller picture of the sanctuary — not just its natural beauty, but the human life built around it.” The jury is the reason to look twice: Ted Orland and Brian Taylor, with Joan Blackmer and Chad Williams curating. Entry is free and takes up to 5 JPEGs of work new to PVA, submitted through the gallery's own entry form (JPEGs at no more than 300 dpi and 6” in any direction, filenames as surname plus title); a hard-copy form is available on request. The exhibition runs 23 March – 2 May 2027 with a reception on Saturday 27 March, 1–3pm, and both invited and open-call artists are hung. THE COSTS, published in full: there is no application fee, but “upon acceptance/selection into the exhibit, there is a $35.00 participation fee for each artist, to be paid with the submittal of the loan agreement” — PVA says it “helps offset exhibit expenses” and that “some participation sponsorships may be available”, so ask if the fee is a barrier. Sales commission is 30% for PVA members and 50% for non-members, and you must be a member in good standing through 2 May 2027 to get the lower rate. TWO PRACTICAL LIMITS: PVA is “unable to accept artwork through the mail for this exhibit” — artists or their representatives deliver in person to Watsonville on 15 March, 10am–2pm — and framed work is capped at 36 inches wide, wired and ready to hang, insured to a maximum of $2,500 per piece. Artists are notified on 22 February 2027, with loan forms and artist statements due 5 March.
