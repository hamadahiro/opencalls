Small Works 2026

Prizes $700 In awards Exhibition

The Northville Art House is a community gallery in downtown Northville, Michigan, and Small Works is its year-end juried holiday show — over 125 works of art on a diminutive scale, 16 inches or less in any direction including any frame. The call is national: artists 18 and over anywhere in the continental United States may submit up to four original works created in the last ten years and not previously exhibited at the Art House. Photography is named outright in the accepted-media line alongside collage, drawing, painting and printmaking on the two-dimensional side, and ceramics, sculpture, assemblage, mixed media and metalwork on the three-dimensional side; the only exclusions are film, video, electronic and AI-generated artwork. The Northville Art House Exhibition Committee — practising artists, art instructors, staff and community leaders — serves as juror, selecting from the submitted images alone, so image quality matters. Entry is $40, or $30 for Art House members, paid after the online form is completed. Awards run first through fourth place plus honourable mentions, totalling more than $700 in cash and prizes, presented at 6 p.m. during the free public opening reception on Friday 13 November. Every work must be for sale — the show is pitched to visitors looking for original art to give as gifts — and sales split 65 per cent to the artist, 35 per cent to the Art House. Work must arrive installation-ready: works on paper framed, two-dimensional pieces wired for hanging (sawtooth hangers accepted but not recommended), three-dimensional pieces free-standing, pedestal-ready or hung with wire or rod. Jury notification is 29 September 2026 by 5 p.m. EST, delivery is 1 and 2 November, the exhibition runs 13 November to 18 December 2026, and pick-up is 18 and 19 December. Shipping, packing and insurance are the artist's responsibility, and shipped work needs a pre-paid return label.
