4th Annual Juried Exhibition

Fourth annual juried group exhibition at Blah Blah Gallery, a Philadelphia gallery at 907 Christian Street whose stated focus is "early-stage and mid-career women and non-binary artists" — the gallery's mission is to "raise a platform and provide a safe space for two groups who are often underrepresented in gallery settings", and it exhibits across multiple mediums. The call itself is announced as open to emerging and mid-career artists "working in any medium", and the gallery has answered directly, on its own call post, that the call is international ("yes!") and that there are no size or medium limitations ("nope!"), so photography is eligible; the call text does not restate the gallery's women and non-binary focus. The show is curated by gallery director Megan Galardi, a Philadelphia curator who has curated exhibitions for the Philadelphia Mural Arts Program. The jury panel is the artist Kevin Sabo, Leah Triplett (Curator of Contemporary Art at the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts) and Tif XB, founder, curator and producer of realtime.247. The exhibition opens in the physical gallery in late January 2027. The $25 fee is charged per application, not per artwork, and the gallery states there is no size requirement; applications go through a Google Form that requires a Google sign-in. The gallery has not published a per-work count, a notification date or an AI policy on any surface it makes readable.
