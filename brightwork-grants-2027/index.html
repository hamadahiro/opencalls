Brightwork Grants 2027

Prize $2,000–$3,000

Two grants from the Brightwork Foundation, the funder behind Anchorlight's Brightwork Fellowship in Raleigh, open to visual artists living full time in any North Carolina county. One $2,000 Professional Development Grant supports workshops, classes, conferences or one-to-one technical instruction, professional documentation of a portfolio, a designer-built website, or equipment and studio materials. One $3,000 Studio Project Grant supports the production of studio-based work, including equipment, research expenses specific to the project, and costs tied to a confirmed future solo exhibition in a public gallery. Applicants choose one grant and submit a proposed budget with a statement of intent or project description, artist statement, bio and recent work; awardees must provide receipts for each purchase. Eligibility is visual artists 21 and over who have lived in North Carolina for at least twelve consecutive months and will stay for the grant period, and who are not enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate degree programme during it — doctoral candidates who have finished coursework may apply. Funds must be used between January and December 2027, and you may not apply for a grant and the Brightwork Fellowship in the same cycle.
