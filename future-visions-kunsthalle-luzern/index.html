Future Visions

Prizes Exhibition Artist fee

Kunsthalle Luzern and the akku Kunstplattform in Emmenbrücke have launched a Switzerland-wide open call on a single question: what does the future look like from here? "The future is not a place we wait for," the call opens. "It takes shape in the images, conflicts and decisions of the present." The two institutions are after work that engages social, technological, ecological or political transformation and builds an independent position out of it — "conceptual, speculative, poetic, critical or experimental approaches from all areas of contemporary" art. It is free to enter and open to artists resident in Switzerland, individuals and collectives alike, from students to established practitioners; existing works and newly planned projects are equally welcome, provided a proposal is "technically and spatially feasible" within what the two spaces can do. Submit at least one and at most three proposed works, each with title, year, material, dimensions and up to five images (video as a link plus a still — no uploaded files), all inside one PDF of 10 MB or less named Surname_Firstname_FV2027.pdf. You cannot apply to just one of the two venues: a single proposal may be assigned to only one of them, but different proposals from the same practice can go to both, and site-specific proposals are the one exception, provided the tie to that space is argued in the application. Selection is by the curatorial teams of both institutions together with three external experts — Katrin Sperry, Michel Rebosura and Sebastian Utzni — on artistic and conceptual quality, thematic relevance, willingness to experiment, and feasibility. Two separate exhibitions follow: Kunsthalle Luzern 4 July – 19 September 2027 (opening Saturday 3 July), akku Kunstplattform 11 July – 5 September 2027 (opening Saturday 10 July). On money, the institutions commit in writing: "The Kunsthalle Luzern and the akku Kunstplattform are committed to fair fees and good working conditions for artists. Exhibition fees are based on the visarte recommendations applicable to smaller institutions," with further contributions towards production costs possible in justified cases from the project budget — no figure is published. Artists pay transport and return themselves; the institutions insure the work from receipt through dismantling, and artists supply their own monitors, projectors and playback equipment for media works. Applications go only through the form on the Kunsthalle Luzern project page — nothing else is accepted — and close Sunday 15 November 2026, with jury feedback by Monday 14 December 2026. The project was conceived by Marius Geschinske, director of the Kunsthalle Luzern. Neither the project page nor the six-page open-call dossier names a medium list beyond "all areas of contemporary art"; the Kunsthalle's group show *Crossroad – Home in Motion* (4 July – 27 September 2026) put photographers among its sixteen positions — Hans U. Alder, whose own site is fotografiealder.com, and Mischa Christen, "a Swiss photographer based in Lucerne".
