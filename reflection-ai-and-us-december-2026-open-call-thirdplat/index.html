REFLECTION: AI AND US — December 2026 Open Call

Thirdplat's free monthly open call for December 2026 treats artificial intelligence as a mirror rather than a subject in itself: "REFLECTION: AI AND US is not simply a theme about artificial intelligence, but an inquiry into what it reveals about the people who create, use and live alongside it." The platform's framing is that every generated image, sentence, voice and film begins with traces of human knowledge and work — "Like an echo, AI repeats and rearranges what we have already made. Like a mirror, it returns our imagination together with our habits, prejudices, desires and fears" — and it names the questions it wants work to take on: authorship, originality, labour, consent and trust, and "Who creates a work when human intention meets machine generation?" Submissions may use AI as a creative tool or as a subject, and equally may "observe, question, challenge, resist or reimagine it". Photography is named outright among the welcomed forms, alongside moving image, writing, craft, sound, performance and cross-disciplinary work. This is publication rather than competition: Thirdplat is an independent online platform that publishes accepted work directly into a permanent public archive with an individual page and artist credit, and its Terms of Use state plainly that "Submission is free" and that "No payment, prize, commission or commercial agreement is created by submitting". Thirteen works are already published under this theme. Submitting runs through a Thirdplat account, and the Terms set the only entry bar: "You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or submit work through Thirdplat." Submissions close 31 December 2026; the January 2027 theme (Counter-Memory) is open alongside it.
