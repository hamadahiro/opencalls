14th Annual Eastern West Virginia Juried Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

Annual open-theme juried exhibition at Berkeley Art Works, the Berkeley Arts Council's gallery in downtown Martinsburg. Open to artists 18 and over living in Berkeley, Grant, Hampshire, Hardy, Jefferson, Mineral, Morgan or Pendleton County — a deliberately regional show for the eastern panhandle and highlands of West Virginia. Original two- or three-dimensional work in any medium or technique, abstract, representational or non-representational, completed in the last three years and not previously exhibited at Berkeley Art Works. Juried by Sonya Evanisko, Professor of Art and Painting/Drawing Coordinator at Shepherd University, who selects a Best in Show, a Merit Award and an Honorable Mention. On view 24 September – 15 November 2026.
