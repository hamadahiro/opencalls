Swiss Art Awards 2027

The Swiss federal art competition, held annually since 1899, is open to artists, architects, critics, editors and curators who are Swiss citizens or resident in Switzerland; the Art section sets no restriction on medium. Applicants submit a PDF dossier, and those selected present an original work in the Swiss Art Awards exhibition at Messe Basel. Students on an art or architecture course cannot enter unless they already hold a master's degree.

DeadlineDecember 3, 2026
PrizesCHF 25,000, Exhibition
Entry feeFree
EligibilitySwitzerland only
LocationBasel, Switzerland
RequirementsDossier of previous work
Submit viaWebsite
ResultsEnd of February 2027

Jury: Federal Art Commission with invited experts

Organized by Federal Office of Culture
