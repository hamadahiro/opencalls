Into the Light: Third Annual Darkroom Members Exhibition

The Halide Project is a nonprofit darkroom and gallery on North 2nd Street in Philadelphia, and its Darkroom Members Exhibition is the annual show of what has come out of that darkroom: work made by current Halide darkroom members, plus anything produced in one of the organisation's workshops. The medium gate is the same strict one the organisation applies to its flagship Living Image call — work must be made using traditional, pre-digital photographic printing, including but not limited to silver gelatin, c-prints, instant film, platinum/palladium, cyanotype, gum bichromate, wet plate collodion and other alternative and historic processes. Analog prints made from digital negatives are eligible; inkjet prints and digital c-prints are not. One submission per person, framed work no larger than 16 by 20 inches including the frame, and the application is a short Airtable form asking about the work and the maker. There is no entry fee. Everything included in the exhibition is published in a printed catalogue. Accepted work must be dropped off at The Halide Project by 4 October — delivery is assumed to be in person, though the organisation will discuss shipping by email — and must stay up for the full run. The show opens with a reception on Saturday 10 October from 5 to 8 pm and is on view to 5 December 2026. Artists keep their copyright, grant promotional use, and the gallery retains a 25% commission on sales.
