Earth Matters

Prize Group exhibition in Chelsea, NYC

Earth Matters is the annual juried open call from Atlantic Gallery, the artist-run cooperative that has operated in New York's Chelsea gallery district since 1974. It invites artists whose work is earth-made as well as those whose practice is earth-focused, exploring our relationship to land and water, geology and ecology, and the interconnected environments that sustain all life - work the gallery describes as residing in the sublime, spiritual, geological, topographical, ecological, biophilic, scientific, cultural, social and sustainable realms, whether non-objective or carrying a deeper message, materially driven or conceptually based. Artists employing earth-derived materials and those exploring earth matter through image, process, research, environmental inquiry or metaphor are equally encouraged to apply. The juror is Judith Kruger, a multidisciplinary American artist recognised internationally for her advocacy and use of historic natural pigments and ecological processes, whose work is held by the Mattatuck Museum, the Hammond Museum, Savannah College of Art and Design and Wofford College, and who also jurored Atlantic Gallery's 2020 juried exhibition. Photography is named in the accepted-media list alongside oils, acrylics, watercolours, pastels, drawings, prints, mixed media and sculpture in any medium; digital and giclee reproductions of original artwork and AI-generated work are excluded. Entry is open to international and US artists 18 and over at $35 for up to three works plus $10 for up to five more submitted at the same time, with no piece exceeding 36 inches wide by 40 inches high and all work made within the last five years. Selected work is shown at the gallery from 16 February to 6 March 2027, and award winners are chosen for inclusion in a later Atlantic Gallery group show in Chelsea. All work must be for sale, and the gallery takes 40% commission. Accepted work has to be delivered to the Chelsea gallery in person or by proxy during regular gallery hours - no shipping option is offered and the gallery disclaims liability for work in transit - so artists applying from outside New York should plan for that before paying the fee.
