Photo Fringe 2026 — Feedback Loop

Prizes Exhibition Bursary

Photo Fringe is a volunteer-led registered charity that has run an open-platform photography festival on a DIY, collectively-funded model since 2003. Registration is now open for the 2026 edition, which runs 2–31 October across Brighton & Hove, Portsmouth and Newhaven with launch events on Friday 2 October. Anyone can take part: artists, photographers, collectives, curators and organisations register their own self-curated exhibition and are then listed in the official festival programme and on photofringe.org, given marketing resources and exhibitor guidance, and folded into a city-wide trail that helps audiences find the work. Exhibitors are responsible for finding and managing their own venue, though the festival shares venue leads and connects artists looking for larger shared spaces; online-only exhibitions are equally welcome, and Photo Fringe events such as artist talks, workshops and photo walks are listed free. The 2026 theme is Feedback Loop — a cycle of action and response, thinking about how images circulate and change as they are seen, shared, interpreted and reworked, and what becomes possible when work is made with others rather than alone. Responding to the theme is encouraged but not compulsory. Contributions towards the festival are £125 for a solo exhibition at a venue and online, £150 for a group exhibition, £60 for solo online-only and £100 for group online-only, all subsidised; a small number of bursaries are available for exhibitors who cannot afford a contribution, by emailing the festival. All registered exhibitors are automatically entered for the Danny Wilson Memorial Awards, supported by Photoworks, whose prizes include a bursary, mentoring sessions and artist talks. Registration closes 1 September 2026.
