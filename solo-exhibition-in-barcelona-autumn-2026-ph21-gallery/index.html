Solo Exhibition in Barcelona — Autumn 2026

Prize Solo exhibition (€4,500 package)

Solo photography exhibition opportunity at PH21 Gallery in Barcelona's Gothic Quarter. The selected photographer receives a solo show (17 October – 10 November 2026) with all printing and installation costs covered, a dedicated website page, and a 50% discount on an accompanying photobook — a package valued at €4,500. Up to ten semi-finalists are additionally selected for the biannual "Photographic Visions" group exhibition, each presenting a three-image mini-series. Curated by Zsolt Bátori (PhD; founder/director, Photolucida board member) and Borbála Jász (art historian; vice-director). Submit a cohesive portfolio of 15–35 images — one or two thematically unified series — with an artist statement and bio; no stylistic or thematic restrictions. Open to photographers 18+ worldwide. Final deadline 17 August: €45, or €30 reduced for students, unwaged applicants and applicants from low-income economies. The €40 / €25 early-bird rates closed on 10 August.
