Revoke Exhibition Open Call

Prize Exhibition

Revoke Photo Collective's exhibition open call is built around the word REVOKE - revocation as an act of creation, in which something is dismantled so something else can emerge. The brief invites resistance, rebellion, transformation, anger, liberation, rupture, memory, desire and reinvention, with no restrictions on interpretation; the collective says it wants personal and unexpected responses to the word. Open to artists working with photography anywhere, up to 15 images per entry. Selected works are curated by Linda Zhengova, a Czech-Chinese photographer, curator and writer whose own practice deals with representation, sexuality and trauma, into a punk exhibition in Melbourne running October 30 - November 1, 2026. The collective states plainly that this is a curated exhibition and that paying the fee does not guarantee a place in the show. Fees cover venue hire, professional printing, exhibition production and general running costs, so there is no further charge if selected - the collective prints the work and asks selected artists for high-resolution files at that point. Entry runs from A$40 for a single image to A$125 for fifteen.
