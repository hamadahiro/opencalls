PASSAGE 2027

Prizes Exhibition Catalogue

Sussex Contemporary's first exhibition of 2027 is also its first international open call: PASSAGE fills BN9 Studio in Newhaven, on the Sussex coast, from 6 to 28 March 2027, open to the public across four weekends. The theme is the single word — artists are invited to read it politically, personally or poetically, with the organisers stating there is no single right interpretation. What makes this unlike most open calls is that it is what Sussex Contemporary calls democratic: there is no jury and no pre-selection, so once you buy an entry your work is in the show. The only limit is space — a maximum of 300 submissions, and entries close when those 300 places are filled or at 23:59 on 31 January 2027, whichever comes first. Entry is priced by the space a work occupies rather than by medium: £20 for small work up to A4, £40 up to A2, £80 up to A0, measured including frame, plinth or mount, and if a piece falls between two sizes the larger tier applies. Each artist may enter a maximum of two works, and both 2D and 3D work is welcome — photography is named alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, ceramics, sculpture, jewellery, illustration and mixed media. Entries are bought through the organisation's own online shop; a confirmation email is followed by a second email linking a private artists' page that carries the submission form, where the image and the work's details are uploaded (one form per entry purchased, and that information builds the catalogue). Every entry includes a copy of the printed exhibition catalogue, which features all works in the show, two invitations to the private view, and inclusion in the exhibition artwork list. All work is for sale and Sussex Contemporary takes 30% commission, with sold pieces staying on the wall until the show closes. Read the organisation's terms before entering, because the collection clauses carry money the call page does not mention: any work not removed by the final collection date is charged £15 per week or part week per piece; unsold work left more than 28 days past that date is deemed abandoned, and Sussex Contemporary may then sell or dispose of it and keep the proceeds; and where a sold piece is not collected by its buyer and the organisation has to ship it back to you, you pay transport and insurance plus a £25 administration fee. Artists deliver and collect their own work, gallery-ready and correctly prepared for display, on drop-off dates communicated to entrants. The exhibition is curated — hung and laid out — by Jo Myles, Sussex Contemporary's creative director, and follows two earlier democratic shows at the same venue, Meander and Efflorescence. This is the same organisation behind the judged Unfold open call with Fabrica in Brighton; PASSAGE is the unjuried, pay-to-exhibit end of its programme, and photographers should weigh the entry price against the fact that inclusion is guaranteed rather than selected.
