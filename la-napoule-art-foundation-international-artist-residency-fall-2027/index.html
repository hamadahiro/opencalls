La Napoule Art Foundation — International Artist Residency Fall 2027

Two one-month residencies at the Château de La Napoule on the Côte d'Azur, running October 12 to November 10 and November 15 to December 12, 2027; applicants pick one. Residents get a single room, a studio, a stipend, and breakfast and dinner on weekdays. Open to artists 18 and over of any discipline or nationality, but the historic château is not wheelchair accessible.
