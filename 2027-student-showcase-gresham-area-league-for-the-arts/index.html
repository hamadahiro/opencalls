2027 Student Showcase

Prize Exhibition

The Gresham Area League for the Arts' student show, hung in GALA's gallery in the Gresham City Council Chambers, Oregon, from 11 January to 19 February 2027 and opened with an artists' reception and award ceremony on 14 January. GALA invites students in grades 6 through 12 from the East County school districts and surrounding areas, and names photography directly alongside ceramics, digital art, drawing, fibre, glass, metalwork, mixed media, painting, printmaking, sculpture and wood; 2D and 3D work are both welcome. Reproducible work — computer-generated art, digitally altered photographs, giclee prints — must be labelled as such, and photographic prints must be signed, numbered as part of a limited edition and meet archival standards. Work must be entirely the student's own and made within the last year: no class step-out pieces, no paint-by-number. The gallery lends frames in four sizes (12x16, 12x18, 16x20 and 14x14) to students who need them and retains 20% commission on sales; submissions must stay clear of nudity, sexual content and divisive themes for a family-friendly multi-use space. Accepted work is delivered in person on 8 or 9 January and stays up for the whole run, with an art talk on 4 February and an education Saturday on 6 February. No entry fee is stated anywhere on the call page. Entries close at midnight on Wednesday 16 December 2026.
