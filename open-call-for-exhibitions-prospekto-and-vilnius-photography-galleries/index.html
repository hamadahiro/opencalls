Open Call for Exhibitions — Prospekto and Vilnius Photography Galleries

Prize Solo or group exhibition

The Lithuanian Photographers' Association invites artists and curators to propose solo, group or curatorial photography exhibitions for its two Vilnius spaces — Prospekto Gallery on Gedimino Avenue and the Vilnius Photography Gallery. Applications are accepted year-round with no fixed deadline; selections are made once a year, in August. Submit an exhibition proposal of up to 500 words naming the preferred gallery and exhibition period, a bio of up to 100 words, and a portfolio with supporting visual material such as documentation of previous installations. Total submission size must not exceed 5 MB. Free to enter.
