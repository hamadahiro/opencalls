Fabula Works — Cycle(s)

Prize Exhibition (printing & promotion covered) prize

Second open call from Rome curatorial platform Fabula Works, themed Cycle(s). All genres welcome; the jury seeks work that moves beyond familiar aesthetics with a distinct perspective. Guest juror is photographer Carolyn Mendelsohn (Nikon Europe Ambassador). Submit 10–25 photographs as a portfolio with biography and artist statement.
