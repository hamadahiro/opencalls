Stories and Traditions — Native American Art Exhibition

Prize Exhibition

Stories and Traditions is an exhibition highlighting artwork by Native American artists in the Mid-Atlantic area of Maryland, Virginia, DC and Pennsylvania, which the organiser notes may include the indigenous peoples of the Piscataway, Lumbee, Pamunkey, Mattaponi, Chickahominy, Nanticoke and Pocomoke. The brief asks what the stories and traditions of native peoples are as the seasons shift into Fall (Harvest) and Winter (Storytelling and Reflection), a time much of the nation spends observing commercialised holidays. Artists of Native American descent are invited to apply through paintings, drawings, sculpture and fiber as well as traditional artisan crafts such as ceramics, beading and jewellery, and the call's accepted-media list also names photography and video alongside those. Entry is free, each artist may submit up to six works, and image, video, audio and PDF files are all accepted. The exhibition runs November 10th 2026 to January 3rd 2027 with a free public reception on Sunday, December 13th from 2 to 4pm. Montpelier Arts Center is a public arts venue in Laurel, Maryland, run by the Maryland-National Capital Park and Planning Commission's Parks and Recreation department in Prince George's County; its building holds galleries, gas- and electric-fired kilns, a digital lab, printmaking presses and classrooms, admission is free, and the centre runs a year-round exhibition programme alongside its Artists on the Rise juried teen exhibition, a Main Gallery call for proposals and a Library Gallery competition for Maryland artists.
