Full Circle — New Beginnings 2026

Prize Exhibition

Mosaic Art Collective marks its fourth anniversary and its move to a new street-level gallery in Manchester, New Hampshire with a show about cycles: the seasons we move through, the relationships that anchor us, the personal transformations that bring us back to ourselves in new form. A return to an old practice, a fresh start after loss, the quiet repetition of daily rituals — the collective wants to see how artists interpret what it means to come full circle. All media are welcome except reproductions and AI-generated work. Entry is $10 for the first work and $10 for each additional, up to four. The exhibition runs 7–28 September 2026 at the collective's gallery on Chestnut Street with an opening reception on 12 September, 5–7pm, and acceptance notices on 30 August. Sales split 75/25 in the artist's favour, and work must arrive ready to hang with d-rings and picture wire or a $5 wiring fee applies at drop-off.
