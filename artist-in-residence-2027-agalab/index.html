Artist-in-Residence 2027

Prizes Residency Exhibition

agalab (Amsterdams Grafisch Atelier) is a printmaking workshop in Amsterdam that runs a year-round artist-in-residence programme. It offers six places, two with a guest room beside the workshop and four without accommodation. Residencies run in monthly cycles, from the first Monday to the last Friday of the month, for one to three months, and the 2027 application form lists a cycle for every month of the year. The residency is self-funded. agalab's own page prices it at €500 a month without accommodation, €1,450 a month with a room (own shower, shared toilets and kitchen, washing machine and dryer, and a bicycle), and €750 for a duo. Materials are not included. Residents pay a €100 downpayment and log their costs on a sheet, and the unused balance is returned at the end. Artists who graduated between 2024 and 2026 can apply for a 50% subsidised place, accommodation not included. Technical introductions beyond the standard ones cost €55 an hour. Residents have 24/7 access to the workshop. Technicians are on hand Monday to Thursday, silkscreen on Monday and Tuesday, intaglio, relief and lithography on Wednesday and Thursday. Techniques include silkscreen on paper and textile, drypoint and aquatint etching, Toyobo photopolymer plates, stone lithography, linocut, woodcut and letterpress. For photographers, the darkroom has a Durst enlarger, developing tanks for 35mm and 120 film, and a basic stock of developer, stop bath and fixer. The month has a set shape. Week one brings introductions to the silkscreen and etching workshops, week three a mid-residency studio conversation with the team, a visiting artist and the other residents, and week four an exhibition at agalab's Open Day. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, with a reply within about four weeks. The programme is aimed at artists with some printmaking experience, but the page says artists with little or none are also welcome to apply. Applicants email the form with a project plan of up to 120 words, a motivation letter, a CV and a portfolio of up to 20 MB.
