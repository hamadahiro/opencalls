Call for Artist Proposals — 2027 Exhibition Schedule

Waseca Art Center, a rural nonprofit in southern Minnesota, is taking proposals for its 2027 exhibition schedule. The centre runs two gallery spaces, the Harguth and the Beckmann, and each shows one exhibit every six weeks, so a successful proposal is a solo or group show rather than a single work. The exhibit committee is looking for proposals that focus on Minnesota subjects, agriculture, nature and community; political commentary will not be considered. The call names no medium restriction, and the centre's own worked example of pricing is a framed photograph. There is no fee to apply. Waseca provides the reception and the marketing for the show and takes a 30% commission on gallery sales, so artists are asked to quote prices inclusive of that fee. Solo applicants send an artist statement of one page maximum, a short bio with a phone number and place of residence, and 6-10 digital images of up to 3MB each that represent the proposed exhibition; group applicants send bios and artist statements for every participating artist, with phone numbers and places of residence for all members, plus the same 6-10 images representing the exhibition. Artists are responsible for shipping or delivering installation-ready work. Proposals are due by 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday, September 15, submitted by email to the exhibit committee or delivered in hard copy to the centre at 200 State St. N. They are reviewed immediately and artists are notified by the end of October.
