Refusing Rehearsing Resisting

Prizes €4,000 Exhibition

VBKÖ, the Austrian Association of Wom*n Artists founded in Vienna in 1910, invites proposals for its annual programme running March 2027 to February 2028. Following feminist, queer, anti-racist, anti-colonial and anti-authoritarian traditions, the call asks for projects that nurture communal care and build a platform for collective rehearsals imagining alternative futures — work that allows for tenderness, is not afraid of ugly feelings, and holds different truths. Proposals must bring together multiple artists, collectives or collaborative practices; solo exhibitions and single-artist presentations are not supported. Exhibition formats activated by public programming — screenings, performances, workshops, lectures — are encouraged. Each selected project receives a total budget of €4,000 covering artist fees and production, plus the VBKÖ space and shared kitchen, graphic design of promotional material, press announcements, photo documentation, up to 10 hours of technical support and use of the house technical equipment. Projects run 4 to 6 weeks. Applicants based outside Vienna must cover travel, accommodation and shipping from the project budget or external funding; VBKÖ provides invitation and support letters to help secure it. Applications go by email as a single PDF with a programme description, CVs for all participants, a financial plan based on Fair Pay principles, and three high-resolution images. Selected proposals are announced at the beginning of October 2026.
