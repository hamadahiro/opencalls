2027 Artist Residency at MCCC

Prizes $350 stipend Gallery residency

The Gallery at Mercer County Community College runs a bi-annual residency programme for artists working in any medium, and is now taking proposals for the Spring 2027 semester. Four residencies are awarded, each three weeks long, in two terms: Term A from 25 January to 12 February and Term B from 22 February to 12 March 2027. Two residencies are designated for Mercer County artists and two for artists living outside the county, so the call is genuinely open beyond the region. Each resident is assigned one of the Gallery's two side spaces to make an exhibition or performance-based project and shows alongside another resident working in the mirroring space. Residents are required to collaborate with MCCC students and staff on their project and to run workshops in the main gallery, and the Gallery says it especially values projects that prioritise student engagement; faculty add their own talks and events around the project spaces. The Gallery welcomes proposals in all media, including but not limited to painting, sculpture, installation, performance, photography and video. A $350 stipend is paid on successful completion. Practical limits are stated plainly: the side spaces are designed for exhibitions rather than studios and facilities are limited, artists transport their own work and materials, the Gallery covers no shipping or travel costs, work can only be done during gallery open hours, and the space must be returned to its original condition. The Gallery does not handle sales but will pass enquiries to the artist. An application is a portfolio of 5 to 10 images of recent work, a resume with current address, an artist statement and a project proposal specific to the gallery space that sets out the student collaboration and workshops.
