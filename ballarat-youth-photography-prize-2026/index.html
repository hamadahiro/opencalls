Ballarat Youth Photography Prize 2026

Prizes 5 × A$150 minimum Exhibition

Developed by GroupFoto, the Ballarat International Foto Biennale's youth council, this free prize is open to young photographic artists aged 12 to 25 who live, work or study in Ballarat or Victoria's Central Highlands — the municipalities of Ararat Rural City, City of Ballarat, Golden Plains Shire, Hepburn Shire, Moorabool Shire and Pyrenees Shire. There are two categories. Open takes photographs of any subject, idea, person, place or moment, with no set theme. The themed category, Connected, invites photographs exploring the many ways we experience connection — relationships with Country, family, friends, culture, identity, community, place or animals, or how technology shapes connection through phones, gaming, social media, online communities, surveillance or access. Each category may be entered once, with a single photograph; series are not accepted. The Biennale says you do not need professional equipment or previous photography experience to be considered, and phone photographs are accepted (upload the highest-quality version you have). Every eligible entry will be printed and included in the exhibition at the National Centre for Photography, running 20 September to 4 October 2026. Five cash prizes of at least $150 each are presented: a winner in each category for each age group (12–17 and 18–25), plus the UFS People's Choice Award, voted during the exhibition and announced on 6 October. Judging is by Ballarat International Foto Biennale staff and members of GroupFoto; GroupFoto participants may enter and have eligible work exhibited but are not eligible to receive a prize. Entrants under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent, and images created using generative AI are not eligible.
