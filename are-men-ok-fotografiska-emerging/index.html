Are Men OK?

Open call from Fotografiska Emerging and Fotografiska Stockholm, in partnership with the men's-issues organisation MÄN and timed to Men's Health Week, inviting emerging photographers and visual artists to explore what it means to be a man in Sweden today. Submissions may address identity, belonging, loneliness, friendship, care, vulnerability, strength, community, tradition or change — through personal stories, documentary perspectives, staged images, intimate observations or broader social reflections, with a particular welcome for voices and experiences rarely given space. Selected works will be presented in a group exhibition at Fotografiska Stockholm in autumn 2026. Curated by Fotografiska's Johan Vikner and Sofia Liljergren. Free to enter.
