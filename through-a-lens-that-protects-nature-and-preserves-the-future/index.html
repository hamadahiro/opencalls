Through a Lens that Protects Nature and Preserves the Future

Prize 15,000 cash per category (currency unnamed)

HIPA runs this competition with the Dubai Environment and Climate Change Authority, inviting photographers from around the world to photograph the natural heritage of the United Arab Emirates. There are four categories. Wildlife covers animals, birds, insects and wild plants in their natural habitats; Natural Landscapes covers mountains, deserts, forests, valleys, coastlines and other natural scenery and phenomena; Underwater World covers marine life, coral reefs, aquatic species and underwater landscapes in the UAE's coastal and marine waters; and Nature Reimagined With AI asks entrants to rework an original nature photograph through artificial intelligence, with the result still showing a recognisable connection to the UAE. Every photograph entered in the three photographic categories must have been captured in the UAE. Entry is free and open to individuals aged 18 and over anywhere in the world, one photo per category and four entries at most. Files must be JPEG, at least 5 MB, no less than 2000 pixels on the longest edge and 300 dpi, carrying no signature, border, watermark or logo; phone and drone photographs are allowed. Basic technical editing is acceptable, but compositing, creative retouching and any manipulation used to create illusions or deceptions is prohibited outside the AI category. The prize table pays 15,000 / 10,000 / 5,000 for first, second and third place in each of Wildlife, Natural Landscapes and Underwater World and 10,000 for first place in the AI category; HIPA publishes those figures without naming a currency. Entrants are asked to follow a published environmental photography ethics code: keep a safe distance from wildlife and never chase or frighten it, do not handle, feed or relocate living things or interfere with nests, breeding sites or resting areas, leave plants and rocks where they are, stay on designated trails, obtain any permits required for protected and marine areas, avoid lighting or sound that alters natural behaviour, and fly drones only under the permits and in the areas allowed in Dubai and the UAE. Submissions opened on 21 August 2026 and close at 24:00 UAE time on 2 September.
