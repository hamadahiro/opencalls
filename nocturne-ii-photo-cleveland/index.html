Nocturne II

Prize Group exhibition at Kindrid Kava

Photo Cleveland's second annual Halloween-season show, seeking photography, video and mixed media "of the macabre, from the shadows, the ghastly and the grim". Selected work hangs at Kindrid Kava in Cleveland, with an opening on Saturday, October 10 from 6-10 PM that includes an experimental noise performance by In Verse; the show is free to the public and work is collected back November 8-10. Entry is $35 for up to three photo and/or mixed-media works or $20 for two videos, with a mix of the two capped at three submissions for $35; artists who were in Photo Cleveland's Intentional Unconventional show pay $25 total, though the guidelines state there is no discount on the two-video entry. Accepted prints must be framed and ready to hang with wire or sawtooth hangers, with a minimum printed image size of 11x14 inches and a maximum frame size of 24x36; videos are digitised for projection, so there is nothing to deliver. Because this is an all-ages event the organisers will not accept work containing nudity, and they do not accept work on religious or political themes. Shipped work is accepted with a $10 handling fee and a self-addressed prepaid return label. Photo Cleveland takes a 25% commission on sales, which it says is reinvested in its exhibitions and operating costs, and the $35 fee covers gallery rental, curation, installation and advertising. On opening night attendees vote for a single People's Choice Award, which carries a free entry to a future Photo Cleveland show and a feature on the organisation's website. Notifications go out by midnight on September 24 and work is dropped off October 4-6.
