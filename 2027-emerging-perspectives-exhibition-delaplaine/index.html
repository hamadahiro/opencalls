2027 Emerging Perspectives Exhibition

Prizes $500 $250 Exhibition

The Delaplaine Arts Center's annual juried exhibition for young adult artists aged 18 to 35, created around 2021 after the centre noticed a gap in participation from that age group, and open nationally. There are no theme, size or media requirements or restrictions, and photography has shown here before - the 2026 line-up included a silver gelatin print. Work must be original in design and personal execution, made within the past two years and not previously displayed at the Delaplaine; no commercially made prints, giclees or reproductions are considered. The Delaplaine's exhibition-wide AI guidelines apply: work that is solely AI-generated is not considered, AI-assisted work is eligible where the artist's input clearly dominates, and any use of AI must be fully disclosed. Judging runs in two rounds - a remote first round through the online portal that decides acceptance, then in-person judging for the Grand Prize after installation - with the People's Choice decided by visitor ballot through the run of the show. The 2027 juror is Sarah Hempel Irani, a Frederick sculptor working in clay, bronze and stone who teaches sculpture at Frederick Community College and made the Claire McCardell statue on Carroll Creek. Awards are $500 for first place and $250 for the People's Choice. Up to three works may be entered; accepted artwork is delivered 22–28 March 2027 and the exhibition runs 3–25 April 2027, with the juror's talk and award announcement on 3 April. Wall-hanging work need not be framed but must be wired to a professional standard (no sawtooth hangers). Artists are not required to offer work for sale; on a sale the Delaplaine retains 30 percent, or 20 percent for members.
