Off the Wall 2026

Prize Exhibition

Since 2015 ARTS Southeast's affordable small works exhibition has been an annual tradition in Savannah's Starland District, presenting eclectic, collectible work by members of the local arts community. Off the Wall is a salon-style show of small works in all media in which pieces can be taken directly off the wall after purchase, so locals and out-of-town visitors can bring home or gift their purchases right away; running through the holiday gifting season, this year's edition will evolve over the course of its run into the new year, with works added to the wall as other works sell. Artists may submit up to four images for consideration and will be notified of first-round acceptances; those whose work sells may be invited to display more, and the organisers encourage multiples, iterative works and series. ARTS Southeast takes no commission on sales - patrons are directed to contact artists directly and artists receive 100% of the sale price, with the 501(c)(3) noting only that a 30% donation would be greatly appreciated - and promotes selected work through its website, social media, a press release and its newsletter. Every piece must be for sale at a retail price between $25 and $300 and must be 30 inches or less in every direction; work may be in any medium, framing is not required and alternative presentations of 2D work are welcome, but framed pieces should arrive wired and ready to hang with D-rings rather than sawtooth hangers. The call is open to current ARTS Southeast Supporters, a membership bought outright with tiers starting at $25 (Student Supporter) and $50 (Artist Supporter). The show is on display 22 October 2026 to 16 January 2027 with an opening reception on Friday 6 November, artwork drop-off 15-18 October during gallery hours and pickup on 17 January 2027; the gallery is closed to the public from 13 December 2026 to 13 January 2027.
