KH7 Artspace — Exhibitions 2026

Prizes Exhibition prize Artist fee prize PR support prize

KH7 Artspace is a contemporary art space in Aarhus, Denmark. The annual exhibitions open call selects programming for the second half of 2026 across the gallery's two spaces.

Available slots: December at KH7Artspace (the main venue) plus September and November at KH7Smallspace. Each slot is for a solo or group exhibition.

All artistic disciplines are welcome including photography. Selected artists receive a small fee and PR support from the gallery. Applicants from outside Denmark must cover their own transport and visa expenses.

Submission is by email — a project description (max 2 A4 pages with images). Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 29; results announced April 13, 2026.
