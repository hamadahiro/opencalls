KH7 Artspace — Exhibitions 2026

Open call for solo or group exhibition proposals at KH7 Artspace in Aarhus, Denmark for the second half of 2026. Available slots: December at KH7Artspace, September and November at KH7Smallspace. All artistic disciplines welcome including photography. Artists receive a small fee and PR support. Applicants from outside Denmark must cover their own transport and visa expenses. Submit project description (max 2 A4 pages with images) via email.

Organized by KH7 Artspace
