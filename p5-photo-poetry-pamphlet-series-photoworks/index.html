P5: Photo Poetry Pamphlet Series — Volumes 5 and 6

Photoworks, the Brighton-based charity with a national remit for photography in England, is looking for two projects to become volumes 5 and 6 of P5, its photo-poetry pamphlet series developed with David Solo and designed by the studio Jane & Jeremy. The series launched in 2025; its first four volumes each pair photography with poetry on the page, and the two new volumes are scheduled to launch in spring 2027. What Photoworks wants is existing, unpublished work in which text and image genuinely act on each other: the call states it is interested in projects where the relationship between the two goes beyond illustration or caption, and submissions are assessed on the strength of both the photography and the writing, how they combine to create layered meanings and further readings, and how the work would translate into the pamphlet form through sequencing, layout and visual presentation. Artists working across photography and poetry may apply individually or collaboratively, and an applicant must be the creator or co-creator of both the photographs and the text, with everything original and the text predominantly in English. Each of the two selected pamphlets is produced in full colour at roughly 6 x 9 inches, 32 to 48 pages, in what the organisation calls a substantial print run, and sold through the Photoworks online shop, selected stockists and related events. Each selected project carries a GBP 1,000 artist fee, 15 complimentary copies, the chance to develop the publication with Jane & Jeremy, and promotion and programming support. Entry is free: the Microsoft Forms application has eight questions and no payment step. It asks for artist name or names, a contact email address, city and country of residence, a biography of 200 words or less per artist, a project description of 600 words or less, a 400-word answer on why the work suits publication in the series, and a link to a draft manuscript or example of the proposed work hosted on a file-sharing service with viewing permission set to anyone with the link - Photoworks states it cannot consider submissions it cannot open. Work that is not yet finished is allowed if the link shows at least 12 pages of images and text and the form gives an estimated completion date. The call is explicitly international and open to artists at all career stages regardless of experience, affiliation or status; applicants must be 18 or over. An online information session with the Photoworks team, David Solo and Siddharth Khajuria, a previous P5 awardee, runs on Wednesday 2 December from 4pm to 5pm and is booked through Eventbrite. The deadline is midnight GMT on Wednesday 27 January 2027.
