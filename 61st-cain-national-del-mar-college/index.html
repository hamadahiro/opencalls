61st CAIN National

Prize $10,000 in purchase awards

An open-theme, all-media national juried exhibition at Del Mar College's Joseph A. Cain Memorial Gallery in Corpus Christi, founded in 1966 and formerly the Annual National Drawing & Small Sculpture Show — renamed to reflect a broader range of media. Photography is named among the accepted 2-D categories alongside drawing, painting, printmaking in traditional media and original watercolor; sculpture of any media is also considered, though nothing requiring ceiling suspension. $10,000 in Purchase Awards are decided by the juror and the college art faculty, and purchased work joins Del Mar's permanent collection. This year's juror is Jamie Bates, a ceramic artist and Associate Professor of Ceramics at the University of Oklahoma, who also gives a free public art talk on February 18. Open to artists residing in the United States, excluding full-time Del Mar art faculty; work must have been created in the last three years and must not have been shown previously in the Annual Show. Each artist may submit three works, one image per artwork. Entries open on CaFÉ on September 1, 2026 and close November 30, 2026; notification goes out January 8, 2027, accepted work must arrive by January 30, and the exhibition runs February 19 – April 19, 2027. Work does not have to be for sale — the gallery publishes a price list and connects artists with buyers but takes no other part in sales — and artists are responsible for all return shipping costs.
