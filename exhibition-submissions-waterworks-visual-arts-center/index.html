Prize Exhibition

Waterworks Visual Arts Center in Salisbury, North Carolina is a non-collecting art museum accredited by the American Alliance of Museums, and it keeps a standing portfolio-review route into its exhibition programme. Each year the museum mounts three major rotating exhibitions of contemporary fine art and craft by local, regional, national and international artists, plus an annual outdoor sculpture show, and its past programme has repeatedly given solo shows to photographers. Submissions are read twice a year, after deadlines on 30 September and 30 January, and are reviewed within sixty to ninety days; the museum is clear that this is review only and not a guarantee of a show. Because the exhibition calendar is planned eighteen to twenty-four months ahead, a successful submission buys real time to prepare or make new work. Applicants send a current CV detailing education, grants, fellowships, awards, teaching, gallery affiliations, major exhibitions and collections, an artist statement written specifically about the body of work submitted and its influences, research, media and concepts, and ten to twenty high-quality images of current work that is available to exhibit, each labelled with title, medium, size, weight and completion date. All work must have been made within the last five years. Everything goes through the museum's SlideRoom portal.
