49th Annual Photography Exhibit

Prizes $250 Best of Show $150 category awards

The Wassenberg Art Center's annual photography exhibition, now in its 49th year, hung in the centre's galleries in Van Wert, Ohio. Entries are juried across six subject categories — Nature (floral, wildlife, botanicals), Portraiture (including photojournalism), Architecture, Landscape (natural only), Abstract/Macro, and Film — with no more than two photographs per category and ten in total per photographer. The Film category asks for traditional analogue work and proof of negative may be requested. Awards run to $250 for Best of Show in Colour and another $250 for Best of Show in Black and White, plus seven $150 excellence awards, one per category and one for Youth Best of Show; the funds are administered by the Van Wert County Foundation. This is a physical, in-person exhibition: accepted work must reach the art center by the delivery deadline of 6 September 2026, and photographers outside driving distance may ship at their own cost both ways. Sales are handled by the art center, which takes a 30% commission. Work previously exhibited at the Wassenberg is not eligible, and no name, logo or title may be visible on the front of a print. Entries close 21 August 2026, jurying is announced 26 August, and the show runs 10 September to 8 October 2026.
