Cantua Editions Open Call for Submissions

Prize Publication

Annual submissions call from Cantua Editions, an independent artist-run publishing house in New Jersey producing limited-edition hand-bound photobooks and ephemera, with publications held by MIT's Rotch Library and Harvard's Fine Arts Library. Seeking primarily photo-based projects exploring how landscapes, environments, and communities shape one another; artists working with archives and historical documentation are also welcome, as are themes of memory, ecology, and preservation. Send a short artist bio, a project statement (max 500 words), and 20–30 images as a single PDF to books.cantuaeditions@gmail.com with the subject line 'Submission – [Your Name]'. Chosen artists are invited to a preliminary meeting toward a publication.

DeadlineSeptember 1, 2026
Entry feeFree
