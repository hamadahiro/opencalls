The Silent Cut: Moments in Motion

Juried exhibition at Fort Works Art, a contemporary gallery in Fort Worth, on cinematic ideas within still images — moments just before or after an action, sequencing that alters meaning, repetition, looping and compressed time. Emphasis on photography while open to all mediums; staged or documentary, sequential or singular. Selected works hang in an 11-week exhibition (October 25, 2026 – January 9, 2027) timed with the Lone Star Film Festival, with an opening reception. Open to artists residing in the United States; shipped works must arrive by October 17. Sales are split 50/50 with the gallery. One caution: the organiser now publishes two closing dates — the CaFÉ portal that takes the entries is set to close 19 September 2026, while the prospectus text reads “Application Dates: July 21, 2026 – September 26, 2026”. The earlier, portal-enforced date is the one stored here.
