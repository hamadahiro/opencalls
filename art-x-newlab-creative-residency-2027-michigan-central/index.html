Art x Newlab Creative Residency 2027

Prizes $30,000 Residency

The fourth year of the Michigan Central Art x Newlab Creative Residency, a twelve-week research-based fellowship in Detroit's innovation district run in partnership with Newlab and Henry Ford Health with funds from the John S. and James L. Knight Foundation. Fellows are selected across two tracks: Art & Tech, with Newlab and Knight Foundation support, for practices at the edge of technology, data visualisation, wearable tech, accessibility and sustainability; and Art & Health, with Henry Ford Health, for artists working across wellness, care and health technology. Each fellow-in-residence receives a $30,000 prize to advance their project, access to Newlab Detroit's fabrication shops and to facilities across Michigan Central and Henry Ford Health, project-aligned research opportunities, a place in a public conversation series, and external mentors who both support the selection review and give ongoing guidance. Two sessions frame the on-site time and applicants rank a preference: 8 February to 30 April 2027, and 17 May to 6 August 2027. Michigan Central's art-programme page describes the residency as foregrounding emerging and mid-career artists based primarily in metro Detroit, and the form asks whether you are a current resident of south-eastern Michigan, but the call invites artists, creatives, researchers and makers "across disciplines and geographies"; the stated requirements are to be over 25, to apply as a single person or entity rather than a group or collaboration, and to be eligible to receive taxable income via an SSN, ITIN or EIN. Photography is one of the seven categories the organiser lists on its own Artwork Archive posting, alongside craft, drawing, film/video/new media, mixed media, painting and sculpture. Applying is free through Michigan Central's Submittable and the organiser has deliberately shortened it, suggesting no more than five hours in total: a project title, a one-sentence and a two-paragraph description with goals, community engagement and expected outcomes, a focus-area paragraph, a needs paragraph on equipment and technical assistance, a high-level budget for the $30,000, and up to five work samples with credit lines. A panel of experts across art, technology and health scores clarity and originality of the idea, alignment with the track themes, feasibility within twelve weeks, and potential for impact and innovation. Past fellows-in-residence include Simon Anton, Ash Arder, Michael Candy, Osman Khan, Salam Rida, Wes Taylor, Sabrina Nelson and Chloe Pascal Crawford. A virtual open house runs on 8 October 2026, and accessibility needs can be raised with the residency team.
