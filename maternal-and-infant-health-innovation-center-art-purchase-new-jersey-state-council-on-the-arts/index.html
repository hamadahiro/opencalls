Maternal and Infant Health Innovation Center — Art Purchase for Mercer County Artists

Prize $1,000 purchase

The State of New Jersey is buying already-finished two-dimensional work from Mercer County artists to hang in the Maternal and Infant Health Innovation Center, a newly built facility at 1 Pennington Avenue in Trenton's Capital Complex and the home of the New Jersey Maternal and Infant Health Innovation Authority — the first state authority in the country dedicated to maternal and infant health outcomes and to racial and ethnic disparities in care. Photography is one of five media named in the call and one of the five the community survey asked for, alongside painting, drawing, printmaking and mixed media; the survey also reported appetite for palettes from bold to muted, for work from abstract to representational, and most of all for images of people and cityscapes, with interest in landscapes, animals and work that reflects what the centre does. There is no preferred style. The total purchase budget is up to $50,000, spread across multiple artists, and no single piece may be priced above $1,000 all-inclusive — anything priced higher is not considered. Free to apply, up to four images of completed, ready-to-install work, framed and wired, fragile materials behind glass or plexiglas, nothing larger than 42 inches in any direction including the frame (a diptych or triptych counts as one work and must fit the same limit). Work that is political, violent or sexual is out, as the building is a public state office. An Art Selection Committee of community members, state workers and arts experts shortlists from the submitted images, then views the shortlisted works in person before deciding; not every shortlisted work is bought, and artists collect unsold work after the viewing. Statuses are communicated by the end of March 2027. Open to New Jersey residents 18 or older who live in Mercer County or keep a permanent studio address there; State of New Jersey employees are barred by statute. A Zoom project overview and Q&A runs on Friday 11 December 2026, 12-1pm, and written questions close 18 December 2026.
