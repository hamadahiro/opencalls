see-zeen Issue 21 Open Call

Prize Publication

The twenty-first issue of an independent online photography magazine run out of New York and Berlin, open to any kind of photographic expression — portrait, landscape, documentary, fashion, abstract and anything else — with no theme set and no entry fee. Anyone over 18, anywhere in the world, may submit a series of six to fifteen images; the magazine asks for a project statement of up to 300 words, a work description of up to 100 words, a short bio with an email address, and website and Instagram links if you have them. Images go in as single JPG files, 2500 pixels maximum on the longest side, 72dpi in sRGB, each filename carrying the photographer's name and project title. Images and text files go together into a Dropbox file request; the magazine asks you to press Upload in Dropbox and then Send on the form at see-zeen.com. Copyright always remains with the photographer; by submitting you agree that see-zeen may publish and distribute the images on its website, social media and other media in connection with its own brand, and that the editors may crop images and make curatorial decisions. Everything submitted is also considered for the magazine's Instagram. The editors say plainly that they are overwhelmed with submissions and cannot contact photographers whose work is not selected, so there is no notification date. The Issue 21 deadline is October 18, 2026.
