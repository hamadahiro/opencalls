36th Annual All Florida Juried Art Show

Prize $1,000 in cash awards

MartinArts' statewide juried survey, in its 36th year, exhibits January 8 – March 10, 2027 at the Court House Cultural Center in Stuart, Florida. All media are accepted, with photography expressly permitted — it is the only medium in which giclées or editions may be entered. Juried by independent curator Lisa Rockford (Rockford Projects), with $500, $200 and $100 prizes plus up to four $50 honorable mentions. Open to professional artists across Florida.
