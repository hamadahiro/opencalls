Montgomery Photo Festival 2026

Prize $3,500 prize

Montgomery Photo Festival is an annual photography festival held in Montgomery, Alabama. The 2026 edition is the festival's eighth cycle.

The structure runs across four process and approach categories: Color, Monochromatic, Alternative Process, and Manipulation. There is no thematic restriction — subject matter is open. Selection is by category type rather than by topic.

Selection is by Carol McCusker. Total prize pool is $3,500. Selected work is shown in the festival's exhibition program in Montgomery.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through ArtCall with up to 3 images. Fee is $35. The 2026 deadline was April 17; results announced May 22, 2026.
