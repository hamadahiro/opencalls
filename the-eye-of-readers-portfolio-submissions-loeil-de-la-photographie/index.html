The Eye of Readers — Portfolio Submissions

Every weekend L'Œil de la Photographie — published in English as The Eye of Photography — gives an issue over to its readers, publishing a selection of portfolios sent in from around the world. The magazine publishes daily in both English and French out of Paris, and the readers' section sits alongside its regular coverage of exhibitions, festivals, books and photographers. The magazine states the section is open to amateurs and professionals alike. Submission is free and rolling: there is no deadline and no entry fee, and selected portfolios are published on the magazine's site with the artist's text and a link to their website. What is asked is a coherent series of 12 to 15 photographs and a text about the series, with a title and, if there is one, a website link; English and French versions of the text are appreciated. Files must be jpg or jpeg, at least 1350 pixels wide for horizontals and 1200 pixels high for verticals, between 150KB and 2MB each, named series-title-lastname-firstname-photonumber.jpg. The magazine is blunt that the specification is a filter in itself: any submission that does not strictly follow these guidelines will not be considered. Entries go through a JotForm the magazine links from its own guidelines page; there is no selection fee, no publication fee and no membership requirement — the paid Premium membership on the same site buys archive access to readers, and is unrelated to submitting.
