Haus-a-rest — Issue 76: Labour, Repetition and Print

Prize Publication

UK-based independent art zine, edited by Jenna Fox and Nichola Rodgers, calling for visual art and writing on labour, repetition and print. Print is framed here both as a medium and as a concept — "a mark left through pressure, contact, repetition or reproduction" — so a print can be an impression, a trace, a copy, a record, a pattern, a surface or evidence of an action. The editors want work on repetitive labour and routine; manual, industrial and domestic work; invisible, unpaid and care work; the process of making and skilled labour; printmaking, stamping, copying and seriality; digital reproduction and the circulation of images; and repetition as ritual, habit or compulsion. They ask what it means to make something by hand in an automated, digitally reproduced world, and how repetition might become a strategy for resistance, survival, memory or transformation. ALL MEDIA WELCOME, with photography named explicitly in the list alongside painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, installation, text, film, video, animation, digital art, sound, performance, textile, ceramics, collage and written work; they particularly welcome work that challenges conventional definitions of what constitutes a print, an artwork, or an act of labour. Open internationally to emerging, mid-career and established artists, and explicitly to students, recent graduates and self-taught artists. Free to enter and free if selected. Submit one to three artworks with a statement of up to 300 words explaining how the work connects to the theme; writers may send prose up to 1,000 words or up to three poems. An Instagram handle is a stated requirement — the editors will not select artists without one, because selected work is tagged across their 11,000-follower platform. There is no cash prize: selected works appear in a curated online group exhibition on haus-a-rest.com and across the zine's social channels, typically online for about six months, and artists retain all rights.
