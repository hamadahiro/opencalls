2027 Foto Forum Santa Fe Photography Award

Prizes $1,000 $500 shipping Solo exhibition

Foto Forum Santa Fe's annual international award is built around a solo exhibition rather than a group hang. The first-place photographer receives a two-month solo show in the gallery's space in the Railyard Art District, down the street from SITE Santa Fe, a $1,000 cash prize, and a $500 stipend towards delivering the work to the gallery and having it returned. The 2027 juror is Leigh Ledare, whose work on agency, intimacy and consent is held by the Museum of Modern Art, the Art Institute of Chicago, MOCA Los Angeles, the Centre Pompidou and Fotomuseum Winterthur, and who held a Guggenheim Fellowship in 2017. The call is open internationally to emerging and established artists. Photographers submit up to twelve photographs from a related body of work that is ready for exhibition, each file between 1 MB and 2 MB. The organisation asks for a cohesive selection rather than a survey of everything an applicant makes, and suggests the artist statement explain how the twelve images relate to each other. Video submissions are not accepted, though video can form part of the resulting exhibition; applicants with video work may submit stills and describe the piece in the statement. The winner then works with executive director Sage Paisner to curate the final show, which may include work not submitted with the application. Entries are read by the outside juror and by Paisner as in-house curator. The organisation notes that artists who entered without winning have been added to the gallery roster. Past winners are Jared Ragland, Federico Estol, Rebecca Moseman, Ciro Battiloro, Jerry Takigawa and Renee Romero. Jury selection begins 15 March 2027, results are announced and the winner contacted on 18 April 2027, and the solo exhibition opens with a reception on 4 June 2027. The entry fee rises in stages: $40 from the opening on 10 November 2026 until 22 January 2027, $50 from 23 January to 24 February, and $60 from 25 February until the 4 March close at midnight Mountain Time. Students, seniors and members can email the gallery for a 20% discount coupon. Foto Forum Santa Fe is a non-profit space programming exhibitions, workshops, visiting artist lectures and community outreach around photography, storytelling, science and technology.
