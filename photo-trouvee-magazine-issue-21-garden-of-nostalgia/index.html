Photo Trouvée Magazine Issue 21 — Garden of Nostalgia

Prize Publication (print)

Print Issue 21 open call themed 'Garden of Nostalgia' for artists who work with or are inspired by vintage found or family photographs — florals, plants, connections to nature, or reflections on roots. Any media accepted. Selected artists receive a designated page spread with artist statement, a complimentary digital issue, and website, newsletter and social media features. The $40 publishing fee (due late October if selected) funds printing, design and a scholarship fund. International submissions welcome.

DeadlineOctober 10, 2026
ResultsLate October 2026
Entry feeFree to submit; $40 if selected
