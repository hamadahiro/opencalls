2028 Bear Gallery Exhibition

Fairbanks Arts Association's annual portfolio call for its Bear Gallery, which programmes two exhibitions a month year-round by Alaskan artists and groups working across media, for local and international visitors. Artists submit a cohesive portfolio rather than a themed entry and may propose a solo show, a group show of two or more artists without a curator, or a curated exhibition where a curator sets the theme and selects the work; proposals for curated juried exhibitions are not accepted. Open to Alaska residents 18 and over who live in the state at least six months of the year, with an individual or group eligible for a Bear Gallery show once every four years. Submit 7–10 work samples made in the last five years, up to three of which may be video, with signatures and identifying names removed so a panel of professional Alaskan artists can review the applications blind. Entry is $35, or $30 for Fairbanks Arts members at the Artist level and above, and AI-generated imagery may not appear in exhibited work. Applications open 1 August 2026 and close at 9:59 pm Alaska time rather than end of day; applicants are notified in early 2027 for exhibitions in 2028.
