Roadblocks

Prize Group exhibition

Studio Montclair, a nationwide nonprofit of exhibiting artists based in Montclair, New Jersey, invites work for Roadblocks, an exhibition about barriers both tangible and abstract. The gallery writes that roadblocks "come in many forms — physical, cultural, societal and psychological" and that these barriers often hinder connection, understanding and progress; artists are asked to examine how such roadblocks are constructed, perceived and sometimes dismantled. All mediums are accepted, including video and installation. The jurors are Debbie Silberberg, MPA, an assemblage and fibre artist, guest curator at the Rockefeller Gallery and a member of the Rockland County Art in Public Places Committee, and Lisa D'Amico, PhD, a painter and mixed-media artist, head curator at the Rockefeller Gallery, a member of the same committee and an advisory board member of the Hopper House Museum. The show hangs in Studio Montclair's Leach Gallery at 641 Bloomfield Avenue. Entry is free for Studio Montclair members and $42 for non-members, for a maximum of three works. Work must have been completed within the past five years, must not have been shown in any previous Studio Montclair exhibition, and giclée reproductions of original art are not accepted.
