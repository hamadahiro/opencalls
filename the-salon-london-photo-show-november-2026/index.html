The Salon — London Photo Show November 2026

Prize Exhibition

Open call for The Salon, a juried group exhibition at Hoxton Arches in London, November 26–29, 2026. Selected images are professionally printed (10×8 inches with a white border) and displayed with the photographer's name in the gallery. Open to photographers at all experience levels, whether or not they have exhibited before. Standout entries receive partner prizes from Think Tank, 36exp and Point 101, including a large-format archival Hahnemühle print and future exhibition space. Submit 1, 3 or 5 images (£35 / £90 / £125) via the entry form; upload instructions follow by email.
