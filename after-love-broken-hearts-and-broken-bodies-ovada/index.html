After Love: Broken Hearts & Broken Bodies

Group exhibition at OVADA's warehouse gallery in central Oxford inviting artists to respond to romantic heartbreak, relationship breakdown and the strategies we use to survive love's endings. The show is part of a four-century historical study of heartbreak as an embodied emotion by Dr Sally Holloway, funded by an Arts & Humanities Research Council fellowship, with partners including Oxfordshire Mind and the Poetry Pharmacy. OVADA calls it a radically inclusive exhibition where all eligible works will be shown - there is no jury - and the hang is arranged salon style by Audrey Baldwin and Sally Holloway as work arrives on install day. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside painting, drawing, mixed media, collage, textiles, printmaking, ceramics, sculpture and video; work must be wall-hung or free-standing and no larger than 2m x 2m. Up to three works per artist. Open to artists 18 and over resident in the UK; work must be delivered in person on Saturday 3 October and collected on 18 or 19 October, as the gallery cannot accept posted submissions. Images of exhibited work also appear in an ArtSteps digital version of the show. The exhibition runs 7-18 October 2026, with a launch on the evening of 7 October.
