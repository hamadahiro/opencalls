Group Book — Connectedness

Prize Publication

Pomegranate Press is an independent publishing house based in New York City - once Richmond, Virginia - which has since 2015 specialised in showcasing contemporary photography and other mediums of visual art through limited-edition, affordable publications and ephemera. For the first time in three years the press is hosting an open call for photographs to be included in a new, expansive group book, and it expects to accept approximately fifty people. The loose theme is Connectedness, and photographers are asked to send three to six photographs in any relation to that idea; you may include as much or as little about yourself and the work as you like. There is no fee to submit. Submissions go by email to pommesubmissions@gmail.com with the subject line '(Your Name) Group Book Submission'; the press asks that images not be sent as expiring links and encourages low-resolution files, since the press is overseeing every submission itself. Submissions are open until 14 September 2026, with accepted photographers notified by mid-October at the latest, and the book is due out in early 2027. The three previous Pomegranate group books were Playfulness, Nothing Left but Healing, and You, Me, and the Moments; the Playfulness volume, made with Balam, ran to 116 pages at 8 by 10 inches, Swiss bound with embossed back-cover text, in a first edition of 500 copies printed in Argentina and featuring around seventy photographers. The press frames this call as a response to print and publication becoming harder to access, hoping the space will give emerging artists an outlet.
