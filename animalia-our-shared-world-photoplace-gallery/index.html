Animalia: Our Shared World

PhotoPlace Gallery's January call for photographs about the diversity of animal life — "From beloved companions and backyard visitors to wildlife, birds, insects, reptiles, amphibians, marine life, and the countless creatures that inhabit our planet". The gallery asks for work that explores "the beauty, character, behavior, and relationships of animals in all their forms", whether that is life in the wild, intimate moments with domestic animals, or the tangle of relationships between animals, people and the natural world, and states outright that "All photographic genres and processes are encouraged." THE JUROR is Wendi Schneider, the American artist behind States of Grace, an ongoing series begun in 2012 of hand-gilded photographs printed on translucent vellum or Japanese kozo and gilded on the verso with precious metals; her work is held at the Museum of Fine Arts, Houston, the Center for Creative Photography, the New Orleans Museum of Art, the Royal Photographic Society, the Memphis Brooks Museum of Art, the Asheville Art Museum and Auburn University Libraries Special Collections, and she sits on the Advisory Council of the Colorado Photographic Arts Center. WHAT GETS TAKEN: up to 35 photographs for the Middlebury, Vermont gallery and roughly 40 more for the Online Gallery, and the gallery is explicit that the two tiers are treated the same — "Online Gallery selections receive the same catalog inclusion, permanent website archive, and promotional opportunities as Exhibition Gallery selections." Every selected artist gets the exhibition catalogue, permanent placement on the PhotoPlace site, promotion through the gallery's channels with a link back to their own website, and eligibility for exhibition awards; the gallery's own page does not put a figure on those awards. THE DATES: entries opened 6 October 2026, selections are announced 23 November 2026, prints for the Middlebury gallery are due 18 December 2026, and the show hangs 1–30 January 2027 with an opening reception on Friday 1 January, 4–7 pm. BEYOND THE FEE this is a print-and-ship show for the 35 gallery selections; the gallery runs in-house printing and display services that international artists are told they may use "to reduce shipping expenses", and notes international return shipping "may involve additional costs". JURYING IS BLIND — files are renamed and jurors see no names. Submissions are open worldwide: "We warmly welcome submissions from photographers around the world." Work accepted into a previous PhotoPlace exhibition may not be resubmitted for three years from that show's opening date, and there is no cap on how many images one photographer may enter; files are JPG, max 5 MB, about 2100 px on the long side. There is no cap on entries: the gallery states “there is no limit to the number of images you may submit”, so the $39 covers the first five and every image after that costs $6.
