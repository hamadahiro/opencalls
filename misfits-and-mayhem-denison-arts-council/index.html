Misfits & Mayhem

Prize Group exhibition

The Denison Arts Council closes its exhibition year at 517 Gallery in the downtown Denison Cultural Arts District in north Texas with the one show on its calendar that has no theme at all. Misfits & Mayhem opens the doors to “the artists and artworks which didn’t quite mesh with the themes of our previous shows” — the council writes that any size, medium, subject matter and style is welcome, and pitches it as a chance for emerging artists to test the waters and for established artists to show something they haven’t previously had the chance to show. Applications are made through a Google Form linked from the call page, which states plainly: “There is no fee for submission or participation in the exhibition.” The gallery keeps a 20% commission on work sold. Artists may enter 2-D wall pieces or prints, with limited shelf and pedestal space for 3-D work, plus small low-priced pieces for the gallery boutique. Work must arrive wired and ready to hang — sawtooth hangers are not accepted, though staff will wire a piece for $10 — and must be the artist’s own original or a reproduction of their own work; AI-generated images are not eligible. Selected artists complete a W-9. The gallery committee responds on 16 October, work is dropped off on 8 November, and the exhibition opens with a reception on 14 November from 4 to 6 pm, has a midway reception on 12 December, and closes on 3 January 2027 when artists collect their work.
