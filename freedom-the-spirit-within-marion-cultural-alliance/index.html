Freedom: The Spirit Within

Prizes Cash awards Exhibition

Marion Cultural Alliance's September 2026 juried exhibition, presented, curated and juried by Lisa Russo (M.F.A., M.Ed.), asks artists what freedom means to them — personal journeys, creative expression, relationships, beliefs, memories and everyday life. Work responding to America's 250th anniversary and the 25th anniversary of September 11 is welcome but the theme is deliberately wider, taking in self-expression, resilience, identity, hope, possibility, remembrance and the enduring human spirit. The call is open to local and regional artists in all media, with photography named alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics, fibre art, mixed media, digital art and fine craft; MCA membership is not required to enter. Each artist may submit up to two original pieces, no giclées or reproductions, suggested size up to 50 inches in any direction, and only work that engages the theme will be accepted. Cash and ribbon awards go to Best of Show, which carries the cash prize, with ribbons for first through fifth place and honourable mention. All exhibited work must be for sale, with proceeds split 70% to the artist and 30% to MCA, whose share funds its arts programming in Ocala and Marion County. Alongside the show, guest curator Lisa Russo runs a hands-on classical drawing workshop, Draw Like the Masters, on Saturday 19 September 2026 — a separate, optional $75 booking. Entries are submitted digitally through ArtCall by midnight on Sunday 16 August 2026; no physical or mailed submissions are accepted.
