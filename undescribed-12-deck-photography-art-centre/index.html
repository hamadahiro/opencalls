UNDESCRIBED #12

Prizes S$1,100 support Mentorship Group exhibition

DECK Photography Art Centre describes itself as a non-profit organisation promoting contemporary photography arts and is the organiser of the Singapore International Photography Festival; UNDESCRIBED is its emerging-artist programme, now in its 12th edition. DECK is explicit that this is not simply an exhibition opportunity: the emphasis is on the process of developing a body of work over several months of consultations, critiques, studio visits and conversations with practising artists and arts professionals, with guest artists, curators and gallerists brought in for closed-door feedback sessions. Two mentors accompany the cohort — Robert Zhao Renhui, who has been part of the programme since 2016 and represented Singapore at the 60th Venice Biennale in 2024, and Alecia Neo, whose socially engaged practice foregrounds collaboration, care and radical hospitality rather than the production of objects. The programme culminates in a three-month group exhibition at the Guoco Midtown Public Art Wall in Singapore, opening 25 March 2027. Each selected participant gets S$800 in production support towards developing and presenting the work, a S$300 artist allowance, dedicated consultation time with the mentors, access to DECK's public resources, and curatorial, production, communications and programming support from the DECK team, plus entry to ASG, DECK's network of past and future UNDESCRIBED alumni. The gate is narrow and specific: Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents only, graduates of the 2026 cohort from an art school or arts programme, working with photography, moving image or expanded photographic practices — so this is a call for one year's graduating class, not for emerging photographers generally. DECK also asks that applicants be able to commit to the full schedule, which it publishes up front: onboarding 27 October 2026, project development 20 November, studio visit and feedback 12 December, an industry conversation 20 January 2027, critique and production 18 February, exhibition opening 25 March. Two things to note. DECK's page dates the deadline 20 October 2026, 11:59pm SGT, and repeats it in its Key Dates block, but labels that date a Friday — 20 October 2026 is a Tuesday, and every other weekday label in the block is correct, so treat the weekday as the organiser's slip and the date as the deadline, or apply earlier if you want margin. And DECK's page states no application fee anywhere, but the application form is a sign-in-gated Google Form that cannot be read without a Google account, so the fee could not be confirmed at source — assume nothing and check the form when you open it.
