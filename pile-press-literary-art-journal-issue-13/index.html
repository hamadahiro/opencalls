Pile Press Literary + Art Journal, Issue 13

Prize Publication

Pile Press is an alternative publishing collective in Portland, Oregon making local and limited-edition print publications, and its bi-annual literary and arts journal is open for Issue 13. The journal carries poetry, art, short fiction, creative nonfiction, photography and comics by women and non-binary creatives, and submissions are open only to US residents identifying as women and/or non-binary. Photographers and artists may send up to three pieces, at 300 DPI or better. Simultaneous submissions are allowed as long as you tell the editors promptly if the work is taken elsewhere; previously published work is not accepted, though the press does not count social media as formal publication. There is no payment on publication, but contributors get a contributor rate on the journal and the reach of a title stocked in several bookshops around Portland. Entry costs $4 through Submittable and the reading period runs from August 15 to September 25, 2026. The press also runs CHUNK, a black-and-white Oregon-centric broadside paper open to Oregon residents of any gender on a rolling basis.
