Photography Now 2027

Prize Museum exhibition

International photography exhibition at CICA Museum, the contemporary art museum in Gimpo, South Korea, open to artists worldwide. The call spans traditional and digital photography through experimental practice, framed around photography's role as representation, medium, expression and communication. Submission is free by email — up to five JPEGs and/or three video links plus a bio. Selected digital works are printed on A3 foam board and installed by the museum's curators; artists sending physical prints or installation pieces ship at their own cost and are asked to keep 2D work under 1 m on the longest side. Accepted artists contribute $78 USD (110,000 KRW) per work toward show processing and maintenance. The exhibition runs April 7–25, 2027 at the museum.
