PhotoMonth 2026 Open Call — Unity and Community

PhotoMonth, East London's international photography festival (founded in 2001, relaunched in 2025), returns in October 2026 with an open-call group exhibition on the theme 'Unity and Community' — reflecting, questioning and celebrating the relationships that shape communities, identities and collective experience amid a drift toward division and discrimination. Open to all photographers and lens-based artists worldwide, regardless of age or career level, in any photographic style or medium, with experimental and expanded forms encouraged. Free to submit; a participation fee applies only if work is selected. Selected work is shown at The Art Pavilion, Mile End Park, London, 15–25 October 2026, with an opening celebration on 14 October. Curated by Cherelle Sappleton, Tom Hunter, Ishy Pryce-Parchment and Leah Mclaine.
