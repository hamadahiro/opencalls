FLOAT Magazine — Let's Get the F@!#k Outta Here

Prizes Print publication Contributor copy

FLOAT is an independent photography magazine that publishes themed print issues alongside online portfolios, exhibitions, and Instagram takeovers. For this call, the issue "Let's Get the F@!#k Outta Here" invites artists to consider humanity's growing fascination with looking beyond Earth — Mars, UFOs, space travel, and extraterrestrial life — and the search for meaning, possibility, or escape it reflects.

The magazine accepts all photographic styles and looks for cohesive bodies of work. Entry is $9 for 1–10 lens-based images (JPEG, 1200px on the long side), paid through the FLOAT store, with submissions completed via a linked Google form. Selected photographers appear in the printed issue and receive a complimentary copy (shipping paid by the artist); they retain full copyright. The deadline is July 10, 2026 at midnight EST.
