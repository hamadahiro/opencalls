Ray and Nancy Loeschner Art Competition 2026

Prize 2 × $5,000 purchase awards

Frederik Meijer Gardens & Sculpture Park in Grand Rapids, Michigan runs the Ray and Nancy Loeschner Art Competition annually for two-dimensional art and photography inspired by its gardens, grounds and collections. Two purchase awards of $5,000 each are made, one for two-dimensional work and one, running under its own name since 2018, for photography; both winning entries are acquired into the institution's permanent collection. Colour, black and white, analogue film and digital photographs are all accepted, and photographers have taken the photography award every year on record - Cari Povenz in 2025, Annemarie Biernacki in 2024, Sara Kani in 2023, Laurie Tennent in 2022, Lynn Strough in 2021. The competition is open to all artists aged 18 and over. You do not have to visit Meijer Gardens to enter; artists are welcome to work on site but may equally take their inspiration from photographs and videos of the place. Entry is free. There is a two-step process: the first round is digital, with a one-page resume, a one-page artist statement explaining how the work is inspired by Meijer Gardens (required for the work to be juried), and images no smaller than 1024 x 768 at 72 dpi, with the dimensions given - print size for photographic entries. A maximum of three entries per artist is allowed. Second-round finalists deliver or ship their original work to Meijer Gardens for judging, and a finalist in the photography category must have their photograph printed for the judges. Submissions opened on 1 September and close at 11.59pm EST on Tuesday 1 December 2026; finalists are notified in December, the finalists are juried in January 2027 and winners are notified in February 2027. Judging is by a panel of Meijer Gardens professional staff and trustees. Digital artwork is accepted in the two-dimensional category but the competition states that AI use of any kind is not accepted. Winning images may be reproduced on posters and note cards with full credit but without further royalties, and previous winners may not enter again.
