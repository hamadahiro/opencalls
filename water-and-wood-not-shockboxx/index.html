Water & Wood

Not Shockboxx, a contemporary gallery in Hermosa Beach, invites artists to submit work for Water & Wood, a free group show running 16-23 January 2027. Up to five pieces may be entered and not all are guaranteed entrance; owner Jessica Accamando and gallery manager Carin O'Hara curate this first edition. The gallery shows both on its own walls and online on Artsy; this call page does not say which.
