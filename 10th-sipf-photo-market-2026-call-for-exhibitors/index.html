10th SIPF Photo Market 2026 — Call for Exhibitors

Prize Exhibition booth

The Singapore International Photography Festival opens its Photo Market to publishers, artists, collectives and creatives who want to show and sell photobooks, self-published zines, journals, prints, art objects and photography merchandise. The market runs across the opening weekend of the 10th SIPF biennale, October 17–18, 2026, within walking distance of the festival's main exhibition venues and free for the public to enter. Booths include a table and two chairs for up to two people, with a reduced Young Creatives rate for current students and graduates of the past three years; a hardship option can be flagged in the form. The market is curated and applications are assessed on a rolling basis, with no payment due until an application is successful.
