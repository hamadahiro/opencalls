beauty

Prize Exhibition

A Smith Gallery's juried online exhibition on the theme of 'beauty' — grace, charm, refinement, allure, delicacy, elegance. Juror Wendi Schneider, a visual artist whose hand-gilded photographs are printed on vellum and Japanese kozo with precious metals applied to the verso, selects up to 47 images. Selected work is featured across the gallery's online exhibition, Facebook and Instagram, a GalleryTalk, the online store and an exhibition catalogue, with 27 images chosen for 'The 27' hardbound fine-art book. The Juror's Award and the Directors' Award each bring an online solo exhibition, 'The 27' and a feature on the gallery blog; a $100 Visitors' Award goes to the most Instagram likes. Jurying is blind — images are renamed and numbered before the juror sees them. Open to professional and amateur photographers worldwide working in all photographic mediums; international entries are welcomed, work previously exhibited in an A Smith Gallery competition is not eligible, and the gallery states it prefers not to accept images created with artificial intelligence while copyright questions remain unsettled. Entry is $40 for the first 5 images and $5 per additional image with no limit on the number submitted, and photographers entering 13 or more images receive a complimentary review from the gallery directors. Entries are due 28 September 2026, results are emailed 9 October, and the exhibition runs 16 October to 26 November 2026 with a reception on 31 October. The gallery retains 30% on sales.
