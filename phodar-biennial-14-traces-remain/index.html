PHODAR Biennial 14 — Traces Remain

Prizes €2,000 Grand Prize €600 Debut Award €600 Humanistic Photography Award Exhibition Publication

The 14th edition of the PHODAR Biennial, Bulgaria's oldest international photography competition — running since 1999 and organised by the PHODAR Foundation in Sofia, with the Sofia Municipality Culture Programme and exhibitions historically staged at the National Gallery — asks for a coherent series on the theme "Traces Remain". The title is borrowed from the novel by the Bulgarian writer Pavel Vezhinov, and the brief runs through Susan Sontag's On Photography: the photograph as a trace of reality, physically connected to what once stood before the lens, and at the same time a reminder of transience. The organisers' questions are the interesting part — "How do we read the traces of the past in the present? What do abandoned spaces, altered landscapes, family archives and marks on the human body reveal? Which traces do we deliberately preserve, and which do we try to erase? Can a photograph sustain a memory when its story has already been lost?" — and they say the theme is open to any genre that answers them, documentary, portraiture or conceptual alike, including work on social conflict, generational relationships and the transformation of surroundings. Entry is free and there are two sections, each author choosing one: the Grand Prize section and Debut, the latter reserved for authors at the beginning of a career without serious national or international achievements yet. A separate Award for Humanistic Photography goes to one author regardless of section. Submissions are a series or photo essay of 8 to 20 images from an ongoing or completed project, JPEG, minimum 3000 pixels on the longest side at 300 DPI, numbered in the order you want them read; the work has to have been made on or after 1 January 2025, with an exception for long-term projects begun earlier and continued since. Stock photography is not accepted, one series per author, and collaborative series by two or more photographers are explicitly welcome. Entry is open to professional and amateur photographers over 18 with no restriction on country of origin or citizenship. Finalists' prints are produced and mounted at the Foundation's expense for the PHODAR Awards exhibition in Sofia, the prints staying with the Foundation; copyright remains with the photographer throughout, and selected authors are shown in an online winners gallery and published in the edition photobook. Past award winners the Foundation names include Donald Weber (VII Photo Agency) and Mila Teshaieva. Note one disagreement between the organiser's own surfaces: the rules page states a deadline of 1 January 2027 while the submission form at entry.phodar.net prints "DEADLINE IS 31.12.2026" — the earlier, form-stated date is the one to work to, and the rules add that the Foundation may extend without notice. Prizes are paid in euros once the Foundation has received the award funding, with any international payment or conversion charges deducted from the prize.
