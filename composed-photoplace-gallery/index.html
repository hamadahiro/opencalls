Composed

Prizes $500 / $300 / $100 Exhibition Catalog

Juried exhibition at PhotoPlace Gallery in Middlebury, Vermont, in collaboration with the Texas Photographic Society, celebrating the art of photographic composition — work where framing, perspective, light, color, geometry and visual organization are fundamental to the image's impact. Juried by documentary photographer Brian Finke, whose work on the authenticity and absurdity of everyday life has appeared in National Geographic, The New York Times and The New Yorker. Up to 35 images are selected for the gallery exhibition and about 40 more for the online gallery, all appearing in the exhibition catalog and remaining on the gallery website. Open to photographers worldwide; enter up to 12 images. Exhibition runs October 2–31, 2026, with prints due September 18.
