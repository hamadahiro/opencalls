Open Juried Photography Show 2026

Prize $100 / $75 / $50 in each of two categories

Hull Artists' Open Juried Photography Show runs from Friday 9 October to Wednesday 18 November at the Nantasket Beach Resort, a hotel across the street from Nantasket Beach on the Massachusetts coast that the association describes as a tourist and business destination with excellent exposure for the show. The exhibit is non-themed and open to photographers of all ages; membership in Hull Artists Association is not required for entry, though members receive discounted entry fees and a better commission split - 70% to the artist against 60% for non-members. Work is judged in two categories: Black-and-White Photography, which includes any monochromatic hue such as sepia, cyan and brown as well as neutral greys, and Color Photography. Each category awards First Place $100, Second Place $75 and Third Place $50, each with a ribbon, plus ribbons for honourable mentions. The juror is Bobbi Lane. Every piece displayed must be original and derived from the artist's own photograph; the association allows enhancement in programs such as Photoshop and Lightroom, but AI-generated images, stock images and clip art are not allowed, in part or in full. Artists may enter up to six pieces for the juror's consideration, though no more than two per artist can be accepted. Maximum artwork size is 41 inches on the longest side including frame, all work must be for sale with the price entered before checkout, and given the venue setting all work must be PG and family-friendly. Accepted work must be hand-delivered: Hull Artists cannot receive or accept shipped art. Entry is $10 plus $10 per piece for members and $15 plus $10 per piece for non-members, so one piece costs $20 or $25 and six cost $70 or $75.
