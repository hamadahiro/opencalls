Project Nexus Supported Residencies 2027 — Nigerian Diaspora Visual Artist

Prizes ₦2,660,000 stipend Accommodation & studio in Lagos

The third residency in the 2027 round of Project Nexus, run by the Guest Artists Space (G.A.S.) Foundation and the Yinka Shonibare Foundation with the support of Deutsche Bank — a four-week residency at G.A.S. Lagos, 8 March – 2 April 2027, for an artist of the global Nigerian Diaspora. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside painting, sculpture, film, installation, ceramics, textiles, printmaking and performance. The residency offers time to develop existing research, build relationships with artists, practitioners and cultural networks in Nigeria, and explore new connections with Nigerian contexts, histories and material practices; there is no requirement to produce a finished work. This one is part-funded and the organiser is explicit about the gap: a ₦2,660,000 residency award towards food, living expenses and materials, a private en-suite bedroom, studio and shared workspace, library access, on-site support and the G.A.S. driver are provided, but international travel, visa costs, insurance and personal or project-related expenses are NOT included, and applicants are encouraged to seek additional mobility or project funding from arts councils, foundations, cultural agencies or universities. Trips outside Lagos and Ijebu are also at the resident's own cost. Applicants must be Nigerian nationals or of Nigerian heritage living and working outside Nigeria, aged 23 or over at any career stage with an established body of work, available for the fixed dates, proficient in spoken and written English, and able to name a referee from a curator, gallerist or cultural institution familiar with their work. The application asks for a single PDF portfolio with research examples, a CV, a short biography (150 words), referee contact details and six written answers of 150 or 200 words — five required, plus an optional one on practice and research — which may be given as a video statement instead. Applications close at midnight WAT on 3 November 2026 and no fee is charged; a selection panel is announced in November 2026 and final selections on 7 December 2026.
