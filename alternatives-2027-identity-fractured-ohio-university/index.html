Alternatives 2027 — Identity Fractured

Prize Exhibition

The 2027 edition of Alternatives, the photography biennial that Ohio University's MFA Photography + Integrated Media programme has run out of The School of Art + Design since 1980. The organisers describe it as "a long running biennial juried exhibition" that has "achieved national recognition for its leadership in forging new definitions in the medium by emphasizing the work of photographers in the conceptual forefront of the practice", and its money comes from an unusual endowment — the legacies of Clarence White, founder of the Clarence White School of Photography in New York, and his son Clarence White Jr., who re-established the school at Ohio University in 1948 and created what the programme calls the first degree-granting photography programme at a major university in the country. Past jurors named by the organisers are Carrie Mae Weems, Duane Michals, Kelli Connell, Jason Reed, Judy Natal and, most recently, Willie Osterman; the 2027 juror is Dan Estabrook. This cycle's theme is Identity Fractured: "Photography is a medium that has the power to capture, preserve, change, and distort history, places, and people. Using this medium we can tell stories about ourselves and others, play pretend, or show real pieces of ourselves... We are taking and analyzing parts of ourselves within every frame. Whether it is self portraiture, photographing others, or even objects. We all place ourselves into an image one way or another." Each artist may submit five files at 10 MB each for a flat $35, through a Google Form that requires a Google account to open. The programme gives its own address as the Ohio University Art Gallery in Athens; the 2027 call itself names no venue. Two disclosures: the School of Art + Design's own Alternatives page had not been updated at the time of listing and still carries the 2025 edition ("Image as Record", juried by Willie Osterman), so the 2027 call lives on the programme's own Instagram and on the submission form; and while previous editions were billed as an international biennial, the 2027 poster reads "A National Photography Biennial" — no residency or nationality rule is published on either surface. Neither readable surface announces a notification date, prize money or exhibition dates, and the entry form is sign-in gated, so it could not be read.
