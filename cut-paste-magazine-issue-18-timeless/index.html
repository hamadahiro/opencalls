Cut+Paste Magazine Issue 18 — Timeless

Prizes Publication Payment

Cut+Paste Magazine is an indie photography publication. Issue 18 is the Winter 2026 issue, themed Timeless — elements and motifs that could belong to countless different centuries, leaning toward timeless winter landscapes and interiors in a neutral and blue colour palette.

Accepted work spans portraits, nature, still life, and macro photography. No nudity, profanity, or AI-generated art, and no work closely copying others' creations.

The magazine pays contributors: $15 base rate plus $5–10 per additional piece selected. Submission is free, through the magazine's Google Form. The Issue 18 deadline is July 5, 2026.
