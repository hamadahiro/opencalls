Marilyn Stafford FotoReportage Award 2026

Prize £4,000 prize

10th edition. Annual award for professional female photographers towards the completion of a compelling documentary photo essay addressing important social, environmental, economic, or cultural issues. Supported by Nikon. Winner receives £4,000 to support the project. Special celebration of 10 years of the prize in September at POST, Brighton & Hove's new centre for photography. Open globally to female or female-identifying photographers aged 18+ at any career stage. Must have completed at least one previous documentary photo essay and have already started the submitted project.

DeadlineMay 18, 2026
ResultsLate June 2026
Entry feeFree
EligibilityWomen, 18+
LocationBrighton, UK
Requirements10 + 15 images
Submit viaFotoDocument

Jury: Andrea Bruce, Photojournalist, NOOR Photo Agency co-owner, Nikon Ambassador · Donna De Cesare, Photojournalist, Associate Professor, University of Texas at Austin · Nina Emett, Director of FotoDocument, Documentary Photographer, Curator · Neo Ntsoma, Photojournalist, Founder of Neo Ntsoma Productions · Isabella Franceschini, 2024 Marilyn Stafford FotoReportage Award winner

Organized by FotoDocument
