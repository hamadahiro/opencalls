2027 Spring Artist in Residence at Hudson Gardens

Prize $1,100 stipend

Presented by the South Suburban Public Art Committee, the Hudson Gardens Artist in Residence programme gives one artist dedicated time and space to work while engaging the public in a garden setting in Littleton, Colorado. The artist works in the Pavilion at Hudson Gardens, a large, light-filled, climate-controlled space with tables, chairs, water and electricity, surrounded by seasonal landscapes, walking paths, wildlife and year-round visitors. This term runs 17 to 22 May 2027. The residency is deliberately public-facing: mornings are closed studio, afternoons are open studio from 1 to 4pm Tuesday to Friday, and Saturday runs 11am to 5pm including the closing reception, so applicants should be comfortable making work while visitors watch and ask questions. The resident develops and presents at least one public engagement activity - a workshop, demonstration, artist talk or interactive experience - with up to $200 of participant materials covered, and a residency coordinator supports setup, logistics and programming. A $1,100 stipend is paid on successful completion; lodging and transport are not provided. The call is open to artists working in all visual art mediums and disciplines, aged 18 and over, individuals only - teams, collectives, organisations and commercial entities are not eligible - and artists from Colorado and beyond are encouraged to apply. The Pavilion is not suited to large-scale fabrication, heavy machinery, extensive ventilation, significant dust, hazardous materials, open flames or specialised industrial equipment; hand-built small-scale ceramics are possible with off-site firing. An application is an artist statement, a residency proposal with goals, a description of the proposed public engagement activity, a CV and 8 to 12 images of work completed in the last five years. Selection is on artistic merit, fit with the garden setting, the quality and accessibility of the proposed public programme, and feasibility.
