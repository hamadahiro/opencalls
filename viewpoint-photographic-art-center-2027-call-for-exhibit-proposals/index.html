Viewpoint Photographic Art Center — 2027 Call for Exhibit Proposals

Prize Solo or group exhibition (~4 weeks) prize

Sacramento non-profit photography center seeking proposals for solo and group exhibitions running January–December 2027. Main Gallery proposals are open to all artists working in the photographic arts; the more intimate Step-Up Gallery is reserved for current Viewpoint members. Exhibits run roughly four weeks in both galleries. Submit a cohesive body of work — a representative selection of 20 images plus a short artist statement — reviewed by the Viewpoint Curatorial Committee. Maximum two proposals per artist; past solo exhibitors must wait three years before another solo show.

DeadlineJune 30, 2026
Entry feeFree
LocationSacramento, CA
Requirements20 images
