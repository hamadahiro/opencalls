Real And Wild — RAW Photography Awards 2026

CANCELLED: RAW cancelled the 2026 edition — the organiser says not enough submissions came in to run it properly, no winners will be announced and no prizes awarded; every entry fee has been refunded in full via Stripe, and uploaded images are being deleted unpublished and unjudged (RAW Junior and the Pet Photographer award are cancelled on the same terms). Originally the inaugural edition of RAW, a nature photography award built around a single rule: every image must be made within 100 km of the photographer's home — 'the wild is not far away.' Five categories: RAW Neighbors (no human elements), Coexistence (human presence), Connection (people and nature), Macro (the small world) and Perspective (the big scene). The Real And Wild Photographer of the Year receives $1,000, with $250 for each category winner; winning and shortlisted images are published in the online RAW Winners Gallery and a photo book, and promoted through a partnership with Atlantic Geographic. Blind judging by professionals. Open worldwide, with a free RAW Junior tier for under-18s. Fees: $10 for one image, $25 for five, $40 for ten. AI-generated or AI-composited images are not permitted. The organiser cancelled the 2026 competition: no winners, no prizes, entry fees refunded.
