Into The Woods

Mosaic Art Collective in Manchester, New Hampshire is taking entries for Into The Woods, an autumn and Halloween themed group exhibition. The collective is looking for art that celebrates autumn - the landscape, the people, the activities, the holidays and the emotions of the season - and says broad interpretations of the theme are welcome. Work in all mediums may apply, with reproductions and AI-generated work excluded. Entry is $10 for the first piece and $10 for each additional one, up to four entries, so a full submission costs $40. Submissions close on Friday 25 September 2026 at 11.59pm and acceptances go out on Sunday 27 September. The show hangs at the collective's gallery at 410 Chestnut Street from 10 October to 1 November 2026, opening with a reception on 10 October from 5 to 7pm. Work is dropped off in person on 30 September, 1 October or 2 October between 4 and 6pm and collected on 4, 5 or 6 November in the same window, which in practice makes this a call for artists who can reach Manchester. Sales are not guaranteed, but when they happen the artist keeps 75 per cent and the collective retains 25 per cent. All work must arrive ready to hang with proper hanging wire; there is a $5 wiring fee at drop-off if yours is not wired, and uncollected work incurs a $10 a day storage charge. Mosaic's exhibitions committee reserves the right to decline work that does not fit the theme or the technical and presentation requirements. There is no cash award; the return is the exhibition.
