Jentel Artist Residency — Winter/Spring 2027

Prizes Residency $300 stipend

Residency at Jentel, a working cattle ranch 20 miles southeast of Sheridan in Banner, Wyoming, for the Winter/Spring session running January 15 – May 7, 2027. Residents receive private accommodation, a separate individual studio, and a $300 stipend distributed in weekly increments to offset living expenses. Open to visual artists in all media — photography included — plus writers and composers. Applicants must be US residents (citizenship not required) aged 25 or over; full-time students are not eligible, and Wyoming residents have a streamlined application. Applications close September 15 at 12:00 noon MST.
