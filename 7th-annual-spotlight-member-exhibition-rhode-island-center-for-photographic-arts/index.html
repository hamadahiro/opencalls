7th Annual Spotlight Member Exhibition

Prize Solo exhibition

Annual juried exhibition for members of the Rhode Island Center for Photographic Arts (RICPA), juried by photographer and educator Neal Rantoul. Each entry is a set of five photographs that together tell a story or present a unified idea, juried as a complete set. One selected member wins a solo show in RICPA's Focus Gallery in 2026. Open to all current RICPA members — membership can be purchased at the time of entry; entry is free for Supporting Members and above and $35 for Exhibiting and Community Members. Selected work is shown in the gallery from August 20 to September 11, 2026 and online, and every entrant receives a live Zoom portfolio review with the juror.

DeadlineJune 15, 2026
ResultsJuly 1, 2026
Entry fee$35 (Supporting members free)
EligibilityMembership required
LocationProvidence, RI
Requirements5 images
