Rauschenberg Medical Emergency Grants - Cycle 38

Prize Up to $5,000

One-time grants of up to $5,000 for recent unexpected medical, dental and mental-health emergencies, funded by the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation and administered by NYFA. The eligible Visual Arts category names Photography outright, alongside craft/sculpture, painting and printmaking, and the programme also covers digital/electronic arts, video/film and choreography. Applicants must be 21 or older, reside in the United States, the District of Columbia, a Tribal Nation or a U.S. Territory, show sustained public-facing artistic activity over the past five years, and meet the income cap - adjusted gross income no greater than $80,000 individual or $160,000 joint, averaged over the last two federal tax returns. Applicants cannot be enrolled in a degree-seeking programme, and cannot have received a Rauschenberg Medical or Dancer Emergency Grant in the past five years. Cycle 38 covers emergencies that began on or after February 1, 2026; applications opened August 18 and close at 5:00 PM ET on September 15, with notification on November 9, 2026. Artists whose medical costs are covered by Medicare or Medicaid may instead request up to $5,000 in documented living expenses. Roughly $148,000 is distributed each cycle and demand outstrips it, so applying is not a guarantee of an award.
