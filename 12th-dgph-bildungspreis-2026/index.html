12th DGPh Bildungspreis 2026

Prize €3,000

The DGPh Bildungspreis is the German Photographic Society's prize for photographic education, awarded in 2026 for the twelfth time and endowed for the first time with €3,000. The DGPh — a non-profit founded in 1951 — states the prize's purpose as discovering and making known interesting projects in the mediation of and with photography. It is looking for innovative and sustainable projects that use photography as a language and reach into society: cultural and museum-education initiatives in which photography is the subject or the tool, media education offerings about photography, school and out-of-school activities, and innovative projects from universities. The society describes the range of possible submissions as deliberately wide — new mediation concepts in schools, photography projects and workshops on selected current themes, innovative formats for making photography tangible, or the creation of a network for photography — and points to the previous years' winning projects as evidence of that openness to very different approaches. The award is explicitly a Förderpreis, offered to projects still taking shape as well as finished ones, and from 2026 it will be awarded every two years. An application needs a substantial project description; photographs and any existing press coverage may be attached, and the online portal asks for four to fifteen image examples of previous work as JPEGs. Entry is free, and submissions are accepted only through the DGPh's own online portal. The jury is made up of the board of the DGPh's Education Section together with further experts in photography mediation, always including the previous year's laureate; the section is responsible for the design and award of the prize.
