American Vision Scholarship

Prize 2 × $15,000 tuition scholarship

ICP's scholarship for photographers currently based in the United States, offering $15,000 toward tuition for the 2027–2028 academic year. Two awards go to students enrolled in one of ICP's One-Year Certificate Programs — either Creative Practices or Documentary Practice and Visual Journalism — against a published annual tuition of $12,500 plus a $60 application fee. The scholarship is open to any applicant currently residing in the United States; recipients are selected by ICP's Scholarship Committee on the strength of their application materials and portfolios, "with a focus on those who demonstrate a distinctive visual voice and strong potential in the field of photography". Both the programme application and the scholarship application must be submitted by March 1, 2027 to be considered; applications for the Fall 2027 admission cycle opened on October 1, 2026. Scholarships are available only to students enrolled in the One-Year Certificate programmes, not to part-time programmes or individual courses, and awards cannot be combined — an applicant may receive only one.
