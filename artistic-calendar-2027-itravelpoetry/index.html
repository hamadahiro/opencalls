Artistic Calendar 2027

iTravelPoetry — a Greek publisher and cultural venue in Chalandri, in the Athens suburbs, whose photography programme runs the Beyond Borders open call and its own festival — is assembling the Artistic Calendar 2027 (Καλλιτεχνικό Ημερολόγιο 2027), a collective edition that turns a year of days into a printed anthology of reader and contributor work. Four entry categories are named and photography is one of them: a poem of up to 30 lines, a text or short story of up to 500 words, a painting or other visual work, or a photograph. The theme is free. Each creator may enter one or two works — €13 for one, €20 for two, plus shipping, and each option includes one copy of the finished book; extra copies are €10 each. Work in another art form, or work over the line or word limit, is priced separately on request. Be clear about what the fee buys: the call names no jury and describes no selection stage for the four listed categories, and its own words are an invitation to participate and a “confirmation of your participation”, so the €13 buys a place in the edition rather than a shot at one — the organiser's framing is that creators carry none of the production cost, and with a €10 copy included the real entry cost is a few euros plus postage. The edition is a printed A5 book, black-and-white throughout (worth weighing if you work in colour), carries an ISBN and a BookPoint registration, and is launched at the publisher's own cultural space at Irakleiou 6, Chalandri. Under Greek copyright law 2121/1993 the copyright stays with the creator and the exploitation rights sit with the publisher, but the works are explicitly not bound editorially to iTravelPoetry, so you may use the same work in other editions of your own or in other collections. Entry is through the form on the call page, which takes the finished file (JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX or PDF) and asks for a postal address for the copy; payment is by bank transfer to an account sent with the confirmation of your participation, with any bank charges on the sender. The form also makes you pick one of four promotion options before it will submit — a photo and CV added (€10 + VAT), a written interview on the publisher's site (€20 + VAT), a professional video feature (€200 + VAT), or “no promotion, calendar entry only” — and that last one is free, so none of it belongs to the entry price. The call is published only in Greek and its form asks for your name in Greek characters, so a non-Greek-speaking photographer should expect to write to the publisher before entering. Submit the final file: the organiser states that no changes are accepted after submission.
