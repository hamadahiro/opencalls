EuroNatur Photo Competition 2026

Prize €1,500

EuroNatur is a German nature conservation foundation based in Radolfzell. The Photo Competition is the foundation's annual call for nature photography from across Europe. The 2026 edition is the competition's 33rd cycle — long-running.

Eligibility is restricted to photographers residing in Europe. Submitted work must depict European flora, fauna, or landscapes — geographically restricted by both photographer and subject.

Top prize is €1,500. Winners are published in EuroNatur magazine and included in EuroNatur's 2027 wall calendar.

Selection is by Kerstin Sauer (Art Director, EuroNatur), Robin Vornholz (Editor, natur magazine), Christiane Stewen (Public Relations, GELSENWASSER AG), Darja Wintersig (event photographer), and Peter Laufmann (environmental journalist).

Submission is by email with up to 5 images. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was March 31; results announced Fall 2026.
