Three Artist Exhibit

Prize Exhibition

Decode Gallery is a submission-based photography gallery at 314 South Convent in a historic neighbourhood of Tucson, Arizona, open to the public on Saturdays from noon to 4pm. Its programme runs on open calls, and the Three Artist Exhibit is the one that asks for a project rather than a theme.

Three photography series are selected by the juror for the show, so each of the three photographers gets a run of their own work rather than a single frame in a group hang. The three spots are not equal: the gallery states the first is for 6 images, the second for 8 and the last for 12. The brief is deliberately wide — a collective series of images of any kind.

Holly Hart, the juror, founded Decode and is a conceptual fine art photographer. She grew up in Southern Oregon and took a BA in Photography from the Art Institute of Seattle in 2011; her work has been published internationally in magazines and exhibited in galleries nationally.

Printing is the gallery's cost, not the photographer's: Decode prints professionally on an Epson printer with Ilford Pearl paper, one exhibition print per image, and hangs the work in museum-quality 22-inch white frames using its own precut mats, resizing (never cropping) the submitted files to suit them. Prints are archived in a flat file for six months after the show. Selected work can optionally be listed for sale, in which case the photographer is notified immediately of any enquiry and Decode takes a 50 per cent commission; all rights to both the file and the print stay with the photographer.

Entry is $40 for up to fifteen photographs, paid by PayPal or Square on the gallery's own submission page. The deadline is 18 December 2026, notifications follow within two weeks, high-resolution JPGs are due two days after the acceptance email, and the exhibition runs 6 to 27 February 2027 with the opening on 6 February.
