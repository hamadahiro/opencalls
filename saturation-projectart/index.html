SATURATION

Prize Exhibition

projectART is a 501(c)(3) arts education nonprofit in Wimberley, Texas, running youth programmes, artist mentorships and a gallery — ARTSPACE on River Road — for rural Central Texas. SATURATION is its next group exhibition, a show about vivid colour, visual intensity and the idea of fullness. The call wants bold palettes, rich surfaces and immersive compositions, but equally work that treats saturation as a concept: abundance, excess, density, emotion, sensory overload. It also invites readings of saturation as a social, psychological, cultural or environmental condition — information overload, consumerism, identity, memory, being engulfed by a feeling, the point at which intensity transforms perception. All visual art media are welcome and submission is free. The exhibition opens with a reception on 9 October 2026 and stays on view at ARTSPACE through 9 January 2027, a three-month run.
