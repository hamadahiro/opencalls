American Natural Landscapes Open Call

Open call from Soft Publishing, the Treviso-based independent publishing house producing contemporary photography books in limited editions, for its next title: photographs of the natural landscapes of the Americas, north and south. Colour or black and white are both welcome, and the organiser asks explicitly for natural rather than urban landscape work. Up to ten high-resolution photographs may be submitted, each at least 2000 pixels on the longest side, alongside the artist's name, email and Instagram handle, a biography of at most 20 words, a project statement or concept of at most 50 words, and full captions for every image. Entries go only through the publisher's Google Form, which requires a Google sign-in. Images generated by artificial intelligence are not accepted, and work depicting violent content is excluded. The submission fee is a two-rung dated ladder printed inside the Google Form: “Early Bird Fee: €11.00 (from September 15th, 2026 to October 15th, 2026)” and “Last Minute Fee: €17.00 (from October 16th, 2026 to December 18th, 2026)”, so entering after 15 October 2026 costs €17. The form also offers paying by pre-ordering the book instead of paying the fee (“you may choose either to pre-order the book via the link … or to pay the €11.00/€17.00 … fee”); the fee is non-refundable and does not guarantee inclusion. Selected artists are published in the book and are offered a discount on ordering their own copies. The call names no age, nationality or residence restriction. Soft Publishing's shop lists its earlier titles — European Landscapes, Eastern European Landscapes, Northern European Landscapes, Contemporary Photography Around the World and Soft magazine n.00 — and its black-and-white open call closed in August 2026.
