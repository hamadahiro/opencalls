Art for the View 2026

Prizes $500 Best in Show Exhibition

The 25th-anniversary edition of Sunnyview's exhibition of art and fine crafts by artists with disabilities. The show debuts at the Art for the View benefit evening at the Fenimore Gallery at Proctors Theatre in Schenectady on November 16, 2026, remains on display into December, and all work is for sale, with proceeds supporting the rehabilitation hospital's mission and the participating artists. Open to artists with physical, cognitive, visual and hearing disabilities; only work made after the onset of disability and completed within the last seven years is eligible. All media are welcome (no reproduction prints). Up to two pieces per artist; the application is short and free.
