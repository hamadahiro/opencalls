Flower & Fauna: A Botanical & Beings Exhibition

A themed winter show at the Avondale Arts Center, the volunteer-run gallery of the Avondale Arts Alliance just east of Atlanta in Avondale Estates. Every work must feature flowers, plants, animals or some combination of the three — the alliance states that anything outside that is disqualified automatically — and both traditional treatments and loose, creative interpretations are invited, with no restriction on medium beyond the subject rule. Entrants may submit one to six pieces in a single entry, one entry per artist, as JPG, PDF or PNG files up to 50MB each; the fee is $15 for three pieces and $30 for six, artists 18 and under enter free with a voucher code, and the alliance asks anyone for whom the fee is a hardship to email before submitting. The call is open only to artists in Atlanta and the surrounding area, because accepted work has to be hand delivered: drop-off is 10am to 2pm on Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November, the gallery does not accept shipped artwork, and a friend or family member may deliver on an artist's behalf. Status emails go out by midnight on Saturday 31 October. The exhibition opens with a 6–9pm reception on Saturday 14 November and closes with another on Saturday 19 December; attendance at neither is required. All work must be priced for sale with the alliance retaining 30% of any sale, and prices cannot be changed after acceptance, so the alliance asks artists to build the commission into the price before applying. Submissions close at 11.59pm on Friday 23 October 2026.
