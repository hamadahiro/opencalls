The Hopper Prize Fall 2026 Grants

The Hopper Prize's autumn cycle, awarding six unrestricted cash grants totalling $13,000: two of $4,500 and four of $1,000. The organisation runs bi-annually and gives $26,000 across the two cycles each year. Grants are open to artists aged 18 and over internationally, working in any media; the prize describes its remit as "the field of visual art in its broadest and most inclusive sense" and has funded photographers, painters, sculptors and video artists at every career stage since 2018. Alongside the six grants, a shortlist of 30 artists per cycle is archived on hopperprize.org with biographies and links, and applicants may opt in to additional exposure on the prize's Instagram feed. Applying is a two-step process: pay the $40 fee, then complete the application form, submitting up to 10 images. The closing date is now published on the organiser’s own page and the earlier caution is withdrawn: re-read 8 November 2026, hopperprize.org renders a live deadline block — "Deadline Tuesday, November 17, 11:59PM Pacific Standard Time" — outside any HTML comment, and 17 November 2026 is indeed a Tuesday. The only commented-out block left in the source is the previous cycle’s "Tuesday, November 11". Note the cutoff is 11:59pm Pacific, not end of day where you are.
