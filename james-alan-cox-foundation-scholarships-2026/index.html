James Alan Cox Foundation Scholarships for Student Photojournalists 2026

Prizes Six $2,500 scholarships Five DSLR cameras

A free, long-running scholarship program for student photojournalists, run by the foundation set up in memory of photojournalist Jim Cox. This cycle awards five $2,500 scholarships to college or technical-school students — four for video work and one for still photography — a sixth $2,500 scholarship to a graduate student for video, and five digital SLR cameras to high-school students, who submit still photographs only. Awards are paid to the student's school and must be used at the start of the next semester. The foundation is explicit about what it wants and does not want: work that "tells a story", broadly defined — breaking news, sporting events, a graduation, a scientific experiment, an emergency response, an unsolicited act of kindness — and emphatically not art photography: "please don't ruin your chances of being considered by entering images of flowers, glamour shots or kittens". Still-photography applicants submit five images (JPEG or PNG, max 2500 px on the long side, 4 MB); video applicants submit two clips of up to three minutes. The full application also asks for a brief essay, two letters of reference, a school transcript and financial data, and a parental release form for applicants under 18. Applicants must be US citizens at a school in the United States (US citizens at US government schools abroad may apply), must have completed at least one year at an accredited institution, must have taken photography or photojournalism coursework, and must have at least one semester remaining when the award is made. A student may hold only one Cox Foundation scholarship in a year. Applications are open from 15 July 2026 to midnight on the evening of 15 November 2026, and awards are announced in early to mid-December. The foundation has granted more than $380,000 to 209 students across eighteen rounds.
