CONTACT Photography Festival 2027 — Open Call Exhibitions

Prize Festival exhibition

CONTACT is the annual city-wide photography festival that has taken over Toronto every May since 1997, and its Open Call Exhibitions programme is the part of it that any photographer can walk into. Where the Core Program is produced with museums, major galleries and artist-run centres, the Open Call strand is artist-organised and artist-produced: if you register a fully organised exhibition at a Greater Toronto Area venue before the deadline, you are in the festival. The organisers are explicit that nothing is filtered — "We do not review or jury submissions" — so this is not a competition but a listing and a platform, with your show appearing in the festival's programme and on contactphoto.com alongside the Core Program. Registration is free and opened on 19 October 2026 for the 31st edition, which runs 1–31 May 2027. CONTACT accepts registrations until 18 January 2027. The programme is open to "emerging and experienced photographers at any career or hobby level", and you do not have to be Toronto-based to take part. THE ONE HARD CONDITION IS THE VENUE: artists and organisers must secure their own exhibition space before registering, and are responsible for every aspect of production and presentation — CONTACT does not produce, fund or install Open Call shows. Any publicly accessible venue in the GTA counts, including outdoor spaces and storefront windows (the GTA means the City of Toronto plus Durham, Halton, Peel and York Regions; Hamilton is outside it). Exhibition dates must fall wholly or partly within the festival month, and the run can be anything from a single night to more than a month. Registration asks for venue details, accessibility information and opening hours, exhibition title and text, and three images (JPEG or PNG, up to 3000px on the long side, captioned "Artist Name, Title, Year") for the website listing; there is no limit on how many works you actually show.
