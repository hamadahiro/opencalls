smallworks 2026

Prize Group exhibition

ROY G BIV's annual small-format holiday show, open to the Columbus nonprofit's own Artist and ROY Alumni members. Every piece must fit inside a 12 x 12 x 12 inch envelope — two-dimensional work included, so photography and lens-based work qualify as long as it arrives ready to hang with the right hardware. You can submit up to five entries and the gallery guarantees each member at least one accepted work, with a maximum of three, which makes this one of the rare juried shows where entering is close to a certainty of being hung. The gallery asks artists to price work 'in the spirit of the season of giving' and splits any sale 65% to the artist, 35% to ROY. Submission is by email with the subject 'smallworks 2026': attach high-quality JPEG or PNG images named only with a number, each no larger than 1.5 MB and never zipped, plus a list of works giving title, date, media, size and price; submissions missing the artist's full name, mailing address, attached images or attached list of works will not be considered. Artists are notified of admittance on Sunday November 15, accepted work is delivered to the gallery at 435 West Rich Street on Saturday November 28 and Sunday November 29 between 12:30 and 5:30pm EST, and the artist is responsible for transport both ways. The gallery states on its mission page that the price of work shown at ROY includes its 35% commission, reinvested in the gallery. ROY G BIV has run for 37 years and given over 1,000 emerging artists their exhibiting opportunity; its alumni hold work in permanent collections at the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Whitney, MoMA PS1, the Louvre, the Getty, Tate Modern and the Columbus Museum of Art, and hold "64 major awards (that we know of), including 2 Guggenheim Fellows". Membership in the Artist or Alumni tier is the only cost, taken through the gallery's Donorbox form, which publishes no fixed price.
