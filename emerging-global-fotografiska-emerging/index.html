Emerging Global

The first edition of Emerging Global, Fotografiska Emerging's recurring international group exhibition, which connects the platform's global open call with Fotografiska's museum programme and rotates between Fotografiska museums. The inaugural show opens at Fotografiska Berlin in February 2027 and asks what a more conscious world means to you: Fotografiska is looking for photographic projects that explore living with greater awareness of ourselves, of one another, of the systems we are part of and of our relationship with the planet, and says it wants honest, raw and courageous work that connects personal experience with wider social and political realities. Suggested - explicitly not exclusive - themes run from climate change and our relationship with the environment to inequality and threats to human rights, consumption and waste, technology and data, community and collective action, speculative futures, and identity and belonging. Documentary, conceptual, staged, archival and experimental approaches are all welcome; the focus is on photographic, concept-driven series, though time- and prompt-based work can also be submitted. Selected artists take part in the Berlin exhibition with production and associated costs covered and organised by Fotografiska, receive an artist fee of €500, and get media and press opportunities plus visibility across Fotografiska's global channels. Entry is free; submit a header image, project title and short summary plus a PDF with at least eight images from one series, a project description and an artist CV. Up to three projects per person. A jury drawn from the Fotografiska Berlin team decides by 15 November 2026. The deadline is not end-of-day: the mission page's own payload closes the call at 2026-10-15T08:00:00Z - 08:00 UTC, 10:00 in Berlin - so 14 October is the last full day to enter.
