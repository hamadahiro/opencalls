2027 Black Creativity Juried Art Exhibition — Teen Category

The teen half of Black Creativity, the longest continually running exhibition of African American art in the United States, shown at Chicago's Griffin Museum of Science and Industry since 1970 and now in its 57th year. The Teen Category is free to enter and open to Black teen artists in the Chicagoland area — within 100 miles of the museum — who are enrolled in grades 9 through 12 in the 2026/27 school year; the General Category runs alongside it for adult artists nationwide. Contemporary work in any visual medium is accepted, and the museum's own exhibition page names photography outright among the media it shows. Submit up to four works made during the last school year or since, anonymously, each original and unsold and not previously selected for a Black Creativity exhibition; a teacher or caregiver may help with the technical side but the artist submits for themselves. The same three jurors judge both categories. Teen awards are $600 for Best in Show, $375 for second place and $250 for third, and selected work hangs in the exhibition from January 18 to April 18, 2027. Accepted work must arrive framed or otherwise finished and ready to install, with delivery and pickup by the artist — 2D work may not exceed 60 inches in any direction, 3D work 30 inches, and nothing may weigh more than 50 pounds. Acceptances go out by November 4, 2026, local drop-off is December 18–19 and work is collected April 23–24, 2027. The museum does not solicit sales, but artists may sell from the walls and no commission is taken. AI-generated work is not accepted.
