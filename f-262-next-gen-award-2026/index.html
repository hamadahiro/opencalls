F/262 NEXT GEN Award 2026

Prizes Solo exhibition €450 Rotary Prize Instax camera

Single-image award for young photographers at the F/262 International Photography Festival in Caldas da Rainha, Portugal, run as its own competition alongside the festival's Photography Awards, Curated Open Call and Photo Book Award. Open worldwide to photographers aged 25 and under at the time of submission, split into two bands: Junior (16 and under) enter free and submit 1–3 images; Emerging (17 to 25) pay €5 per image and submit 1–5 images. Both bands must upload proof of age as a PDF. Any subject is welcome and any device qualifies, including analogue cameras and phones; the festival's 2026 sub-theme 'Shaping Shores / Moldar as Margens' is suggested but not required. A shortlist of up to 10 photographers is selected after the deadline. The overall winner takes the title NEXT GEN Photographer of the Year 2026 and a dedicated solo exhibition at the CEF (Centro de Experimentação Fotográfica) in October–November 2026. Gold, Silver and Bronze each receive a festival exhibition plus a Rotary Prize of €450, €200 and €100 respectively, KOYLAB print vouchers of €250, €150 and €100, BlackRapid straps and SmartAlbums software; Gold also receives an Instax Mini Evo Cinema camera. All shortlisted finalists join the NEXT GEN group exhibition and receive a finalist certificate and festival catalogue feature. Photographers keep full copyright; the festival takes a non-exclusive licence for up to three years. Submissions close 19 August 2026, World Photography Day, with the winner announced in September.
