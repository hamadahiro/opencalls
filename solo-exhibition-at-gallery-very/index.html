Solo Exhibition at Gallery VERY

Gallery VERY, which describes itself as one of Boston's longest-running independent artist-run galleries, is looking for one artist to give a five-week solo exhibition in its 500-square-foot space in the SoWa arts district. No exhibition proposal is required — the gallery says it is selecting an artist, not a pre-planned exhibition, and Gallery Director John Guthrie will then work with the selected artist to develop a show for the space roughly six months after selection, with new work considered as the exhibition takes shape. Photography is named alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, ceramics, mixed media, installation, video and interdisciplinary practices, and there are no restrictions on age, education, career stage or professional status. The gallery provides professional installation, an opening reception, a public artist talk, promotion, sales representation for the run of the show, and a permanent exhibition page on its website; sales are split 50/50, the artist covers transport both ways, and work is not insured by the gallery. Applications ask for 8–10 work samples that show a broad picture of your current practice (video artists may send 2–3 clips of up to three minutes with stills), a 200-word statement, a 100-word bio and a two-page CV. Location eligibility is regional: artists must live and work within approximately 250 miles of Boston, and the selected artist is expected to be present in person for the opening and the talk. VERY marks ten years in 2026 and presents four to five exhibitions a year.
