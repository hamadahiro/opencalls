Residency for Artists Based in the Baltic Region 2027

This Gasworks open call is for an artist at a pivotal stage in their career based in Lithuania, Latvia or Estonia, and it is two things at once: an eleven-week, fully funded residency in London from 7 July – 21 September 2027, and a solo exhibition at Gasworks in July–September 2028. Between the two, the artist develops the work in dialogue with Gasworks and the Upė Foundation Curatorial Fellow, so the residency, the research and the exhibition sit inside one longer framework of exchange rather than ending when the studio does. The residency is supported by Upė Foundation as part of a multi-year partnership with Gasworks designed to support artistic and cultural production with the Baltic region and its diasporas. Gasworks' residencies are self-led, non-prescriptive and process-based: time and space to develop a practice, undertake research, engage with the city and build professional networks. The selected artist receives 24-hour access to a fully accessible private studio alongside other residents and Gasworks' community of London-based studio holders; a single room in Gasworks' Residency House, a two-storey, five-bed house with three communal bathrooms and a kitchen shared with four other resident artists; economy return flights and taxi transfers from the artist's home country, including one cabin and one hold bag; all visa costs and support with the visa application; a weekly stipend of £175 for living expenses; a materials budget of up to £800; a prepaid TFL zones 1+2 travel card; studio visits with Gasworks' staff, external curators, artists and educational groups; an Open Studio event and presentation; access to Gasworks' basic tools and AV equipment; a dedicated residency page with a short video interview filmed and edited by Gasworks; and at least two individual studio visits with other artists, curators and arts professionals. The call is for contemporary visual artists based in the Baltic region (Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia) with at least a moderate level of spoken English; no medium is excluded. The residency FAQ adds that an artist currently studying outside the region but keeping a permanent address in it is still eligible to apply. Gasworks cannot host duos or collectives, or accommodate partners or children, because the funds and the room are for one person. Applying is free: one PDF of no more than 15MB uploaded through the form at the end of the opportunity page, containing a cover sheet, a 250-word statement on your practice, a 250-word statement on your plans for the residency, up to 15 images or video/sound clips with captions, a CV of no more than three pages, and any relevant documentation such as articles, exhibitions or publications; spoken answers may replace the two written statements as sound or video links. Applications close at 1pm UK time on 2 February 2027, not at midnight. A panel of Gasworks representatives and external advisors with specialist knowledge of the contemporary art scenes in the Baltic region and the UK shortlists three to five artists for an online interview in the week beginning 15 March 2027, with the successful applicant notified in the week beginning 22 March 2027. Gasworks prioritises artists making high quality work who have not had the opportunity to work in London before.
