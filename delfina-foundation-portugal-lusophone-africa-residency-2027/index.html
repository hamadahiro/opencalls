Delfina Foundation — Portugal & Lusophone Africa Residency 2027

Prizes Funded residency £1,200 materials budget

A twelve-week, fully funded London residency at Delfina Foundation for contemporary visual artists born in Portugal, Angola, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau, Cape Verde or São Tomé and Príncipe, run in partnership with the UK branch of the Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation. The residency sits inside Delfina's 'open' summer 2027 season — independent research rather than a set theme — and runs 28 June to 17 September 2027. The package is unusually complete: a return economy flight, UK airport transfers, reimbursement of visa fees where required, a £40-a-day living allowance, a £1,200 materials budget, a London travel pass, and a single bedroom with a desk in the Delfina house, which takes up to eight residents around shared bathrooms, laundry, kitchen, library and dining room. Alongside time and space, the season's programme includes guided visits to museums, galleries, non-profits and artist-run spaces in and beyond London, organised encounters with artists, curators, academics and researchers, and professional development in the form of presentations, crits, portfolio reviews and mentoring. Applicants must be seeking to develop their practice through research, be able to explain how an international residency would serve their career and local networks, and be confident discussing their work in English, since the interview is held in English. Artists currently based in the UK are not eligible. The application is a Cognito form asking for a CV and either a portfolio PDF or a personal website, with your practice described in five keywords and again in fifty words, plus contact details for two professional referees. Duos and collectives are discouraged by the accommodation — Delfina asks that one member represent the group — and the foundation states plainly that it welcomes D/deaf, disabled and neurodivergent applicants and will discuss adapting the residency to access needs. Previous unsuccessful applicants are explicitly encouraged to apply again. Applications close 23:59 BST on Sunday 4 October 2026 and everyone is notified by mid-November.
