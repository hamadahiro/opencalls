reFocus Awards - World Photo Annual 2026

reFocus's flagship annual competition across 22 categories — from abstract, architecture and fine art to photojournalism, street, wildlife and film/analog — crowning an International Photographer of the Year and Discovery of the Year alongside gold, silver and bronze category winners. Over $5,000 in cash prizes, with winners receiving a published interview, online exhibition and international media exposure. Judged by photo editors and creative directors from The New York Times, Robb Report, Financial Times HTSI, Condé Nast Traveller UK and Rizzoli. Single images or series of 2–5; tiered fees from $20 for a student single image to $50 for a professional series, with the regular window closing 24 September and an extended entry deadline of 17 November; winners announced December 9, 2026.
