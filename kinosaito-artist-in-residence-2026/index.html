KinoSaito Artist-in-Residence 2026

Prizes $1,500 stipend Studio & housing

Four-week fall residency (November 8 – December 6, 2026) at KinoSaito, an arts center in a renovated former Catholic school in Verplanck, New York. Two professional artists each receive a 900 sq ft studio with loft living quarters (kitchenette and private bathroom) and a $1,500 stipend for living costs and supplies. All media are accepted, including photography and lens-based work; currently enrolled students are not eligible.
