Luz e Sombra — National Photography Competition

National photography competition exploring the relationship between light and shadow across themes of heritage, nature, architecture, and daily life. Submit a series of 6 photographs with a conceptual synopsis (1,500 chars) and bio (1,000 chars). Six finalists selected — works published in an artist-edition book, shown in a traveling exhibition at three venues, and featured in the Égide digital gallery. Open to professional and amateur photographers who are Portuguese citizens or residents of Portugal.
