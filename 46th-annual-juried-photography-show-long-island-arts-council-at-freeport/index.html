46th Annual Juried Photography Show

Prizes Exhibition Cash awards

One of the tri-state area's longest-running juried photography exhibitions, presented each November by the Long Island Arts Council at Freeport at the Freeport Memorial Library. No theme and no subject — photographers submit their best work for a juried competition with cash awards and certificates, a month-long exhibition, and a reception honouring the artists. Open to photographers age 21 and over; up to five images per entry. Accepted prints must be framed (minimum 11×14 in, maximum 30 in on any side; panoramas excepted). Work shown in a previous LIACF Juried Photography Show is not eligible.
