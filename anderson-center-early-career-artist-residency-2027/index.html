Anderson Center Early Career Artist Residency 2027

Prize $625/week stipend

The Early Career Artist Residency is the funded strand of the Anderson Center's program, supported by the Jerome Foundation. A month-long cohort of five artists works together at the Tower View estate in Red Wing, Minnesota, in a program built specifically for artists early in their development who have not yet received substantial recognition.

Unlike the general residency, this one pays. Selected artists each receive a $625 per week stipend, a travel honorarium, a documentation budget covering services such as photography, video and audio, art-making resources, lodging and studio space, and chef-prepared communal dinners. There is no fee to apply.

The program is interdisciplinary and open to all disciplines, with the cohort model and cross-disciplinary exchange treated as central to what the residency offers. Visual artists are given a studio, and the shared facilities on the estate include a darkroom and a printmaking studio with an etching press — relevant if you work in analogue or alternative process.

Eligibility is tightly drawn. Applicants must be legal residents of Minnesota or of New York City's five boroughs; must not be enrolled in a degree-granting program from the time of application through the residency; must self-identify as early career, meaning roughly two to ten years of generative experience beyond formal training; and must have a practice centred on creating entirely new work. Artists with more than ten years of experience are generally ineligible, though the Anderson Center makes exceptions for those who stepped away for caregiving, illness or education.

The deadline is 12 p.m. noon Central Standard Time on 12 January 2027. Jury review takes place in late January and early February, and selected artists, the wait-list and runners-up are notified by 2 March 2027.
