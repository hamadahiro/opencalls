Oh My, Octopi!

Prizes $150 $75 $50

Winter theme show at The Firefly Artists, the Northport, New York artist cooperative founded in 2011 and now fifty resident members strong at 90 Main Street. The theme is the octopus: "Take a dive into the mysterious depths where eight arms create endless possibilities." The gallery's application for this call names photography directly - "All original media is acceptable, such as drawing, painting, photography, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, collage, fabric work, glasswork, jewelry and digital media" - while "AI-generated art is not" eligible, nor is work made in a workshop or class. Artists from throughout the Long Island region are invited and the gallery says off-island submissions are fine, but hand delivery and pickup at Northport are required: work goes in on 10 or 11 January and comes out from 11 February. Cash awards of $150, $75 and $50 plus honorable mention; the exhibition runs 12 January - 11 February 2027 with a reception on 28 January. Accepted work must be framed or at the very least wired and ready to hang, no more than 36 inches on the longest side, and must be for sale, with the gallery taking a 35% commission. The gallery's two surfaces disagree on how much one artist may enter: the application says one piece for $35 plus up to two more at $5 each and prints three work slots, while the PayPal page prices a ladder up to eight images ($70). The lower figure is stored here - ask the gallery before paying for more than three. The application itself is a Google Form.
