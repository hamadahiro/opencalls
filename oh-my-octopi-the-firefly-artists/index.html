Oh My, Octopi!

Winter theme show at The Firefly Artists, the Northport, New York artist cooperative founded in 2011 and now fifty resident members strong at 90 Main Street. The theme is the octopus: "Take a dive into the mysterious depths where eight arms create endless possibilities." The gallery's application for this call names photography directly - "All original media is acceptable, such as drawing, painting, photography, printmaking, mixed media, sculpture, collage, fabric work, glasswork, jewelry and digital media" - while "AI-generated art is not" eligible, nor is work made in a workshop or class. Artists from throughout the Long Island region are invited and the gallery says off-island submissions are fine, but hand delivery and pickup at Northport are required: work goes in between Thursday 7 and Sunday 10 January and comes out from 11 February. Cash awards of $150, $75 and $50 plus honorable mention; the exhibition runs 12 January - 11 February 2027 with a reception on 28 January. Accepted work must be framed or at the very least wired and ready to hang, no more than 36 inches on the longest side, and must be for sale, with the gallery taking a 35% commission. The application's own header now prints the cap - "Work Submission: Max 8 images", "Entry Fee: $35.00 ($5 ea. for additional)" - which matches the PayPal ladder. The application itself is a Google Form.
