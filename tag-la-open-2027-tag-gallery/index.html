TAG LA Open 2027

Prizes $600 / $400 / $250 cash Up to 8 Awards of Excellence

Annual all-media juried open at TAG, The Artists Gallery — a not-for-profit artist cooperative founded in 1993 on Museum Row in Los Angeles — running January 6–22, 2027 with an awards reception on Saturday, January 9. Photography is named outright in the accepted-media list: "Artists may submit: Painting, Drawing, Photography, Printmaking, Mixed Media, Sculpture, Digital, and Video art." The juror is Andi Campognone, who built, programmed and opened the Museum of Art and History (MOAH) in Lancaster, California and served as its Manager of Arts and Museums for over a decade; she directs the Kipaipai Professional Development Workshops and is a grant panelist for the Los Angeles County Arts Commission. THE MONEY: three cash awards — 1st $600, 2nd $400, 3rd $250 — plus Awards of Excellence, which the prospectus numbers twice and inconsistently: the summary block says "up to EIGHT AWARDS OF EXCELLENCE" while the general-information block below it says "EIGHT AWARDS OF EXCELLENCE" flat. Treat eight as a ceiling, not a promise. THE DEADLINE YEAR IS MISTYPED THROUGHOUT THE PROSPECTUS AND THE WEEKDAYS SETTLE IT: the Important Dates block reads "Exhibit Entries Open Tuesday, October 6, 2027" and "Deadline for Entries, Wednesday December 2, 2027, 10:59 pm PST" — both impossible for an exhibition that opens 6 January 2027. October 6 and December 2 fall on a Tuesday and a Wednesday in 2026, not 2027, and the platform record's own structured dates are 2026-10-06 (open) and 2026-12-03T07:59:59Z (close) — that is 23:59:59 on 2 December 2026 Pacific. Stored as 2026-12-02. The same year slip runs through acceptance ("Wednesday, December 9" — a Wednesday in 2026) while every date from drop-off onward is correctly 2027. ELIGIBILITY IS LOCAL AND NARROW: "open to artists 18 years and older residing in Los Angeles County." COSTS BEYOND THE FEE: all work must be for sale and TAG takes a 40% commission on sales made during the show and for 30 days after it ends; artists who ship must prepay return labels, and work has to be delivered in person on January 2–3 or received by 6 pm January 2. Hanging work is capped at 70 inches high by 40 wide framed, sculpture at 25 lbs. Work previously exhibited at TAG, work made in a classroom or under an instructor's supervision, and work already committed to another show between January 2 and February 24, 2027 are all excluded. AI: "No AI accepted" — stated once in the summary and once in the body; the gallery also publishes a standing "TAG Statement on AI Art" page on its own site.
