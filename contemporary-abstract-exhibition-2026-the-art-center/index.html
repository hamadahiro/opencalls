Contemporary | Abstract Exhibition 2026

Prize Exhibition

The Art Center is a community gallery at 1 Washington Street in downtown Dover, New Hampshire, and runs a rotating programme of national juried calls alongside solo shows and a resident-artist programme. Contemporary | Abstract 2026 is the winter slot: a juried open call whose brief is a sensibility rather than a medium.

MEDIA, STATED PRECISELY: the prospectus's only sentence on what it accepts is "All Contemporary | Abstract works will be considered". Measured on both surfaces that carry this call - theartcenterdover.com/exhibitions.php and the ArtCall prospectus at contemporary-abstractexhibition2026.artcall.org - the word photography appears ZERO times as an accepted medium; the single 'photograph' token on the ArtCall page is the publicity clause ("reserves the right to photograph an accepted work"), and the two 'painting' tokens are the juror's MFA and the canvas framing exemption. This is a silence, not an affirmative. What answers the medium question is the org's own programme and its own roster. The programme: Across America, also run by The Art Center through ArtCall, launched as a national photography exhibition. The roster: the gallery's member-artist page lists FOURTEEN members whose own one-line self-description is photographer or photography - Steve Arcone, Susan Irene Correia, Brian Crowley, Larry Elbroch, Pamela Hodgdon, Vera Kaufman, Pat Kennedy-Corlin, Tom Levasseur, Sylvia Mauro, Jim Munro, Richard Sorensen, Ron St. Jean, Jean Stimmell and Michael Tomolonius - and two of them, Levasseur and St. Jean, held named solo shows in the 2026 season on this same exhibitions page.

DATE STRINGS THAT CONTRADICT THEMSELVES ON THE ORGANISER'S OWN PAGE, and are not ours to correct: the ArtCall prospectus prints "Submissions Deadline - October 23, 11:59 PM" and "Notifications - October 25, 2026" in its header block, then prints "Delivery by - November 4, 2024" and "Notifications Made - October 25, 2025" further down. The stored deadline matches the org's own exhibitions page, which reads "Deadline: 10/23/2026"; October 23 2026 is a Friday. The stored resultsDate takes the 2026 reading of October 25 because the exhibition opens November 9 2026; the "2024" and "2025" strings are stale copy from earlier editions of this call and are recorded here rather than silently fixed.

NO CASH AWARD IS PUBLISHED. The prospectus names a juror and an exhibition and nothing else - no prize list, no purchase award, no catalogue. The $30 non-member fee buys a juried selection and a seven-week hang in a real gallery; members pay nothing. Weigh that against the mandatory-sale condition: every accepted piece must be for sale and cannot be withdrawn during the run, at a 30% gallery commission.
