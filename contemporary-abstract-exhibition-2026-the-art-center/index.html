Contemporary | Abstract Exhibition 2026

The Art Center is a community gallery at 1 Washington Street in downtown Dover, New Hampshire, and runs a rotating programme of national juried calls alongside solo shows and a resident-artist programme. Contemporary | Abstract 2026 is the winter slot: a juried open call whose brief is a sensibility rather than a medium.

On media, the prospectus's only sentence on what it accepts is "All Contemporary | Abstract works will be considered". Neither theartcenterdover.com/exhibitions.php nor the ArtCall prospectus at contemporary-abstractexhibition2026.artcall.org names photography as an accepted medium: "photograph" appears only where the organiser "reserves the right to photograph an accepted work", and "painting" only in the juror's MFA and the canvas framing exemption. What answers the medium question is the org's own programme and its own roster. Across America, also run by The Art Center through ArtCall, launched as a national photography exhibition, and the gallery's member-artist page lists fourteen members who describe themselves as photographers - Steve Arcone, Susan Irene Correia, Brian Crowley, Larry Elbroch, Pamela Hodgdon, Vera Kaufman, Pat Kennedy-Corlin, Tom Levasseur, Sylvia Mauro, Jim Munro, Richard Sorensen, Ron St. Jean, Jean Stimmell and Michael Tomolonius - two of whom, Levasseur and St. Jean, held named solo shows in the 2026 season on this same exhibitions page.

The organiser's own page contradicts itself on dates: the ArtCall prospectus prints "Submissions Deadline - October 23, 11:59 PM" and "Notifications - October 25, 2026" in its header block, then prints "Delivery by - November 4, 2024" and "Notifications Made - October 25, 2025" further down. The org's own exhibitions page reads "Deadline: 10/23/2026", and the exhibition opens November 9 2026, so notifications are October 25, 2026; the "2024" and "2025" strings are stale copy from earlier editions of this call.

NO CASH AWARD IS PUBLISHED. The prospectus names a juror and an exhibition and nothing else - no prize list, no purchase award, no catalogue. The $30 non-member fee buys a juried selection and a seven-week hang in a real gallery; members pay nothing. Weigh that against the mandatory-sale condition: every accepted piece must be for sale and cannot be withdrawn during the run, at a 30% gallery commission.
