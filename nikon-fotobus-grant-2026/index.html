Nikon Fotobus Grant 2026

Prizes €5,000 / €3,000 / €2,000 Nikon camera

A project-continuation grant for emerging photographers from Fotobus e.V., the Hannover-based photography society, funded with Nikon. Three awards are made — €5,000, €3,000 and €2,000 — and each of the three funded projects also receives a Nikon camera, with the full sum paid out immediately on announcement and no restrictions on how it is spent. What is funded is the continuation of a photographic project already under way: all genres of photography are welcome, group applications from two or more photographers are allowed, and the work may use text, found images, documents or drawings alongside the photographs as long as its core is photographic. Video works are not accepted and AI-generated images are excluded outright — the society funds photography only — while manipulation and staging are permitted in conceptual work provided they are disclosed in the captions; applicants also accept the World Press Photo Code of Ethics. Eligibility runs on either of two alternatives, with nationality irrelevant to both: being enrolled as a student at a university in Germany, Austria or Switzerland at the time of applying (no age limit, and any subject, not only photography), or being no older than 30 and resident in Germany, Austria or Switzerland. Membership of Fotobus is not required. One application per person, though you may additionally join a group application, and an unfunded project may be entered again next year. The application is free and goes only through Picter: a project description of up to 3,000 characters answering the organisers' set questions, a maximum of 30 images from the unfinished project, and a CV of up to 1,500 characters — the quality of the edit and the sequence are explicitly part of the jury's assessment. The jury meets in mid-November, a shortlist of 20 is published on 1 December 2026 and the three funded projects on 15 December 2026, with the one-year funding period running to 15 December 2027 and a final report due at the end of it. NOTE ON THE CLOSING DATE: the organiser's own page advertises 1 November 2026 at 23:59 German time, but the Picter form that applications must go through is set to close on 31 October at 23:59 German time — treat 31 October as the operative date.
