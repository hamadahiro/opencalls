FRESH EYES 2027

Prizes Publication Exhibition at Haute Photographie Fair

FRESH EYES is the worldwide open call run by Amsterdam-based GUP Magazine (Guide to Unique Photography), which has followed emerging photography talent since 2005 and has published its annual talent book since 2012. The 2027 edition invites emerging photographers from anywhere in the world to show their vision and expertise in their chosen photographic field, and the selected talents are presented in an atmospheric softcover publication. Each selected photographer gets a minimum of four pages in the book, three copies sent to them, international distribution to galleries, curators, magazines, newspapers and creative photo agencies, and promotion through the GUP and FRESH EYES channels. One image from each selected artist is displayed in a group presentation and book launch at the Haute Photographie Fair in Rotterdam in March 2027, with a free entrance ticket for the photographer and one guest, and portfolio reviews at the fair. Three exceptional talents are chosen by the curators as FRESH EYES Exceptional Talent Award winners and go into the book for free. A jury of GUP editors, gallerists and photographers reads the applications. Applying is free, but this is a pay-if-selected call and the numbers on the organiser's own site do not agree: the Info page says the rate for every selected participant is €350 excluding VAT and shipping, while the FAQ answers "what are the costs?" with €395 excluding VAT, asks "why should I pay €395?", and then answers that question with €350 - so budget for the higher figure. GUP states that it makes no profit from the fee and that it is invested in production, design and distribution of the books, the print exhibition and the launch event; shipping is extra if you cannot collect your copies at the launch. Submit a low-resolution portfolio of fifteen images with a brief artist statement; the FAQ sets a floor of ten images and asks for JPEGs between 500KB and 1MB each, up to 10MB in total. Only photographs are considered - pictures of installations, performances or books are not - and photographers previously selected must apply with a new body of work. Submissions close on October 21st.
