CUPOTY CHALLENGE 2026: Close to Home

Prize £300 / £125 / £75

The themed contest that runs alongside the annual Close-up Photographer of the Year competition, and for 2026 the theme was chosen by vote — "the CUPOTY community has voted in record numbers on the theme and Close to Home came out on top!" The brief is "overlooked wonders in your home, garden or on your doorstep": "close-up, macro and micro photographs taken in a home and its immediate surroundings: the home's rooms, outbuildings (such as sheds, garages and greenhouses), garden, balcony, doorstep or the street directly outside", your own home or that of family or friends. The organisers suggest "frost on a window, a spider in the shed, a mushroom in the garden, the patterns in a soap bubble, a bee in a flower, moss on the step, a leaf brought in from the garden and photographed on the kitchen table", and they rule out "public gardens, parks, allotments, nature reserves, holiday accommodation and commercial premises". Open to "all professional and amateur photographers of any age and nationality" — entrants of 17 or under may enter with a parent or guardian making the payment — and there is no limit on how many entries you buy, nor any date limit on when the pictures were taken. Process is wide open: "any type of camera may be used to capture an image for the Competition, including a mobile phone, scanner or camera trap", and a scan or photograph of "an image made directly on light-sensitive material, such as a photogram, cyanotype or lumen print" counts. Judging is anonymous (each file is given an identifying number) by "a panel of photography experts". The overall winner takes £300, second £125 and third £75, with winner and finalist pictures shown in an online winners gallery and "included in any book or exhibition of CUPOTY". Entrants keep copyright: "CUPOTY respects your rights and does not claim copyright for pictures you submit", while shortlisted and winning images are licensed for competition promotion for five years. Two dates matter: paid entry closes 30 November 2026 "wherever you live in the world", after which you can no longer pay, but "images for entry can continue to be sent to CUPOTY up to 7th December". Shortlist in March 2027, winners in April 2027.
