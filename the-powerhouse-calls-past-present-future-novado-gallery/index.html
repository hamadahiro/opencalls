The Powerhouse Calls: Past, Present & Future

Juried exhibition at Novado Gallery in Jersey City, made with the Jersey City Landmarks Conservancy, built around a single building: the Hudson & Manhattan Railroad Powerhouse, the Industrial Romanesque Revival hall that electrified the tunnels now carrying the PATH trains from 1908 to 1929 and has stood boarded up a few hundred feet from the gallery for most of a century since. The call asks artists to respond to the Powerhouse and to the cultural landscape that grew around it — the Powerhouse Arts District — “literally, abstractly, conceptually, or metaphorically to its history, architecture, neighborhood, and the city’s industrial and cultural evolution”, and the show is explicitly tied to the Save the Powerhouse preservation campaign rather than being a neutral theme. Photography is named in the eligible media alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, video, installation and mixed media. Open to emerging, mid-career and established artists aged 18 or over, with no geographic restrictions; up to three works per artist, each original, related to the theme and available for sale. Accepted work must stay for sale for the run of the show at a maximum retail price of $10,000, and a sale is split 50% to the artist, 25% to the gallery and 25% to the Landmarks Conservancy, with the artist paid by check or Zelle within 15 days of the gallery receiving payment. Two-dimensional work is capped at 48 by 72 inches excluding the frame; three-dimensional at 48 by 60 by 30 inches including any base. Gallery owners Anne Novado and Eleazar Sanchez jury the submissions for quality, originality, relevance and cohesiveness, and may take one, two, three or none of an artist’s works. Entries go in by email with images, a current CV and the fee; incomplete or unpaid submissions are not considered. The gallery has extended the deadline to 11:59pm on Wednesday 11 November 2026, as its call page and an email from the gallery both state; the downloadable entry form (The-Powerhouse-Calls_CaFE.pdf) and the paragraph above the key dates still give the original Friday 6 November, 6pm ET. Acceptances are emailed by 11:55pm ET on Sunday 15 November. Accepted artists supply a bio and a statement of roughly 50–150 words each, and the exact accepted work must be delivered — substitutions are refused and work may not be withdrawn before the closing date. Hand delivery is 6–7 December, shipped work arrives 7–8 December, the opening reception is Saturday 12 December 6:30–8:30pm, the show runs to 30 January 2027, and pickup is 31 January: hand-delivered work left after 1 February incurs a $30-per-day, per-artwork storage fee.
