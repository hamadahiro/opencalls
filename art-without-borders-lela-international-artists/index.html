Art Without Borders

LELA International Artists, a Los Angeles 501(c)(3) that has run annual international artist workshops and exhibitions since 1996 and for many years has taken part in the city of Nagasaki's annual 8.9 Peace Exhibition, invites artists and photographers worldwide to Art Without Borders, a virtual international exhibition on the theme of art that crosses borders and connects people across cultures, countries, languages and experiences. There is no set subject: entrants are asked for original work reflecting their own experience, imagination, culture, environment, identity or vision of an interconnected world. Photography is named in the accepted-media list alongside drawing, painting, mixed media, collage, printmaking, fiber art, digital art and sculpture. Up to three works per artist; entry is $45, or $35 for LELA members, paid through EntryThingy. Betty Ann Brown, PhD - art historian, critic and curator, professor of art history at California State University Northridge since 1986 and a past president of the boards of both the Los Angeles Woman's Building and the Southern California Women's Caucus for Art - is the sole juror and also selects the awards: $300 first, $200 second, $100 third and up to four honorable mentions. Work created under an instructor's direct supervision is not eligible, and no AI-generated artwork is accepted. Selected work stays online for the run of the show and afterwards in the archived-exhibitions pages indefinitely. Artists may offer work for sale and deal directly with buyers; LELA takes no commission. The exhibition is web-only, opening with a Zoom event on 6 November 2026 and running to 1 January 2027. Note that the organiser prints two schedules: an Important Dates block partway down the entry form still reads Thursday 1 October with a 2 November opening, while the form's own header, the EntryThingy deadline panel and LELA's own events page all give 10 October and a 6 November opening - the later set is the one the entry portal enforces.
