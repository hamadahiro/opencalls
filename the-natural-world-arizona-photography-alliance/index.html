Juried Theme Gallery: The Natural World

The third Juried Theme Gallery call of 2026 from the Arizona Photography Alliance, a Tempe-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit running the programme since 2024. The theme is The Natural World: Earth, Wind, Fire and Water as primordial forces that are “neither simply benevolent nor destructive” — fire that warms and consumes, water that sustains and floods, wind that carries seeds and gathers into a gale. Juror Richard Laugharn, an artist working on the natural and cultural histories of the Sonoran Desert Borderlands with an MFA in photography from Arizona State University and work in the Nelson-Atkins Museum and the Center for Creative Photography, picks one first-place winner for a $100 cash award plus two honorable mentions featured on the alliance's website and social channels. Every entrant is represented in the online exhibition, which opens 1 December 2026 — the juror selects one image from each submission. Submit up to three images, no larger than 2 MB and 1,500 pixels on the long side; all photographic media are welcome, analog, digital or alternative process, and AI photography is not accepted. All rights stay with the photographer, entrants may offer their selected image for sale and handle any sale themselves, and AZPA board members may not submit. Entry is open to members and non-members, with the lower rate for members; there are no refunds once entered.
