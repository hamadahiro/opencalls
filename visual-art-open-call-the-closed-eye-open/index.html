Visual Art Open Call

Prize Publication

The Closed Eye Open is a small art-and-literature journal — its current issue is Issue XV — that describes itself as "an exploration of the many forms of self-expression" and sets out to publish work "that may not necessarily connect with other publications" — "Pieces that are weird, unconventional, or even experimental may very well fit with what we're going for." Its visual-art call is open for upcoming issues, and photography is named in the accepted forms: "Visual art may include painting, drawing, photography & print-making, digital art, and hybrid forms." Submit up to six pieces as JPEGs at whatever file size suits you — higher resolution is requested later if a piece is taken — with a statement or cover letter, a short third-person bio, links, and optionally a title list as you want it to appear. Each piece is generally viewed individually, so a submission may deliberately vary in style and subject; if you would rather it be read as one body of work, say so in the cover letter. Simultaneous submissions are accepted on the condition that you withdraw anything placed elsewhere, and work posted only on your own website, blog or social account does not count as previously published. Rights are light-touch: acceptance grants the journal online publishing and archival rights, and after publication all rights revert to the artist, with a request for a credit if the work appears elsewhere later. Issues come out online as PDFs, two or three a year, alongside an ongoing *Maya's Micros* feature updated roughly twice a month. The editors invite people from outside the art world as much as inside — "from established writers & artists, to those in academic disciplines, to those who may be new to the world of sharing their work with a larger audience" — and say a scientific, spiritual or liberal-arts starting point is equally welcome. Submissions run through Submittable and carry a $4.50 fee; the visual-art category closes 30 September 2026. No payment to contributors is mentioned and the journal names no notification date.
