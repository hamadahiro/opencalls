Brussels Street Photography Festival 2026

Prize €5,200

Brussels Street Photography Festival is an annual festival in Brussels, Belgium. The 2026 festival runs May 28–31. The competition has two parallel tracks: Singles (up to 10 individual photos) and Series (5–7 cohesive street photographs).

Total prize fund is €5,200. Winning work is exhibited at the festival.

Eligibility is open globally. Submission is through JotForm with 1–10 images depending on track. Fee is tiered €12–€45 by track and submission scale. The 2026 deadline was March 25.
