The Photography Foundation Awards 2026

The Photography Foundation is the UK's largest photography-specific charity, and its annual awards are run as a fundraising project for that charity's work with emerging creatives. Every entrant gets one free image entry; each additional image is £8 and those fees are treated as donations. There are seven categories — Black and White (sponsored by Black+White Photography Magazine), Landscapes, urban and natural (Manfrotto), Music, Portraits & People (Capture One), Social Documentary (LIMI London), Street Photography and Wedding Photography — and entrants may submit as many images in as many categories as they wish. One overall winner receives £2,000 from Leica towards a new or ongoing photographic project. The winner and runner-up of the Black and White category are published in Black+White Photography Magazine; the Portraits & People winner gets a year of Capture One and a one-day pass to shoot at the Foundation's studio; the Landscapes winner receives a Manfrotto ONE Photo Alu tripod with XPRO ball head. Winners, runners-up and highly commended entrants are all exhibited at the annual Awards Exhibition, hosted by Leica London in 2025 and 2026 and by The Photobook Cafe before that. Judging is strictly anonymous — entrant details are stripped from the images and the eighteen judges vote independently of one another. The awards are explicitly open to amateurs as well as professionals, with entries accepted from all countries, and images must have been taken during 2026. The entry form does not sell single extra images: its dropdown offers one free entry, a 3-image pack at £16, 5 images at £32 or 10 at £72 — exactly £8 for every image past the free one, though the smallest paid outlay is £16 — alongside an optional £5–£100 donation that is not required to enter. The clause that bars AI also bars composites: entries must be authentic camera works, and "composite images, digital artwork, or images that incorporate elements not captured by the photographer through a camera lens are not eligible", with standard post-production allowed and the original RAW file open to request. An image that has already won the title of Overall Winner of another photographic competition cannot be entered, and files are JPEG or TIFF between 800KB and 5MB.
