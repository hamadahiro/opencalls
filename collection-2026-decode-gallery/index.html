Collection 2026 - Decode Gallery

Prize Exhibition prize

Decode Gallery is a Tucson, Arizona photography gallery founded by conceptual fine art photographer Holly Hart, who also serves as juror for the gallery's calls. "Collection 2026" is the gallery's open-theme exhibition — no themes, no subject restrictions, no limits beyond technical quality.

The selection structure is generous: at least 26 images are selected for the physical exhibition. What's unusual: the gallery covers all professional printing on Epson printers with Ultra Premium Luster paper, plus museum-quality framing in 22×22 inch white frames with precut white mats — the artist provides only the high-resolution JPEG. Prints are retained in the gallery's flat files for two years after the exhibition for further promotion and sales.

Sales terms split commission 50/50 between artist and gallery. Selected work is also promoted across Decode's website, social media, and editorial publications. The 2026 exhibition runs August 1 through 29 in Tucson.

Entry fee is $40 for up to 5 images. Eligibility is open worldwide to amateur and professional photographers at all skill levels. The deadline is June 27, 2026; selected artists are notified within two weeks of the deadline.
