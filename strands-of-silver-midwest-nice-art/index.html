strands of silver

Juried online exhibition from midwest nice art, the Sioux Falls artist-run project, exploring the beauty, complexity, and inevitability of time. The brief invites work on aging, memory, transformation, impermanence, and the spaces between beginnings and endings — from the wonder of childhood to the reflection of later years, a cherished memory or a fading photograph. All media and styles are welcome, including photography. Open to artists worldwide.
