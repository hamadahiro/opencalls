Between Here and There

The Greenwich Art Society's annual member exhibition is juried this year by Antonia Pocock, a curatorial assistant at the Whitney Museum of American Art, who organised Claes Oldenburg: Drawn from Life and has written for Art in America, Texte zur Kunst and Nka. The theme is the space between where we were, where we are and where we are going — physical, emotional, personal or imagined — and the society invites work reading it as movement, memory, transformation, discovery or simply a moment of pause. Photography is a named category alongside painting, drawing, pastel, watercolour, sculpture, original digital art, fibre arts, traditional printmaking, mixed media and collage; digital prints of original art and AI-generated work are excluded, and entrants confirm the work is their own and not AI-generated. Work must have been completed in 2023 or later and must not have been shown before in the Bendheim, Flinn or GAS Galleries or accepted into a previous GAS juried exhibition. Wall works are limited to 36 inches wide by 60 inches high framed or unframed, wired and ready to hang. Entering makes you a member of the society through August 2027: $75 covers membership plus one or two pieces, $100 covers three. Over $2,000 in cash and other awards is presented at the closing reception. Accepted work is posted on 10 October 2026, drop-off is 17-18 October, and the exhibition runs 22 October to 19 November 2026 at the Bendheim Gallery in Greenwich. All work must be for sale, with 30 per cent of each sale divided between GAS and the Bendheim Gallery.
