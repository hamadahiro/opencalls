CALYX Journal Art Submissions

CALYX Press, the oldest feminist press in the United States, takes free year-round art submissions for its biannual journal, whose every issue carries a glossy full-colour art insert. Open to women and nonbinary artists; the call names no medium, and the journal has published photography in a Winter 1982 special photography issue and the Winter 2012 edition. The Art Collective considers submissions only in May and December, so response times vary.
