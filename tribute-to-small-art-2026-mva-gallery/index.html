Tribute to Small Art 2026

Prizes $200 NYC exhibition

National call for small-format work at MVA Gallery (Modern Visual Arts) in historic Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. Against an age of supersized images, the gallery makes the case for smallness: work no longer than 18 inches on the longest side, with no restriction on style or subject matter. Artists, photographers and sculptors at every level are invited and all photographic processes are welcome; there is no cutoff date for when the work was made. Open to US residents aged 18 and over; submit 3 to 9 works. Awards are an Art of Distinction award carrying a featured exhibit in the gallery's second room, an optional $200 Director's Choice, honourable mentions that come with free entry to a future MVA show, and an annual grand prize of a New York City exhibition opportunity drawn from every artist shown at the gallery during the year. Work must be wired for hanging; photographs framed in black or white with white or off-white mats if presented traditionally, and plexiglass preferred over glass for shipped pieces. The gallery takes 30% commission and artists cover shipping both ways. Exhibition runs 24 January – 21 February 2027.
