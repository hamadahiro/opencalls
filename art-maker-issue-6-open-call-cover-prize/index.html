ART MAKER Issue 6 — Open Call + Cover Prize

The sixth issue of ART MAKER, an artist-run contemporary art magazine put out two to three times a year by Meg Rogers Eldredge from Northborough, Massachusetts. The theme is simply OPEN — “send us your best work, the work that you are most proud of” — and the juror is the Austrian painter and lithographer Kurt Stimmeder, who took the First Award for Art on Paper at the 2026 OSTEN Biennial in Skopje. Photography is named outright in the accepted-media list, alongside painting, drawing, sculpture, textiles, collage, printmaking, video, animation, sound, performance documentation, installation and digital work; recent work from 2022 onwards is eligible as well as new work made for the call. Each artist makes one application of three to ten images (at least three are required to be considered, and the fee tier has to match the count — submit eight images on the $16 tier and the magazine will invoice you for the difference). What selected artists get is a two-page feature in the print and digital issue with no additional publishing fee, the option to extend that to a four- or six-page interview, features on the website, newsletter and social channels, a free digital copy and discounted print copies — and, since ART MAKER posts each cycle's issue to a mailing list of more than 100 galleries and curators, a route to the people who programme shows. New this issue is a cover prize: the artist whose work is chosen for the cover of Issue 6 receives $150 to Blick art materials (or the equivalent where Blick does not trade) plus the six-page interview. Works made with AI are not considered. Applicants must be 18 or over; the call is international, and the magazine runs a small scholarship fund for artists in regions facing war, famine or severe poverty. CAUTION: the entry form is still unpublished, re-checked 5 November 2026 - the APPLY NOW button on the call page leads to an EntryThingy call whose page title and whole body read "This call isn’t open yet" and "ART MAKER Magazine hasn’t published this call", and the magazine’s EntryThingy board reports "0 Active Calls on EntryThingy" and "No active calls at this time", so nobody can enter at this moment even though the November 6 deadline is still advertised; the same state has now held for seventeen days, since 20 October, with one day left on the advertised deadline, and the magazine publishes no fee or deadline anywhere except this call page.
