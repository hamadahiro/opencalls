FOTODOKS Open Call 2027

Prizes €500 artist fee Exhibition

FOTODOKS, the biennial festival for contemporary documentary photography in Munich, is building the central group exhibition of its thirteenth edition (October 2027) from an open call on care — care as emotional, physical, psychological, social and relational work, and its entanglement with politics, labour, ethics and intimate relationships at a moment when 'community' and 'belonging' circulate as branded buzzwords. The festival seeks existing lens-based, documentary-driven stories of care with a photorealistic and honest intention, asking how documentary photography meets today's imaginaries of care after the dismantling of welfare-state models and the rise of 'self-care' as a slogan for overworked bodies. Submit one project of 5-10 images, in English or German; individuals, duos and collectives are all eligible, and the curatorial team also extends direct invitations alongside the open call. Selected artists receive an artist fee of EUR 500 (EUR 800 for duos, up to EUR 1,200 for collectives), a travel budget of up to EUR 150 within Germany, EUR 300 within Europe or EUR 500 from outside Europe, accommodation for the four-day opening week, full production of the work for the exhibition, inclusion in the festival catalogue with five printed copies, and a presentation on the FOTODOKS website. FOTODOKS encourages applicants of all ethnic backgrounds, religions, ages, genders, disabilities, sexual orientations and social statuses, does not sell exhibited work, and leaves copyright with the artist. Results early spring 2027.
