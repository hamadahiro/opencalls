PhotoWings x Photoville Educator Exhibition Grant 2027

Prizes $500 Exhibition

The tenth edition of a grant that puts school students’ photographs into a major public festival. Photoville, with PhotoWings, invites middle and high school educators to propose an exhibition of their students’ work for the 2027 Photoville Festival, which spreads across Brooklyn Bridge Park and all five boroughs. Six educators are selected, and each receives "Curatorial, design, printing, and logistical support from the Photoville team", "A public exhibition of their students’ work as part of the 2027 Photoville Festival" and "A $500 cash grant". READ BOTH OF PHOTOVILLE’S OWN PAGES, BECAUSE THEY STATE ELIGIBILITY DIFFERENTLY. The festival homepage gives only a school-level bar: "This program is ONLY open to Middle and High School Educators who work directly with students. School Administrators and Elementary and College programs will not be considered." The grant’s own info page — the route that homepage links as its "info page" — adds a condition the homepage never mentions, and softens the geography: "This grant is open to all middle and high school educators who have participated in a Photoville Education program in the past. Please note that New York City based schools will be given priority in the selection process." So New York is a stated priority rather than a bar, and the grant’s own published recipient list runs past the five boroughs: alongside schools in the Bronx, Park Slope and Staten Island it names the Issaquah High School Photo Club and the all-Tribal "Our Community Record Two Eagle River School, Confederated Salish & Kootenai Tribes". The prior-participation clause, though, is stated flatly, so an educator new to Photoville should ask before building a proposal. The grant builds on "9 years of collaboration with educators to develop lesson plans inspired by photography projects", and what it funds is the exhibition that comes out of teaching one: educators "implement a photo-based lesson from the Photoville Educator Resource library in their classroom and produce a culminating exhibition of student work". Photoville notes that "for the past 8 years we have seen a vastly dynamic array of students’ work". Proposals go through Photoville’s own submission form, which closes "January 4, 2027 at 11:59pm ET"; the festival’s separate artist call for proposals closes earlier, on 22 December 2026. Photoville Festival is supported in part by public funds from the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs in partnership with the City Council.
