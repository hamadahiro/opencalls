Lake Garda Photo Challenge

Prizes €1,500 Sirmione stays

Season-long photo contest from the Sirmione Award for Photography, inviting images of Sirmione and the wider Lake Garda basin. Two main themes — Sirmione and Lake Garda — each award €1,500 first prize, €500 second, and a two-night stay in Sirmione for third. Up to three photographs may be entered across the main themes (any shooting date), plus an optional fourth for the bonus tracks Margins (nature reclaiming abandoned spaces) or In the Heart of the Night (low-light work), which carry hospitality prizes; a special award honors the area's historical and archaeological heritage. Flat €10 registration regardless of the number of photographs; reflex, compact and phone cameras admitted.

DeadlineOctober 31, 2026
Entry fee€10
LocationSirmione, Italy
RequirementsUp to 4 images
AI policyNot accepted

Jury: Roberto Bellini, Photographer · Luigi Brozzi, Photographer · Antonello Perin, Photographer
