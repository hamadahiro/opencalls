Exhibit Your Work — Exhibition Proposals

Photosynthesis, the volunteer-run photography gallery, darkroom and studio on Battersea Street in Sydenham, Ōtautahi Christchurch, takes exhibition proposals all year through an expression-of-interest form. Its exhibitions run in sponsored seasons of ten weeks at no cost to the exhibitors, with one or two shows on at a time, each for two or three weeks. Proposals are welcome from emerging, mid-career and established artists, collectives, curators and community groups, for photography in the broadest sense: anything made using light. The expression of interest covers the proposed show's content, representative images, duration and dates, information about the artist and what is needed from the gallery's volunteers. A curation panel of community peers reviews it and recommends accepting, revising or declining the show, and a selected artist receives a letter of acceptance. Exhibitors install the show, hold an opening on its first Wednesday, staff the gallery on run days and pack out, with volunteer help on request, and are encouraged to run workshops, talks or demonstrations. The large wall is 13.8 m long with a 2.4 m display height; the smaller area has 9 m of wall with optional suspended panels. Exhibiting artists can print at the gallery's rentable digital workspace. The page names two 2026 seasons, from 29 June and from 28 September, both already under way; no later season is published yet.
