2026 Members Exhibition — Hunterdon Art Museum

Prizes Solo exhibition $100

Annual members exhibition at the Hunterdon Art Museum in Clinton, New Jersey. Juror Bryant Small, associate curator at the Morris Museum, selects one artist for a solo exhibition in the museum's River Gallery during the 2027–28 season, and a $100 museum prize goes to another artist. Open to current museum members — non-members may join when applying — with work from the last five years not previously shown at the museum; media include photography, sculpture, fiber, glass, printmaking, oils, acrylics, watercolors, pastel, pencil, and collage. Up to 5 images ($40 covers the first three).

DeadlineJuly 30, 2026
ResultsOn or about August 21, 2026
Entry fee$40 (3 images), $10 each additional
EligibilityMembership required
LocationClinton, NJ
RequirementsUp to 5 images
