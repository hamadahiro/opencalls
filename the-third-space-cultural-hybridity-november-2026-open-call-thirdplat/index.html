THE THIRD SPACE: CULTURAL HYBRIDITY — November 2026 Open Call

Prize Publication

Thirdplat's free monthly open call for November 2026 asks what happens when cultures with their own histories, identities and forms of expression meet through globalisation. The platform's own framing: "A local craft may adopt digital tools, folk music may enter an electronic composition, different languages may share a single poem, or traditional imagery may find a new life in global fashion and film. These encounters can produce connection, tension, resistance and unexpected new forms." It wants work that "begin[s] with specific cultural materials, symbols, customs, languages or techniques and show[s] what happens when they come into contact", and it poses the hard question directly: "When does exchange become appropriation, and when does a hybrid form develop a voice of its own?" Photography is named outright among the welcomed forms — "Whether manifested as moving image, photography, writing, craft, sound, performance or cross-disciplinary work, Thirdplat welcomes original and respectful creations that explore cultural fusion, conflict, transformation and the new identities formed in between." This is publication rather than competition: Thirdplat is an independent online platform that publishes accepted work directly into a permanent public archive, and its Terms of Use state plainly that "Submission is free" and that "No payment, prize, commission or commercial agreement is created by submitting". Six works are already published under this theme. Submitting runs through a Thirdplat account and the platform's Terms of Use set the only entry bar: “You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or submit work through Thirdplat.” Submissions close 30 November 2026; the platform's December theme (REFLECTION: AI AND US, closing 31 December 2026) and January 2027 theme (Counter-Memory) are open alongside it.
