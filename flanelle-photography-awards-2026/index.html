Flanelle Photography Awards 2026

The first annual awards from Flanelle, the independent Montreal fashion-photography magazine, aimed at "the images that stay with you" and open to photographers worldwide at any level, amateur or professional. Four categories: Women's Fashion and Men's Fashion (styled, concept-driven work for editorial or publication), Beauty (editorial beauty and make-up as the primary subject), and Emerging Photographer, reserved for photographers with less than two years' experience and open across the other three themes. A Photographer of the Year Grand Prix is drawn from all submissions with no separate entry. An entry is up to eight photographs submitted as a series, $25 per entry, and you may enter more than one category. The jury is named and sits outside the magazine for three of its four seats: Steven Branco (Editor-in-Chief, View the VIBE), make-up artist Marika D'auteuil (Makeup Artist of the Year at the Contessa Awards), Dan Blake (co-founder of the London College of Style, stylist with Vogue and GQ credits) and Flanelle's own founder and editor-in-chief Sarah-Eve Leduc; all entries are scored on creativity, technical quality, relevance to a fashion and editorial aesthetic, and emotional impact, with deliberation on 18–20 December. There is no cash prize. Each category winner is featured on the cover of an upcoming Flanelle print issue (the magazine values this at $800), receives a free year of a Kavyar Pro Biz account, a hard copy of the issue and a year in the Flanelle Directory, with a free year of Kavyar Pro for all credited team members — a stated total value over $1,600, most of it a publication credit and software rather than money. Entrants must be 18 or over and own the rights to everything submitted; images must be free of watermarks, borders or identifying text; children's fashion is not accepted; completely AI-generated images are not eligible.
