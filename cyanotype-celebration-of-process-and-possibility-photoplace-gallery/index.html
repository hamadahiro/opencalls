Cyanotype: A Celebration of Process and Possibility

Prizes $300 Juror's Award Exhibition Catalog

Juried exhibition at PhotoPlace Gallery in Middlebury, Vermont celebrating cyanotype in any form — traditional contact prints, camera-made cyanotypes, photograms, digital negatives, toned and wet cyanotypes, mixed media and experimental approaches, as long as cyanotype is the primary component of the finished work. Juried by Sam Wang. Up to 35 images selected for the gallery exhibition and 40 more for the online gallery; all selected work appears in the exhibition catalog and remains on the gallery website. Exhibition September 4–26, 2026, closing with World Cyanotype Day workshops and artist talks on September 26. Prints due August 21.
