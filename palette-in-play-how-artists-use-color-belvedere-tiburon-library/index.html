Palette in Play: How Artists Use Color

Juried group exhibition at THE Gallery, the exhibition space of the Belvedere Tiburon Library in Tiburon, California, asking Bay Area artists for the work that best exemplifies how they use colour. The gallery wants submissions that help it “unpack and decode your color choices” — does your use of colour capture feeling, does it manipulate mood, or is your love of colour self-evident? Glowing, vibrant work that shapes perception and muted, calm choices that still the viewer's mind are equally welcome. Photography is named in the accepted media alongside painting, drawing, fine art prints, mixed media and textile/fabric art. The show is two-dimensional only, including low-relief work up to five inches deep; sculpture, suspended work and reproductions of your own art in another medium (an offset lithograph of your painting, a giclée of your watercolour) are not considered, and art created using artificial intelligence will not be considered. The library is a family destination and the gallery asks that subject matter suit that audience. THE Gallery blind-juries most of its exhibitions and asks that artist statements omit the artist's name. Selected work hangs 19 November 2026 to 13 January 2027, with an opening reception on 19 November, and is seen by the several thousand visitors who attend gallery and library events or stroll through the library. Entry is $35 for up to three submissions, each of which may carry two additional detail images. All work must be for sale, with 70% to the artist and 30% to THE Gallery; pieces must be ready to hang, between 8 x 10 inches and 72 x 60 inches, and under 50 pounds. Drop-off and pick-up are in person in Tiburon. The prospectus names no cash award — selection into the show is the outcome.
