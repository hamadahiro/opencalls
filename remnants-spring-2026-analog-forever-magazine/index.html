Remnants — Spring 2026 Online Exhibition

Prize Online exhibition prize

Analog Forever Magazine is a publication and online platform dedicated to analog and alternative-process photography, founded by Michael Behlen. Remnants is the platform's Spring 2026 themed group exhibition.

The brief reads photography against loss and absence — family members, homes, ways of life, places, objects, and memories. The thematic register favors interior, narrative, or memorial work over straight documentation.

Eligibility is restricted by process: film and analog only. Single-image submission. Behlen curates the selection personally rather than as part of a jury.

Submitters are encouraged (not required) to include a paragraph on what the photographed subject means to them and why. Application is free, submitted through JotForm.

The exhibition launches online May 29, 2026 — same date as results are announced. The 2026 deadline was May 1.
