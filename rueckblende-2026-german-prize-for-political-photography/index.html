Rückblende 2026 — German Prize for Political Photography

Germany's national prize for political photography asks professional press photographers living in Germany for work on the political year 2026. Entry is free, with a €7,000 first prize, a Leica-funded award for best series and a touring exhibition that runs from Berlin to Bonn, Mainz, Koblenz and Brussels. The portal closes at noon on 30 November, and entries in the best-series category are capped at the first 50.

DeadlineNovember 30, 2026
Prizes€7,000, Exhibition tour, Catalogue
Entry feeFree
EligibilityGermany only, 18+, Professional only
LocationBerlin, Germany
Requirements1–4 single photos, or a series of 5–6 images
AI policyNot accepted

ResultsAward ceremony January 2027
