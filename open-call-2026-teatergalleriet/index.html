Open Call 2026

Prize Exhibition

An open, free call for exhibition proposals at a Swedish artist-run gallery that has been running since 1984. Teatergalleriet publishes the call in Swedish and English side by side: "Teatergalleriet in Kalmar is a non-profit, artist-run association that has presented solo and group exhibitions in our gallery in central Kalmar since 1984. We showcase a broad range of artistic practices of a professional standard and create space for diversity, new perspectives, and artistic exploration." The association was founded by working artists, and its stated aim is to give practising artists the chance to exhibit in Kalmar and to build public interest in art and craft. The call itself enumerates no media at all; what settles whether a photographer belongs here is the gallery's own exhibition archive, and it does, if thinly - Emelie Carlen's SIRENS' SONG (October 2025) is listed with a "Fotogram" (photogram) installed on the upper floor alongside a video projection and ceramics, so camera-less photographic work has been shown in this programme. The rest of the archive runs to embroidery, tapestry and painting, printmaking, cartographic drawing and sculpture, so a photographer should read this as a genuinely open programme rather than a photography venue. THE TIMELINE IS LONG AND THE GALLERY SAYS SO PLAINLY: applications are reviewed in January 2027 with outcomes notified in February 2027, and because "Teatergalleriet's exhibition programme is planned approximately one year in advance, successful proposals may be scheduled for exhibition from 2028 onwards." What a selected artist gets is a show in the gallery at Olof Palmes gata 1 by Larmtorget in central Kalmar, plus this commitment: "For artists who are offered an exhibition, Teatergalleriet undertakes to apply for exhibition remuneration through Sveriges Konstforeningar (The Swedish Art Associations)" - applied for on the artist's behalf, not guaranteed. There is no entry fee stated anywhere on the call and no cash prize. THE BINDING TERMS SIT ON A SEPARATE PAGE, NOT ON THE CALL: the gallery's "For utstallare" (For exhibitors) page carries a VILLKOR (conditions) block the call never mentions. Two items cost the artist money - "Vi tar 30 % inkl moms av forsaljningen av verk for att driva galleriet" (the gallery takes 30% including VAT of any sale of work, to run the gallery), and "Fraktkostnader och boende for konstnaren ska tackas av utstallningsersattningen", i.e. shipping and the artist's accommodation are to be met out of that exhibition remuneration rather than on top of it. The exhibitor is responsible for mounting the show, though the gallery's intendent can assist; against that, the gallery produces and pays for a poster and fly-posts it in Kalmar, invites the press to a viewing and invites its members to the vernissage, and it lists the sound and moving-image kit it lends - a 49-inch 4K monitor, a Full HD projector, speakers, headphones, media players, microphones and a 400 W PA. The same page records that the selection body meets once a year, at the turn of the year, and that it may ask a shortlisted applicant for further material. Applications go by email to the gallery as one single PDF of no more than 10 MB containing a CV of at most one page, an artist statement or presentation of at most one page, an exhibition proposal with ideas for the use of the gallery space of at most four pages including images, and a portfolio of at most ten pages. The deadline is 1 November 2026.
