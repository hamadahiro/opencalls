Brightwork Fellowship 2027

Anchorlight's flagship studio-based fellowship for one North Carolina artist a year at a pivotal moment in their career: a 500-plus square foot studio at Anchorlight in Raleigh, a solo exhibition in its zero-commission gallery, and an unrestricted award of $50,000. Anchorlight is home to 28 artist studios and a 1,500 square foot gallery; the Brightwork programme began in 2017 and relaunched under the current model in 2022, with the Brightwork Foundation formed in late 2025 to steward it. Applicants must have lived in North Carolina full time for at least twelve consecutive months and commit to staying for the fellowship. The same cycle also offers two Brightwork Grants to NC-based visual artists — a $2,000 Professional Development Grant and a $3,000 Studio Project Grant — but you may apply for only one of the three. Practising visual artists 21 and over of any career stage are eligible; performers, filmmakers, musicians and writers are not.
