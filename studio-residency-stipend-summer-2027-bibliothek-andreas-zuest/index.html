Studio Residency Stipend — Summer 2027

Prizes Residency CHF 750 subsidy

The Bibliothek Andreas Züst is an art library housed in the Alpenhof, a panorama lodge at 1,110 m above the Rhine Valley in Oberegg, Appenzell Innerrhoden, built around the collection of the Swiss artist, photographer and natural scientist Andreas Züst. Once a year it offers a six-week work and living space, and the next residency runs 4 July – 15 August 2027. The stipend is deliberately collaborative: it is open to duos and collectives of up to five people, both established teams and newly founded ones that want to try working together. The organiser's own information sheet lists the disciplines it covers — visual arts, literature, new media, music, theatre, design, architecture, film, photography, applied arts and art-related academic disciplines — and asks for projects that engage with the library as a whole or one of its sub-areas, in the tradition of libraries as places of knowledge. Applications from families and people with children are welcome. Accommodation, food and use of the studio are free of charge, travel to and from the Alpenhof by public transport is covered, and each resident gets a Half-Fare card for the month; a separate request can be made for an additional living-cost subsidy of up to CHF 750. Accident insurance is the resident's own. Everyone has a private bedroom, though the information sheet notes that a work space outside the bedroom is not possible for every resident; the library and the rooms and niches around the house are open for concentrated work. Life on site is self-organised, cooking and cleaning included — residents take turns making the shared dinner from a stocked kitchen. A maximum of ten stipendiaries is taken in total. Applicants from outside Switzerland need an entry permit, and the call is aimed at people resident in Europe. Application is by email as a single PDF of no more than 7 MB, containing the completed application form, a project description (max. 1 A4 page), documentation of previous projects (max. 5 pages), a CV for each collaborator (max. 1 page each) and a page on the motivation and way of working together as a collective. Residents are chosen by a panel of library representatives and one external member, announced by 31 January 2027; the panel gives no reasons for its decision. The programme is sponsored by Verein Ida, Zürich.
