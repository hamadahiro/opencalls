ICP Curatorial Vision Scholarship

A $10,000 scholarship from the International Center of Photography for one emerging curator, covering most of the $12,500 tuition for ICP's Online One-Year Certificate in Curatorial Practice in Photography. The award “supports an emerging curator in developing the critical perspectives, research, and curatorial approaches needed to engage with photography and visual culture today”, and ICP frames it as “contributing to a more diverse and expansive conversation around the photographic image”. One recipient is chosen on the strength of the application materials and portfolio, and finalists may be invited to a Zoom interview. Both the programme application (through SlideRoom, $60 fee) and the separate scholarship application must be submitted by 18 October 2026, so a recipient's remaining cost is $2,500 of tuition plus that application fee. Scholarships are only available to students enrolling in ICP's One-Year Certificate programmes - part-time courses and Open Education workshops are not eligible - and awards cannot be combined, so an applicant may receive only one. ICP encourages applicants to apply for every scholarship they are eligible for. Decisions arrive by email with the admission letter; ICP is not an accredited institution, so the certificate does not qualify for federal student loans.
