JRNL Magazine Open Call 2026

JRNL is FotoFilmic's recurring photography magazine. This open call (Summer/Fall 2026 issue) accepts all lens-based contemporary practices — from film and alternative or historical processes to digital and hybrid methods. Photographers submit up to 25 images from a single cohesive project or series, with work created after January 1, 2016. Selected photographers each receive 10 full spreads (20 pages) in the A4-format magazine plus a complimentary print copy; the issue launches November 13, 2026 in Paris during Paris Photo week.
