16th Annual International Juried Exhibition

Prizes $1,000 / $500 / $300 $200 People's Choice

Gallery 110's annual juried exhibition, running since 2011, invites emerging and established artists worldwide to submit original work in any two- or three-dimensional medium — photography included — for a show in the gallery's Pioneer Square space from 4 to 27 February 2027. The 2026 juror is Ryan Hardesty, Executive Director of the Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art at Washington State University, who spent twelve years at the Northwest Museum of Arts & Culture in Spokane and has held positions at the Institute of Contemporary Art in Boston, the Rose Art Museum at Brandeis and the Fogg Art Museum at Harvard. Hardesty has chosen an open call with no set theme. He awards first, second and third prizes of $1,000, $500 and $300, and there is a $200 People's Choice award. Gallery 110 is an artist-run 501(c)(3) non-profit supported by 4Culture and the City of Seattle Office of Arts & Culture; accepted artists get professional reviews and inclusion in the monthly First Thursday Art Walk, which draws over a thousand visitors. Applications are accepted from 21 July to 31 October 2026. Finalists are notified by email and phone in December 2026 and are responsible for delivery and prepaid return shipping. Open to artists 18 and over.
