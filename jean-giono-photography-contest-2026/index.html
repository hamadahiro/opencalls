Jean Giono Photography Contest 2026

Prize €500 / €300 / €200 prize

Free photography contest from the Friends of Jean Giono association, organised in partnership with Regards Alpins (a member association that runs photographic events tied to literature). Photographs — single image or coherent series — inspired by an excerpt from Giono's novel Noé, meditating on extraordinary landscapes and the act of seeing them through a lens. The 2026 Rencontres Giono festival is themed "Giono and Photography," running July 29–August 2 in Manosque; winners are announced at the festival on August 1, 2026. Open to amateurs and professionals of any age and country.

DeadlineJune 15, 2026
ResultsJuly 20, 2026
Entry feeFree
LocationManosque, France
Requirements1 image or series
Submit viaEmail

Jury: Sylvie Giono, Jean Giono's daughter (Co-Chair) · Hans Silvester, Photographer (Co-Chair)

Organized by Les Amis de Jean Giono
