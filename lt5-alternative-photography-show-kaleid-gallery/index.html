LT5 Alternative Photography Show

Prize Exhibition

KALEID Gallery is a San Jose, California artist-run gallery. The LT5 Alternative Photography Show is the gallery's recurring call for work made through alternative photographic processes — toy and plastic cameras, Polaroid, pinhole, photograms, and other process-restricted approaches.

Selection is by curator Robertino Ragazza.

Selected work is shown at KALEID Gallery in San Jose, with the opening reception on May 1 during South First Fridays Art Walk — the monthly downtown San Jose gallery event. The exhibition runs May 1–29, 2026.

Eligibility is restricted by process: alternative photography only. Submission is by email with up to 2 works. Application is free. The 2026 deadline was April 12; results announced April 15, 2026.
