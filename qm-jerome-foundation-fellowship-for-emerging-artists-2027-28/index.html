QM-Jerome Foundation Fellowship for Emerging Artists 2027–28

Prizes $20,000 × 2 Solo exhibition

The eighth iteration of the Queens Museum's fellowship for emerging visual artists living in the five boroughs of New York City. Two artists each receive $20,000, professional development consultations with QM staff, and four mentorship and feedback sessions over the year with an established New York artist of their choosing. Each fellowship runs one year and culminates in a solo project exhibition at the Queens Museum in 2028; the award covers the artist fee, materials and production costs for the work made for that show. Emerging is defined as at least two but no more than ten years of practice outside a degree programme, with no solo exhibitions at established commercial galleries or museums and no major grants; age and formal training are not factors. Applications are reviewed by QM curatorial staff and two guest jurors, with in-person interviews for shortlisted candidates in November.
