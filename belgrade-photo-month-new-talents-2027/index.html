Belgrade Photo Month — New Talents 2027

The ninth edition of the Belgrade Photo Month festival opens its New Talents call to a younger generation of photographers, free to enter and open to any nationality, professional or amateur, on one hard condition: you must have been born after 1 January 2002. The theme is REBORN — to be reborn is to enter a new state, suggesting change but also continuity — and the festival explicitly leaves the reading open, welcoming any photographic genre or artistic approach that works through people, places, memories, identities, communities, nature or society. Entries are one project of eight to fifteen photographs, titled and numbered in viewing order, at a maximum 1920 pixels on the long side, sent by email with an artist's statement, a short CV including your birth date, and a text about the proposal of no more than 3,000 characters in English. AI photographs are not accepted. Proposals submitted to previous editions cannot be resubmitted. The jury — Joan Alvado, Julio Pantoja, Shirin Abedi, Sigrid Debusschere and Vesna Latinović — picks three best proposals plus the best local Serbian proposal, then awards first, second and third place and a prize for the best local proposal; all four projects are shown in the New Talents 2027 exhibition during the festival in Belgrade. The festival does not publish what the prizes consist of. Copyright and all other author's rights stay with the photographer, who is credited in every official festival publication and in the exhibition; nominated and winning photographs join the Belgrade Photo Month collection, and entrants accept that the organisers and general sponsors may use submitted photographs to promote the festival. Submissions close 14 February 2027 at 23:30 Belgrade time, successful authors are notified 7 March 2027 and the exhibition opens 7 May 2027.
