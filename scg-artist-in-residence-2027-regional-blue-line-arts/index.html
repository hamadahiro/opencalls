SCG Artist in Residence & Teaching Program 2027 — Regional

Prizes $10,000 honorarium 3-week residency

Three-week teaching residency (April 9–30, 2027) at Blue Line Arts in Roseville, California, funded by Placer Community Foundation. The selected artist receives a $10,000 honorarium plus $1,470 per diem and studio space, in exchange for an artist lecture, open studio hours, school tours, and two adult workshops. Open to artists 18+ residing in Placer, Nevada, Yolo, El Dorado, Sacramento, or Sutter-Yuba counties who have not previously held an SCG residency at Blue Line.
