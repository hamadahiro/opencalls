Exhibition Call for Entries — Detroit

Prize Exhibition

The photobook publisher Subjectively Objective is opening "a new series of exhibitions at our soon to be expanded Detroit area gallery space" and taking submissions of up to ten images. There is no theme: "This is an open call with no specific theme, as this will allow us the greatest ability to create a variety of interesting exhibitions. Our primary focus is on contemporary landscape and documentary photography, but even if your work falls outside of that, please don't hold back from submitting." Work not placed in one of these shows is still considered for the publisher's other channels — "our Substack Features, Instagram Features, and Mini and Monthly Monograph Magazines" — and returning contributors are explicitly invited back. READ THE COST STRUCTURE BEFORE YOU APPLY: the $10 submission fee is only the entry, and "if selected, the artist will be responsible for an exhibition fee that covers the cost of producing their exhibition print(s), the opening, and promoting the physical exhibition." You pick the largest print size you want to be considered for — you remain eligible for smaller ones — and that choice sets the bill if you are taken: $30 per image for a small print (about 15x12 inches), $45 medium, $65 large (25x20), $90 XL (30x24), $150 XXL (40x32), $300 for a full eight-foot wall, $350–600 for a wall of larger prints, and $600+ for multiple walls, the last being "for those open to the possibility of participation in a small group show or even a solo exhibition". Printing is done in house by founder Noah Waldeck, who "will be using the same expert techniques he uses to make museum quality prints for his custom lab clients", and after the show he will send your print on if you cover shipping. The submission fee itself is waivable: "If you can't afford the submission fee, please contact me and we'll figure something out." Instructions arrive as a downloadable PDF once the fee is paid.
