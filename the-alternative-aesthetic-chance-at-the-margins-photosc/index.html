The Alternative Aesthetic: Chance at the Margins

Prize Exhibition

PhotoSC is a non-profit photography gallery in Columbia, South Carolina, built around a continuing dialogue about photography and the photographic canon. Marking photography's 200 years of experimentation, The Alternative Aesthetic: Chance at the Margins goes looking for the accident and the sleight of hand — the idea that chance has sat at the heart of photographic and scientific discovery from the very beginning, and that the non-traditional method has always been the alternative to the cultural order of photography. The call seeks historical techniques, photosensitive chemicals and unusual materials, and the textures, colours and effects that are impossible with standard digital or silver gelatin work; experimentation is explicitly encouraged. The juror is Michael Kirchoff, Editor-in-Chief of Analog Forever Magazine, founding editor of Catalyst: Interviews, an ongoing juror for Photolucida's Critical Mass and a former board member of APA/LA. Photographers may submit up to five works for $35, with submissions open from 5 August to 13 September 2026 and accepted artists notified by 4 October. Accepted work must be printed no smaller than 8×10 inches and no larger than 16×20, framed and ready to hang, and delivered by the 23 October receipt-of-work date on the call timeline (a line of prose lower down the same page still says 30 September, which predates the 4 October notification). The exhibition opens 29 October at 918 Lady Street, Columbia, with a reception the same evening, and hangs until 29 November 2026. Sales are encouraged at a 40/60 gallery/artist split; photographers retain copyright. No AI-generated imagery is accepted.
