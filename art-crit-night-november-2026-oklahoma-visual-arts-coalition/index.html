Art Crit Night — November 2026

A free, curator-led public critique run by the Oklahoma Visual Arts Coalition, the statewide service organisation for Oklahoma artists. Two Oklahoma visual artists are selected per edition to present their work in person at 1515 Lincoln Gallery in Oklahoma City, receive structured feedback from the guest curator, and take questions from an audience of artists, arts professionals and collectors. Selected artists receive a $100 stipend and the application is free. The November 12, 2026 edition is led by Alexa Goetzinger, an independent curator, grant writer and development consultant based in Oklahoma City, who curated ArtNow: Materials and Boundaries at Oklahoma Contemporary in 2026, co-curated the national print exchange Connect: Collect and its exhibition Print as Object for Oklahoma City University's Hulsey Gallery from 2019 to 2023, and was previously Associate Director of OVAC itself. Work is shown to the room by projector, so documentation quality matters and detail shots help; smaller physical works can sometimes be accommodated, performance must be submitted as video, and artists may ask for the critique to focus on a website, an artist statement or a residency application instead of a body of work. The application asks for an artist statement, a bio, a stated goal for the critique and artwork samples. No medium is enumerated — the call says only "Oklahoma visual artists" and nothing excludes photography, whose projected presentation the format suits; OVAC's Grants for Artists programme has funded photographers, but this programme publishes no roster that affirms it. Applications close October 11 and artists are notified October 20.
