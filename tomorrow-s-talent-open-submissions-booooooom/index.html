Website & Instagram Features

Prize Publication

Booooooom is a long-running arts and culture platform for artists, photographers, illustrators and designers, and this is its standing rolling call. Membership is the gate: joining lets you submit your work to be considered for features on the site and its social channels, gives unlimited submissions to all of Booooooom's own open calls and opportunities, unlocks the member-only list of open calls it compiles from around the web, and takes 20% off the shop. The platform lists this strand as "Website & Instagram Features - Open for Submissions", region international, deadline rolling, and says submissions go through a member-only form as many times as you like; film and video are not currently accepted. Membership is $10 USD a month, auto-renewing and cancellable at any time; the annual tier the platform used to sell is no longer offered. Booooooom's two competitive strands are on a separate cycle and are not open today: the Art & Photo Book Award is marked closed for submissions, the Illustration Awards open in summer 2026 and the Photo Awards in autumn 2026, both taking pre-registrations now. The Tomorrow's Talent art-book series that this listing was originally named for is between editions - Tomorrow's Talent 5 was called for in July 2025 and the book shipped in March 2026, and no new volume is open.
