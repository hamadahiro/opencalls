Prize Publication

Booooooom is a long-running arts and culture platform for artists, photographers, illustrators and designers, and this is its standing rolling call: submit work to be considered for features on the website and its social channels. Submissions are free and made through a form on the submit page itself; every submission is automatically published to the platform's public Submissions feed, and staff review recent submissions for potential features. Three guidelines govern it: no explicit content (nudity, violence) because submissions appear on site automatically, no advertising outside promotional material for exhibitions, and by submitting you give Booooooom permission to display your images on the site and its social channels. The membership page that previously sold a $10-a-month tier now carries only a newsletter signup and an opportunities list; the platform's competitive strands sit on a separate cycle - the Art & Photo Book Award is marked closed for submissions, the Illustration Awards are listed as opening September 2026 and the Photo Awards in autumn 2026, both still marked Coming Soon. At the time of checking the submissions platform was temporarily unavailable for maintenance. The Tomorrow's Talent art-book series this listing was originally named for is between editions - Tomorrow's Talent 5 was called for in July 2025 and the book shipped in March 2026, and no new volume is open.
